# Corpus Differentiation Workstream 2: Stone Lung and Swine Terminal Procedures

## Judgment

Workstream 2 is complete. *The Siege of the Stone Lung* and *When the Swine Kneel* still share ancient infrastructure, incomplete preparation, multiple approaches, costly remedies, and consequences that survive technical success. They no longer use the same terminal procedure.

Stone Lung now treats every machine stroke as a contested military exchange. Swine now treats every intervention as a hypothesis tested by changing animal and surface reports. The former asks which force holds a road under enemy initiative. The latter asks whether Veyr is still listening to the inhabited places through which pressure moves.

## Paragraph-level comparison

The pre-repair finales repeated the same functional sequence:

1. three approaches enter an old machine;
2. earlier encounters provide crews, measurements, authority, and route advantages;
3. a short visible clock escalates through three phases;
4. multiple stations require distributed staffing;
5. missing preparation becomes time, hazard, or collateral cost;
6. several complete remedies trade survival among communities; and
7. a final accounting preserves partial success and later legal judgment.

Those functions are sound in isolation. Side by side, they made the finales feel like variants of one distributed engineering procedure.

## Distinct dramatic ownership

| Shared function | Stone Lung owner | Swine owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Approaches | Military roads carrying troops, orders, wounded, and technical crews | Ways into the hollow whose meaning depends on reports from the surface corridor above |
| Escalation | Bas acts, Venn answers when able, and a named sector comes under attack on each Lung stroke | A herd changes behavior, a surface corridor becomes dangerous, officials deny a report, or the hollow answers an intervention |
| Distributed work | Battlefronts, escorts, command links, and enemy interruption | Runners, handlers, farm lanterns, speaking pipes, College needles, and lost communication |
| Time | Repeated breaths cost named ground, formations, or evacuation roads | No fixed cycle allotment; the pattern continues until a remedy changes the reports or the living survey is lost |
| Remedy proof | A truce, held road, surviving force, and pressure release must coexist | Surface herds and water behavior must confirm that the buried intervention worked |
| Partial success | Held and lost sectors, withdrawals, prisoners, and control of the Lung | Surviving lines, named damaged corridors, water service, warnings, and animals that still refuse or root |
| Moral center | Two armed settlements contest air, command, and enforceable access | Veyr must name the inhabited place selected to bear hydraulic loss |

## Stone Lung repair

Exactly three authoritative fields changed on `the-stone-lung`: `summary`, `opening_view`, and `content`.

The finale retains the Lung's physical compression, draw, and release. Those strokes are now inseparable from the siege:

- Bas chooses an initiative before the company acts;
- Venn may countermand, concede a ceasefire corridor, or continue the assault;
- Brann's reserve, the Gate, the Foundry, the southern corridor, and the Countermine remain active fronts;
- a repeated stroke costs a named sector, unit, escort, or evacuation road rather than an abstract grace cycle; and
- each complete outcome is also a military position that one or both armies may accept, exploit, or renew.

The former generic `Stations` section is replaced by `Battlefronts and commands`. The resolution ends when the pressure crisis and battle reach a position no commander can immediately overturn.

## Swine repair

Exactly three authoritative fields changed on `the-deep-bell`: `summary`, `opening_view`, and `content`.

The fixed three-cycle resonance procedure and station staffing are removed. The finale now runs through a living survey:

- Southgate and Rillcross herds report phase, movement, splitting, and subsidence;
- College instruments distinguish repair from broken silence;
- chapel doctrine ties every buried line to an inhabited surface name;
- Hall authority protects messengers, rationing, warnings, and evacuation;
- every major intervention below produces a new report above; and
- lost communication removes knowledge rather than a generic station bonus.

The finale explicitly has no fixed allotment of three cycles and no abstract grace extension. A remedy is not complete until the surface reports show what changed.
