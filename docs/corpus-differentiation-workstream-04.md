# Corpus Differentiation Workstream 4: Aurelune and Seven Reeds Court Differentiation

## Judgment

Workstream 4 is complete. *The Concord of Aurelune* and *The Mandate of Seven Reeds* were already different in setting, stakes, and constitutional question, but their current GM-facing layers still shared too much generic court grammar: commissions, bargains, public records, drafting rooms, and culminating audiences could sometimes be described with nearly interchangeable labels.

The repair does not make either adventure less procedural. It assigns the procedures to different sources of authority. Aurelune is now visibly a living coalition whose banners, seasons, court objects, and magically constrained recognitions must inhabit one undertaking. Seven Reeds is visibly provincial administration under incomplete sovereignty: the Emperor already possesses authority, while present holders must submit assets and named field officers must receive executable orders.

## Comparative diagnosis

The comparison covered four matched functions:

1. the opening commission and its limits;
2. the rhythm of faction encounters;
3. how bargains, concessions, and side agreements become operative; and
4. what the final audience actually decides.

The prior source already contained the correct distinction. The remaining overlap came from generic headings and a few framing paragraphs that treated both adventures as coalitions negotiating one settlement.

## Start commissions

Aurelune begins with a petitioner commission beside Mara Venn. That commission provides standing to ask the court for a Concord; it does not allocate offices or command Aurelune. The opening now emphasizes that the Argent Canopy recognizes speakers and relations, while the king's refusal leaves lawful nouns open around which banners can gather.

Seven Reeds begins with a temporary evidentiary commission. The Witnesses may request records, enter findings, cross-examine claims, and draft recommendations, but they cannot move a soldier, open a granary, redirect a ferry, or acquire a Reed by investigating it. Their authority remains advisory until the Throne adopts and issues the Mandate.

## Faction encounter rhythm

Aurelune's principal house encounters now ask what each banner can truthfully recognize:

- what Spring recognizes about Orison's agency;
- what the White Hart recognizes as answerable surety;
- what the Noon Spear recognizes as executable command;
- what the Red Rose recognizes as public satisfaction; and
- how the remaining banners leave their own material court objects and join the seasonal call.

Seven Reeds removes the repeated heading `Bargains available here`. Each consultation now owns an administrative output: an allocation Kazetada can separate, a Harvest arrangement, a Sword transition, a sacred procedure, a Judgment circuit, divided inspection powers, placed road duties, created waterworks offices, or provincial terms Reedwater witnesses can support.

## Bargain and allocation representation

Aurelune keeps bargains because bargains are its proper political material. The Petition Chamber now renders compatible bargains as shared seasonal light and incompatible bargains as broken lines before any seal touches the page. Its matrix shows where banner recognitions touch, and certification asks what one coalition can truthfully inhabit.

Seven Reeds treats concessions as inputs to government rather than sources of authority. Every operative choice must terminate in an office, present possessor, act of submission, ordinary executor, resource, field recipient, effective hour, acknowledgment route, and remedy. Great Clan support is evidence that implementation can work; it is never a vote authorizing the Emperor.

## Final audiences

The Crown Conclave asks whether the coalition survives proximity to the Sunseed. The relic casts the proposed powers into four seasonal shadows, exposing banners that still imagine different custodians, commands, or returns. Seals, declarations, and royal joinder matter because they express a coalition already made visible.

The Second Audience is dispatch, not ratification. The Emperor tests submissions, named recipients, burdens, first-day orders, fallbacks, and delayed receipt. The final movement is Imperial issuance: the question is not whether enough interests approve, but whether an authenticated order can leave the capital and become provincial government in Reedwater.

## Authoritative repair

The repair changes **thirty-three authoritative fields**:

- sixteen in Aurelune: the adventure explanation; three summaries; the opening court, nine faction bodies, Petition Chamber, and Crown Conclave bodies; and
- seventeen in Seven Reeds: the adventure explanation; three summaries; the opening court, ten consultation bodies, Chamber, and Second Audience bodies.

Most body changes are section-heading replacements. The only paragraph-level source rewrites clarify the Argent Canopy's recognition, the Petition Chamber's living text, the Sunseed's seasonal reading, the Witnesses' inability to command, the Chamber's field chain, and the Second Audience's dispatch function.

No title, identifier, opening view, clue, revelation, route, outcome, validation threshold, or journal event changed.
