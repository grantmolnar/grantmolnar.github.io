# Adventure Corpus Quality Record

## Purpose

This document records the completed editorial quality program for the bundled adventure corpus. The authoritative evidence is the current adventure JSON, maintained companion material, generated packets, demonstration journals, and executable corpus tests—not historical session notes.

## Completed quality passes

Each bundled adventure completed the same publication-oriented review sequence:

1. causal and narrative coherence;
2. lead density and route resilience;
3. first-pass encounter introductions;
4. sequence-level encounter-introduction revision;
5. source-level voice and compression; and
6. final cross-file voice and consistency review.

Additional adventure-specific repairs were completed where the ordinary sequence exposed a structural or presentation problem.

## Maintained evidence

For each adventure:

- `adventure.json` is the authoritative authored source;
- `generated/` is reproducible output;
- `play-state.example.json` and `archives/` are subordinate demonstration evidence;
- the adventure README identifies maintained table aids and design material; and
- corpus integration tests verify current topology, prose contracts, route resilience, reference coverage, and generated output.

The former per-adventure build plans, session records, editorial audits, and stress-test reports were consolidated and removed during beta maintenance. They are not compatibility-bearing artifacts and should not be recreated in the maintained corpus.
