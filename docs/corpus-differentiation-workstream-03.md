# Corpus Differentiation Workstream 3: Glass Saint Residual Separation

## Judgment

Workstream 3 is complete. *The Glass Saint* was already structurally and morally distinct from *The Siege of the Stone Lung* and *When the Swine Kneel*. Its residual overlap came from GM-facing scaffolding that described the Bell Chapel and Vale Manor through generic stations, approaches, zones, states, operations, and ledgers.

The repair preserves the complete five-session reconstruction. It changes diction and dramatic ownership, not routes, clues, authorities, ritual logic, or outcomes. The current adventure now sounds like copied grief becoming a public voice through pages, panes, vows, breath, doors, ropes, and peal.

## Comparative target

Workstream 2 assigned Stone Lung to contested military command and Swine to changing herd reports. Glass Saint therefore needed no new escalation engine. Its proper center was already present:

- a page survives by being copied, witnessed, carried, or read;
- a pane gives borrowed memory a face;
- the vow-bed supplies contradictory public language;
- a living Vale supplies voice without consent unless the knot is reversed;
- doors are both Olyra's historical act and the rite's recurring command image;
- breath can be lent under bounded consent; and
- a bell makes a private lie or coercive imperative impossible to contain.

The comparison asked whether each current instruction made those materials do the work, rather than merely relabeling a generic civic procedure.

## Residual diction

The remaining convergence was concentrated in three encounters and the overview that carried their state forward.

- The Bell Chapel was introduced as a laboratory with four stations and a three-part working set.
- The Grand Belfry asked the GM to fix a crew, divide work across levels, choose an operation, and remove completed work from the remaining procedure.
- Vale Manor was divided into six approaches, fixed positions, five apparatus zones, negotiation states, an exact procedure, resolution states, and five aftermath ledgers.

Every phrase was operationally intelligible. Together, however, they repeated the same abstract organization used by engineering and distributed-procedure finales elsewhere in the corpus.

## Authoritative repair

Exactly seven authoritative fields changed:

1. the adventure explanation;
2. Bell Chapel summary;
3. Bell Chapel content;
4. Grand Belfry summary;
5. Grand Belfry content;
6. Vale Manor summary; and
7. Vale Manor content.

The Bell Chapel now contains four speaking workings. Its counterkey answers the rite in three pieces, and every answer changes what the city will hear.

The Grand Belfry now begins by naming every hand on a rope. The tower stacks danger across floors, each choice defines what bronze may carry, and a sounded note cannot be unsounded.

Vale Manor now opens by reading the city already gathered at the glass. Its six routes are ways through doors; its household stands at the glass; five joined things give the saint a body; Edrin responds only to a hearing that already exists; and the three notes borrow body, voice, and reach in order. Every command opens one door and breaks another. What survives decides the ending, and the city keeps five reckonings after the bells.

| Route state | Pages, witnesses, and permissions arriving before the garden doors open |
| Technical intervention | Strip, cord, pitch, rope, knot, pane, and bell |

| Negotiation | Edrin recognizes a hearing only when it already has witnesses, copies, and custody |

The named mechanical states remain available where they help the GM adjudicate. They no longer determine the prose architecture.
