# The Black Bell Redoubt

**Role:** necessary  
**Tags:** command, battle, signals

Ren's black bell makes one reserve audible across the army and guards the broadest exit.

## Opening View

The first peal moves the battlefield. Grave cohorts leave their trenches; three living couriers ride for the Abbey, the canal, and Vossgard; a village road empties before anyone there can have heard the order. Legate Ossa Ren slides one reserve marker and watches Bell-Warden Vark drag the next command through the black mechanism with his chained arms. The second stroke is not yet sound. It is weight descending toward the Warrant.

## Encounter Material

## Situation

The Warrant reaches the redoubt between peals. One sequence turns programmed dead toward the Tithe road; another clears the Abbey road for a reserve column; living couriers wait for the longer order. Ren's formations are shifting before the tower comes into bow range.

The Bell coordinates only what still answers it. Reaching the mechanism, cutting couriers, forcing the reserve to deploy, and controlling the roads are separate acts. The Compact can silence the tower while an organized field command still moves toward Vossgard.

## Command

Legate Ossa Ren has three cohorts of grave infantry, living couriers, several reserve companies, and four vampire lieutenants in motion around the tower. She uses the simple peals to move the dead, keeps living officers on the courier roads, and retreats toward Vossgard if the reserve remains coherent.

Bell-Warden Vark is physically bound to the mechanism. He continues sounding programmed orders while fighting occurs around him and cannot surrender.

If available, Brigadier Holt can pin the reserve while a strike reaches the tower. Captain Vale can intercept couriers or exploit false orders.

## Ground

- An open bell tower visible across the lower plain.
- A low redoubt with mutually supporting trenches.
- Resonance ditches that amplify the bell and disorient troops crossing them.
- Courier stables and a protected command shelter.
- The bell road running directly to Vossgard.
- Side roads to the Tithe Villages and Red Abbey.

The tower can be reached quickly only by crossing exposed ground or entering the resonance ditches.

## Objective

Stop the redoubt from coordinating the field army and decide whether to destroy the network, seize it, or force the reserve into a decisive battle.

## Courses of action

### Tower raid

Cross the killing ground, disable Vark, and silence the bell while allied troops avoid a full engagement. Command breaks quickly; Ren may still withdraw an intact reserve to Vossgard.

### Seize and exploit the bell

Capture the tower and protect a signal crew long enough to send false timings. Reinforcements may arrive late, a garrison may expose itself, or dead troops may hold while the Compact moves. An incorrect sequence can also send formations toward civilians or Compact troops.

### Draw out the reserve

Threaten a road or feign a weak column, then strike Ren when she commits. Ren’s reserve may be drawn away from Vossgard at the cost of time and the formation used as bait.

### Direct assault

Send Holt's infantry into the redoubt while a strike destroys command points. This offers the best chance to reduce the reserve and the highest chance of straining the army.

### Courier war

Use Vale's riders to isolate the camp, intercept couriers, and leave Ren only the bell's simple commands. The reserve may be contained without a storm, but the mobile screen remains committed.

### Bypass under disruption

Silence or mask the bell, then move toward the Abbey, Sluice, or Vossgard before Ren restores command. Tempo survives; so does the reserve.

## Voss's response

Ren uses the reserve to punish an overextended assault, not to defend the bell at any cost. If the tower falls while her cohorts remain coherent, she withdraws by the bell road. Vark continues programmed signals even if they no longer fit the battle, creating predictable but dangerous movements.

At turn four, Ren is the officer who executes Voss's one primary reaction: she either recalls one surviving outer garrison or commits the reserve against the weakest Compact axis. Do not also grant every other position a full withdrawal on the same threshold. At turn six, she begins withdrawing the mobile field command into Vossgard under the general concentration order unless the bell road is blocked or the Redoubt is contained.

## Results

### Strong result

The bell is captured, Ren's reserve is destroyed or surrendered, and the bell road is held. The Compact can misdirect remaining dead formations, prevent coordinated relief, and close the central breakout route.

### Costly results

- The bell is destroyed, but Ren withdraws most of the reserve to Vossgard.
- The reserve is broken in open battle, but the Compact army becomes strained or spent.
- False signals achieve a major advantage and collateral harm the Warrant must own.
- Ren surrenders living troops after the dead are isolated, but demands protection from local reprisals.
- The redoubt is held only by assigning substantial forces that cannot join the final assault.

### Reduced

The bell network and field reserve can no longer conduct organized operations. The bell road is under Compact control.

### Contained

Compact forces keep the reserve inside the redoubt and block the bell road. This closes the route while tying down enough troops to matter at Vossgard.

### Bypassed

The reserve coordinates surviving positions, escorts withdrawals, attacks exposed columns, and provides Voss with his strongest conventional breakout force. An intact bell network also makes bypassed garrisons more dangerous in combination than they would be alone.

## What carries forward

- **Captured bell:** can issue limited false timings or deny dead formations coordinated action.
- **Order staff and couriers:** improve surrender outreach and identify where living troops are concentrated.
- **Field reserve destroyed:** prevents a major relief or breakout action.
- **Bell road held:** closes the central military exit.
- **Reserve withdrawn:** substantially strengthens the outer defense of Vossgard.

## Returning

A captured bell is useful enough to invite a counterattack. A contained redoubt may collapse after nearby garrisons surrender. The Warrant can also return to use the tower for campaign-wide orders, casualty collection, or the announcement of postwar terms.
