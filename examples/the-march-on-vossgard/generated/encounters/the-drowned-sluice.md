# The Drowned Sluice

**Role:** necessary  
**Tags:** engineering, water, assault

Marr's three lock marks decide whether black water carries supply, assault, escape, or ruin.

## Opening View

The chalk line vanishes under black water. Engineer Sila Marr marks it again one step higher while drowned infantry rise along the stair and a dry barge withdraws behind an iron grille. Far below, the Sunken Turn answers the Outer Chain, then the Underkeep Gate answers both. Marr looks toward the low roofs beyond the embankment. “One more change floods them.” The towpath doors open, and the chamber chooses a current.

## Encounter Material

## Situation

At the Outer Chain, the lowest flood mark has been scraped away and water rises whenever the Underkeep crew turns its wheel. Drowned infantry hold the ledges; chained bodies pull the lock mechanism; work at the Sunken Turn can be heard before the next chamber is visible. The route must be taken in order even when the fighting is not.

Preserved, the canal carries barges, dismantled engines, and assault troops beneath Vossgard. Denied in sequence, it closes the same road to Voss. Broken without control, it floods inhabited low ground and may leave the Underkeep Gate usable from inside.

## Command

Lock-Marshal Ors Keln spends drowned infantry, boat crews, and the dead chained into the machinery one chamber at a time to preserve the Underkeep Gate. He can abandon an outer mechanism without yielding the route behind it.

Engineer Sila Marr can accompany the Compact openly if the villages or their labor records are secure. She brings chamber order, load limits, and the names below each flood mark; she brings no safe passage. Without her, Merrow's Gatewrights must reconstruct the sequence under pressure and with greater risk.

A Gatewright company is present if committed. Without engineers, brute force can destroy or force the canal but rarely preserve it.

## Ground

- The Outer Chain chamber, accessible from the lower plain.
- A flooded towpath leading to the Sunken Turn.
- Maintenance ledges and vertical shafts exposed to sudden water movement.
- The Underkeep Gate beneath Vossgard.
- Escape barges stored in a dry side basin.
- Low inhabited ground that will flood if the outer gate is destroyed carelessly.

The three mechanisms create simultaneous demands: troops must hold cleared chambers while engineers work farther inward.

## Objective

Deny Voss the canal and decide whether to preserve it as a supply and assault route or destroy it as quickly as possible.

## Courses of action

### Sequential clearance

Escort engineers through all three locks and hold each chamber while they restore the mechanisms. The canal and flood control survive, at the cost of time and repeated counterattacks.

### Boat assault

Haul light boats overland or capture them from the side basin, then strike for the Sunken Turn and Underkeep Gate. Surprise shortens the operation; failure traps troops between controlled water levels.

### Divert the canal

Open the old spillway through the low fields and lower the main channel for infantry. The water protects the assault by entering farms and homes unless the population was evacuated.

### Break the Outer Chain

Destroy the outer gate and let the flood tear through the lock system. A controlled escape vanishes with the route; drowned troops, low ground, and the Ashen Gate road lie in the same current.

### Seize the barges and reverse the locks

Enter the side basin, capture the escape craft, and turn the lock sequence against Keln. Exact timing and troops at separated mechanisms can trap the canal force without ruining the route.

### Block rather than open

The Compact collapses the outer entrance or holds it with a small fort. This closes the exit at lower cost than full clearance but yields no protected supply route or lower assault path.

## Voss's response

Keln yields outer chambers to draw engineers inward, then changes water levels to isolate them. He sacrifices drowned troops readily but protects the Underkeep Gate and barges. Threaten uncontrolled destruction and Keln opens selected channels toward inhabited ground.

At turn four, Keln stages stores, selected prisoners, and crews for movement. If Voss chooses the Sluice for his primary reaction, one convoy passes through the lower canal; otherwise the preparation is visible without moving the entire reserve offscreen. At turn six, any surviving mobile crews man the escape barges under the general concentration order, unless the locks are controlled or the entrance is effectively contained.

## Results

### Strong result

All three mechanisms are controlled, the canal is navigable, the barges are captured, and the lower gate is in Compact hands. The army gains protected supply and an independent assault route while Voss loses the escape.

### Costly results

- The canal is destroyed and the exit closed, but low ground floods and the army gains no route.
- The canal opens, but engineer casualties or lost equipment strain the army.
- The lower gate is blocked, but Keln and the barges withdraw into Vossgard.
- The Compact preserves the route by diverting water through evacuated villages, creating a major reconstruction obligation.
- Securing the route requires a substantial garrison in the lock chambers.

### Reduced

Keln's canal force can no longer operate, and the Underkeep Gate is controlled or permanently blocked.

### Contained

The outer entrance is held or collapsed so that Voss cannot use it conventionally. The route is closed to both sides, and troops remain assigned to prevent reopening.

### Bypassed

The canal remains an enemy supply line and escape route. Drowned troops can threaten the Compact flank, and Voss can organize a barge breakout if the fortress battle turns against him.

## What carries forward

- **Open canal:** stabilizes army condition and provides a lower assault route.
- **Captured barges:** move troops, prisoners, and supplies under protection.
- **Closed Underkeep Gate:** removes the final military exit.
- **Flooded low ground:** worsens population posture and postwar recovery unless evacuation was handled well.
- **Surviving drowned force:** supports Voss at the lower gate.

## Returning

An open canal becomes a moving logistical front. Voss may counterattack a lock, the Compact may evacuate prisoners through it, and the Warrant must decide whether scarce engineers remain or march to Vossgard.
