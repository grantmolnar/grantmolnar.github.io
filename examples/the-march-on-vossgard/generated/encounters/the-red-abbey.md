# The Red Abbey

**Role:** necessary  
**Tags:** assault, prisoners, logistics

Rane's knights, Voln's prisoners, and Voss's blood reserve queue for the ridge road.

## Opening View

The Abbey’s retreat has a queue. Blood casks take the padded wagons; prisoners wait in the lower yard; eighteen vampire knights claim the ridge road first. Quartermaster Nera Voln stands at the gate with a custody ledger and an unraised strip of white cloth. When a prisoner falls, she halts one cart. Dame Halwen Rane does not. “Blood before baggage,” she orders, and the portcullis begins to rise.

## Encounter Material

## Situation

The ridge road is already moving. Blood casks roll toward Vossgard's western postern, prisoners wait in wagon lines below, and Rane's knights saddle beneath the ruined nave. Voln's crews can move one stream quickly or divide their guards and risk all three.

Voss needs the blood, prisoners, and mounted force to leave before the Compact closes the ridge. The Warrant must choose which asset to stop, which people to protect, and whether the cloister becomes a held road, surrendered depot, or consecrated ground.

## Command

**Dame Halwen Rane** commands eighteen vampire knights, mounted retainers, and the dead assigned to the ridge road. She will sortie aggressively if she believes she can destroy the Warrant or a Gatewright detachment, but otherwise preserves her knights for Vossgard.

**Quartermaster Nera Voln** commands the living garrison, prison guards, and blood-handling crews. She is a collaborator whose authority depends on Voss, but she wants her soldiers to survive. She may surrender the outer abbey in exchange for safe conduct and a formal hearing rather than summary execution.

**Chaplain Iven Sol** accompanies the Compact if Rusk committed consecrated support or the Warrant requests him. He can reclaim the cloister yard as protected ground if held long enough, but cannot assault the vaults alone.

## Ground

- Outer wall and gate controlling the lower road.
- Cloister yard capable of being reconsecrated.
- Prison yard separated from the blood vault by an internal wall.
- Stable court and ridge gate for the vampire knights.
- Blood vault with controlled drainage channels and firebreaks.
- Ridge road leading directly to a postern of Vossgard.

A small strike team cannot hold the prison, vault, and ridge gate at once without allied support.

## Objective

Deny Voss the blood and vampire reserves while deciding how much strength the prisoners receive.

## Courses of action

### Storm before deployment

Lead an immediate breach while Compact troops attack the walls. Speed may catch the knights in the stable court; heavy assault gives Voln little reason to preserve prisoners or infrastructure.

### Cut the ridge and force a sortie

Send a mobile detachment behind the abbey while the main force demonstrates below. Rane must fight outside the walls or abandon the depot; Voln keeps the prison and vault during the battle.

### Negotiate the living garrison out

Offer Voln safe conduct, lawful custody, and protection from Rane. Her living garrison can open the outer gate and prison yard while Compact troops fight the knights. The unpopular terms commit the Compact to hearings instead of immediate punishment.

### Strike the vault and prison separately

Split the effort: one team disables blood distribution while another secures prisoners. Both objectives survive, and Rane gains a clean ridge withdrawal unless another force blocks it.

### Destroy the depot

Fire, sunlight charges, or the vault drains can ruin the blood reserve quickly. An uncontrolled destruction risks prisoners, useful medical stores, and the abbey structure. It may still be the correct choice under severe time pressure.

### Contain the ridge

The Compact fixes the abbey with infantry and blocks the ridge road without taking the walls. Enough troops close the exit and prevent reinforcement while prisoners and blood remain in enemy hands.

## Voss's response

Rane protects the knights and ridge road first, the blood reserve second, and the living garrison last. Voln protects her soldiers and records first, then bargains with whatever leverage remains. A public record of honored surrender terms brings Voln forward sooner.

At turn four, Rane prepares a blood-and-prisoner convoy. If Voss chooses the Abbey for his primary reaction, that convoy moves and the knights cover it; otherwise the loading remains visible and can still be interrupted. At turn six, surviving mobile knights withdraw under the general concentration order unless the ridge is blocked or the Abbey is contained.

## Results

### Strong result

The vampire knights are destroyed or captured, prisoners survive, the blood reserve is denied, and the Compact holds the ridge road. The cloister yard becomes a consecrated staging area for the final assault.

### Costly results

- The blood reserve is destroyed, but prisoners or valuable supplies are lost.
- The prisoners are rescued and the abbey taken, but Rane escapes with the knights.
- The knights are destroyed in a sortie, but Voln burns the vault and withdraws with hostages.
- Voln surrenders the position under broad terms that preserve lives but create a difficult postwar justice question.
- The Compact holds the abbey only by assigning a large garrison, worsening its strength at Vossgard.

### Reduced

The abbey can no longer supply organized vampire operations, and the ridge road is under Compact control. Surviving knights or guards no longer form an organized command.

### Contained

A sufficient Compact force blocks the ridge road and fixes the garrison. The exit is closed for the finale, but the containing troops cannot join the assault and the blood reserve may still support Voss internally.

### Bypassed

Rane, the blood reserve, and the ridge road remain available. Vampire knights reinforce Vossgard, wounded vampires can recover more readily, and Voss has a conventional northwestern breakout route. The Warrant can still attack through another approach.

## What carries forward

- **Blood reserve denied:** vampire troops cannot repeatedly recover during the finale.
- **Consecrated cloister:** safe staging and casualty point near Vossgard.
- **Rescued prisoners:** witnesses, guides, and a visible fulfillment of Compact promises.
- **Captured ridge road:** closes one military exit.
- **Escaped vampire knights:** strengthen Vossgard or raid an exposed rear.
- **Surrender terms for Voln:** become precedent for other living officers.

## Returning

A captured abbey can be counterattacked by surviving knights or used as a hospital and staging base. A contained abbey may surrender after the Black Bell falls. Prisoners rescued here identify family hostages, compromised officers, and custody claims that shape the stand-down and settlement at Vossgard.
