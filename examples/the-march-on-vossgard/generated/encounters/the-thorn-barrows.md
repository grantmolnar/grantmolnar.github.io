# The Thorn Barrows

**Role:** necessary  
**Tags:** cavalry, mobility, battlefield

Morcant's remounts and covered lanes turn every unsupported movement into prey.

## Opening View

One horse returns without its rider. The saddle is warm, the reins cut cleanly, and an arrow pins the Compact dispatch to the saddle roll. Between the cairns, handlers change mounts beneath iron thorns while covered lanes take messengers toward the Bell and the canal. No road marks the central gap. Then Morcant’s horn answers itself from two barrows, and every sealed stone gives back a different number of hooves.

## Encounter Material

## Situation

Vale's scouts find riderless horses behind three sealed mounds and tracks entering lanes that do not return. Morcant has split remounts among the barrows, posted wight outriders on the visible approaches, and left false fires for slow columns.

The eastern road is the March's fastest light route. It will not carry the siege train, but it moves scouts, couriers, infantry, and mounted reserves between fronts. Leaving it to Morcant makes every later change of plan pay in escorts, lost messages, or a raid on the rear.

## Command

Morcant begins with roughly ninety night riders, several wight outriders, and the shadow-fed coursers kept within the barrows. She spends a mound, remount string, or false objective to preserve the mounted force, then attacks support rather than the strongest line.

Vale brings the Dawn Lancers if the axis received adequate support. She can contest Morcant in the open, intercept a withdrawal, or screen another column, but cannot clear every barrow and watch every road at once.

The dead interred in the oldest mounds remain inert until fighting or deliberate demolition breaks their seals. Once opened, a mound becomes an environmental military hazard to both armies.

## Ground

- Three remount barrows linked by covered lanes.
- Thorn gaps that permit cavalry passage but constrain infantry formations.
- Elevated cairn crowns with long sight lines.
- Old seals that can be collapsed to block a lane at the risk of releasing uncontrolled dead.
- A western route back to the causeway and southeastern routes toward the Black Bell and Drowned Sluice.

## Objective

Displace or fix the night riders long enough to move Compact forces across the barrows and secure lateral freedom for the rest of the campaign.

## Courses of action

### Strike the remount barrows

Attack mounts, fodder, and handlers instead of chasing every rider. Morcant must fight for mobility or withdraw on foot, placing volatile shadow creatures among sealed graves and allied troops.

### Hunt Morcant

Let Vale screen the crossings while a strike tracks or challenges Morcant. Her death or capture breaks coordinated raiding; every feigned retreat forces a choice between pursuit and the exposed column.

### Hold the central gap

Fortify one decisive lane while Compact infantry crosses. The immediate movement succeeds under controlled risk, and Morcant retains most of her riders for another axis.

### Force the riders west or south

Conduct a broad sweep with Vale and drive Morcant toward the Causeway, Black Bell, or Sluice. A prepared receiving force turns two operations into one battle; a weak one is overwhelmed.

### Collapse selected barrows

Seal lanes with demolition or radiant fire. Containment denies movement to both armies; breaking old seals may release uncontrolled dead and permanently narrows Compact mobility.

### Bypass at speed

Cross with a small column by night or magical concealment while the riders raid elsewhere. The march keeps its tempo and leaves Morcant free to strike the Gate, villages, or supply road.

## Voss's response

Morcant attacks support rather than strength. Commit to one barrow and she raids another. Pursue her personally and she sends riders toward exposed wagons or civilians. If her mounts are threatened, she concentrates and accepts battle.

On march turn two, an unresolved rider force makes its first major raid. The target is whichever of the Gate, a supply column, or an exposed village best reflects the opening allocation. Announce later raids early enough for the Warrant to answer them; never apply them as arbitrary losses.

## Results

### Strong result

Morcant is killed or captured, the remount system is seized, and the barrow lanes remain usable. The Compact gains operational mobility and can screen later movements or relief attempts.

### Costly results

- The route opens while Morcant escapes with most riders. The march gains tempo without ending the raid threat.
- The mounts are destroyed through fire or unstable magic. The rider force is crippled, but allied casualties or released barrow dead strain the army.
- Morcant is driven into another encounter, creating an immediate combined battle there.
- The Compact secures the route by permanently collapsing lanes. Voss loses mobility, but so does the invading army.

### Reduced

Morcant's command and remount system can no longer operate as an organized mobile formation. Individual riders may survive.

### Contained

Vale and the Dawn Lancers hold the principal gaps and keep Morcant from leaving in strength. The Compact gains a secure flank, but its cavalry remains tied down and cannot screen the final siege.

### Bypassed

The night riders remain fully operational. They raid supply, punish lateral movement, escort withdrawing garrisons, and may appear as relief at Vossgard. Their continued existence does not prevent a rapid assault, but it makes every unsupported rear objective more expensive.

## What carries forward

- **Captured shadow mounts:** allow selected troops to redeploy rapidly in the Shadowfell.
- **Dawn Lancers freed:** can screen an open Vossgard exit or intercept relief.
- **Collapsed lanes:** limit both enemy and allied movement.
- **Surviving night riders:** generate visible raids and reinforce later positions.
- **Morcant captured:** provides a high-value prisoner for exchange or surrender negotiations and may divide surviving riders over whether to recover or abandon her.

## Returning

A contained barrow front may need relief after Morcant gathers strength. A reduced barrow line becomes the quickest way to respond to a raid or shift between first- and second-belt encounters. Released dead must be cleared before local traffic can resume.
