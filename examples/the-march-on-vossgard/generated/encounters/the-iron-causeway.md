# The Iron Causeway

**Role:** necessary  
**Tags:** assault, engineering, logistics

Taal offers a living surrender above Krail's charged bridge and waiting grave cohort.

## Opening View

Captain Arven Taal raises a white engineer’s glove above the east gatehouse. Beneath it hangs a roster of his soldiers’ families in Vossgard. He points once to the demolition galleries. There, Krail’s dead feed powder into numbered chambers while six bolt throwers wait for the siege train to commit. The span is still whole; the surrender is not. A range bolt lands behind the lead wagon, closing the road without yet breaking it.

## Encounter Material

## Situation

The lead ram stops short of the west gate while Krail's bolts walk down the span. Grave infantry hold the parapets; Taal's living engineers stand below beside charges already run into the bridge. The lifting works and shadow-plain entrances divide the position. Winning the visible road does not secure the galleries.

Voss's order is simple: let the siege train commit to the road, break the span, and leave the Compact's heaviest weapons stranded under fixed fire. Captain Arven Taal wants his living soldiers and engineers to survive the order. Standard-Bearer Krail intends to carry it out even if they do not.

## Command

At the first parley, Captain Arven Taal hangs a white engineer's scarf from the west gate while his two living companies keep their hands on the lifting works and charges. His soldiers' families have been moved into Vossgard. He will surrender only if the terms protect those families and put Compact troops between his engineers and the grave infantry during the handover.

Standard-Bearer Krail answers by turning six fixed bolt throwers toward the span and sending dead cadres into the galleries. He follows Voss's delay order literally and will destroy the bridge even if living troops remain on it.

If the axis received adequate support, Colonel Merrow and Gatewright assault crews are present. Otherwise only a small engineer section and whatever infantry the Warrant redirected are available.

## Ground

- A narrow raised road exposed to missile fire.
- West and east gatehouses controlling the span.
- Demolition galleries accessible from the gatehouses or from the shadow plain beneath.
- A central lifting mechanism that can isolate either half of the bridge.
- The plain below, which slows living troops and physically grasps unattended equipment.

## Objective

Open the road for the Compact and decide whether preserving the causeway is worth the time and casualties required.

## Courses of action

### Direct breach

Send Holt's infantry or another assault force against the east gatehouse and break the parapet defense. Speed opens the span; a second team must still reach the galleries before Krail fires the charges.

### Under-span infiltration

Cross the shadow plain with a small Gatewright team, enter the demolition galleries, and seize or cut the firing system while the main column fixes the defenders above. The bridge may survive with fewer mass casualties, but the dead hold the confined ground.

### Surrender under fire

Offer Taal terms for the living garrison and their families, then protect the handover from Krail. Hold the firing levers, separate living troops from dead cadres, or bind the Compact publicly to recover named fortress households.

### Deliberate destruction

Clear the east bank and turn the defenders' charges or Gatewright explosives against the span. Demolition reduces the position quickly and denies the bridge to both armies until a major reconstruction restores it.

### Bypass and contain

Move through the villages or barrows while a battalion fixes the causeway garrison. Tempo and a later surrender remain available only if the containing force can hold the east gate; otherwise the garrison can raid the Gate road or demolish the span when the siege train approaches.

## Voss's response

Taal tries to preserve his soldiers until he believes the Compact will actually protect them. Krail treats hesitation as time to prepare a cleaner demolition. Negotiation without physical control of the galleries gives Krail the handover as his firing moment.

At turn four, Taal receives a withdrawal-and-demolition packet. If Voss chooses the Causeway for his primary turn-four reaction, the useful engineers begin withdrawing and the bridge is prepared for immediate destruction; otherwise the preparations remain visible and answerable. At turn six, any mobile living engineers withdraw under the general concentration order. A contained garrison cannot leave without first breaking the battalion holding the east gate, while Krail's dead remain with the charges ready.

## Results

### Strong result

The causeway is intact, Krail's cohort is destroyed, and Taal's living garrison surrenders. The heavy column and bulk supply can advance. The surrendered engineers can assist at the Drowned Sluice or Vossgard.

### Costly results

- The causeway is intact, but the assault heavily strains the army.
- The causeway is destroyed after the position is taken. The western bank is secure, but the siege train cannot pass without a later reconstruction operation.
- Taal escapes with his engineers after disabling the charges. The route opens, but Voss gains skilled fortress engineers.
- Broad parole wins the road quickly and leaves local victims and Compact officers to contest the settlement later.

### Reduced

The grave cohort is destroyed or dispersed, the living garrison no longer fights, and the causeway cannot support organized enemy action. The road may be intact or destroyed.

### Contained

A Compact battalion holds the east gate and fixes the garrison. The position cannot raid freely, but the battalion is unavailable elsewhere and the heavy train cannot pass.

### Bypassed

Taal and Krail retain the bridge complex. On a later march turn they can raid a convoy, force the siege train to halt, or send engineers and grave infantry to Vossgard. At the finale, an uncontained causeway requires a rear guard and worsens the army's ability to sustain simultaneous objectives.

## What carries forward

- **Intact heavy route:** the siege train can reach Vossgard.
- **Surrendered engineers:** improve later engineering operations and support a lawful settlement.
- **Destroyed bridge:** closes the enemy position but removes the Compact's heavy route.
- **Surviving grave cohort:** becomes a rear-area or fortress reinforcement.
- **Family guarantee:** binds the Warrant to protect specified households in Vossgard.

## Returning

A contained causeway can later surrender, be stormed, or be abandoned by its living garrison. A destroyed causeway can be rebuilt at the cost of a march turn and a major Gatewright commitment. A previously intact causeway may become the target of a Voss counterattack if the heavy train is using it.
