# The Ash Warrant

## Summary

The bounded cross-command commission that lets its bearers redirect committed forces, receive surrenders, and bind field promises only when authority, support, records, and later review are named.

## Reference Material

## Blackened plate between separated commands

The Ash Warrant is a portable field commission issued by Marshal Aveline Rusk for an army whose columns cannot wait on ordinary written orders. Its bearers may cross command boundaries, redirect forces already placed within reach, receive military surrender, record lawful requisition, grant temporary parole pending hearing, and make local agreements in the Dawn Compact's name. The blackened field plate makes that authority visible; it does not make it unlimited.

## Unit, enforcing hand, matériel, end, review

A Warrant order or promise identifies the people or unit affected, the officer who must enforce it, the troops, stores, transport, custody, or engineering capacity that support it, the record that preserves it, and the review that ends or confirms it. Couriers return duplicate terms to Rusk's obligation ledger. Emergency words may begin action, but lasting protection depends on an accountable hand and a route by which the promise can be checked.

## No blank line supplies a missing formation

The Warrant cannot create absent formations, unassigned guards, missing wagons, engineers, food, lawful jurisdiction, or a safe route. It cannot turn a bypass into a victory, a receipt into delivery, a parole into acquittal, or a provisional military arrangement into permanent government. Current allocations, bargains, requisitions, casualties, surrenders, custody, routes, and settlements belong to campaign state and play.
