# The Red Abbey

## Summary

The desecrated cloister turned blood depot, prison compound, cavalry mustering ground, and northwestern military road into Vossgard.

## Reference Material

## Ridge postern, prison yard, vault, nave

The Red Abbey crowns a ridge northwest of Vossgard. Its outer wall and lower gate enclose a ruined nave used as a stable court, a prison yard, a warded blood vault, and a cloister yard that can be reclaimed as consecrated ground. The ridge gate opens onto a direct road to a postern in Vossgard's western wall.

## Blood, prisoners, knights on one road

The Abbey joins preserved blood, prisoners selected for feeding, and mounted vampire reserves within one defensive complex. Internal walls separate the prison from the vault, controlled channels and firebreaks govern the blood stores, and the ridge road gives the knights a route independent of the lower plain. A small force cannot simultaneously secure the prisoners, vault, cloister, stable court, and road without outside support.

## Consecrate only held ground

The cloister can become protected ground and a casualty station only through actual rites, time, and protection.
