# The Black Bell

**Reference ID:** `cab5eeb5-ca45-443e-a7f7-2425c38b1ea4`  
**Kind:** Object  
**Aliases:** Black Bell, the Bell, the field bell  
**Tags:** object, signal system, field command, couriers

## Summary

The iron signal instrument whose peals, programmed sequences, couriers, and roads let Voss coordinate dead formations and living officers across the lower March.

## Reference Material

## Peal, courier, receiver, road

The Black Bell hangs in an open tower above a redoubt, resonance ditches, courier stables, and roads to Vossgard, the Tithe Villages, and the Red Abbey. Its voice carries farther through the Shadowfell than flags, horns, or shouted command. Simple peals move programmed dead; more intricate sequences call reserves, clear roads, coordinate withdrawals, and tell living officers which prepared order is active.

## Programmed dead beneath living command

The Bell works as a system rather than a magical verdict. A warden draws the sequence through the mechanism, couriers and officers interpret orders the dead cannot, roads carry the response, and subordinate commands decide what coherent force can obey. A captured sequence may misdirect troops only where receivers recognize it and where the signal fits the order they are prepared to execute.

## Silence, seizure, isolation, contradiction

The Bell may be destroyed, disabled, seized, isolated from couriers, contradicted, or left sounding after its command network has failed. This record establishes none of those outcomes. It does not reveal current peals, reserve strength, courier movement, authenticated false orders, tower control, road control, or whether any formation reached Vossgard.
