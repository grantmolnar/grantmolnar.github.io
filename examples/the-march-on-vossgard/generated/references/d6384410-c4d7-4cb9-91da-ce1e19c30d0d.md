## Summary

The vampire ruler of the Gloam March, commanding its living levies, dead formations, cavalry, blood logistics, and fortress system through organized roads and subordinate commands.

## Reference Material

## Seventy-three years of roads under one command

Othmar Voss rules the Gloam March from Vossgard. Living subjects provide grain, labor, soldiers, and scheduled blood; dead formations hold roads and prepared positions; vampire officers command mobile striking forces. His rule is military administration as much as terror, joining levy households, blood allotments, couriers, bells, fortifications, and the three approaches to his fortress.

## Withdraw the force that can still obey

Voss trades ground for time and measures survival in coherent formations, usable exits, prisoners, stores, and blood. He prefers a withdrawal or breakout that preserves command over a glorious death at an outer position. His subordinates receive bounded delay, removal, reserve, and concentration duties, and their ability to answer later orders depends on what their encounters actually preserve.

## Gate, bell, canal, levy, blood

Voss can issue orders through his existing officers, roads, signal systems, and fortress. He cannot silently restore a reduced formation, move a contained command without breaking its line, or gain an unannounced route merely because time advances. His current location, surviving forces, open exits, blood reserve, prisoners, surrender, flight, and final disposition remain campaign state and play.
