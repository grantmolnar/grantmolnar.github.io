## Summary

The Gloam March fortress whose known walls, three military exits, living garrison, prisoner yards, and inner keep gather the consequences of every outer operation.

## Reference Material

## Bell gate, ridge postern, Underkeep Gate

Vossgard consists of an outer wall, the Bell gate, the abbey ridge postern, the Underkeep Gate, a garrison court, prisoner yards, and an inner keep above Othmar Voss's coffin vault. The ridge road, bell road, and military canal are known military exits rather than hidden revelations. Border surveys, deserters, and captured planning establish this shape before the final assault.

## Every outer position arrives at the wall

The Iron Causeway determines whether heavy engines and bulk supply can approach. The Tithe Villages connect guides, food, levies, hostages, and surrender channels. The Thorn Barrows govern mobile relief and lateral movement. The Red Abbey joins blood, prisoners, cavalry, and a western postern. The Black Bell coordinates the broad road and field reserve. The Drowned Sluice carries supply, assault, or organized escape beneath the eastern terraces.

## Breach, surrender, prisoners, pursuit, night command

Taking a gate, killing a vampire, receiving a living garrison, guarding prisoners, closing exits, pursuing flight, and establishing provisional administration are separate acts. This record does not state which roads are open, what forces survive, who commands or surrenders, where prisoners or civilians are held, whether Voss is present, or what government follows the battle.
