# The Drowned Sluice

**Reference ID:** `e510af2a-eb44-4055-945b-b09c79985a47`

**Aliases:** Drowned Sluice, the Sluice, the military canal

## Summary

The three-lock military canal beneath Vossgard's eastern terraces, capable of carrying barges and engines or flooding inhabited low ground when operated without sequence and control.

## Reference Material

## Outer Chain, Sunken Turn, Underkeep Gate

The old military canal reaches Vossgard through the Outer Chain, the Sunken Turn, and the Underkeep Gate. Flooded towpaths, maintenance ledges, vertical shafts, chained gates, and a dry side basin connect the chambers. The route was built to move barges, stores, broken-down engines, and troops below the fortress approaches rather than across the exposed plain.

## Chamber order against flood marks

Each mechanism changes pressure and level elsewhere. Chamber order, load limits, flood marks, gate timing, and crews determine whether the canal carries supply or becomes a weapon. Low inhabited ground lies outside the embankment; careless destruction can reach homes and fields that are not part of the fortification. Village labor records and canal knowledge preserve names beneath the marks even when military maps do not.

## Crew, protection, sequence, load

The canal requires sequence, engineers, protected work space, and control of chambers already passed.
