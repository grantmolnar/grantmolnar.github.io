# Captain Jessa Vale

**Reference ID:** `bdc6dade-caff-49e9-8c4d-1f6b5eca9469`

**Aliases:** Jessa Vale, Vale, Captain Vale  
**Tags:** Dawn Compact, Dawn Lancers, reconnaissance, mobile response

## Summary

The Dawn Lancer captain whose mobile command scouts, screens, intercepts, pursues, and answers exposed roads while remaining too small to hold every route at once.

## Reference Material

## Distance written in remounts and hours

Jessa Vale commands the Dawn Lancers, the Compact's fast reconnaissance and response formation. Her riders find movement before it reaches a supply road, screen a march, intercept couriers, test an apparent gap, and turn verified information into time for a slower command. Vale reads distance through remounts, patrol intervals, exposed flanks, and which road still lacks a watcher.

## Screen, intercept, report, redeploy

The Lancers can contest hostile cavalry, isolate couriers, exploit a false signal, pursue a broken command, or screen relief while another formation attacks. They cannot simultaneously clear every hidden position, hold a fixed line, escort every convoy, and retain a mobile reserve. A screen is real only while riders, remounts, and a named route remain assigned to it.

## A courier mark does not close the road

Vale can provide reconnaissance, warning, interception, and bounded mobile support. Her scouts do not reduce a position offscreen or establish discoveries that play has not earned. Current patrols, remount condition, casualties, route coverage, pursuit, and availability remain campaign state.
