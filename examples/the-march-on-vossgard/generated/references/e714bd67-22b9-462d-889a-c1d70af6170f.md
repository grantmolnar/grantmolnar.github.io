# Marshal Aveline Rusk

**Reference ID:** `e714bd67-22b9-462d-889a-c1d70af6170f`  
**Kind:** Person  
**Aliases:** Aveline Rusk, Rusk  
**Tags:** Dawn Compact, campaign command, Ash Warrant, obligations

## Summary

The Dawn Compact marshal who commands the Ashen Gate campaign, allocates scarce formations, and keeps the obligation and review boundaries behind the Ash Warrant.

## Reference Material

## Gate ledger: road, beds, wagons, reserve

Aveline Rusk commands the Dawn Compact's bridgehead and the campaign launched through the Ashen Gate. She reads an offensive in formations, hospital capacity, wagon room, reserves, and the one road by which troops, prisoners, civilians, and promises must return to daylight. Her office joins military command to responsibility for what the army can actually sustain.

## Field plate returned with duplicate terms

Rusk issues the Ash Warrant as a bounded cross-command commission. Its bearers may redirect committed forces, receive battlefield surrenders, record requisitions, grant temporary parole pending hearing, and make enforceable local agreements when ordinary orders cannot arrive. Returning couriers carry duplicate terms to her obligation ledger so protection, custody, supply, and later review survive the distance between columns.

## Allocate only named strength

Rusk can allocate available formations, redirect a freed reserve, demand corrected reports, and review provisional military and civil arrangements. She cannot create troops, transport, custody, or stores that were never assigned; erase command responsibility; grant permanent sovereignty; or convert an unsupported promise into an enforceable one. Current allocations, losses, obligations, routes, and settlement orders belong to campaign state and play.
