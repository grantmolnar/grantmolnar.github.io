# The Iron Causeway

**Reference ID:** `ed795697-5230-4578-8ea0-e625d05b61ff`  
**Kind:** Place  
**Aliases:** Iron Causeway, the Causeway, the heavy road  
**Tags:** place, bridge, heavy route, demolition

## Summary

The black-stone bridge complex whose span, gatehouses, lifting works, and demolition galleries govern whether heavy matériel can move toward Vossgard.

## Reference Material

## West gate, east gate, span, galleries

The Iron Causeway carries the campaign's heavy road across a plain where shadow clutches at wheels, animals, and unattended equipment. The bridge complex consists of a raised black-stone span, narrow parapets, west and east gatehouses, and a central lifting mechanism that can isolate either half. Fixed firing positions dominate troops committed to the road.

## Visible road above, demolition order below

Demolition galleries run below the span and can be reached from the gatehouses or from the shadow plain. The position was built to separate the visible fight from the engineering decision: a force may hold the parapets while another crew owns the charges, the lifting works, or the approach by which repairs must be made. Living engineers and dead cadres can therefore receive incompatible orders inside one fortification.

## Record the load path that remains

A heavy route remains useful only when the span, gates, lifting mechanism, galleries, approaches, crews, and protection required for movement are accounted for. This record does not establish current damage, control, surrender, family guarantees, demolition readiness, engineer availability, or whether the siege train crossed. Those are campaign-state facts.
