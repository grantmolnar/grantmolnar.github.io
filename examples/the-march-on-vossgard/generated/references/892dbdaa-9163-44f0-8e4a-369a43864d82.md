# Provost Liora Dane

**Reference ID:** `892dbdaa-9163-44f0-8e4a-369a43864d82`  
**Kind:** Person  
**Aliases:** Liora Dane, Dane, Provost Dane  
**Tags:** Dawn Compact, civil affairs, field police, surrender records

## Summary

The Compact provost who joins civil affairs to field-police authority and makes surrender, requisition, protection, custody, abuse, and later review administratively real.

## Reference Material

## Name the protected people and holding officer

Liora Dane leads the campaign's civil-affairs officers, interpreters, and field police. Her office records who surrendered, what was requisitioned, which people or property received protection, what force enforces that promise, who holds custody, and where a complaint or hearing must go. She treats a roster, receipt, witness, guard detail, and review date as parts of the same obligation.

## Guards, transport, record, hearing

Dane can authenticate surrender records, organize temporary parole and custody pending hearing, document lawful requisition, separate protected people from active combatants, and investigate Compact abuse. She can help living authorities understand what an army has actually promised. A white cloth, verbal assurance, or officer's sympathy does not itself supply guards, transport, food, jurisdiction, or safe passage.

## Custody preserves the question

Dane's office preserves responsibility rather than erasing it. She cannot grant blanket immunity, decide permanent sovereignty, promise resources the army has not committed, or turn temporary military custody into final judgment. Current terms, prisoners, requisitions, accusations, guards, hearings, and postwar arrangements remain campaign state and play.
