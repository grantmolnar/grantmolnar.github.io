# Reference Index: The March on Vossgard

## People

- [**Marshal Aveline Rusk**](e714bd67-22b9-462d-889a-c1d70af6170f.md) — The Dawn Compact marshal who commands the Ashen Gate campaign, allocates scarce formations, and keeps the obligation and review boundaries behind the Ash Warrant. Aliases: Aveline Rusk, Rusk.
- [**Brigadier Tamsin Holt**](b9b91d52-59d8-41ba-b73e-05ab1d3e15c4.md) — The Grey Banner brigadier who turns available infantry into bounded assault, containment, and surrendered-ground assignments without pretending one formation can hold every road. Aliases: Tamsin Holt, Holt, Brigadier Holt.
- [**Colonel Dain Merrow**](1fe01544-1445-48cb-b8af-c8f2d630a7cf.md) — The Gatewright colonel responsible for siege infrastructure, demolition, heavy movement, and the scarce engineering capacity that determines what remains usable after seizure. Aliases: Dain Merrow, Merrow, Colonel Merrow.
- [**Captain Jessa Vale**](bdc6dade-caff-49e9-8c4d-1f6b5eca9469.md) — The Dawn Lancer captain whose mobile command scouts, screens, intercepts, pursues, and answers exposed roads while remaining too small to hold every route at once. Aliases: Jessa Vale, Vale, Captain Vale.
- [**Provost Liora Dane**](892dbdaa-9163-44f0-8e4a-369a43864d82.md) — The Compact provost who joins civil affairs to field-police authority and makes surrender, requisition, protection, custody, abuse, and later review administratively real. Aliases: Liora Dane, Dane, Provost Dane.
- [**Count Othmar Voss**](d6384410-c4d7-4cb9-91da-ce1e19c30d0d.md) — The vampire ruler of the Gloam March, commanding its living levies, dead formations, cavalry, blood logistics, and fortress system through organized roads and subordinate commands. Aliases: Othmar Voss, Voss, Count Voss.
- [**Dame Kasia Morcant**](2cfd0b01-d287-4d40-b785-ea11bc1aa001.md) — The Gloam March's mobile vampire commander, preserving night riders, remounts, and lateral freedom so an unresolved gap can become the next battlefield. Aliases: Kasia Morcant, Morcant, Dame Morcant.

## Places

- [**The Ashen Gate**](545ffe74-1efc-4b32-bdf6-8ec831f34e51.md) — The iron-pinned daylight passage and single bridgehead road through which the Compact must move troops, supply, prisoners, civilians, casualties, and every promise it means to sustain. Aliases: Ashen Gate, the Gate, the bridgehead.
- [**The Iron Causeway**](ed795697-5230-4578-8ea0-e625d05b61ff.md) — The black-stone bridge complex whose span, gatehouses, lifting works, and demolition galleries govern whether heavy matériel can move toward Vossgard. Aliases: Iron Causeway, the Causeway, the heavy road.
- [**The Tithe Villages**](c7b208a6-4626-40b4-8321-54b840918905.md) — Garren, Orra, and Lume: the March's broad grain ground, three distinct councils, and the households from which Voss takes labor, levy service, blood, and hostages. Aliases: Tithe Villages, Garren, Orra, and Lume, the three villages.
- [**The Red Abbey**](7247ca22-2063-4332-ba40-0bf7face6275.md) — The desecrated cloister turned blood depot, prison compound, cavalry mustering ground, and northwestern military road into Vossgard. Aliases: Red Abbey, the Abbey, the ridge depot.
- [**The Drowned Sluice**](e510af2a-eb44-4055-945b-b09c79985a47.md) — The three-lock military canal beneath Vossgard's eastern terraces, capable of carrying barges and engines or flooding inhabited low ground when operated without sequence and control. Aliases: Drowned Sluice, the Sluice, the military canal.
- [**Vossgard**](613c3c19-2fd2-4de7-93ed-ba0e279e1777.md) — The Gloam March fortress whose known walls, three military exits, living garrison, prisoner yards, and inner keep gather the consequences of every outer operation. Aliases: the fortress, Voss's fortress.

## Objects

- [**The Ash Warrant**](be727f0b-3d2e-44b8-9309-896dcc57726c.md) — The bounded cross-command commission that lets its bearers redirect committed forces, receive surrenders, and bind field promises only when authority, support, records, and later review are named. Aliases: Ash Warrant, the Warrant, blackened field plate.
- [**The Black Bell**](cab5eeb5-ca45-443e-a7f7-2425c38b1ea4.md) — The iron signal instrument whose peals, programmed sequences, couriers, and roads let Voss coordinate dead formations and living officers across the lower March. Aliases: Black Bell, the Bell, the field bell.
