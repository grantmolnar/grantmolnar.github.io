# Colonel Dain Merrow

**Reference ID:** `1fe01544-1445-48cb-b8af-c8f2d630a7cf`  
**Kind:** Person  
**Aliases:** Dain Merrow, Merrow, Colonel Merrow  
**Tags:** Dawn Compact, Gatewrights, engineering, siege train

## Summary

The Gatewright colonel responsible for siege infrastructure, demolition, heavy movement, and the scarce engineering capacity that determines what remains usable after seizure.

## Reference Material

## Span, gallery, lock, pylon, load

Dain Merrow commands the Gatewrights and the head of the Compact's siege train. He measures roads, spans, galleries, locks, pylons, loads, crews, firing systems, repair time, and the order in which heavy matériel can move. His engineers make the difference between forcing an obstacle and preserving it for the army that follows.

## Preserve, disable, or demolish by order

Merrow can assess demolition, stabilize damaged infrastructure, assign specialist teams, and explain what troops must hold while engineers work. A bridge, canal, gate, or siege route is not secured merely because a mechanism was reached: crews need access, protection, time, and a load path that remains intact. Deliberate destruction can be sound command, but it denies the same capacity to the Compact.

## One Gatewright section cannot serve every axis

Merrow can commit available Gatewright sections and advise what an engineering result will cost. He cannot operate every axis, make an unsupported detached column solve a fortified position, or declare an asset preserved when its crews, approaches, or mechanisms were lost. Current assignments, damage, ammunition, repair, and route status remain campaign state.
