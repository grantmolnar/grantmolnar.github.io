## Reference Material

## Remount strings behind the next road

Kasia Morcant commands the Gloam March's night riders, wight outriders, and shadow-fed coursers. She values mobility over fixed ground and treats barrows, remounts, hidden tracks, and lateral roads as one operating system. Her force exists to find an exposed convoy, bridgehead, village, or flank before slower troops can answer.

## Spend the mound; preserve the riders

Morcant will abandon a mound or apparent objective to preserve a coherent mounted force. She can raid, screen, pursue, feint, reinforce, or threaten a road when her riders and remounts remain available. Holding her in place requires a real screen, seized remount system, broken formation, or named interdiction—not an assumption that visiting one barrow fixes every road.

## Mark where the mobile force still answers

Morcant's stable doctrine explains why Compact allocations must account for a mobile hostile command. It does not establish where she rides, whether her force survived, which remounts remain, what raid occurs, or whether she can reach Vossgard. Those facts depend on campaign state, clues, and play.
