# Brigadier Tamsin Holt

**Reference ID:** `b9b91d52-59d8-41ba-b73e-05ab1d3e15c4`  
**Kind:** Person  
**Aliases:** Tamsin Holt, Holt, Brigadier Holt  
**Tags:** Dawn Compact, Grey Banners, infantry, containment

## Summary

The Grey Banner brigadier who turns available infantry into bounded assault, containment, and surrendered-ground assignments without pretending one formation can hold every road.

## Reference Material

## Battalion frontage on the assigned road

Tamsin Holt commands the first Grey Banner battalions in the Vossgard campaign. Her infantry can fix a prepared position, carry a breach, hold an interdiction line, receive surrendered ground, and provide the visible force that makes a military order more than a courier's sentence. Holt thinks in companies, frontage, relief, casualties, and the road a formation must still protect after taking its objective.

## Breach order or interdiction line

A Grey Banner assault may reduce defenders when specialists open the way; containment instead names the road, gate, or line the battalions must hold and leaves the enemy dangerous behind it. Holt can divide work among battalions and support a strike against command, but ordinary infantry does not automatically preserve a bridge, silence a signal system, settle a surrender, or capture a position's distinctive asset.

## Mark the companies still present

Holt can report what her battalions can attempt, accept a bounded mission, and keep surrendered soldiers or ground under military guard pending civil process. She cannot occupy every exposed road at once or restore companies already spent elsewhere. Her current location, strength, casualties, containment duty, and availability remain campaign state.
