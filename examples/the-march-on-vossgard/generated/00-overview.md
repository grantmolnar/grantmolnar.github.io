## Synopsis

The Ashen Gate opens at dawn. By dusk, the Dawn Compact has an army in the Shadowfell and one road home. Count Othmar Voss holds the Gloam March with living levies, grave infantry, vampire cavalry, and blood enough to carry war across the mortal border. Marshal Aveline Rusk issues the Ash Warrant: authority to cross between columns, redirect scarce formations, receive surrenders, and bind the Compact when written orders arrive too late. The commission has three orders—break Voss's field army, take Vossgard, and deny him an organized remnant.

## Premise

The Dawn Compact has entered the Shadowfell through one road, with Voss's army between its bridgehead and Vossgard. The Ash Warrant can redirect formations and bind field promises. Its bearers must break the defenses, preserve enough of the March to hold or evacuate it, and close every organized escape.

## Explanation

The Ashen Gate's grey daylight prevents Voss from massing enough dead at the bridgehead to throw the Compact back. He will trade the outer March for hours, strip people and stores, gather every surviving command at Vossgard, and leave by whichever of three military exits remains open. A direct spearhead reaches him sooner with exposed roads and fewer troops for prisoners or occupation. A methodical encirclement spends strength on civilians, blood stores, command relays, and containment while the fortress hardens. Every bypass preserves tempo and leaves a named enemy force free to act later. Victory depends on the battlefield the Warrant builds, the promises backed by officers and stores, and the government or withdrawal the remaining army can defend.
