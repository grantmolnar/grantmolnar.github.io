# Party Design: The Witnesses of the Broken Dike

The Witnesses of the Broken Dike arrive with river mud still embedded in the stories told about them. None is neutral; no expertise settles the whole province; and each witnessed act can be used as evidence for an office the character may not wish to create. Their disagreements remain productive because every member helped save Reedwater and drew a different conclusion from the rescue.

The characters may be adapted to any rules system. Their mechanical roles matter less than their witnessed acts, clan obligations, personal debts, and authority to contradict one another before the court. They are one demonstration commission, not prerequisites hidden inside the authoritative adventure.

## Fresh-party commission

A fresh group should not inherit these five names, this route, or this settlement. Build the commission from four requirements instead.

1. **Recorded experience:** each character witnessed at least one relief operation and either another operation or a burden imposed by it.
2. **A partial claim:** each character has a patron, office, household, oath, technical judgment, or public act that makes one architecture attractive.
3. **A contradictory debt:** each character also made, enforced, benefited from, or preserved a decision whose cost another witness can name.
4. **Standing to disagree:** the Imperial record contains enough of the character's testimony that an interested delegation must answer it rather than dismiss it as rumor.

A fresh commission need not be balanced by clan. A single-clan group can have different field duties, family patrons, local obligations, Imperial appointments, temple relationships, or recorded disputes. An Imperial or mostly local group can carry the same tensions through the offices it served and the resources it consumed. The Emperor chose a commission whose disagreements are useful, not a committee whose composition magically guarantees impartiality.

The temporary title grants access to named delegates, requests for records through Imperial officers, entry of findings and objections, and submission of a primary and fallback. It grants no field command. Any promise obtained from a delegation remains conditional until Imperial adoption, a submission instrument, and an authenticated order change the corresponding chain in Reedwater.

## Doji Kiyoe

**Clan and function:** Crane courtier, relief administrator, and keeper of the southern granary correspondence.

**What she did at Reedwater:** Kiyoe arrived with Doji Masuyo's second barge train two days after the eastern dike failed. She turned a collection of temple stores, merchant advances, and half-legible village tallies into a civilian distribution system. When a Lion officer demanded that all rice at Reed Bank be classified as military supply, Kiyoe refused him in writing and sent the refusal under both Crane and Imperial seals. The officer obeyed. Three days later, Kiyoe quietly released a portion of the household reserve to prevent a garrison company from dispersing in search of food.

**What the court can claim about her:** The Crane can say she proved civilian Harvest works. The Lion can say her own exception proved the garrison needed a lawful supply covenant. Reed Bank can say the rice was theirs before either clan arrived.

**Public position:** Harvest must remain a civilian office. Seed rice and household relief must be assessed before military allotments, and no commander may alter those assessments at a granary door.

**Private fear:** Kiyoe knows the Crane relief network has become more than emergency charity. Its white cords, clerks, debts, and public gratitude form the skeleton of a provincial administration. She fears that surrendering too much will let less careful officials inherit the work and call its failures Crane vanity. She also fears that keeping too much will prove Otomo Kazetada right about relief becoming conquest.

**Preferred opening settlement:** A Miya title over Harvest, Crane execution for three years, outside audit, district successor wardens, and a fixed Lion supply covenant. She initially treats provincial witness standing as useful at granaries and dangerous everywhere else.

**Red line:** Harvest cannot be subordinated to Sword, and seed rice cannot become an emergency reserve through a commander's declaration alone.

**Debt carried into court:** Kiyoe used grain pledged to Reed Bank to hold one Lion company together. The choice probably saved northern villages. It also means she cannot honestly claim that civilian and military stores remained wholly separate.

**How she exerts pressure:** Kiyoe gives opponents the exact honorable sentence they would prefer the record to contain, then asks which operative surrender would make the sentence true. She rarely threatens. She makes refusal sound like authorship of the uglier account.

**Table voice:** Complete public sentences, almost no visible hesitation, and one concrete noun—key, ledger, sack, seal—placed where elegance must become administration.

**Growth demanded by the adventure:** Kiyoe must learn that local standing is not merely a shield for Crane wardens. Reedwater must be able to challenge the civilian administrators she trusts, and public credit must end rather than perpetuate the Crane claim.

## Akodo Naritomo

**Clan and function:** Lion officer, strategist, and temporary commander of the ford patrols north of Reed Bank.

**What he did at Reedwater:** Naritomo was traveling with a small Lion escort when the flood cut the northern road. He gathered scattered troops, village guards, and surviving provincial retainers into one chain of command. He held the granaries against looters, stopped two young Matsu officers from accepting oaths of fealty from frightened headpeople, and ordered his own soldiers flogged for taking rice without entry in the storehouse register. He also kept eleven villages under military checkpoints after the immediate danger passed because no credible replacement force had appeared.

**What the court can claim about him:** The Lion can say he proved command must be real. The Crane can say his checkpoints delayed barges and spoiled rice. The villages can say banditry returned wherever his patrols withdrew.

**Public position:** Reedwater needs one accountable commander, a garrison that can act before petitions arrive from Otosan Uchi, and a supply covenant that cannot be rewritten by civilian resentment.

**Private fear:** Naritomo believes many courtiers would rather watch a province fail under elegant divided authority than admit a soldier was necessary. He also knows some Lion officers have begun to enjoy the gratitude of occupied villages. If the court delays demobilization without changing their legal status, those officers will teach the next generation that emergency service creates fealty.

**Preferred opening settlement:** A three-year Lion execution of Sword beneath a revocable Seppun commission, fixed supply, thirty-day emergency powers, and district-by-district demobilization only when named replacements arrive.

**Red line:** No withdrawal schedule may remove troops from a district without identifying the receiving officer, force, stores, and effective hour.

**Debt carried into court:** Naritomo authorized the continued occupation of Three Crossings after the road reopened. The order protected the route and multiplied requisitions from the same households. Ide Qulan's convoy records make the duplication impossible to dismiss.

**How he exerts pressure:** Naritomo asks who gives the order at dusk when two lawful authorities disagree and the water is rising. He is willing to concede title, audit, and term, but he forces every proposed constraint to name the person who carries responsibility after it operates.

**Table voice:** Verb-first, sparing, and sequential. He names the receiving officer, effective hour, stores, and next order before he accepts that a transfer exists.

**Growth demanded by the adventure:** Naritomo must distinguish a real command from a long command. He must accept that public submission strengthens rather than decorates his commission, and that training a provincial successor is part of victory rather than preparation for humiliation.

## Kitsuki Sadae

**Clan and function:** Dragon magistrate, recorder of emergency claims, and examiner of the drowned provincial archive.

**What she did at Reedwater:** Sadae entered the governor's clerkhouse after the first water receded and recovered seal impressions, tax rolls, and fragments of the work-levy register. She created three chains of custody because no surviving office could certify the others. She also protected the identity of a customs clerk who accused a relief officer of replacing a manifest. The accusation was partly correct, partly mistaken, and politically useful to several people before Sadae could separate the three facts.

**What the court can claim about her:** The Dragon can say she proved divided offices need one law. The Scorpion can say her protected witness survived because she used secrecy she now distrusts. Every delegation can find one preserved record that helps it.

**Public position:** Every Reed requires an external forum, mandatory reasons, substitute jurisdiction after recusal, and a record that survives the officeholder. Emergency action may precede review; it may not erase review.

**Private fear:** Sadae knows law can become a form of possession. The person who defines admissible evidence, acceptable delay, and proper appeal may govern without holding an office. She is more comfortable discovering an inconsistency than deciding which lawful burden should still be imposed.

**Preferred opening settlement:** Imperial titles, Dragon execution of Judgment, reciprocal inspection, written reasons, strict recusal rules, and a review capable of replacing failed officeholders.

**Red line:** No actor may exercise final authority on the basis of evidence that no independent reviewer can eventually examine.

**Debt carried into court:** Sadae promised her customs witness that the source would not be named until a safe route existed. Bayushi Namika can provide that route. Accepting it requires Sadae to admit that bounded secrecy may serve law rather than defeat it.

**How she exerts pressure:** Sadae turns every courteous abstraction into a sequence: who receives notice, who decides, who acts meanwhile, who hears the appeal, what record is made, and what remedy remains after the harm cannot be undone.

**Table voice:** Numbered questions, careful distinctions, and sudden silence when the missing fact is more damaging than an accusation.

**Growth demanded by the adventure:** Sadae must accept timely provisional authority, protected sources, and local testimony that is not perfectly documentary. She must also prevent her own common circuit from becoming permanent Dragon tutelage.

## Kaiu Masami

**Clan and function:** Crab engineer, survey officer, and author of the emergency brace that kept the western embankment standing.

**What she did at Reedwater:** Masami arrived with a small Kaiu team after the first breach. When the western embankment began to fail, she cut an orchard wall, flooded pasture held by a minor samurai household, and used the released water to reduce pressure on Reed Bank. She had no living governor from whom to seek approval. She wrote her name on the order before giving it. Later surveys showed that the choice saved more households than it harmed. The harmed households remain harmed.

**What the court can claim about her:** The Crab can say authority must equal blame. Reedwater can say the person who saved a village also decided which land would drown. The Phoenix can say her team stopped before cutting through a marked shrine boundary even while the embankment failed.

**Public position:** Waters requires decisive technical execution, assigned labor and materials, work-site command, and a short emergency authority. Political sacrifice must be named by a titleholder, but engineers must not be punished for refusing to certify courtly compromises as sound designs.

**Private fear:** Masami believes the next heavy rain may arrive before the court's architecture can train anyone. She is tempted by a Reconstruction Directorate that joins Waters, Roads, labor, and materials under one short commission. She knows such a body could preserve the province and teach its officers to resist their own sunset.

**Preferred opening settlement:** An Imperially titled hundred-day Reconstruction Directorate with Kaiu design authority, Unicorn road execution, assigned labor and materials, fast appeal, and an automatic end before autumn review.

**Red line:** The court may choose a costlier or slower design. It may not order an engineer to describe that political choice as technical necessity.

**Debt carried into court:** Masami's emergency cut preserved Reed Bank while consuming land outside the village tally. The household burden ledger does not yet contain the full loss. She must place it there before asking Reedwater to trust another emergency commission.

**How she exerts pressure:** Masami draws the missing beam, empty storehouse, untrained crew, or flooded district beneath a proposed clause and asks whether the court wants the work or merely the title of having ordered it.

**Table voice:** She sketches while others speak, uses materials as verbs, and reserves moral language for the moment a technical choice assigns sacrifice.

**Growth demanded by the adventure:** Masami must accept that technical speed cannot decide political sacrifice. She must yield Roads, witness standing, and termination certification to institutions outside her directorate without pretending those checks make construction impossible.

## Ide Qulan

**Clan and function:** Unicorn diplomat, caravan negotiator, and coordinator of the first western relief road.

**What he did at Reedwater:** Qulan joined remount stations, temple lodging, merchant carts, ferrymen, and village relays into one route while every clan carried different warrants. He kept the road open by accepting requisitions before valuation could be agreed. Saburo of the Three Crossings lent twelve horses and received seven different promises of repayment. Four horses returned. Three died. Five entered other relief chains and have never been traced.

**What the court can claim about him:** The Unicorn can say he proved movement is governance. The witnesses can say he proved an open road can impoverish the households that make it open. The Scorpion can say his duplicate manifests are the only records that expose several contradictions.

**Public position:** Roads must have one dispatch law, minimum open routes, bounded closures, prior valuation where possible, automatic debt where it is not, and a cumulative ledger that sees what every other Reed has already demanded.

**Private fear:** Qulan believes Rokugani courts mistake stationary seals for control. He suspects the Imperial families will preserve authorship while leaving provincial execution unfunded. He also distrusts any reconstruction body allowed to close roads for its own convenience and certify the closure as necessary.

**Preferred opening settlement:** Miya title, Unicorn execution through the first planting season, no private toll privilege, public manifests, local station successors, and a Reedwater road compact.

**Red line:** No district may lose every route for messages, food, and petitions to a closure declared solely by the office that benefits from it.

**Debt carried into court:** Qulan's road kept thousands supplied by taking animals from households that may not plant next season. He cannot ask the Empire to honor Unicorn service while treating those losses as unfortunate informality.

**How he exerts pressure:** Qulan follows an order outward. He names each station, porter, horse, ferry, witness, and correction required before the court's sentence becomes true. When someone calls that detail provincial, he asks where Imperial authority is supposed to live between capitals.

**Table voice:** Route outward, receipt home. His sentences travel through people and places, then return carrying the debt the original order tried not to see.

**Growth demanded by the adventure:** Qulan must accept a temporary concentration of reconstruction authority and admit that not every route can remain open. In return, he must force the concentration to price closures, preserve a minimum network, and train local successors who can refuse the Unicorn later.

## The company's internal fault lines

The five demonstrated Witnesses begin with different architectures:

- Kiyoe favors an Imperial spine with strong Crane Harvest execution.
- Naritomo favors an honored occupation under exact military commission.
- Sadae favors paired custodians and a common legal circuit.
- Masami favors a short Reconstruction Directorate.
- Qulan favors Reedwater succession with temporary outside execution.

Their disagreements are personal as well as institutional.

- Kiyoe believes Naritomo's checkpoints converted dependence into political leverage. Naritomo believes Kiyoe's grain exceptions prove Harvest cannot pretend Sword is merely another claimant.
- Sadae distrusts Masami's emergency discretion. Masami thinks Sadae's ideal record is easiest to produce after the district has drowned.
- Qulan can document requisitions issued by Naritomo's patrols and Kiyoe's convoys against the same households. Both need his evidence and resent its symmetry.
- Masami wants authority to close the western road during construction. Qulan considers an unpriced closure another requisition imposed on people who cannot sit at court.
- Kiyoe initially wants Reedwater testimony admitted through Crane sponsorship. Sadae and Qulan insist witnesses require standing that survives disagreement with their sponsors.
- Naritomo supports local succession in principle but considers Kenta's patrol roster dangerously thin. Qulan sees training and staged authority as the only way it will ever become thicker.

No character can solve the adventure by winning every dispute. Their eventual settlement works because each forces the others to surrender the form of power most natural to their own expertise.

```text
Chrysanthemum Throne

  -> Shrine of Listening Water
  -> Hall of Joined Timbers
  -> Garden of White Gravel
  -> Ministry of Divided Ink
  -> Theater of a Thousand Sleeves
  -> Stone-and-Moss Court
  -> Evening of the Chrysanthemum Moon
  -> Chamber of Seven Reeds
  -> Second Audience
```

In shorthand:

```text
Throne -> Miya -> Unicorn -> Witnesses -> Crane -> Lion -> Phoenix -> Crab
       -> Seppun -> Otomo -> Scorpion -> Dragon -> Moon -> Chamber -> Audience
```

The demonstrated route is intentionally provincial before it is constitutional. The Witnesses first learn how orders travel, who bears them, and what the relief already consumed. Only after confronting the clan executors and material design does it ask the Imperial families, Scorpion inspectors, and Dragon jurists to formalize the result.

## Demonstrated final settlement

The Witnesses call their primary architecture **Reedwater Succession under the Hundred-Day Brace**.

It combines a short reconstruction concentration with an explicit transfer of records, training, resources, and staged authority to provincial successors:

- Harvest receives an Imperial title, Crane civilian execution through the first harvest, protected seed and household stores, a declining Lion supply covenant, outside audit, and district successor wardens.
- Waters receives a hundred-day Kaiu execution commission over the approved emergency brace, assigned labor and materials, and work-site command. Political sacrifice requires named Imperial authorization, immediate notice to a provincial chair where possible, automatic hearing, and restoration debt.
- Roads receives Miya title and Unicorn execution through the first planting season, one dispatch law, a minimum open route, compensated requisition, public manifests, and district successors. Waters may close a route only through the stated emergency hierarchy and must fund restoration or substitution.
- Sword receives Seppun title and removal authority, Lion execution under a declining roster, fixed supply, staged demobilization, and transfer to Natsugawa Kenta's provincial patrol structure as it reaches named capacity.
- Judgment receives an Imperial title and a Dragon-administered common circuit with mandatory recusal, named substitutes, interim orders, deadlines, and autumn transfer to a mixed provincial bench.
- Shrines receive Imperial protection, Phoenix consultation and ritual coordination, local recognized speakers, emergency bypass with spiritual debt, and succession by Reedwater's shrine communities rather than hereditary Phoenix control.
- Accounts remain an Imperial office. Scorpion inspectors receive access, comparison, preservation, and protected reporting but no provincial title or final sanction. Records are divided among Miya custody, Dragon review, and public dispositions.
- Reedwater's witnesses receive proclamations and corrections, access to the burden ledger, petition without patron ownership, a review trigger, and return testimony. Rei holds the first rotating provincial chair.
- Autumn review may renew, narrow, replace, remedy, compensate, or activate failure succession. It measures transfer of provincial capacity, not merely competence of outside executors.

The fallback is a **Narrow Imperial Peace**: immediate submission, protected seed, fixed military supply, a hundred-day Waters commission, bounded Roads, and a later commission for the unresolved offices. It is viable but deliberately less ambitious.

## Resulting political state

- **Crane:** retain civilian Harvest execution and public credit, but return title, accept local standing, and surrender any claim that relief creates permanent office.
- **Lion:** retain real command for a short term, receive lawful supply, lower standards before recommission, and train the provincial force that will end their commission.
- **Phoenix:** secure consultation, remedy, and continuity of shrine communities without obtaining an invisible veto or permanent title.
- **Dragon:** establish the common circuit while accepting deadlines, provisional action, protected sources, and eventual provincial succession.
- **Scorpion:** lose Accounts title, gain bounded inspection, preserve sources, and submit final findings to review and public disposition.
- **Unicorn:** execute Roads without toll privilege, accept cumulative debt and local successors, and gain enforceable limits on closures and duplicated requisitions.
- **Crab:** receive authority equal to the emergency work, but not authority to choose political sacrifice, close roads without account, or certify the end of their own term.
- **Imperial families:** retain authorship, title, removal, proclamation, and review while accepting that implementation depends on clan and provincial capacity rather than seals alone.
- **Reedwater:** bears relocation, labor, debt, and temporary outside rule, but gains named compensation, enforceable standing, records, resources, and an architecture designed to transfer rather than merely review occupation.
