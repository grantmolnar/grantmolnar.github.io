# Lead List: The Mandate of Seven Reeds

Organized by source encounter (outgoing view).

## Hall of the Chrysanthemum Throne (`hall-of-the-chrysanthemum-throne`)

- **Crane relief named before claim** (`crane-relief-named-before-claim`) → Grain is not spoil (`the-crane-can-yield-command-if-it-keeps-the-harvest-from-becoming-spoil`); unlocks `pavilion-of-first-rain`. Discovery: Heard in the opening audience; noting the separation between thanks and office is enough.
  The Emperor publicly credits the Crane barges for preventing famine but does not connect that service to ownership. Masuyo therefore has honor she can preserve even if she yields military command.
- **Seven days and seven proclamations** (`seven-days-and-seven-proclamations`) → An order must survive the road (`miya-will-support-what-can-be-proclaimed-village-by-village`); unlocks `hall-of-open-roads`. Discovery: Heard in the opening command or by asking why the party has seven days rather than a longer court season.
  The common holding order expires at the close of the seventh day, and the Emperor requires the Mandate to be carried into Reedwater rather than merely sealed in Otosan Uchi. Each office must have a proclamation and field order naming recipient, route, acknowledgment, and effective hour.
- **The army held at the ford** (`the-army-held-at-the-ford`) → Command must be real before it can be bounded (`the-lion-can-share-administration-if-command-is-not-made-decorative`); unlocks `hall-of-red-standards`. Discovery: Heard in the recital of provisional orders or confirmed by a direct procedural question.
  The Emperor ordered the Lion army to hold rather than withdraw and did not condemn its initial intervention. A bounded commission can therefore be framed as continued service, not forgiveness.
- **The Emperor's first prohibition** (`the-emperors-first-prohibition`) → Divide the power before the blame (`otomo-will-divide-authority-before-it-divides-blame`); unlocks `ministry-of-divided-ink`. Discovery: Heard in the opening command; asking whether emergency service creates a claim makes the prohibition explicit.
  The Emperor refuses to let any Great Clan receive Reedwater whole as a reward for arriving with force. The sentence establishes that service may be honored while governing powers are divided.
- **The Emperor's refusal to count** (`the-emperors-refusal-to-count`) → The Emperor tests failure, not applause (`imperial-judgment-tests-dangerous-combinations-and-failure-paths-not-endorsement-counts`). Discovery: Heard if the party frames the opening task as collecting endorsements.
  The Emperor states that he can command obedience without a majority and asks instead which proposal can survive the refusal, failure, or misconduct of its strongest supporter.
- **The Emperor's unanswered question** (`the-emperors-unanswered-question`) → The Mandate must remain the Emperor’s act (`the-emperor-will-hear-a-mandate-that-restores-his-authorship-without-rewarding-mobilization`); unlocks `the-second-audience`. Discovery: Heard at the end of the opening audience.
  The Emperor asks which clan will yield something it presently holds because Imperial command requires it, not because another clan purchased the concession. The court offers no immediate answer.
- **The flooded shrines named among the losses** (`the-flooded-shrines-named-among-the-losses`) → Sacred duty must survive the work (`the-phoenix-will-bless-reconstruction-only-where-sacred-obligations-survive-it`); unlocks `shrine-of-listening-water`. Discovery: Heard during the opening account; calling attention to the order of losses makes its legal use clear.
  The Imperial recital lists destroyed shrines and displaced keepers alongside roads and granaries. Sacred restoration is part of the public purpose, not a late Phoenix addition.
- **The sword beneath the throne** (`the-sword-beneath-the-throne`) → A clan sword may serve beneath an Imperial hand (`seppun-can-accept-a-clan-garrison-under-an-imperial-sword`); unlocks `garden-of-white-gravel`. Discovery: Heard by asking whether the Throne intends to replace every clan soldier with Seppun troops.
  The Emperor says Reedwater needs a sword but that the sword will serve his peace. He leaves open a Great Clan commander serving under an Imperial commission.

- **The ministry's grain precedent** (`the-ministrys-grain-precedent`) → Grain is not spoil (`the-crane-can-yield-command-if-it-keeps-the-harvest-from-becoming-spoil`); unlocks `pavilion-of-first-rain`. - **The three seals never kept together** (`the-three-seals-never-kept-together`) → Food, force, and self-certification make a private province (`no-clan-may-safely-hold-harvest-sword-and-accounts-together`). Discovery: Found in the ministry’s comparative office records.
  Kazetada shows that stable Imperial provinces keep the powers to feed, coerce, and certify apart even when one governor coordinates them. Their union creates a government able to justify itself.
