# Encounter Index: The Mandate of Seven Reeds

## Hall of the Chrysanthemum Throne (`hall-of-the-chrysanthemum-throne`)

**Role:** necessary, start

The flood left Reedwater's practical hands alive but severed every lawful joint above them. The Witnesses receive seven days to allocate authority without commanding the province themselves.

## Ministry of Divided Ink (`ministry-of-divided-ink`)

**Role:** necessary

Otomo Kazetada cuts each Reed into title, execution, audit, and succession, then asks which powers may safely share a seal. His ink makes gratitude expensive and concentrated authority visible.

## Garden of White Gravel (`garden-of-white-gravel`)

**Role:** necessary

Seppun Tomoe will honor a Great Clan sword only after it has visibly obeyed: standard lowered, claim surrendered, field officer named, term bounded, supply separated from granary control, and removal made real.

## Hall of Open Roads (`hall-of-open-roads`)

**Role:** necessary

Miya Shun sends every elegant clause across a broken bridge. Dates, messengers, recipients, acknowledgments, petitions, and remembered corrections decide whether the Mandate reaches Reedwater as law or arrives as rumor.

## Pavilion of First Rain (`pavilion-of-first-rain`)

**Role:** necessary

Doji Masuyo has made relief look like government. She can surrender command, but not the civilian granaries, seed distinctions, named creditors, and public memory that keep gratitude from becoming spoil.

## Hall of Red Standards (`hall-of-red-standards`)

**Role:** necessary

Akodo Tadanobu can lower the Lion standards without abandoning the villages beneath them if command remains real, supply is written, withdrawal follows replacement, and obedience enters the record as completion rather than disgrace.

## Shrine of Listening Water (`shrine-of-listening-water`)

**Role:** necessary

Three drowned bells carry incompatible obligations. Isawa Etsuko demands a sacred procedure with a speaker, deadline, override, recorded debt, and remedy, so necessity cannot turn consultation into pious decoration.

## Stone-and-Moss Court (`stone-and-moss-court`)

**Role:** necessary

Kitsuki Harunori turns the clean face of law into the earth. Judgment must reach evidence, recuse without vanishing, permit urgent action, preserve appeal, and remain an Imperial circuit rather than Dragon possession.

## Theater of a Thousand Sleeves (`theater-of-a-thousand-sleeves`)

**Role:** necessary

Seven empty robes accuse an absent eighth. Bayushi Namika divides inspection into access, preservation, protected reporting, restraint, review, and sanction, exposing where secrecy shelters truth and where it shelters power.

## Courtyard of Bells (`courtyard-of-bells`)

**Role:** necessary

Every bell marks a road, a delay, or a taking. Ide Sachiko can make Reedwater move if priority is singular, requisition becomes valued debt, closures preserve one minimum route, and households survive the map drawn across them.

## Hall of Joined Timbers (`hall-of-joined-timbers`)

**Role:** necessary

A polished beam bears nothing; broken timbers carry the room. Kaiu Masashige demands authority equal to the work while Reedwater’s witnesses force the court to price labor, land, sacred ground, displacement, and the district chosen to receive the water.

## Guesthouse of the Bent Reed (`guesthouse-of-the-bent-reed`)

**Role:** necessary

Four flood-stained cloaks wait beyond the innermost wall. Reedwater’s witnesses expose the arithmetic hidden inside honorable bargains and demand standing that survives after the court no longer needs their testimony.

## Evening of the Chrysanthemum Moon (`evening-of-the-chrysanthemum-moon`)

**Role:** optional

Seven reflected moons turn seating, poems, gifts, thanks, and refusals into public memory. Ceremony can bind honor around an exact undertaking; it cannot smuggle title, release, or burden past the drafting table.

## Chamber of Seven Reeds (`chamber-of-seven-reeds`)

**Role:** necessary

Red cords expose every dependency among office, work, stores, emergency power, succession, remedy, proclamation, and the field receipt that makes a capital order real.

## The Second Audience (`the-second-audience`)

**Role:** necessary, end

The final audience is dispatch, not ratification: the Emperor tests recipients, submissions, burdens, fallbacks, and the first orders that must leave before the court disperses.
