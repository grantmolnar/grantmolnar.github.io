# Garden of White Gravel

**Role:** necessary  
**Tags:** court, imperial, seppun, military

Seppun Tomoe will honor a Great Clan sword only after it has visibly obeyed: standard lowered, claim surrendered, field officer named, term bounded, supply separated from granary control, and removal made real.

## Opening View

One messenger brings two lawful orders for the same patrol before sunset. Seppun Tomoe sets both seals upon the bronze chrysanthemum and leaves one deliberate bootprint across the raked gravel. Wrapped spearheads, checkpoint tokens, and an empty place for surrender wait behind her. The contradiction must travel farther than the ceremony: one commander, one supply chain, named replacement, and an answer when the second order reaches the field.

## Encounter Material

## The order behind the lowered standard

Tomoe has placed a covered Lion spearhead, a Crane granary key, and an Imperial dispatch rod on the white gravel. She asks the Witnesses to move each object from present possession to lawful command. A bow does not move it. A promise does not move it. The object moves only when they name the receiving officer, effective hour, subordinate orders, replacement capacity, and hand empowered to end the commission.

“Lower. Surrender. Receive. Obey. Withdraw,” Tomoe says, giving each verb one place in the chain. Reedwater lacks enough force to discard every emergency command at once, but necessity cannot mature into title merely by lasting. She does not require an Imperial officer at every patrol fire. She requires every sword to lead back to an Imperial commission, a named superior, a defined term, and a removal power that can reach the field. “Show me the links.”

## The mobilized armies

Lion troops hold the northern granaries. Crane ashigaru and armed retainers protect the southern barges. Crab work camps are guarded by their own soldiers. Unicorn convoy escorts control several western crossings. None openly defies the Emperor. Each has reasons its current position should continue until someone else proves able to replace it.

Tomoe will not advise the Emperor to reward visible disobedience. Before the final audience, she wants each force to perform some unmistakable act of submission: lower a clan standard beneath an Imperial banner, surrender a disputed checkpoint, accept an outside quartermaster, release a requisitioned road, or publish a withdrawal schedule.

The act need not humiliate the clan. It must be legible from the road, in the barracks, and in the next order a frightened lieutenant receives.

## Submission at distance

Most granary keys, field standards, checkpoint tokens, and work-site ledgers remain in Reedwater. The delegates bring governing copies, seals, or tokens sufficient to surrender the legal claim they represent. Tomoe treats the court act as incomplete until it is paired with an authenticated order to a named field officer, an effective hour, the relevant inventory or roster, a route for receipt, and a remedy if the transfer is refused or delayed.

Until the order arrives, the former commander keeps only the frozen duties needed to prevent a gap. After receipt, a contrary local order is disobedience rather than an alternative interpretation. A submission can therefore be ceremonially visible in the capital and operationally exact in the province without pretending one room contains every sword and key.

## Forms of acceptable command

### A Seppun commission

A clan commander receives the Sword Reed under an Imperial commission. The commander retains operational control but can be removed by a named Seppun authority. This is the cleanest military chain and the most visible limit on the clan.

### Split garrison and emergency command

One office controls ordinary patrols while another receives temporary authority during flood, riot, or invasion. Tomoe accepts the distinction only if the trigger and termination of emergency command are exact.

### Rotating command

The clans alternate command by season or district. Tomoe considers this workable for patrol administration and dangerous during a fast crisis unless one officer can supersede the rotation.

### Imperial garrison with clan detachments

The Seppun hold the Sword Reed while the Great Clans provide detachments. This strongly preserves Imperial authorship but strains Seppun manpower and risks making distant commanders responsible for local failures they cannot prevent.

## Tomoe's own vulnerability

A large permanent Seppun role would increase her family's burdens and expose the palace guard to provincial politics. She will accept that burden if no better arrangement exists, but she does not secretly seek it. Careful witnesses can distinguish institutional pride from personal appetite.

She also knows the next flood may come before the court's seven days have ended. If the Waters Reed and Sword Reed cannot cooperate immediately, the best constitutional settlement may arrive after another village is lost.

## What to record

- who commands ordinary patrols;
- who may declare and end an emergency;
- what visible act of submission each mobilized clan will perform;
- who can remove the commander;
- how the garrison receives food without controlling Harvest; and
- what happens if the appointed commander refuses an Imperial order.
