# Reference Index: The Mandate of Seven Reeds

## People

- [**Otomo Kazetada**](06b9c081-61b3-494b-a8d9-0c2d9221d44f.md) — A senior Otomo minister who preserves Imperial authorship by separating title, execution, audit, and succession and exposing dangerous concentrations of power. Aliases: Kazetada, Senior Minister Otomo Kazetada.
- [**Seppun Tomoe**](0f0f98d1-1df4-4fa9-87ac-7231ddc63339.md) — A palace-guard commander who requires every provincial sword to lead back to a visible Imperial commission, a removable commander, and an order that can reach the field. Aliases: Tomoe, Commander Seppun Tomoe.
- [**Miya Shun**](cdaf6388-9ed4-4df7-bb8a-218934b414c4.md) — A senior Imperial herald who tests whether an order can cross Reedwater's broken roads with dates, recipients, acknowledgments, corrections, and a return path for petitions. Aliases: Shun, Herald Miya Shun.
- [**Rei of Reed Bank**](d11869d8-5fc7-40a6-b2b6-451ff030d755.md) — Reed Bank's headwoman totals the cumulative household cost of lawful orders and insists that relocation, labor, and requisition name the capacity they consume. Aliases: Rei, Headwoman Rei.
- [**Brother Hojun**](298bc3fb-a199-4fcd-9b49-36f442507546.md) — The keeper of a drowned riverside shrine speaks for displaced shrine communities, their obligations, and the successors who must carry them after reconstruction. Aliases: Hojun, Keeper Hojun.
- [**Saburo of the Three Crossings**](0e8c4594-3da6-403e-b886-424d1e590eb5.md) — The master of a crossing association explains when public service bankrupts the carriers and households an open-road order assumes. Aliases: Saburo, Master Saburo.
- [**Natsugawa Kenta**](dc9e55ca-3328-4f9e-8881-f5bf9ea5c5d7.md) — A low-ranking provincial samurai who tests whether temporary commissions leave Reedwater with recognized local officers, records, resources, and lawful succession. Aliases: Kenta, Natsugawa.
- [**Doji Masuyo**](55bb719c-6a0c-424e-890b-79005dfa32fd.md) — The Crane relief architect separates civilian granary execution, protected seed, named creditors, and exact public credit from permanent ownership of Harvest. Aliases: Masuyo, Lady Doji Masuyo.
- [**Akodo Tadanobu**](d228eef5-90b5-40f6-bb24-79cea04e1ea5.md) — The Lion commander demands a traceable chain, written supply, real emergency authority, replacement before withdrawal, and submission remembered as completion rather than defeat. Aliases: Tadanobu, General Akodo Tadanobu.
- [**Isawa Etsuko**](49ec59bf-90f1-4af7-a386-3dbecfbb4c56.md) — The Phoenix principal turns sacred necessity into named speakers, rites, deadlines, overrides, restoration debts, remedies, and review outside the office disturbing the site. Aliases: Etsuko, Master Isawa Etsuko.
- [**Kitsuki Harunori**](c3e35968-2ca2-4622-9e80-c373cc1d461c.md) — The Dragon magistrate requires comparable evidence, stated reasons, recusal with a substitute, timely appeal, preserved records, and judgment outside the interested office. Aliases: Harunori, Magistrate Kitsuki Harunori.
- [**Bayushi Namika**](909402eb-2030-4b7c-a0b9-fbca1b296d18.md) — The Scorpion principal separates inspection into access, preservation, protected reporting, restraint, review, and sanction so useful secrecy cannot become invisible rule. Aliases: Namika, Lady Bayushi Namika.
- [**Ide Sachiko**](4952957e-5858-4388-8dec-3cc5755e1339.md) — The Unicorn negotiator makes every Roads clause travel through priority, dispatch, requisition, closure, receipt, repayment, and a successor able to keep the stations working. Aliases: Sachiko, Lady Ide Sachiko.
- [**Kaiu Masashige**](dda4a93a-90b1-4b8b-9074-afe167442977.md) — The Crab engineer draws authority, labor, materials, burden, deadline, failure, and maintenance as one load path and refuses to hide political choice beneath a technical seal. Aliases: Masashige, Engineer Kaiu Masashige.

## Places

- [**Reedwater Province**](985f1d87-52db-4cf3-bc45-4100ec25f524.md) — A flood-severed province whose practical deputies, districts, routes, shrines, stores, camps, and households can continue narrow duties but cannot replace the lost joints of government by habit alone. Aliases: Reedwater.

## Objects

- [**The Cumulative Burden Ledger**](5c6dad77-cabe-4856-9c38-18c8d2399411.md) — A shared record that totals overlapping labor, stores, transport, land, displacement, prior service, timing, receipts, and remedies across all seven offices. Aliases: Burden Ledger, Resource-and-Burden Ledger.
- [**The Submission Register**](8002df92-4f09-4c39-9a1b-ca8f3654ab24.md) — A transfer record that joins a court act to an authenticated field order, named recipient, effective hour, inventory, acknowledgment route, narrow holdover duty, and failure remedy. Aliases: Register of Submission, Submission Instruments.

## Other

- [**The Seven Reeds**](ad3b1038-5dfa-4abf-8948-3c2230654852.md) — Reedwater's seven provincial office domains—Harvest, Waters, Roads, Sword, Judgment, Shrines, and Accounts—may divide title, execution, audit, resources, emergency power, succession, term, and review. Aliases: Seven Reeds, Provincial Reeds.
