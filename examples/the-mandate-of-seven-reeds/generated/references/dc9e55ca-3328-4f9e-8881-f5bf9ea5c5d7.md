# Natsugawa Kenta

## Summary

A low-ranking provincial samurai who tests whether temporary commissions leave Reedwater with recognized local officers, records, resources, and lawful succession.

## Reference Material

## The seal after imported banners depart

Natsugawa Kenta is a low-ranking provincial samurai whose vassal house lost land and records in the flood. He asks which order can still be carried after Great Clan executors depart and which local person will be recognized when holding only experience without seal, pay, stores, staff, or a surviving register.

## Successor, clerk, store, horse, pay

Kenta seeks a provincial succession process with deputies, qualification, training, records, resources, pay, review, and lawful removal. A named successor without animals, clerks, paper, rice, tools, or a receiving act is ceremonial abandonment. Restoring status without capacity is no more durable than importing capacity without local standing.

## Provincial service without clan absorption

Kenta can accept Great Clan executors, tested local houses, fixed training periods, outside review, and removal for incompetence or misconduct. He rejects absorption into clan vassalage as the price of recognition. His standing does not assign a Reed, certify a successor, restore a house, or determine a term; those decisions remain with the commission, field receipts, and Imperial judgment.
