# The Submission Register

**Reference ID:** `8002df92-4f09-4c39-9a1b-ca8f3654ab24`  
**Kind:** Object  
**Aliases:** Register of Submission, Submission Instruments  
**Tags:** record, submission, transfer, field-receipt

## Summary

A transfer record that joins a court act to an authenticated field order, named recipient, effective hour, inventory, acknowledgment route, narrow holdover duty, and failure remedy.

## Reference Material

## Enter the act, not the symbol alone

Lowered standards, displayed keys, opened ledgers, surrendered seals, gifts, and spoken obedience matter only when an instrument identifies the present holder, the act performed at court, the authority relinquished or accepted, and the field order that carries the change beyond the room.

## Recipient, courier, hour, acknowledgment

A complete entry names the receiving officer, courier, authentication, effective time, inventory, subordinate commands or custodians affected, acknowledgment route, and the narrow duty that remains while the message travels. It also names what happens when the recipient is absent, refuses, cannot perform, or reports that the transfer is incomplete.

## Receipt does not prove performance

The register preserves the terms and evidence of submission; it does not perform them. This sheet proves no current surrender, receipt, transfer, inventory, standard-lowering, key delivery, field obedience, office allocation, or Imperial acceptance.
