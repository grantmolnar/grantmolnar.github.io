## Summary

A shared record that totals overlapping labor, stores, transport, land, displacement, prior service, timing, receipts, and remedies across all seven offices.

## Reference Material

## Count the household once across seven demands

The ledger joins a demand to the office requesting it, the district and household bearing it, the people or property required, prior service, planting and seasonal timing, displacement, transport, duration, compensation, receipt, review, and remedy. Separate lawful orders can become impossible when each office counts only its own need.

## Capacity, sacrifice, prior service, remedy

The record distinguishes available capacity from a sacrifice the court chooses to compel. It can expose duplicate calls, false equality among unequal districts, missing livelihood terms, material diversion, unpaid debt, and a successor promised without staff, stores, pay, records, or training.

## The total changes only through live entries

The ledger's structure is stable; its totals and judgments are not. This sheet records no current quota, labor call, store, route, relocation, valuation, capacity finding, sacrifice, receipt, amendment, or remedy.
