# Seppun Tomoe

**Reference ID:** `0f0f98d1-1df4-4fa9-87ac-7231ddc63339`  
**Kind:** Person  
**Aliases:** Tomoe, Commander Seppun Tomoe  
**Tags:** Imperial court, military, command, submission

## Summary

A palace-guard commander who requires every provincial sword to lead back to a visible Imperial commission, a removable commander, and an order that can reach the field.

## Reference Material

## A standard lowered into a named hand

Seppun Tomoe commands part of the palace guard and receives the military clauses of the Reedwater settlement. She speaks in verbs—lower, surrender, receive, obey, withdraw—and distinguishes necessary force from the title that force begins to resemble. A command chain must name a superior, a field officer, a term, and a hand able to remove that officer.

## The field order behind the bow

Tomoe requires every mobilized force to perform an unmistakable act of obedience without turning submission into humiliation. A standard, key, checkpoint token, or governing seal may be surrendered at court only with an authenticated order, named recipient, effective hour, inventory or roster, route for acknowledgment, narrow holdover duties, and a remedy for delay or refusal. Ceremony makes obedience visible; dispatch makes it real.

## Command that ends when the commission ends

Tomoe can supervise a clan commander, separate ordinary and emergency command, accept a bounded rotation, or carry a larger Seppun burden when no workable alternative exists. She does not seek a permanent Imperial garrison for its own sake. Her office does not determine which commander, force, supply covenant, emergency trigger, submission, or removal term the Mandate will actually adopt.
