## Subtract every burden already borne

Rei is headwoman of Reed Bank, a settlement repeatedly asked for labor, transport, stores, land, and patience by offices that count their demands separately. She totals household size, prior service, planting, displacement, and the other calls already placed on the same people. Equal quotas among unequal villages are not equal burdens.

## A village remains present after testimony

Rei seeks receipts, access to the cumulative burden ledger, a petition route, review triggers, and the right to return with implementation testimony. She can identify false capacity and require a relocation package to name water, seed, fields, roads, livelihood, and possession dates rather than merely point to higher ground.

## Land, water, seed, graves, date

Rei can accept harsh levies and even relocation when the complete exchange is explicit and survivable. She does not possess a veto, speak for every Reedwater community, or hold authority equal to a Great Clan delegate. Any quota, labor call, site, compensation, relocation order, receipt, or finding remains changing provincial and commission state.
