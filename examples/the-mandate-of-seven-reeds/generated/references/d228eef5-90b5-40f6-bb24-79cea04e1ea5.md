# Akodo Tadanobu

**Reference ID:** `d228eef5-90b5-40f6-bb24-79cea04e1ea5`  
**Kind:** Person  
**Aliases:** Tadanobu, General Akodo Tadanobu  
**Tags:** Lion, Sword, command, submission

## Summary

The Lion commander demands a traceable chain, written supply, real emergency authority, replacement before withdrawal, and submission remembered as completion rather than defeat.

## Reference Material

## One command, one named superior, one end

Tadanobu treats Sword as responsibility for patrols, checkpoints, musters, wounded, camp security, and the officers who must answer when orders conflict. He accepts Imperial title, removal, a fixed term, reduced force, and outside review when the remaining command is coherent enough to act.

## Rations that do not confer Harvest

A military chain that can be starved or redirected by unwritten allotment is decorative. Tadanobu therefore requires predictable supply and named emergency variance while refusing civilian ownership of Harvest, Accounts, or the relief debt. Replacement duties must pass district by district before a position is abandoned.

## Standard, roster, recipient, effective hour

Tadanobu can lower standards and surrender claims when the act changes command, reaches field officers, and enters the record as obedience to the Emperor's peace. His record does not establish a present commander, roster, garrison allotment, withdrawal schedule, submission, bargain, or result.
