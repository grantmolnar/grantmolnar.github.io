## Summary

Reedwater's seven provincial office domains—Harvest, Waters, Roads, Sword, Judgment, Shrines, and Accounts—may divide title, execution, audit, resources, emergency power, succession, term, and review.

## Reference Material

## Seven tablets, seven domains

Harvest governs granaries, seed, assessment, and emergency distribution. Waters governs dikes, canals, ferries, work levies, and flood authority. Roads governs stations, bridges, remounts, convoy priority, and requisition. Sword governs garrison, musters, patrols, and emergency command. Judgment governs magistrates, appeals, land disputes, and crimes involving officeholders. Shrines governs sacred boundaries, ritual labor, stipends, and local spiritual relations. Accounts governs taxes, customs, audit, relief debt, and the records by which obligations survive.

## Title, execution, audit, succession

An office can separate Imperial title, ordinary execution, audit, assigned resources, ordinary emergency action, extraordinary sacrifice, ordinary succession, failure succession, term, transition, review, and remedy. Several offices may share a holder, but dangerous combinations require visible constraint and an implementation chain that survives distance.

## A constitutional grammar awaiting names

The seven lacquered tablets make authority discussable; they do not appoint anyone. This sheet establishes no present holder, executor, auditor, successor, resource grant, emergency trigger, term, dependency, amendment, submission, or Imperial judgment.
