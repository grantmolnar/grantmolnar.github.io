## Seed keys outside the sword hand

Masuyo insists that household rations, seed rice, military supply, and emergency reserves remain distinct. A commander may receive a written covenant; ordinary military order does not alter village allotments or open the seed reserve. She can accept Imperial title, outside audit, a fixed term, and trained local successors while preserving civilian granary work.

## Service entered without provincial title

Public language determines who may claim repayment, whose service is remembered, and whether relief becomes precedent for rule. Masuyo may press for exact credit and enforceable debt without deciding the current Harvest holder, quota, concession, coalition, amendment, or proclamation. Those remain live commission state.
