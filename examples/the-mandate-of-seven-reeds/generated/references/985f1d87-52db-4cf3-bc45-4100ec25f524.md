# Reedwater Province

**Reference ID:** `985f1d87-52db-4cf3-bc45-4100ec25f524`  
**Kind:** Place  
**Aliases:** Reedwater  
**Tags:** province, flood, implementation, burden

## Summary

A flood-severed province whose practical deputies, districts, routes, shrines, stores, camps, and households can continue narrow duties but cannot replace the lost joints of government by habit alone.

## Reference Material

## The flood killed the lawful joints

The eastern dike drowned the governor, the holders of the Seven Reeds, their seal-bearing chief clerks, and the first lawful substitutes empowered to appoint, replace, or overrule province-wide offices. District clerks, wardens, patrol officers, ferrymen, shrine keepers, village officials, and other practical deputies survived with authority to preserve life and repeat existing orders inside narrow bounds.

## Relief commands laid across one province

Northern granaries, southern villages, western roads, damaged crossings, drowned shrine ground, work camps, customs houses, and the broken eastern water line depend on one another. Distance from court turns dates, couriers, recipients, inventories, field receipts, and correction routes into part of the law rather than clerical detail.

## District voices, no single provincial mouth

Reedwater is not one village, claimant, clan possession, or grateful beneficiary. Its households bear cumulative calls for labor, grain, transport, land, worship, security, and patience. This sheet establishes no current capacity, burden, route, officeholder, consent, district priority, relocation, remedy, or provincial judgment.
