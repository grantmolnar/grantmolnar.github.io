## Summary

A senior Otomo minister who preserves Imperial authorship by separating title, execution, audit, and succession and exposing dangerous concentrations of power.

## Reference Material

## One tablet, four authorities

Otomo Kazetada is a senior minister of the Imperial family. His courtesy is exact, his memory for precedent is long, and he treats Great Clan distrust as one of the beams holding up the Empire rather than an embarrassment to be planed smooth. He asks who profits when an arrangement fails, who records that failure, and whether those two hands share a seal.

## The clause marked for dangerous company

Kazetada reads each provincial office as at least four authorities: public title, daily execution, outside audit, and succession after death, refusal, or excess. He opposes any arrangement that lets one interest control Harvest, Sword, and Accounts together. Emergency service may justify repayment, credit, or a bounded commission; arrival after a disaster does not create permanent title. Compensation and jurisdiction should be written separately.

## Review written in Imperial ink

Kazetada can assign ministry scribes, identify dangerous combinations, supply precedents for Imperial title with specialist execution, and require a narrow, public Imperial review of an exclusive bloc. His marks advise the Throne; they do not allocate a Reed, create a coalition, or decide the Emperor's judgment. Any named holder, term, concession, amendment, or review trigger remains work of the live commission and final audience.
