# Saburo of the Three Crossings

**Reference ID:** `0e8c4594-3da6-403e-b886-424d1e590eb5`  
**Kind:** Person  
**Aliases:** Saburo, Master Saburo  
**Tags:** Reedwater, roads, requisition, transport

## Summary

The master of a crossing association explains when public service bankrupts the carriers and households an open-road order assumes.

## Reference Material

## Receipt, condition, debtor, due date

Saburo is master of an association of carters, ferrymen, hostel keepers, and caravan guarantors. He is a commoner with access to money, routes, and information that samurai governments require but often decline to recognize as political standing. Sponsorship may protect his testimony; it does not make that testimony the sponsor's property.

## Payment a household can use

Saburo asks for condition records, usable valuation, named debtors, operationally timed repayment, and public toll rules. He distinguishes coin, tax credit, replacement property, and trade priority by which household can survive each remedy and how long it must wait. A receipt that cannot be redeemed before a carrier fails is an accurate record of ruin.

## Open road, bounded taking

Saburo can accept requisition, tolls, delayed coin, and clan sponsorship under common rules. He does not speak for every road user, control route priority, or convert an acknowledged loss into payment by naming it debt. Any closure, taking, valuation, debtor, repayment date, manifest, or route order remains live Roads and Accounts state.
