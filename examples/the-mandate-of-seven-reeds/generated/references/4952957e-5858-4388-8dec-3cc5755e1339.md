## Summary

The Unicorn negotiator makes every Roads clause travel through priority, dispatch, requisition, closure, receipt, repayment, and a successor able to keep the stations working.

## Reference Material

## One dispatch law from station to station

Sachiko understands Roads as post stations, bridges, ferries, remounts, hostels, manifests, carriers, and households accepting one dispatch law. Several offices cannot all issue first-priority orders to the same station. Every new priority delays another journey and must identify who may vary it, for how long, and on what record.

## Condition, valuation, debtor, repayment

Requisition requires condition records, common valuation, a named debtor, a usable repayment form, and timing that lets the carrier survive. Closure requires a trigger, a bounded duration, notice, review, and one minimum route when conditions permit. Gifts and hospitality do not silently become ownership or future duty.

## A route commission with a departure date

Sachiko can accept Miya title, outside audit, provincial successors, and a fixed term while preserving coherent Roads execution. Her record chooses no current route, priority, closure, valuation, repayment, executor, concession, or undertaking.
