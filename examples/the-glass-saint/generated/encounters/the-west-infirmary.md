# The West Infirmary

**Role:** necessary  
**Tags:** location, history, witnesses

A working hospice preserves Olyra’s defiance in doors, frames, cloth, and living terms no Vale or trustee may claim wholesale.

## Opening View

“Not the glass,” Caldra Fen says, finishing a bandage. “The people who remember it.” Behind her, clear panes fill the shrine gaps above sleeping patients. The old fever doors preserve both commands: seal us in, cut us out. Survivor names cover one table, paired with questions for a saint who may answer in borrowed words. No family has signed away its testimony, relics, or grief.

## Encounter Material

Caldra finishes a bandage before she discusses the record. She controls the fever doors and every approach to the occupied ward. Ilye Aven keeps the bed-frame tallies and will make rubbings without moving the frames. Tomar Ordel holds a family copy of Olyra’s final instructions and distrusts every Vale, Iria included. The Sorn line appears in the burial register; Tavia’s crime grants neither guilt nor authority to her relatives. Care continues while the city debates its dead.

The evidence remains in use: names cut into bed frames, burial cloth marked by Olyra’s hand, the council order nailed to one fever door, axe cuts opening the other from within, and histories kept by separate households. Empty shrine frames show where Alis removed the colored panes. Edrin’s recent measurements trace a maintenance route toward the winter garden, and his written question—would the families testify if the saint called them?—has Caldra’s answer beneath it: they will testify for Olyra; they will not be commanded by her image.

Commitment costs labor and trust. Caldra speaks if another healer covers the ward and the record stays outside trustee and Vale custody. Ilye brings rubbings and the tally key, never the frames. Tomar reads the family copy if Iria acknowledges the concealment without asking forgiveness in the same breath. Two other families will sign but will not enter the court. Meet those terms to gain authentication, public witnesses, and a West Door delegation. Pressure patients, take objects, or promise vindication from the Glass Saint, and particular witnesses withdraw. The history remains.

Tavia may seek refuge here. If trustee seizure threatens the House, she brings the cut leaf because her ancestor’s name appears on it. Caldra will not hide her from a lawful public charge or hand her into a closed museum transfer. She insists that confession, leaf, and family testimony be heard together. Show Tavia the chapel’s vocal cost or Alis’s warning and she turns toward cooperation. Condemn Edrin alone and she remains unmoved; she already knows the theft was unlawful and judged silence worse.

Late in the day, ordinary window glass begins repeating nearby prayers. The fragments attack no one and answer nothing; they show the rite gathering the city’s language. A completed agreement can send rubbings, testimony, a West Door delegation, or the maintenance route forward, but no patient or original frame leaves the ward. Without investigator agreements, Ilye still sends a rubbing to Nessa Quill and Tomar still tells the West Door families the question has reopened. This place moves on its own terms.

**If skipped or reached late.** Names on a page create no promise of testimony, custody, or a breath-giver from Caldra. West Door families follow commitments already made in the court; otherwise they remain at the hospice or withdraw independently. The doors, copies, empty frames, and maintenance measurements remain. If a bell command reaches the ward, resolve what it does to patients and staff before treating the hospice as uncomplicated support.
