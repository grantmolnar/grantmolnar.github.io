# Vale Manor

**Role:** necessary, end  
**Tags:** location, finale, social, occult

At the winter garden, every copied page, opened door, altered bell, willing witness, and surviving pane determines what Edrin can still make speak.

## Opening View

“You came before the bells,” Edrin Vale says. He stands inside the winter garden’s rope circle with the stolen silver beneath the suspended heartglass and an open dossier at his feet. Old shrine panes cast faces across the servants, the doors, and the witnesses gathering beyond the wall. One hand rests on the inner knot. Under the floor, the bell trunk gives a low tremor, and every pane returns a different face.

**After the bells, the city keeps five reckonings.** **Custody:** name a holder and access rule for every surviving object; split custody may include watch, infirmary, House, museum, or a new trust. **Publication:** distinguish council entry, witnessed reading, distributed dispute, and resealed originals; spoken or copied knowledge persists. **Prosecution:** Edrin answers for conspiracy, receiving stolen civic property, and attempted compulsion; Tavia for sabotage, Lio’s injury, theft, and evidence removal, with cooperation and prevented harm as mitigation; Dorr and participating trustees for concealment and obstruction; Iria politically and professionally for what she knew, withheld, certified, or disclosed. **Surviving compulsion:** mark affected rooms or wards, concrete emergencies, and the institutions responding before dawn. **Olyra’s memory:** record what is authenticated, disputed, fractured, memorialized, exhibited, or managed into another silence.

End when immediate coercion and physical danger are resolved. Close with named custody, testimony, accusations, and one visible public consequence. The city may finally know that Olyra opened the west doors without agreeing what her defiance asks of it now.
