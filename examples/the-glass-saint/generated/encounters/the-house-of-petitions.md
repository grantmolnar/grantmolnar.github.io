# The House of Petitions

**Role:** optional  
**Tags:** location, social, records

Petitioners expose the vow-bed’s contradictory language, judge Tavia’s confession, and decide how evidence will move through the city.

## Opening View

A signed accusation reaches the rear copy window before its author reaches the front desk. Then another follows it. Shelves rise over cures, pardons, debts, births, disappearances, and refusals. Four caucuses argue across one table; copyists mark a warning-bell chart; petitioners press signed sheets into unfamiliar hands. Nessa Quill climbs onto a chair, not to quiet the room, but to keep every sheet moving while the doorway thickens.

## Encounter Material

Four caucuses crowd Nessa Quill’s copy tables, each already moving a different packet. Open Hands want cures, pardons, debt relief, publication, and a stopped peal. Returned Names want answers for the missing and may accept Edrin’s rite. West Door Circle carries infirmary petitions and rejects both the obedience legend and compulsory command. Quiet Keepers fear that street action will close the office. Nessa keeps sheets moving between them; no one gets to make the room desire one ending.

The ledgers show that the vow-bed carries more than names. Its engravings repeat incompatible commands: obey lawful authority, protect children, punish betrayal, forgive debt, return the absent, make the city pure. Edrin tested mirrored silver against different petition sets; the figure’s language changed with the cards. Current copies can weight, contradict, or bound an answering form, but one afternoon cannot overwrite six decades of silver. The practical question is which sheets leave, in whose hands, and whether they can be heard before the bells.

Tavia is a culpable witness in motion. If she arrives unopposed, she brings the cut archive leaf, a bloodied cuff, and Edrin’s order to distribute the record before sunset. In **Defiance**, she admits the theft to Nessa, refuses trustee custody, and withholds the manor service route while she tests whether the investigators mean to bury the pages. Proof of the vocal cost plus custody outside private trustee control moves her to **Cooperation**. She then gives the full transfer statement, names chapel deliveries and the service route, and helps issue a warning, expecting arrest afterward. Under Renn’s public seal she testifies. A closed transfer ordered by Dorr ends her cooperation. Threatened with private seizure, destroyed evidence, or summary violence, she **Flees** to the infirmary with the leaf. Left unpersuaded and uncornered, she distributes copies and later goes to the manor to demand publication before the bell. Sabotage, Lio’s injury, and theft remain hers in every state.

Choose how the pages travel. A **certified reading** joins Senna’s or Caldra’s authentication to Nessa’s copy and creates a hearing Edrin may trust. A **distributed packet** places deposition, trustee minute, and witness statements with several keepers; suppression becomes difficult and rumor accelerates. A **public-custody pledge** names the future holders of records and recovered silver. A **warning assembly** sends petitioners toward belfry or manor, creating witnesses and crowd pressure. A **quiet escrow** protects records while yielding Dorr the first public account.

A seizure order begins a scene. The Quiet Keepers may divide from the other caucuses. Renn can reject private confiscation if the House names custodians and holds no stolen object. Investigators may negotiate split custody, pass copies through rear windows, read the order aloud, or defend the building. Violence wins paper and confirms Edrin’s belief that testimony receives force. If the House fractures, each caucus carries a piece elsewhere: Open Hands to the court, Returned Names toward the manor, West Door Circle to the infirmary, Quiet Keepers into private storage. Failure disperses the route; it never deletes it.

**If skipped or reached late.** Tavia gives Nessa the cut leaf and names the voice risk only if that knowledge has reached her. Open Hands begin public copies, Returned Names carry one packet toward the manor, West Door Circle sends one to the infirmary, and Quiet Keepers hide one off-site. Current petitions enter no apparatus by themselves; someone must carry and place them. A late group finds a constituency distributed across named destinations.
