## Summary

The dead museum founder who replaced Olyra’s heartglass with a devotional copy, hid the deposition, moved the shrine panes home, and left the warning that bell work could make a Vale voice common property.

## Reference Material

## The first concealment

Alis Vale founded the museum after serving as a council archivist. She removed Olyra’s authentic heartglass from the public reliquary, installed the painted devotional copy, hid Olyra’s deposition and the patrons behind the fever-door order, and retained the relic at Vale Manor. She later moved the first shrine’s colored panes from the infirmary into the winter garden.

## Records left behind

Alis’s donor letters, transfer receipt, private ledger, and marginal warning join the museum substitution to the manor apparatus. She called the heartglass a family witness and warned that, when rung through the city, the shrine glass could make a family voice common property. Those records authenticate both the concealment and the danger without turning Alis into a living witness.

## Legacy

Alis’s acts gave later Vales custody, leverage, and liability. Her papers can move Tavia, divide trustees, and expose the vocal cost. They cannot supply current consent, settle ownership, or tell Iria and Edrin what family accountability requires now.
