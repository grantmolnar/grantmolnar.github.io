Iria Vale is the museum curator. She works through accession, disclosure, witness charters, counter-signed copies, and written custody entries. ## Vale accountability

Iria wants the concealed history published, but she belongs to the family that kept Olyra’s heartglass and benefited from the false display. She must make her prior obedience to closed review, family knowledge, custody choices, and use of the museum seal visible as the inquiry advances. A public acknowledgment can unlock household cooperation and sharpen the case against continued concealment; it cannot surrender another Vale’s property or excuse Edrin’s coercive plan.

## Voice risk and limits

The mouth note takes the nearest living Vale inside the winter garden’s outer pane circle. Edrin begins nearest. Iria becomes vulnerable only through her actual position after entry, never merely through kinship or curatorial office. Her charter grants no manor search, compelled testimony, ownership, warrant, or certification against her family. She may speak for herself and her office; independent custody and authentication must come from other hands.
