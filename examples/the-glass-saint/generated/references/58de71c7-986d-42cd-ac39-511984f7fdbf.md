## Summary

The watch captain who can preserve evidence, protect people, detain named suspects, and execute narrow written authority without deciding publication or relic ownership.

## Reference Material

## Office and practice

Captain Ors Renn commands the watch response around the rupture, procession, and manor. He works through named suspects, exact written scope, public seals, custody logs, evacuation, and bounded force. He is an enforcement authority, not an investigator who converts every supported claim into permission.

## Available authority

At the museum Renn can seal the gallery, preserve evidence, and detain a named suspect. With a property-recovery, emergency-entry, or safety-inspection order, he can bring officers through the named route, protect servants and ringers, seal listed objects, and restrain people identified within the order. His public seal can protect Tavia’s testimony and give technical intervention lawful cover.

## Limits

Renn cannot certify Olyra’s history, decide which archive packet investigators should copy, choose publication, transfer ownership, surrender every object to Dorr, or extend a narrow order by convenience. Every action remains bounded by the authority actually written or by an immediate danger he can name. At Vale Manor his exact order determines entry, detention, seizure, evacuation, and the overreach Dorr may later allege.
