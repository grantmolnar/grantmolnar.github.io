## Summary

The senior trustee and procession marshal who protects civic order, institutional custody, and the full peal by turning dangerous evidence back toward sealed process.

## Reference Material

## Office and manner

Helian Dorr is provost, senior museum trustee, and procession marshal. He speaks through schedules, holds, routes, seals, crowd order, and paper that can be entered as the city’s official account. He is not Edrin’s occult partner. His obstruction is procedural, reputational, and material.

## What he wants

Dorr wants the procession preserved, the rupture contained as vandalism and theft, and credible evidence returned to a review he can stage-manage. He fears panic, scandal, loss of institutional custody, and the consequences of admitting that trustees repeated the original concealment. He can accept a short delay, bounded inspection, or divided custody when immediate danger becomes harder to suppress than the record.

## Authority and limits

Dorr controls the trustee agenda, museum containment orders, procession seal, and official peal direction. He may suspend public archive access, request seizure, order holds within civic authority, and place written refusals into the record. He cannot unmake witnessed copies, invent Tavia’s location, erase independent testimony, command private Vale property without process, or make unsupported dismissal survive public authentication. Reports, runners, bells, signed statements, and witnesses define what he knows. His orders remain evidence, including narrow orders, refusals, and obstruction.
