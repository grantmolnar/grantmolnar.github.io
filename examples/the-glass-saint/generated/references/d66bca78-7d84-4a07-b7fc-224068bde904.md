## Reference Material

## The working ward

Caldra Fen runs the hospice in the surviving west wing. She controls patient access, the fever doors, and the conditions under which staff or families leave the ward to testify. Care continues during the investigation; the city’s need for proof never converts patients, frames, cloth, or family memory into unattended evidence.

## Testimony and refuge

Caldra can authenticate Olyra’s work through living testimony, the doors, household copies, and material kept outside Vale and museum custody. She will speak when another healer covers the ward and the record has a protected holder. She may shelter Tavia long enough to join confession, the cut leaf, and family testimony, but she will answer a lawful public charge.

## Limits

Caldra speaks for her ward and her own conditions, not for every survivor family. She will not promise a breath-giver, hand patients into a procession, warehouse recovered relics, or let Olyra’s image command the people whose memory authenticates her defiance.
