## Summary

The keeper of the House of Petitions who keeps incompatible caucuses copying, names public custodians, and turns authenticated pages into a hearing without pretending the House wants one outcome.

## Reference Material

## Keeper of moving sheets

Nessa Quill keeps the House of Petitions. She coordinates copyists, family packets, public readings, rear-window distribution, and named keepers while four caucuses argue over what the record should require. Her work preserves words before institutions engrave, burn, seal, or place them beneath a relic.

## Public custody

Nessa can join an authenticated source to a House copy, distribute a packet among several holders, record a public-custody pledge, or assemble witnesses for the belfry and manor. She needs named custodians and a plan that survives seizure pressure; otherwise each caucus carries its own piece toward court, infirmary, manor, or private storage.

## Caucus limits

Nessa cannot make Open Hands, Returned Names, West Door Circle, and Quiet Keepers desire the same ending. She can keep sheets moving, separate a public record from Edrin’s coercion, and protect Tavia’s statement under open custody. She cannot turn copied petitions into consent for the rite or guarantee that a crowd remains orderly.
