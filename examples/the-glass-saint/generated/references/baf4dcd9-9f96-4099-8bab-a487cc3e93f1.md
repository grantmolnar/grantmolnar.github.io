# The House of Petitions

**Reference ID:** `baf4dcd9-9f96-4099-8bab-a487cc3e93f1`  
**Kind:** Organization  
**Aliases:** House of Petitions, the House  
**Tags:** petitions, copy office, caucuses, public record, distributed custody

## Summary

An independent copy office whose four caucuses preserve petitions, distribute disputed records, and provide public custody without sharing one desired command or finale.

## Reference Material

## Four tables, one ledger

The House preserves words before they are engraved, burned, sealed, or laid beneath civic relics. Open Hands seek cures, pardons, and debt relief. Returned Names seek missing people and may favor an answering saint. West Door Circle carries infirmary memory and rejects compulsory command. Quiet Keepers fear that street action will close the office.

## What survives the window

Its ledgers show what the petition-engraved silver accumulated over six decades. Copyists can authenticate transfer timing, spread the deposition and concealment minute, name custodians, assemble witnesses, and create a public hearing that Edrin can hear before the bells. If seizure splits the room, each caucus carries a surviving packet to a known destination.

## No petition speaks for the whole city

The House can preserve and weight public language; it cannot author a reliable command, consent for living speakers, or a single civic judgment. Current petitions enter the apparatus only when someone carries and places them. The House may hold copies or recovered silver under a named pledge, while changing caucus positions remain on the public-pressure ledger.
