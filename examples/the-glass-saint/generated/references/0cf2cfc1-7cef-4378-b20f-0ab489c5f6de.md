## Summary

The archive registrar who authenticates exact records, logs every order, and protects originals without choosing absent investigators’ packet or recipient for them.

## Reference Material

## Keys, seals, and copies

Senna Drail is registrar of the restricted archive. She controls its keys, shelf marks, custody log, and certification seal. She can identify the substitution ledger, Olyra’s deposition, the modern concealment minute, and the contact records that join Edrin to the bell work. Exact copies carry weight because Senna can show what drawer, seal, gap, recipient, and witness belong to each one.

## What she requires

Senna certifies for a lawful recipient or for investigators prepared to defend her office in public. A watch seal, named public custodian, counter-signed reading, or council entry can meet that need. When a request would expose originals or leave a copy easy to bury, she names the missing recipient, protection, or custody step instead of supplying an off-screen rescue.

## Boundaries

Senna preserves and authenticates records. She does not select the investigators’ priority packet, make private evidence public without a carrier, surrender originals to a crowd, or turn the archive into a faction. If Dorr closes the stair, her log preserves the order and the copies already released; late arrivals inherit that changed access state.
