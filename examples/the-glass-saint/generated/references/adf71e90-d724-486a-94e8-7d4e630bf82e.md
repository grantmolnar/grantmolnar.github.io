# The Grand Belfry

**Reference ID:** `adf71e90-d724-486a-94e8-7d4e630bf82e`  
**Kind:** Place  
**Aliases:** Grand Belfry, the belfry, the crown tower  
**Tags:** bell tower, Grand Belfry, crown bell, answer-pins, relay towers

## Summary

The three-level crown tower that carries witness, mouth, and command notes; its crew, machinery, and crown link govern reach while the operating sheet owns each live rope and pin state.

## Reference Material

## Rope room, wheel loft, crown deck

The ringing room holds ropes, brakes, the peal order, and warning chart. The wheel loft holds moving wheels, counterweights, and three live stage pins. The crown deck holds bell mouths, the tuning ledger, and sight lines to the court and relay towers. Rope lanes, loose weights, and humming metal make position and crew control part of every intervention.

## Three notes and two echoes

The west tenor’s witness pin carries the body note. The silver bell’s mouth pin carries the voice note. The crown pin launches the command. East Gate and River Ward can repeat a crown note that reaches them, but they cannot begin body or mouth stages. Lifting the crown pin protects the city even when earlier stages remain possible.

## What bronze remembers

Jori Kest directs the crew, while Dorr’s sealed order supplies their initial protection. Technical proof plus lawful or public cover can support controlled removal, warning, or retuning. The ritual-and-bell sheet records every sounded note, altered rope, lifted pin, tied branch, relay, injury, and frightened ringer; this record preserves the tower’s stable structure and role.
