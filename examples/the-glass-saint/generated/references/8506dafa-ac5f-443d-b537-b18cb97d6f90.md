# Saint Olyra

**Reference ID:** `8506dafa-ac5f-443d-b537-b18cb97d6f90`  
**Kind:** Person  
**Aliases:** Olyra, the saint of obedience  
**Tags:** Ash Fever, west infirmary, heartglass, public memory, saint

## Summary

An Ash Fever healer whose defiance was recast as obedience; her surviving heartglass carries relic residue, never a recoverable person or final civic judgment.

## Reference Material

## Historical record

Olyra served during the Ash Fever. When the council ordered the west infirmary sealed and refused reinforcement, she opened its doors from within, organized water and burial work among the patients, and died there. Her unsigned deposition, patient indexes, family copies, marked cloth, bed-frame names, and the two fever doors preserve different parts of that act.

## Civic image and surviving material

The later civic cult reversed her defiance into obedience. Alis Vale removed Olyra’s authentic heartglass from the museum reliquary, installed a painted devotional copy, and moved panes from the first infirmary shrine to Vale Manor. The procession, sermons, petitions, relic, and suppressed papers therefore carry competing public Olyras.

## Authority and limits

Olyra is dead and cannot consent to custody, choose a current cause, command the city, or certify what later institutions say in her name. The heartglass contributes residue to Edrin’s working, while the vow-bed, copied records, living petitions, borrowed voice, reflected crowd, and bells contribute the rest. Any Glass Saint is a disputed composite assembled by the living. Survivor families retain authority over their own testimony, relics, grief, and present commitments.
