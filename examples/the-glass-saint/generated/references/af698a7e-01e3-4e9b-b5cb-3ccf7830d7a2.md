## Summary

## Reference Material

## The two fever doors

During the Ash Fever, Olyra broke the council seal from within, opened the west doors, organized water and burial work, and died among the patients. Survivors built the first shrine here. Alis Vale later removed its colored panes to the Vale winter garden, leaving empty frames and a service relationship that can still reveal a maintenance route.

## The ward still working

The west wing remains a working ward under Sister Caldra Fen. Bed-frame names, burial cloth, the nailed order, axe cuts, household copies, and living testimony authenticate what the public relic omits. Ilye Aven keeps the tallies; Tomar Ordel holds family instructions; separate households set their own terms for appearing, signing, or keeping evidence.

## Care before testimony

The infirmary can authenticate Olyra’s defiance, shelter records, trace the shrine panes, and place West Door witnesses into a public hearing. It remains a care site. Patients and frames stay where care requires them, no family becomes a ready-made chorus, and late bell effects must be resolved as hazards to the ward rather than convenient support.
