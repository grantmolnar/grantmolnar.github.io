# Reference Index: The Glass Saint

## People

- [**Saint Olyra**](8506dafa-ac5f-443d-b537-b18cb97d6f90.md) — An Ash Fever healer whose defiance was recast as obedience; her surviving heartglass carries relic residue, never a recoverable person or final civic judgment. Aliases: Olyra, the saint of obedience.
- [**Iria Vale**](d15869c0-3889-4f6e-aa25-b840bfea30b7.md) — The museum curator who opens the independent inquiry and presses for disclosure; her Vale position, prior closed review, museum seal, and possible place inside the mouth circle limit what she may certify, surrender, or safely risk. Aliases: Iria, Curator Vale.
- [**Edrin Vale**](b9e6abc5-6255-46a3-b218-fd7682b47e4c.md) — The principal antagonist: a meticulous Vale historian who exposes a true civic concealment by building a rite that can steal one living voice and compel the city through its bells. Aliases: Edrin, Alis Vale’s grandson.
- [**Tavia Sorn**](ded2083d-b24d-4d98-ad09-b785016fee09.md) — An assistant conservator and culpable accomplice who steals the vow-bed to force publication, then may turn against the rite when she learns that its second note takes a living voice. Aliases: Tavia, Assistant Conservator Sorn.
- [**Provost Helian Dorr**](d656408a-6014-4c49-a264-dee525fbbfdf.md) — The senior trustee and procession marshal who protects civic order, institutional custody, and the full peal by turning dangerous evidence back toward sealed process. Aliases: Helian Dorr, Provost Dorr, Dorr.
- [**Captain Ors Renn**](58de71c7-986d-42cd-ac39-511984f7fdbf.md) — The watch captain who can preserve evidence, protect people, detain named suspects, and execute narrow written authority without deciding publication or relic ownership. Aliases: Captain Renn, Renn.
- [**Registrar Senna Drail**](0cf2cfc1-7cef-4378-b20f-0ab489c5f6de.md) — The archive registrar who authenticates exact records, logs every order, and protects originals without choosing absent investigators’ packet or recipient for them. Aliases: Senna, Registrar Drail.
- [**Sister Caldra Fen**](d66bca78-7d84-4a07-b7fc-224068bde904.md) — The healer who runs the surviving west infirmary, protects patients and survivor families, and offers testimony only with ward coverage and custody beyond trustees and Vales. Aliases: Caldra, Sister Fen.
- [**Nessa Quill**](b29d5f11-8145-4c6a-b5a3-b2f97af88d1b.md) — The keeper of the House of Petitions who keeps incompatible caucuses copying, names public custodians, and turns authenticated pages into a hearing without pretending the House wants one outcome. Aliases: Nessa.
- [**Bellmaster Jori Kest**](ca4d3059-1eac-4b0b-b13b-4115e8751d38.md) — The bellmaster who commands the Grand Belfry crew and can strip, lift, warn, or retune only when technical proof and lawful or public protection reach the tower. Aliases: Jori Kest, Kest, Bellmaster Kest.
- [**Alis Vale**](f418156c-5ad3-4a30-ac15-35051eb07a4d.md) — The dead museum founder who replaced Olyra’s heartglass with a devotional copy, hid the deposition, moved the shrine panes home, and left the warning that bell work could make a Vale voice common property. Aliases: Alis, museum founder.

## Places

- [**The West Infirmary**](af698a7e-01e3-4e9b-b5cb-3ccf7830d7a2.md) — The surviving hospice where Olyra opened the fever doors; its patients, frames, cloth, copies, and families preserve evidence outside the museum and Vale household. Aliases: West Infirmary, the hospice, the west wing.
- [**The Vale Winter Garden**](2496cf62-c44a-4a52-b1dd-8ed40244f0e5.md) — The manor chamber where shrine panes, petition silver, heartglass, mouth knot, and bell trunk meet; routes and custody may change, but its five joined stations define the finale’s physical logic. Aliases: winter garden, Vale Manor winter garden, the garden.
- [**The Grand Belfry**](adf71e90-d724-486a-94e8-7d4e630bf82e.md) — The three-level crown tower that carries witness, mouth, and command notes; its crew, machinery, and crown link govern reach while the operating sheet owns each live rope and pin state. Aliases: Grand Belfry, the belfry, the crown tower.

## Organizations

- [**The House of Petitions**](baf4dcd9-9f96-4099-8bab-a487cc3e93f1.md) — An independent copy office whose four caucuses preserve petitions, distribute disputed records, and provide public custody without sharing one desired command or finale. Aliases: House of Petitions, the House.
