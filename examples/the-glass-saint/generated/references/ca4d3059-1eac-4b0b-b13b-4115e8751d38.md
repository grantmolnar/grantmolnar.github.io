## Summary

The bellmaster who commands the Grand Belfry crew and can strip, lift, warn, or retune only when technical proof and lawful or public protection reach the tower.

## Reference Material

## Crew authority

Jori Kest directs the Grand Belfry. He assigns hands to ropes, stops or resumes work, reads the warning chart, and protects ringers from unexplained machinery. Dorr’s sealed peal order shields the crew until a warrant, signed hold, named public witnesses, or immediate technical danger gives Kest a defensible reason to alter it.

## What he can verify

The chapel strip can identify the linked pins; the pitch table and Mela Fen’s ledger can match stages to bells; Tavia’s statement can establish deliberate sabotage. With one technical proof and lawful or public protection, Kest can organize controlled work: strip all stage pins, lift the crown pin, sound a warning, or retune the third note.

## Limits

Kest controls the belfry and crew, not the heartglass, petition record, Vale household, or final custody. He acts on evidence that reaches him. A sounded note remains part of the ritual history, and every cut rope, lifted pin, tied branch, frightened junior, and altered order carries forward.
