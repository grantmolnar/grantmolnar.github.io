# Encounter Index: The Glass Saint

## The Shattered Gallery (`the-shattered-gallery`)

**Role:** necessary, start

The broken case yields three evidence chains while museum, watch, and trustee authority compete to seal them first.

## The Procession Court (`the-procession-court`)

**Role:** necessary

A collapsing civic rehearsal turns Tavia’s transfer into partial public testimony and forces an early choice between pursuit, protection, and publication.

## The Archive Vault (`the-archive-vault`)

**Role:** necessary

Four record packets prove substitution, suppressed history, modern concealment, and ritual contact—but custody determines what each proof can do.

## The Trustees’ Chamber (`the-trustees-chamber`)

**Role:** optional

A divided hearing converts distinct kinds of proof into warrants, delay, public record, or a signed refusal that can travel farther.

## The West Infirmary (`the-west-infirmary`)

**Role:** necessary

A working hospice preserves Olyra’s defiance in doors, frames, cloth, and living terms no Vale or trustee may claim wholesale.

## The House of Petitions (`the-house-of-petitions`)

**Role:** optional

Petitioners expose the vow-bed’s contradictory language, judge Tavia’s confession, and decide how evidence will move through the city.

## The Bell Chapel (`the-bell-chapel`)

**Role:** necessary

A rehearsal already speaking in borrowed voices reveals body, mouth, and reach; strip, cord, and deposition can answer the peal at different costs.

## The Grand Belfry (`the-grand-belfry`)

**Role:** necessary

Every rope is a public choice: the divided ringers can silence, confine, warn, or carry the rite, and bronze remembers what they do.

## Vale Manor (`vale-manor`)

**Role:** necessary, end

At the winter garden, every copied page, opened door, altered bell, willing witness, and surviving pane determines what Edrin can still make speak.
