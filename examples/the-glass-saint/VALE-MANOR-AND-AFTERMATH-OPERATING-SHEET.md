## Body, voice, and reach

### Before the witness note

Available actions include negotiation, inventory, watch seals, zone-by-zone dismantling, speaker repositioning, counterkey preparation, evacuation, and custody transfer. The apparatus is not yet a creature or combatant.

### Witness note

**Requires:** intact witness branch, heartglass, vow-bed, and at least four joined pane frames.

**Effect:** a translucent outline forms and recites incompatible petition fragments. It cannot recognize people, reveal unknown facts, or answer questions.

**Still actionable:** seal or remove heartglass; separate vow-bed; release enough panes; tie off mouth or crown branch; negotiate; prepare bounded answer.

### Mouth note

**Requires:** intact mouth branch and a Vale inside the outer pane circle touching or exposed to the closed inner knot.

**Effect:** nearest Vale loses natural voice; body uses that timbre without will or memory.

**Prevention:** open, cut, or reverse knot; remove every Vale from circle; stop mouth branch before completion.

**After completion:** move speaker to safety, stop crown, or prepare pre-dawn voice restoration. Carrying the speaker away does not itself restore speech.

### Bounded answering form

**Requires:** all three counterkey parts, Olyra's deposition, intact heartglass and vow-bed, a willing breath-giver, witnessed consent, reversed mouth knot, and crown launch prevented or confined.

**Limits:** no more than three questions; answers draw only from physically present records and petitions; no unknown facts; no self-certification as Olyra. Conflict appears as contradiction, silence, or irrelevant petition language.

**Cost:** willing speaker is mute until dawn; no involuntary permanent voice seizure.

### Crown note

| Belfry state | Range |
|---|---|
| Crown pin removed or crown branch stopped | No launched command |
| Retuned crown | Winter garden and immediate west lawn only |
| Crown intact; one relay intact | Winter garden plus that relay's wards |
| Crown and both relays intact | Procession route and connected wards |

After launch, a command already heard cannot be recalled. Remaining relays can still be stopped before they answer.

## The stolen voice before dawn

A completed involuntary mouth transfer persists after manifestation.

**Required:** original speaker's written consent, intact heartglass, reversal cord, mouth-pitch cue, and one independent witness.

**Working:** cover or empty the vow-bed; place the sealed heartglass above it; tie the inner line outward; let the speaker affirm or refuse by writing or gesture; sound or hum the mouth-pitch cue through the tuning strip.

**Result:** the natural voice returns and the heartglass becomes acoustically inert.

**Lasting loss:** heartglass destroyed after transfer, reversal cord unavailable, dawn passes, or speaker refuses. Do not repair the consequence automatically because Edrin surrendered or the crown was stopped.
