# Party Design: The Counterseal Witnesses

The Counterseal Witnesses are one demonstrated group assembled under Iria Vale’s emergency charter. They are not named in the authoritative adventure, and fresh tables need not reproduce these roles. Their purpose is to test whether four people with different practical habits can divide civic, mechanical, evidentiary, and human work without becoming a universal expert committee.

## Aven Rook

Aven is a civic copyist who left the council record office after being ordered to summarize a housing inquiry without naming the families displaced by it. Aven knows seals, copy chains, docket thresholds, and the difference between a document that is true and a document that can survive institutional denial.

Aven’s repeated question is: **Who can authenticate this, who can hold it, and who can still read it tomorrow?**

Aven cannot settle whether a witness should speak, whether a custodian should accept an object, or whether certification settles a record’s moral interpretation.

## Mira Thane

Mira is a former watch investigator now working as an independent fire and structural examiner. She reads damage direction, tool marks, sight lines, crowd movement, and the limits of a scene after too many authorities have handled it.

Mira’s repeated question is: **What happened here, and which part of that conclusion survives contamination?**

Mira can define a theft packet or safe search. She cannot turn physical evidence into a warrant by herself or treat suspicion as guilt.

## Pell Orison

Pell repairs hoists, civic bells, stage machinery, and warehouse counterweights. He understands ropes under load, crew trust, brake posts, moving bell lanes, and why a dangerous improvised bind is not the same operation as safe live-pin removal.

Pell’s repeated question is: **What is moving, what bears the load, and what can be stopped without pretending we have the missing tool?**

Pell can explain and perform mechanical intervention. He does not control Jori Kest’s crew, choose the procession’s meaning, or certify occult history.

## Lysa Ord

Lysa organizes patient advocates and public witnesses for people who cannot safely appear before a council alone. She distinguishes publicity from protection, testimony from rumor, and a crowd’s presence from consent to be placed in danger.

Lysa’s repeated question is: **Who is being asked to bear this in public, and what have they actually agreed to?**

Lysa can help witnesses state and preserve commitments. She does not speak for the West Door families, Tavia, petition caucuses, or any absent person.

## Demonstrated division of work

The group divides by task rather than by permanent narrative function.

- Mira and Pell stabilize the Gallery while Aven and Lysa preserve custody and witness lines.
- Aven leads archive prioritization, but Senna chooses what she can lawfully certify.
- Lysa helps the Procession Court witnesses give separate statements and refuses to turn the West Door families into automatic allies.
- Pell works with Kest and Mela at the Belfry, but names the missing tuning strip and accepts a rougher manual bind.
- All four negotiate at the Manor. Edrin’s state changes because of public anchors, custody, and witnesses—not because one party member is “the face.”

## Why this route is useful

The demonstration follows a failed-warrant public route and skips the West Infirmary and Bell Chapel. The Counterseal Witnesses therefore lack two bodies of evidence and several powerful operations. Their competencies do not compensate for those omissions.

They reach a successful but bounded result: a witness-only manifestation, no stolen voice, no launched command, divided custody, public records, named prosecutions, and unresolved civic meaning. Another table could choose direct pursuit, a full counterkey route, covert entry, destruction, bounded answering, or even a permitted full awakening.
