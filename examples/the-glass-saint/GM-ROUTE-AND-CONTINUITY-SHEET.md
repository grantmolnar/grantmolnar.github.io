# GM Route and Continuity Sheet: The Glass Saint

Use this sheet beside the public-pressure, ritual, and manor operating sheets. It tracks route-open play without reducing the adventure to one clock or one influence score.

## Four separate records

| Record | Question | Typical entries |
|---|---|---|
| Access | Where can investigators physically go, and by what route? | charter access, public square, warrant scope, family entry, service route, maintenance passage, direct force |
| Knowledge | What do investigators know, and how strongly? | lead, working conclusion, publicly durable claim |
| Authority and public record | What may someone lawfully or credibly do? | counter-signed copy, watch seal, signed witness, warrant, council entry, written refusal, public reading |
| Ritual state | What still functions? | witness/mouth/crown pins, relay pins, counterkey parts, knot state, heartglass, vow-bed, pane crown, speaker position |

Do not use one record as a substitute for another.

## Evidence strength

- **Lead:** one clue identifies a person, place, object, or hazard worth acting on.
- **Working conclusion:** one strong direct observation or two compatible clues support table decisions and targeted intervention.
- **Publicly durable claim:** authenticated material, independent corroboration, named testimony, or a public demonstration makes quiet denial costly.

A revelation may be established for play before it is publicly durable. Record the limit.

## Split-team reunion report

When groups reunite, each reports four things:

1. **Observed:** what they directly saw, heard, copied, or tested.
2. **Changed:** what object, route, order, witness state, custody, or bell state they altered.
3. **Committed:** who consented, signed, accepted custody, promised testimony, or refused.
4. **Inferred:** what remains a theory, lead, or uncorroborated claim.

No absent NPC accepts terms. No object moves twice. No off-screen team learns information without a messenger, public bell, written copy, or reunion.

## Off-screen continuity

### Shattered Gallery

Ossa inventories; Renn seals; Dorr may trustee-seal unclaimed originals. Lio remains a witness. Existing copies and notes persist.

### Procession Court

Tavia reaches the House unless intercepted. Edrin’s carriage continues to the manor. Witnesses disperse to their named destinations. The procession advances.

### Archive Vault

Senna protects originals and logs orders. Dorr may close the public stair. No priority packet is copied or distributed without player action.

### Trustees’ Chamber

Dorr preserves containment and the full peal based on reports actually received. Signed orders and refusal remain evidence. Other trustees retain their divisions.

### West Infirmary

Caldra and families make no automatic commitment. Existing court commitments persist. Physical records and access measurements remain.

### House of Petitions

Tavia gives Nessa the cut leaf. Unless investigators intervene, caucuses disperse copies toward the court, manor, infirmary, and private storage. No packet enters the apparatus automatically.

### Bell Chapel

The three counterkey parts remain where Edrin left them unless moved. He does not return for them. Late recovery remains note-specific and useful.

### Grand Belfry

Kest follows Dorr’s latest valid order absent contrary proof or protection. Mela remains with the ledger, Karel presses for the full peal, and the pins sound only when the crew and manor are physically ready.

## Event pressure

Advance only when a visible act occurs.

1. Gallery rupture and public confusion.
2. Dorr receives a public accusation or request and issues containment.
3. Edrin completes manor assembly and Dorr authorizes the final bell test.
4. Procession takes position when its hold ends, fails, or is overridden.
5. Witness note sounds through an intact witness pin and ready crew.
6. Mouth note sounds only if Edrin closes the inner knot around a valid Vale speaker.
7. Crown note launches only through an intact crown pin and sounding crown bell; relays answer only if still fitted.

Write the trigger beside each advance. Do not advance because the table visited “too many” encounters.

## Failed-action doctrine

A failed action changes one bounded thing:

- time or procession position;
- one evidence packet’s custody or contamination;
- one witness’s willingness, safety, or public exposure;
- one order’s scope;
- one counterkey component;
- one bell, rope, pin, or crew position;
- one door or route’s legitimacy or danger; or
- one negotiation state.

It never deletes every route, invents consent, erases copied evidence, grants an antagonist omniscience, or converts an optional institution into a mandatory gate.

## Finale checklist

Before Edrin speaks, record:

- manor door or route;
- Iria and Tavia locations;
- public anchors;
- proposed custody for heartglass, vow-bed, dossier, panes, and counterkey;
- witness, mouth, crown, and relay states;
- warning peal state;
- exact evidence held and where copies exist;
- Renn’s written authority;
- people present to witness or accept custody;
- old engraving imperative, current petition imperative, and most repeated public imperative.

After immediate danger ends, record separately:

- body formation;
- voice transfer and restoration;
- command and range;
- object condition and custody;
- publication state;
- prosecution and inquiry;
- injuries and detentions;
- surviving compulsion; and
- Olyra’s public memory.
