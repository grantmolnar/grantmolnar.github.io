# Design Notes: The Glass Saint

## A civic lie is not repaired by a supernatural verdict

The historical core should remain clear. Saint Olyra defied the council, opened the sealed west infirmary, and died among people the city had chosen to abandon. The later cult made her a saint of obedience because that version preserved civic authority.

Edrin is correct about the concealment and wrong about the remedy. The adventure should never imply that the city merely needs a more authentic oracle. The Glass Saint cannot settle the historical or moral dispute because it is built from relic residue, public petitions, documentary fragments, and the expectations of the people who assembled it.

A bounded answering rite may produce useful testimony, emotional recognition, or a new disputed source. It does not produce the author’s final answer.

## Public process is part of the adventure’s machinery

The mystery concerns evidence, but evidence only matters through custody, witnesses, copies, warrants, hearings, sermons, petitions, and bells. These are not administrative interludes between the real supernatural scenes. They are the means by which the city decides what can become common knowledge.

Investigators can convert discoveries into different forms of leverage:

- a lawful warrant;
- protected archive copies;
- a same-day council entry;
- named public witnesses;
- a warning peal;
- petitioners gathered at the manor;
- an authenticated record outside Vale and museum custody; or
- direct physical access without institutional support.

No one route supplies all of them, and none is a prerequisite for action.

## The antagonistic roles remain distinct

Edrin Vale is the principal antagonist because he knowingly risks erasing a living voice and compelling a city. His grievance and evidence remain sound. He prefers argument, accepts personal cost, and may surrender to a credible public hearing. Those qualities make the choice harder; they do not make coercion harmless.

Provost Helian Dorr is not secretly an occult conspirator. He protects the procession, the museum, and civic order at the expense of people whose evidence is inconvenient. His resistance should be procedural, reputational, and material. He can be exposed, persuaded, divided from other trustees, bypassed, or forced to make his obstruction public.

Tavia Sorn helped plan the rupture and removed the vow-bed. She is culpable. She also diverges from Edrin by insisting that the record reach the public before the rite. Evidence of the vocal cost can change her conduct because she did not knowingly accept it. Cooperation does not erase theft, injury, or sabotage.

Iria Vale is neither a neutral patron nor a disguised villain. She gives investigators access and wants the truth published, but her office, family, and prior obedience to closed review constrain her authority. Her choices must become public as the adventure progresses.

## The full adventure remains route-open

The encounters are not a compulsory tour. Investigators may follow the Vale carriage directly from the opening and reach the manor with little knowledge, weak public support, and no precise countermeasure. That route is dangerous and consequential, never forbidden.

Other investigators may build a public case, secure the bells, recruit witnesses, find Tavia, or authenticate Olyra’s history before confronting Edrin. The reward is not permission to enter the finale. It is a richer set of actions that the finale materially recognizes.

Skipped encounters continue to exist. Dorr can issue orders, Tavia can move, petitioners can gather, the bells can sound, and Edrin can prepare without waiting for investigators to arrive.

## The bell clock should create events, not bookkeeping

The procession supplies a deadline, but the GM should advance visible situations rather than deduct abstract units after every conversation. The intended escalation is event-based:

1. the museum rupture and public confusion;
2. trustee containment and copied records entering the crowd;
3. answer-pins beginning to resonate in lesser towers;
4. the procession taking position and the first long note;
5. the second note taking a Vale voice; and
6. the third note carrying a command through every prepared bell.

The completed operating sheets tie each movement to a visible trigger and record what can delay, redirect, or localize it. Material changes persist. A removed answer-pin stays removed. A copied record remains copied. A witness who has spoken publicly cannot be made private again by simple fiat.

## The manifestation’s language comes from actual play

The Glass Saint’s command should be drawn from the petitions, slogans, public arguments, and interventions established during the session. The likely families of command include obedience, confession, opening doors, forgiving debts, returning the absent, protecting children, punishing betrayal, or refusing abandonment.

Each can produce aid and harm. The command is not selected to reward the players for guessing the GM’s politics. It is a material consequence of what the city has asked its saint to be.

## Governing voice

Prefer museum, legal, liturgical, domestic, and mechanical language over generalized occult fog. Use accession numbers, cracked wax, copied leaves, bell ropes, window frames, petition grooves, public seals, carriage felt, and breath. The supernatural should become legible through changes in those objects.

The adventure should remain emotionally restrained around Olyra. The dead woman is not available to endorse a faction cleanly. Living people carry grief, evidence, ambition, fear, and responsibility in different proportions.

## Completed expansion boundary

The first expansion session established the route skeleton and provisional encounter procedures. Sessions 2 through 5 completed the civic investigation, bell system, manor finale, route continuity, and fresh-play demonstration. The later clue-density, encounter-introduction, and voice passes refined that completed architecture without reopening its structural assumptions.

## Authority is a stack of limited powers

The emergency witness charter, museum custody, watch seal, trustee order, council record, and public witness are not interchangeable forms of permission. Each can do something the others cannot.

The charter opens museum-controlled spaces and protects counter-signed observation. It does not compel testimony or authorize a manor search. The watch can preserve evidence, detain a named theft suspect, and execute a narrow warrant. The trustees can issue that warrant, delay the procession, or enter records into the civic docket. Senna can authenticate documents. Nessa and the infirmary families can make evidence public and independently held. No one office should become a universal key.

This division lets investigators assemble authority, bypass it, or expose its refusal. It also prevents a failed hearing from becoming a dead end: written denial, partial delay, belfry inspection, split custody, and public testimony are all meaningful intermediate outcomes.

## Public pressure consists of persistent facts

Do not reduce the city to a favor score. Track five concrete facts instead:

- how far the real cause of the rupture has spread;
- where authenticated copies now exist;
- what warrant or emergency authority the watch possesses;
- what the procession is presently doing; and
- which named witnesses have committed to speak.

These facts can move in different directions. Investigators may obtain a warrant while losing public trust, distribute records while weakening chain of custody, or keep the court calm while Dorr controls the first account. Once created, the facts persist. Copies do not vanish because the trustees regain a room. A signed denial remains signed. A witness who speaks in public cannot be returned to private ignorance.

## Witnesses have conditions, not hit points

Lio Marr needs treatment and protection from pressure. Senna Drail needs a lawful recipient and protection from dismissal. Caldra Fen needs coverage for the hospice and custody outside the museum and Vale family. Ilye Aven will authenticate rubbings but not surrender bed frames. Tomar Ordel requires a public Vale acknowledgment. Nessa Quill needs named custodians before she can keep the petitioner caucuses together.

Failure with one witness should change that person’s commitment and the form of available evidence. It should not erase the underlying route or silently substitute another identical witness.

## Tavia remains culpable in every disposition

Tavia’s divergence from Edrin concerns publication, custody, and the vocal cost. It does not undo the planned sabotage, the injured docent, or the stolen silver. She may cooperate, testify, flee private custody, or demand a public hearing without becoming innocent.

Conversely, refusing to cooperate does not turn her into a second mastermind. Her default work is distributing evidence. When threatened, she moves toward public or infirmary custody rather than developing a new coercive plan. Her state changes what investigators can learn and whom they can bring to the manor; it does not replace Edrin as the principal antagonist.

## The bell network has separable stages

The answer-pins are not interchangeable ritual batteries. The witness, mouth, and crown pins perform different actions, while the two lesser-tower pins only relay a command already launched by the crown bell. This distinction supports partial success and prevents the lesser towers from becoming invisible backup copies of the whole rite.

A belfry intervention should state which stage it changes. Preventing city-wide compulsion is not the same as preventing the glass body, protecting a Vale voice, or resolving the relic’s custody. Conversely, arriving after the first note does not make the tower irrelevant. Unsounded stages remain actionable.

## Counterkeys provide capability, not permission

The chapel working set has three parts because ritual knowledge should be divisible and losable without turning into a binary quest token. The tuning strip supports safe removal. The reversal cord supports the physical knot. The pitch table and deposition cues support alignment and bounded answering.

Possessing the set does not make Jori, Dorr, the watch, or petitioners agree with its use. Technical proof, lawful cover, public protection, and physical control remain separate resources.

## Pressure advances through acts and channels

Do not advance the rite because a generic clock loses a segment. Advance it when Dorr issues an order, Edrin completes the apparatus, the bell crew takes position, Edrin closes the inner knot, or the crown bell actually moves. These acts create visible opportunities for intervention.

Edrin and Dorr receive information only through named channels. Runners, watch reports, petition messengers, bell signals, and public witnesses can carry news. Private discoveries do not become antagonist knowledge merely because the GM knows them. Bounded responses should follow what each actor learns, wants, and can materially do before sunset.

## Physical changes persist across scenes

Removed pins, cut ropes, damaged stairs, convinced ringers, sounded warnings, and broken orders remain part of the adventure state. Dorr can send officers and Edrin can adapt to a local rite, but neither can restore a bell network between scenes by authorial convenience.

The same rule applies to partial failure. A damaged counterkey narrows operations. A frightened ringer leaving changes crew requirements. A launched command cannot be recalled, though intact relays may still be prevented from repeating it.

## The manor receives state; it does not reset it

Vale Manor should begin by transferring the public and ritual facts already created in play. Removed pins, signed denials, distributed copies, injured witnesses, public commitments, and altered procession routes remain true. The finale is not a clean arena in which earlier work becomes flavor text.

Each approach should change access, legitimacy, witnesses, custody, and physical position. A warrant is not the same as Iria's family claim. A service route is not the same as the infirmary maintenance passage. Public arrival gives Edrin the hearing he demanded while placing more people inside the range of a confined command. Direct force remains possible and should remain materially dangerous rather than morally forbidden.

## The household is not a cult or combat squad

Edrin owns the coercive choice. The household may believe the concealment was wrong, protect servants and property, or have been misled about the apparatus without becoming his minions. Leva Orm and Peren Holt have exact jobs, information, and breakpoints. Evacuated servants are people, witnesses, and liabilities—not encounter balance.

This keeps the principal conflict on evidence, coercion, and public process. Violence can occur, but it should arise from player action, watch intervention, machinery, crowds, or an actual attempt to preserve the rite rather than a default room of loyal guards.

## The mouth cost follows geometry, not authorial convenience

The ordinary mouth stage targets the nearest living Vale inside the outer pane circle when the note completes. Edrin begins on the speaker mark and therefore bears the intended cost. Iria is endangered only by her actual position or by an intervention that removes Edrin while leaving her inside. A non-Vale cannot become the involuntary substitute because the finale needs a victim.

The bounded answering form is separate. It uses the reversed mouth knot, complete counterkey, witnessed consent, and a willing breath-giver. Its temporary muteness and limited answers are the cost of a chosen procedure, not a disguised version of Edrin's coercion.

## Finale outcomes are orthogonal records

Record body, voice, command, range, relic condition, documentary custody, witnesses, injuries, and legal responsibility separately. Stopping the crown note can still leave a mute Vale. Destroying the heartglass can end coercion while destroying unique evidence and the only pre-dawn voice-restoration route. A witnessed surrender can preserve every object while leaving prosecution and institutional reform unresolved.

No ending is canonical merely because it preserves the most material. No destructive ending is automatically honest merely because it prevents future misuse. The source should make the consequences legible and let the table choose.

## Aftermath does not collapse accountability

Edrin, Tavia, Dorr, the trustees, Iria, the museum, bell crew, and watch may all face consequences, but for different conduct. Historical truth does not excuse sabotage or compulsion. Cooperation and prevented harm mitigate without erasing culpability. Institutional concealment may produce inquiry, removal, or reform without being retroactively rewritten as occult conspiracy.

Olyra's history can become authenticated while her motives, theology, and present meaning remain disputed. The Glass Saint is a new event for the city to interpret, not a final witness that closes the old one.

## Access, knowledge, authority, and ritual state remain separate

Route-open play needs four records. Access says where investigators can physically go and by which route. Knowledge says what they can support and whether it is a lead, working conclusion, or publicly durable claim. Authority says what a charter, seal, warrant, witness, custodian, or public record permits. Ritual state says what material mechanisms still function.

Do not let one category silently grant another. A clue does not create a warrant. A warrant does not authenticate a claim. Physical access does not supply a counterkey. A stopped note does not settle custody.

When investigators divide, each returning team reports what it observed, what it changed, who committed or refused, and what remains inferred. No absent person accepts a proposal, and no NPC or object appears in two scenes without travel and elapsed time.

## Unvisited encounters continue through fixed channels

The city does not pause outside the current encounter. Tavia moves from the court to the House unless intercepted. Edrin’s carriage continues to the manor. Dorr acts only on reports, accusations, and requests that reach him. Senna protects and logs the archive rather than choosing absent investigators’ evidence for them. Petition caucuses carry copies to named destinations. The chapel counterkey remains where it was left. The belfry crew follows the latest order and proof actually presented.

Late arrival changes available witnesses, custody, legitimacy, procession position, or ritual stage. It never empties a location, erases evidence, or grants an antagonist knowledge without a messenger, report, bell, copy, or witness.

## A demonstration is not a canonical route

The Counterseal Witnesses demonstrate one failed-warrant public route while skipping the Infirmary and Chapel. Their names, route, clue discoveries, and witness-only outcome are subordinate validation artifacts. Any later revision must improve the authoritative adventure for fresh investigators and update the demonstration when necessary. The adventure never bends to preserve that journal.
