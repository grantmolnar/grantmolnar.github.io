# Public Pressure and Witness Ledger

This aid operates the civic half of *The Glass Saint*. It tracks durable facts created by play. It is not a score, a mandatory sequence, or a substitute for recording what actually happened.

## Five public facts

| Fact | Initial state | Possible later states | Immediate use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rupture account | **Contained:** Dorr calls it vandalism | **Disputed:** the stolen vow-bed and Vale carriage are named; **Named:** Olyra’s deposition and trustee delay are public; **Organized:** named people have committed to a reading or hearing | Determines whether Dorr can define the event privately and whether Edrin believes the city is already listening |
| Archive copies | **None outside the vault** | **Working copy; Certified packet; Split custody; Distributed packet; Council record** | Determines authenticity, survivability under seizure, and the credibility of a hearing |
| Watch authority | **Scene seal only** | **Named-suspect detention; Property-recovery warrant; Belfry inspection; Emergency manor entry** | Determines what Renn can do openly and which officers will accompany investigators |
| Procession status | **Full published order** | **Quarter-hour hold; West Door withdrawal; Warning announced; Route accelerated; Public assembly moving toward manor** | Determines the visible deadline, crowd location, and who reaches the finale |
| Testimony | **No commitments** | Record named commitments separately; written, chamber, procession-court, and manor testimony are distinct | Determines whether records are merely possessed or already part of a public hearing |

Never move a fact backward without a specific event. A seized certified packet may become inaccessible, but any distributed copies and witness knowledge still exist. A procession hold can expire, but the written order remains evidence.

## Evidence custody

| Custody form | Strength | Cost or vulnerability | Strongest immediate effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| In-place watch seal | Clean physical chain | Evidence remains at the museum and may be delayed by trustee order | Theft warrant and later prosecution |
| Ossa Pell handling with investigator countersignature | Precise movement record | Still museum custody | Preserves sabotage packets while allowing examination |
| Senna Drail certified copy | Strong documentary authentication | Risks Senna’s office and can be challenged if the original is sealed | Independent authentication and council entry |
| Watch-sealed copy packet | Strong lawful use | May be inaccessible during a trustee dispute | Warrant or emergency order |
| House of Petitions escrow | Public, named, hard to suppress | Dorr calls it partisan; Returned Names may push toward the rite | Credible public hearing and Edrin negotiation |
| West Infirmary escrow | Independent historical credibility | Must not burden patients or remove protected objects | Corroborates Olyra’s deposition |
| Distributed packet | Survives seizure | Chain weakens unless every copy is signed | Makes suppression impossible and raises public pressure |
| Same-day council record | Public and legally recognized | Requires proof and witnesses; does not itself stop the rite | Meets Edrin’s publication condition most directly |

Investigators may split packets. There is no requirement that all evidence share one custodian.

## Proof categories and authority outputs

1. **Theft:** missing vow-bed plus Vale transfer evidence or Tavia’s admission.
2. **Imminent danger:** chapel notes, a live answer-pin, or another demonstration of peal-borne compulsion.
3. **Independent authentication:** Senna’s chain, infirmary material evidence, or authenticated House copies under a named custodian.
4. **Public witness:** two counter-signed court statements, a petitioner commitment, or a witness prepared to speak in the chamber.

| Proof combination | Available output |
|---|---|
| Theft + Vale destination | Narrow property-recovery warrant |
| Imminent danger + independent authentication | Belfry inspection and one-quarter-hour procession hold |
| Theft + imminent danger | Emergency manor entry and watch support |
| Independent authentication + public witness | Same-day council record and protected reading |
| Any three categories | Combined warrant, procession hold, and public record |
| All four categories | Trustee division; continued obstruction enters the public record |

A denied request creates a signed denial. Record which proof the trustees rejected and who supported the refusal.

## Factions and breakpoints

### Museum staff

- **Iria Vale** wants publication and access, but must choose whether to retain the museum seal or speak as a Vale witness.
- **Senna Drail** protects authentication procedure rather than institutional reputation.
- **Ossa Pell** protects the collection and the custody log. She can oppose both Dorr and reckless handlers.

### Trustees

- **Helian Dorr:** accepts bounded delay and split custody; resists uncontrolled publication.
- **Irena Moss:** supports relic recovery and divided physical custody; resists crowd access to originals.
- **Halwen Gorse:** supports delay only when danger to the procession is demonstrated.
- **Maelin Tress:** supports public entry once the history is independently authenticated.

### House of Petitions caucuses

- **Open Hands:** publish and stop the peal.
- **Returned Names:** allow the saint to answer; may move toward the manor.
- **West Door Circle:** publish the infirmary history and reject compulsion.
- **Quiet Keepers:** preserve copies and avoid a closure order.

### West Infirmary families

The Fen, Aven, Ordel, and Sorn lines share history, not policy. Record commitments household by household.

## Tavia state

Choose the state produced by play; do not advance her along a scripted track.

- **Unlocated:** moving from the Procession Court to the House with the cut archive leaf.
- **Shadowed:** leads observers to the House and its entrances.
- **Intercepted:** admits cutting the vow-bed free and demands public custody.
- **Defiant at the House:** distributes evidence but withholds the manor route.
- **Cooperative:** shown the vocal cost and a credible non-private custody plan; gives full testimony and route information.
- **Watch detained:** testifies while held under Renn’s seal; private transfer orders threaten cooperation.
- **At the infirmary:** fled threatened private seizure with the cut leaf; Caldra requires a public hearing.
- **Moving to the manor:** no credible hearing exists and she intends to demand publication before the bell, not assist the compulsion.

In every state, record her responsibility for planned sabotage, Lio’s injury, theft, and evidence distribution. Cooperation mitigates present danger; adjudication still follows.

## Failure-forward route tests

| Route | Strong success | Failure-forward result |
|---|---|---|
| Social court investigation | Counter-signed transfer chain and protected witnesses | One witness leaves, one statement survives, and the crowd’s rumor state changes |
| Lawful trustee route | Warrant, hold, and public record | Signed denial, partial authority, or a named trustee defection |
| Covert archive route | Fast access to selected packet | Information survives, chain is disputed, and Senna’s position changes |
| Public exposure | Organized reading and distributed custody | Rumor accelerates, procession response hardens, but suppression becomes impossible |
| Tavia pursuit | Interception or cooperative shadowing | Tavia reaches the House or infirmary with the cut leaf; route continues elsewhere |
| Witness recruitment | Named in-person testimony | Written statement, object rubbing, or family-level rather than individual commitment |
| Direct manor pursuit | Arrival before public machinery advances | Weak custody, no hearing, and little authority—but the finale remains reachable |
