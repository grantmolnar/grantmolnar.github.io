# The Chapel of the First Survey

**Role:** necessary  
**Tags:** rural, historic, exploration

Surveyor families guard the doctrine, route keys, and dead that Veyr wants to use without remembering.

## Opening View

Water climbs the chapel floor while the surveyor families carry mortar, lamp oil, and name boards into the crypt. Anja Veil means to seal the founder’s tomb before Sergeant Venn’s warrant arrives; Tomas cuts the wet mortar out as quickly as she lays it. Six lamps pull in different directions. A coffin knocks below. Anja sets the crypt key beside a blank custody agreement. The approaching riders can already see that no one has signed it.

## Encounter Material

## Situation

Water lifts through the crypt joints while surveyor families carry mortar, lamp oil, and name boards below. The crypt is both archive and burial place. Anja means to seal the founder's tomb before agents arrive with a warrant for "obsolete civic instruments"; Tomas cuts out the wet mortar as fast as she lays it. Six lamps pull apart, and another pulse may drop a burial aisle before custody is settled.

Dast's plans name the chapel as the last First Survey repository, not what survives there. The warrant seeks the missing doctrine before outside readers find it; it does not show that Dast knows the safe sequence. The families know official interest usually takes more than it returns.

## Cast

- **Keeper Anja Veil**, elected by the surveyor families, is stern and exact about custody. She wants Veyr to name what it did to the Surveyors' Office.
- **Tomas Veil**, her nephew, wants the records used now and regards Anja's caution as another surrender.
- **Sergeant Korr Venn**, leading the seizure detail, has orders to take bronze instruments and burn contaminated papers. He believes he is containing panic.
- The **recorded voices of the first surveyors** survive in resonant memorial plates. They are not ghosts; correct tones wake stored answers from bronze.

## Immediate pressures

The lower crypt floods from below. Another strong pulse will bring down one burial aisle and crush its archive niches. Korr arrives before nightfall. Anja will not open the founder's tomb without terms for custody, attribution, copies, and the handling of human remains.

Desecration is the quickest entrance and the quickest way to destroy context, allies, and interpretation. Durable access comes through consent, service to the chapel, or discovery of the surveyors' non-funerary sequence.

## Running the encounter

The crypt is a compact problem of tone, water, masonry, and custody. Six tomb aisles correspond to the six sounding lines. Bronze rods in the memorial floor can be tuned against the current pulse. Correct alignment opens archive niches and releases brief recorded statements. Force amplifies the vibration, cracks the masonry, and sends coffins from their shelves.

The records name the Bell as both warning instrument and pressure-control device. They also preserve the division of knowledge. After the Surveyors' Office was dissolved, Waters retained auxiliary load controls and partial service diagrams; the chapel families kept the six-line sequence, route keys, and doctrine for resting, retuning, or sacrificing a line. Neither archive can operate the system alone.

The bronze remembers why the office ended. During the Night of Broken Foundations, surveyors vented a collapse beneath the Tanneries to save central Veyr. Sixty-three people died. The council condemned the judgment, dissolved the office, and quietly kept the machinery. The descendants do not call the decision clean. They refuse to let the city remember only enough history to repeat it.

Korr's warrant forces custody into the open. Law may delay him; evidence may change his understanding; deception, disarmament, or proof that destruction imperils Veyr may stop the seizure. He can change sides without pretending his orders never existed.

## Developments

Trust may bring Anja to the Deep Bell to direct the tuning crown. Otherwise she provides copies, keeps the originals, and demands public accounting after the crisis. Named chapel teams can copy, dry, and interpret records while the company moves if custody and protection are settled. Damaged archives leave fragments in the memorial plates but make the safe counter-ring harder to reconstruct.

The chapel contributes more than instructions: the city's previous sacrifice, the concealment that followed, the missing half of the operating system, and a custodian who will not permit another district to vanish inside an engineering euphemism.
