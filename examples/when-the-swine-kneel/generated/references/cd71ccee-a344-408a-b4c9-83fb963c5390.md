## Reference Material

## Custody held for erased families

Anja Veil is elected keeper of the Chapel of the First Survey by the surviving surveyor families. She guards memorial plates, bronze rods, funeral records, and technical fragments because Veyr dissolved the old Surveyors' Office, divided its instruments, and removed much of its work from public memory. She wants the city to name that history rather than borrow the surviving doctrine as anonymous emergency equipment.

Anja knows the surveyors' tuning sequence and the rule lost from modern plans: a line is identified by its surface passage and the people above it, not merely by a number on a buried rod. Correct tones can wake recorded answers from the memorial bronze. Force may open stone faster while destroying context, custody, and the sequence needed to interpret what remains.

## Custody cannot choose the remedy

Anja can authorize access to chapel holdings, interpret the old sequence, and refuse the unacknowledged sacrifice of another district. She does not control the Hall, College, farms, pumps, or every later claim to the First Survey. Her access terms, records available, route, cooperation, custody, protection, and judgment of a proposed operation remain live.
