## Summary

Veyr's organized slaughtering and livestock-trade interest, able to mobilize factors, crews, contracts, and compensation claims while lacking unilateral authority to make commercial loss a public-health finding.

## Reference Material

## The market can act faster than the record

The Slaughterers' Compact coordinates factors, slaughter crews, contracts, processing capacity, and claims arising from livestock brought into Veyr. The Convocation of Grain magnifies its concern: delay, rumor, dehydration, injury, and unsold animals can ruin drovers, farms, yards, and processors before a technical inquiry reaches a conclusion.

The Compact therefore prefers remedies that make the market legible quickly. Condemnation and cull convert frightened animals into counted loss, clear crowded pens, and stop a rumor from following living evidence through the city. Its counsel and factors can negotiate payment, mobilize crews, contest a stay, and press veterinary or civic authorities to act. Commercial coordination is not by itself quarantine authority, proof of disease, title to seized evidence, or permission to destroy an independent indicator.

## Loss does not become neutral because it is priced

Compact officers can state real costs that investigators and public officials should not dismiss. They also represent one interested institution among farmers, handlers, water officers, inspectors, and the city. A lawful response must distinguish the Compact's contracts and crews from the public authority needed to condemn, confiscate, quarantine, or compel compensation.

The Compact can mobilize commerce, but present crews, contracts, culls, payments, herd custody, closures, losses, and obligations belong to the encounter and journal.
