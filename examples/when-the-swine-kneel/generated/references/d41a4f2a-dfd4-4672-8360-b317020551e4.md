## Animals read before instruments answer

Ina Rill is Harl's daughter and an expert handler who moves among Rillcross holdings. She knows individual pigs well enough to separate sickness, crowding, scent, ordinary fear, and trained response from the strange anticipatory behavior at buried lines. At Southgate she can show that calm animals stop several breaths before paving and metal begin to beat.

Ina notices when the pattern changes. Separate herds answer separate corridors; water refusal, rooting, turning, kneeling, and sudden lying down do not mean the same thing. With agreed clocks, assigned lines, signals, and protected messengers, she can help farmers maintain a live surface relay while investigators work elsewhere.

## Observation depends on bodies and roads

Ina's expertise does not make the pigs prophetic or place every herd under one command. Readings fail when animals are dispersed, seized, panicked, killed, or cut off from trusted handlers; reports fail when roads or messengers fail. Her location, custody, access to herds, current readings, cooperation, protection, and survival remain state to be recorded in play.
