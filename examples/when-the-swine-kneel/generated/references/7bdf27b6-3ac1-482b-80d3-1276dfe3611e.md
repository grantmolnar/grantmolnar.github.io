## Reference Material

## The station must survive the order

Oren Salk is chief engineer at Nine-Mile Pump House. He knows which pumps can be unloaded, which valves will hammer, how long the intake can hold against a phase change, and which exhausted workers can still perform a dangerous sequence. He kept the station running because an uncontrolled stop could burst the intake, flood the lower hall, and kill the crew before it relieved the buried strain.

Oren distinguishes refusal from procedure. A staged reduction can be engineered when load order, crew positions, gauges, and downstream effects are named. A shouted shutdown by someone who does not understand the machinery is another form of sabotage. With recognized authority and protection from retaliation, he can turn a civic decision into executable station work.

## Engineering authority ends at allocation

Oren may judge a sequence technically impossible, assign trained hands, stop an immediately lethal operation, and explain what each reduction costs. He cannot choose the city's ration priorities, excuse falsified gauges, decide Dast's custody, or declare an inhabited corridor expendable. His station control, crew assignment, load order, cooperation, injury, and willingness to execute any proposed remedy remain live.
