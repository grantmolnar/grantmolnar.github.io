## Reference Material

## A breeder before a spokesman

Harl Rill is a respected breeder in Rillcross. He lost two litters after sows refused one ditch, brought the dead piglets and a rooted bronze pin to the Hall, and became the reluctant spokesman for families whose testimony powerful offices called panic. He knows breeding, water habits, ordinary herd fear, and the difference between a repeated pattern and one frightened animal.

Harl's credibility rests on restraint. He reports the sequence the farms observed—water refusal, rooting at buried metal, turning toward Veyr, and kneeling at the bell—without claiming that the pigs know its cause. He will accept hard measures when their necessity is demonstrated and their burden is not silently assigned to the farms alone.

## Witness authority is neither custody nor command

Harl can organize farm observers, identify holdings and herds, compare family accounts, and speak for shared practical needs. He does not automatically control every household, quarantine order, animal, road, or technical interpretation. His location, testimony, public standing, protection, cooperation, herd access, and response to any proposed sacrifice remain live.
