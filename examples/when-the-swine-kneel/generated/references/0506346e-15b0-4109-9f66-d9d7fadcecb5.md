## Reference Material

## The office arrives before its master

Jalen Orr is a deputy warden in the Office of Waters and a regular representative of Corven Dast. He minimizes early reports as drought, crowding, imitation, theft, or panic while recording every question that may expose the office. He claims rooted pins as civic property and can arrive as the named proctor for impoundment of College records or closure of experiments.

Orr's method is procedural rather than theatrical. A soft seal, transport cart, property classification, safety order, or watch escort can remove evidence without publicly denying that it existed. Junior inspectors and guards may obey because the paper appears regular even when its purpose is concealment.

## Delegated custody has a chain

Orr can issue or carry orders within the authority actually delegated to him, document objections, and maintain a chain of possession. He cannot make an irregular seizure lawful by naming public safety, transfer permanent ownership through a temporary hold, or prove Dast's innocence by obeying him. Orr's office, allegiance, location, cooperation, custody claims, orders, access to agents and records, and eventual responsibility remain live.
