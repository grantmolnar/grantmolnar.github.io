## Certification is narrower than command

A College finding can identify a repeatable observation, state the apparatus and uncertainty, preserve witness, and survive copying into public process. It cannot allocate water, authorize seizure, choose a district for collapse, settle archive ownership, or turn an incomplete model into a safe operating sequence. Charter dependence makes institutional courage costly without making a compromised result true.

A result survives through particular apparatus, traces, witnesses, and signatures. Sealed rooms, remaining volumes, certification, copies, and custody stay with play.
