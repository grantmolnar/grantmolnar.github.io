## Reference Material

## The third line enters the city in a herd

Southgate Stockyards receive the animals, drovers, factors, inspectors, and slaughter crews drawn to Veyr for the Convocation of Grain. Pens, troughs, scales, sorting lanes, retaining walls, cull gates, and an old dry passage let handlers move thousands of animals only while weight and fear remain distributed. The yards are commercial infrastructure before they are evidence.

The old weighing works stand above the urban station on the Deep Bell's third sounding line. A response seen at Southgate can be compared with the same line farther out in Rillcross. The concentrated herd makes changes legible at city scale: refusal at one trough follows a water channel, a turn toward a gate shows pressure moving beyond the old station, and mass kneeling marks the central phase. These patterns require comparison and handling knowledge; they do not make every frightened animal a reliable instrument.

## Market remedies spend living evidence

The stockyards join several kinds of power without making them interchangeable. Yard staff can prevent a stampede. Commercial factors can count delay and loss. Veterinary and quarantine officers can make bounded findings. Hall orders can protect evidence or postpone an act. None of those powers alone decides what the buried system is doing.

The yards do not preserve a herd in the abstract. Animals, gates, injuries, water, cull orders, quarantine, custody, and market loss belong to the visit played.
