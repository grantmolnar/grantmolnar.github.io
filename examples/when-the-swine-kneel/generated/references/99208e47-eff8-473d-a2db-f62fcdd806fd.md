## Reference Material

## Six lines cross property one holding at a time

Rillcross lies south of Veyr in a belt of farms, lanes, wells, ditches, low ridges, and shared water. The Deep Bell's six sounding lines pass beneath different holdings before converging on the city. Southgate provides a dense urban reading on the third line; Rillcross exposes all six farther outward.

No household sees the full figure. One family observes pigs refusing a ditch while drinking from a nearby well. Another sees rooting around a bronze pin. A third records animals turning toward Veyr or lying down along a subsiding arc. Compared by time, location, water source, and individual animal, those bounded reports can distinguish contagion, imitation, and ordinary herd fear from a pressure pattern.

## The map depends on people who still live above it

Farm families own animals, land, paths, wells, carts, lamps, and local knowledge rather than existing as replaceable stations. A useful relay must respect separate households, quarantine boundaries, road conditions, and the fact that a technical choice below may spend a named surface corridor. Rillcross can provide observers and a distributed warning network only through continued cooperation and communication.

The lines do not turn households into fixed stations. Herds, wells, roads, consent, quarantine, runners, signals, and sacrifice must be read from the current farms.
