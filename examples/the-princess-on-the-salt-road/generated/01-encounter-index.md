# Encounter Index: The Princess on the Salt Road

## The House of Blue Lamps (`house-of-blue-lamps`)

**Role:** necessary, start

In a shuttered dye house below the occupied Assembly, Ianthe lays the bronze seal on a stained table, defines the compact of her protection, and asks which cost the road should spend first.

## The Gate of Horns (`gate-of-horns`)

**Role:** necessary

At Aulon’s south gate, a royal passage charter, a narrowing wicket, and Kallias’s white receipts turn speed into public testimony—or public violence.

## The Dry Aqueduct (`dry-aqueduct`)

**Role:** necessary

Beneath the baths, lime throats, old water, and a single order of march divide people from horses and force authority to travel as weight.

## The Cypress Gate (`cypress-gate`)

**Role:** necessary

The funeral road leaves Aulon under a peace older than Kallias’s warrant: no arrest, no named feud, and no blade drawn before the last stone lion.

## The House at Three Cypresses (`house-at-three-cypresses`)

**Role:** necessary

A courier house offers four horses, finite stores, suspect provisions, and rival terms; every preparation gives Dorion another mile.

## The Red Bridge (`red-bridge`)

**Role:** necessary

At the mounted road’s hinge, remounts, signal shutters, waterworks, and the bronze seal wait for signatures while pursuit closes from the north.

## The Reed Villages (`reed-villages`)

**Role:** necessary

Confiscated boats and punished households can hide Ianthe, carry witnesses, or evacuate civilians, but no channel moves one burden without assigning another.

## The Beacon Hill (`beacon-hill`)

**Role:** necessary

A false white flame has already named Ianthe an abducted principal; the tower can correct the harbor only through records, witnesses, and a signal Myrine will trust.

## Myrine Harbor (`myrine-harbor`)

**Role:** necessary, end

Seven stones separate sanctuary from custody while magistrates, soldiers, assassins, records, seal, and departing ship each follow a different lane.
