# The Beacon Hill

**Role:** necessary  
**Tags:** escort, signal, final-approach, pursuit

A false white flame has already named Ianthe an abducted principal; the tower can correct the harbor only through records, witnesses, and a signal Myrine will trust.

## Opening View

Myrine is already answering the steady-white flame. Harbor spears close the processional road below while the Sea-Lark’s crew clears her deck. Keeper Phorbas holds a clay strip naming Ianthe an abducted principal; his apprentice points to an arrival time that does not match the bridge register. A second black tube rests unopened beneath the signal table. Three descents offer three kinds of custody. The basket can change the warning in moments. Records and witnesses must make anyone believe the correction.

## Encounter Material

## Situation

The first thing the escort sees from Beacon Hill is Myrine already acting on someone else’s nouns. The tower’s low wall cannot stop a company, but its basket, shutters, clay strips, and descending roads decide whether the harbor prepares for a traveler, an armed company, an abduction, or a petition before Ianthe reaches the stones.

The basket presently burns **steady white**, Myrine’s warning for *a royal or civic principal carried under armed compulsion*. Kallias’s relay named Ianthe’s household escort as armed abductors and Dorion as the officer attempting lawful recovery. Below, the harbor guard has closed the processional road, set spear lines at the white stones, and forbidden the Sea-Lark to receive any armed company until Ianthe’s freedom can be tested.

The warning is falsely authored and visibly plausible: Ianthe approaches neutral ground with armed retainers while soldiers pursue her. A corrected flame proves only that someone reached the basket. Ledger, clay strips, witnesses, formation, and Ianthe’s own words decide whether Myrine receives a report or watches a captured tower lie.

Bring forward the road’s open pursuit posture. **Unfixed** grants time to inspect and choose. **Named** means accurate descriptions have arrived. **Closing** puts riders on the inland ridge or lower olives. **Contact** places Dorion, assassins, or both within the precinct. The posture decides who can act before the next bell. The harbor weighs the competing accounts separately.
## What the hill controls

Beacon Hill concentrates four different forms of access. They should not be collapsed into a single “take the tower” objective.

**The signal account.** The beacon basket, colored salts, shutters, and authenticated ledger tell Myrine what kind of company approaches. A corrected signal can prepare sanctuary, distinguish assassins from bodyguards, or request a weapons exception. A contradictory or obviously seized signal makes the harbor more cautious rather than automatically friendly.

**The visible road.** The broad Salt Steps descend directly from the tower to the sanctuary quay. They are fastest and expose every movement to the harbor guard, the magistrates, and anyone on the inland ridge.

**The concealed descents.** The Olive Stairs and Ropewalk Track avoid parts of the visible road, but each reaches a different part of Myrine. Concealment can preserve Ianthe's life while worsening the abduction story if she emerges from the wrong place with armed companions.

**The last division point.** Horses, wounded allies, the bronze seal, public witnesses, and the armed bodyguard company need not descend together. Once the escort commits to a descent, transferring among routes becomes slow, conspicuous, or impossible.

## The tower precinct

### The inland saddle

The Salt Road narrows between two limestone shoulders before entering the tower yard. A low customs wall has gates wide enough for two horses abreast. The wall cannot stop a determined company, but it can force riders to dismount or bunch under observation. A bronze speaking disk lets the keeper address arrivals without opening the gate.

A traveler coming from the Red Bridge can see the steady white beacon before reaching the saddle. A traveler from the Reed Villages comes up beneath the eastern terrace and may reach the cistern court without passing the main gate if guided by a pilot. Naevan's western forest path ends at an abandoned charcoal platform below the saddle; his outriders will not cross the tower yard as an armed elven company.

### The cistern court

A spring-fed cistern occupies the eastern side of the yard. Signal crews use its water to quench baskets and wash colored salts from tools. The court includes a mule shed, a two-room infirmary, and a grated culvert that opens onto the upper Olive Stairs.

The cistern court is the best place to hide wounded people, change clothes, bind weapons, or move a small group toward the concealed descent. It is also where an Ash Knife posing as a Myrine litter bearer waits if the assassins have reached the hill first. The false bearer carries a genuine harbor badge taken from a murdered porter and knows enough procedure to sound helpful. The badge proves access, not loyalty.

### The signal room

The lower tower chamber contains the code boards, day flags, night shutters, colored mineral salts, and the bound ledger of messages sent and received. Each formal signal has three parts: the visible code, the keeper's brass seal impressed in a clay strip, and a written entry naming the observer or authority behind it. Myrine can see only the visible code immediately, but the sealed strip travels down by message runner or the rope basket beside the Salt Steps.

The current white warning has a valid-looking clay strip bearing a duplicate of the Red Bridge road seal. Inspection reveals that the message names Dorion as its eyewitness even though the dispatch time predates his possible arrival at the relay. This does not prove every part of the warning false, but it gives Keeper Phorbas lawful grounds to supersede it.

### The upper basket

A stone stair climbs to an open platform around the iron beacon basket. The platform has waist-high crenellations, a shutter frame, two spare fuel cages, and a speaking trumpet aimed toward the harbor. In daylight, flags and mirror boards carry the same codes. At night or in smoke, the basket matters.

The upper platform is exposed to arrows from the inland shoulder and to a hidden approach along the old maintenance ledge. It is a strong place for Ianthe to appear publicly, but a poor place to keep her for long. The Ash Knives prefer either a close attack here, where every defender's attention is on the signal, or a shot from the ledge timed to make Dorion appear responsible.

### The eastern terrace

The terrace holds two olive presses, a ruined watch hut, and the first turn of the Olive Stairs. From here, the escort can see the eastern fish market and the roof of the sanctuary hall, but not the white stones themselves. A small group can begin the concealed descent without crossing the signal platform.

Fisher pilots from the Reed Villages recognize the terrace by three whitewashed jars set into the parapet. If the villages sent witnesses or boats, one of their runners waits here unless intercepted.

### The chain overlook

A narrow western spur overlooks Myrine's chainhouse and the Ropewalk Track. The harbor chain passes through a tower pulley below, and the Sea-Lark lies beyond it at the league quay. The overlook offers the earliest view of the departure preparations and the fastest path to the chainhouse. It does not lead directly to sanctuary.

Anyone descending this way with weapons or the bronze seal may appear to be attacking the harbor works or attempting to board without judgment. The route is useful for protecting the ship, intercepting assassins, moving the seal separately, or reaching a delayed gangplank. It is a politically dangerous route for Ianthe herself.

## People at the beacon

### Keeper Phorbas Eren

Phorbas keeps the tower by refusing to certify more than he has received, seen, or tested. He can authenticate the clay strips, identify the hand that set a shutter, and state which formations are visible on each road. He cannot declare Ianthe free merely because she holds the seal or declare Dorion lawful merely because his message arrived first.

His sentences preserve evidentiary edges: “I received this. Pheren saw that. The harbor must decide the rest.” This is not timidity. A beacon whose keeper supplies the missing conclusions becomes a weapon any captor can operate.

Phorbas will send uncertainty, free petition requested, assassin danger, mixed companies, disputed authority, or a corrected route. He will not send innocence. Give him witnesses, a visible act, and wording that leaves judgment where Myrine’s law placed it.
### Pheren Vale, signal apprentice

Pheren is nineteen and has spent the crisis carrying messages between the tower and Myrine. She saw the first white dispatch arrive in the hands of a rider wearing a road-sergeant's cloak over city sandals. She was ordered not to record that irregularity because the clay seal appeared valid. She will tell Ianthe or a bodyguard who treats her as a witness rather than a servant.

Pheren knows the message-basket route, every shutter code, and the maintenance ledge used by the Ash Knives. If frightened or ignored, she tries to run the corrected clay strip down the Salt Steps herself. Protecting her may matter more than holding the tower.

### Harbor Sergeant Timon Quill

Timon commands eight Myrine spears sent up after the white warning. His orders are to prevent any armed company from descending the Salt Steps until Captain Eudora grants an exception. He does not claim authority to arrest Ianthe, Dorion, or the bodyguards on Aulonite soil. He can close the road, receive surrendered weapons, escort unarmed petitioners, and carry a report to the magistrates.

Timon is suspicious of everyone who asks him to decide Aulon's succession. He responds well to a concrete protection plan: who walks with Ianthe, where weapons remain, who guards the rear, and how Myrine will distinguish assassins from disarmed bodyguards. He responds badly to attempts to rush the gate while claiming sanctuary makes all conduct lawful.

### Niko Sarn, Kallias's advocate

Niko is a civic clerk carrying a soldier’s emergency across a civilian procedure. He arrived with Kallias’s protector decree and a petition asking Myrine to “restore a vulnerable royal citizen to lawful household care.” His work is to make the abduction account respectable enough to delay a contrary one.

Niko notices real defects. If the bodyguards lied to Ianthe, restrained her after danger passed, excluded witnesses, or let foreign scouts choose her route, he records it. He is dangerous when the escort has supplied him with facts and weak when ordered to deny what every witness can see.

His diction is derivative authority: clause, seal, filing, household, interval, review. Under pressure he asks who will sign the sentence he is being asked to defend. Niko may expose a forged time or unauthorized violence if Dorion or Kallias treats legality as decoration; he serves emergency power because he believes it must remain answerable to forms.
## The signal lexicon

Use the following codes as public information once the escort reaches the signal room or questions a keeper.

| Signal | Harbor meaning | Immediate effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Steady white | Principal under armed compulsion | Close the processional road; prepare a coercion inquiry |
| White broken by three dark intervals | Authority disputed; principal's will unverified | Admit an unarmed petitioner under guard; hold armed companies apart |
| Gold above blue | Royal or civic principal travelling by declared choice | Prepare formal reception; still apply sanctuary weapon rules |
| Gold broken by red | Principal requests sanctuary under threat of murder | Send magistrates, medical aid, and a limited armed exception |
| Red in paired bursts | Assassins or pirates among mixed parties | Separate companies by identity rather than presumed allegiance |
| Blue low in the basket | Harbor works or chainhouse threatened | Reinforce chainhouse and delay ship movement if necessary |
| No flame after an authenticated warning | Tower status unknown or seized | Treat every later arrival cautiously; do not infer safety |

The most truthful corrected signal is often **gold broken by red** or **white broken by three dark intervals**, not simple gold. Ianthe is travelling by choice, but the harbor has not yet heard her petition, and multiple armed parties may be converging. A nuanced signal wins less immediate trust than a triumphal one but is harder for Kallias's advocate to discredit.

Changing the visible code takes moments if the basket is controlled. Authenticating it requires Phorbas or a lawful deputy, a clay strip, and a runner or message basket. Without authentication, Myrine treats the change as evidence that the tower may be contested.

## Three descents to Myrine

### The Salt Steps

The processional road descends in long white switchbacks from the tower gate to the sanctuary quay. Mounted travelers can use it at a walk. The harbor can see nearly the entire descent, and witnesses on the road can see one another.

This is the fastest route for Ianthe, the strongest route for a public account, and the easiest route for Dorion to contest. Timon opens it for Ianthe and a small unarmed or peace-bound escort once the signal is corrected or a direct examination satisfies him. He does not permit two armed companies to descend together.

The Salt Steps contain three exposed turns where bodyguards can hold pursuit, exchange custody, or suffer attack. The lower turn ends at the spear line outside the white stones. An escort that fights its way down may save time and lose the presumption that Myrine can safely admit it.

### The Olive Stairs

A narrow footpath leaves the eastern terrace, passes through abandoned olive plots, and enters Myrine through the upper fish market. Horses cannot use it. Litters require four carriers and move slowly. The path is screened from the inland ridge but contains two choke points and a dry well where attackers can wait above the route.

The Olive Stairs reach the harbor behind the public spear line. This can evade Dorion or an attack on the processional road, but it places Ianthe among civilians before she has petitioned and can make the bodyguards look as though they bypassed lawful examination. Fisher witnesses, Pheren's authenticated strip, or a Myrine guide can preserve legitimacy.

This is the best route for a small concealed principal group and the worst route for a large armed formation. It can also carry a decoy in Ianthe's cloak while she descends publicly.

### The Ropewalk Track

The western service track zigzags beneath the chain overlook to Myrine's ropewalk and chainhouse. Pack animals can descend; riding horses cannot do so safely. The track reaches the Sea-Lark's quay before it reaches the sanctuary steps.

The Ropewalk Track is useful when the ship or chainhouse is under threat, when the seal travels separately, or when wounded bodyguards need a direct route to the league quay. It cannot substitute for Ianthe's petition. If she uses it, she must cross the crowded harbor later to reach the white stones.

Myrine reinforces this route when the beacon shows low blue. Dorion may try to send a small seal-recovery group down it while he holds the Salt Steps. Ash Knives may use it to attack the Sea-Lark, forcing the escort to choose between preserving extraction and protecting Ianthe's immediate descent.

## Bypassing the hill

Beacon Hill is the strongest place to authenticate the approaching company, not the only physical road to Myrine. Riders from the Red Bridge may remain on the lower Salt Road and reach the spear court without climbing the tower. Boats from the Reed Villages may land at the fish stairs or league quay below it. A runner carrying an authenticated strip can also separate from Ianthe and attempt to prepare the harbor while the principal uses another approach.

Bypassing the hill saves time and avoids a tower already held by Dorion or assassins. It surrenders control of Myrine's first account. Unless another authenticated message arrived, the harbor sees mixed armed parties without a trusted explanation and begins with stricter identification, separation, and coercion inquiry. That is a harder reception, not a closed sanctuary.

A late corroborating clue discovered on the hill may still establish who controlled an earlier gate, signal, or route. It affects the public record; it does not require the escort to revisit the earlier encounter.

## The chain clock seen from the hill

The harbor's departure is visible and announced by bells.

**Before the first chain bell,** the Sea-Lark remains fully moored, cargo nets are down, and the processional road can deliver Ianthe to the white stones with time for a complete hearing.

**Between the first and second bells,** the chain crew clears small craft from the channel and the Sea-Lark draws in cargo lines. Sanctuary remains fully available. Boarding after the petition requires prompt coordination with the captain.

**Between the second and final bells,** the gangplank may be withdrawn and the ship held only by spring lines. The magistrates can still hear Ianthe, but the bodyguards may need the Ropewalk Track, a harbor order, or a boat to join her aboard.

**At the final bell,** the chain reaches the tower pulley and the Sea-Lark begins departure. Myrine can still grant sanctuary after the ship sails. Losing the ship changes extraction, not the legality of Ianthe's petition.

Choices on the hill cost time when they require a formal inquiry, repair, treatment of wounded people, searching the ledge, or moving groups among descents. Correcting the flame itself is quick. Making the correction credible is what consumes the remaining road.

## Ianthe's declaration

Ianthe can speak from the upper platform through the harbor trumpet. The distance cannot complete sanctuary, but it can prevent the first armed formation below from defining her before she arrives.

She names herself, whose choice brought her, whom she fears, and what she will ask of Myrine. She does not praise her bodyguards as a condition of freedom. If they deceived, restrained, or endangered her, she says so and then states whether those acts continue. Her habit is to divide the claim until each person must own a part: “Dorion is ordered to keep me alive. The Ash Knives are not. My guards advised this road. I chose it.”

The declaration gains force from Phorbas’s ledger, Pheren’s testimony, visible road witnesses, and the formation Myrine can see. It also fixes Ianthe beneath every eye on the hill. The bodyguards secure the platform and exits. Ianthe decides whether public authorship is worth exposure.
## Dorion at the hill

Dorion's priorities here are ordered:

1. keep Ianthe alive through the descent;
2. prevent an uncontested public signal that Kallias ordered her unlawful seizure;
3. recover the bronze seal;
4. prevent the bodyguards from entering Myrine as her recognized lawful escort; and
5. preserve a route by which she can still be returned without open war.

He may offer **joint descent**: Ianthe walks between two unarmed Myrine sergeants, the bodyguards and Dorion both bind weapons and remain separated, the seal is deposited with Phorbas or Timon, and the magistrates question Ianthe privately. Dorion expects the questioning to delay the ship and give Kallias's advocate room to argue. The offer is not false. It also places the bodyguards and seal where Dorion can contest them later.

If assassins attack, Dorion fights to protect Ianthe and the signal crew. Once the immediate danger ends, he renews his custody claim. He will not order a charge down the Salt Steps against Myrine's spear line unless the bodyguards are visibly murdering or carrying Ianthe, because doing so would convert recovery into interstate aggression.

Dorion can be persuaded to admit publicly that his warrant requires Ianthe alive, that she is presently walking without bonds, or that the Ash Knives are a separate threat. Any such admission gives Phorbas grounds for a mixed or corrected signal. Dorion may accept this to prevent the assassins from using the false white flame as cover.

## The Ash Knives on the hill

The assassins do not need to hold the tower. They need one credible opening while every armed company watches the others.

Choose or combine their approach according to earlier clues and consequences:

**The false litter.** An assassin in Myrine porter dress offers to carry a wounded Ianthe or companion down the Olive Stairs. The genuine badge and correct route make the offer plausible. The litter poles conceal a narrow thrusting blade and a vial meant for the principal's lips.

**The maintenance ledge.** A climber reaches the upper basket from the blind western face and attacks during the signal change or public declaration. If the bodyguards respond with missile fire, the assassin tries to fall wearing a scrap of Dorion's colors.

**The red diversion.** A second agent ignites the mule shed or colored-salt cabinet while the first moves against Ianthe, Pheren, or the authenticated message. The objective is not to burn the whole tower but to force defenders to choose what they preserve.

**The chainhouse feint.** A blue signal or sabotaged messenger suggests an attack on the Sea-Lark. The escort can send strength down the Ropewalk Track, but doing so weakens the principal group. The threat may be real: killing Ianthe remains primary, yet destroying the escape ship makes later attacks easier.

Captured Ash Knives know enough to confirm that Kallias did not hire them. Their testimony may help Dorion and the bodyguards cooperate, but Myrine will still ask who had custody and whether Ianthe spoke freely.

## Protection decisions before descent

Before leaving the hill, ask the table to name the following:

- Who accompanies Ianthe within speaking distance?
- Are their weapons surrendered, left at the tower, peace-bound, concealed, or carried under an exception?
- Who holds the bronze seal, and by which descent does it travel?
- Which witnesses descend with the principal, which carry the authenticated strip, and which remain to testify about pursuit?
- Who guards the rear against Dorion or assassins?
- What is the rendezvous if the routes separate?
- What signal or phrase distinguishes a bodyguard, harbor officer, village witness, and false guide?

Ianthe participates in this counsel. She strongly prefers a route that lets Myrine see her walk by choice. She accepts a concealed route when there is a specific lethal threat, not merely because public speech is inconvenient. She may entrust the seal to a separate group if doing so protects the petition from becoming a struggle over property.

## Objective-driven encounter frames

### Correct the account

The escort must secure Phorbas, the signal materials, and a credible basis for superseding the white warning while Niko argues delay and pursuit approaches. The objective ends when Myrine receives an authenticated correction, when the tower's credibility is destroyed, or when the escort abandons signaling for another protection plan.

### Hold the three turns

Ianthe and a small escort descend the Salt Steps while another group delays Dorion or assassins at successive bends. The defenders need not defeat the pursuit. They must preserve distance, prevent a missile attack, and avoid conduct that convinces Myrine the white warning was right.

### Save the witness

Pheren carries the authenticated strip down the message path while an assassin or Kallias's advocate attempts to intercept, discredit, or replace her. Ianthe may descend separately. The objective is the integrity of testimony rather than possession of the tower.

### Two threats, three routes

An attack on the Sea-Lark or chainhouse coincides with danger to Ianthe on the Salt Steps. The escort divides among principal, ship, and signal. Success can be mixed: Ianthe may gain sanctuary without the ship, or the ship may remain available while the seal or a bodyguard group falls into Dorion's hands.

### Joint escort under knives

Dorion, the bodyguards, and Myrine sergeants begin a negotiated descent when the Ash Knives attack. Cooperation is tactically real and politically temporary. The objective is to get Ianthe alive to the white stones without allowing either armed claimant to convert emergency formation into accepted custody.

## Outcomes and failure forward

**Authenticated free-petition signal.** Myrine opens the Salt Steps for Ianthe, sends magistrates to the sanctuary hall, and may grant a limited weapons exception. Pursuit remains dangerous, but the harbor no longer assumes the bodyguards are abductors.

**Authenticated disputed-authority signal.** Myrine admits Ianthe under a guarded coercion inquiry and holds all armed companies apart. This is slower but legally strong. The Sea-Lark may become difficult to reach before departure.

**Tower seized without credibility.** The escort controls the terrain but not the account. Myrine closes the visible road and reinforces the chainhouse. The Olive Stairs remain possible with witnesses or a guide; the finale begins under suspicion.

**Signal lost or tower disabled.** The harbor treats the tower as contested. Ianthe can still petition, but no signal advantage applies. Damage to the basket, message rope, cistern, or stair should be recorded separately.

**Dorion holds the hill.** Ianthe may still descend under joint guard, escape by a concealed route, or force a public examination. Dorion's physical control does not give him authority inside Myrine. If he controls both Ianthe and the seal without witnesses, the road becomes a custody negotiation rather than immediate defeat.

**The seal goes by another route.** Ianthe reaches the sanctuary while Dorion, assassins, or bodyguards contest the chainhouse or fish market. The petition can succeed without the seal. Ownership of the seal changes the political aftermath.

**The Sea-Lark is threatened.** Harbor resources shift from sanctuary reception to chainhouse defense. Ianthe may choose to secure sanctuary first and lose the ship, or divert protection to preserve extraction while the petition is delayed.

**Ianthe is wounded.** Myrine permits medical aid without treating unconscious movement as a valid petition. The bodyguards must preserve her life and her later ability to speak. A serious wound changes the route and clock; it does not authorize someone else to petition for her.

**An Ash Knife is exposed publicly.** Dorion's abduction story weakens, Myrine becomes more willing to distinguish armed groups, and temporary cooperation is possible. The custody dispute survives the revelation.

Whatever happens, the next encounter is Myrine Harbor unless Ianthe freely accepts Naevan's full extraction or Dorion carries her back beyond any plausible immediate recovery. Beacon Hill decides the conditions of arrival, not whether the harbor exists.
