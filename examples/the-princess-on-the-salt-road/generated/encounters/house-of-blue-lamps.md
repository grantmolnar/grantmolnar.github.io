# The House of Blue Lamps

**Role:** necessary, start  
**Tags:** escort, briefing, route-choice, politics

In a shuttered dye house below the occupied Assembly, Ianthe lays the bronze seal on a stained table, defines the compact of her protection, and asks which cost the road should spend first.

## Opening View

The bronze speaking rail comes apart three streets uphill, each blow carrying through the dye house. Myrto bars the shutters and sets three route tokens on a red-lined courier board. Ianthe places the royal seal between them as the palace beacon changes from gold to Kallias’s white. Four knocks sound at the door, then one—the correct signal, known to too many hands. Soot marks Ianthe’s cheek; her voice does not shake. “Counsel me plainly.”

## Encounter Material

## Situation

Myrto has shuttered the blue lamps and cleared one dye table without cleaning it. Indigo stains the grain, vinegar and wet fleece sharpen the smoke, and the covered cistern still hides the old palace maintenance mouth. Three streets uphill, soldiers are prying the bronze speaking rail from the Assembly steps so no citizen can lawfully call the body to order.

Princess **Ianthe Aulonid** arrived ten minutes ago in a servant’s gray cloak, soot across one cheek and the palace seal under her tunic. She chose this emergency house, dismissed retainers whose uniforms would betray it, and sent for the bodyguards because they are the smallest company she trusts to contradict her and still obey the answer. She is frightened, tired, and entirely present.

Ianthe lays the seal on the stained table. Kallias needs both princess and bronze, but they do different work. An impression can move horses, grain, soldiers, and wax. It cannot cross the white stones or speak the petition Myrine must hear from her.
## Ianthe's compact

Ianthe does not ask the bodyguards to swear that she will never be endangered. She knows such an oath would reward anyone who locked her in a cellar. She asks for three promises:

> Counsel me plainly. Obey when I decide. Do not make me safe by making me absent.

She accepts the same obligations: state what she is preserving, hear objections when time permits, and never spend a bodyguard merely to preserve her dignity. The bodyguards may disagree, delay her, conceal information from enemies, or pull her from immediate danger. Give them her reasons before a decision whenever possible. Her judgment governs the compact. When bodyguards overrule it, the record must name the danger, the act, and the moment at which her authority returns; her own decisions still carry consequences.

Ianthe's objectives, in order, are:

1. Reach Myrine alive and speak a free petition before the second sunset.
2. Prevent Kallias from using her voice or seal to counterfeit lawful continuity.
3. Preserve enough visible Aulonite support that sanctuary does not become permanent foreign wardship.
4. Avoid civil bloodshed where a slower, costlier, or more humiliating road can prevent it.

She can surrender the seal, renounce succession, accept elven protection, or return to Aulon. Each remains possible, but only through her stated assent and a record of who heard it.

## What happened in Aulon

King Leandros died three nights ago after a fever witnessed by physicians, priests, and half the winter council. Kallias did not poison him. The coup began because the old constitutional balance had no accepted answer for a half-elven adult heir, a divided Assembly, and an army whose wartime commander still held emergency warrants.

At dusk, Kallias occupied the Assembly, the mint, the gate towers, and the messenger loft. He announced that Ianthe had asked him to preserve order. She went to the Assembly portico and denied it before soldiers closed the square. Captain Dorion Vey was ordered to escort her back to the palace. Ianthe left through a kitchen court while palace servants raised three different alarms.

Kallias circulates two claims: grief has made Ianthe unfit, and armed retainers are carrying her coastward against sound counsel. Shape the second around the actual bodyguards—foreign blades, Aulonite retainers outside civic command, or local dupes serving a foreign plot. A public appearance can break the abduction story and fix her position in the same moment. Concealment buys distance while Kallias names what the unseen miles mean.

## The hard deadline

Myrine Harbor lies thirty-six road miles southeast. The neutral league trireme **Sea-Lark** arrived for Leandros's funeral and will depart when the harbor chain rises at the second sunset. Its captain has authority to carry a lawful petitioner away from Aulonite jurisdiction, but not to remain while Kallias's cavalry turns the harbor into a battlefield.

The sanctuary steps remain after the ship leaves, but the protection becomes weaker. Myrine can shelter Ianthe inside its walls; it cannot prevent Kallias from surrounding the polis, closing its roads, and demanding arbitration while his version of events hardens. Reaching the harbor after the chain rises is survival, not full success.

Treat delays as visible choices rather than hidden penalties. A public speech, rescued villagers, exhausted mounts, a repaired aqueduct, a negotiated truce, or a careful crossing may cost time. Name the lost distance whenever a choice consumes a meaningful part of the road.

## Three ways out

Myrto has gathered enough current information to make all three departures credible.

### The Gate of Horns

The south gate opens directly onto the Salt Road. It is the fastest path for riders and the best place to produce public witnesses. Kallias has a full squad there, but the gate charter reserves passage for the royal household until the Assembly formally suspends the house. That has not happened. Ianthe can invoke the charter, force the guards to violate it before citizens, or create enough confusion for a mounted break.

The cost is pursuit. The gate remounts can put Dorion on the road within an hour, and every witness can describe the escort.

### The Dry Aqueduct

A maintenance channel beneath the old baths passes under the southeast wall. It cannot take horses, a carriage, or anyone unwilling to crawl through lime dust and old water. The lower sluice was sealed after a collapse, but repair workers still know the cistern access.

The aqueduct offers the longest unseen lead and makes it easy to send the bronze seal by another route. It also leaves the escort on foot in the olive hills, and a badly handled sluice can flood the tunnel or announce the escape at the bath court.

### The Cypress Gate

A small western gate opens into the necropolis and the old funeral road. A sacred truce forbids drawn steel between the inner gate and the last grave marker. Kallias has not closed it because violating the truce would turn every old family against him. Mourning carts depart before midnight with grave cloth, oil, and offerings for Leandros's coming funeral.

The route provides witnesses who value custom more than Kallias. It also requires the escort to protect Ianthe among crowds without openly carrying weapons, and the Ash Knives know that everyone will first suspect the strategos if she dies there.

All three roads reach **the House at Three Cypresses**, an old courier station beyond the first hills. The escort can divide, use a decoy, send the seal separately, or arrange more than one exit. Ianthe will accept separation from the seal. She will not accept being sent unconscious or misinformed down a road selected in her name.

## Keeping the route open

The House at Three Cypresses is the prepared rendezvous. Every exit also continues toward Red Bridge or the Reed Villages, and Myrto can name those broad continuations, though she does not know their present patrols, water, or signals.

- From the Gate of Horns, the Salt Road continues toward the Red Bridge; a marked turn near the Heron mile-stone reaches the reedward towpath.
- From the Dry Aqueduct, an eastern shepherd ridge meets the bridge road, while an old feeder channel runs south-west toward the Heron levee and Reed Villages.
- From the Cypress Gate, a vineyard cross-road bends east toward the bridge, and the funeral road continues toward the reedward causeway.

Skipping the courier house saves time and denies Dorion one predictable rendezvous. It also gives up guaranteed resupply, a controlled counsel circle, early discovery of tampered provisions, and the clearest statement of Dorion's and Naevan's terms. Ianthe asks the escort to name which of those benefits they are deliberately sacrificing. She accepts a direct route when the house is watched, the deadline is tight, or division makes convergence more dangerous than useful.

Clues record where route facts can be discovered or corroborated; roads remain governed by distance, terrain, and control. A later witness who confirms what happened at an earlier gate strengthens the public account without opening a physical road backward through the journey.

## The starting cast

**Princess Ianthe** has not slept, grows more formal as fear rises, and keeps asking who bears each proposed cost. She knows the Salt Road and Myrine's law, and she can shoot from horseback well enough to discourage a pursuer. Formation fighting, assassin searches, repairs, piloting, and sustained defense remain the bodyguards' work.

**Myrto Sable** is broad, sixty, blue to the elbows, and incapable of whispering without sounding angry. She has one cart, four ordinary horses, two palace saddles, mourning cloth, lime workers' tokens, and three changes of clothing. She will remain behind and expects arrest.

**Timon Vale**, Ianthe's seventeen-year-old page, knows every palace rumor and almost no roadcraft. Ianthe wants him sent separately to a relative inside Aulon. Timon insists that public loyalty means nothing if everyone useful leaves. Keeping him adds a vulnerable witness; leaving him creates a future hostage; giving him a decoy task can turn him into an active ally.

## Immediate pressure

Before the route is chosen, someone knocks four times, pauses, and knocks once. That is a correct palace signal used by half a dozen people. The caller is a frightened stable groom with news that Dorion has searched two other safe houses and has not yet reached this street. The groom may be genuine, followed, or an Ash Knife using the signal. In every case, the coup, Dorion's search, and the assassination threat remain in force; the immediate choice is how much the escort reveals while verifying a person under time pressure.

If the house is compromised, Myrto can open the cistern wall, start a dye fire as cover, or drive the cart into the street while the escort uses another exit. Failure changes the departure conditions; it does not end the journey.
