# The Gate of Horns

**Role:** necessary  
**Tags:** escort, city-exit, public, military

At Aulon’s south gate, a royal passage charter, a narrowing wicket, and Kallias’s white receipts turn speed into public testimony—or public violence.

## Opening View

Wheels lock against wheels beneath the bronze horns. Kallias’s clerk reads the new warrant—armed retainers detained, Ianthe returned, the royal seal preserved—while the older gate charter remains cut into the tower above him. Soldiers count spearheads; teamsters count soldiers. At the livestock wicket, inspection passes from cargo to faces. Lieutenant Phaedra Corin raises one hand. The wicket narrows around the next traveler before anyone has established whose law governs passage.

## Encounter Material

## Situation

Kallias’s white command lamps burn above the paired towers. The great doors are shut, but the livestock wicket remains open because a polis cannot stop eating merely because its constitution has failed. Teamsters count spearheads; citizens linger where they can hear; a clerk reads the new warrant until the words begin to sound older than the paper.

Lieutenant **Phaedra Corin** commands twenty soldiers trained under Leandros and paid by Kallias at dusk. Her order is narrower than the barred gate: detain armed retainers outside civic command, return Ianthe to protected residence, preserve the seal. It neither authorizes killing the princess nor repeals the household passage charter. Phaedra knows both limits—and knows she must survive every decision she postpones until Dorion arrives.
## Ways through

**Invoke the gate charter.** Ianthe may show herself, name the old law, and require Phaedra either to open the road or publicly place Kallias's warrant above the house and Assembly. This creates excellent witnesses and may bring soldiers or citizens into Ianthe's service. It also tells Dorion exactly when and how she left.

**Pass as road traffic.** Mourning cloth, a dye cart, workers' cords, or a convincing inspection dispute can move a small group through the livestock lane. The bronze seal is difficult to conceal from a serious search but can be carried by someone who does not resemble the princess's company.

**Create two truths at once.** Ianthe can appear at the gate while riders, a cart, or the seal depart elsewhere. Kallias's officers are prepared for disguise but not for a princess who publicly declares one intention and then takes another lawful route.

**Break the gate.** The escort can seize the winch, raise the livestock bar, fight through the wicket, or open the doors to the crowd. This is fast once begun and leaves no ambiguity. It also gives Kallias the civil violence he needs for his story.

## Beyond the gate

Three roads show before the escort is fully exposed. A courier spur reaches the House at Three Cypresses. The pavement runs on to Red Bridge and its remounts. At the Heron mile-stone, fish carts take a packed towpath toward the Reed Villages without crossing the bridge deck. Horses lose speed there, and an opened sluice turns earth to mud, but a public departure can still break into concealment before Dorion closes.

Ianthe does not insist on displaying herself merely because this is the public route. She favors an open charter challenge when Kallias's abduction story is the least recoverable threat and the escort has a credible plan for crowd and assassin control. If the escort has already passed covertly, she will not step back into view for symbolism alone. She may instead send a named witness, written declaration, or recognizable supporter to preserve part of the public account.

The escort may bypass the courier house and continue directly toward either middle road. Doing so preserves momentum but forfeits prepared remounts, treatment, and the controlled exchange of offers.

## Protection problem

The danger is not a single line of guards. It is the overlap of soldiers who will not willingly kill Ianthe, hidden Ash Knives who will, and citizens who may surge toward her when they recognize her. A bodyguard can protect her by controlling distance, sight lines, and who reaches her first. Killing every uniformed opponent may make the actual assassin's work easier.

An Ash Knife named **Sera Noll** waits in a grain cart with a short bow under sacks. She will not attack unless Ianthe becomes visible and the crowd gives her a plausible escape. Sera has no special immunity to detection. Her presence should be signaled by a cart entered twice on the tally, a driver who does not know the miller's name, and a clean bowstring in flour dust.

## Consequences

A public passage gives Ianthe named witnesses, possible volunteers, and a direct answer to the abduction story. It also advances Dorion's pursuit and closes easy use of the Red Bridge unless the escort secures remounts or delays the gate cavalry.

A covert passage preserves distance but lets Kallias claim that private blades smuggled the grieving princess away. A violent passage may split the gate soldiers: some pursue, some refuse, and some join. Record who crossed the threshold beside Ianthe. At Myrine, witnesses are not decoration; they can attest that she traveled by choice.
