# The Ash Knives

**Reference ID:** `a4c3844d-a690-499a-9520-9e59f57d1919`  
**Kind:** Organization  
**Aliases:** Ash Knife cells, the Knives  
**Tags:** assassins, antimonarchists, sabotage, false colors

## Summary

Small antimonarchist cells that seek to end the royal succession by exploiting loyalist colors, confined ground, procedural openings, and distrust among larger armed parties.

## Reference Material

## Neither protector nor heir

The Ash Knives reject the choice between Kallias's emergency government and Ianthe's royal claim. They intend to end the succession before either general or heir can use it. Their objective is distinct from Kallias's custody project even when an attack made in loyalist colors strengthens his account or turns pursuers and bodyguards against one another.

## Few people, prepared openings

The Knives cannot defeat companies in open formation. They use poison, copied tokens, false uniforms, damaged tack, confined routes, panic, fire, hidden blades, compromised messengers, attacks on witnesses and records, and threats to infrastructure or transport. They prefer moments when procedure predictably separates a principal from weapons, guards, or trusted witnesses.

## Confusion is part of the weapon

A successful cell makes each larger force misidentify the danger, spend protection on the wrong surface, or convert temporary cooperation into a custody mistake. Exposing an assassin can create real common defense without making Dorion an ally, proving every escort act lawful, or resolving who speaks for Ianthe.

Current cells, identities, access, weapons, poisons, targets, signals, casualties, captures, testimony, surviving plans, and attack outcomes remain live encounter and operations-sheet state.
