## Reference Material

## One post, several systems

Six red-stone arches carry the Salt Road across Kestros Gorge. The western abutment joins the remount stable, signal mast, bridge counterweights, crossing office, maintenance ledge, and Heron Sluice. The deck's customary red line separates the old Aulon road district from Myrine's protected trade corridor; it is a place for bounded parleys and transfers, not a sanctuary boundary.

## Work that must be named

The older road code, later warrants, requisition receipts, signal entries, water accounts, and crossing register make bridge action reviewable. A defensible order identifies the resource, duration, witnesses, and person who answers afterward. Broad allegiance does not explain who may spend horses, close a barrier, send a political signal, or change village water.

## Separate control surfaces

The deck carries wagons and mounted companies. The stable changes pace. The mast sends claims toward Beacon Hill. The lower ledge bypasses the deck for people and light equipment. The Heron Sluice feeds the upper reed channels. Holding, disabling, or authorizing one surface does not automatically grant the others, and damage to one function creates costs elsewhere.

Current control, personnel, remount numbers, signals, register entries, bridge damage, barrier position, water state, pursuit, truce, requisitions, and civilian consequences remain live encounter and operations-sheet state.
