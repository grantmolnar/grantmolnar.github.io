## Reference Material

## Aulonite before anyone's ward

Princess Ianthe Aulonid is the adult heir of Aulon's old royal house, daughter of King Leandros and Serathiel of the White Cypress. She is twenty-seven, trained in civic law, a practiced rider, and a poor liar. When Serathiel returned to her own people, Ianthe stayed. She understands herself as Aulonite before anyone's elven cousin, protected guest, or political instrument.

## Counsel me plainly

Ianthe trusts guards who will contradict her and still obey the decision once she has heard the cost. She asks exact questions, notices who has not spoken, dislikes theatrical courage, and becomes more formal when afraid. Her protection compact treats understanding, voluntary choice, and the ability to speak publicly as things an escort must preserve rather than obstacles to be removed.

## A living voice, not a bronze command

The bronze seal can requisition horses, open granaries, and authenticate decrees. It cannot consent, fear, petition, or speak for Ianthe. Only her living, uncoerced speech can begin Myrine sanctuary, and she may separate her person from the seal or delegate work without surrendering authorship. Her route, condition, custody, promises, petition, decisions, and political acts remain live adventure state.
