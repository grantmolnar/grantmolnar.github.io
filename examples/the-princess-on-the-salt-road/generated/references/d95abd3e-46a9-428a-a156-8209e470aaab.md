# The Sable Courier Houses

**Reference ID:** `d95abd3e-46a9-428a-a156-8209e470aaab`  
**Kind:** Organization  
**Aliases:** Sable courier network, House of Blue Lamps, House at Three Cypresses  
**Tags:** couriers, households, signals, obligations

## Summary

A family courier network whose city dye house, hill station, marks, ledgers, and household obligations carry people and testimony without making royal service free.

## Reference Material

## Two houses, one road

The Sable family has maintained royal courier houses for three generations. The House of Blue Lamps is a working dye house inside Aulon with old palace access behind its cistern. The House at Three Cypresses is a limestone change station where the city exits converge, separate, and hand riders toward the bridge or reeds. Myrto Sable keeps the city house; her elder sister Thessa keeps the hill station; Thessa's daughter Eleni knows the official riders, bridge mast, and first reed channels.

## Marks that travel

Courier badges, shutter lamps, route tokens, dry-well signs, ledgers, recognition phrases, and named fallbacks let separated people coordinate without sharing one road. None is self-authenticating. Marks age, codes change, badges are copied or stolen, and a message is only as trustworthy as its chain of people and records.

## Service leaves an account

The houses serve the road through finite labor, animals, shelter, local knowledge, and reputation. Their ledgers also preserve royal arrears, requisitions, fines, repairs, and the names of households made to bear official travel. A prince or princess may request service and issue an obligation; rank does not turn the household into expendable scenery or guarantee that a later government will pay.

## A network, not sanctuary

The Sable houses can relay, provision, hide, witness, or divide a journey within their actual capacity. They cannot erase who passed, make a copied token true, guarantee that a station remains safe, or decide custody for the person they assist. Current household presence, available stores and mounts, arrests, damage, signals, messages, route knowledge, and accepted obligations remain live encounter and ledger state.
