# The Cypress Gate

## Summary

Aulon’s western funeral gate and old road, protected between the inner arch and last stone lion by a visible civic peace older than military command.

## Reference Material

## The road reserved for the dead

The Cypress Gate opens from Aulon's western wall into an older necropolis and the funeral road. It exists so processions do not cross the market. Gate keys, procession books, tomb wardens, family witnesses, and wheel marks preserve a civic memory that does not depend on the strategos of the day.

## The funeral peace

Between the inner arch and the last stone lion, no officer may arrest, no enemy may proclaim a blood feud, and no living hand may draw steel. The peace is civic rather than magical: breaking it remains physically possible and publicly legible. Wardens may bind weapons, test a mourning claim, surround a suspect without blades, and record who broke the peace and why.

## Roads after the lion

The funeral road carries horses south through tombs and abandoned vineyards. A hidden postern reaches the Horn road; a vineyard crossing reaches the Salt Road above Red Bridge; a farther causeway turns toward the reedward channels. The route trades open force for custom, witnesses, restraint, and slower travel.

Current processions, wardens, observers, assassins, weapon bindings, testimony, route choice, and any breach or preservation of the peace remain live encounter state.
