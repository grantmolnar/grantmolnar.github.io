# Myrine Harbor

**Reference ID:** `64f67879-a64e-46ec-ab14-1e5ef542dffd`  
**Kind:** Place  
**Aliases:** Myrine sanctuary, the seven white stones  
**Tags:** sanctuary, neutrality, records, harbor

## Summary

A neutral harbor whose sanctuary depends on visible separation, free crossing, spoken remedy, three recorded judgments, and protected transfer rather than possession of a person or seal.

## Reference Material

## Neutrality made from visible labor

Myrine Harbor joins the upper spear court, seven white stones, petitioner and claimant lanes, sanctuary hall, registrar's tables, chainhouse, League Walk, fish stairs, and league quay. Guards, chain crews, magistrates, substitutes, registrars, witnesses, and sailors perform different parts of neutrality. No one office owns the entire result.

## The sanctuary sequence

A petitioner establishes identity, armed claimants are separated, and the petitioner crosses the seven stones freely. The petitioner then names self, choice, fear, and requested remedy in a living voice. Three magistrates or publicly entered substitutes record whether they heard free speech and grant protection within Myrine law. Only after the third affirmative record does the harbor transfer the petitioner under sanctuary.

## Protection is not constitutional judgment

Myrine may separate companies, set weapon conditions, protect a hearing, preserve duplicate records, hold disputed property, shelter a petitioner, or transfer someone to lawful transport. It need not decide who should govern Aulon. The bronze seal may establish identity or support a property claim; it cannot cross freely, state a fear, or choose a remedy. A ship may carry a sanctuary transfer; it cannot create one.

Current arrivals, lanes, weapons, witnesses, magistrates, substitutions, petition wording, judgments, records, seal custody, chain position, ship availability, transfer, arrests, and final remedy remain live encounter and operations-sheet state.
