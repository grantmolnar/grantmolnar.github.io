# Serathiel of the White Cypress

## Summary

Ianthe's elven mother, whose distant household can make her daughter genuinely safe while also deciding when kin-protection ends and political wardship begins.

## Reference Material

## The mother who left Aulon

Serathiel of the White Cypress is Ianthe's mother. She returned to her own people ten years before the road, after every family appearance in Aulon became a referendum on foreign rule. Ianthe stayed. The separation left real kinship, affection, and political suspicion on both sides of any later offer of protection.

## A court behind the forest road

Serathiel's household has standing to receive Ianthe as kin and the power to keep ordinary Aulonite pursuit from simply taking her back. That safety is not neutral sanctuary. White Cypress protection carries its own judgment about danger, departure, and who may act for a protected guest; hospitality, kinship authority, and wardship are distinct possibilities rather than interchangeable words.

## Kinship cannot speak the petition

Serathiel may authorize an envoy and household protection, but neither motherhood nor superior safety turns an imposed transfer into Ianthe's choice or speaks a Myrine petition in her place. Any active offer, accepted limit, communication right, destination, custody arrangement, or political authorship remains live state in the escort ledger and aftermath record.
