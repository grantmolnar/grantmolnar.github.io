# The Aulonite Bronze Seal

**Reference ID:** `c487ef1c-b7f3-46ea-8ce8-cf61b5655c6e`

## Summary

A palm-weight state seal bearing three hills above an open hand: powerful enough to authenticate and requisition, incapable of consent, fear, petition, or political authorship.

## Reference Material

## Authority cut into bronze

The Aulonite seal is a palm-weight bronze matrix bearing three hills above an open hand. Within the offices and laws that recognize it, an impression can authenticate decrees, requisition horses or stores, open granaries, commission couriers, and support a claim to public property. The act should leave wax, witnesses, receipts, ledgers, or another record through which the authority can later be reviewed.

## The object and the person do different work

The seal can establish that someone possesses a recognized instrument or that a document bears its impression. It cannot prove who freely chose to use it, whether the bearer is Ianthe, whether an escort acts with continuing consent, or which constitutional claim is just. It cannot cross sanctuary stones, name fear, request protection, or substitute for living speech.

## Portable, divisible, contestable

The seal can travel apart from Ianthe, support a decoy, authenticate a bounded obligation, be deposited as disputed property, or become the subject of a separate recovery effort. Those possibilities make custody of the object politically important without making it custody of the princess.

Current possession, concealment, impressions, requisitions, receipts, loss, damage, impoundment, route, legal effect, and final disposition remain live encounter, ledger, and aftermath state.
