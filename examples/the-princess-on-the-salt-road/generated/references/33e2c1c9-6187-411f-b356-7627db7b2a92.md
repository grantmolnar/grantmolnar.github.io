## Summary

Serathiel’s forest household, whose kin-protection can defeat ordinary pursuit while creating a separate jurisdiction over reception, danger, and departure.

## Reference Material

## Kinship with institutions behind it

The White Cypress is Serathiel's forest household and court, not a poetic name for private maternal affection. It has standing to receive Ianthe as kin, riders and roads outside ordinary Aulonite relay, and enough force and custom to make its protection real. That protection enters a jurisdiction with its own authority over guests, household danger, and departure.

## Aid and reception are different acts

A bounded request can name guides, scouts, messages, a decoy trail, or evacuation for threatened households, together with endpoint, numbers, duration, and withdrawal. Full reception places the protected person under White Cypress authority until that authority judges the danger ended. Hospitality, limited assistance, kin protection, and wardship must therefore be named rather than exchanged as synonyms.

## Boundaries between roads

White Cypress riders do not become an Aulonite command section merely because they share an enemy. Their household authority does not extend through Myrine's armed lanes, complete an Aulonite petition, or turn a safer destination into Ianthe's unspoken consent. The same limits make a clear offer trustworthy: help is what the record says it is.

Current envoys, riders, messages, aid, terms, written requests, routes, presence, reception, custody, withdrawal, communications, and political authorship remain live encounter, ledger, and aftermath state.
