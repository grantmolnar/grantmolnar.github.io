## Summary

The last height above Myrine, where a visible code, authenticated clay strip, ledger entry, witnesses, and three unequal descents shape the harbor’s first interpretation.

## Reference Material

## The hill that supplies the first nouns

Beacon Hill stands between the inland road and Myrine Harbor. From its crown, the keeper can see the Red Bridge mast, reed channels, harbor approaches, and the upper works beyond the breakwater. Its low wall cannot decide a battle. Its basket, shutters, speaking trumpet, message route, and visible formations tell the harbor whether to prepare for a traveler, armed company, disputed authority, assassination danger, or petition.

## A signal has a chain

A formal message joins a visible code, the keeper's brass seal on a clay strip, and a ledger entry naming the observer or authority behind it. Seizing the basket changes what the harbor sees; it does not authenticate the hand, timing, witness, or conclusion. The keeper may report what was received, observed, or tested without deciding Ianthe's freedom or Aulon's government.

## Three descents, three destinations

The Salt Steps are the fastest and most public route toward the white stones. The Olive Stairs conceal a foot party through terraces and the fish market. The Ropewalk Track reaches the chainhouse and league quay rather than sanctuary. Horses, weapons, witnesses, wounded people, a principal, and a disputed object may therefore require separate routes and rendezvous.

Current flame, code, clay strips, ledger custody, keepers, witnesses, route control, weapon posture, arrivals, bell state, descent choices, and harbor inferences remain live encounter and operations-sheet state.
