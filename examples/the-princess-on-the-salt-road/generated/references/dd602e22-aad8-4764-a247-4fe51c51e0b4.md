## Summary

Ianthe's White Cypress cousin and Serathiel's envoy, who offers real protection while naming the jurisdiction carried by every forest road and leaf-sealed promise.

## Reference Material

## Envoy beneath the white cypress

Naevan is Ianthe's cousin and Serathiel's envoy beyond Aulon. He speaks for a household able to shelter kin, move through forest country, and defend people placed under its protection. He does not arrive as an Aulonite officer, a neutral magistrate, or a bodyguard under the escort's command.

## Name aid and custody separately

Naevan distinguishes temporary assistance from full household reception and refuses to disguise one as the other. A bounded request should name the help, the people protected, the endpoint, and the road back; full reception places the protected person under White Cypress authority until that authority judges the danger ended. The distinction is part of his integrity, not a hidden exception discovered after agreement.

## Protection carries jurisdiction

Naevan will fight assassins and defend people lawfully under his protection. He will not serve under Aulonite command, attack an Aulonite captain merely for pursuing a warrant, or enter Myrine as an armed company entitled to ignore its lanes. His current presence, route, available aid, written terms, separation point, extraction status, and any custody outcome remain live adventure state.
