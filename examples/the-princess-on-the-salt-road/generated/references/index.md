# Reference Index: The Princess on the Salt Road

## People

- [**Princess Ianthe Aulonid**](644282b7-3868-49ce-b3f0-d3dc07adc9dd.md) — Aulon's twenty-seven-year-old half-elven heir, trained in civic law and determined that protection preserve her informed choice, living voice, and public authorship. Aliases: Ianthe Aulonid, Ianthe, Princess Ianthe.
- [**Strategos Kallias Arven**](04503a2a-aaaf-45e2-861a-82086beb6b08.md) — Aulon's wartime strategos, whose plausible diagnosis of a constitutional crisis became a coup built around military continuity, controlled testimony, and Ianthe's apparent assent. Aliases: Kallias Arven, Kallias, Strategos Kallias.
- [**Captain Dorion Vey**](f02f94a8-169f-4631-a568-097aa3cd1874.md) — Kallias's recovery captain, exact inside a bad warrant: he records every bounded concession, protects the principal his commission requires alive, and never mistakes tactical cooperation for consent. Aliases: Dorion Vey, Dorion, Captain Dorion.
- [**Naevan of the White Cypress**](dd602e22-aad8-4764-a247-4fe51c51e0b4.md) — Ianthe's White Cypress cousin and Serathiel's envoy, who offers real protection while naming the jurisdiction carried by every forest road and leaf-sealed promise. Aliases: Naevan, Serathiel's envoy.
- [**Serathiel of the White Cypress**](160fb7e4-c2bb-43d0-a1c2-6a93cdbe9d26.md) — Ianthe's elven mother, whose distant household can make her daughter genuinely safe while also deciding when kin-protection ends and political wardship begins. Aliases: Serathiel.

## Places

- [**The Gate of Horns**](7629c10a-cdd0-461c-a726-46e8d5d97bce.md) — Aulon’s public southern gate, where the royal-house charter, livestock traffic, witnesses, and the mounted Salt Road make passage fast and reviewable. Aliases: Horns Gate, south gate.
- [**The Cypress Gate**](1f0a8db8-dbf8-4a1a-8d4f-b4bf1dbbc83e.md) — Aulon’s western funeral gate and old road, protected between the inner arch and last stone lion by a visible civic peace older than military command. Aliases: western funeral gate, necropolis gate.
- [**The Red Bridge**](39a5afa6-d1c3-43d3-9f9a-ecffcfd76d03.md) — Six red arches joining the mounted road, remount post, signal mast, crossing register, lower works, and Heron Sluice without making control of one control of all. Aliases: Kestros bridge, bridge post.
- [**The Reed Villages**](84c000cd-1862-436c-9f8e-c98fce5634f9.md) — Three linked settlements whose boats, pilots, households, channels, and shared water turn concealment and evacuation into finite civic choices. Aliases: Kalamai, Vathys, and Nesos, reed channels.
- [**Beacon Hill**](bcee41cb-fe8c-4fd1-99a3-00906f76d751.md) — The last height above Myrine, where a visible code, authenticated clay strip, ledger entry, witnesses, and three unequal descents shape the harbor’s first interpretation. Aliases: Myrine beacon, beacon tower.
- [**Myrine Harbor**](64f67879-a64e-46ec-ab14-1e5ef542dffd.md) — A neutral harbor whose sanctuary depends on visible separation, free crossing, spoken remedy, three recorded judgments, and protected transfer rather than possession of a person or seal. Aliases: Myrine sanctuary, the seven white stones.

## Organizations

- [**The Sable Courier Houses**](d95abd3e-46a9-428a-a156-8209e470aaab.md) — A family courier network whose city dye house, hill station, marks, ledgers, and household obligations carry people and testimony without making royal service free. Aliases: Sable courier network, House of Blue Lamps, House at Three Cypresses.
- [**The White Cypress**](33e2c1c9-6187-411f-b356-7627db7b2a92.md) — Serathiel’s forest household, whose kin-protection can defeat ordinary pursuit while creating a separate jurisdiction over reception, danger, and departure. Aliases: White Cypress court, Serathiel's household.
- [**The Ash Knives**](a4c3844d-a690-499a-9520-9e59f57d1919.md) — Small antimonarchist cells that seek to end the royal succession by exploiting loyalist colors, confined ground, procedural openings, and distrust among larger armed parties. Aliases: Ash Knife cells, the Knives.

## Objects

- [**The Aulonite Bronze Seal**](c487ef1c-b7f3-46ea-8ce8-cf61b5655c6e.md) — A palm-weight state seal bearing three hills above an open hand: powerful enough to authenticate and requisition, incapable of consent, fear, petition, or political authorship. Aliases: bronze seal, royal seal, city seal.
