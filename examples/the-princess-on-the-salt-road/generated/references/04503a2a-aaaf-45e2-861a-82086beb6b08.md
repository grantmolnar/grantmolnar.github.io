## Summary

Aulon's wartime strategos, whose plausible diagnosis of a constitutional crisis became a coup built around military continuity, controlled testimony, and Ianthe's apparent assent.

## Reference Material

## War office beneath white lamps

Kallias Arven is Aulon's professional strategos, elected for war while King Leandros lived. After the king died before physicians, household officers, and Assembly witnesses, Kallias faced a divided Assembly, an adult half-elven heir, and an army still answering wartime warrants. He chose occupied gates, closed institutions, and command lamps over an uncontrolled vote. His diagnosis is not wholly false. His remedy is a coup.

## Continuity in another person's voice

Kallias wants emergency rule to look like lawful continuity. A living Ianthe, an intact bronze seal, and a protector proclamation that appears voluntary can make military control resemble constitutional consent. Public bloodshed, contradictory witnesses, and records he does not control expose the difference. His political method therefore values disciplined force, bounded warrants, and a public account in which armed retainers appear to be the danger.

## Command cannot manufacture assent

The strategos can issue military warrants, direct officers, hold civic infrastructure, and argue that order requires protection. He cannot make coerced speech free, make the seal consent, or replace Myrine's sanctuary judgment with Aulonite command. His operative orders, force locations, pursuit posture, reports, concessions, custody claims, and changing public account belong to encounters and the pursuit, custody, beacon, and harbor instruments.
