# Beacon and Harbor Operations

This sheet keeps the final approach legible at the table. It is not a hidden scorecard. State visible signals, bells, weapons, custody, and route divisions openly.

## Beacon Hill control surfaces

| Surface | What it changes | What remains outside it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Beacon basket and shutters | Myrine's first interpretation of the approaching company | Whether Ianthe completes sanctuary |
| Keeper's authenticated clay strip | Whether a changed signal is trusted as more than a seized flame | Who should govern Aulon |
| Salt Steps | Fastest and most visible approach to the white stones | Safe passage for two armed companies together |
| Olive Stairs | Concealed foot approach through the fish market | A lawful petition by itself |
| Ropewalk Track | Direct access to chainhouse and league quay | Access to the sanctuary hall |
| Tower witnesses | Evidence of Ianthe's voluntary travel and attacks on the road | Automatic innocence for every bodyguard act |

## Signal lexicon

| Signal | Meaning in Myrine | Harbor response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Steady white | Principal under armed compulsion | Close Salt Steps; prepare coercion inquiry |
| White with three dark intervals | Authority disputed; will unverified | Admit unarmed petitioner; separate armed parties |
| Gold above blue | Principal travelling by declared choice | Prepare reception; retain weapon rules |
| Gold broken by red | Free petitioner under assassination threat | Send magistrates, medical aid, and limited armed protection |
| Red in paired bursts | Assassins or pirates among mixed parties | Distinguish parties by identity and conduct |
| Blue low | Chainhouse or harbor works threatened | Reinforce western harbor and reassess departure |
| Extinguished after warning | Tower status unknown or seized | Distrust later arrivals; rely on direct testimony |

A changed flame without an authenticated strip is useful but incomplete. A truthful mixed signal is more durable than a falsely simple declaration that everything is safe.

## Three descents

### Salt Steps

- Fastest route to the white stones.
- Horses may descend at a walk.
- Visible to harbor, pursuit, and road witnesses.
- Best for a public free-choice account.
- Requires separation, surrender, peace-binding, or an exception for armed companions.

### Olive Stairs

- Concealed foot route through abandoned terraces and the fish market.
- No horses; litters are slow and conspicuous.
- Best for a small principal group, decoy, witness runner, or emergency evasion.
- Risks appearing to bypass lawful examination unless accompanied by a guide or authenticated report.

### Ropewalk Track

- Pack route to the chainhouse and Sea-Lark's quay.
- Best for the seal, ship defense, wounded companions, or a separate boarding group.
- Does not reach the white stones and cannot substitute for petition.
- Armed arrivals may be mistaken for attackers on harbor works.

## Chain clock

| State | Visible condition | Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Before first bell | Gangplank down; cargo nets open | Full hearing and ordinary boarding remain available |
| First bell | Channel clearing; cargo rising | Delay begins to threaten coordinated boarding |
| Second bell | Gangplank withdrawn or quick-released | Boarding requires direct harbor action, a boat, or chainhouse access |
| Final bell | Chain at pulley; Sea-Lark moving | Sanctuary remains available; immediate extraction does not |

Advance the clock for fictional delay: inquiry, repair, treatment, searching, fighting, route transfer, or negotiation. Do not advance it because the table discussed a plan.

## Sanctuary procedure

1. **Establish identity.** Seal, magistrate recognition, witnesses, household tokens, or Dorion's acknowledgment may suffice.
2. **Separate claimants.** Myrine assigns lanes and weapon conditions to bodyguards, soldiers, and other armed parties.
3. **Cross freely.** Ianthe crosses seven white stones unbound, undirected, and without a drawn weapon.
4. **Speak.** She names herself, whose choice brought her, whom she fears, and what remedy she requests.
5. **Record three judgments.** Three magistrates or publicly named substitutes affirm whether they heard free speech and grant protection.
6. **Transfer under sanctuary.** Myrine escorts Ianthe to the hall, city protection, or Sea-Lark according to the granted remedy.

No one else can complete the petition for an unconscious or silenced Ianthe. Medical protection can preserve her until she can speak.

## Weapon options

| Option | Advantage | Cost or risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Remain armed outside | Strong road and spear-court defense | Separation from Ianthe and the petition |
| Surrender weapons | Full access through hall and League Walk | Reduced response if an assassin is already inside |
| Peace-bind selected weapons | Visible compromise; chosen protectors remain close | Drawing becomes a public breach requiring justification |
| Limited armed exception | Ready defense in a named zone | Requires a specific threat and explicit responsibilities |
| Conceal weapons | Surprise against assassins | Severe loss of trust if discovered |

## Adversary priorities

### Dorion

1. Keep Ianthe alive.
2. Contest coercion and delay an uncontested political finding.
3. Recover the seal.
4. Keep the bodyguards from becoming her recognized lawful escort.
5. Avoid war with Myrine.

Dorion may defend Ianthe against assassins, accept sanctuary after the third record, and continue contesting seal, arrests, and public interpretation.

### Ash Knives

1. Kill Ianthe before or after the petition.
2. Break the third record or attack its people and tablets.
3. Create panic between Myrine, Dorion, and the bodyguards.
4. Strand Ianthe by attacking chainhouse or Sea-Lark.

Their attacks should target predictable procedural openings rather than appear from nowhere.

### Myrine

1. Hear Ianthe freely.
2. Prevent armed companies from fighting across the sanctuary line.
3. Protect harbor works and neutral treaty standing.
4. Preserve the records and execute the remedy granted.

Myrine is not a passive finish line and is not secretly allied with Kallias.

## Route split record

Before descending, record for every group:

- principal, object, or witness carried;
- leader;
- descent;
- weapon condition;
- rendezvous;
- recognition phrase or signal;
- fallback if the Sea-Lark leaves; and
- adversary believed to know the route.

## Mixed-success prompts

At resolution, answer each question rather than announcing “the princess is safe.”

- Did Ianthe speak freely, and what remedy did she choose?
- Who holds the seal?
- Did she sail, remain in Myrine, accept elven protection, or return under Dorion?
- Which bodyguards remained with her, and which paid the road's cost?
- What did the magistrates record about escort conduct?
- Which witnesses survive, and what account reaches Aulon first?
- Which communities receive retaliation, protection, or recognition?
