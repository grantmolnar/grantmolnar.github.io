# Pursuit and Custody: The Princess on the Salt Road

## Purpose

This document is a table aid for the open road pressure introduced at the House at Three Cypresses. It does not replace the encounter prose in `adventure.json`. Its purpose is to make time, pursuit, negotiation, and rival protection legible enough for informed bodyguard decisions.

## The pursuit posture

Track one current posture for Dorion's principal force. Announce it and name the facts that support it.

| Posture | What Dorion knows and can do | Typical signs | What can worsen it | What can reduce it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Unfixed** | He knows Ianthe escaped but not which route now carries her. Scouts question roads and posts rather than concentrating. | Distant horns, searches at several exits, riders asking rather than blocking | A named witness, captured helper, seal use, obvious tracks, a failed decoy | A clean route change, contradictory witnesses, a credible false trail |
| **Named** | Dorion has a probable route or rendezvous but not a reliable pace or exact position. He sends heralds and messages ahead. | White-cloth demands, road questions ahead of the escort, signal shutters changing | Delay on the named route, a recorded requisition, a helper revealing pace | Route division, a decoy believed, leaving the named road before the next relay |
| **Closing** | Dorion knows route and pace and has infrastructure sufficient to intercept at the next major encounter. | Fresh cavalry dust, posts closing, remounts withheld, messages arriving before the escort | Waiting, using the obvious remount line, a credible beacon confirmation | Denying remounts, breaking or subverting a signal, forcing Dorion to protect civilians or fight assassins |
| **Contact** | Dorion can demand custody, blockade the obvious road, negotiate face to face, or attempt seizure. | Visible riders, herald within speech, controlled crossing or road | A failed parley, attack under truce, loss of an escape route | Successful separation, negotiated passage, terrain cavalry cannot use, a decisive decoy |

The posture is not a countdown to combat. **Contact** can produce a bargain, joint action against the Ash Knives, surveillance, a running escape, or custody without a battle. **Unfixed** does not mean the road is safe from assassins or local patrols.

## Pursuit principles

1. **Dorion must travel.** He cannot appear beyond geography, remounts, and signals merely because the escort delayed.
2. **Knowledge and reach are related but distinct.** Witnesses may fix Ianthe's route while making open violence politically costly.
3. **Infrastructure can be contested.** Remounts, bridge orders, courier badges, lamp codes, and boats are objectives, not background scenery.
4. **A decoy must carry something believable.** The seal, a known rider, a public witness, official clothing, or a credible signal can draw pursuit. An empty cloak does not automatically fool professionals.
5. **State reversals are real.** A route split or false beacon can move the posture backward. Do not preserve escalation merely because it has already occurred.
6. **State the change.** Describe the evidence and name the posture when it changes.

## Travel windows

Use named windows instead of hidden points. Exact hours are unnecessary unless a scene makes them important.

| Window | Expected road position | What a substantial delay risks |
|---|---|---|
| **Departure night** | Leaving Aulon and reaching the first ridge | Dorion fixes an exit; the escort reaches the rendezvous exhausted or after dawn |
| **First dawn** | House at Three Cypresses or an equivalent improvised rendezvous | The Red Bridge refreshes the pursuit; reed households receive searches before warning |
| **First day** | Crossing the Red Bridge or entering the Reed Villages | The escort loses mounted daylight, boat access, or time to repair a false harbor story |
| **First sunset** | Beyond the principal crossing, resting briefly, or changing routes | A full rest becomes politically expensive; Dorion can place messages at Beacon Hill |
| **Second dawn** | Approaching Beacon Hill or the lower coast | Little time remains to correct signals, gather witnesses, or recover from a divided route |
| **Second day** | Beacon Hill and Myrine's approach | Every major detour threatens arrival before the harbor chain rises |
| **Second sunset** | Sea-Lark departure and chain rise | Myrine sanctuary remains possible, but neutral extraction and immediate leverage are lost |

A substantial delay is normally an hour-scale undertaking: full rest, careful evacuation, extended parley, rebuilding transport, or waiting for a reply. Brief counsel, ordinary inspection, and coherent parallel tasks should not each consume a window.

## Custody ledger

When Ianthe changes hands or accepts protection, record three separate answers.

| Question | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Who has physical custody?** | Bodyguards, Dorion's riders, Naevan's outriders, Myrine's harbor guard, Ianthe moving alone |
| **Whose law claims custody?** | Aulonite recovery warrant, White Cypress kin-protection, Myrine sanctuary, no settled claim |
| **Who authors the public meaning?** | Ianthe in her own voice, Kallias through a proclamation, witnesses through testimony, an armed company through appearances |

A scene can change one answer without changing the others. Dorion may seize the seal and surround Ianthe while failing to obtain assent. Naevan may keep Ianthe physically safe while closing the road to Myrine. Myrine may recognize sanctuary while arresting the bodyguards.

## Dorion's usable truces

Dorion may offer or accept the following without ceasing to be a pursuer.

### Assassin truce

Both sides keep Ianthe on her chosen side of a visible line, identify the suspected Ash Knife, inspect tack or food, and exchange prisoners or evidence afterward. The truce ends when the immediate threat is contained.

### Wounded and civilian truce

Both sides stop pursuit long enough to recover casualties, move mourners, clear a burning house, or evacuate children from a crossing. Neither side improves a firing position. Messages may continue.

### Crossing truce

At a bridge, channel, or narrow descent, the parties agree on order of movement and forbid attacks while anyone is in the hazard. Custody demands resume on safe ground.

### Witness truce

Ianthe states her position before named witnesses while Dorion states his order. Neither side treats the statement itself as surrender. The witnesses may leave with copies before pursuit resumes.

A truce should change posture, time, or public standing. It should not be decorative dialogue before an inevitable fight.

## Dorion's settlement boundary

Dorion can negotiate procedure, witnesses, weapons during transfer, temporary movement, amnesty terms within his warrant, and release of low-level detainees. He cannot concede Myrine, elven custody, retention of the seal after surrender, restoration of the Assembly as a precondition, or a binding end to Kallias's authority. A proposal outside that boundary can still expose what the escort values or create a public refusal.

## Naevan's protection boundary

Full extraction places Ianthe under White Cypress protection and judgment. Limited aid requires Ianthe's written request and may provide two scouts, one guided decoy, message carriage, or evacuation of two households. Naevan will fight assassins and defend people under his protection. He will not serve under Aulonite command, attack Dorion solely for pursuing, or enter Myrine as an armed company.

## House at Three Cypresses transition checklist

Before the escort leaves the rendezvous, state:

- the current travel window;
- the pursuit posture and why;
- Ianthe's physical and political condition;
- who carries the bronze seal;
- which mounts, cart, badges, and signal tools leave the house;
- whether the tampered tack and cordial were discovered;
- what Dorion believes the escort agreed to;
- what Naevan expects;
- which households have been warned, evacuated, exposed, or left unaided; and
- the primary route, split route, rendezvous, and fallback.

These facts should enter the Red Bridge or Reed Villages as active circumstances, not be reset between encounters.
