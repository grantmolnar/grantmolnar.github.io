# Full Playthrough: The Narrow Shield

Blue dye marks the first room; black harbor water marks the last. Between them, this playthrough demonstrates one coherent path through *The Princess on the Salt Road*, not a preferred solution. The Narrow Shield visits seven of nine encounters, divides body from seal after the courier house, accepts elven help without yielding destination, reaches Myrine beneath a disputed white flame, and discovers that successful protection may leave two guards, the bronze seal, and much of the road’s evidence ashore.

The route is:

**House of Blue Lamps -> Dry Aqueduct -> House at Three Cypresses -> Reed Villages / Red Bridge -> Beacon Hill -> Myrine Harbor.**

The Reed Villages and Red Bridge scenes occur in parallel. The play journal records them in the order the table resolved them, not as an assertion that one group traveled through both places.

## Session One: Under Stone, Under Oath

### The House of Blue Lamps

The adventure opens after midnight. Myrto has barred the dye-house shutters, but light still leaks blue through the seams. Ianthe has removed the palace gold from her clothing and kept the bronze city seal on a cord beneath her tunic. Timon Vale wants to come. Myrto wants everyone out before the next patrol reaches the lane.

Damon asks whether Ianthe is directing an escape or accepting an escort arranged by the household. She answers by giving the compact in her own words: counsel her plainly, act without permission only against an immediate danger she cannot judge, and obey when she decides. She adds the sentence that governs the rest of the journey: “Do not make me safe by making me absent.”

The Narrow Shield reviews all three exits. The Gate of Horns is fastest and gives excellent public witnesses, but Dorion would know their road immediately. The Cypress Gate preserves funeral peace but forbids the formation Damon wants and risks damaging a custom used by ordinary mourners. The Dry Aqueduct is slow, cramped, and horseless, but it leaves the pursuit Unfixed.

Thaleia urges a public declaration at the Gate of Horns. Phaedra argues that Kallias already controls the first account and that one clean hour matters more than a crowd they cannot protect. Ianthe chooses the aqueduct. She sends Timon to Thaleia's aunt with a copied appointment of the Narrow Shield and orders him to appear before any remaining Assembly witnesses at dawn. Timon is not a decoy; his task is to establish that Ianthe chose her escort before leaving the city.

The group studies Dorion's recovery warrant and the Ash Knife broadside. They understand that Dorion needs Ianthe alive, while the assassins need only confusion. They also understand the second-sunset deadline and the free-petition rule. In the rush, no one opens Serathiel's sealed letter. The first missed opportunity is therefore political rather than tactical: the Narrow Shield leaves without learning that full elven extraction would place Ianthe under White Cypress judgment.

Myrto gives them lime-worker cords, a physician's lamp, food, two coils of rope, and a padded false seal case. Ianthe keeps the real seal. The group enters the dye cistern before Kallias's patrol knocks at the front door.

**State leaving the encounter:** departure night; Sea-Lark deadline secure; pursuit Unfixed; Ianthe physically and politically in the Narrow Shield's custody by her own direction; seal with Ianthe; Timon remains inside Aulon as a voluntary witness.

### The Dry Aqueduct

Damon sets marching order before the lime throat: Phaedra first, Ianthe second, Melia third, Thaleia fourth, Damon last. A tug on the left-hand rope means halt; two quick taps mean reverse; a low whistle means the front has found open ground. The arrangement matters when a patrol above removes a maintenance stone and lowers a lamp into the channel. Phaedra smothers the light with a wet lime cloth while the others hold still beneath it.

At the broken bowl, Pela the Limeburner demands a pardon for her imprisoned brother in exchange for opening the lower sluice quietly. Ianthe refuses to promise a pardon she cannot presently enforce. She writes instead that Pela aided her by free agreement and that any future Aulonite hearing must distinguish that act from participation in violence. Pela calls it thin payment. Thaleia answers that it is exact payment. Pela accepts and shows them how to relieve the swollen gate through the maintenance crack rather than breaking it into the bath drains.

The careful work costs time but preserves concealment. Melia finds a boot print in the wet lime where no worker should have stood. The Narrow Shield cannot tell whether it belongs to a patrol scout or a smuggler. Damon does not chase the uncertainty. The expedition remains Unfixed.

Phaedra examines the old water marks and identifies two forward options: the gorge road toward Red Bridge and the cracked feeder toward the Reed Villages. She also confirms that the bronze disk can travel apart from Ianthe. The group misses Pela's cross-ridge marks toward the Cypress funeral road. This closes no route, but it removes a later retreat option from their working map.

They emerge before dawn in an olive cutting south-east of Aulon. Phaedra leaves one true sign for any household courier: three chalk cypresses with the middle trunk broken. It means the principal is alive, the rendezvous remains intended, and no one should follow the visible tracks without a second sign.

**State leaving the encounter:** first dawn approaching; deadline secure; pursuit Unfixed but Aulon knows the bath district may matter; no mounts; seal still with Ianthe; the Narrow Shield knows both middle roads and can divide later.

### The House at Three Cypresses

Thessa Sable admits the group by the broken-middle-tree sign. She has four fresh horses, two tired mares, a mule, courier badges, a dispatch cart, and less secrecy than Myrto promised. Two stable hands have been arrested, and their families are being fined. The Narrow Shield has reached a place of supply by bringing danger to a household already paying for loyalty.

Thessa's inspection of the cordial is interrupted by Dorion's herald at the outer wall. Melia checks the cups and finds bitter residue under the wax. She replaces the drink and warns the household. No one gives equal attention to the remount tack. The cut girth prepared for Ianthe remains undiscovered and is placed on Phaedra's horse when the company divides.

Dorion's white-cloth offer is read in the yard. He promises amnesty for the bodyguards, release of several road detainees, and a thirty-day protector proclamation if Ianthe returns with the seal. Ianthe insists that the herald hear her answer in front of Thessa, the household, and the Narrow Shield: she will submit to a public Assembly convened without soldiers; she will not enter private recovery custody or name Kallias protector first. Dorion's offer is credible, but it cannot reach her governing objective.

Naevan arrives by the orchard wall with a leaf-sealed letter from Serathiel. Because the Narrow Shield missed the first letter, his terms land with greater force. Full extraction would protect Ianthe but make her departure subject to White Cypress judgment. Ianthe refuses it. She does request limited aid in writing: two scouts to create a forestward trail and assistance evacuating two threatened reed households if Naevan reaches them first. Thaleia records that the request gives no command over Ianthe and no authority to divert her from Myrine. Naevan accepts the words and remarks that Kallias will quote the signature without the limits.

The counsel circle produces the Narrow Shield's decisive split. Phaedra proposes that she and Thaleia carry the real bronze seal openly to Red Bridge, requisition remounts, and create an authenticated trail. Ianthe, Damon, and Melia will use Thessa's covered cart to reach the Reed Villages, then proceed by boat. The seal detail's genuine mission is to deny Dorion the bridge remount reserve and preserve a public record of royal authority. It is not an empty decoy.

Damon objects that only one sworn shield will remain with Ianthe. Ianthe answers that Melia is sworn, Naevan's two scouts will watch the northern track without joining her formation, and hiding all four guards around her would let Dorion concentrate every rider on the real body. She orders the division.

The groups record their commitments:

- **Principal group:** Ianthe, Damon, Melia; covered cart to the Heron levee; recognition sign a blue thread knotted twice; rendezvous eastern terrace of Beacon Hill; fallback direct descent by the Olive Stairs if the tower is hostile.
- **Seal group:** Phaedra, Thaleia; mounted road to Red Bridge; real seal and duplicate appointment leaves; recognition sign a courier tablet scored diagonally; rendezvous Beacon Hill's inland saddle; fallback lower towpath to the reed channels.

Phaedra takes the horse with the cut girth. Thessa enters Ianthe's answer and chosen division in the courier ledger. This protects the public account and moves the pursuit from Unfixed to Named.

**State leaving the encounter:** first day; deadline secure but reduced; pursuit Named toward both the bridge and reed approaches, with greater confidence on the seal detail; physical custody with the two sworn members beside Ianthe; legal claim disputed among Ianthe, Kallias, and White Cypress; public meaning preserved by Thessa's ledger.

## Session Two: Two Roads, One Principal

### The Reed Villages — principal group

The covered cart reaches Kalamai while soldiers are dragging boats onto the confiscation landing. Naevan's scouts have arrived first and placed a false trail toward the forest road, but they have not entered the villages as an armed company. Demea Pheros wants Ianthe to leave before more houses are searched. Mara Seven-Oars wants the princess to see who pays for every elegant route decision.

Ianthe walks the drying racks openly with Damon at one shoulder and Melia at the other. She does not reveal the seal because she no longer carries it. She tells the assembled pilots that she intends to petition for a free Aulonite convocation and will not demand boats without terms. Demea asks for the release of three arrested pilots and transport for two households whose homes stand below the Heron flood line.

The Reed Villages group cannot satisfy every claim with the available boats. Mara's capacity board makes the choice concrete. A hidden principal can leave immediately. A public flotilla can carry witnesses but not horses or all evacuees. Recovering confiscated boats adds capacity and risks Contact with Sosthenes's soldiers.

The false loyalist Philon Vask arrives with the correct blue-lamp phrase and urges Ianthe into a narrow skiff. Melia notices that his physician's cordial has been sealed from the outside after filling. Damon asks who assigned the pilot. Philon names a man who died the previous winter. The Reed Villages group exposes him before the village witnesses. An Ash Knife token falls from his sleeve when he reaches for Ianthe.

Lieutenant Sosthenes attempts to use the confusion to take Ianthe into “protective” custody. Damon offers an assassin truce instead: Philon is bound on the landing; both sides inspect the remaining boats; Ianthe stays with her chosen guard; Sosthenes may send one witness to observe. Sosthenes agrees because he does not want a dead princess under his command. The truce does not settle custody.

During the inspection, a second Ash Knife ignites the drying racks and cuts the boat chain. The encounter divides objectives: Damon holds the landing and keeps Sosthenes's soldiers from closing on Ianthe; Melia treats a burned child and guards the water jars from poison; Ianthe and Mara organize villagers to save the outer racks; Naevan's two scouts recover the drifting skiffs without drawing weapons against Aulonite troops.

The Reed Villages group wins the boats but pays time. Ianthe chooses an evacuation convoy rather than the fastest hidden-principal route. Two threatened households, the three released pilots, and public witnesses travel by Pilots' Run. Ianthe's skiff uses the Black Thread in parallel, with Damon, Melia, and Mara. Each group has the same eastern-terrace rendezvous and blue-thread recognition sign.

The choice preserves people and testimony but makes the Sea-Lark deadline doubtful. The group also misses a precise piece of harbor law: they do not learn from the pilots that sanctuary remains available after the ship departs. At this stage, they still treat second sunset as a single all-or-nothing threshold.

Ianthe refuses Naevan's renewed offer of full extraction. She thanks the scouts and releases them before Myrine's white stones. The written request and visible elven aid will strengthen Kallias's foreign-influence claim, but the villagers can testify that Ianthe directed the aid toward Aulonite households and continued toward Myrine.

**State leaving the encounter:** first sunset; deadline doubtful; pursuit remains Named on the principal but lacks exact channel; Ash Knives exposed publicly; two households and three pilots protected; principal and witnesses divided on coordinated water routes.

### The Red Bridge — seal group

Phaedra and Thaleia approach the bridge at speed. The cut girth tears on the north slope. Phaedra falls hard, losing the first movement and injuring her shoulder. The missed tack inspection becomes a concrete cost without ending the branch. Thaleia takes the seal case while Phaedra remounts on the tired courier mare.

Melas Orro convenes a bridge hearing rather than obeying the seal immediately. Thaleia presents Ianthe's appointment, the courier ledger copy, and a narrow command: preserve the crossing, deny unauthorized seizure of the remount reserve, and maintain the Heron Sluice for the villages. She does not claim Ianthe is present. The order is valid and creates an authenticated record of the seal's route.

Dorion reaches the bridge during the hearing. The pursuit is Contact with the seal detail, not with Ianthe. He knows the division is likely but cannot know whether it is a decoy or a genuine transfer of state authority. He offers a crossing truce while the bridge counterweight is repaired and asks Thaleia to surrender the seal under receipt. She refuses private transfer and proposes neutral bridge custody until Ianthe's location is known. Dorion cannot concede that much.

An Ash Knife hidden in the lower works starts a fire below the bridge and releases the counterweight, hoping Dorion's riders and royal retainers will kill one another over the falling mechanism. Phaedra sees the fire signal and calls the assassin truce before anyone charges. Dorion's men clear the deck; Phaedra and Melas secure the stable court; Thaleia carries the order to the sluice crew; one of Dorion's scouts captures the saboteur alive.

When the truce ends, Dorion demands the seal again. Phaedra cannot outride fresh cavalry with an injured shoulder and a tired mare. Thaleia decides that preserving the duplicate order, bridge witnesses, and the principal's separation is more important than being captured with the bronze disk. She deposits the seal in Melas's treaty chest under the bridge post's three-party receipt. Dorion immediately places the post under his authority and takes physical possession of the chest, but he cannot erase the receipt or the order already copied to Beacon Hill.

The seal group has lost the object while accomplishing most of its mission. Six pursuit remounts are unsaddled and dispersed among village supply teams rather than killed. The bridge and sluice remain intact. Dorion has the seal and must decide whether to follow the principal, defend the recovered state property, or race to Myrine with a legal claim. He chooses Myrine. Phaedra and Thaleia take the lower towpath toward Beacon Hill.

They do not inspect the Myrine treaty cords kept at the post and therefore miss an early opportunity to plan peace-bound weapons. They will learn the rule later under more pressure.

**State leaving the encounter:** first sunset; seal in Dorion's physical custody under a witnessed bridge receipt; pursuit Contact with the seal detail and only Named on Ianthe; bridge and sluice intact; remount reserve denied without killing horses; Phaedra wounded but mobile.

### Beacon Hill — reunion

The principal group reaches the eastern terrace before second dawn. The seal group arrives at the inland saddle shortly afterward. The reunion works because both groups preserved exact signs and fallbacks. No named adversary has appeared in two places at once: Dorion moved from Red Bridge only after the seal confrontation; Naevan's scouts left the principal group before the hill.

Keeper Phorbas has already received contradictory messages. Thessa's ledger copy says Ianthe travels freely. The Red Bridge record says the bronze seal entered Dorion's custody under dispute. Niko Sarn's signal says armed foreigners are forcing the princess toward the harbor. Pheren Vale has not yet authenticated any of them.

Ianthe chooses a public correction rather than a covert descent. She appears in the cistern court with the reed pilots, the evacuated households, Thaleia, and the injured Phaedra. She states that she divided the escort, requested limited elven aid, and still seeks Myrine by her own decision. The signal sent is not “uncontested royal traveler.” It is the stronger and slower truth: **free petitioner under disputed authority, armed escort divided, assassin threat confirmed, bronze seal absent.**

The authenticated message opens a coercion inquiry and asks for a limited weapons exception. It does not guarantee that the Sea-Lark waits.

Dorion reaches the inland saddle with the sealed bridge chest. He offers to descend jointly if Ianthe accepts a private examination before crossing the white stones. Thaleia insists that any examination be visible and recorded. Ianthe agrees to answer one question apart from Damon but within sight of Melia and Myrine's sergeant: did the Narrow Shield compel her to leave? She answers no and adds that they advised against several choices she made.

An Ash Knife attacks during the signal change from the maintenance ledge while a second agent lights the colored-salt cabinet. Dorion's archers can reach the climber but risk striking Pheren. Damon holds the stair; Phaedra, despite her shoulder, pulls Pheren behind the shutter wall; Melia keeps Ianthe low without dragging her from view; Dorion orders his men to contain the fire rather than seize the principal. The assassin is captured wounded in Dorion's colors. The evidence weakens Kallias's abduction account and creates a genuine joint defense that transfers no custody.

The Narrow Shield chooses the Salt Steps. Concealment has done enough. Ianthe descends publicly with Thaleia, Melia, Damon, two reed witnesses, and Myrine Sergeant Timon Quill. Phaedra takes the Ropewalk Track with several harbor guards to warn the chainhouse and preserve the Sea-Lark. Dorion follows the Salt Steps under separate Myrine guard with the sealed bridge chest. The final division is explicit and geographically real.

At the second chain bell, the Narrow Shield learns the missed harbor-law nuance: sanctuary will remain if the Sea-Lark sails. Ianthe orders everyone to prioritize the petition over the ship. Phaedra may defend extraction, but no one may interrupt the white-stone procedure merely to preserve boarding.

**State leaving the encounter:** second day between the second and final bells; petition legally possible, ship departure imminent; authenticated disputed-authority signal; Ianthe alive, wounded only by exhaustion, and publicly directing the descent; seal with Dorion under witnessed receipt.

## Session Three: Seven Stones and Three Judgments

### Myrine Harbor

Myrine separates the arriving groups at the spear court. Captain Eudora Pell allows Damon one peace-bound spear inside the sanctuary hall because two Ash Knives have been exposed and the authenticated beacon message requests a limited exception. Melia and Thaleia surrender their weapons. Dorion's company remains outside the white stones; Dorion and Niko enter unarmed with the bridge receipt and Kallias's petition. Phaedra is already at the chainhouse with Myrine guards.

The first stage establishes identity without the bronze seal. Thaleia presents the Assembly roll and appointments; the reed witnesses identify Ianthe; Timon's dawn statement has reached Myrine by a separate coastal messenger. Dorion does not deny identity. He contests capacity and custody.

Myrine orders a brief visible separation. Ianthe crosses the first stones with Registrar Olyra and Magistrate Lysa while the bodyguards remain behind the painted line. Damon dislikes the distance but obeys. Ianthe confirms that the Narrow Shield acted under her direction, that she requested limited elven help, that the seal traveled separately by her order, and that she still seeks sanctuary.

At the fourth stone, an Ash Knife disguised as a bloodied reed witness creates panic in the spear court. The attacker is not trying to win a battle; he wants Myrine to see armed bodyguards rush the petitioner. Damon holds position until the attacker produces a blade. Drawing his peace-bound spear breaks the wax in full view. He strikes the assassin away from Ianthe and stops rather than pursuing into Dorion's line. The broken seal becomes evidence of necessity instead of an unexplained violation.

At the chainhouse, the second attack begins. An agent cuts a spring line while another attempts to foul the chain winch, forcing Phaedra to choose between boarding and preserving the ship. She stays ashore, helps Captain Helike secure the Sea-Lark with a replacement line, and sends the ship's boat toward the League Walk. Her decision makes her own boarding unlikely but keeps extraction possible for Ianthe.

Ianthe reaches the white stones unbound and with no weapon in hand. Her petition is concise: she names herself, says Kallias has confined the Assembly and seeks her assent under force, says the Ash Knives seek her death, and asks Myrine to protect her person while Aulonite citizens and councilors convene freely under neutral witness. She does not ask Myrine to crown her. She does not conceal the elven aid.

Niko challenges the record before the third judgment, arguing that a princess guided by foreign scouts and armed retainers cannot prove free choice. Thaleia answers with the courier ledger, bridge receipt, reed testimony, and Ianthe's willingness to qualify the bodyguards' advice. Dorion asks Myrine to hold the bronze seal as disputed Aulonite state property even if sanctuary is granted. Ianthe accepts that separation rather than delay the petition or trade village sureties for the object.

An Ash Knife makes the last attempt against the record, spilling treated ink across Olyra's tablet. The first two judgments already exist. The third magistrate dictates to a substitute tablet while Melia washes Olyra's hands and identifies the irritant. Damon and Dorion's unarmed aide jointly restrain the attacker. Neither side owns the cooperation.

The third judgment is recorded before the final bell. Myrine recognizes sanctuary and places Ianthe under treaty protection. The bronze seal goes into a Myrine treaty chest pending a later property hearing; Dorion loses physical custody but can truthfully say he recovered it from the road. The Sea-Lark's boat reaches the League Walk with time for Ianthe, Melia, and Thaleia to board. Damon remains at the spear court as a named witness to the attacks and to answer for the broken peace binding. Phaedra remains at the chainhouse because the gangplank is already gone and the replacement line must be cast off from shore.

Dorion accepts the protected loss. He orders his riders not to challenge Myrine's chain and publicly denies only Ianthe's accusations against Kallias. This preserves him as an adversary who can still choose law over a futile war.

The Sea-Lark departs with Ianthe before the chain reaches the pulley. She leaves without the bronze seal and without half of the Narrow Shield, but not as cargo, captive, foreign ward, or silent symbol. Damon and Phaedra remain free under Myrine protection. The reed witnesses and duplicate records stay ashore in separate custody. Two evacuated households receive temporary harbor lodging. Red Bridge and the Heron Sluice still function. Kallias retains an emergency government and a usable argument about elven involvement, but he does not possess Ianthe's assent or the only public account.

## Final state

- **Ianthe:** alive, capable, and under Myrine sanctuary; aboard the Sea-Lark by her own decision.
- **Petition:** complete, with three recorded judgments and duplicate witness chains.
- **Bronze seal:** held by Myrine as disputed Aulonite state property pending hearing.
- **Narrow Shield:** Melia and Thaleia sail with Ianthe; Damon and Phaedra remain free and recognized in Myrine.
- **Dorion:** denied custody of Ianthe, credited with recovering the seal, and publicly associated with defending her against assassins.
- **Ash Knives:** three agents captured or exposed; the organization survives but loses its ability to hide entirely inside Kallias's pursuit.
- **Naevan:** gains proof that Ianthe requested limited elven aid but not authority over her destination.
- **Road communities:** two households evacuated, three pilots released, and the bridge and sluice preserved; arrests and fines remain an immediate political obligation.
- **Public account:** divided among Ianthe's petition, Myrine's judgments, Thessa's courier ledger, Red Bridge receipts, reed witnesses, and Kallias's competing proclamation.

This is a strong success, not a clean one. As the Sea-Lark clears the rising chain, Ianthe stands aboard without the bronze hand that once opened every Aulonite storehouse. Damon and Phaedra remain between the spear court and chainhouse; the seal rests in Myrine’s chest; duplicate records travel by different hands. The bodyguards succeed because they protect the choice crossing the white stones, then release physical custody without pretending the road’s other debts have vanished.
