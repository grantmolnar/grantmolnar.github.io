# Bridge and Channel Operations

Use this sheet when the escort reaches the Red Bridge, Reed Villages, or divides between them. It converts the middle road into explicit resources and consequences rather than an abstract travel check.

## Inherited state

Before either encounter begins, state:

- current pursuit posture and the information supporting it;
- Ianthe's condition and current physical custody;
- who carries the bronze seal;
- available mounts and their condition;
- accompanying witnesses, prisoners, wounded people, and evacuees;
- Dorion's last position and any active truce;
- Naevan's promised aid and whether Ianthe signed a request; and
- the chosen route, rendezvous, and fallback for every divided group.

Do not restore separated equipment, people, or knowledge between encounters.

## Red Bridge resources

| Resource | Available | Fast use | Cost or exposure |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Cavalry remounts | 6 | Refresh a pursuing or escorting company | Reserved under Dorion's order; seizure is overt |
| Postal horses | 4 | Carry a small escort rapidly to Beacon Hill | Leaves slower companions or requires division |
| Road mares | 2 | Carry Ianthe, wounded riders, or a decoy | One is targeted by Ash Knife sabotage |
| Bronze-seal requisition | 1 authenticated use at the post | Opens stores, remounts, dispatch tools, and a signal | Creates a route record and grounds for an amber signal |
| Signal mast | Immediate in clear weather | Warn, authenticate, misdirect, or refuse confirmation | The same line helps pursuit and protects against assassins |
| Wagon barrier | Open, closed, or jammed | Controls carts and horses on the deck | Does not block the lower works |
| Lower ledge | People and light equipment | Bypass the deck or reach the reed towpath | Slow, exposed, and unsuitable for mounted movement |
| Heron Sluice | Closed, ordinary, open, or damaged | Change channel speed and cavalry access | Direct civilian and agricultural consequences |

## Bridge signals

| Signal | Ordinary meaning | Likely pursuit effect |
|---|---|---|
| One amber opening | Authenticated royal bearer or command | Names the bridge route and may concentrate pursuit |
| Two white openings | Princess believed coerced | Supports Kallias's abduction narrative and armed recovery |
| One black opening | Assassins, sabotage, or uncontrolled threat | May create cooperation or caution rather than pure pursuit |
| Amber over black | Authenticated traveler departed by lower or reedward route | Names the broader route but not necessarily the exact group |
| No confirmation | Bridge refuses to authenticate reports | Preserves ambiguity and denies emergency warning |

The operator sends a claim, not objective truth. Record who controls or witnesses the mast.

## Heron Sluice consequences

| State | Boat movement | Pursuit | Civilian consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ordinary | All normal routes navigable | Cavalry restricted to levees and major crossings | No new harm |
| Closed | Narrow channels become shallow and trackable | Mounted search reaches farther along banks | Boats ground; fields and household water suffer |
| Fully open | Prepared boats gain speed | Formation and bank movement become difficult | Low homes, walkways, and seed beds flood |
| Damaged | Behavior must be read or repaired | Both sides lose reliable planning | Seasonal irrigation may fail |

## Reed Village boat capacity

| Craft | Number | Capacity | Route limits |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Broad fishing punt | 2 | Pilot plus 4 armored, 6 light, or 1 horse with 2 handlers | Salt Cut and Pilots' Run; Black Thread only at good water |
| Narrow skiff | 3 | Pilot plus 2 adults | All channels, including Black Thread |
| Ferry barge | 1 | 4 horses, 12 adults, or 2 households with goods | Salt Cut and Pilots' Run only; slow and conspicuous |
| Household coracle | 6 | One light adult or several children and goods | Messages, rescue, and household movement; poor combat craft |

Initially, Kallias's post holds one punt, two skiffs, and the ferry barge. The villages have hidden one punt and one skiff; two skiffs lack confiscated plugs and oars.

## Route comparison

| Route | Best use | Principal cost | Typical pursuit response |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bridge deck | Speed, witnesses, horses, lawful confrontation | Public exposure and concentrated force | Occupy north approach, stable, and mast |
| Lower bridge works | Bypass and route division | No horses; slow and vulnerable crossing | Control parapets, sluice, and towpath exits |
| Salt Cut | Large company, horses, witnesses, evacuees | Visible, blockable, and slow | Levee containment and confiscated boats |
| Black Thread | Hidden Ianthe with small guard | Weak formation, pilot dependence, no heavy group | Search forks, pressure pilots, follow mistakes |
| Pilots' Run | Fast boat approach to Beacon Hill | Easier to read and pursue | Race to eastern landing or signal Beacon Hill |
| Cypress Drain | Elven extraction or westward decoy | Changes destination and custody if used in earnest | Hold Aulonite boundary or publicize foreign intervention |
| Levee and towpath | Foot fallback and cross-route movement | Exposed, tiring, poor for wounded people | Mounted interception where banks widen |

## Route split record

For each group, record:

| Field | Required answer |
|---|---|
| Principal or object | Ianthe, seal, witnesses, horses, prisoners, civilians, or signal tools |
| Leader | Person empowered to make immediate decisions |
| Route | Exact bridge level, channel, drain, or levee |
| Rendezvous | Place and travel window |
| Recognition | Lamp, phrase, token, boat marking, or named witness |
| Fallback | Action if blocked, late, or unable to authenticate the other group |

Apply threats according to adversary information. A decoy is useful until evidence disproves it, not until the GM tires of it.

## Objective-driven encounter menu

### Red Bridge

- Keep Ianthe out of the assassin's sight line.
- Secure a specific number of sound remounts.
- Prevent or transmit one signal.
- Hold the wagon barrier while a group crosses.
- Rescue someone from the lower ledge.
- Operate the sluice before the water state changes.
- Preserve the bridge hearing or truce witnesses.
- Capture an assassin without yielding custody.

### Reed Villages

- Open the boat chain.
- Free named pilots and household sureties.
- Stop a mirror or runner from confirming the route.
- Move Ianthe from a compromised boat.
- Protect homes or drying racks from fire.
- Reach a particular channel fork before containment closes.
- Transfer the seal, witnesses, or wounded people between craft.
- Evacuate one or two households without losing the Myrine route.

An encounter ends when the objective is won, lost, abandoned, or transformed. Do not require extermination of every hostile participant.

## Transition to Beacon Hill

Before beginning the final approach, state:

- Ianthe's route and companions;
- seal location;
- surviving mounts and boats;
- witnesses traveling toward Myrine or Aulon;
- bridge signal and log;
- sluice and village condition;
- Dorion's force, route, and custody claim;
- Naevan's involvement and separation point;
- the chosen Beacon Hill landing or descent; and
- whether the second-sunset departure remains secure, doubtful, or lost.
