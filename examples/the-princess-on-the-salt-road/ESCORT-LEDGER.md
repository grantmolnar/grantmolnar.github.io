# Escort Ledger: The Princess on the Salt Road

## Principal

### Princess Ianthe Aulonid

**Wants:** a free petition at Myrine; the bronze seal kept from Kallias; visible Aulonite support; minimum civil bloodshed.

**Will concede:** comfort, secrecy, the seal, succession, personal dignity, and even the direct road if concession preserves a free later choice.

**Will resist:** being treated as unconscious cargo, a private transfer to Dorion, permanent elven wardship disguised as rescue, and killing uncertain citizens merely because they stand in the road.

**Bodyguard relationship:** she gives counsel a real hearing and expects obedience after a decision. Repeated deception by the bodyguards may preserve her life while dissolving the sworn relationship.

## Pursuers and rivals

### Strategos Kallias Arven

**Objective:** preserve Aulon under one emergency government and use Ianthe's visible assent to legitimate it.

**Needs:** Ianthe alive, the seal intact, and as few public images of coercion as possible.

**Does not need:** the bodyguards alive, Myrine friendly, or the Assembly free.

### Captain Dorion Vey

**Objective:** execute Kallias's recovery order without beginning a civil war on the road.

**Methods:** warrants, mounted pursuit, remount control, road signals, pardons, separation, temporary truces, and force when witnesses can be controlled.

**Credible offer:** a thirty-day protector proclamation, transfer of the bronze seal, amnesty for prior escort offenses, bodyguard accompaniment to the palace gate, and limited release of local detainees. Dorion can alter witnesses, transfer procedure, and short truces; he cannot allow Myrine, elven custody, retention of the seal, or restoration of the Assembly as a precondition.

**Limit:** he will not knowingly permit Ianthe's murder. He can fight beside the escort against the Ash Knives and resume pursuit afterward. He will not falsely promise that Kallias stops pressing Ianthe after thirty days.

### The Ash Knives

**Objective:** end the royal succession before either Kallias or Ianthe can claim it.

**Methods:** poison, false loyalist tokens, panic, damaged tack, isolated attacks, and blame placed on the coup.

**Limit:** they are few. They need confusion, confined ground, or competing armed parties.

### Naevan of the White Cypress

**Objective:** deliver his cousin alive to Serathiel's court.

**Methods:** six outriders, forest roads, old kinship, and a sincere argument that Aulon's institutions have already failed her.

**Credible offer:** full extraction makes Ianthe a protected guest whose departure is subject to White Cypress judgment. A written request can instead purchase two scouts, a guide and decoy trail, messages, or evacuation for two households. The written request also strengthens Kallias's foreign-influence claim.

**Limit:** he will not place his escort under Aulonite command, attack Dorion merely for enforcing an Aulonite warrant, enter Myrine as an armed elven company, or promise that Ianthe may return whenever she wishes.

## Pressure frame

Track pressures openly in fiction rather than through a concealed universal meter.

### Time

The Sea-Lark leaves at the second sunset. State openly when a choice costs a meaningful portion of the road. Delay may purchase allies, repaired routes, witnesses, rest, or civilian safety.

### Pursuit

Dorion's force changes with information and infrastructure. Public departure gives him certainty. The aqueduct gives uncertainty. Red Bridge remounts and Beacon Hill signals give speed and concentration.

State the current posture openly: **Unfixed**, **Named**, **Closing**, or **Contact**. A posture advances because a route is witnessed, a seal use is recorded, a helper is captured, fresh horses are available, or a credible beacon confirms the approach. It recedes because a decoy is believed, the route divides, a relay fails, or witnesses discredit the report. Dorion must still travel through the world; he does not appear merely because a meter filled.

### Public standing

Witnesses expose the route and protect Ianthe's claim of free movement. Concealment protects the body and leaves Kallias's abduction story unanswered. Neither is a simple success track.

### Escort condition

Mounts, formation, rest, equipment, and company cohesion affect what protection is physically possible. These costs should arise from chosen roads and concrete events.

## Route integrity

Use the physical matrix in `GM-OPERATING-SHEET.md`. The clue graph may contain backward corroboration and does not define literal travel. Every city exit can continue to the courier house, Red Bridge, or Reed Villages; both middle roads can reach Beacon Hill or descend directly to Myrine; and the bridge and reeds connect through the Heron works.

The House at Three Cypresses and Beacon Hill are advantageous, not compulsory. Skipping the house gains time and loses prepared resources, sabotage warnings, and complete custody terms. Skipping the beacon gains time and loses an authenticated harbor account. A necessary encounter remains structurally integrated without becoming a mandatory scene.

For divided routes, assign threats from adversary knowledge and transport. Do not duplicate Dorion, Naevan, or a named assassin across groups. Record actions in causal order.

## Middle-road resource frame

### Red Bridge

The bridge concentrates four coupled resources: the crossing, company-sized remounts, the Beacon Hill signal line, and the Heron Sluice feeding the Reed Villages. Control of one does not automatically grant the others. Damage must be recorded by function.

### Reed Villages

The villages possess enough boats to move a small hidden escort immediately or a larger mixed convoy after confiscated craft are recovered. Horses, witnesses, prisoners, evacuees, and a strong bodyguard formation compete for the same capacity.

### Route division

For every group, name what it carries, who leads, the exact route, rendezvous, recognition sign, and fallback. Pursuers act on known and credible information. The GM should not collapse a divided plan back into one column when danger appears.

### Infrastructure and civilian cost

Closing the Heron Sluice exposes channels and harms water access. Opening it fully can speed prepared boats and flood low homes. Destroying it threatens the season's irrigation. These consequences are visible before the decision whenever the escort has inspected the works or spoken with a pilot.

## Mixed outcomes to preserve

- Ianthe petitions and sails with the seal.
- Ianthe petitions but the seal returns to Kallias.
- Ianthe gains sanctuary after the Sea-Lark departs.
- Ianthe accepts elven extraction and survives as a foreign ward.
- Dorion recovers Ianthe alive, but public witnesses make coerced assent unusable.
- The bodyguards hold the road and are arrested or killed after the petition succeeds.
- Ianthe renounces succession freely, denying both Kallias and the royalist faction the claim it expected.
- The Ash Knives kill Ianthe but fail to erase testimony about who protected and who pursued her.

The adventure should not compress these into a binary “princess saved” result.

## Final-approach resource frame

### Beacon account

Track the visible flame, its authenticated clay strip, the keeper or witnesses supporting it, and what Myrine can actually infer. Control of the basket without credibility is terrain control, not narrative control.

### Descent division

The Salt Steps, Olive Stairs, and Ropewalk Track reach different harbor surfaces. Record Ianthe, seal, witnesses, wounded companions, rear guard, and ship-defense group separately. Do not teleport a group from the chainhouse to the white stones when the other objective becomes urgent.

### Sanctuary record

Identity, free crossing, spoken petition, three judgments, and protected transfer are distinct stages. An attack can interrupt one without retroactively deleting completed stages. Lawful substitutes and duplicate tablets make the process robust but can consume the chain clock.

### Harbor weapon posture

Record who remains armed outside, who surrendered weapons, whose weapons are peace-bound, and who holds a limited exception. The posture determines access and public interpretation as well as tactical response.

### Finale objective frame

Resolve Ianthe's life, capacity to speak, petition, three records, seal, Sea-Lark, bodyguard standing, witnesses, and road-community consequences independently. The adventure's final answer should name custody and political authorship, not merely location.
