This sheet governs the social struggle around Judith Crowl. It does not replace the authored clue graph and does not turn the village into a single morale score. Record named acts, witnesses, households, and consequences.

## Three kinds of refusal

### Personal refusal

A person may reject a gift, cure, custody agreement, written name, or command accepted on their behalf. This ends Judith's claim through that carrier once the carrier is physically removed or contained. It does not revoke another resident's acceptance.

### Household revocation

A household refuses Judith's access to its threshold and hearth. Each present resident withdraws their own welcome, discovered carriers are removed, stolen ash is replaced, and an independent witness records the act. This blocks direct hearth passage and weakens commands tied to that home.

### Public revocation of the Renewal

Before midnight, a household publicly states that its prior token, signature, or gratitude does not invite Judith or the Unwelcome Court. The statement must be heard by a credible witness and attached to a material act: surrendering the token, striking the signature, ringing the truthful bell, or placing the refusal before the Mercy Book.

A private household can be secure while still falsely counted in Judith's public ritual. A public revocation can remove the household from the common welcome while leaving an undiscovered private carrier dangerous. These are related but distinct operations.

## Household revocation procedure

1. List every known token, gift, signature, ash sample, custody paper, written name, and marked resident.
2. Ask each present resident what they personally accept or refuse. Do not let one adult silently decide for all others.
3. Remove or contain the physical carriers. Do not burn name papers or marked gifts casually.
4. Replace stolen hearth ash or flame with one freely shared for this purpose.
5. State the refusal in ordinary language. The declaration should identify Judith and the kinds of claims being withdrawn.
6. Record the act with an independent witness and the names each resident chooses to use.
7. Test the threshold without inviting danger inside: observe the lintel, flame, token heat, or a controlled marked object.
8. Decide whether the household will also revoke publicly before midnight.

A suggested declaration is: “No gift purchased this door. Judith Crowl and every claim carried by her token, ash, writing, or servant are refused here.” Exact words are not magical. Consent, authority over one's own welcome, physical carriers, and witness matter.

## Practical effects of resistance

Use these effects when supported by named commitments.

- After **silence is broken**, villagers can approach privately without assuming that speech is suicide.
- After **authority is contested**, Judith cannot rely on every warrant, guard, or seizure being obeyed without question.
- After **a witness is protected**, destroying one document no longer restores her monopoly on the story.
- After **two households commit**, food, carts, bedding, watchers, and messengers can be assigned without treating the company as omnipresent.
- After **an institution divides**, Judith must choose between secrecy and visible coercion.
- After **one public revocation succeeds**, other households may repeat the act if they receive a safe place, witness, and clear handling instructions.

These are permissions, not automatic mass conversion. Name who acts and what they risk.

## Retaliation ladder

Advance retaliation when the company removes a channel, protects a witness, or creates a public commitment. Judith chooses targeted responses; she does not automatically deploy every resource.

### After the gallows is challenged

Choose one:

- seal Saint Mercy House;
- attack the Sedge Croft through Quill and hearthlings;
- return Pell's sickness;
- post Odo's paid-witch-finder accusation;
- command one marked guard publicly.

### After evidence survives the croft

Choose one:

- obtain a lawful-looking seizure warrant;
- burn one route while attacking a different evidence cache;
- move one or two name-captives from Mercy House toward Crow Wood;
- compel a compromised witness to recant;
- offer a genuine cure or food delivery to a household considering resistance.

### After a witness is protected

Choose one:

- target the witness's carer or dependent rather than the refuge directly;
- circulate an exact private fact through a surviving spy channel;
- command an accepted guest to request entry;
- move records or prisoners before the witness can guide the company;
- offer the witness a private bargain that would save one named person.

### After a household revokes

Choose one:

- test the threshold with stolen ash or a marked servant;
- call in debt, tenancy, grain, or custody through ordinary law;
- move harm into a resident through the Worm in White;
- make the revoking household appear responsible for a public injury;
- attack another household that was preparing to copy the act.

### After public revocation begins

Choose one:

- force the Renewal ceremony to begin early;
- split the crowd with a real distribution of food or medicine;
- seize the Mercy Book and move it toward Blackbriar Hall;
- put a stolen-name child before the crowd speaking in a parent's borrowed voice;
- order Cleft to clear the green while Judith remains publicly absent.


## Judith's physical location and response budget

Judith's body begins at Saint Orra's Gallows. Keep one location for it thereafter. Moving by road takes ordinary travel. Moving through a hearth requires the intact Guest pact, an active marked hearth at the destination, the relevant ash, and a valid threshold claim. Moving through a mirror requires the intact Child pact and prepared surfaces at both ends. The Worm in White does not move her body.

A familiar, commanded guard, borrowed voice, hearthling, Glass Nurse, or patron manifestation may act elsewhere without being Judith herself. Such agents cannot carry Judith's keys, receive bodily wounds, be physically restrained as Judith, or make her occupy two places.

Between two substantial company operations, Judith may spend **one major response** through one surviving channel. A substantial operation includes a rescue wave, completed household revocation, pact-work stage, critical evidence seizure, route capture, prolonged control change, or feast-component neutralization. She may also issue one ordinary order to a servant already able to receive it. A split company does not multiply this budget.

If the company lingers, advance the relevant encounter's own clock. Do not convert delay into unlimited remote action.

## Response persistence

A reprisal may threaten a person, transport route, uncopied page, unsecured vessel, or remaining household claim. It does not reverse completed state.

- A copied record remains copied.
- A witness already delivered to a protected destination remains there unless someone reaches that destination.
- A valid revocation remains valid until a resident or accepted guest creates a new claim.
- An opened refuge door, lifted body, honest burial, returned name, or neutralized feast component does not reset.

Record the new danger and the surviving accomplishment together.

## Limits on retaliation

- Judith cannot use a household hearth after complete revocation unless another resident or accepted guest creates a new claim.
- She cannot command an unmarked person merely because they are afraid.
- She cannot know facts that no surviving channel carried.
- She cannot transfer every wound or illness without using the White Pits and exposing the pattern.
- She cannot move all captives instantly; wagons, servants, routes, and time remain material.
- She cannot make ordinary villagers forget a witnessed contradiction, though she can punish, confuse, isolate, or discredit them.
- She prefers domination over indiscriminate slaughter. When she kills, it should protect a method, punish refusal, or make another person participate.
