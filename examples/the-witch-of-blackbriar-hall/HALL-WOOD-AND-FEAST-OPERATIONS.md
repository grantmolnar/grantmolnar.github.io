# Session 7 demonstrated state

## Crow Wood

Jo Rusk, Perrin Mett, and Haro Venn are recovered from hidden wagon compartments. Garran Pike unlocks the wagon, removes written restraints, identifies transports, and leads the company to the Hall tunnel after Olla's body fails to recognize him. Two crow messages are captured and one is altered. Olla later stops walking when lifted from the White Pits.

## Blackbriar Hall

Cora Pike and six ordinary staff defect. Corin Pike is rescued. The living false shelter ledger, accusation drafts, injury ledgers, contingency matrix, and pact-renewal records are secured or copied. Relief stores remain under witnessed emergency inventory. Odo Vane takes the master Mercy Book below; Varro Cleft takes the token key and six marked guards. Judith's one physical body enters the Underhall through an intact Guest route.
