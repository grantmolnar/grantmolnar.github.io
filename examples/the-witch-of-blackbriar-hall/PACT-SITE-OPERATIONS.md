# Pact-Site Operations

Use this sheet when the company investigates, weakens, or severs Judith Crowl's three pacts. The sites are not three identical ritual rooms. Track material acts, people, records, labor, exposure, and Judith's remaining channels separately.

Completed work persists. Judith may retaliate, move an unsecured object, attack a witness, or exploit an intact channel. She cannot rebar the refuge after it was correctly opened, make an honestly identified corpse anonymous again, or repossess a name that was properly returned and whose vessel was broken afterward.

# Session 7 demonstrated pact state

## Guest in Ash

All three acts are completed. Eight workers pull the original west doors outward. The nineteen names are restored from the chapel roll, material marks, and released memories. Mara Sedge freely offers her cleansed household hearth; the living false ledger burns while the restored roll is read. The Guest pact is severed before the Underhall entry.

## Worm in White

All thirty-one founding dead are identified and lifted. Twenty-seven receive honest burning during the pit operation. Rell and Siva Vane, Jessa Bale, and Tev Noll remain wrapped because the first witnesses are not sufficiently independent. The pact is therefore only narrowed when the company leaves the pits. The final four receive separate witnessed disposition during the Underhall confrontation, severing the Worm pact then.

Five renewal victims—Auda Vey, Elian Darr, Ilyra Fen, Bram Kett, and Toman Rusk—are recovered under certain identities. Two additional later remains stay under chapel custody for identification.

## Child Behind Glass

Nell Sedge, Amey Fen, Jo Rusk, Lark Merrow, Perrin Mett, Sella Darr, and Corin Pike pass unborrowed-reflection tests, claim their own names, receive free witness responses, and break their vessels only afterward. Five living former residents and four dead owners are also restored. Three damaged unidentified vessels remain covered and unresolved. The Child pact is severed before the Underhall entry.
