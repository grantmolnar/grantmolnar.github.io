# Blackbriar Vale Ledger

Use this ledger to keep the village concrete. Blackbriar Vale contains sixty-three inhabited households and roughly three hundred people. Judith's control is extensive but not uniform: forty-seven households already display a blackthorn token, eleven different households have signed the Mercy Book without displaying one, and five have avoided both through poverty, distance, or active refusal. The Renewal of Mercy is meant to consolidate those claims before midnight. Its public oath lets Judith argue that the reeve and assembly answered for the vale, but it does not automatically connect an explicitly refusing household. The Underhall can therefore begin with fewer than sixty-three active doors and produce a smaller patron claim if resistance has already broken the common account.

Do not treat a household as a single opinion. Record who accepted a gift, who signed, who tends the hearth, who lives there now, and who is prepared to revoke their own welcome.
