# GM Operating Sheet: The Witch of Blackbriar Hall

Use this sheet to coordinate the village, pact sites, moving captives, Judith's responses, and the Hollow Feast. Detailed rosters and procedures remain in the specialist ledgers. This sheet preserves the state distinctions most likely to collapse during play.

## Opening state

- It is shortly after dawn. Ysra's original inspection writ predates Mara's arrest; Caldus's addendum reached the company on the road before first light.
- Judith's body is at Saint Orra's Gallows.
- Mara is at the pyre; Tomas holds the torch; Pate is hidden with Adela Merrow.
- Nell, Amey, Lark, Sella, and Eda are hidden in Saint Mercy House.
- Jo is in transit through Crow Wood, Perrin is on the service route, and Corin is at Blackbriar Hall.
- All seven current name vessels are in the Mercy House mirror dormitory. The vessels coordinate the Glass Nurse and prepare the bays; they are not living choir voices.
- Forty-seven households display tokens, eleven other households have Mercy Book signatures, and five have neither.
- All three pacts are intact.
- The Mercy Book and household token network are operational. The captive choir is prepared but not operational until at least four living controlled children reach the Underhall.
- The feast clock begins when the execution stay is invoked or Mara is removed and Judith sends Odo the altered Renewal schedule.

## Adventure-turn loop

After a substantial company operation:

1. Record people, evidence, carriers, and practical care changed by the operation.
2. Record clues actually observed and revelations actually established.
3. Update physical access, route control, and any site clock.
4. Update pact work, household revocations, and feast components.
5. Determine what Judith can actually know through surviving channels.
6. Move Judith's body only through an ordinary road, valid hearth passage, or complete reflected route.
7. Spend at most one major Judith response before the next substantial company operation.
8. Expose the response's trace, victim, route, or carrier.
9. State the next visible deadline or opportunity.

A split company does not multiply Judith's major responses. Choose where she spends attention. Other locations advance only through their own declared clocks and personnel.

## Commission authority

The writ permits records inspection, evidence preservation, temporary suspension of Mara's execution, and protection of named witnesses in immediate danger. It does not grant unrestricted search, command over every guard, permanent custody of children, ownership of the Hall, or permanent government.

Record the basis for every later expansion:

- discovered unlawful confinement or imminent harm;
- consent of a household, victim, guardian, or institution;
- a public limited order from Pell, Caldus, or another responsible local officer;
- ordinary self-defense or detention of an immediate attacker; or
- a material counter-rite undertaken by the affected families and free witnesses.

If no basis exists, the company may still choose to act, but the legal and political consequence remains part of the state rather than disappearing behind the commission seal.

## Clock and delegated work

Midnight is the earliest full common welcome, not permission to erase unfinished scenes. After each substantial operation, advance the visible feast stage and record the time pressure it creates. Do not count travel as free; do not make one road choice consume an arbitrary number of unplayed encounters.

Local work may continue in parallel only when the record names:

1. the responsible crew or household;
2. the procedure and materials it possesses;
3. the witnesses, care, and authority it still needs;
4. the route by which progress or danger will be reported; and
5. what Judith can attack through a surviving channel.

The company must return when a delegated operation reaches a decision it cannot lawfully or practically make. Parallel labor creates capacity, not automatic completion.

## Judith's concession rule

Judith never offers restitution, shared custody, or surrender of a claim. A cure moves illness into someone else. A child's release requires another household page. Safe departure leaves the vale claimed. When publicly exposed, she punishes the first person who answers her practical challenge. Do not treat Saint Mercy House as a balancing public good: Judith made its care effective because useful rooms and records produce dependence she can inventory.

## One-major-response rule

A **substantial company operation** is one of the following:

- a rescue or evacuation wave;
- a completed household revocation;
- a material pact-work stage;
- a critical evidence seizure or public reading;
- capture of a route, wagon, or institutional area;
- a prolonged confrontation that changes control; or
- neutralization of a feast component.

Between two such operations, Judith may make one major response through one channel she still possesses. She may also give one ordinary instruction to a servant already able to receive it. A site clock may advance because the company lingers or fails loudly, but that does not grant Judith extra remote actions.

## Irregular-clue rule

- Record only clues actually observed.
- Keep the clue's authored source when its physical carrier moves.
- Do not duplicate a clue because a page, crow, vessel, or witness changes location.
- Do not grant a complete severance procedure from a broad statement that three pacts exist.
- Broad truths may remain well supported on compact routes; narrow procedures may remain single-source or unknown.
- The finale may reveal an active carrier or consequence without supplying all missing remote steps.

## Late and skipped encounter rule

For every delayed encounter:

1. Move no more than the actual personnel and transport allow.
2. Name the asset, victim, or record moved.
3. Name the route and destination.
4. Leave fixed structures, copied evidence, and completed work in place.
5. Create a trace, witness, or pursuit opportunity.
6. Preserve a narrower operation at the original site.

Quick limits:

- Saint Mercy House moves one transfer wave, not every resident and record.
- Blackbriar Hall moves one critical record class or captive movement per response step.
- Crow Wood sends prisoners to an actual Hall, Underhall, refuge, or road destination.
- The founding thirty-one cannot be removed from the White Pits offstage.
- Opened refuge doors, lifted bodies, returned names, and witnessed revocations do not reset.

## Direct-assault procedure

When the company reaches Blackbriar Hall early:

1. Set Hall response to the step justified by warning actually received.
2. Keep Judith at her recorded location; do not place her in Hall by assumption.
3. Move only the selected record or captive movement already completed.
4. Let attackers discover the Underhall through the study stair, undercroft, Corin's marks, or service tunnel.
5. In the Underhall, show the three feast components physically.
6. On the first use of each intact pact, show its carrier and recipient.
7. Permit component disruption, rescue, seizure, retreat, or pursuit without requiring an established revelation checkbox.
8. Do not improvise missing remote severance obligations.

A direct assault can be consequential and successful without becoming a complete solution to every pact.

## Failure-forward ladder

When a substantial operation fails, choose the narrowest fitting result:

1. one person is moved, captured, injured, or publicly discredited;
2. one record class is damaged, moved, or left uncopied;
3. one carrier or route remains active while another is exposed;
4. one household claim survives or becomes locally dangerous;
5. one feast component advances while another remains contestable;
6. one patron manifests through a subset of doors rather than the whole vale; or
7. Judith escapes through one surviving channel and leaves a trace.

Never replace a mixed result with “Judith wins,” “the ritual succeeds,” or “the clue is lost.” State the people, objects, doors, and channels that changed.
