# Full Playthrough: The Blackbriar Commission

The Blackbriar Commission was already traveling under Canon Ysra's burial-and-Mercy-House inspection writ when Caldus's parish rider reached it before dawn with news of Mara's accelerated execution. The company enters the vale with four narrow duties and no authority to rule it. By the following dawn, Sabren Holt, Lysa Marrin, Toma Brack, and Neris Kade will have argued over who may halt an execution, where thirty-nine displaced residents can sleep, how thirty-one lime-bound bodies can be lifted, and whether Judith should survive to testify. This is one demonstrated route, not the adventure's prescribed answer.

The four-session journal visits all ten encounters, spots seventy-two clues, deliberately misses eleven, establishes all eighteen revelations, and records thirty-six durable consequences. The route begins with rescue, but rescue repeatedly creates law, care, transport, burial, evidence, and household work. The Commission saves Mara, secures her seven packets, evacuates Saint Mercy House, intercepts the red wagon, raids Blackbriar Hall, reopens the chapel, severs two pacts, carries a partial Worm claim into the finale, and reaches the Underhall before Judith can complete the common welcome.

## Session One: The Torch in Tomas's Hands

### Saint Orra's Gallows

The parish rider's addendum puts the company on the gallows road before first light. It arrives while Judith Crowl places the torch in Tomas Sedge's hands and tells him that a good son proves his innocence by burning his mother. Mara is bound to the post. Pate is absent. Nell is missing. Reeve Pell is trying to read an emergency sentence without looking at either child.

Sabren presents Canon Ysra's sealed commission and asks the reeve three questions in public: who certified the physical evidence, who authorized a child to serve as executioner, and where the court recorded Nell as recovered. Pell cannot answer the third. Beadle Odo Vane tries to convert the inspection authority into a request that may be considered after sentence. Sabren reads the stay clause aloud and makes Pell either honor it or publicly admit that Judith, not the civil court, commands the green.

Judith does not retreat into ambiguity. She tells Tomas that if he refuses, Pate will be taken into Saint Mercy House before sunset and Mara will burn after dark where nobody will hear her. Tomas drops the torch. Captain Varro Cleft orders a marked guard to lift it. Piers Vey's voice changes before his body moves. The chapel bell name makes him falter long enough for Neris to cut the guard token from his wrist and for Lysa to pull Mara clear of the pyre frame.

Toma examines the poppet without treating it as mystical proof. The Blackbriar clove resin is visible, the stuffing is cut from petition forms, and the binding thread is newer than the supposed discovery. Sila Darr, protected behind the commission line, says Cleft forced her to place it after Nell vanished. Pell issues a temporary stay and orders Mara held under Sabren's custody rather than Judith's.

Judith crosses the courthouse threshold through Pell's warming token without receiving a fresh invitation. She speaks once through Piers after leaving the green, proving both the written-name channel and the fact that her body is elsewhere. She orders the crowd to repeat the Renewal oath. Sabren interrupts the response and reads Mara's narrower household revocation instead. Eleven households refuse the oath aloud; twenty-three remain silent; the rest answer under fear or habit.

The Commission sees the red cloth tied to crows departing toward Crow Wood, the seizure inventory sending property to Blackbriar Hall, Caldus's reference to the chapel bell, and Mara's claim that her seven comparison packets remain beneath the croft floor. They do not observe the private handoff by which a crow places a report directly into Judith's palm. Later evidence establishes the courier system more exactly.

**Judith's body:** Leaves the green by carriage for Blackbriar Hall.

**Major response spent:** None. Her threat and marked-guard command occur within the opening confrontation.

**State leaving the encounter:** Mara alive under temporary legal protection; Tomas and Pate accounted for; Pell publicly committed to the stay; Sila Darr identified as a coerced witness; Piers released from one active command; eleven households publicly refuse the Renewal oath; Judith's body en route to Blackbriar Hall.

### Sedge Croft

Mara refuses to let the commission carry her records away without first replacing the croft's stolen ash. Lysa brings Pate from Adela Merrow's cottage. Sabren records the household members, the gift through which Judith claimed Nell, and each person's consent to revocation. Tomas removes the blackthorn horse. Pate identifies Nell's voice inside it. Toma scrapes out the written name and the ring of stolen hearth ash without speaking the name near the window glass.

The family speaks a complete withdrawal before Mara, Tomas, Pate, Sabren, and two village witnesses. The old ash is sealed as evidence. Fresh fuel from the Darr Washhouse is laid in the hearth. When Judith tests the threshold through a soot hand, the hand stops at the sill and leaves a visible print rather than crossing.

Neris lifts the workroom boards and finds all seven prosecution packets. The papers establish a repeated sequence: Judith first removes or compromises a dependent, then manufactures symptoms or evidence, then uses ordinary officers to formalize the accusation. Nell's map identifies Saint Mercy House, its mirror dormitory, the laundry drain, and a black pool among reeds. Her blackthorn horse contains her full written name and household line. Mara's margin note points to the Chapel of the Free Witness and insists that names and witnesses, not faith alone, govern the surviving counter-rites.

Quill attacks the croft before the copies are complete. The familiar enters through the chimney carrying a strip ordering every marked guard to seize Mara and burn the packets. Neris nets it over the cold wash basin. Its bone tongue turns uphill when the order is repeated, and its traveling ash ring matches the croft sample. Sabren copies the message before Toma removes the ash and breaks the tongue hinge. Quill survives as a finite, grounded creature rather than a mystical broadcast.

The Commission deliberately prioritizes the packets, Nell's map, and the revocation over a complete search of the bedroom. They miss the white lime caught beneath Nell's feather box. The White Pits remain discoverable through Mercy House, the chapel, Crow Wood, and Blackbriar Hall.

Mara wants to accompany the Commission immediately. Lysa refuses until her burns and smoke exposure are dressed and until Pate has a named guardian. Mara accepts Adela Merrow as Pate's guardian, asks Tomas to remain with him, and goes with Sabren only as far as the parish yard to read the first packet publicly. She does not become an permanent company attachment who must enter every dangerous site.

**Judith's body:** Reaches Blackbriar Hall.

**Major response spent:** Quill's croft attack, carried through a written order and stolen ash. It exposes one message strip, one ash packet, and the uphill handoff.

**State leaving the encounter:** All seven prosecution packets preserved in three custody sets; Sedge household revocation complete; croft usable as a six-person refuge; Quill captured and stripped of one message channel; Mara publicly identifies the prosecution pattern; Saint Mercy House and Moonless Mere routes established.

### Session close

The Commission divides responsibility rather than people. Sabren and Mara begin a public reading under Pell's stay. Lysa arranges three receiving sites for Mercy House residents. Toma asks the Vey Bellhouse to prepare bronze and clean leather for the chapel bell. Neris studies Quill's route and the red prison cloth.

Judith sends no second major reprisal. The Commission knows she is at Blackbriar Hall and that she has learned the croft attack failed only if another courier reached her.

## Session Two: The White House and the Red Road

### Saint Mercy House

Sabren invokes the commission at the petition hall while Lysa asks for the resident count, infirmary register, and receiving plan that any legitimate institution should already possess. Sister Harl gives polished answers but cannot reconcile thirty visible children with the sleeping and meal rolls. Fen Corren passes Neris a sole-leather copy through the cobbler drain.

The Commission uses the inspection clock instead of pretending secrecy remains perfect. At the first delay, residents compare rehearsed answers. At the second, Ressa Pold opens the kitchen route. At the third, Harl moves the feast checklist and emergency transfer order. Neris intercepts the courier at the east bridge and sees the red-curtained wagon signal. At the fourth, Tib Wale hoods Nell, Amey, Lark, and Sella and orders the seven vessels crated.

Lysa and Matron Eda Wren take the suspended infirmary. Eda Lorn, Dall Oat, Ysa Bell, Marric Soil, and Noma Feld are matched to their bottles, cooling cases, stretchers, and receiving beds. Lysa refuses to move the bottles as one load. Each remains with its patient and an identified carrier. This slows the evacuation but prevents Judith from turning one broken crate into a room-wide release.

Nell refuses to leave without all seven vessels. The Commission covers each in dull cloth, records its owner separately, and places it with a free carrier. The four onsite children are rescued through the granary passage with visible residents and patients. Small groups use the laundry drain to reach the Darr Washhouse. The front court remains open long enough for Sabren to read the admissions chronology, feast checklist, and token list before Judith can describe the movement as a secret abduction.

Sister Harl sees that the feast checklist assigns seven named children as consumable voices and that Judith has prepared a transfer order leaving the rest of the house as public cover. Harl gives Neris the hidden stair key, orders Ressa to open the flour-bin wall, and holds the east bridge against Tib. She does not receive absolution. Sabren records her prior name movements, punishments, and orders before accepting her help.

Judith's response comes through Reeve Pell's cure receipt. Linn Pell collapses on the green as her husband's old wasting sickness returns in public. Judith's borrowed voice offers to stop the attack if Lysa certifies the House and returns the vessels. Lysa instead sends Matron Wren with the matched treatment notes, isolates the receipt, and names the transferred illness as Judith's act before witnesses. Linn survives but remains seriously ill.

The Commission secures the Mercy Book's token index copies, admission chronology, feast checklist, emergency transfer order, infirmary book, Fen's copies, and punishment record. Odo Vane has already taken the master Mercy Book toward Blackbriar Hall. They miss the Glass Nurse repeating conversations from marked rooms because Neris covers and breaks its active sight lines before allowing it to speak at length.

The House cannot be emptied safely in one wave. Thirty children, nine adult dependents, five infirmary patients, four hidden child captives, Eda Lorn, staff, records, food, and bedding move to the chapel yard, Darr Washhouse, Merrow Cottage, and the parish granary under named guardians. Twelve residents choose to remain temporarily with Matron Wren in the unmirrored public rooms until beds open. They are not counted as abandoned or automatically loyal to Judith.

**Judith's body:** Remains at Blackbriar Hall.

**Major response spent:** Returns Pell's old illness through the cure receipt and demands public certification. The response exposes the receipt, its household reach, and Judith's inability to release all infirmary bottles remotely.

**State leaving the encounter:** Nell, Amey, Lark, Sella, and Eda Lorn rescued; all seven current vessels secured; Jo, Perrin, and Corin still absent; five infirmary patients moved with bottles; independent evidence copied; Harl cooperating under recorded culpability; Mercy Book master copy and three absent bodies moving toward Blackbriar Hall.

### Crow Wood

Neris uses the intercepted east-bridge order, Quill's bone tongue, and red wagon cloth to enter Crow Wood along a real service track. The wood still misdirects marked carriers, but the Commission has removed or covered its active marks. Toma cuts three blackthorn route stakes. Lysa keeps every rescued person outside the pursuit rather than turning the children into guides.

The prisoner wagon divides at the old charcoal marker. A visible cage carries blankets and a crying voice but no prisoner. Neris notices that the wagon weight remains rear-heavy and opens the hidden restraint bays. Jo Rusk and Perrin Mett are inside, each with written commands sewn into the restraints. Haro Venn lies alive beneath the floor chest, drugged after refusing a third transfer. The chest contains custody papers, red cord, three crow messages, and a map of the service tunnel beneath Blackbriar Hall.

Garran Pike calls Olla from the orchard track. The Worm in White walks her body with the ankle bell she wore in life. Garran insists Judith preserved his wife. Toma shows him the lime split at Olla's joints, the command threads entering from the north orchard, and the fact that she does not recognize the mill song he uses as proof of memory. He demands a concrete break: Garran must unlock the wagon, remove every prisoner carrier, name the bodies he transported, and lead the Commission to the Hall tunnel. Garran complies after Olla turns toward Judith's supper phrase rather than toward him.

Cleft's four-guard escort arrives before the Commission can control the polished false wall. Neris closes the reflected route by dulling the panel, but one crow escapes carrying the fact that the wagon has fallen. The Commission captures two messages and alters a third to report that the prisoners continue toward the orchard. Cleft's escort follows the false response long enough for Jo, Perrin, and Haro to reach the western coppice under Dena Moor's protection.

The Commission finds lime in Garran's oldest cart and hears the Underhall feast beneath the root-cut service road. They do not follow the crows west far enough to see that the familiars refuse the original Burned Refuge threshold. The refuge remains locatable through Blackbriar records and chapel rolls.

Garran remains in Sabren's custody and leads the Commission onward. Olla's body walks toward the White Pits after the surviving Worm command. Toma records that Garran's cooperation does not erase years of transport, concealment, and corpse use.

**Judith's body:** Blackbriar Hall, warned that the wagon was attacked but initially misinformed about which route the survivors took.

**Major response spent:** None. The escort, Olla, and messages are consequences already set in motion by the Mercy House transfer and pursuit clock.

**State leaving the encounter:** Jo, Perrin, and Haro rescued; service tunnel mapped; Garran cooperating in custody; two crow messages captured and one false report sent; Olla's body moving toward the White Pits; Judith knows the wagon failed but not the receiving sites.

### Blackbriar Hall

The Commission enters through the service tunnel before Judith can reconcile the false crow report with the missing wagon. Cora Pike admits them after seeing Corin's apprenticeship marks on the tunnel wall and learning that his name vessel has been secured. She draws six ordinary staff away from Cleft's command and keeps the relief store from being burned during the alarm.

The raid is not a complete conquest of the estate. Sabren secures Judith's accusation drafts, the three injury ledgers, the contingency matrix, and the order to strip the Sedge Croft. Neris reaches the private dining room and sees three chairs, each with voluntary renewal payments. Toma takes the living false shelter ledger from the ash archive in a covered iron box. Lysa finds Corin carrying Mercy Book leaves between the records room and undercroft. His written strip is removed; he is reunited with Cora and placed beyond the reflected corridor.

Judith confronts the Commission in the ash gallery. She offers Sabren a full confession that begins after the famine, Lysa an hour without transferred illness, Toma exact names for the White Pits, and Neris a reflected route to the missing prisoner from her former courier service. Every offer is materially useful and requires an accepted gift or narrowed account. All four refuse.

The Commission cannot secure everything. Odo Vane carries the master Mercy Book into the Underhall. Varro Cleft takes the token-table key and six marked guards below. Judith retreats through a copied hearth before the Commission has severed any pact. Neris sees the ash path and records its direction; she does not pretend the retreat duplicated Judith's body.

The Commission misses the private moonless appointment calendar and does not inspect the entire ash gallery closely enough to match every socket to every household token. They already possess independent routes to the mere and know that tokens carry claims. The night-transfer schedule is also lost when Odo burns one desk drawer, but the Mercy House records have already established the institution's custody system.

The relief stores remain at the Hall under Cora Pike, Matron Wren, and a written emergency inventory. Sabren refuses to seize them as undifferentiated witch property. Food, fuel, medicine, and blankets remain available to households that revoke Judith rather than becoming payment for continued loyalty.

**Judith's body:** Enters the Underhall through an intact Guest hearth route.

**Major response spent:** Judith's direct confrontation and four tailored bargains. It reveals her body, the active ash route, and the exact concessions she wants.

**State leaving the encounter:** Corin rescued; all seven current bodies and vessels now controlled by free guardians; living false shelter ledger secured; critical Hall records copied; relief stores protected; Cora and six staff defect; Odo, Cleft, six marked guards, master Mercy Book, and token key below; Judith in the Underhall.

### Session close

The Commission does not descend immediately. Two pacts still make Judith effectively unkillable, the choir can still use names even when bodies are rescued, and the village-wide welcome still has all three components available. Sabren sends the public revocation form to every receiving site. Lysa confirms all seven current children are alive and medically stable enough to choose whether to attend the mere. Toma assembles carts, masks, shrouds, and pyre fuel. Neris closes the Hall's reflected corridor and leaves Cora in control of the service tunnel.

## Session Three: Three Debts Answered

The session follows the commission at decisive handoffs rather than treating all labor as sequential. Bellhouse workers begin before the Hall raid ends; refuge wedges, pit carts, masks, shrouds, pyre fuel, receiving tents, and household witness rosters are prepared by named crews while the company secures the manuals and assigns authority. Each later scene records what those crews completed and what still requires a decision.

### Chapel of the Free Witness

Brother Caldus, Piers Vey, Hett Vey, Dena Moor, and workers from six households open the shuttered chapel before noon. They preserve the closure strips as evidence, remove six hidden tokens, clear the blackthorn barrier, and keep the original threshold open rather than replacing it with a defensible door that would contradict the chapel's office.

Toma lowers the honest bell, replaces its pin with Vey bronze, hangs it on clean leather from the Darr Washhouse, and builds a carrying yoke from the Merrow cart. Sabren commissions the bell publicly: it will state where bodies were found, what evidence supports each name, and who denied burial. The bell is not declared holy enough to fix missing labor.

The chapel yields the Saint Vey refuge roll, pox procession chronicle, missing burial fragments, Auda Vey trial record, Judith's rejected confession memorandum, threshold manual, burial office, naming office, and public withdrawal form. The confession memorandum shows that Judith asked how to bind three guests, was told the acts would be murder and sacrilege, and left angry. It removes any plausible claim that she entered the pacts without understanding their costs.

The Commission does not recover the water-damaged diagram of the many-doored Underhall. They already know the Underhall's location and feast components from Mercy House, Crow Wood, and Blackbriar Hall. The missed document does not become retroactively legible merely because it would be useful.

The chapel becomes the central refuge and coordination site. Twelve Mercy House residents move from temporary public rooms into prepared beds. Pell places the civil stay, seizure dockets, and guard-release orders in chapel custody. Twenty-eight households make private revocations; nineteen of them read public revocations before witnesses. Eleven others destroy tokens but do not yet speak publicly. Five remain actively aligned with Judith or too divided to act.

**Judith's body:** Underhall.

**Major response spent:** Judith commands Piers through the lingering written copy of his name to strike the repaired bell. The bell interrupts the command; Piers completes the swing and then publicly names his prior willing service. The response exposes the last usable Vey name strip, which Sabren destroys.

**State leaving the encounter:** Honest bell commissioned and portable; pact manuals and rolls copied; chapel functioning as refuge, care site, and evidence repository; twenty-eight private and nineteen public household revocations; Piers free of the active name command; burial and naming crews organized.

### Burned Refuge

Toma leads eight workers to Saint Vey's granary with the living false ledger, chapel roll, wedges, ropes, and the freely offered brazier of Mara Sedge's restored hearth. Mara does not need to enter the burned structure. She understands the danger, removes Judith's remaining carrier, freely offers the flame, and witnesses from the open yard.

The workers pull the original west doors outward. The inward-burning soot tries to make the pressure feel like hands closing from inside. Nobody accepts the Guest's offer of painless passage. Once the doors open, the dead speak in fragments without the patron translating them.

Sabren and Dena Moor restore all nineteen names from the chapel roll, body marks, carved objects, wheel notches, tent ties, weights, and released memories. Uncertainty is recorded where a household mark supports order but not age. The honest record states that Judith promised shelter, barred the doors, and invited the Guest inside.

The living false ledger burns in Mara's freely offered brazier while the restored roll is read. Ash tries to creep back over each name and fails. Copied doors across the valley swing outward. Hearth passage and threshold denial end. Judith remains in the Underhall because the severance removes a route; it does not teleport her body.

The Commission misses the youngest soot child's direction to the “white house.” Saint Mercy House has already been found and evacuated. Six other refuge clues remain available and reinforce the pact's founding act, the White Pits road, the feast orientation, Judith's identity, and her formerly distributed defenses.

**Pact result:** Guest in Ash fully severed.

**State leaving the encounter:** Original doors open; nineteen names restored; false ledger destroyed correctly; refuge dead able to speak without the Guest; hearth passage, inward fire, threshold denial, and soot concealment ended.

### White Pits

The honest bell reaches the north orchard with two carts, sixteen rotating workers, long tools, dry brushes, masks, shrouds, and three prepared pyres. Lysa establishes an exposure line and refuses to let eager households enter the lime without assigned tools and relief.

The founding thirty-one are identified household by household. No single source does all the work. Caldus supplies fragments; families identify hooks, wash marks, teeth, linen codes, tallow seals, mill tokens, bell fittings, charcoal beads, prayer rings, boot nails, combs, buttons, and suppressed initials. Toma states uncertainty where evidence is combined. Sabren names Judith's burial diversions and Odo's suppressed dockets. The bell sounds for each group.

The Commission also recovers Auda Vey, Elian Darr, Ilyra Fen, Bram Kett, Toman Rusk, and two other renewal remains. Garran identifies Toman's transport and Odo's night order. He is not allowed to call cooperation expiation.

The founding thirty-one are lifted from quicklime before full dark. Twenty-seven receive honest burning under the bell. Four bodies—Rell and Siva Vane, Jessa Bale, and Tev Noll—remain wrapped and identified because their families contest whether the available pyre and witness are sufficiently independent of the officials who concealed them. Toma refuses to declare the operation complete for convenience. The Worm in White pact remains intact but drastically narrowed.

The renewal dead are all removed from Judith's command. Auda, Elian, Ilyra, Bram, and Toman are returned to their households for separate public rites after the feast; the two newly discovered remains remain under chapel custody pending identification.

The Worm tries to transfer Judith's wounds into the four unburned founding bodies. The honest bell makes the white threads visible. Lysa cuts one thread from Tev Noll before it enters a breathing worker, but Toma's left forearm is burned by quicklime when Olla's commanded body falls across the extraction line. Olla is laid beside her identified Pike kin and stops walking once lifted from the root.

The Commission misses the Mercy House infirmary tags on two later bodies because those tags dissolved during the lift. The infirmary records already preserve the route and identities.

**Pact result:** Worm in White partially broken. All thirty-one founding dead identified and lifted; twenty-seven honestly disposed; four remain wrapped and protected. Corpse command and wound displacement survive only through those four; broad sickness transfer and pit-root command are no longer available through removed bodies.

**State leaving the encounter:** Founding thirty-one identified and lifted; twenty-seven disposed; four pending independent rites; seven renewal remains recovered, five identified with certainty; Olla no longer animated; Toma injured but mobile; Worm pact narrowed but not severed.

### Moonless Mere

All seven current children choose their own level of participation. Nell, Amey, Jo, Lark, Perrin, Sella, and Corin are present with guardians and covered vessels. None is required to watch another return. Lysa maintains a warm recovery tent beyond reflected sight. Neris closes the carriage causeway and cuts neighboring reed routes before each basin test.

A free witness answers with the relationship by which they know the child. The vessels warm, hooks go slack, and the silver copies are broken downward into separate covered trays.

The drowned cabinet yields twelve older vessels. Five living former residents are reached through prepared witnesses and records. Four dead names are returned to memory and household account. The three damaged vessels remain covered, separately numbered, and unresolved. Nobody breaks them merely to claim completion.

Neris rejects the Child's reflected route to her former missing prisoner. Sabren refuses a true private fact about Pell offered in exchange for leaving one current vessel unreturned. Lysa refuses a borrowed voice that would let her call every Mercy House resident by the exact tone they trust. Toma refuses the Worm's identifications carried through the standing reflection.

The standing reflection finally copies Judith rather than the observer. It replays Judith offering the first seven children's names willingly, asking what additional names would buy command of dreams, and returning with a larger list. The Child pronounces Judith Crowl's name once and withdraws.

The Commission misses Nell's reflected reminder about the loose board because Nell is present in her own restored reflection and no longer needs to communicate through a hanging copy. The croft evidence was already secured.

**Pact result:** Child Behind Glass fully severed.

**State leaving the encounter:** All seven current names and nine identifiable older names returned; sixteen vessels broken after correct return; three damaged unidentified vessels preserved covered; borrowed rooms vanish; reflected routes and sustained name command end; all seven choir bays lose usable name control.

### Session close

Two pacts are severed. Judith is now bodily mortal and physically locatable. The remaining Worm pact can animate or receive harm through four protected but unresolved bodies, but cannot make her absent from an ordinary wound.

The common welcome still has the master Mercy Book and the token network. The captive choir is neutralized. Neutralizing either remaining component will collapse the valley-wide invitation, though isolated household claims will survive wherever tokens and signatures remain.

The Commission returns to Blackbriar Hall with twenty-eight private household revocations, nineteen public revocations, seven restored children, one injured gravewright, and no reflected shortcut.

## Session Four: The Hollow Feast

### Underhall of the Hollow Feast

Cora Pike opens the service tunnel. Judith has reached the fourth feast stage. Places are laid. Odo has read the household names. Gifts have answered through forty remaining token sockets. The children are absent, but he has substituted seven servants reading copied strips. The substitution produces voices without owned names and cannot satisfy the choir. Judith orders Sister Harl to bring the children. Harl enters instead with the Mercy House punishment record and reads the names of those she helped confine.

The common welcome therefore rests on the Mercy Book and token network alone. It remains operational while both continue.

Sabren goes to the Mercy lectern with Mara, Caldus, Pell, and copied household revocations. Odo offers to testify against Judith if Sabren preserves his office and suppresses the seven prosecution packets. Sabren refuses immunity and offers only physical protection for truthful testimony. Odo tries to cut the master index from the book and flee. Mara pins the loose pages under the iron writing board while Sabren severs the binding between names and concealed obligations. The master book remains evidence, but it no longer functions as one joined invitation.

At the token table, Cleft commands six marked guards to defend Judith. Piers's released name and Pell's lawful order free Will Noll from one lodging oath. Cora Pike and Hen Joss disarm two others. Edrin Bale continues fighting voluntarily after his command breaks; Lysa treats Linn Pell in sight of him and shows that Judith returned the illness without restoring his wife. He yields. Samet Kett swallows his coin and remains with Cleft until Neris strikes the token key from the captain's hand.

The Mercy Book's neutralization collapses the common welcome. Thirty-four connected doors swing shut or outward according to prior revocations. Eleven privately revoked households remain closed but silent. Five active Judith households stay open as local claims. The token network is still dangerous; it no longer constitutes one valley-wide threshold.

Judith calls the Worm in White. Four white threads lead from her body to the wrapped founding dead at the chapel. Toma rings the honest bell from the Underhall while four independent household witnesses complete the contested statements above. At the chapel, the four bodies are burned under separate witnesses. The threads break. The Worm pact severs during the confrontation rather than being declared complete in advance.

Judith is now a mortal practitioner without patron protection. She remains dangerous: she has ordinary knowledge, prepared blades, poison, command over willing lieutenants, and fifteen years of practiced cruelty. She offers Tomas Sedge a final choice through an open household door: strike Mara and Judith will leave Pate unnamed; refuse and Pate's token will burn inward. Tomas is not present in the Underhall and the Sedge token was already revoked. The threat fails because the practical protection was completed, not because the Commission wins a contest of will.

Judith tries to reach the service tunnel. Neris closes it with Garran's road key. Cleft attacks Cora to reopen the token route. Lysa keeps Cora alive while Piers and Hen Joss restrain Cleft. Sabren orders Judith to surrender under the stayed prosecution, the recovered murder evidence, and immediate danger to the vale. Judith laughs and drives a black glass knife toward Nell, who is behind the open service gate only long enough to identify the final copied strip.

Neris intercepts the strike. Toma's ordinary iron pry bar breaks Judith's wrist. Judith reaches for a hearth route that no longer exists and a reflected exit that no longer answers. Sabren gives one final surrender order. Judith instead cuts her own throat shallowly, expecting the wound to travel to the pits. It remains on her body. Lysa attempts to stop the bleeding because custody and testimony would serve the survivors. Judith tears the wound open again while naming the people she believes still belong to her. She dies in the Underhall by her own refusal of capture, after every patron channel that would have displaced the injury has been severed.

Her death does not end the remaining local claims. The five actively aligned households retain tokens. The three unidentified vessels remain dangerous. Odo, Cleft, Garran, Harl, Pell, and several guards require separate judgments. The relief network must continue without Judith's ownership.

The Underhall itself provides four final confirmations: the empty Guest chair recoils from the opened refuge, the Worm threads disappear as the last four bodies receive honest disposition, the broken chairs remove Judith's protections, and the rite continues through the Book until its binding is physically severed. These are consequences of prior work, not substitute instructions delivered only at the finale.

**Final feast state:** Choir neutralized; Mercy Book neutralized but preserved as evidence; common welcome collapsed; token network remains as five active and several unresolved local claims.

**Final pact state:** Guest, Worm, and Child severed.

**Judith:** Dead in the Underhall after refusing surrender; body retained under guarded civil and chapel custody.

## Demonstrated end state

### Mara and the Sedge family

Mara survives. Her conviction is stayed and then voided for fabricated evidence, unlawful procedure, and Judith's control of witnesses and officers. Nell's name is restored. Tomas is not made responsible for the execution, the confrontation, or Judith's death. Pate remains with Adela Merrow during the final operations. The Sedge Croft remains a six-person refuge and evidence site.

### The seven current children

Nell Sedge, Amey Fen, Jo Rusk, Lark Merrow, Perrin Mett, Sella Darr, and Corin Pike are alive, free of sustained name command, and placed with named guardians. None remains in the Underhall choir. Their restoration does not erase trauma, custody disputes, medical needs, or anger at adults who cooperated with Judith.

### Saint Mercy House

The House is not burned or abandoned. Its residents are distributed among the chapel, Darr Washhouse, parish granary, Merrow Cottage, and supervised unmirrored rooms while beds and food are organized. Matron Wren and Ressa Pold continue care. Sister Harl is removed from authority and held under protected house arrest while her cooperation and culpability are recorded separately.

### The pacts

- **Guest in Ash:** Severed by opening the original doors, restoring all nineteen names, and burning the living false ledger in Mara's freely offered hearth.
- **Worm in White:** Narrowed at the pits and severed during the finale when the final four founding bodies receive honest disposition under independent witnesses.
- **Child Behind Glass:** Severed after seven current and nine older recoverable names are returned in unborrowed reflections and their vessels broken afterward.

### The feast components

- **Mercy Book:** Master binding severed from concealed obligations; pages and index preserved as evidence.
- **Token network:** No longer joined into a common welcome. Five active household claims and several uncertain signatures remain for local revocation work.
- **Captive choir:** Neutralized before entry because all seven children and names are free.

### Judith and principal collaborators

Judith dies after refusing surrender and attempting to rely on severed wound displacement. Odo Vane is captured with the Mercy Book and offers testimony without immunity. Varro Cleft is captured after ordering continued violence. Garran Pike cooperates, identifies routes and bodies, and remains in custody for transport, confinement, and corpse use. Sister Harl is removed from office and protected as a witness. Reeve Pell retains no unilateral authority over the investigation; his stay and later cooperation are weighed against years of false seizures.

### The dead

All thirty-one founding plague dead are identified, lifted, and honestly disposed. Five named renewal victims are recovered for household rites. Two additional renewal remains await identification. The burial operation therefore ends the pact without pretending every later disappearance has already been resolved.

### Evidence and authority

Three independent custody sets survive: chapel copies, Sabren's sealed commission file, and household-held packets. The principal evidence includes the seven prosecution packets, Mercy House records, Hall accusation drafts, living-pact records, injury ledgers, feast checklist, token lists, crow messages, prisoner papers, burial rolls, and Judith's rejected confession memorandum.

Canon Ysra's commission does not become a permanent government. Pell's emergency powers are suspended. A temporary council of Caldus Merrow, Cora Pike, Matron Wren, Dena Moor, Mara Sedge, and one rotating household delegate controls food, refuge, evidence access, and witness protection until outside civil and chapel authorities arrive.

### Continuing work

- Revoke or adjudicate the five active household claims.
- Identify the three damaged name vessels without exposing them.
- Identify two later bodies from the renewal trench.
- Maintain food, fuel, medicine, and beds formerly organized through Judith.
- Decide charges and protections for Pell, Harl, Garran, guards, and ordinary staff without collapsing command, fear, cooperation, and willing cruelty into one category.
- Repair household titles and property seizures produced by seven false prosecutions and likely additional cases.
- Keep the children and former residents in control of how their names and testimony are used.

## What this route demonstrates

### Judith remains hateable without becoming mechanically arbitrary

She chooses the torch, the returned illness, the tailored bargains, the manipulated dead, and the final attack. Her reach always comes through a body, record, token, name, hearth, familiar, or servant. Removing a channel changes what she can do rather than merely changing narration around an omnipotent villain.

### Rescue and counter-rites remain material

The Commission does not “free the children” with one victory or “cleanse the pits” with one spell. Bodies, bottles, guardians, routes, witnesses, carts, shrouds, names, vessels, hearths, records, and care remain separate obligations. Success depends on villagers accepting concrete work as Judith's system breaks.

### Incomplete pact work creates a real finale

The Worm pact remains partially active after the White Pits. The final four bodies matter during the confrontation. Their delayed disposition makes the remaining defense visible without invalidating the twenty-seven completed rites.

### Irregular clues survive demonstrated play

The journal spots seventy-two of ninety-five clues, records eleven visit-specific misses, and leaves twelve second-look alternatives unseen. Demonstrated support ranges from two to seven spotted clues per revelation rather than becoming uniform. The most public facts—Judith's culpability, the three pacts, and the invitation machinery—accumulate broad support. Exact locations and counter-rites remain narrower.

### Killing Judith is permitted but not sufficient

The Commission neutralizes the choir and Mercy Book before Judith dies. Her death does not sever a pact, revoke a token, restore a name, identify a body, preserve evidence, or feed a displaced resident. It ends the woman who kept claiming every solution as proof of ownership; it does not perform the work she hoarded.

At first light, Tomas places the unused torch beside Sabren's evidence board. Outside Blackbriar Hall, households queue with tokens, receipts, empty medicine bottles, burial tags, and claims to confiscated goods. For the first time, the line is not waiting for Judith to decide whose need counts. Six temporary custodians sit at separate tables, and every transfer is read back to the person whose name it bears.
