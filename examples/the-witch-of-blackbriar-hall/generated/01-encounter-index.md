# Encounter Index: The Witch of Blackbriar Hall

## Saint Orra's Gallows (`saint-orra-gallows`)

**Role:** necessary, start

Judith places Mara's torch in Tomas's hands, making an illegal execution a public receipt for obedience.

## Sedge Croft (`sedge-croft`)

**Role:** necessary

Quill and three hearthlings tear through Mara's stripped house while seven case packets, Nell's route, and two planted witnesses remain inside.

## Saint Mercy House (`saint-mercy-house`)

**Role:** necessary

Saint Mercy House turns bread, beds, medicines, copied names, and transferred illness into a custody system disguised as charity.

## Blackbriar Hall (`blackbriar-hall`)

**Role:** necessary

Petitioners queue beside winter stores and confiscated property while Judith's ledgers, couriers, pact records, and stair below remain in active use.

## The Burned Refuge (`burned-refuge`)

**Role:** necessary

Nineteen barred refugees still knock inside the granary where Judith converted promised shelter into the Guest's title.

## The White Pits (`white-pits`)

**Role:** necessary

Thirty-one pox dead wait beneath orchard lime, still carrying the illnesses, wounds, and bodies Judith charged to them.

## Chapel of the Free Witness (`chapel-of-the-free-witness`)

**Role:** necessary

The doorless chapel preserves the rolls, bell, and witness rites that restore shelter, burial, and naming to the people who must answer for them.

## Moonless Mere (`moonless-mere`)

**Role:** necessary

Silvered names hang above a pool that keeps its own night, where the Child lends Judith voices, recognition, and reflected roads.

## Crow Wood (`crow-wood`)

**Role:** necessary

Crows, ash packets, prisoner wagons, and a service tunnel cross one wood whose false turns follow the objects travelers carry.

## Underhall of the Hollow Feast (`underhall-of-the-hollow-feast`)

**Role:** necessary, end

Beneath sixty-three carved doors, Judith joins signed gratitude, household carriers, captive voices, and three old betrayals into one invitation.
