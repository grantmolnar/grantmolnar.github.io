# Reference Index: The Witch of Blackbriar Hall

## People

- [**Judith Crowl**](fc38cc24-165f-4df6-bf04-7813911d6b8d.md) — Blackbriar Vale's relief patron and deliberate witch, who turns real aid into inherited debt through three chosen betrayals and a finite network of hearths, names, records, servants, and roads. Aliases: Judith, Mistress Crowl, the witch of Blackbriar Hall.
- [**Canon Ysra Vale**](7dc115f7-e717-427c-ba9b-33f28fbd2364.md) — The canon whose burial discrepancies and unanswered Mercy House letters produced a narrow inspection commission able to preserve evidence and witnesses without governing Blackbriar Vale. Aliases: Ysra Vale, Canon Vale, Ysra.
- [**Brother Caldus Merrow**](1ab6d356-b394-4528-b5c5-0530f8d56a65.md) — Blackbriar parish clerk, compromised burial witness, and keeper of the dates, inks, fees, and chapel fragments that turn missing bodies into a provable institutional pattern. Aliases: Caldus Merrow, Brother Caldus, Caldus.
- [**Mara Sedge**](9123fa4b-e1bd-45c7-acf2-7656663ee5b3.md) — A Blackbriar midwife whose exact birth and illness records expose the repeated structure behind seven prosecutions, and whose practical law begins with keeping named children alive. Aliases: Mara, Mara Sedge the midwife, the Sedge midwife.
- [**Nell Sedge**](24f7323c-e560-49a1-8f44-95c715c2a2d0.md) — Mara Sedge's thirteen-year-old daughter, an observant captive cartographer who counted the mirror dormitory, named seven linked children, and learned the safe order for returning a stolen name. Aliases: Nell, Nell Sedge of the mirror dormitory.
- [**Tomas Sedge**](69daecdb-c3fe-4aab-99ad-4a9feafe9720.md) — Mara Sedge's eleven-year-old son, a direct witness to Judith's public coercion who knows Nell's hiding places and Quill's route and responds to concrete protection rather than adult moral summaries. Aliases: Tomas, Tomas Sedge of the gallows green.
- [**Reeve Ansel Pell**](d23d66ad-f4d2-46c3-b62d-68d7dc725cca.md) — Blackbriar Vale's frightened civil magistrate, cured through another man's suffering and practiced at hiding his own false seizures inside passive grammar and borrowed ordinances. Aliases: Ansel Pell, Reeve Pell, Pell.
- [**Beadle Odo Vane**](a356a5b7-8460-49ac-85ec-593b435280f7.md) — A willing court collaborator who turns Judith's injuries into charges, custody, and household obligations, and whose love of office survives exposure more readily than his loyalty to any person. Aliases: Odo Vane, Beadle Vane, Odo.
- [**Captain Varro Cleft**](5af5fa39-8bd9-4dea-9c0c-f0998fd3b7a1.md) — Judith's Blackthorn commander, a willing enforcer who turns official language into permission for violence and manages guards, prisoners, coerced testimony, and the roads connecting accusation to disappearance. Aliases: Varro Cleft, Captain Cleft, Cleft.
- [**Sister Harl**](03ef5b0b-c002-4f38-9a3b-47a03a379e8d.md) — The steward of Saint Mercy House, culpable for hidden naming and coercive transfers yet competent at keeping a real institution alive, and most vulnerable when Judith treats residents as expendable feast material. Aliases: Harl, Sister Harl of Saint Mercy House, Mercy House steward.
- [**Corin Pike**](adb089a8-d281-4d33-beaa-74629823bc6a.md) — A fifteen-year-old Pike Mill apprentice used as Judith’s page, who knows the separated Mercy Book leaves, hidden page-number key, service routes, and coercive naming system from inside Blackbriar Hall. Aliases: Corin, the Blackbriar page, Pike Mill apprentice.
- [**Garran Pike**](d8e9dfa9-6c79-43e3-b8ca-e645c023469c.md) — Judith’s practiced roadkeeper and prisoner-wagon driver, who knows every fixed landmark, hidden compartment, route key, and handoff joining Mercy House, Crow Wood, Blackbriar Hall, the White Pits, and the Underhall. Aliases: Garran, Judith’s roadkeeper, the prisoner-wagon driver.

## Places

- [**Saint Mercy House**](426ebd86-3426-4b5a-b2ce-1b6c09b43e68.md) — Blackbriar’s effective charitable house and coercive custody institution, joining public care, hidden naming, transferred illness, records, dormitories, infirmary work, and routes to Judith’s wider household system. Aliases: Mercy House, the House, Judith’s charitable house.
- [**Blackbriar Hall**](b2bfc545-8e6b-446b-a961-fe60db81ffc9.md) — Judith Crowl’s public household government, where charity stores, petitions, seizures, accusation work, service routes, pact channels, and the descent to the Hollow Feast meet under one roof. Aliases: the Hall, Judith’s house, Blackbriar estate.
- [**The Burned Refuge**](46c9f075-d08e-4251-8fba-c86f47b30411.md) — The granary where Judith barred nineteen promised refugees inside and welcomed the Guest in Ash, leaving an outward-opening door, restored names, and a freely offered hearth as the material terms of repair. Aliases: Saint Vey’s refuge granary, the burned granary, the famine refuge.
- [**The White Pits**](9588d099-9c55-4750-b5f6-271d63b32456.md) — Judith’s orchard burial ground, where thirty-one plague dead were erased in quicklime and later bodies were added, joining false fees, transferred illness, unburied remains, and the Worm in White’s claim. Aliases: the orchard pits, quicklime trenches, the founding trenches.
- [**Chapel of the Free Witness**](e2455050-3963-46d2-8736-0dd826037218.md) — Blackbriar’s deliberately open chapel, preserving the honest bell and divided threshold, burial, and naming offices that require free witnesses, material repair, and public responsibility. Aliases: the Free Witness chapel, the closed chapel, Blackbriar chapel.
- [**Moonless Mere**](2fe9af67-c2c0-49d4-b491-a7d220b3c95e.md) — The black water where Judith lowers silvered names to the Child Behind Glass, preserving reed shore, vessel hooks, an unborrowed basin, drowned records, and reflected routes that depend on controlled identities. Aliases: the Mere, the name-water, Judith’s drowned cabinet.
- [**Crow Wood**](b4ac4a8e-4b89-4bd9-a9b1-4ffe2274f9eb.md) — A fixed woodland road exchange whose streams, ridge, culvert, burned boundary, tunnel, roosts, and carrier-dependent false turns connect Judith’s prisoners, messages, bodies, stores, and pact routes. Aliases: the Wood, Judith’s road exchange, Blackbriar roadwood.
- [**Underhall of the Hollow Feast**](bae1dfda-a1f6-468a-a083-be9ebb6057ee.md) — The chamber beneath Blackbriar Hall where sixty-three household doors, the Mercy lectern, token table, seven choir bays, dead settings, patron chairs, and Judith’s place assemble a valley-wide welcome. Aliases: the Underhall, the Hollow Feast chamber, Judith’s account hall.

## Organizations

- [**The Blackthorn Men**](d2957684-e1f2-405d-87f9-65521364f2cb.md) — Judith’s distributed guard and transport force, divided between willing officers, marked bodies subject to visible command, and ordinary paid retainers who remain capable of refusal. Aliases: Blackthorn guards, Cleft’s company, Judith’s guards.
- [**The Unwelcome Court**](d119cb74-4f99-4e9a-8ae5-b500cfbe95f3.md) — The otherworldly court Judith sought through three deliberate betrayals, able to inherit a valley-wide household invitation only through surviving patron claims and at least two joined feast components. Aliases: the Court, Judith’s three guests, the common welcome.

## Objects

- [**The Mercy Book**](4d5e6282-bbb2-481c-99d4-31e581fe1737.md) — Judith’s distributed household account, whose master index joins separated pages of gifts, signatures, custody, and accepted obligations into the written-host component of the Hollow Feast. Aliases: Mercy Book, Judith’s household book, the master account.
- [**Blackthorn Tokens**](91b081d9-3044-46e9-8b0e-a9e1db7624b3.md) — Physical household and command carriers—tokens, splinters, coins, marked gifts, cure receipts, oaths, and stolen ash—through which Judith can identify a claimed door, redirect a bearer, or seize a marked body. Aliases: Judith’s household marks, token network.
- [**The Honest Bell**](c85a1b9d-90dd-4958-be4a-e0fc6eaca068.md) — The Chapel of the Free Witness’s repairable portable bell, which makes concealed transfers visible and gives bodies, wounds, names, and public refusals a shared audible witness without completing their obligations automatically. Aliases: honest bell, Auda Vey’s bell, the Free Witness bell.
- [**Silvered Name Vessels**](9f7dfd7a-646b-4d48-86a2-684ac88398a0.md) — Folded written names sealed behind narrow mirrors, allowing the Child Behind Glass to coordinate identity, command, and reflected passage while remaining distinct from each owner’s body and living voice. Aliases: name vessels, silvered slips, mirror-name vessels.

## Other

- [**The Guest in Ash**](3a669960-c05d-4bd8-ae4b-33137d486977.md) — The patron welcomed through nineteen refugees barred inside a promised granary, granting carrier-bound hearth passage, inward fire, threshold denial, and soot concealment while enforcing plainly accepted invitations. Aliases: the Guest, the door-shaped Guest, Judith’s first guest.
- [**The Worm in White**](20388d1b-f4cb-40a7-b65f-686bb4e48be3.md) — The patron welcomed through thirty-one plague dead erased in quicklime, able to move sickness, animate abandoned flesh, spoil stores, and place Judith’s wounds on the unburied. Aliases: the Worm, the white patron, Judith’s plague guest.
- [**The Child Behind Glass**](5d4b8115-88b1-4952-b4b1-1a67b35d3d8e.md) — The patron served through stolen written names lowered into Moonless Mere, lending borrowed voices, dreams, false recognition, written command, reflected roads, and the Glass Nurse. Aliases: the Child, the Glass Child, Judith’s mirror patron.
