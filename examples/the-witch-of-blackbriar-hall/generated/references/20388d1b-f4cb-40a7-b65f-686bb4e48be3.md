## Reference Material

## A tally made from unreturned bodies

Families paid Judith to carry thirty-one plague dead to honest ground. She removed their tags, laid them in quicklime beneath the orchard, and denied each body a final witness. The Worm in White entered through that missing account. Later bodies and patients could renew or exploit the channel without becoming the founding betrayal themselves.

## Harm travels through a prepared line

The Worm can move illness between labeled bodies, animate abandoned remains, spoil stores, return a purchased cure, and shift Judith’s injuries onto the unburied. It acts through bodies, white roots, bottles, wounds, burial omissions, and prepared routes. A body still moving is not thereby restored, and a transferred symptom does not identify who consented to bear it.

## The dead require an honest disposition

Identify the founding thirty-one, lift them safely from quicklime, state where they were found and who denied them, and give each honest burial or controlled burning beneath the honest bell. Partial identifications and lifted bodies are real progress. Count patients, bottles, workers, transfers, manifestations, and final dispositions where the operation actually leaves them.
