## Reference Material

## Difficult woodland, not a changing world

Crow Wood does not brood or rearrange itself. Saint Orra’s brook runs south, the white quarry ridge remains east, the Burned Refuge marks the western boundary, the orchard culvert leads toward the White Pits, and the root-cut tunnel reaches Blackbriar Hall. Wagon grade, stream flow, ruts, and fixed landmarks remain honest even when a traveler’s attention does not.

## A carrier makes the wrong road persuasive

Blackthorn, stolen ash, controlled names, Mercy custody papers, marked gifts, and guiding crows can make one junction seem familiar and another disappear from notice. Removing or isolating the carrier stops further redirection; it does not move the traveler back or erase the road already taken.

## Where channels exchange cargo

The prisoner road, familiar roosts, orchard track, Mercy track, service road, and refuge boundary let paper, ash, bodies, prisoners, stores, and reflected commands change hands. Start each visit by marking the actual wagon, messages, prisoners, crows, escorts, carriers, keys, and last witnessed destinations. The map fixes geography, not traffic or control.
