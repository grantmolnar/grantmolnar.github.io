# Sister Harl

**Reference ID:** `03ef5b0b-c002-4f38-9a3b-47a03a379e8d`

## Summary

The steward of Saint Mercy House, culpable for hidden naming and coercive transfers yet competent at keeping a real institution alive, and most vulnerable when Judith treats residents as expendable feast material.

## Reference Material

## Care kept by ownership

Sister Harl administers Saint Mercy House. She knows its intake forms, beds, kitchens, infirmary, resident needs, name repetitions, punishment routines, and transfer routes. The house feeds children and treats patients because Harl can organize people and stores. She also collects names, orders coercive discipline, conceals rooms, and accepts custody as leverage over families.

## The institution before the resident

Harl believes dependency should be used without apology. She has told herself that Judith preserves Saint Mercy House and that hidden naming is the price of keeping it open. Evidence of abuse alone makes her defend the institution more fiercely. Proof that Judith intends to spend residents rather than preserve them can break that argument only when paired with a credible plan for beds, food, medicines, carriers, receiving households, and immediate safety.

## Competence does not settle loyalty

Harl can identify residents, medical burdens, bottle lines, keys, transfer requirements, and the work needed to move vulnerable people without turning rescue into abandonment. That competence can serve Judith, an evacuation, or a negotiated handover depending on what happens. Her allegiance, control, cooperation, route, custody decisions, transfers, survival, and role at the Hollow Feast remain live adventure state.
