# The Honest Bell

**Reference ID:** `c85a1b9d-90dd-4958-be4a-e0fc6eaca068`  
**Kind:** Object  
**Aliases:** honest bell, Auda Vey’s bell, the Free Witness bell  
**Tags:** Free Witness chapel, public witness, transferred harm, burial rite

## Summary

The Chapel of the Free Witness’s repairable portable bell, which makes concealed transfers visible and gives bodies, wounds, names, and public refusals a shared audible witness without completing their obligations automatically.

## Reference Material

## A bell closed under the wrong law

The honest bell belonged to the Chapel of the Free Witness until Judith used a famine ordinance to close the chapel and divide its fittings. Its beam, clapper, metal, fastening, and surviving records can be reunited into a portable instrument. The bell’s authority comes from free public witness and accurate use, not sacred rank or possession alone.

## What the sound reveals

At the White Pits, the bell states where the dead were found and who denied them. Near transferred illness, it makes white movement visible. Against commands resting on the false chapel closure, it can release a marked body. At the Underhall, it lets wounds and the dead answer under their own names rather than Judith’s feast response.

## Sound does not finish the work

The bell does not identify a body without evidence, provide a burial place, return a name, supply labor, or make a witness free. A repairable bell is not yet repaired, commissioned, present, or audible. Record its parts, carrier, location, audience, sounding, effect, and survival each time the company relies on it.
