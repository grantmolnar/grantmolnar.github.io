# The Guest in Ash

## Summary

The patron welcomed through nineteen refugees barred inside a promised granary, granting carrier-bound hearth passage, inward fire, threshold denial, and soot concealment while enforcing plainly accepted invitations.

## Reference Material

## Welcome betrayed at the door

Judith’s first pact began when she promised nineteen road people shelter, barred Saint Vey’s granary from outside, and welcomed the Guest in Ash across the trusted threshold. The Guest’s claim is not fire in general. It is shelter deliberately converted into confinement and an account that says the betrayed were safely received.

## An offer must be accepted

The Guest can offer warmth, passage, concealment, a dead guide, or safety from another channel. Questions, silence, threats, and “not now” do not accept. Taking the offered coal, saying “come in,” deliberately crossing a door the Guest creates, or otherwise agreeing to the stated exchange gives it a path through that person’s next accepted shelter. Existing marked hearths and stolen ash provide finite carriers; they do not make every flame Judith’s door.

## Repair the obligation, not the scenery

Open the original granary doors outward, restore all nineteen names under witness, and burn the false shelter ledger in a freely offered hearth belonging to someone Judith betrayed. Judge progress from those material acts and from bargains knowingly accepted in play. The patron sheet does not make an offer accepted, place a carrier, weaken a threshold, or sever the pact.
