## Reference Material

## The man who keeps the roads

Garran Pike knows the weight of a concealed passenger, the sound of a drugged prisoner against floorboards, the lime that works into a wheel after a body run, and the difference between a false turn and a fixed grade. For years he has moved children, witnesses, confiscated goods, sealed orders, and unburied dead while telling himself that transport is not judgment.

## Olla walking beside the wagon

Judith keeps Garran obedient through Olla Pike, his wife, whose body the Worm in White can force along the orchard road. Garran knows that the thing standing under Olla’s ankle bell is not a restored wife. He nevertheless treats each delivery as payment for one more night in which her face remains visible. That grief explains the bargain; it does not absolve the locked compartments behind him.

## A road action proves a break

Garran’s stable competence includes the iron road key, crow handoffs, the prisoner road, Mercy track, orchard route, and service tunnel. None of that proves he still holds the wagon, papers, prisoners, or key. Track Olla and his allegiance where events place them; a change of heart counts only when it becomes a material road action.
