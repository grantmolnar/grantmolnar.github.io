# The Unwelcome Court

**Reference ID:** `d119cb74-4f99-4e9a-8ae5-b500cfbe95f3`  
**Kind:** Organization  
**Aliases:** the Court, Judith’s three guests, the common welcome  
**Tags:** three patrons, common invitation, Hollow Feast, household claim

## Summary

The otherworldly court Judith sought through three deliberate betrayals, able to inherit a valley-wide household invitation only through surviving patron claims and at least two joined feast components.

## Reference Material

## Three guests, not one hidden master

The Unwelcome Court is the relation among the Guest in Ash, the Worm in White, and the Child Behind Glass. Judith did not discover it by accident. She chose one betrayal for each patron and later built relief, custody, burial, household, and record systems around the access purchased. The Court is not a fourth pact that restores a broken chair or replaces a patron whose obligation has been completed.

## A common invitation must be assembled

The Court can enter Blackbriar Vale as one household only when Judith’s Hollow Feast joins at least two of three components—the Mercy Book, token network, and captive choir—and at least one patron claim remains capable of inheriting the welcome. Smaller household claims may survive after the valley-wide rite fails, but they do not become the same common invitation by rhetoric alone.

## No automatic midnight

Time gives Judith pressure, not omnipotence. The Court requires actual pages, carriers, living voices, doors, bodies, routes, and a surviving violation. Its three patrons may still exist while the common invitation fails. Read manifestation, inheritance, household exposure, and final outcome from the feast actually assembled.
