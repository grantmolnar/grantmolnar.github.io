## Two of three make the common welcome

The Hollow Feast joins three components: written hosts in the Mercy Book, physical household carriers in the token network, and at least four controlled living voices in the captive choir. The valley-wide welcome operates while any two remain joined. Judith’s place coordinates them but is not itself a component. A surviving pact gives the Unwelcome Court a patron through which to inherit the assembled claim.
