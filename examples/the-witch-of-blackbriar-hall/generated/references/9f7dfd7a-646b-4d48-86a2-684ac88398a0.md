# Silvered Name Vessels

**Reference ID:** `9f7dfd7a-646b-4d48-86a2-684ac88398a0`  
**Kind:** Object  
**Aliases:** name vessels, silvered slips, mirror-name vessels  
**Tags:** stolen names, mirror dormitory, safe handling, Child Behind Glass

## Summary

Folded written names sealed behind narrow mirrors, allowing the Child Behind Glass to coordinate identity, command, and reflected passage while remaining distinct from each owner’s body and living voice.

## Reference Material

## A written name behind borrowed silver

A name vessel is a folded slip bearing a chosen name, sealed between a mirror’s backing and glass. Judith’s current system uses seven narrow vessels around a central mirror that combines their reflections into the Glass Nurse. The object can carry identity and command across distance; it is never the same thing as the person whose name it holds.

## Cover before carrying

Safe handling begins with dull unpatterned cloth. Do not read the name aloud in a reflected room. Keep the vessel with a free carrier, record the owner separately, and preserve it for an absent person rather than declaring that person rescued. Covering prevents observation and reflected coordination but does not heal the owner or restore the name.

## Return before breaking

Use the owner or a free witness to establish identity, find an unborrowed reflection, speak the name back, wait for the reflection to belong to the owner, and only then break the vessel. The safe sequence is stable; every vessel’s owner, location, covering, condition, witness, restoration, injury, and final disposition must be established separately.
