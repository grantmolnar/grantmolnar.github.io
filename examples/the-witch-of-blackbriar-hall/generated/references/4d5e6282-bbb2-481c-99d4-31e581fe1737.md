# The Mercy Book

## Summary

Judith’s distributed household account, whose master index joins separated pages of gifts, signatures, custody, and accepted obligations into the written-host component of the Hollow Feast.

## Reference Material

## Mercy entered as title

The Mercy Book records households through the aid Judith claims they accepted: food, fuel, cure, burial, bed, custody, protection, or place. Its fraud is not that every entry invents the benefit. It treats the benefit as permanent authority over names, rooms, heirs, thresholds, and dependents.

## One book carried in pieces

The master index joins separated leaves carried among Saint Mercy House, Blackbriar Hall, servants, and the Underhall. Each leaf contains only a few households so no ordinary carrier holds the complete plan. Signatures and entries become the written-host component only when pages and index are materially joined at the Mercy lectern.

## Evidence and machinery must be separated

The Book can be removed from its lectern, divided from its index, corrected under free witness, rendered unusable after evidence is copied, or destroyed after stolen names and necessary records are separated. Burning joined pages while the Guest remains active can send signed ash toward household hearths. Treat every page, index, signature, carrier, correction, and destruction as material state rather than assuming the component is assembled.
