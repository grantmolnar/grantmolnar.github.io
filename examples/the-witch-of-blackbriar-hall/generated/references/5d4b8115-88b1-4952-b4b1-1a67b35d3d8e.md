## Summary

The patron served through stolen written names lowered into Moonless Mere, lending borrowed voices, dreams, false recognition, written command, reflected roads, and the Glass Nurse.

## Reference Material

## A price Judith returned to pay again

The Child Behind Glass named its price before Judith paid it. She copied children’s chosen names onto silvered slips, sealed them behind mirrors, and lowered the names through Moonless Mere. Saint Mercy House then turned intake, dormitory, transfer, and record work into a steady acquisition system. The Child’s apparent youth does not make the bargain innocent or accidental.

## A name opens a bounded surface

The Child lends voices, dreams, false recognition, written commands, reflected routes, and the composite Glass Nurse. Those acts require a controlled name and an uncovered corresponding surface. A vessel can support command at a distance, but it is not the owner’s body, consent, testimony, or living voice. Covering glass blocks one surface; it does not return the stolen name.

## Identity must return before glass breaks

Establish the owner through the person or a free witness, use an unborrowed reflection, speak the name back, wait for the reflection to belong to its owner, then break the vessel. A hook or written name identifies possible leverage, not the owner’s body, route, consent, restoration, or safety. Track each separately.
