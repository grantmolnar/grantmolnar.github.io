# Crow Wood

**Role:** necessary  
**Tags:** forest, pursuit, familiars, rescue

Crows, ash packets, prisoner wagons, and a service tunnel cross one wood whose false turns follow the objects travelers carry.

## Opening View

A red-curtained wagon moves through Crow Wood on white wheels that leave lime in the ruts. One prisoner knocks Saint Orra's bell rhythm from the visible cage; another answers behind a wall the wagon should not have. At the fork, a crow drops a paper strip into Garran Pike's hand. He turns an iron road key, and the broad track appears to bend east although Saint Orra's brook still runs south. The knocking stops. A child's borrowed voice gives the wrong turn.

## Encounter Material

## Situation

Start by naming what is moving now. The red-curtained wagon may carry the adventure's original prisoners, transfers from Saint Mercy House, people seized after public resistance, or no captives at all. Quill and ordinary crows may hold message strips; Garran may still hold the iron key; the Glass Nurse may occupy a polished panel; one route may already belong to witnesses or defectors.

Judith redirects attention through a physical carrier rather than moving the wood. Streams, quarry ridges, wagon grades, and the root-cut tunnel remain honest. Record two fixed landmarks, the object that pulled the traveler, and the cargo's last witnessed destination; pursuit then becomes road work rather than an argument with scenery.
## Fixed landmarks

Five features remain in the same relation regardless of magic.

- **Saint Orra's brook** runs south toward the gallows road.
- **The white quarry ridge** lies east of the prisoner road and can be seen through the canopy at three high points.
- **The burned western boundary** adjoins the Burned Refuge. Crows refuse to cross the original threshold line.
- **The old orchard culvert** carries the north track toward the White Pits.
- **The root-cut tunnel mouth** lies beneath a split blackthorn on the northeast slope and reaches Blackbriar Hall.

A route record using two fixed landmarks remains usable after the marked paths shift.

## The marked-path rule

A traveler can be redirected only when carrying at least one active carrier:

- a blackthorn token or splinter;
- stolen hearth ash;
- a written name controlled by Judith;
- a Mercy House custody paper;
- a marked gift accepted from Judith;
- or a crow physically guiding the route.

At a junction, the carrier warms, bleeds soot, reflects the wrong sky, or pulls the traveler's attention toward Judith's assigned destination. Removing, covering, or separating the carrier stops further redirection. It does not teleport the traveler back to a prior location.

The wood can obscure a route; it cannot move a stream, ridge, wagon rut, prisoner, or tunnel mouth offstage.

## Active routes

### Prisoner road

The broad central track carries the red-curtained wagon. It loops around the same stand of alder unless the driver has Garran's iron road key or follows a crow carrying the current route strip. Cutting the red curtains removes one visual command surface but does not stop the wheels.

### Familiar road

A narrow canopy path joins three roost platforms. Messenger strips travel from the village to the middle rookery, where a second crow carries them toward the hall or pact site. Capturing one bird reveals one message and the next handoff, not the entire network.

### Orchard track

White lime dust, old wheel nails, and cracked apple seeds mark the route to the White Pits. Garran uses it for bodies, exposure victims, and the obedient remains of Olla Pike.

### Mercy track

A covered path reaches Saint Mercy House's east bridge. Its low branches are cut to the height of the house transfer wagon. Children have tied thread under bark and scratched room numbers on two boundary stones.

### Service road

The hidden road descends to Blackbriar Hall's undercroft gate. Its final turn passes under a polished black panel. While the Child pact remains intact, the panel can show the road empty to pursuers or make a wagon appear to turn toward the orchard. Covering, breaking, or viewing it in an unborrowed reflection reveals the true descent.

### Refuge boundary

The western path reaches the Burned Refuge. Familiars land only outside the barred-door line. A person who crosses through correctly opened doors can use the boundary as temporary shelter from the Guest and from crow pursuit.

## Garran Pike

Place Garran according to the current route state: driving the prisoner wagon, waiting at a crow handoff, holding the service tunnel, following Olla, or already separated from the key. His first calculation is material—what the axle carries, which strip redirects the next turn, who controls the false wall, and whether Judith can still make Olla walk beside him.

Olla's ankle bell is his strongest leash. Pit evidence, the bell, or a credible release can break the excuse that each delivery buys one more night with her face. The locked compartments behind him remain a separate problem. Garran changes sides only when he turns the wagon, surrenders the key, cuts a route, releases a prisoner, or fights the escort that would take the road back.

### Garran's field methods

- He sends the loud crow one way and the loaded axle another.
- He strikes the person carrying a token, name strip, road key, or evidence satchel so the useful object changes hands.
- Red cord between trees rings the rookery and tunnel bells.
- One prisoner remains visible; the others travel behind the polished false wall.
- His retreat line always ends at Olla or the service tunnel, whichever Judith can still use.
- He offers one captive for the map or ledger that could free the rest.

Garran speaks in road facts: “Rear wheel is carrying two more bodies than the cage shows.” “Third turn repeats if the red strip stays on you.” “My wife is east of the culvert, and no, I will not call that alive.”

If he breaks, require a road action: turn the wagon, surrender the key, cut the rookery cords, fight Cleft's escort, or carry Olla beneath the honest bell. Surrender without such an act leaves the route in Judith's hands.

## The prisoner wagon

The red-curtained wagon has white wheels because quicklime remains worked into the hubs. Its current load depends on prior events, but at the beginning of the adventure it is prepared to carry **Jo Rusk**, **Perrin Mett**, **Haro Venn**, and any people transferred from Saint Mercy House or the gallows.

The wagon has four operational compartments.

1. **Driver box.** Garran's road key hangs beneath the seat. A speaking tube lets him hear the rear compartment.
2. **Visible cage.** One prisoner is displayed here to draw rescuers toward the strongest lock.
3. **False wall.** A polished panel conceals two narrow restraint bays and permits the Glass Nurse to enter.
4. **Floor chest.** Written names, custody papers, crow messages, and spare red cord are kept separately from the bodies.

Breaking the visible cage does not open the hidden bays. Covering the polished wall prevents the Glass Nurse from crossing and makes the panel's depth obvious. Removing the floor chest stops route redirection through prisoner names but may cause the wagon to lose the hidden road and overturn if driven recklessly.

Prisoners can contribute. Jo knows the cooperage knock and which turns repeat. Perrin knows the service tunnel grade. Haro knows which wheel was changed after a body run. A newly transferred captive brings information from the encounter they left.

## Familiar messages

Twenty-one ordinary messenger crows and the familiar **Quill** use Crow Wood. They are animals altered and trained by Judith, not a shared mind.

Thoughts do not travel by themselves. Each ordinary crow can carry:

- one folded message strip;
- one thimble-sized ash packet;
- or one narrow written-name tag.

It cannot carry all three safely. A captured crow repeats the last spoken command it heard, then tries to reach its next roost. The strip identifies sender, intended handoff, and urgency mark. Ash identifies a household by smell and iron label. A name tag can support one command or reflected route until covered.

Quill can mimic voices it has heard and carry two items. It cannot invent knowledge. Its bone tongue points toward the person or place named in Judith's last delivery command.

Destroying or dispersing the rookery delays messages and forces Judith to use servants, hearths, or mirrors. It does not retroactively erase what she already learned.

## The Glass Nurse in the wood

The Glass Nurse can enter only through an uncovered polished surface carrying a stolen or controlled name. In the wagon it appears as a familiar adult chosen from a prisoner's memory. It tries to obtain a voluntary response—following, answering to a false name, or opening a reflected door—because those acts make transport easier.

It cannot drag a whole wagon through glass. It can move one person at a time between the wagon panel, Mercy House, Moonless Mere, and Blackbriar Hall while corresponding mirrors remain uncovered.

Cover the surface, remove the written name, establish an unborrowed reflection, or sever the Child pact to end that route.

## Pursuit clock

Use the clock during an active transfer. Advance it for elapsed time, loud failure, or a deliberate Judith response. Every step creates new evidence or a new intercept point.

1. **Crows lift.** Three messages leave by separate roosts. Capturing one reveals the wagon's next intended junction.
2. **The wagon divides its trail.** Garran sends an empty decoy cart toward the orchard. Wheel width and the prisoners' knocks distinguish it.
3. **The Glass Nurse opens the false wall.** One captive is prepared for reflected transfer. Covering the panel traps the Nurse on whichever side it currently occupies.
4. **Blackthorn escort arrives.** Cleft sends four guards, at least one marked and one ordinary. Their route reveals the hall tunnel.
5. **Olla walks.** If the Worm remains intact, Olla Pike's body approaches from the orchard track. Her ankle bell and wounds show the White Pits' control.
6. **The gate closes.** The wagon reaches the undercroft. Its gate can still be forced or followed, but the Hall advances its response and the Feast receives any remaining captives.

A failed pursuit does not make the wagon vanish. Record its destination, prisoners, transferred papers, and the route now exposed.

## Operations in the wood

### Rescue the prisoners

Rescue requires stopping or boarding the wagon, opening each occupied compartment, separating written names and custody papers, and choosing destinations that do not simply return captives to Judith's routes. A fast rescue can precede complete evidence recovery. A careful rescue can preserve the floor chest, route key, and witness testimony.

### Capture the road

Control requires the iron key or a reliable landmark map, a covered service panel, disabled rookery signals, and watchers at the tunnel and Mercy junction. Cutting trees at random may block rescue carts without securing any route.

### Intercept messages

A message can be read, copied, altered, delayed, or followed. Feeding false information requires preserving the sender marks and knowing which channel Judith will trust. A false strip can draw a limited response—one wagon, servant, crow flight, or Judith inspection—not command her whole network.

### Turn Garran

Garran may be defeated, captured, killed, or induced to break with Judith. Evidence about Olla creates the possibility; a concrete alternative for her body and protection from Cleft makes action possible. Garran will not become a moral spokesman. Survivors decide what justice follows.

### Reach the Underhall road

The service tunnel can be entered by following the wagon, using Garran's key, tracking Corin's page marks, or navigating from fixed landmarks. Reaching it before the wagon permits an ambush, an opened gate, or a prisoner diversion toward the refuge, chapel, or village.

## Judith's bounded responses

Judith can affect Crow Wood only through remaining channels.

- With the Guest intact, she can emerge from the tunnel hearth or send inward fire through a marked wagon brazier.
- With the Worm intact, she can animate Olla and transfer injury into bodies on the orchard route.
- With the Child intact, she can use the Glass Nurse, false panels, and written-name commands.
- With crows available, she can learn what their strips, eyes, and return routes convey.
- With Garran loyal, she can alter convoy decisions. Without him, another driver needs the road key or a crow guide.

She cannot perceive every tree, move prisoners without transport, or rearrange fixed landmarks.

## Leaving Crow Wood

Record separately:

- prisoners rescued, moved, injured, or delivered;
- wagon compartments and evidence secured;
- Garran and Olla's states;
- ordinary and marked guards' decisions;
- messages intercepted, copied, altered, or delivered;
- crow roosts active;
- carriers removed from travelers;
- service, orchard, Mercy, and refuge routes mapped or controlled;
- Glass Nurse panels covered or active;
- and whether Blackbriar Hall was warned.

A strong result rescues the convoy, exposes three routes, captures the communication system, and opens the hall tunnel. A partial result may save people while losing the floor chest, follow a failed rescue into the hall, or break Garran while the crows still carry warning. The wood always converts movement into evidence and a contestable route.

## If Crow Wood is skipped or reached late

The pursuit clock moves prisoners and messages to specific destinations rather than removing them from play.

- A wagon that reaches the undercroft leaves tracks, gate witnesses, custody papers, and the occupants now recorded at Blackbriar Hall or in the Underhall.
- A reflected transfer names the prepared panel and destination. Covering that destination later can trap or redirect the route.
- Delivered crow messages change only what the strip and returning bird could report. Intercepted or delayed messages remain unavailable to Judith.
- Fixed streams, ridges, quarry cuts, road grades, roosts, dropped cargo, and the refuge boundary remain mappable after a convoy passes.
- Prisoners already rescued and routes already captured do not reappear under Judith's control because another transfer succeeds.

A late arrival may become pursuit, route reconstruction, message interception, or preparation for a returning convoy. The wood is never simply empty because the first wagon completed its run.
