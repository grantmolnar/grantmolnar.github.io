### Safe handling

1. Cover the mirror with dull, unpatterned cloth.
2. Do not read the written name aloud in a reflected room.
3. Keep the vessel with a free carrier and record its owner separately.
4. Preserve vessels for absent owners rather than declaring them rescued.
5. Return the name in an unborrowed reflection before breaking the vessel.

Covering blocks observation and coordination; it does not return the name. Early breakage harms the owner and does not sever the Child Behind Glass pact.

## Judith's public counterattack

Choose one concrete cruelty after Judith has made the practical burden visible:

- score a vessel until one child publicly recants;
- return a transferred illness to a paying patron in the crowd and blame the company;
- command a marked guardian to seize their own dependent;
- offer immediate food and beds only to residents who denounce the rescue;
- reveal a true private failure that divides one receiving household.

The counterattack should use a known channel, create a protectable victim, and expose Judith's ownership. It should not grant her unexplained knowledge or allow her to remove every captive and record offstage.
