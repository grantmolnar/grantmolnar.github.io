# Rehearsal and Witness Record: A Wedding for the River

This sheet runs the Flood Meadow rehearsal and the final Wedding Between the Banks. It does not prescribe the couple's settlement. It makes pressure, choices, boundaries, and certification visible.

## Rehearsal pressure board

Pressure advances only through a visible fictional cause. Announce the new state, the practical consequence, and at least one available response. No pressure can override consent.
