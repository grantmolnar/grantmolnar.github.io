# Aftermath and Seasonal States

The wedding closes a folio, not a life. Its signatures assign the next morning's keys, notices, tools, and duties; the first spawning closure, hard freeze, flood warning, serious illness, and next joining light reveal whether those terms can bear ordinary strain.

The demonstrated aftermath follows the canonical journal. The alternate states begin from other honest certification outcomes.

## Demonstrated immediate aftermath

### The folio at the House

Tomas and Neris sign one operational schedule beneath two household descriptions. The descriptions are printed at equal rank and neither is called a concession, provisional wording, or translation of the other.

The folio records:

- Tomas and Neris chose marriage separately and again together;
- the eastern rooms and willow house are both recognized dwellings;
- neither partner requires leave to answer an existing duty at the other dwelling;
- mill property, machinery, debt, wages, and succession remain under the Vale deed and account books;
- Neris holds domestic access and the office of water correspondent without receiving ownership or inherited debt;
- Tomas accepts six bounded river-adoption duties;
- the river household accepts six reciprocal warning, rescue, evidence, notice, bodily-limit, and exit duties;
- care is assigned to named people and tasks without default household custody;
- future-child standing is not decided;
- names remain asymmetric and append-only;
- gifts were accepted only in declared forms;
- Agnes and Serein abstained from different recognition lines; and
- the old flood produced a new warning protocol and two separate statements, not a joint settlement.

The veil wraps the closed folio overnight. The open iron spiral clasps the outer cord without encircling a person. Mara's handstone supports the water bowl on the House table until Tomas and Neris carry it to the eastern rooms.

### The feast

Pella serves the feast after the bounded grievance interval. The protected trout do not appear. Brin Quickwater tells the return story before anyone else can turn it into a joke at his expense, names the children and laborers who repaired the mistake, and receives no ceremonial absolution.

The broken-ring table form works. Deep-Reed can leave without asking a bench to move. Agnes receives written repetitions of the speeches she cannot hear. One guest remains by the fountain; another sleeps in Pella's upper room. The empty abstention token stays on the table until the meal ends.

After the difficult parts, conversation loosens. Approval does not become unanimous. Sella marks the widened route, written repetitions, source cards, and abstention tokens for ordinary market stock instead of letting them vanish as one-wedding favors.

### The first night and first morning

Tomas and Neris leave the feast by the neutral path, collect the signed folio, and choose privately where to sleep. The record does not require the first night to prove either household description.

At first light:

- Neris receives keys to the eastern domestic rooms and water-record chest;
- Mara keeps the deed chest, wage books, and mill-operating seals;
- Tomas and Rain-at-Noon test the daylight willow flags;
- Elsin and Jory Salt test the runner chain;
- Kest hangs paired hand bells at the mill and willow house;
- Agnes keeps the veil's custody record but not unilateral control over its future ceremonial use; and
- Orren enters the iron spiral as accepted work whose opening must remain visible if reused.

The Four Open Hands depart with copies limited to their witness scopes. Mera keeps the authoritative original.

## Seasonal tests of the demonstrated marriage

### First spawning closure

The first significant test is not romantic. Neris posts a closure that costs the mill two profitable days. Tomas honors it. Mara asks for the evidence and customer notice required by the compact. Neris provides both. The mill workers see that water authority is an office with procedures, not a spouse's private preference.

If the closure is poorly signaled, use the correction process rather than treating the marriage as failed. The relevant questions are whether the threshold was met, who received notice, and what loss follows from the error.

### First hard freeze

Tomas keeps the first freeze watch. Before the second, he asks Elsin to take part of it because a downstream warning duty will keep him away. Elsin agrees after he tells her directly rather than allowing Mara to assign her by implication. This is the first visible proof that her apprenticeship was preserved as a person's work rather than as a clause protecting an abstract successor.

Neris stays at the eastern rooms during the freeze and returns beneath the willows afterward. Tomas calls both movements ordinary household life. Neris calls them movement between homes. The schedule works without resolving the noun.

### First flood warning

The new system is tested in rain. Willow flags rise before dusk. The paired bells answer. Jory's market runner reaches the mill after dark with the third confirmation. Mara stops the wheel; Tomas opens the lower sluice; Elsin clears the grain carts; Rain-at-Noon relays downstream.

No single signal bears the whole handoff. Agnes and Serein are both absent. The protocol succeeds because named offices, not reconciled elders, hold it.

After the water falls, the House opens a narrow hearing. Agnes and Serein may attend, send statements, or abstain. The hearing evaluates the new protocol rather than retrying Perrin's death.

### First serious illness

When one spouse is unable to answer ordinary questions, the care compact becomes concrete. The named carer nearest the person provides immediate help and notifies the other spouse and household contacts. No household removes the patient merely by claiming custom. Any prior direction governs; absent that, care is coordinated through the spouse and named adults.

Test notice, access, and actual capacity. Introduce no hidden custody rule after the witnesses have already named the people and acts that govern care.

### Next joining light

At the next annual joining light, the couple may append changes without rewriting the original record. Possibilities include:

- a river byname Tomas has actually earned and freely accepts;
- a revised residence rhythm;
- an added care signatory;
- a succession clarification under the Vale deed;
- a public account of the first flood-warning test;
- a decision about whether the veil, spiral, or threshold boat should be reused; or
- no changes at all.

The House reads the original open clauses before adding new terms. Continuity does not require pretending the first record foresaw every later life.

## Relational aftermath

### Agnes and Neris

Agnes does not become immediately easy with river practice. Neris does not begin calling the veil beautiful. Their workable relationship begins with custody and use: Agnes teaches how the flax is stored; Neris records when the cloth may be displayed; both can refuse a future use.

### Serein and Tomas

Serein tests Tomas through warnings and hearings rather than symbolic obedience. Tomas answers well when the signal is exact and poorly when Serein assumes urgency excuses ambiguity. Their conflict becomes procedural enough to correct.

### Mara and Neris

Mara and Neris become the most effective pair because their jurisdictions are clear enough to disagree. Mara controls the work accounts. Neris controls water correspondence. Each needs the other's records during closures and repairs.

### Elsin and Rain-at-Noon

Elsin learns the three warning forms. Rain learns the mill's shift bell and customer-board system. Neither is appointed as a cultural representative; they are two named people holding a practical relay.

### The Four Open Hands

Adel adds a model for parallel descriptions to his traveling forms. Kest adds a rule that every ceremonial threshold needs a return path and a non-symbolic bypass. Sella keeps the seven-basket method and the visible abstention token. Oren writes no wedding song. He preserves the twelve-pulse joining measure and leaves the old-flood silence unfilled.

## Alternate certification states

### Cross-bank marriage certified without stated boundaries

Use this state only when both principals, both household speakers, and the witnesses accept one represented household form and no open matter limits certification. Do not reach it by deleting asymmetry from the record.

Immediate effects:

- both household recognition lines are signed;
- adoption and domestic standing are complete;
- the folio still preserves property, care, and name limits;
- unresolved emotional injuries may remain; and
- the first seasonal tests proceed as above.

### Marriage certified; river adoption deferred

Tomas and Neris marry, but the adoption duties are omitted or left for later hearing.

Immediate effects:

- water duties continue through the existing Concord lease and any separately accepted offices;
- Serein cannot treat marriage as adoption;
- the threshold boat may be omitted or used only as a mutual household threshold;
- later adoption requires another consent sequence and exact reciprocal obligations; and
- the marriage is not described as incomplete.

### One household withholds recognition

The couple may still marry under House witness if consent and represented functions are clear.

Immediate effects:

- the withholding household controls its own gift, office, property, labor, and signature;
- the record names the exact abstention;
- no absent signature is generalized into rejection by every member of that household;
- practical duties accepted by named individuals remain valid; and
- later recognition is appended rather than backdated.

### Ceremony witnessed without House certification

The couple completes a rite, but the witnesses cannot attest one or more represented functions.

Immediate effects:

- record what was observed, what remained unclear, and who declined certification;
- return or preserve gifts according to their declarations;
- do not enter unaccepted duties as though the ceremony created them;
- households and local institutions may recognize the relationship differently; and
- a later House hearing may certify new or clarified terms without pretending the earlier witness was complete.

### Voluntary postponement

Tomas or Neris chooses not to proceed under the current terms.

Immediate effects:

- Pella serves the prepared food as a gathering;
- perishable goods are used or distributed rather than treated as evidence of breach;
- gifts return to declared custodians unless separately accepted;
- the House seals the current draft and records privacy instructions;
- the joining light passes without creating automatic fault; and
- any future date requires a new repeated-choice check, not merely resumed logistics.

### Process ended without marriage

Either principal may end the commission.

Immediate effects:

- the House separates property, labor, gift, lodging, and hearing obligations from the ended marriage plan;
- households may grieve or become angry without gaining a claim over either person;
- accepted non-marital safety improvements, such as the warning protocol or widened market route, may remain if their named parties still consent; and
- the adventure closes without treating affection in the premise as a promise the principals were required to fulfill.

## Continuing adventure hooks

These are consequences of the marriage, not concealed threats behind it.

- A later mixed hearing must decide whether a new mill expansion changes water-compliance authority.
- Another cross-bank couple asks to copy Tomas and Neris's folio, including its parallel household descriptions.
- The first flood test exposes a weak downstream relay outside either household's control.
- Elsin's apprenticeship creates a succession question the marriage deliberately left under the Vale deed.
- A river child chooses temporary residence at the mill, testing named care without invoking default custody.
- Agnes lends the veil for a funeral, and Neris must decide whether household trust includes that use.
- Orren is asked to reproduce the open spiral and must decide whether a transformed wedding object has become a general river-safe symbol.
