# Encounter Index: A Wedding for the River

## The House of Open Measure (`house-of-open-measure`)

**Role:** necessary, start

Two identical compacts lie beneath two river stones. Their three sentences place Tomas and Neris in different homes, offices, names, and lines of care.

## Vale Mill and Hearth (`vale-mill-and-hearth`)

**Role:** necessary

At the mill, flour, wages, a water lease, a white veil, a handstone, and a child's chalked shift board force welcome into exact terms.

## The House Beneath the Willows (`house-beneath-the-willows`)

**Role:** necessary

Under the willow roots, Serein names the price of adoption, Orren opens the ring he forged, Neris defends two homes, and Rain asks for a warning that reaches a human hand.

## The Market of Seven Baskets (`market-of-seven-baskets`)

**Role:** necessary

Pella Nine-Spoons empties seven baskets onto the market tables until every claim about hospitality becomes a name, source, seat, bed, task, gift, abstention, or debt.

## The Doorless Shrine (`shrine-of-the-open-door`)

**Role:** necessary

Keeper Ansel clears the rite down to six acts, then tests every vow, name, exchange, and open clause in words Tomas and Neris can repeat.

## The Reedwright's Yard (`reedwrights-yard`)

**Role:** necessary

Dessa Reedwright prices the metaphors: safe footing, clean water return, visible stations, named labor, and a fallback ready before the rain decides for them.

## The Bridge of Three Tunes (`bridge-of-three-tunes`)

**Role:** necessary

On boards that magnify every missed step, Iven and Moss teach four and six to meet without making either tune background—and mark the silences no music may cross.

## The Flood Meadow and Old Ford (`flood-meadow-and-old-ford`)

**Role:** necessary

At the old ford, chairs, weather, work, guests, and the memory of Perrin's flood test whether the planned ceremony still says what the couple chose.

## The Wedding Between the Banks (`wedding-between-the-banks`)

**Role:** necessary, end

At joining light, Tomas and Neris choose, revise, postpone, or refuse each public act. The witnesses sign only the marriage, boundaries, abstentions, and open lines they saw.
