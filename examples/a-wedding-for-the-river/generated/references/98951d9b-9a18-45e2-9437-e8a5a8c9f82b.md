## Reference Material

## Household and office

Serein-at-Alderfall is Neris's mother and a senior current-keeper. She keeps current records and speaks for adoption within her office. Ask for a blessing and she speaks like water over stone; ask who must answer a black reed knot at midnight and every word acquires an edge.

## What she wants

Serein wants Tomas directly answerable to warnings, closures, evacuation, rescue, and hearing so river obligations no longer stop at the mill gate. She accepts that he may live mainly at the mill, remain Vale kin, retain his property and trade, and refuse danger beyond the duties he actually undertakes. Adoption that supplies only ceremonial praise is not adoption to her.

## Authority and limits

Serein may accept, condition, or refuse river adoption and reciprocal commitments belonging to her office. She cannot answer for Neris's marriage, transfer Vale property, erase Tomas's mortal kinship, impose unrestricted labor, or make the wedding settle the old flood. Her account of Perrin's death is true without being the only true account.
