# Tomas Vale

**Reference ID:** `84016966-e56a-4bc9-a1a5-ddf8dfab7c7d`  
**Kind:** Person  
**Aliases:** Tomas, the miller  
**Tags:** couple, mill, consent, river adoption

## Summary

A practical miller who wants to marry Neris, keep his Vale belonging, and accept only river duties precise enough to survive an ordinary Tuesday.

## Reference Material

## Work and manner

Tomas Vale is thirty, broad through the shoulders, patient with machinery, and slow to notice when other people have mistaken his silence for consent. He runs the Cresswater mill with his widowed sister Mara. He promises gates opened, rooms repaired, and shifts covered; ask for a declaration and he asks which ordinary day must survive it.

## What he wants

Tomas wants to marry Neris and make the mill's eastern rooms their ordinary shared dwelling. He expects river work to call either partner elsewhere, but initially describes the willow house as another beloved home rather than a second continuing household. He will become river kin when the warning, evacuation, closure, rescue, and hearing duties are exact. He will not trade one belonging for another.

## Authority and limits

Tomas may consent to the marriage, accept personal river duties, and agree to changes in his own work and residence. He cannot answer for Neris, confer Vale property or succession by himself, bind Mara or the mill workers to unnamed labor, or turn marriage into authority over river work. He remains Tomas Vale unless he later earns and accepts a river byname.
