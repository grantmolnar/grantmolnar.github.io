# Mara Vale

**Reference ID:** `ae5f5abd-2082-4b3d-8b75-fe3d0d03a50c`  
**Kind:** Person  
**Aliases:** Mara, the mill operator  
**Tags:** Vale household, mill operations, labor, property boundaries

## Summary

Tomas’s widowed sister and co-operator of the Cresswater mill, generous toward Neris but exact about which welcome, labor, property, care, and succession promises she has authority to make.

## Reference Material

## Work and manner

Mara Vale is thirty-four, widowed, and responsible for the mill’s accounts, purchases, wages, repairs, tenant quarrels, and the work Tomas volunteers himself to do twice. She judges flour by touch, reads lateness in the knots on grain sacks, and distrusts any beautiful sentence that hides an unnamed shift or liability.

## What she offers

Mara welcomes Neris as a true resident of the eastern rooms, with keys, domestic authority, household purchasing, a worker-council seat, and a water-compliance role. She recognizes the willow house as another continuing dwelling. She can assign mill hands within safe limits, help implement the paired warning protocol, and revise the handstone’s public use with Neris.

## Authority and limits

Mara may bind her own labor and the mill operations entrusted to her. She may not transfer Tomas’s property, the Concord water lease, worker wages, debt, Elsin’s future, or the whole mill through a wedding clause. Care duties must name the people accepting them. Her caution is not hostility to the river household; it is refusal to let welcome become an unpriced claim on workers, tenants, or a child.
