# Neris Reed-in-Rain

**Reference ID:** `43e8bf1a-b173-4faf-b6fc-28ba2ed78d8f`

## Summary

A lower-channel current-keeper who wants to marry Tomas without surrendering her river work, willow-house standing, changing names, or right to object.

## Reference Material

## Work and manner

Neris Reed-in-Rain keeps spawning records, negotiates closures, and carries warnings along the lower channels. She is river-born, not immortal, and not the Cresswater speaking through one mouth. She hears consequences quickly, though beautiful language can make her wait too long before objecting.

## What she wants

Neris wants to marry Tomas and share the mill's eastern rooms while retaining the House Beneath the Willows as a home whose duties may call her without permission. She wants both continuing households recognized rather than one home treated as real and the other as an absence. She will not become the sole translator, symbolic bridge, or living water lease between them.

## Identity, authority, and limits

Neris presently answers to Reed-in-Rain. Her stable witness identity also preserves her parentage, earlier child-name, and dwellings; later bynames append rather than erase. She may consent to marriage, residence, personal promises, and her own use of names and gifts. She cannot confer river-household adoption, speak for every river guest, transfer the mill, or commit another person's care, labor, or grievance.
