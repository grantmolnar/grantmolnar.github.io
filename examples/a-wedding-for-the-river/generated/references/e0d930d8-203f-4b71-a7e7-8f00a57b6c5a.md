## Summary

The House of Open Measure recorder who preserves the paired filings and drafts the folio but cannot replace an independent witness or answer for a principal.

## Reference Material

## Office

Mera Quill is the resident recorder of the House of Open Measure. She received the two sealed household readings, opens them together, preserves the paired record, advises on form, and drafts the final folio. Because she kept the filings and will write the instrument, she cannot also serve as the independent field witness who certifies it.

## Working practice

Mera separates observed, proposed, accepted, contested, refused, and unasked matters. She records people and offices separately, protects private consent checks, preserves different signature scopes, and can schedule a later hearing for a clause that does not belong inside the wedding.

## Authority and limits

Mera may explain House procedure, maintain registers, test whether wording is operable, and refuse to enter knowingly incompatible or unchosen meanings as one certifiable term. She cannot choose the marriage, fill a pause, accept for an absent principal, turn family assent into personal consent, or make her own observation stand in for the free witnesses'.
