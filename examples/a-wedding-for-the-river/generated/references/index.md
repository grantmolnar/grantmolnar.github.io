# Reference Index: A Wedding for the River

## People

- [**Tomas Vale**](84016966-e56a-4bc9-a1a5-ddf8dfab7c7d.md) — A practical miller who wants to marry Neris, keep his Vale belonging, and accept only river duties precise enough to survive an ordinary Tuesday. Aliases: Tomas, the miller.
- [**Neris Reed-in-Rain**](43e8bf1a-b173-4faf-b6fc-28ba2ed78d8f.md) — A lower-channel current-keeper who wants to marry Tomas without surrendering her river work, willow-house standing, changing names, or right to object. Aliases: Neris, Reed-in-Rain, Small-Reed-After-Thunder, Neris of Vale Mill.
- [**Mera Quill**](e0d930d8-203f-4b71-a7e7-8f00a57b6c5a.md) — The House of Open Measure recorder who preserves the paired filings and drafts the folio but cannot replace an independent witness or answer for a principal. Aliases: Mera, resident recorder.
- [**Agnes Vale**](668c5a2b-02d0-4e03-a2e8-7422583a443a.md) — Tomas’s grandmother, keeper of the white veil, and a principal bearer of Vale memory from the flood that killed Perrin. Aliases: Agnes, Tomas's grandmother.
- [**Serein-at-Alderfall**](98951d9b-9a18-45e2-9437-e8a5a8c9f82b.md) — Neris’s mother and a senior current-keeper whose office can confer river adoption only through exact, reciprocal, bounded duties. Aliases: Serein, Neris's mother, senior current-keeper.
- [**Mara Vale**](ae5f5abd-2082-4b3d-8b75-fe3d0d03a50c.md) — Tomas’s widowed sister and co-operator of the Cresswater mill, generous toward Neris but exact about which welcome, labor, property, care, and succession promises she has authority to make. Aliases: Mara, the mill operator.
- [**Orren Underbridge**](8fb5b0b1-2389-44ab-86f3-7f334e0d1771.md) — Neris’s elder brother, a repairer of gates and hinges whose six nights of worked iron must be acknowledged and corrected without turning care into confinement. Aliases: Orren, Neris’s elder brother.
- [**Rain-at-Noon**](17c2b683-f309-4687-90a9-65438f70f0e2.md) — Serein’s younger sister, a dry-witted warning carrier and grievance steward who keeps the cracked mill bell as proof that a sign sent is not yet a sign received. Aliases: Rain, warning carrier.

## Places

- [**Vale Mill and Hearth**](7110bb5a-b845-4e64-a43b-2536a05069c4.md) — A working Cresswater mill, multigenerational Vale home, and leased water operation where every wedding promise must survive workers, tenants, debt, flood signals, and an ordinary week. Aliases: Cresswater mill, the Vale mill, the mill, Vale hearth.
- [**The House Beneath the Willows**](d635958c-fcff-4993-a528-e05f6f4fc0d5.md) — A river household beneath a broad willow reach: Neris’s continuing home, Serein’s adoption office, and the place where kinship becomes named warning, rescue, care, grievance, and hearing duties. Aliases: the willow house, the willows, the river household, willow-house home.

## Organizations

- [**The House of Open Measure**](6f8bbb6f-b719-491e-80b0-b276fc770d08.md) — The Concord of Banks witness office that records where unlike households touch, preserving consent, authority, refusal, limits, and endings without reconciling the households or deciding for them. Aliases: the House, Open Measure, the witness office.
