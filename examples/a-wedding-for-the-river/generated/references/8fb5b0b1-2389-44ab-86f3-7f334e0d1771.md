# Orren Underbridge

**Reference ID:** `8fb5b0b1-2389-44ab-86f3-7f334e0d1771`

**Aliases:** Orren, Neris’s elder brother  
**Tags:** river household, craft, worked iron, gift boundaries

## Summary

Neris’s elder brother, a repairer of gates and hinges whose six nights of worked iron must be acknowledged and corrected without turning care into confinement.

## Reference Material

## Craft and manner

Orren Underbridge repairs fish gates, bridge shoes, and water hinges. He speaks in conclusions and becomes clumsy when a sentence approaches tenderness. He trusts labor that leaves its joints visible and worries that the mortal bank will praise river kin at the wedding, then curse the first closure that costs flour.

## The worked-iron gift

Orren forged a closed ring from a flood-gate fitting as an attempt to learn a mortal wedding form for his sister. Among his own kin, the closed circle can mean containment, debt without outlet, and a promise unable to breathe. Neris will not wear it. Orren can open it into a spiral, divide it into clasps, leave a rush-bound gap, place it in a threshold, or withhold it. He will not melt away the six nights of mistaken care merely to make the error disappear.

## Authority and limits

Orren controls his craft, his gift, and the labor he personally accepts. He can lead platform lifting, bridge pegging, rescue, and craft teaching. He cannot answer for Neris, confer river adoption, assign the wider household, or make his gift necessary to the marriage. Correction must preserve both the receiver’s refusal and the maker’s truthful labor.
