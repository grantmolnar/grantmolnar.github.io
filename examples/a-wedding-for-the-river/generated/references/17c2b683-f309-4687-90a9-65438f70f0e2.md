## Summary

## Work and manner

Rain-at-Noon carries warnings, verifies river source marks, and speaks with jokes dry enough that mortals sometimes wait for a punch line that has already passed. During the old flood she carried river signs toward the mill and came away with a cracked yard bell. She keeps it because warning is a completed handoff only when somebody receives, understands, acknowledges, and acts.

## Grievances and the old flood

Rain can steward a bounded grievance: speaker, respondent, unfinished duty, next act, date, and later hearing, without a judgment of character. She will keep Perrin Vale’s death out of a wedding feast interval, accept stewards, and send it to a separate hearing. She will not say no warning was sent, assign the death wholly to the Vales, or perform reconciliation for ceremonial neatness.

## Authority and limits

Rain may carry and translate warnings, verify intake and return points, teach warning lore, operate the paired protocol, and accept or decline a public grievance entry. She cannot confer adoption, speak for Neris or Serein, promise regular care, or turn the cracked bell into a verdict. Her preferred settlement is practical: paired river and mill measures, named keepers on both banks, acknowledgment, escalation, and a rule for absence.
