## Summary

## Reference Material

## Beneath the willows

The House Beneath the Willows occupies a broad root-bound reach open to current and air. Stepping stones and low platforms join sleeping shelves, reed pens, mussel baskets, silt pigments, and ledgers weighted beneath glass. It is a household, not a court. Neris belongs here as daughter, sister, current-keeper, and principal rather than as an envoy between banks.

## Duties that travel

Serein speaks for adoption within her office; Orren repairs gates and hinges; Rain carries warnings and stewards bounded grievances. Adoption can add exact warning, closure, evacuation, rescue, and mixed-hearing duties without requiring permanent residence or erasing mortal kinship. The household owes reciprocal signals, current readings, aid, recovery, review, and honest thresholds.

## What the current cannot claim

The willow house remains Neris’s continuing dwelling after marriage and may receive Tomas under its rules. Care may occur here by choice or necessity without becoming custody. The household cannot claim mill property, Mara, workers, future children, unrestricted labor, or Neris’s personal consent. Its gifts, adoption, testimony, and recognition may each be offered, revised, deferred, or withheld by the person who holds that authority.
