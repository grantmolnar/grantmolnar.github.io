# Agnes Vale

**Reference ID:** `668c5a2b-02d0-4e03-a2e8-7422583a443a`  
**Kind:** Person  
**Aliases:** Agnes, Tomas's grandmother  
**Tags:** Vale household, veil, Perrin Vale, old flood

## Summary

Tomas’s grandmother, keeper of the white veil, and a principal bearer of Vale memory from the flood that killed Perrin.

## Reference Material

## Place in the household

Agnes Vale is Tomas's grandmother. She wants Neris welcomed into the Vale sequence without Tomas, Perrin, or the household dead being politely erased. The white flax veil is both her own wedding heirloom and the cloth later laid over her drowned husband.

## The veil and the flood

Agnes offers continuity, not conquest, but fear can make her speak as though the gift already has an accepted office. She remembers Perrin remaining at the wheel after the river warning came too late to become a received command. Her memory is true and incomplete; it need not become Serein's memory before the warning system can be repaired.

## Authority and limits

Agnes may offer, transform, keep private, or withhold the veil. She may give testimony, attend, abstain, or state the Vale remembrance she accepts. She has no right to Neris's face, name, residence, burial, work, or consent, and she cannot make Perrin's grief a condition of the marriage.
