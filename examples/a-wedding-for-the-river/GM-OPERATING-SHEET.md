# GM Operating Sheet: A Wedding for the River

Use this sheet at the table. The ledgers contain detail; this page keeps authority, route state, and the finale legible.

## Adventure in one sentence

Neutral free witnesses have three evenings to discover what Tomas Vale and Neris Reed-in-Rain are actually agreeing to, help them make six public functions legible, and sign only what the witnesses truly observe.

## Never collapse these four structures

| Structure | What it answers |
|---|---|
| Calendar | What closes, begins, or arrives next? |
| Clue graph | What useful conclusion has become available or corroborated? |
| Authority | Who may accept, refuse, lend, alter, bind, or certify this exact matter? |
| Ceremony state | What has actually been accepted and can appear at joining light? |

All six preparation sites are physically available after the opening House scene. The rehearsal and wedding are scheduled events. Clues open procedures and understanding; they are not locked doors.

## Three-evening calendar

### First evening

- Open at the House of Open Measure.
- Conduct separate desire and terms questions.
- Make both households and all four preparation workshops available.
- Market food reservations and scarce labor begin closing.

### Second day and evening

- Continue household and preparation work in any order.
- Confirm named guests, source food, site, fallback, and six-function plan.
- Return to the House only when privacy, comparison, or revised drafting is useful.

### Third day

- Finish construction and final sourcing.
- Hold the rehearsal or a shortened site walk.
- Repeat consent after the couple has seen what others intend to perform.
- At joining light, play the six functions and complete the scoped record.

A postponed wedding costs deposits, travel, food, joining light, and expectation. It does not cost a life or trigger a supernatural disaster.

## Record every matter in one state

- **Observed:** the witnesses saw or heard it.
- **Proposed:** somebody offered it; nobody should treat it as accepted yet.
- **Accepted:** the person with authority accepted the exact term.
- **Conditional:** accepted only with a named limit, substitute, steward, or later hearing.
- **Refused:** the authority holder withheld what they control.
- **Contested:** relevant speakers give incompatible accounts or authority remains disputed.
- **Unasked:** nobody has obtained an answer.

When split groups reunite, each reports: **observed, proposed, accepted, contested**.

## The six certifiable functions

| Function | Minimum visible evidence | Common false substitute |
|---|---|---|
| Consent | Tomas and Neris each choose privately and publicly after correction remains possible | Family applause, compelled truth, or lack of objection |
| Household standing | Each accepted standing and retained boundary is spoken by an authorized person | One family receiving the other, a prop, or seating order |
| Names | Current names, stable identity, and accepted records are stated and corrected | Matching surnames or an invented river byname |
| Exchange | Giver declares intent; receiver accepts, transforms, privatizes, defers, or refuses | Object merely appearing in the ceremony |
| Promises | Each principal can paraphrase continuing, changed, interrupted, and released duties | Poetic beauty or identical wording |
| Departure | Tomas and Neris choose a shared first movement after the other functions | Music, boat, crowd, or procession carrying them away |

A shortened ceremony may omit procession, dance, decoration, public grievances, and most gifts while preserving all six functions.

## Authority keys

### Only Tomas and Neris may decide

- whether they marry;
- their own names and personal promises;
- duties they personally possess authority to accept;
- whether to pause, revise, proceed with boundaries, or postpone.

### House of Open Measure may decide

- private procedure;
- record form;
- whether a witness can honestly certify a stated scope.

It may not choose the marriage or force a household compact.

### Other NPCs may decide only their own sphere

- Agnes: veil, attendance, testimony, personal recognition.
- Mara: mill operations, worker safety, office and property within her authority, accepted care duties.
- Serein: river adoption and reciprocal river-household duties within her office.
- Orren: ring, craft labor, participation, personal duties.
- Pella: market promises, food source, labor, feast timing.
- Ansel: shrine officiation and archive wording.
- Dessa: structural safety and craft labor.
- Iven and Moss: music they will lead and cues they can honestly perform.
- Every guest: their own attendance, food, lodging, grievance, gift, dance, silence, and abstention.

No one controls the whole wedding.

## Split-party procedure

1. Mark where Tomas, Neris, and every traveling NPC actually are.
2. State what each group knows before resolving its scene.
3. Let groups observe, ask, reserve, draft, and carry offers.
4. Do not let one group accept for an absent principal.
5. Resolve time-sensitive actions in actual order.
6. On reunion, update the common ledger using the seven states above.
7. A named NPC cannot appear in two simultaneous scenes without time to travel.

A witness signs only what they directly observed or later verified through explicit repetition. Different witness scopes are allowed.

## Social-roll rule

Set the stake before rolling.

A success may gain candor, speed, trust, a better substitute, or a concrete commitment. A failure may cost time, trust, scale, labor, recognition, or privacy.

A roll never creates consent, property authority, household unanimity, or metaphysical certainty.

On failure, choose one:

- the speaker gives a clear refusal;
- the speaker gives a conditional answer;
- the answer requires a named private setting or steward;
- the proposal remains unclear and must not enter the compact;
- a reversible preparation is lost or reduced.

Always reveal the boundary.

## Skipped-encounter fallbacks

| Skipped encounter | Use this fallback |
|---|---|
| Vale Mill | No automatic veil, Vale standing, or mill authority. Ask Tomas what he personally controls; leave Mara’s and Agnes’s spheres open. |
| Willow House | No river adoption, household-wide river duties, or automatic ring. Neris may marry while retaining her existing standing. |
| Market | Serve only sourced confirmed food; reduce guests; withhold undeclared gifts; omit public grievance. |
| Shrine | Use six plain functions and ordinary-language promises; no prop completes a function by itself. |
| Yard | Use a known safe fixed site; omit boat, moving rill, and late structural improvisation. |
| Bridge | Use spoken cues, silence, optional accompaniment, and no compulsory movement. |
| Rehearsal | Conduct a short site walk and full private repetition; leave the old flood open; certify more narrowly. |

## Late-discovery triage

Ask what the fact changes.

- **Consent or authority:** pause the affected function; repeat the relevant private question; return the term to the actual authority holder.
- **Safety or access:** adapt before continuing; omit or relocate the unsafe element.
- **Gift, hospitality, or performance meaning:** declare, transform, privatize, defer, refuse, or omit.
- **Historical corroboration:** append it to the record or a later hearing; do not undo unrelated acts.

Reopen the smallest scope. Never spring an unknown taboo that retroactively ruins a reasonable choice.

## One-sided household outcomes

### Vale cooperates; river household does not

- Personal marriage: possible.
- Vale recognition: possible within actual authority.
- River adoption: unavailable.
- Reciprocal river duties: unavailable.
- Neris retains existing river standing.
- Record the later hearing or explicit absence of adoption.

### River household cooperates; Vale does not

- Personal marriage: possible.
- River adoption: possible if Tomas and authorized river officers accept exact terms.
- Vale standing or mill transfer: unavailable.
- Neris gains no mill office, debt, property, or succession by implication.

### Both households refuse

- Personal ceremony: possible if Tomas and Neris freely proceed.
- Cross-bank household certification: unavailable.
- House record: ceremony observed, boundaries named, certification withheld or narrowed.

Internal division is normal. Record Agnes, Mara, Serein, Orren, and others separately.

## Pressure board

Track only visible causes.

| Pressure | Advance when | What it threatens first |
|---|---|---|
| Water | Current crosses a marked threshold or weather changes observed conditions | Site, access, rill, boat, route |
| Work | A named task loses labor, material, or time | Scale, decoration, movable structures |
| Guests | A named person’s participation, access, lodging, or attendance changes | Seating, witnessing, feast, music |
| Open obligations | A consent-bearing or authority-bearing term remains unresolved | Specific function or certification scope |

Pressure does not override consent. Let players trade scale, ornament, route, labor, public participation, or certification breadth for safety and clarity.

## Rehearsal minimum

When time is short, test these in order:

1. Say the six functions without props.
2. Put Tomas and Neris through the actual wording and correction points.
3. Test Deep-Reed’s hearing, Agnes’s route, wet/dry footing, service access, and relocation.
4. State the old-flood outcome: protocol, later hearing, separate truths, or open matter.
5. Question Tomas and Neris separately again.

If rehearsal is skipped, repeat steps 2, 3, and 5 immediately before seating guests.

## Wedding procedure

1. Announce actual water, work, guest, and obligation state.
2. Conduct the last private repetition.
3. Set primary or fallback site.
4. Play all six functions; pause only the affected function when correction is needed.
5. Make abstentions, withheld gifts, and absent household recognition visible without turning them into disruption.
6. Begin the feast at the fixed cue; use the bounded grievance interval only if it was prepared.
7. Complete the witness folio in exact scopes.

## Certification choices

- **Cross-bank marriage certified:** both personal marriage and accepted household boundary compact are observed.
- **Marriage certified with stated boundaries:** marriage is clear; one or more duties, offices, recognitions, or clauses remain explicitly limited.
- **Marriage certified; river adoption deferred:** marriage is clear; adoption lacks consent or authority.
- **Ceremony witnessed without House certification:** the couple proceeds, but witnesses cannot attest the required institutional scope.
- **Voluntary postponement recorded:** either principal freely requests delay.
- **Process ended without marriage:** one or both principals no longer choose it.
- **Different witness scopes:** individual witnesses sign only what they observed and can support.

Never write “both families agreed.” Name every person, office, gift, duty, abstention, and open matter that carries weight.

## Encounter prompts

### House of Open Measure

- What do Tomas and Neris each think they are choosing?
- Which shared words conceal different consequences?
- Who actually has authority over each proposed term?

### Vale Mill and Hearth

- What belongs to marriage, household recognition, property, operation, debt, succession, or personal care?
- What is Agnes trying to give through the veil?
- What can Tomas promise without speaking for Mara or the deed?

### House Beneath the Willows

- What exact duties make adoption real?
- What does the river household owe in return?
- What does Neris retain even if no adoption occurs?

### Market of Seven Baskets

- Which named guest needs what?
- Who sourced, paid for, prepared, and can safely serve each course?
- What does each gift declare before it enters the ceremony?

### Shrine of the Open Door

- What will an observer actually see for each function?
- Can each principal paraphrase the promises in ordinary language?
- Which names and boundaries must remain asymmetrical?

### Reedwright’s Yard

- Is every body, object, and route safe at the actual waterline?
- What survives relocation?
- Which device can be omitted without losing a function?

### Bridge of Three Tunes

- Who leads, who answers, and where do the traditions genuinely meet?
- Where must music stop?
- Who may sit, remain silent, or abstain?

### Flood Meadow and Old Ford

- What fails when props are removed?
- What changes after the couple sees the real ceremony?
- What practical handoff can improve without forcing grief into reconciliation?

### Wedding Between the Banks

- What is accepted now?
- What changed late, and what is the smallest affected scope?
- What can each witness honestly sign?
