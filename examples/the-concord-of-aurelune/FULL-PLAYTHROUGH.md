# Full Playthrough: The Grey Lantern Delegation


> **Demonstration status:** This is one worked route through the adventure, not a specification for the player characters or the preferred coalition. `adventure.json` governs fresh play. When source and sample diverge, update the sample.

## Fixed route

```text
Canopy -> Ilyrion -> Maelith -> Vaudren -> Namaris -> Thalan -> Serevin
       -> Serathiel -> Orelle -> Masque -> Namaris -> Petition -> Thalan -> Conclave
```

The delegation wins exactly seven seals, all from the seasonal houses:

```text
Maelith + Serathiel + Vaudren + Orelle + Namaris + Thalan + Serevin
```

Ilyrion supports the departure and refuses the status bargain. House Eiral stays outside the coalition. The Ashen Bough remains vacant. Theron certifies in office, and Caelir joins only after seven seals have made refusal unlawful.

## Court Day One: The noun beneath the Canopy

### The Argent Canopy

The Grey Lantern Delegation enters on the morning after the Pall completes its outer ring around Orison. Mara Venn carries the council's emergency mandate. Tamsin Rook carries a second instrument naming herself as a possible custodian of the Sunseed. Neither page uses the word *embassy*. The council did not know whether Aurelune would recognize Orison as the sort of state entitled to send one.

Nara places a black-frosted paving stone from Orison's noon market beside the glass root. Under the Sunseed it warms and casts the hard-edged shadow it had before the sky failed. The Dawnward keepers have reproduced the test. No one in the throne hall disputes the remedy or the deadline: the root-casket must enter the Crown Passage before moonrise after Court Day Seven, or the Pall will close the Lantern Road and feed itself.

Caelir refuses the undertaking as proposed, then opens gates, healers, and transport enough for perhaps one person in three. He also orders his officers to prepare a six-day Crown expedition if command, passage, and replacement defense can be made executable without joint custody.

Tamsin asks whether the offer is conditioned on abandoning the petition.

Caelir answers, "I have imposed no such condition."

Lethiel asks whether the king will continue to describe evacuation as his plan if the delegation accepts the gates and continues to seek the Sunseed.

"It will remain my plan," Caelir says.

They accept the gates. Mara writes beneath the offer: **temporary relief; no settlement of the Sunseed petition**. Caelir permits the annotation and never adopts it.

The first consequential choice is standing. The Lantern Road compact guarantees an emergency audience and passage, not equality. Tamsin nevertheless asks the Chamberlain to record Orison as a sovereign treaty partner petitioning for joint custody. Theron Eiral answers that the court recognizes the council's mandate and the delegation's authority to petition. He does not say that Aurelune already recognizes Orison as a treaty equal. The noun entered in the calendar is **petitioning polity**.

Tamsin opens her mouth. Nara lays two fingers on the emergency mandate before the objection becomes the business of the morning. They accept **petitioning polity** for the calendar and enter the public purpose as **the restoration of natural day to Orison without transfer of its civil government**.

The delegation does not request an Ashen Bough hearing. Aven Tal's claim is attractive to Tamsin and Lethiel. Mirath's regency would help Garran. Both doctrines would outlive the crisis, and neither vote is necessary if seven seasonal banners can be assembled. Tamsin states the choice publicly: the delegation will not decide a succession merely to obtain an emergency seal.

The Chamberlain calendars the Masque, a drafting session on Court Day Six, and the Crown Conclave on Court Day Seven. He confirms that his official certification cannot be withheld from a valid text even if House Eiral never seals it.

Before the delegation leaves, each seasonal house gives enough of its position to make an approach possible. The delegation does not mistake those statements for support. Garran writes the first rule in the delegation's ledger:

> A route to a seal is not a seal.

### The Unfurling Branch

Tamsin goes directly to Ilyrion. She wants the first public declaration to establish that Orison is not being rescued into dependency.

Lady Saelira offers more than sympathy. She will seal if Orison is named a treaty partner, Mara becomes a genuine co-custodian, and no Eladrin officer can redirect the undertaking without mortal consent. She also offers a separate recognition compact if the central petition remains narrow.

Nara asks whether Ilyrion will guarantee Orison's share of the liability while insisting on sovereign status.

Saelira says that sovereign partners may owe debts. She will not accept a debt structured as tribute or a trade privilege structured as tutelage. Rethan Sol produces a guarantee through which Ilyrion and Orison would share verified losses without granting Orelle control over reconstruction.

Garran asks whether Mara's refusal right would apply to route changes made under attack.

Saelira answers that no free people should be governed without consent.

Lethiel asks the narrower question: may an expedition commander alter route and tactics within the named undertaking without waiting for Mara's consent?

Saelira says yes. The refusal right concerns redirection of the undertaking and use against Orison's government, not ordinary command.

The meeting yields a workable recognition package. It does not yield a declaration. Saelira wants the delegation to make equal status the organizing principle of the whole coalition. Tamsin wants the same. Nara and Garran are unwilling to promise it before the security and liability houses have stated what they will require.

The delegation leaves with Ilyrion as a possible Spring seal and with the recognition compact available as a side instrument. It also leaves with a warning: if the final petition makes Orison merely a protected participant beneath Crown and military control, Saelira will support the rescue and oppose the settlement.

Saelira keeps that promise. She never lets support for departure be mistaken for approval of the settlement.

## Court Day Two: Persons, precedence, and the price of being seen

### The Gallery of Silver Kin

Prince-Regent Othalan Maelith receives the delegation beside portraits of fostered heirs who later ruled allied realms. Every portrait records honors, education, marriage, and safe return. None records whether the guest could have left before Maelith considered the bond discharged.

Othalan's preferred surety is Mara herself. She would reside in Aurelune until the Sunseed returned. She would retain title, servants, correspondence, and personal safety. She would not be free to depart without ending joint custody.

Tamsin asks whether Mara may leave at any time without forfeiting rights or the continued loan.

Othalan says no.

The word is small enough to fit under every painted smile in the Gallery. Hospitality is no longer available as a name for the bargain.

Maelith does not insist on fosterage. The house needs named persons whose obligations survive the crisis. Nara and Othalan work through a personal bond secured against named estates. Tamsin can answer for her custody. Lady Senra Maelith can answer for the royal guard and the Crown's return obligations. The bond is reciprocal: if either custodian obstructs return, exceeds the undertaking, or abandons required protection, the other side receives enforceable claims.

Mara remains outside the personal bond. Her authority concerns Orison's civil consent, which neither Tamsin nor Lady Senra may pledge away.

Othalan will accept this structure if the final petition retains a royal custodian and if Ilyrion does not use the instrument to declare all forms of Eladrin wardship illegitimate. Tamsin refuses the second condition as stated. Namaris can later keep the emergency petition from becoming a general doctrine without endorsing wardship.

The other obstacle is Vaudren. Othalan says he has never denied the Red Rose the honor due it. Lethiel asks whether he will acknowledge that placing Lady Senra before Lord Cael at the last equinox publicly diminished Vaudren's standing, regardless of the genealogical rule he believed correct.

Othalan says that it did diminish their standing.

He will say the same beneath witnesses. He will not apologize for applying the order he believed lawful. The distinction is enough to continue.

Maelith gives a conditional declaration: reciprocal personal surety, preserved royal custody, and no general anti-wardship doctrine can secure the Spring seal. The declaration is not yet public.

### The Red Rose Pavilion

The pavilion servants have prepared the entrance order for three possibilities. Lethiel asks for the version in which neither Maelith nor any sponsor precedes the delegation. The delegation enters alone.

Lord Avar Vaudren does not demand that Maelith confess dishonor. He demands a public record that the slight occurred and that the coming undertaking is not Maelith's act under which lesser houses assemble.

Lethiel proposes four parts:

1. Othalan acknowledges that the equinox order diminished Vaudren's public standing.
2. Vaudren acknowledges that Maelith's genealogical position remains disputed rather than adjudicated.
3. Neither house receives sole authorship of the Sunseed petition.
4. Vaudren receives responsibility for the public proclamation and return ceremony, subject to the exact instrument rather than personal embellishment.

Avar asks whether responsibility means authority to name the undertaking.

Lethiel answers no. The petition names itself. Vaudren gives it voice.

Avar says that a herald who cannot alter the words may still determine which words a realm remembers. He accepts the office.

The house will declare at the Masque if Maelith gives the acknowledgment beneath the oath-mirrors. It will seal a petition titled in neutral or shared terms. It will withdraw if the final text is presented as a Maelith initiative or exclusive royal gift.

Tamsin calls the office ornamental. Lethiel points to the pavilion's three prepared entrances and asks which one is ornament. She watches servants move a carpet six inches so no house appears to follow another. “I understand,” she says. She does not say she respects it.

## Court Day Three: A rule with descendants

### The Amber Quill Archive

Archivist-Lord Pelion Namaris receives the delegation in a reading room where every quotation appears with an illuminated path to later cases. The device is not charitable. It makes selective citation visible to anyone who asks for the path.

Pelion reads the current language once and marks four places where a hostile reader would smile.

First, **restoration of natural day** has no completion test. The Sunseed could remain in Orison while experts disagree whether ordinary dawn has returned.

Second, **joint custody** names no physical custodian, no use authority, and no procedure when custodians disagree.

Third, Mara's refusal right could be read as a veto over every military decision because the undertaking has not been separated from its execution.

Fourth, the personal bond says that Tamsin answers for return but does not define what act constitutes return.

Nara asks for the later cases limiting the precedent Pelion first cites. He produces them without protest. The narrowest model keeps Crown title, grants physical custody to named persons, gives operational command to one office, lets a mortal representative refuse a change of undertaking, and defines return through replanting, inspection, and resumption of the Dawnward.

Namaris will seal if the final instrument contains exact custody, audit, inspection, return, and precedent language. The house also accepts side covenants for surety, credit, finance, and recognition, provided they are indexed and do not contradict the Crown instrument.

The archive gives Tamsin a way to preserve Ilyrion's recognition compact without making the relic petition a general constitutional declaration. It also gives Thalan a likely route: a necessity finding, a relic-specific sunset, and later review.

Pelion warns that a material amendment releases every seal tied to the changed article. It does not release unrelated seals merely because the coalition is embarrassed. Reaffirmation can occur in the same sitting if the exact corrected text is available.

The delegation asks the archive to begin an adverse index: every precedent that cuts against the petition will be listed beside the authorities supporting it. Namaris gives a private declaration contingent on the finished language.

### The Hall of First Frost

Duchess Eirath Thalan does not argue that Orison's destruction is acceptable. She argues that a foreign catastrophe does not automatically become constitutional necessity within Aurelune.

Tamsin presents the permanent planar alteration measurements. Nara demonstrates that the Pall will become self-sustaining and that evacuation does not restore the city, its river system, or the breach through which Shadowfell influence is spreading. Thalan accepts that the undertaking may satisfy the Concord's old necessity standard.

She does not accept Ilyrion's desired precedent. A petition recognizing a general right of foreign polities to compel royal relics will not receive her seal. A narrow instrument can.

The delegation offers:

- Crown ownership throughout;
- a named relic and undertaking;
- an outside term;
- a sunset barring citation outside emergencies involving permanent planar alteration;
- a later constitutional review; and
- shared public credit that does not celebrate the king's defeat.

Thalan asks whether the delegation intends to compel Caelir and then describe him as a willing benefactor.

Lethiel says no. If he remains opposed, the record will say he was compelled. If he joins, the record will state the time and terms of his joinder.

Eirath approves the distinction. She will not declare until security and term are concrete. She tells them that voluntary royal joinder would resolve much of her remaining concern, but her seal cannot depend on a favor the king may withhold after the other houses commit.

They leave with two Winter routes and no Winter seal. Frost preserves possibilities as readily as it preserves bodies.

## Court Day Four: Two emergencies at once

### The Silent Fir Lodge

Caldus Serevin displays the eastern marches without flourish. The Dawnward will weaken. The Gloam Host can exploit that weakness. He does not claim to know that it will.

Garran asks for the baseline separating ordinary movement from a breach. Serevin has one: ward loss across two named crossings, confirmed by independent stations, paired with hostile movement beyond the seasonal norm. He had not included the final condition in his opening demand for recall authority.

Nara asks whether Serevin omitted it because he considers troop movement sufficient.

“No,” Caldus says. “The court generally trusts its border warden to recognize hostile movement.”

Tamsin looks at the blank space where that discretion would sit in the petition.

Tamsin says the petition will not.

They construct an objective trigger. Serevin may order immediate defensive measures when the baseline is crossed. Recall of the Sunseed requires confirmation that the defined breach exists. The confirming process cannot be controlled by the expedition commander alone or by Serevin alone. Namaris will define the inspectors and records.

Serevin also needs forces. He will command the eastern reserve. Serathiel need not command it. Orelle can finance substitute wards and reserve pay. The delegation's allies can provide companies, but Aurelune must open its passages and supply depots before the Conclave if the commitment is to be credible.

Caldus gives a conditional declaration. He will seal an eight- to ten-day undertaking with funded reserve orders, measured recall, and no clause treating ordinary seasonal movement as a breach.

The six-day royal alternative would reduce Aurelune's exposure. It would also leave Orison little margin for transport, use, and return. Serevin says only that it is safer for his marches. He does not call it sufficient for the rescue.

### The Noon Spear Court

Marshal Ysandre Serathiel subjects the delegation to the war-table test. A Shadowfell incursion divides the road. The Dawnward reports a breach. Mara refuses a route that would place the Sunseed in exclusive Crown control.

Garran's first answer keeps command with the Grey Lantern Delegation. Naevra guards the Sunseed. Mara controls the undertaking. Serevin controls the border response. The arrangement contains no final authority when the road and border emergencies compete.

Ysandre lets the brass companies wait against one another. “Everyone may prevent the disaster assigned to them. Who may order the expedition to move?”

Garran asks for all prepared command schemes. Corven Ashe produces five. Two divide command coherently without placing Orison under Crown control.

The final working scheme gives Ysandre supreme command of the expedition. Captain Naevra Sol is royal co-custodian and commander of the Sunlance Guard. Tamsin is the delegation's co-custodian. Garran commands Grey Lantern field operations within Ysandre's campaign orders. Mara may refuse a change of undertaking or use against Orison's civil government. Naevra may stop an act that would damage the Sunseed or exceed the undertaking. Neither refusal power controls ordinary route and tactics.

Garran studies the command lines until the brass marker bearing his name disappears beneath Ysandre's. He asks whether she intends to command Orison's government after arrival.

Ysandre says no.

He asks whether Ysandre will accept text stating that she has no such authority.

She says yes.

Serathiel also accepts Serevin command of the eastern reserve. The house does not need to control both theaters. It needs a coherent answer in each.

Ysandre gives a conditional declaration tied to an eight-day operational schedule, funded reserve, substitute wards, and purpose-limited vetoes. Garran yields the command he expected to hold. The delegation gains the military package it could not otherwise assemble before departure.

### The Golden Sheaf Exchange

Lady Mirelle Orelle has priced every element of the plan, including investments likely to remain Orelle property. Nara asks which costs become unrecoverable if the Sunseed returns on time and no Dawnward breach occurs.

Line by line, three million crowns becomes less than one million in unrecoverable exposure. The remainder buys transport, wardstones, reserve pay, and reconstruction capacity that Orelle will still own or sell after the sky returns.

The delegation accepts that someone must fund those things. It rejects sovereign trade privileges and unlimited indemnity. The settlement provides:

- banner guarantees divided among Maelith, Vaudren, Orelle, Namaris, Thalan, and Serevin;
- compensation only for verified unrecoverable loss;
- ordinary, competitively valued reconstruction contracts in Orison;
- no exclusive customs rights;
- an audit date and valuation method controlled by the petition; and
- funding for the eastern reserve and substitute wards regardless of whether the king joins.

Ilyrion's proposed guarantee remains available, but Saelira will not join a final coalition in which Orison's political authority is narrower than the recognition compact promises. Orelle will not count an unsigned future compact as collateral.

Mirelle gives a conditional declaration. She also begins spending before the seal. The wardstones and reserve contracts are real preparations, not promises that become true only if the petition wins.

At sunset, seven seasonal banners can be named without wishful counting: Maelith, Serathiel, Vaudren, Orelle, Namaris, Thalan, and Serevin. Every one is conditional. Ilyrion belongs to another possible coalition; it is not an eighth seal waiting to be collected.

## Court Day Five: The Masque of Plain Faces

The Masque does not conceal identities. Each guest wears a plain silver half-mask that reflects the mouth and leaves the eyes unobscured. Oath-mirrors preserve statements and the questions left unanswered.

The delegation comes to the Masque to give private conditions witnesses before the king can offer each house a cleaner bargain alone.

Othalan Maelith gives the agreed acknowledgment. He states that the equinox order diminished Vaudren's standing before the court, even though he continues to believe the genealogical order was lawful. Avar Vaudren accepts the acknowledgment and states that he will not require Othalan to confess a motive he denies.

Vaudren declares for a petition with neutral or shared authorship and a public office for the proclamation and return. Maelith declares for reciprocal personal surety and preserved royal custody. Neither house claims precedence over the other in the undertaking.

Tamsin and Lady Senra declare the reciprocal bond. Tamsin answers for her custody and obedience to return. Senra answers for the royal guard's protection and for Crown compliance with the joint-custody article. Mara is explicitly not a hostage, ward, or surety.

Orelle announces the guarantor shares. Serevin and Serathiel announce the eastern reserve division. Nara states the measurements that will define Dawnward failure. Namaris witnesses the side instruments and records their dependency on the central text.

Caelir waits until every banner has named its ground. The six-day expedition he ordered studied on the first morning is now executable because the court has supplied a passage schedule, command structure, border replacement, and priced liabilities. He offers each house a way to keep its public ground and leave the others.

To Maelith, he offers exclusive royal custody and a place for Lady Senra in the royal expedition.

To Serathiel, he offers undivided command.

To Vaudren, he offers first place in the evacuation procession and sole voice of the royal relief.

To Orelle, he offers Crown assumption of the financial risk.

To Thalan, he offers no compelled precedent.

To Serevin, he offers a six-day term.

Every offer satisfies something the house has truthfully said it wants. Together they abandon joint custody and reduce the undertaking to evacuation and a rapid royal attempt.

Lethiel asks each house to state whether its public ground concerns relief of people or restoration of Orison. The answers are not uniform. Maelith cares about answerable custody. Serathiel cares about a workable mission. Orelle cares about assigned risk. Thalan cares about necessity and precedent. Serevin cares about the marches. Vaudren cares about public order.

No single appeal can hold them. They did not join for one reason.

The delegation answers house by house. The royal expedition has no eight-day schedule, no reserve funded beyond Crown discretion, no reciprocal bond, no neutral authorship, and no civil refusal right for Orison. No banner becomes nobler during the argument. Each discovers that the bargain it wants has been mortised into bargains the king is asking it to abandon.

House Eiral does not declare. Theron says in his banner capacity that the coalition is coherent enough to govern but too narrow to deserve the Twilight Laurel's ownership. In his official capacity, he confirms the drafting appointment.

Saelira Ilyrion attends and refuses to declare. She says the delegation has preserved Orison against conquest but has not recognized it as an equal custodian. Tamsin does not accuse her of abandoning the city. Saelira does not accuse the delegation of selling it. Their disagreement becomes part of the public record.

## Court Day Six: The page under seven hands

### Return to the Amber Quill Archive

The Masque has given every promise a witness and every dependency a name. Pelion can now draft an instrument instead of arranging aspirations in legal order.

The archive separates the central instrument from six associated instruments:

- reciprocal personal surety;
- neutral public credit;
- reserve deployment and substitute wards;
- bounded finance and liability;
- audit and inspection protocol; and
- Orison's acceptance of the undertaking and civil refusal right.

The Ilyrion recognition compact remains unsigned. Tamsin asks whether it can be docketed as a future instrument without becoming a condition of the present seals. Pelion says yes, if the present petition neither claims it exists nor contradicts the authority it would later recognize.

The delegation accepts **protected participant** for Orison. Mara is not a co-custodian of the Sunseed. She can refuse redirection of the undertaking and use against Orison's civil government. Tamsin, not the city, is the mortal physical custodian. The clause preserves power and denies status in the same breath.

Tamsin asks Saelira once more whether Ilyrion can seal that structure.

Saelira says no.

The answer removes the last comforting fiction from the count. Ilyrion is not a spare vote. The seven seasonal houses hold, or the petition fails.

### The Chamber of the Fourfold Petition

The petition hangs over the open center of the square table. Every defined term lights the places where it appears.

The first complete draft provides:

- Crown title;
- joint custody by Naevra and Tamsin;
- Serathiel expedition command;
- Mara's refusal right against redirection and civil subordination;
- an eight-day term;
- objective recall after measured Dawnward failure;
- bounded liability for verified unrecoverable loss;
- exact audit and return; and
- a sunset and later constitutional review.

Seven banners place provisional seals.

The royal advocate then identifies a conflict. The draft gives Naevra authority to stop any act that threatens **the safe execution of the undertaking**. Elsewhere, Serathiel has final authority over safety within the undertaking. The phrase could give the royal custodian a general veto over route and tactics.

The royal advocate calls the phrase protective authority. Garran says, “That is Crown command wearing the word *safety*.”

The Chamberlain rules that the conflict is material. Serathiel's, Maelith's, Serevin's, and Vaudren's seals release because their command, surety, recall, and authorship bargains depend on the allocation of authority. Orelle, Namaris, and Thalan remain sealed: liability, exact drafting, and narrow precedent are unchanged, though all three may reconsider voluntarily.

The delegation corrects the article. Naevra may stop an act that presents a defined risk of damage to the Sunseed or uses it outside the named undertaking. She may not direct route, tactics, Orison's government, or ordinary expedition safety.

The Grey Lantern journal initially records that Vaudren's seal remained attached because the public-credit article did not change. Lethiel corrects the entry. Vaudren's declaration depended on neutral authorship, and a broad royal safety veto would have made the undertaking functionally royal. The original operation remains visible and is voided by the correction.

Maelith and Serevin reaffirm after reading the corrected article. Serathiel wants the eight-day campaign schedule attached before reaffirming. Vaudren wants the final title fixed. The Chamber recesses without a certified petition.

Most of the sixth day is gone when the Chamber recesses. Caelir sends the six-day royal instrument in a red case. It is complete, coherent, and ready for signatures.

Tamsin closes the case. The delegation refuses it.

## Court Day Seven: The king joins after losing

### Return to the Hall of First Frost

The delegation goes to Thalan before the Conclave because royal joinder is now a real possibility rather than a hopeful phrase.

Eirath reviews the corrected protective veto, eight-day term, sunset, and final title. She asks whether the delegation will accept the king's joinder if he demands shared credit and active protective authority.

Tamsin says it will accept authority already bounded by the corrected article and credit that does not convert the Concord into a royal gift. It will not surrender certification as the condition of joinder.

Thalan approves. Her seal remains attached. She also agrees to present the necessity finding at the Conclave, making clear that the crisis satisfies the old standard without creating a general foreign right to royal relics.

They leave Thalan with a path Caelir may take after the law has ceased to ask his permission. His seal may improve the undertaking. It will not validate the majority.

### The Crown Conclave

The Sunseed rises from the glass root beneath the throne. The empty Ashen standard stands beside the unsealed Twilight Laurel. The omissions are visible.

The Chamberlain reads the petition. Orelle, Namaris, and Thalan confirm that their seals remain. Maelith and Serevin reaffirm the corrected authority article. Serathiel receives the attached campaign schedule and reaffirms. Vaudren receives the title:

> The Crown and Concord of Aurelune for the Restoration of Orison

Avar reaffirms and accepts responsibility for proclamation and return.

There are seven seals with all four seasons represented.

Theron Eiral certifies the petition in his official capacity. He does not place the Twilight Laurel beside it.

Caelir presents his final alternative anyway. The royal instrument offers immediate departure, six days, exclusive Crown custody, Crown assumption of cost, Serathiel command, and evacuation as the guaranteed minimum outcome. It is coherent and attractive. It would save lives.

The seven houses remain. None needs to pretend the royal instrument is worthless; each must admit that it abandons a promise already given beneath witnesses.

Theron counts again. Spring. Summer. Autumn. Winter. Seven.

The Crown is bound.

Caelir then asks for three amendments to the certified settlement:

1. fix the term at eight days with no discretionary extension;
2. retain the shared title naming Crown and Concord; and
3. state the corrected royal protective authority expressly in the opening custody article rather than only in the command schedule.

These are material but not a new undertaking. The dependent houses read the exact text. Serathiel confirms the schedule fits eight days. Vaudren confirms the title. Maelith confirms the reciprocal bond. Serevin confirms that objective recall remains. Orelle confirms the reserve funding. Namaris confirms that the definitions are unchanged. Thalan confirms that voluntary joinder now satisfies one of its accepted constitutional routes.

All seven reaffirm.

Caelir places the Crown seal beside them.

Caelir has not changed his judgment of the compulsion. He has changed his judgment of what a defeated king should do next. Crown passages open. The Sunlance Guard receives orders instead of cautions. Dawnward keepers begin the transfer as participants rather than witnesses to a seizure.

Saelira Ilyrion blesses the departure and condemns the noun **protected participant**. Her opposition no longer threatens the vote. It follows the Sunseed out of the hall as unfinished business.

Theron records that the Ashen Bough remains vacant by the delegation's initial choice. No emergency doctrine of blood, adoption, or regency has been created.

Mara accepts the undertaking for Orison and refuses Eladrin governance of Orison in the same sentence. Pelion has written the sentence so neither half can be quoted without the other.

Tamsin and Naevra take joint physical custody. Garran receives Ysandre's first campaign order. Nara accompanies the Dawnward keepers through the transfer measurements. Lethiel stands beside Vaudren's heralds while they read words neither the Crown nor any single house owns.

The Sunseed enters the Crown Passage before the last safe departure. The eight-day custody term begins as the root-casket leaves the throne, while the Grey Lantern route from Orison's western gate remains open toward the Pall's center.

## Immediate settlement

The throne hall empties. The bargain does not.

Orelle's reserve and ward contracts begin. Namaris opens the audit. Serevin takes command of the eastern reserve. Thalan calendars the constitutional review. Maelith records the reciprocal bond. Vaudren controls the public proclamation but not its legal meaning. Serathiel commands the expedition and must now honor the limits it accepted in order to receive command.

House Eiral remains outside the political coalition while Theron defends the validity of the certification as an act of office. The Ashen claimants return to a succession dispute not decided by Orison's deadline. Ilyrion begins assembling support for a later recognition compact.

Caelir's joinder prevents his officers from treating the Concord as a humiliation to be obeyed minimally. It gives him real public credit and a continuing protective role. Seven seals compelled the Sunseed's release; the Crown seal purchases cooperation the Concord could not compel in the same form.

The victory is strong enough to leave visible teeth marks.

- Orison retains civil authority and control over redirection, but it is not recognized as an equal co-custodian.
- Garran gives up supreme command.
- Tamsin binds herself personally until return.
- Aurelune receives strong inspection, recall, and liability rights.
- Orelle and Namaris gain durable post-crisis influence.
- The king joins and therefore shares the story of the rescue.
- Ilyrion's reform remains unfinished.

No hidden truth breaks the court open. Seven houses, one king, one endangered city, and four adventurers put their hands on the same arrangement. Each can describe it truthfully. None describes the same victory.
