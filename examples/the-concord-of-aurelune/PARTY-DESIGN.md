# Delegation Design: The Grey Lantern Delegation

**Status:** Finalized demonstration companion, voice-revised, and exercised by `play-state.example.json`.


## Authority boundary

This company is one worked example, not the adventure's assumed party. The authoritative source does not require the Grey Lantern name, these four characters, this route, these seals, or this outcome. A fresh group may divide civil, military, technical, and diplomatic authority differently or pursue another lawful coalition. If a future source revision invalidates part of this example, revise the example; do not weaken the adventure to preserve it.

## Purpose of the checked-in journal

The Grey Lantern Delegation demonstrates the court as politics rather than a puzzle box. The law is read aloud. The houses name their interests. The useful questions expose scope, price, and authority, not a hidden culprit. The delegation must choose among bargains that cannot all survive together, then make the chosen coalition say one thing on one page.

The journal exercises the following adventure and runtime behaviors:

1. the delegation visits a house whose support it ultimately does not obtain;
2. a seven-seal coalition is assembled entirely from the eight seasonal banners;
3. House Eiral's political seal and the Chamberlain's official certification remain separate;
4. the Ashen Bough remains vacant by deliberate choice rather than omission;
5. the same court encounters are revisited after other bargains change their value;
6. a material drafting amendment releases dependent seals and requires reaffirmation;
7. an inaccurate journal operation is corrected without erasing the original record; and
8. the king joins a petition voluntarily only after seven banners have made compulsion certain.

The delegation establishes every authored revelation while leaving the Twilight Laurel Apartments and Ashen Bough Hearing unvisited. It also leaves a substantial number of redundant clues unseen.

## The delegation

The **Grey Lantern Delegation** carries emergency plenipotentiary authority from Orison's surviving council. Its members earned the commission by crossing the forming Pall twice: once to bring out the first measurements of the failing sky, and once to return with witnesses after neighboring governments called those measurements panic. Their second crossing also recovered the noon-market stone that Aurelune's Dawnward keepers use to verify the Sunseed's effect.

The old Lantern Road compact gives Orison a right of emergency audience and opens a Crown Passage between Aurelune and Orison's western gate. It does not recognize Orison as a treaty equal. The council's commission makes the Grey Lanterns lawful petitioners; the noun under which the court receives them remains a political choice.

Envoy Mara Venn accompanies them and speaks for Orison's civil government. The adventurers may bind themselves and exercise the emergency mandate; Mara alone may accept terms governing the city after the heroes have gone. Aurelune repeatedly tests that division by treating service to Orison as though it conferred title over Orison.

Their device is a grey lantern with four open shutters. It swears nothing and outranks no one.

### Tamsin Rook

Tamsin is a human paladin and the delegation's formal head. She once served as a magistrate in Orison's river wards. She resigned after refusing an order to keep evacuation bridges closed until merchant traffic had cleared. The council chose her because she knows what emergency authority can do, and how quickly it learns to praise itself.

**Useful habits**

- Forces an offer into named powers, duration, and consequences.
- Distinguishes gratitude, dependency, custody, and political consent.
- Can give a binding personal promise without pretending that her promise binds Orison.

**Pressure point**

Tamsin begins convinced that a rescue which denies Orison's equality is defeat in ceremonial dress. She is willing to risk the majority for Ilyrion's recognition program. The deadline forces her to separate the status she wants the court to concede from the authority Orison must retain before the seventh day ends.

**Likely concern in this adventure**

She leads with treaty-partner standing, refuses fosterage, and keeps Mara's right to reject any redirection of the undertaking. In the final instrument, Orison is a protected participant rather than a sovereign co-custodian. Tamsin signs that clause and does not forgive the noun.

### Lethiel Greenglass

Lethiel is an Eladrin bard who left Aurelune two decades ago and became an Orison citizen. He knows which entrance insults a host, which genealogy permits an interruption, and which true sentence is meant to be misunderstood. Several courtiers remember him as a household performer and assume exile has made him hungry to be invited back through the proper door.

**Useful habits**

- Recognizes when order of entry, sponsorship, and public credit are exercising real authority.
- Restates an elegant implication as the exact assertion a courtier has avoided.
- Creates public settlements in which neither party must adopt the other's account of motive.

**Pressure point**

Lethiel despises the court's obsession with precedence, which makes him slow to admit how much power precedence carries. Vaudren's grievance looks petty until Lethiel sees it governing sponsorship, entrances, and who may claim the rescue. He must repair the public wound without accepting Avar's entire account of it.

**Likely concern in this adventure**

He arranges Maelith's acknowledgment, denies either Maelith or Vaudren sole authorship, and fixes the final public title as **The Crown and Concord of Aurelune for the Restoration of Orison**. That title becomes one of the terms Caelir later accepts when he joins.

### Nara Stoneglass

Nara is a dwarven abjurer, civic engineer, and veteran of failed planar wards. She stabilized Orison's first shelters and produced the measurements showing when the Pall would begin feeding itself. At court, she is the only member of the delegation who can challenge Aurelune's ward estimates without asking an Eladrin expert to translate them.

**Useful habits**

- Separates gross exposure, recoverable investment, and unrecoverable loss.
- Converts promises of protection into ward strength, inspection rights, crews, and replacement schedules.
- Reads a legal definition beside the measurement or mechanism it is meant to govern.

**Pressure point**

Nara is impatient with symbols and first reads Orelle's three-million-crown total as theft in ceremonial arithmetic. The Dawnward measurements change her mind about the risk, not about the markup. She will make Orison pay for damage it causes; she will not let Orelle purchase a decade of influence by calling influence an expense.

**Likely concern in this adventure**

She builds the substitute-ward and reserve budget, narrows Orelle's indemnity to verified unrecoverable loss, and insists that Namaris define baseline measurements before Serevin receives recall authority. Her technical work makes the king's strongest security objection answerable rather than merely unpopular.

### Garran Vale

Garran is a half-orc ranger and former captain of Orison's western companies. He ordered the retreat from the first districts swallowed by the Pall. Most of his soldiers lived. Several neighborhoods remained beyond the last defensible line. He has not decided whether the order was right; he has decided that no one who was absent may call it clean.

**Useful habits**

- Tests a command scheme against simultaneous failures rather than ideal execution.
- Distinguishes the authority to protect an artifact from authority over a city or campaign.
- Can recognize a military concession that is necessary without treating necessity as virtue.

**Pressure point**

Garran expects to command the Sunseed expedition. Serathiel can mobilize the escort and make the eastern reserve real before the deadline. Holding both Summer command and Winter defense requires Garran to decide whether the title he expected is worth a weaker plan.

**Likely concern in this adventure**

He yields supreme expedition command to Marshal Ysandre Serathiel, retains field authority over the delegation's operations, and secures a purpose-limited royal veto rather than a general Crown command. He regards the arrangement as workable and politically dangerous.

## Working disagreements

The route grows from four disagreements.

- Tamsin wants Ilyrion's equality clause in the central petition; Nara and Garran fear that the resulting coalition will not survive the military and liability articles.
- Lethiel wants Maelith to satisfy Vaudren publicly before receiving anything; Tamsin does not want Orison's deadline spent adjudicating an old procession.
- Garran resists Serathiel command; Nara argues that command is the price of a credible reserve and not equivalent to civil control.
- Tamsin sees royal joinder as an attempt to claim the victory; Lethiel sees a chance to make the king publicly share obligations that compulsion alone cannot create.

No expertise resolves these disputes by itself. The company works because each member can force the others to name a cost they would rather call necessary. None receives the settlement proposed on the first morning.

The route leaves House Eiral outside the political coalition and never convenes the Ashen Bough Hearing. Theron Eiral nevertheless certifies the final petition in office. At the Conclave, the unsealed Twilight Laurel and the empty Ashen place remain where everyone can see them.

## Final seven seals

- **Spring:** House Maelith
- **Summer:** House Serathiel and House Vaudren
- **Autumn:** House Orelle and House Namaris
- **Winter:** House Thalan and House Serevin

House Ilyrion refuses the final petition because Orison is not made a sovereign co-custodian. House Eiral declines to add an eighth seal to a coalition it considers governable but politically narrow. The Ashen Bough remains vacant.

## Final settlement

The certified instrument provides:

- Crown ownership and joint physical custody by Captain Naevra Sol and Tamsin Rook;
- Marshal Ysandre Serathiel as expedition commander, with Garran retaining field command of the Grey Lantern Delegation;
- Mara Venn's authority to reject use outside the restoration of Orison or against Orison's civil government;
- an eight-day term;
- a royal protective veto limited to damage to the Sunseed and use beyond the named undertaking;
- objective recall after a measured Dawnward failure, confirmed through the defined inspection process;
- a funded eastern reserve under Serevin command;
- bounded liability for verified unrecoverable losses;
- a reciprocal personal bond between Tamsin and Lady Senra Maelith;
- exact audit, return, and sunset language; and
- neutral public credit under the title **The Crown and Concord of Aurelune for the Restoration of Orison**.

Certification precedes royal joinder. Seven seasonally balanced seals bind the Crown before Caelir adds his own. He joins for the eight-day limit, the shared title, and an express protective authority already bounded by the text. The Crown seal purchases active cooperation; it does not manufacture validity after the fact.

## Resulting political state

- **Ilyrion:** supports the rescue but opposes the final status settlement and preserves the issue for later reform.
- **Maelith:** accepts reciprocal surety rather than fosterage and receives no senior authorship.
- **Vaudren:** receives public acknowledgment and neutral credit without a general precedence ruling.
- **Serathiel:** commands the expedition but cannot govern Orison or redirect the undertaking.
- **Orelle:** finances the ward package and receives bounded reconstruction contracts rather than sovereign trade privileges.
- **Namaris:** controls audit and return definitions, increasing the archive's post-crisis influence.
- **Thalan:** obtains a narrow necessity finding, sunset, and later constitutional review.
- **Serevin:** commands the funded eastern reserve and receives objective rather than unilateral recall.
- **Eiral:** certifies the petition but does not own the coalition.
- **The Ashen Bough:** remains vacant; neither inheritance doctrine is decided under emergency pressure.
- **Caelir:** is lawfully defeated, negotiates meaningful amendments, and joins the resulting undertaking without becoming its sole author.
