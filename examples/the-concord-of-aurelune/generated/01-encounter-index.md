# Encounter Index: The Concord of Aurelune

## The Argent Canopy (`the-argent-canopy`)

**Role:** necessary, start

Caelir's refusal leaves lawful nouns open; beneath the listening Canopy, Orison must name the standing, purpose, and calendar around which banners can gather.

## The Unfurling Branch (`the-unfurling-branch`)

**Role:** necessary

Ilyrion offers Spring only if Orison enters the rescue as a polity with an operative hand in its own preservation.

## The Gallery of Silver Kin (`the-white-hart-gallery`)

**Role:** necessary

Maelith demands a living guarantor whose obligations survive command changes without quietly making Orison a ward.

## The Noon Spear Court (`the-noon-spear-court`)

**Role:** necessary

Serathiel puts command, eastern defense, and the loan term on one war table and asks which orders remain executable.

## The Red Rose Pavilion (`the-red-rose-pavilion`)

**Role:** necessary

Vaudren makes an old precedence wound public and demands satisfaction that carries responsibility rather than costume.

## The Golden Sheaf Exchange (`the-golden-sheaf-exchange`)

**Role:** necessary

Orelle prices the absent Sunseed in grain, wards, villages, and political credit, then asks who signs each loss.

## The Amber Quill Archive (`the-amber-quill-archive`)

**Role:** necessary

Namaris separates custody from ownership, recall from failure, and elegant language from operative law.

## The Hall of First Frost (`the-hall-of-first-frost`)

**Role:** necessary

Thalan asks what necessity permits today without becoming a standing license against the Crown tomorrow.

## The Silent Fir Lodge (`the-silent-fir-lodge`)

**Role:** necessary

Serevin releases Winter only when the eastern marches receive troops, warding, and a recall rule that can be measured.

## The Twilight Laurel Apartments (`the-twilight-laurel-apartments`)

**Role:** optional

Theron separates public duty from House Eiral's seal and tests whether the coalition can govern its own contradictions.

## The Ashen Bough Hearing (`the-ashen-bough-hearing`)

**Role:** optional

A succession hearing may supply an eighth banner, but only by making the coalition own the doctrine that seats it.

## The Masque of Plain Faces (`the-masque-of-plain-faces`)

**Role:** optional

Oath-mirrors give private conditions authors and let every abandoned promise keep its witness until dawn.

## The Chamber of the Fourfold Petition (`the-chamber-of-the-fourfold-petition`)

**Role:** optional

Lines of seasonal light expose where seven banner bargains touch, and each amendment shows which recognitions and seals it breaks.

## The Crown Conclave (`the-crown-conclave`)

**Role:** necessary, end

The Sunseed rises among four seasonal shadows, forcing each banner to decide whether its public reason can inhabit the coalition that will carry it.
