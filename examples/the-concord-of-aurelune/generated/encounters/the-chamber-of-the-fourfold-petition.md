# The Chamber of the Fourfold Petition

**Role:** optional  
**Tags:** court, drafting, petition

Lines of seasonal light expose where seven banner bargains touch, and each amendment shows which recognitions and seals it breaks.

## Opening View

One word changes above the square table. Three lines of light snap; a seal lifts; two side covenants begin glowing different colors. Spring, Summer, Autumn, and Winter stand around the same open center, each entered by another door. Pelion reaches for the adverse index. Mara points to the severed line between custody and command. "Whose promise," she asks, "did that word just release?"

## Encounter Material

## Situation

The Chamber of the Fourfold Petition has four doors and no central entrance. Spring, Summer, Autumn, and Winter enter separately and meet around a square table cut from one pale tree. The center is open. Above it, the petition hangs in lines of light, each defined term connected to every place it governs.

Contradictions shine in different colors. Compatible bargains share a line of seasonal light; incompatible ones break apart before a seal touches the page. The chamber judges neither justice nor prudence. It makes the coalition visible enough that seven houses cannot seal one page while imagining seven different rescues.

Theron Eiral presides as Chamberlain. Pelion Namaris reads precedent. Mara Venn speaks where Orison has secured authority to speak. The Crown sends an advocate even when the king stays away. Houses may bring counsel, but Orison's petitioners control the petition unless they have granted a sponsor co-authorship.

Come early for model clauses, return with successive drafts, or arrive on the sixth day carrying nearly finished terms. A seal becomes meaningful only when the text identifies the undertaking, custody, command, term, liability, recall, and return—and when each banner can see which promise it will lose if one of those articles changes.

## Three pages around one living text

The chamber keeps three related but distinct dockets.

### The Crown instrument

This is the petition the banners seal and the Chamberlain certifies. It must contain a complete legal undertaking. It cannot delegate its essential meaning to an unpublished promise.

### Associated instruments

Recognition, personal surety, finance, credit, deployment, audit, and border covenants may sit beside the petition. The chamber indexes them and tests them for contradiction. A side covenant may give a house a reason to seal. It may not secretly reverse the central instrument.

### The declaration record

The chamber receives transcripts from the Argent Canopy, the Ashen Bough Hearing, and the Masque. Declarations do not count as seals. They show what a house previously said would be sufficient and make changes of position legible.

Keep a bargain private. It then cannot rely on the chamber to reconcile that bargain with the rest of the coalition.

## How recognitions gather on one text

### 1. State the undertaking

The first article must name what the Sunseed is being used to accomplish.

- **Destroy the Pall** is operationally direct but may permit methods that damage Orison.
- **Preserve Orison** protects the city but leaves disagreement about whether evacuation counts as preservation.
- **Restore natural day to Orison** identifies the desired condition and may require continued custody after the Pall first breaks.
- **Prevent permanent Shadowfell dominion over Orison** is broad enough to justify military action and broad enough to invite it.

Mara Venn can insist that the undertaking not be changed without Orison's consent. Serathiel can insist that ordinary operational decisions remain under command. Both can be true if redirection is distinguished from execution.

### 2. Allocate custody and command

Custody answers who physically holds and legally controls the Sunseed. Command answers who directs people and operations. The court habitually treats them as though one implies the other.

Usable arrangements include:

- joint physical custody by a royal custodian and a mortal co-custodian;
- party command subject to a royal veto limited to damage, prohibited uses, or redirection;
- Serathiel expedition command with Orison retaining civil authority and consent to a changed undertaking;
- rotating custody at named stages; or
- Crown ownership and protective authority without exclusive possession.

A clause giving Caelir "all authority necessary to preserve the Sunseed" is not narrow merely because preservation is a narrow noun. Namaris asks who decides what is necessary.

### 3. Fix the term and return

The petition needs an outside date even if it also ends on completion.

- **Six days** materially reduces Aurelune's exposure and may be too short to save Orison.
- **Thirteen days** supports the expedition plan and increases Dawnward risk.
- **Completion plus an absolute limit** is usually workable if completion is defined.
- **Return upon royal demand** is a unilateral recall under another name.

Return may mean physical delivery to a named official, replanting beneath the throne, or restoration of the Dawnward. These events do not necessarily occur at the same time. Orelle and Namaris care about valuation between delivery and restoration.

### 4. Assign liability

The chamber distinguishes loss from expenditure and fault from risk.

Possible structures include:

- capped shares among participating banners;
- allied guarantees held in trust;
- insurance funded by Orelle and repaid from a defined source;
- liability for proven misuse but not ordinary Dawnward risk;
- Crown assumption of specified background risk; or
- Orison reconstruction revenue without permanent trade privilege.

A promise to make Aurelune whole is not complete until the text identifies which losses count, who values them, and what property is reachable.

### 5. Define defense and recall

The security package may sit partly in deployment covenants, but the petition must state what happens if the Dawnward materially fails.

A strong objective trigger identifies:

- a measurable ward threshold or actual incursion;
- the officials who verify it;
- the response available before full recall;
- who may order recall; and
- whether the undertaking can continue under a shortened route or substitute protection.

Serevin can be given a duty to report and local command without a unilateral power to end the expedition. Serathiel can be given reserves without command over the eastern marches. The text should not solve a command rivalry by granting both officers final authority over the same act.

### 6. State status, credit, and precedent

These clauses determine the political order created by the rescue.

- Orison may be named a treaty partner, protected ally, ward, or participant only for this undertaking.
- Credit may belong to the Crown, the court, the participating banners, Orison's petitioners, or no sole author.
- The instrument may be relic-specific, emergency-only, expressly nonprecedential, or subject to later constitutional review.

A nonprecedential clause limits citation while the event remains in the record. A neutral credit clause leaves boasting untouched while denying sole authorship in the Crown instrument.

## Seals and amendments

A banner seal applies to one exact text. The chamber records why the house says the text is acceptable, but the legal force attaches to the words rather than the explanation.

Amendments fall into three practical classes.

### Corrections without changed effect

Spelling, numbering, cross-references, and a definition made explicit without changing its scope may be certified as corrections. The Chamberlain reads the correction aloud. Any banner may demand reaffirmation, but the correction does not automatically release seals.

### Material amendments to one article

A changed command power, term, liability share, recall trigger, status clause, or associated instrument releases every seal whose stated condition depends on that change. Other banners remain sealed unless they reserved broader conditions. The chamber asks each house to state that dependence on the record.

### A changed undertaking

Changing the named purpose or replacing joint custody with an exclusive scheme releases all seals. That amendment creates a new petition.

A released house may reaffirm immediately. It need not renegotiate from the beginning unless the amendment actually defeats its settlement. Public declarations and prior bargaining make reaffirmation easier, not automatic.

A seal cast by a temporary or scope-limited banner-holder is also bounded by the order that created the office. A correction within the authorized undertaking may be reaffirmed normally. A changed undertaking, a use outside the authorized covenants, or a term extending beyond the office requires renewed authority before that seal can attach to the revised text.

## What the Chamberlain can recognize

Theron examines the petition in his official capacity. Certification requires:

- seven seals on the same exact text;
- at least one Spring, Summer, Autumn, and Winter seal;
- a named undertaking and outside term;
- internally coherent custody, command, recall, liability, and return;
- a preserved royal interest; and
- associated instruments that do not contradict the petition.

Theron must identify a defect specifically. He cannot say only that the text is unwise or politically dangerous. He may require correction, schedule a correction session, and refuse to certify language whose validity depends on an aggressive interpretation.

House Eiral may withhold its banner while the Chamberlain certifies. It may seal while Theron warns that another clause is defective. The roles do not merge because the same person performs them.

## The Crown draft

The royal advocate files Caelir's alternative once the coalition's working draft becomes credible. It offers a six-day royal expedition under exclusive Crown custody, a defined evacuation program, lesser relics for protected districts, and full Crown liability for royal acts. Orison receives consultation but no authority to redirect or refuse royal use within the expedition.

The Crown draft leaves the petition valid and gives wavering houses a complete alternative consistent with their public purposes. Borrow its security clauses, negotiate toward voluntary joinder, or reject it and hold seven seals.

## Shapes one coalition may truthfully inhabit

### Draft a narrow Crown instrument with broad side covenants

The petition carries only the undertaking, custody, command, term, recall, liability, and return. Recognition, surety, credit, finance, and deployments sit in associated instruments. This limits precedent and makes contradictions easier to isolate. It depends on houses trusting covenants outside the central text.

### Draft one broad political compact

The petition itself names Orison's equality, neutral credit, shared defense, and constitutional limits. The victory becomes difficult to disaggregate later. Every amendment threatens more seals.

### Build staged custody

The Sunseed begins under joint custody, passes to expedition custody on departure, and returns to royal custody after the Pall breaks or the term expires. Staging can reconcile several houses and creates dangerous transition points if poorly defined.

### Seek royal joinder

Adopt enough Crown protections that Caelir can join voluntarily without obtaining exclusive command. This helps Eiral and Thalan and may cost reformist credit or operational freedom.

### Hold a bare majority

Refuse late improvements that would release one of its seven seals. The resulting instrument may be inelegant but valid. The king will test every seam at the Conclave.

## How the sitting may close

### Seven houses seal the same undertaking

The coalition leaves with a complete exact petition, at least seven seals including all four seasons, associated instruments indexed without contradiction, and a correction plan for any narrow defect. Each sealed house can state why the actual text satisfies it.

### A sound text that sheds support

- Orison receives real authority only through a side covenant whose guarantors now own part of the political victory.
- The security package holds Winter and Summer at the price of a shortened term or a restrictive recall article.
- Liability is bounded by granting Orelle influence over valuation and post-rescue repayment.
- Thalan's sunset protects the Crown and narrows the reformers' precedent.
- Vaudren's credit clause keeps Summer balance but makes royal joinder less likely.
- A late amendment releases one or more seals and consumes the time reserved for final lobbying.

### The page proves the coalition never agreed

Ask the chamber to preserve ambiguous language because different houses understood it differently. Namaris identifies the conflicting effects, Theron refuses certification, and the chamber records that the coalition has not yet agreed on one undertaking. The petition remains amendable; the court day is lost and the Crown draft becomes the only complete instrument on file.

## What enters the certified record

- the exact Crown instrument and every associated instrument;
- which banners are sealed, released, declared, open, or opposed;
- the stated dependency of each seal on particular articles;
- every correction the Chamberlain has required;
- the final seasonal composition;
- whether Caelir has been invited to join and on what terms;
- whether Orison's representative has accepted the undertaking; and
- how much time remains for reaffirmation before the Conclave.

## On a return

The chamber remains open until the Conclave. A late correction is possible; a late redesign is a new petition. Let the political cost follow the houses whose bargains change.
