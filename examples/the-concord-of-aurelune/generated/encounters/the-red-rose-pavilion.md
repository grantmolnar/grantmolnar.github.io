# The Red Rose Pavilion

**Role:** necessary  
**Tags:** summer, banner, honor

Vaudren makes an old precedence wound public and demands satisfaction that carries responsibility rather than costume.

## Opening View

The musicians begin three different entrances and stop at the same sour chord. Six servants still hold the pavilion open: white carpet, twilight runner, bare red cloth. Avar Vaudren waits beneath a thornwood crown trained around one deliberate gap; Senra's place card stands a handspan before his heir's. He does not ask which entrance is fair. He asks which one the court is meant to remember.

## Encounter Material

## Situation

The Red Rose Pavilion is built anew for every audience. Silk walls, thornwood poles, carpets, flowers, musicians, and even the direction of entry are selected to state the host's judgment before the host speaks. Three arrangements await Orison's petitioners. The servants need only learn whether Maelith, Eiral, or no sponsor will cross the threshold first.

Lord Avar Vaudren does not ask whether Orison deserves rescue. He asks what the rescue will make true at court and whose name will be attached to it.

At the previous equinox procession, Maelith placed Lady Senra before Vaudren's heir. Maelith calls the order genealogically correct. Vaudren calls it a deliberate public diminution. The oath permits both statements.

Avar receives the petitioners beneath a trellis trained into the shape of a crown with one missing thorn.

> House Vaudren will not place its rose beneath a hart that has not acknowledged where it stepped.

He will join after public satisfaction, equal precedence for this undertaking, or a settlement in which neither house leads. Private peace is welcome. It is not the same thing as a repaired public order.

## House Vaudren

- **Lord Avar Vaudren** knows that precedence controls invitations, sponsorship, command, and whose account becomes the court's memory.
- **Lord Cael Vaudren**, his heir, would accept a restrained settlement and will not purchase it by contradicting his father before witnesses.
- **Mistress Ivara Quill**, herald of the house, records entrances, titles, silences, and acknowledgments with equal care.
- **Othalan Maelith** can end the quarrel by owning a chosen act, deepen it with an apology for pain, or step aside for neutral authorship.

Avar deceives through the measuring stick. No house has sacrificed more—if one begins the count with Vaudren's greatest campaign. No apology is more unprecedented—if adequacy is never mentioned. He presents rank as a fact while selecting the interval, witness, and ceremony by which rank is measured.

## What the Red Rose must recognize

Vaudren will seal through any one of four doors:

1. Othalan publicly acknowledges the chosen act that diminished Vaudren;
2. the court fixes equal or alternating precedence for this undertaking;
3. neither Maelith nor Vaudren receives authorship of the coalition; or
4. Vaudren bears a visible office or risk that creates independent honor in the rescue.

A private apology may reconcile two people. It repairs nothing at court unless Avar chooses to put the reconciliation where the injury occurred: before witnesses.

## Satisfactions Avar will own

### Exact apology

The meaningful formula is not apology for pain. Maelith must acknowledge choice and consequence:

> I chose an order that diminished House Vaudren before the court. I did not intend every consequence, and the choice was mine.

Othalan may alter the last clause, offer equal precedence now, or accept neutral authorship instead. The exact words determine whether Avar calls the matter satisfied.

### Adjudicated precedence

Namaris can identify the legal difference between genealogical precedence and ceremonial precedence. Eiral can chair a determination limited to the Sunseed undertaking. This resolves the present coalition without deciding every future procession.

### Neutral authorship

The petition is presented as an act of the whole court under Eiral or under no house name. Vaudren accepts if Maelith also renounces senior credit. Several other houses dislike losing public ownership of their concessions.

### The Rose bears the visible risk

Vaudren can provide the public champion, co-custodian, escort, or guarantor. The house gains honor through service rather than precedence. Serathiel contests military roles; Maelith may offer Lady Senra for the same purpose.

### Formal challenge

Vaudren may demand a ceremonial contest of advocacy, endurance, or arms. It assigns satisfaction under court custom, not truth. Serve as advocate, judge, second, or refuse the spectacle.

## Public and private settlement

Avar offers an easy private bargain: Maelith sends a personal apology, Vaudren privately promises not to obstruct, and neither house changes its public account. This may calm the feud. It does not produce a declared seal and remains vulnerable to later claims about leadership.

A public bargain is harder because every noun creates precedent. It is also more stable. The Masque of Plain Faces provides the strongest venue, but the Pavilion can host witnesses immediately if the petitioners want speed.

## Ways to bring the Rose into the same procession

### Demand the apology now

Bring Maelith into the Pavilion and resolves the grievance before either house seals. This can win both houses and lets the argument consume the coalition's first public day.

### Move to neutral authorship

Deny both houses senior credit. Eiral becomes more important. Vaudren can declare without forgiveness, and Maelith must decide whether security matters more than precedence.

### Give Vaudren an independent office

A Vaudren co-custodian, guarantor, or public advocate creates honor without rewriting procession law. The office must have real responsibility, not merely costume.

### Accept private reconciliation

The personal feud cools. Vaudren remains open rather than declared until the public record changes elsewhere.

### Exclude the grievance from the rescue

Tell both houses their quarrel is irrelevant. This may impress Ilyrion and Serathiel. Vaudren will not seal a coalition that allows Maelith to lead under cover of urgency.

## How the Rose may leave the pavilion

### Satisfaction before witnesses

Vaudren declares through public satisfaction or a durable neutral-credit arrangement that does not require humiliating Maelith.

### Honor purchased elsewhere

- Maelith apologizes publicly and demands greater control over custody or surety in return.
- Vaudren receives a real expedition office, adding another authority to the command structure.
- Neutral authorship wins Vaudren and Eiral while disappointing houses that need visible credit.
- A formal challenge settles custom and makes the court remember the rescue as Vaudren's arena.

### Courtesy without repair

Accept a graceful private apology and announce that the grievance is resolved. Avar truthfully states that no public satisfaction occurred. Maelith truthfully states that it apologized. Both accounts weaken the petitioners' credibility.

## What the heralds will repeat

- whether Maelith acknowledged choice and consequence;
- the public or private status of any reconciliation;
- the coalition's authorship and credit formula;
- any Vaudren office in custody, escort, or advocacy;
- the current rule of precedence for the undertaking; and
- whether the Masque must ratify the settlement.

## On a return

Vaudren becomes relevant whenever the coalition changes who speaks first, signs first, commands publicly, or receives credit. Avar can yield the old grievance for a visible present office. He will not accept that visible order is merely symbolic when every other house is using it to measure power.
