# The Unfurling Branch

**Role:** necessary  
**Tags:** spring, banner, recognition

Ilyrion offers Spring only if Orison enters the rescue as a polity with an operative hand in its own preservation.

## Opening View

Silver shears close, but not on the pear branch. Saelira lifts the boundary marker it crossed and plants it farther out. Behind her, mortal trees bear plaques reading treaty, gift, theft, rescue, defiance. Rethan Sol sets two blank instruments on the soil: one preserves Crown title; the other leaves room for Orison's own hand. Saelira plants the shears between them. No one has decided which document must come first.

## Encounter Material

## Situation

House Ilyrion receives petitioners in a terraced conservatory where mortal trees grow among flowers that bloom only in the first hour of Spring. Each tree bears a plaque naming the treaty, gift, theft, rescue, or act of defiance by which it came to Aurelune. Lady Saelira Ilyrion conducts the audience with silver shears in hand. Whenever a branch crosses its allotted border, she decides whether to prune it or move the marker.

Saelira wants the Sunseed used. She also wants the rescue to prove that mortal polities may enter Aurelune's highest acts as parties rather than dependents. She will not spend her banner on a petition that calls Orison a ward, places every mortal beneath Eladrin command, or treats consent as a door that can be closed only once.

Her offer is plain enough to quote and broad enough to fight over:

> House Ilyrion will seal an undertaking in which Orison stands as a party to its own preservation.

She leaves the binding surface open and gives the court a sentence by which to measure any retreat.

## House Ilyrion

- **Lady Saelira Ilyrion** is generous, ambitious, and pleased when the generous act also wins the argument she has wanted for a century.
- **Rethan Sol**, her jurist, knows how to make reciprocal obligations remain reciprocal after the stronger party begins “protecting” the weaker.
- **Envoy Mara Venn** wants recognition and mistrusts language Orison cannot honor while half its government is underground.
- **Aven Tal** appears often enough that every Ilyrion concession is also read as a vote in the Ashen succession.

Saelira practices moral compression. Every personal surety becomes wardship; every reservation about recognition becomes a taste for domination. Her examples are genuine, and her categories leave compromise looking shameful.

Ask her what authority she will allow an expedition commander inside the named undertaking. Ask whether sovereign partners may owe indemnity. Ask which recognition term must be operative now and which may live in a witnessed compact. She answers each narrower question cleanly.

## What Spring must recognize

Ilyrion needs three things, though they need not all occupy the same document:

1. Orison is named a treaty partner, co-principal, or sovereign participant rather than a ward.
2. A mortal representative holds real custody, oversight, or authority over the undertaking.
3. Orison may refuse a redirection of the undertaking or a use against its civil government.

A side compact, declaration, or reciprocal custody protocol can carry part of the bargain if another credible banner guarantees it. Saelira cares less about the binding surface than about whether the power survives translation into that surface.

## Forms Saelira will accept

### Full recognition in the petition

The petition names Orison as a treaty partner and co-custodian. This is the clearest route to Ilyrion's seal and the hardest route through Maelith and Thalan.

### Recognition by side compact

The central petition remains narrow. A parallel compact, witnessed by Eiral or Namaris, recognizes Orison's standing and limits later Eladrin direction. Ilyrion accepts this if the side instrument is adopted before its seal is fixed.

### Reciprocal personal surety

A named Orison envoy and a named Eladrin noble each enter the other's protection for the duration. Ilyrion can treat this as equality rather than wardship. Maelith can treat the same arrangement as personal security.

### Public guarantee by a third banner

Another house promises to oppose any use of the expedition to subordinate Orison. Eiral, Namaris, or even Vaudren can make the promise meaningful. A private assurance is not enough.

### Aven Tal's accession

If Aven is seated and seals on recognition grounds, Ilyrion can accept a narrower central clause because the coalition itself now embodies the principle. This route ties Orison's rescue to a disputed inheritance rule.

## The draft waiting on the table

Saelira asks Orison's petitioners to approve a declaration titled **The Equal Dawn**. Its strongest clause states that no Eladrin commander may alter the expedition's purpose without the consent of Orison's co-custodian. Serathiel reads this as an operational veto. Maelith reads the title as an accusation. Thalan reads the declaration as a general constitutional proposition hidden inside emergency relief.

Sign, revise, defer, or replace the text. Saelira will state exactly what was accepted. A tolerable revision has no public life until she writes it.

## Ways to turn the Spring banner

### Adopt the Equal Dawn

Ilyrion becomes declared immediately. The coalition gains a clear reformist center and makes military and dynastic compromises more expensive.

### Separate status from command

Recognize Orison as sovereign while defining a narrow military chain of command. This can satisfy Ilyrion and Serathiel if command cannot change the undertaking itself.

### Build reciprocal guarantees

Bring Maelith, Namaris, or Eiral into the conservatory and trades personal surety for political equality. The resulting settlement is more durable and gives every participant less than its preferred language.

### Promise a later constitutional compact

Ilyrion may declare support for a narrow rescue petition if a second instrument is calendared before the Conclave. Saelira demands a sponsor and a date, not an aspiration.

### Refuse to constitutionalize the rescue

Ilyrion does not become an enemy. Saelira may still seal a text that prevents wardship in practice. What the petitioners lose is the first Spring banner willing to say, before witnesses, that Orison enters as an equal.

## How Spring may leave the branch

### A usable Spring declaration

Ilyrion declares support on terms compatible with at least one plausible Maelith or Serathiel settlement. The petitioners obtain a drafted recognition clause or side compact rather than a verbal understanding.

### Recognition with a bearer

- Ilyrion declares for full recognition, and Maelith announces that no personal surety can coexist with the adopted language.
- Ilyrion accepts reciprocal surety, but Mara Venn or another mortal representative must personally bear the risk.
- A later compact wins Ilyrion, but the court calendar becomes tighter and Eiral demands a credible sponsor.

### Principle without a seal

Accept Ilyrion's rhetoric without fixing any operative language. Saelira praises the coalition publicly but does not declare. Other houses react to the broad principle while the coalition has gained no seal.

## Record before leaving

- Orison's named political status;
- the identity and powers of any mortal co-custodian;
- whether the Equal Dawn or a replacement declaration is public;
- whether recognition sits in the petition or a side compact;
- whether Aven Tal's claim has become part of the coalition; and
- what right Orison retains if Eladrin command changes the undertaking.

## On a return

Ilyrion is easiest to revisit after another house proposes language that appears protective but changes Orison's status. Saelira will trade breadth of recognition for stronger enforcement, or stronger symbolism for a narrower operative clause. She will not pretend those are the same victory.
