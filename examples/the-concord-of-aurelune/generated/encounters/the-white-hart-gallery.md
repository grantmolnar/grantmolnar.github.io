# The Gallery of Silver Kin

**Role:** necessary  
**Tags:** spring, banner, surety

Maelith demands a living guarantor whose obligations survive command changes without quietly making Orison a ward.

## Opening View

The empty family chair has already been moved beside Mara Venn. Across from it, Lady Senra rests one hand on an unsigned reciprocal bond. Master Halven turns a plaque between protected guest and fosterling while Othalan recites the offered comforts: rank, household, residence, protection. None is false. Then he asks who will answer, in a living name, if the Sunseed does not return.

## Encounter Material

## Situation

The Gallery of Silver Kin contains no antlers. Its walls are crowded with the people House Maelith acquired by birth, marriage, adoption, fosterage, hostage exchange, sworn friendship, and negotiated protection. Each portrait bears a silver plaque naming the relation. The plaques are exact. The painted faces are serene.

Prince-Regent Othalan Maelith does not object to saving Orison. He objects to trusting a city, an army, or a temporary coalition with the return of the Sunseed. A city cannot be shamed, married, disinherited, confined, or made to answer before a household court. A named person can.

> House Maelith will not demand that Orison become our ward. We require a person whom the law can hold.

His first proposed person is Envoy Mara Venn. The instrument calls her a fostered guest, preserves her rank and household, guarantees her comfort, and gives Maelith authority over her movement and future succession claims. Othalan has not lied about a single protection. He has merely begun with the warmest column of the ledger.

## House Maelith

- **Prince-Regent Othalan Maelith** trusts obligations that can sit at a table, inherit an estate, and be shamed by name.
- **Lady Senra Maelith**, his eldest daughter, offers herself as reciprocal surety without pretending the gesture is symbolic.
- **Master Halven Roe**, Keeper of Relations, can recite the powers of fosterling, hostage, ward, adopted child, and protected guest without consulting a book.
- **Lord Avar Vaudren** is present even when absent; one place in the last equinox procession still governs who may stand beside whom.

Othalan governs through kinship nouns. He begins with education, safety, dignity, and affection; residence, marriage, departure, and succession wait for a narrower question. He never misstates the bond. He begins where it sounds most like love.

## What the White Hart must recognize

Maelith will seal when a named person, office, or estate can be made to answer for custody and return. The guarantee must survive a change in command and cannot rest on Orison's ruined treasury or the petitioners' good intentions.

Othalan will accept:

1. narrow temporary fosterage stripped of unwanted family powers;
2. reciprocal hostages or protected guests;
3. personal guarantees by Great Banners;
4. a Namaris bond enforceable against named estates; or
5. a royal co-custodian whose office answers for return.

He will trade control for answerability. He will not accept sincerity as a legal person.

## Relations Othalan will recognize

### Narrow fosterage

Mara Venn or another senior representative becomes Maelith's fosterling for thirteen days. Strip succession, marriage, residence, and command from the instrument while preserving protection and answerability. Othalan asks what remains of the guarantee.

### Reciprocal hostages

Lady Senra travels under Orison's protection while a mortal representative resides with Maelith. This satisfies Ilyrion more readily and creates a severe personal cost if the undertaking fails.

### Banner surety

Two or more houses pledge their estates or offices to answer for return. Orelle can value the risk; Namaris can define default; Thalan approves the constitutional restraint. The coalition must decide which houses will expose themselves.

### Royal co-custody

A named Crown representative remains physically with the Sunseed. This is Maelith's easiest non-familial settlement and makes independent mortal command harder.

### Marriage or adoption compact

A marriage promise or sealed adoption can satisfy Maelith and transform succession politics. No one at court calls marriage or adoption symbolic. Use either only when the adventurers are willing to inherit the relation along with the seal.

## The Vaudren grievance

Maelith's seal cannot be discussed in isolation from the Red Rose. Othalan claims that the previous procession followed the genealogical order recorded in the Gallery. Vaudren claims the procession was a political act that publicly diminished his house.

Othalan first offers sorrow for whatever pain accompanied the correct order. The apology concedes feeling, preserves the act, and satisfies no one who came to change the record. He can be pushed toward one of three meaningful concessions:

- acknowledge that he chose the order rather than merely followed it;
- state that Vaudren's precedence is equal in the present undertaking; or
- accept neutral authorship under Eiral rather than Maelith leadership.

A private apology protects his dignity. A public one can win Vaudren.

## Ways to bring the Hart beside Orison

### Draft narrow fosterage

Turn Maelith's preferred form into a limited security instrument. This is exacting work, but it can coexist with Orison's sovereignty.

### Offer reciprocal surety

Lady Senra's willingness makes this real. The arrangement may satisfy Ilyrion and Maelith together, while drawing the rescue into personal succession politics.

### Assemble banner guarantors

Shift risk from a mortal person to allied houses. Orelle and Namaris become important; the coalition gains durability and more veto points.

### Preserve royal co-custody

Maelith declares readily if a named royal custodian remains. Serathiel and Thalan approve. Ilyrion requires a mortal co-custodian with actual authority.

### Settle Vaudren first

Ask Othalan to make a public concession before discussing his seal. This can unlock two Summer and Spring relationships at once, or make Maelith feel it has paid before receiving anything.

## How the Hart may leave the Gallery

### Answerability without subordination

Maelith declares on a precise form of personal surety that does not silently subordinate Orison and does not foreclose Vaudren's support.

### Bonds that bite

- A mortal envoy accepts fosterage and loses meaningful freedom for the duration.
- Lady Senra becomes reciprocal surety, binding Maelith strongly and exposing her to the expedition's dangers.
- Royal co-custody wins Maelith but gives the Crown a practical means to slow or redirect the undertaking.
- Maelith apologizes publicly and joins, while its followers demand senior credit elsewhere.

### The warm noun remains undefined

Accept the phrase protected guest without defining Maelith's powers. Ilyrion reacts as though wardship has been conceded, while Othalan still refuses to declare until the legal instrument is complete.

## Record before leaving

- the named person or office answering for the Sunseed;
- the powers attached to fosterage, protection, or hostage status;
- whether surety is reciprocal;
- which estates or banners guarantee return;
- the status of the Vaudren grievance; and
- whether Maelith expects senior authorship or accepts neutral credit.

## On a return

Every custody change reopens Maelith's bargain. Othalan is willing to exchange stronger personal security for less command, or weaker personal control for broader banner guarantees. He does not accept that a coalition's sincerity is itself an enforceable relation.
