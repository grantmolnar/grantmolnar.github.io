# The Silent Fir Lodge

**Role:** necessary  
**Tags:** winter, banner, border

Serevin releases Winter only when the eastern marches receive troops, warding, and a recall rule that can be measured.

## Opening View

Caldus Serevin does not greet you. A living wall drops one silver needle onto the eastern map, then another. Most lie in the old rings of musters, funerals, patrols. Three do not: scouts at Half-Light, siege beasts west of the valley, fires out of season. Beside them wait an unsigned ward contract, a reserve order, and a recall clause whose trigger is still blank.

## Encounter Material

## Situation

The Silent Fir Lodge stands at the eastern edge of the royal demesne. Its walls are living trunks cut with rings marking every recorded movement of the Gloam Host for two centuries. Fresh silver needles cross a relief map as reports arrive. Warden Caldus Serevin has placed every current report on the table before Orison's petitioners enter.

All of them describe movement. Some are musters, funerary marches, supply rotations, or patrols answering Aurelune's own concentration of force. Three are wrong for the season: scouts near the Half-Light Causeway, siege beasts moved one valley west, and repeated signal fires beyond the usual calendar.

Caldus does not claim to know that invasion is coming. He claims that an intelligent enemy can see the Dawnward thin and that the marches cannot be made to carry Orison's rescue without orders, troops, and a lawful trigger.

His opening sentence is true and weighted:

> Every eastern report describes movement.

The useful question is not whether he lied. It is which movement exceeds the baseline, which he judges hostile, and what authority he wants before either judgment can be tested.

## The actual border risk

The Dawnward does three things while the Sunseed remains beneath the throne.

- It obscures the timing of seasonal passages through the eastern passes.
- It strengthens six ward stations against shadow creatures and siege magic.
- It lets Serevin move a reserve between the Half-Light Causeway and the Fir March in hours rather than days.

Removing the Sunseed weakens all three. It does not open the border. The Gloam Host must still choose to attack, concentrate forces, and cross defended ground. A credible replacement package makes an opportunistic invasion expensive enough that Caldus can recommend the loan.

## The replacement package

Serevin divides the solution into four separable obligations.

### Substitute warding

Six portable wardstones can maintain the most important stations for thirteen days. Orelle can finance them. The stones do not preserve the seasonal passages and remain valuable property after the crisis.

### Eastern reserve

Serathiel can assign troops to the Half-Light Causeway under Serevin's local command. This answers the border need without granting the marshal command of the Orison expedition. Serathiel may demand credit or a stronger voice elsewhere in exchange.

### Allied deployment

Orison's allies can send troops directly. Those troops will not reach Orison in time for the central expedition if they are diverted east. Ilyrion accepts the deployment only if it is requested as allied burden sharing rather than imposed as tribute.

### Recall procedure

If an actual border breach occurs, Aurelune needs authority to protect itself. Caldus's preferred language lets him recall the Sunseed upon his own certified finding. He will accept less unilateral alternatives if they can act quickly.

## Recall models

### Unilateral recall

Serevin declares a breach and orders immediate return. This is fastest and can doom Orison on evidence no other custodian has reviewed.

### Objective trigger

The petition defines a breach through observable conditions: an organized hostile force crosses the ward line, two stations fail or are bypassed, and the eastern reserve cannot contain the advance. Serevin certifies the facts; the recall follows automatically. Namaris and Thalan prefer this model.

### Recall panel

Serevin presents the evidence to a royal custodian and an expedition custodian. Two of three may recall. Eiral can chair communications and resolve whether the trigger has been met. This is slower and more governable.

### Emergency suspension

Serevin may order the Sunseed held ready for return for a fixed number of hours while evidence is confirmed. The expedition loses time but not necessarily the undertaking. This route is useful when reports are serious and incomplete.

### Reinforcement before recall

The security covenant commits additional troops or ward power automatically when defined warning signs appear. Recall becomes the final step rather than the first.

Caldus accepts that **movement** cannot itself be the trigger. He insists that a plan with no response to genuine invasion is not a plan.

## House Serevin's seal

Serevin can support through several combinations.

- substitute wards plus an eastern reserve and an objective recall trigger;
- a shorter term plus a smaller reserve and a recall panel;
- allied troops under Serevin command with no unilateral recall;
- a fully funded Dawnward package with emergency suspension rather than recall; or
- Caelir's voluntary joinder, if the king assumes responsibility for reinforcing the marches.

The house's red line is not lack of perfect safety. It is making the eastern border absorb the risk without resources or lawful authority.

## Coalition consequences

### Serathiel

Putting the reserve under Serevin command allows Ysandre to yield expedition command while still claiming military success. Putting all forces under Serathiel may win Summer support and lose Serevin's trust.

### Orelle

The ward package can be translated into measurable performance, duration, and delivery terms. Orelle finances it as a bounded contract rather than an unlimited guarantee.

### Thalan

A sober record of both Orison's danger and Aurelune's exposure strengthens the necessity finding. Exaggerating the Gloam Host's present intent weakens Thalan's confidence in the whole petition.

### Eiral

A multi-party recall mechanism demonstrates governability. A unilateral power held by a house already opposed to the loan makes Eiral fear that the coalition cannot carry out what it authorizes.

### The Crown

Caelir proposes a six-day term as the cleanest answer to border risk. Caldus agrees that six days is safer for Aurelune and states that it may be operationally insufficient for Orison. The offer is a real loan structured to place the risk of delay on the city being rescued.

## Orders the border needs

### Adopt the threat baseline

Ask Caldus to sign a schedule separating ordinary movement, warning signs, and actual breach conditions. This limits later alarmism and also binds the expedition to act when the defined signs occur.

### Allocate troops

Forces assigned east cannot simultaneously escort the Sunseed, hold Orison's perimeter, or operate the evacuation gates. The adventurers choose where scarcity falls.

### Choose the recall model

Speed, evidence, and shared authority cannot all be maximized. The chosen model will become one of the petition's most dangerous clauses.

### Fund the package

Wardstones and reserve orders require Orelle or another guarantor. A promise to fund them later does not move a single soldier before the Dawnward weakens.

## How the Fir may leave the watch

### A defended march and a usable Sunseed

Secure a signed baseline, a funded replacement package, a named eastern commander, and a recall mechanism that answers invasion without allowing ordinary movement to terminate the undertaking.

### Security purchased with flexibility

- Allied troops defend Aurelune instead of accompanying the expedition to Orison.
- Serevin yields unilateral recall only after gaining local command over Serathiel's reserve.
- The petitioners accept an emergency suspension that may consume crucial hours at the Pall.
- The term shortens because the coalition declines to fund all six wardstones.

### Alarm retains the recall power

Dismiss the border risk as a pretext. Caldus remains opposed, publishes the complete reports without a shared baseline, and allows the king to describe the petition as a plan that asks the marches to trust optimism.

## Enter in the watch record

- the signed threat baseline;
- the number and funding of substitute wardstones;
- the size, source, and commander of the eastern reserve;
- the objective warning and breach conditions;
- the recall or suspension mechanism; and
- which forces are no longer available for Orison.

## On a return

The Lodge changes whenever the term, Dawnward package, or Gloam Host posture changes. Caldus does not invent new threats to reopen a bargain. He does use every genuine movement to test whether the coalition still accepts the risk it asked him to bear.
