# The Noon Spear Court

**Role:** necessary  
**Tags:** summer, banner, command

Serathiel puts command, eastern defense, and the loan term on one war table and asks which orders remain executable.

## Opening View

A brass company slides one valley east. Three hands reach for it; Ysandre's spear-butt strikes the glass before any can claim command. One baton points toward Orison, one to the eastern marches, one across the Crown Passage. Beyond them, wardstones remain unbought and reserves unordered. "The Sunseed can save Orison," Ysandre says. "Now tell me which realm goes undefended while it does."

## Encounter Material

## Situation

The Noon Spear Court is a parade ground beneath a sky fixed at high summer. Maps lie under glass in the paving stones. Brass companies move across the eastern marches, the roads to Orison, and the dark ring of the Pall as messengers alter the estimates. No one contests the distances. The dispute is over whose hand may move the pieces.

Marshal Ysandre Serathiel grants Orison's premise before anyone can accuse her of hiding behind doubt:

> The Sunseed can save Orison. An uncommanded Sunseed can lose two kingdoms.

Ysandre wants one expedition commander with authority over route, protection, tempo, and return. She prefers herself or a royal officer. She also wants soldiers, wards, or allies placed on the eastern border before the Sunseed leaves.

The danger is real. The Dawnward will thin. The Gloam Host will see it. A thirteen-day expedition needs guards, engineers, transport, reserve plans, and an answer for the hour when the road and the border both fail. Serathiel's deception lies elsewhere: she presents the assumptions favorable to her command as though they arrived with the terrain.

## House Serathiel

- **Marshal Ysandre Serathiel** hears every promise as an order missing authority, supply, or contingency.
- **Captain Naevra Sol** commands the Sunlance Guard and can guard the Crown's interest without commanding the entire expedition.
- **Strategist Corven Ashe** has prepared several workable divisions of authority. He begins with the one Ysandre prefers.
- **Envoy Mara Venn** can say what Orison will accept from an allied commander and what it will treat as foreign rule.

Ysandre deceives by choosing the conditions under which only her solution works. No new allied troops. No substitute ward. No divided command. No shorter term. Under those assumptions, her conclusion is often impeccable. Ask for the assumptions and the other war plans appear.

## What the Noon Spear must recognize

Serathiel needs three functions filled:

1. one coherent expedition command;
2. a reserve, substitute ward, or allied force protecting Aurelune; and
3. an answer to delay, route change, and a genuine border emergency.

House Serathiel requires simultaneous dangers to produce orders, not a committee in which every member discovers a veto.

## Command structures on the table

### Eladrin expedition command

Serathiel or a royal officer commands the whole undertaking. This is militarily simple, wins several conservative houses, and risks turning Orison's rescue into an intervention governed from Aurelune.

### Divided command by purpose

Command movement and use of the Sunseed. Captain Naevra commands its guard. Orison's envoy controls consent to any change in the named undertaking. The arrangement can work if disputes have a defined arbiter and no role can veto ordinary action by silence.

### Purpose-limited royal veto

A royal co-custodian may stop actions that would damage the Sunseed, exceed the undertaking, or violate an objective recall trigger. This preserves a royal interest without giving the Crown general command.

### Replacement reserve

Mortal allies, another Eladrin house, or hired fey troops reinforce the eastern marches. Serathiel accepts troops under Serevin's border command even if she does not command the expedition.

### Substitute Dawnward

Orelle finances temporary wards, Namaris defines performance, and Serevin identifies the vulnerable crossings. This is expensive and can satisfy Serathiel without moving large formations.

### Shorter term

A six-day loan sharply reduces risk to Aurelune. It may be operationally insufficient for Orison. Serathiel will not describe the shorter term as sufficient unless asked the exact probability of success.

## The war-table test

Serathiel invites the adventurers to issue orders for three simultaneous events:

- the expedition road divides around a Shadowfell incursion;
- the Dawnward reports a confirmed breach; and
- Orison's co-custodian refuses a route that places the Sunseed under exclusive royal control.

The exercise records whose authority the adventurers expect to prevail. Each answer becomes a draft command principle that other houses may quote.

Refuse the staged crisis and demand every written alternative. Corven produces five command schemes, including two withheld from the opening exercise. Ysandre respects an answer that survives the table.

## Ways to turn the Summer spear

### Give Serathiel command

The house declares immediately and provides a complete escort. Ilyrion and Orison demand purpose limits; Vaudren may resent military authorship.

### Build divided command

Distinguish custody, protection, operational command, and political consent. This can support a broad coalition and creates more points at which a crisis can deadlock.

### Secure the border first

Bring Serevin, Orelle, or allied commanders into the Court and close the military objection materially. Serathiel then bargains over expedition command rather than existential risk.

### Accept a short term with extension machinery

The petition begins with six days and permits extension by several custodians if objective milestones are met. Thalan approves the restraint. Orison bears the danger of delay.

### Reject command as a price of compassion

Serathiel remains opposed but may provide technical assistance. A coalition can win without this Summer banner; the king's charge of military irresponsibility becomes stronger.

## How the Spear may leave the table

### Orders everyone can obey

Serathiel declares on a command scheme that protects Aurelune without giving any one actor the practical power to defeat the undertaking for unrelated reasons.

### Command with a price

- Exclusive command wins the seal and narrows Orison's independence.
- Divided command preserves politics but requires an arbiter, delay rule, and deadlock procedure.
- Substitute wards protect Aurelune but create debt, audit, and maintenance obligations.
- A shorter term wins conservative support and makes the expedition's margin thin.

### Everyone can stop the expedition

Promise everyone a veto over the dangers they fear. Serathiel demonstrates that the Sunseed could remain motionless while every actor truthfully waits for another's consent. No seal is declared until the command text is repaired.

## Write into the campaign order

- the named expedition commander;
- the distinction between custody, guard command, political consent, and operational authority;
- the eastern reserve or substitute ward package;
- the response to an objective border breach;
- the initial term and any extension rule; and
- whether the war-table principles became public.

## On a return

Serathiel is easiest to revisit after Serevin or Orelle has put troops, wards, and payment on the table. She may surrender the baton itself; she will not accept a plan whose orders disappear when two dangers arrive at once.
