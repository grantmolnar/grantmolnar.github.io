## The trust beneath his throne

Caelir III is King of Aurelune and the Sunseed's royal guardian. He accepts that the relic can restore Orison's day. His opposition is to surrendering the trust, not to saving lives: uprooting the Sunseed weakens the Dawnward, disturbs the seasonal roads, and creates a precedent every neighboring realm may invoke.

## Six days under the Crown

Caelir can open royal gates and passages, commission officers, fund evacuation, file a Crown instrument, and commit the Crown to exact custody, command, liability, recall, and return duties. He prefers a six-day expedition under exclusive Crown custody and command, with evacuation and lesser relics as fallbacks. That plan is genuine relief and a political rival to Orison's petition.

## What seven banners can compel

Caelir cannot veto a valid Concord of Open Hands. Seven seasonally balanced Great Banners may compel joint custody once Theron certifies one coherent instrument that preserves a royal interest. Caelir may join after certification; his joinder improves cooperation but does not create validity. If compelled, he honors the text, interprets ambiguity in favor of the Crown and Aurelune, and withholds generosity rather than performance. The Oath of the Unbroken Word forbids knowing falsehood, not omission, narrow scope, or exact distinctions between rescue, custody, command, and consent.
