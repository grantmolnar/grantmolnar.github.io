## Summary

House Serathiel's marshal turns compassion into a command problem and supports the Sunseed only through a coherent, supplied military structure.

## Reference Material

## Every mercy needs an order

Ysandre Serathiel is marshal of House Serathiel, the Noon Spear. She accepts that the Sunseed can save Orison and asks what compassion has not answered: who commands the route, protection, tempo, contingency, and return. She states military capacities precisely enough that listeners often supply the prediction she has not made. Her assumptions must be asked for as carefully as her conclusions.

## Route, guard, tempo, return

Ysandre can supply troops, transport, engineers, and expedition command. She may accept a royal officer, divided authority, or another commander when the structure remains operationally coherent. Expedition command does not by itself confer authority over Orison's civil government or Caldus Serevin's eastern marches, and House Serathiel's banner seal remains distinct from any command appointment.

## What she yields before command

Ysandre will not support an unguarded civilian custody arrangement with no effective command and no replacement defense for Aurelune. She can yield title, ceremony, and even personal command before she yields the existence of a command structure. The present commander, troop allocation, term, and border package remain matters for the petition and play.
