# Orison

## Summary

The mortal city beneath the Pall is the endangered polity whose sky, civil agency, and political future the Concord may preserve or redefine.

## Reference Material

## A city under a failing sky

Orison is the city enclosed by the Pall. Its defenders drove away the Shadowfell host that began the siege-work, but much of the surviving government and population remains under a failing sky. The surviving council sealed an emergency commission authorizing its envoy and petitioners to seek the Sunseed; that commission does not predefine a fresh delegation's identities, offices, route, or coalition.

## Standing without wardship

The Lantern Road Compact secures Orison an emergency audience and access to a Crown Passage. It does not by itself make Orison Aurelune's treaty equal. Orison retains civil authority over its people and may accept or reject the undertaking and any redirection imposed in its name. Expedition command can govern the operation without governing the city, and protection does not make Orison a ward unless exact terms do so.

## Which city survives the rescue

Evacuating citizens, breaking the Pall, restoring natural day, preserving the city, and rebuilding its institutions are different undertakings. The Sunseed can restore Orison's sky, while collateral, trade privileges, fosterage, military authority, and public credit can determine what polity survives the rescue. Those choices belong to the active petition rather than this record.
