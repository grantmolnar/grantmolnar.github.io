# Reference Index: The Concord of Aurelune

## People

- [**Theron Eiral**](97bd3a99-1a45-45ad-9d0d-ffd1418e6db9.md) — The Lord Chamberlain must certify a lawful Concord while House Eiral's banner remains a separate political choice. Aliases: Lord Chamberlain Theron Eiral, Chamberlain Theron.
- [**King Caelir III**](e6b95d47-51e1-4be3-9ee3-901d903e1254.md) — Aurelune's king guards the Sunseed, builds a genuine Crown-led rescue, and must honor any valid Concord he cannot prevent. Aliases: Caelir III, Caelir.
- [**Envoy Mara Venn**](9b4e0d4c-ff0d-4ab6-a045-1467020b73df.md) — Orison's civil envoy carries the emergency mandate, proves the Sunseed remedy, and guards the city's consent inside any rescue instrument. Aliases: Mara Venn, Mara.
- [**Marshal Ysandre Serathiel**](2c1e9617-655b-47d5-adca-d8710d016456.md) — House Serathiel's marshal turns compassion into a command problem and supports the Sunseed only through a coherent, supplied military structure. Aliases: Ysandre Serathiel, Ysandre, Marshal Serathiel.
- [**Comptroller Mirelle Orelle**](51f114d4-1818-4465-95be-3124ec3faa39.md) — House Orelle's comptroller makes every rescue cost name its bearer and separates genuine loss from capital and acquired advantage. Aliases: Mirelle Orelle, Mirelle, Comptroller Orelle.
- [**Archivist-Lord Pelion Namaris**](9f9b61d5-545c-4a2d-875a-283c191a4594.md) — Aurelune's archivist-lord tests the Sunseed petition against precedent, exact drafting, audit, and the rule a later hostile court might claim. Aliases: Pelion Namaris, Pelion, Archivist-Lord Namaris.
- [**Warden Caldus Serevin**](39900332-f46e-4c17-9c27-4ef56995f6ac.md) — The warden of Aurelune's eastern marches demands a measurable defense and lawful response before the Sunseed weakens the Dawnward. Aliases: Caldus Serevin, Caldus, Warden Serevin.

## Places

- [**Orison**](ef010fba-64a6-4f48-b912-c6a1867db6f8.md) — The mortal city beneath the Pall is the endangered polity whose sky, civil agency, and political future the Concord may preserve or redefine. Aliases: the city of Orison.

## Organizations

- [**The Ashen Bough**](7b0624aa-9728-4a8f-aa8f-5c70e68b5560.md) — House Avarre's vacant, unseasoned Great Banner may become a tenth vote only through a lawful judgment that preserves the distinct claims of Mirath Avarre and Aven Tal. Aliases: House Avarre, the Ashen seat.

## Objects

- [**The Sunseed**](eb53de5a-93b2-4383-bd1a-0adbd6fb0b93.md) — A royal relic whose remembered daylight can restore Orison if its root-casket enters the Crown Passage before moonrise after Court Day Seven. Aliases: Aurelune's Sunseed.

## Other

- [**The Pall**](f837f83d-51fd-43ea-872c-44d96ba9beb7.md) — A self-sustaining Shadowfell siege-work is replacing Orison's daylight, memory, and seasons and will close the last usable road after Court Day Seven. Aliases: the Pall of Night.
- [**The Concord of Open Hands**](f92c1071-7366-4f0b-b3e1-298d3e2d2a45.md) — Aurelune's constitutional path lets seven seasonally balanced Great Banners compel joint custody of a royal relic through one certified, governable undertaking. Aliases: the Concord, Open Hands.
- [**The Oath of the Unbroken Word**](d91a965f-f0d9-42dc-9257-f2f430f81a1f.md) — Aurelune's court oath forbids knowing falsehood and bad-faith sealing while leaving omission, narrow scope, and selective context as political weapons. Aliases: the Unbroken Word, the truth oath.
- [**The Lantern Road Compact**](6d94f652-9012-4baa-a80b-dc9c474c30bc.md) — Orison's compact guarantees an emergency audience and access to a Crown Passage, but not treaty equality, custody, or command of the rescue. Aliases: Lantern Road compact, Orison's compact.
