# The Sunseed

**Reference ID:** `eb53de5a-93b2-4383-bd1a-0adbd6fb0b93`  
**Kind:** Object  
**Aliases:** Aurelune's Sunseed  
**Tags:** royal relic, deadline, custody

## Summary

A royal relic whose remembered daylight can restore Orison if its root-casket enters the Crown Passage before moonrise after Court Day Seven.

## Reference Material

## Dawn beneath the throne

The Sunseed burns inside a clear glass root beneath King Caelir III's throne and lays the same mild dawn across the Argent Canopy. A paving stone from Orison's noon market warms beneath it, sheds the Pall's black frost, and casts its former shadow. Aurelune's Dawnward keepers have repeated the test: the remedy is demonstrated, not speculative.

## The road closes before custody begins

The root-casket must enter the Crown Passage before moonrise after Court Day Seven, before the Pall closes the last road to its center. The custody term begins only when the Sunseed is uprooted, so the passage deadline and the negotiated loan term are different clocks.

## What uprooting places at risk

Uprooting the Sunseed thins the Dawnward and disturbs Aurelune's seasonal roads. Crown ownership can survive joint possession and purpose-limited use, but any valid instrument must preserve a royal interest and state exact custody, command, term, liability, recall, and return.
