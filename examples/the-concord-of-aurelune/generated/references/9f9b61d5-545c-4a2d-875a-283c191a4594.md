## Reference Material

## The later case waits in the index

Pelion Namaris is archivist-lord of House Namaris, the Amber Quill. He asks not only which precedent governs, but which later judgment limits it and which sentence an unfriendly court may quote a century later. The Oath permits him to select an accurate controlling passage without volunteering every distinction; asking for the limiting record is part of dealing with him.

## What the amber quills can separate

Pelion can retrieve precedent, expose drafting defects, define audit and review procedures, separate title from custody and use, and stabilize return, amendment, and sunset language. He can test whether associated covenants remain public, enforceable, and compatible with the central petition. He does not certify the Concord—that duty belongs to Theron Eiral—and his preferred precedent does not become law merely because he supplied it.

## Words that survive their authors

Pelion will not support operative language whose effect depends on goodwill after the vote. He accepts narrow emergency findings, relic-specific rules, later review, and carefully indexed side covenants. The current draft, cited authorities, audit appointments, and House Namaris seal remain changing court state.
