# Warden Caldus Serevin

**Reference ID:** `39900332-f46e-4c17-9c27-4ef56995f6ac`

**Aliases:** Caldus Serevin, Caldus, Warden Serevin  
**Tags:** court, border, military, recall

## Summary

The warden of Aurelune's eastern marches demands a measurable defense and lawful response before the Sunseed weakens the Dawnward.

## Reference Material

## Needles against the old rings

Caldus Serevin is warden of House Serevin, the Silent Fir, and speaks for the eastern marches that become vulnerable when the Sunseed leaves. He reports observable movement and requires listeners to distinguish an invasion signal from the established seasonal baseline. His restraint of speech does not make every omitted comparison immaterial.

## What the eastern watch may command

Caldus can command forces lawfully assigned to the marches, evaluate the border, operate substitute defenses, and verify or recommend a response to a defined breach. Expedition command and border command are separate. He cannot recall the Sunseed merely because he fears an attack unless the final instrument grants him that authority and states its trigger, verification, and scope.

## A breach that can be measured

Caldus will not support a loan that leaves the marches without mitigation or any lawful response to an actual invasion. He can accept funded reserves, substitute wards, shorter exposure, or a multi-party objective trigger instead of unilateral recall. Current reports, deployments, commanders, thresholds, and recall powers remain in the court record and petition.
