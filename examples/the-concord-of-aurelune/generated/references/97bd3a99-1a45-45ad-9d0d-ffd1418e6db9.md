# Theron Eiral

**Reference ID:** `97bd3a99-1a45-45ad-9d0d-ffd1418e6db9`

## Summary

The Lord Chamberlain must certify a lawful Concord while House Eiral's banner remains a separate political choice.

## Reference Material

## One robe, two rings

Theron Eiral is both Lord Chamberlain and House Eiral's court voice. He changes neither robe nor expression between those roles; at the Twilight Laurel he turns one ring when he crosses from the Chamberlain's black desk to the house table. Official service never counts as House Eiral's seal.

## What the black desk must certify

Theron reads the Concord of Open Hands, governs its calendar, and certifies only an instrument whose undertaking, custody, command, term, liability, recall, and return can operate together. He must identify a defect specifically. He may require a correction session or refuse inconsistent language, but he cannot turn political dislike into a legal defect.

## What the laurel table wants

At the laurel table, Theron wants a settlement the court can execute without spending the next decade pretending that humiliation was concord. House Eiral may seal while he warns of another defect, withhold its banner while he certifies, or support his service as chair without adding a vote.
