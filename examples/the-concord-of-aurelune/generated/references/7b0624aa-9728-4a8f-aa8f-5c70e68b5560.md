# The Ashen Bough

## Summary

House Avarre's vacant, unseasoned Great Banner may become a tenth vote only through a lawful judgment that preserves the distinct claims of Mirath Avarre and Aven Tal.

## Reference Material

## One empty chair, two true claims

The Ashen Bough is House Avarre's vacant Great Banner and counts for no season. Vaelor Avarre died childless by blood. His elder sister, Lady Mirath Avarre, is the nearest dynast and served three times as his military regent. Vaelor also executed a valid sealed adoption of the mortal-born Aven Tal, making Aven his child for household, estate, and personal obligations, but died before presenting the adoption to the Crown as a transfer of the banner. No controlling judgment decides whether adoption alone carries that public office.

## Six banners, five banners, or none

A final succession judgment requires six of the nine sitting banners, including two seasons. A temporary emergency regency requires five and may be limited to the Sunseed undertaking. The court may instead divide household duties without a vote or leave the seat vacant. Any bearer or regent may cast only the authority the judgment actually grants; a changed undertaking, unauthorized covenant, or term beyond that authority may require renewed judgment.

## What judgment may and may not settle

Mirath's claim concerns public office, blood continuity, and proven capacity. Aven's concerns the obligations created by chosen kinship and valid adoption. Neither claim is fraudulent merely because it leaves the other legal noun unresolved. The current holder, regency, doctrine, seal, support, and later succession outcome remain products of play, and both claimants remain political actors after judgment.
