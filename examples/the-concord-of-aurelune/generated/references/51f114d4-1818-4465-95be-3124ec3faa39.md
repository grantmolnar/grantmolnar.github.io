## Reference Material

## Loss, Capital, Advantage

Mirelle Orelle is comptroller of House Orelle, the Golden Sheaf. She prices the Sunseed undertaking through three public ledgers: Loss for what is consumed, Capital for assets that retain value, and Advantage for monopolies, access, ownership, and political reach acquired as compensation. Her totals are correct inside the categories she has chosen; the useful question is which category made the total possible.

## What her ledgers make executable

Mirelle can finance replacement wards, transport, reserves, insurance, and reconstruction, and can structure capped guarantees among several bearers. She can make material promises executable and auditable. She cannot make reusable Orelle property into an unrecoverable public loss by naming it so, bind Orison to privileges it has not accepted, or turn House Orelle's seal into the source of every financial function.

## Every bearer and benefit named

Mirelle will not accept unlimited Aurelune liability paired with complete immunity for Orison. She does accept shared risk, fault-based liability, returned reserves, ordinary contracts, and narrow compensation when each bearer and benefit is named. The current valuation, guarantors, collateral, privileges, and final bargain remain in the ledgers and petition.
