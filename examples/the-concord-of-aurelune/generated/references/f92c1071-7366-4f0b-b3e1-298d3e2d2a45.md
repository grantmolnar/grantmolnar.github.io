# The Concord of Open Hands

**Tags:** constitutional law, petition, custody

## Summary

Aurelune's constitutional path lets seven seasonally balanced Great Banners compel joint custody of a royal relic through one certified, governable undertaking.

## Reference Material

## Seven seals and every season

The Concord of Open Hands permits seven of Aurelune's ten Great Banners to compel the Crown to place a royal relic in joint custody for one named undertaking. Spring, Summer, Autumn, and Winter must all be represented. The exact petition must name a maximum term, preserve a royal interest, and state custody, command, liability, recall, return, and the undertaking it serves.

## Seal, certification, acceptance, joinder

Banner seals supply the lawful majority. The Lord Chamberlain certifies that one internally consistent instrument was lawfully adopted; Theron's certification and House Eiral's political seal are separate acts. Orison's acceptance does not replace banner seals. The king cannot veto a valid Concord and may join only after certification; royal joinder improves cooperation without creating validity.

## What a changed word releases

A material amendment releases every seal whose stated ground depends on the changed text. Corrected exact language may be reaffirmed in the same sitting. Associated covenants may carry recognition, finance, surety, deployment, or credit when they remain public, enforceable, and compatible with the central petition. The Concord creates no general right to every royal relic unless the enacted text actually does so.
