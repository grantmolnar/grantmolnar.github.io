# The Pall

**Reference ID:** `f837f83d-51fd-43ea-872c-44d96ba9beb7`  
**Kind:** Other  
**Aliases:** the Pall of Night  
**Tags:** Shadowfell, deadline, siege-work

## Summary

A self-sustaining Shadowfell siege-work is replacing Orison's daylight, memory, and seasons and will close the last usable road after Court Day Seven.

## Reference Material

## Dusk after the host has gone

The Pall is the Shadowfell siege-work raised over Orison. The host that kindled it was driven away, but the work continued without its makers, replacing daylight, remembered weather, and seasonal change with permanent dusk. Ordinary forces can hold parts of the perimeter and evacuate some people; they cannot preserve the city intact.

## The ring closes at moonrise

The Pall completed its outer ring on the night before Aurelune's opening audience. At moonrise after Court Day Seven it closes the Lantern Road, erases the remaining planar approaches, and begins sustaining itself. The Crown Passage can still place the Sunseed's root-casket at Orison's western gate before that closure, and one road from the gate still reaches the Pall's center. This passage deadline is distinct from the custody term, which begins when the Sunseed is uprooted.

## Which rescue the Sunseed performs

A stone from Orison's noon market remembers warmth and shadow beneath the Sunseed, demonstrating the remedy. Properly borne and planted at the Pall's center, the relic can force the blighted region to remember its rightful sky. Breaking the siege-work, restoring natural day, preserving Orison, and evacuating its people remain legally and operationally different possible undertakings.
