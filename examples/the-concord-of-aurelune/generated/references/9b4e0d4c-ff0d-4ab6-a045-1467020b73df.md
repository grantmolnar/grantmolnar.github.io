## Summary

## Reference Material

## A sealed commission and a black-frosted stone

Mara Venn is Orison's civil envoy beside the adventurers. She carries the surviving council's sealed emergency commission and brought the noon-market paving stone whose remembered warmth and shadow demonstrate that the Sunseed can restore Orison's day.

## What Orison may accept

Mara states what Orison will accept from allied custodians and commanders, speaks wherever the emergency mandate gives the city standing, and may accept or reject the undertaking named in the final instrument. She can require Orison's consent before the undertaking is redirected while leaving ordinary execution to an agreed commander. Her acceptance does not replace seven banner seals, grant treaty equality by itself, or define the adventurers' identities, offices, coalition, or route.

## The promise a changed word releases

Mara wants a rescue Orison can govern rather than relief that turns the city into a ward or places its civil authority under foreign command. She distrusts promises the besieged government cannot perform while much of it remains underground. Personal surety, fosterage, liability, and consultation bind her only through exact terms she accepts; none follows automatically from her office. At the drafting table she asks which promise a changed word has released.
