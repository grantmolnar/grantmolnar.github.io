## Summary

Orison's compact guarantees an emergency audience and access to a Crown Passage, but not treaty equality, custody, or command of the rescue.

## Reference Material

## An audience and one Crown Passage

The Lantern Road Compact between Orison and Aurelune guarantees Orison an emergency audience and access to a Crown Passage. Together with the surviving council's sealed commission, it gives Orison recognized standing to petition during the Pall emergency. It does not itself decide whether the city enters as an independent polity, royal guest, or jointly sponsored petitioner.

## Standing is not sovereignty

The compact does not recognize Orison as Aurelune's treaty equal, transfer custody of the Sunseed, appoint an expedition commander, or authorize the Crown to govern Orison. Those questions require the Concord petition, Orison's acceptance, or separate instruments. Passage access and political status remain distinct authorities.

## The road closes before the loan begins

The Crown Passage can carry the root-casket to Orison's western gate before moonrise after Court Day Seven. One road from that gate still remembers the way to the Pall's center. Once the Pall closes the Lantern Road and begins sustaining itself, the passage cannot cross the ring. This travel deadline does not start or shorten the custody term, which begins only when the Sunseed is uprooted.
