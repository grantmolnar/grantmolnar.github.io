# Heist Ledger: The Cauldron of Nine Silences

## Objective

Before the fifth bell, prevent Lord Mael Taran from burning the cauldron's nine accumulated silences in the Great Muster. Preserve the cauldron intact for King Branoc's first Full Asking, or conduct that Asking inside Dunwarren before the power is spent.

The coalition's agreed first question is:

> What command did you give Edric Taran at the western gate?

The preferred result is intact extraction or witnessed seizure. Immediate Full Asking, temporary sabotage, partial bargain, and destruction remain valid but materially different outcomes.

## Why now

- Red Fen broke the levy screen five weeks ago and forced emergency underpinning of Dunwarren's western gate.
- Those works exposed Branoc's sealed command chamber three weeks ago.
- Efa Rill recovered Branoc's oath-ring before Mael closed the excavation.
- The ring is the first true personal anchor found since Branoc's death; he has never been summoned.
- Gwyne offered a three-witness chapel authentication; Mael demanded sole emergency custody and searched the excavation workers before any hearing.
- The household memorial rolls brought for renewal are being chained into Mael's Great Muster war register.
- At the fifth bell, the Muster begins consuming the power needed for a whole Full Asking.
- At dawn, after the Muster's answer is presented, the nobles renew the Gate Compact under Branoc's alleged final command.
- Gwyne notified Mere House because extraction required a keeper and destination; Mother Eluned rejected the timing and sent the Green Larks independently.

## Heat

**0 — Unmarked.** The crew's covers and movement attract no coordinated attention.

**1 — Noted.** One named witness remembers a face, cover, item, claimed task, or lead box. Local questioning begins.

**2 — Traced.** Two regions share a description. Guards verify invitations, tally cords, roof authority, chapel business, or excavation connections.

**3 — Hunted.** Captain Vane coordinates the living guard against a specific crew. Routes remain open through speed, leverage, split action, public testimony, or faction help.

**4 — Fort sealed.** Gates close, messages change, and Mael advances the Great Muster. The feast continues under guard; the heist becomes an extraction or witnessed seizure under public crisis rather than an automatic failure.

Heat rises for conspicuous contradictions, harmed household staff, fires, dead guards, exposed tools, displaying Branoc's ring without controlling the witnesses, or leaving a witness time to report. It can fall through a credible scapegoat, altered records, Lady Aderyn's authority, Nessa Pike's protection, a Green Lark disturbance, or a public revelation that redirects security toward the ritual dispute.

## Underkeep operating summary

### Barrow Stair

- **Name** compares one dead person's name to an anchor or memorial authority. Mael's warrant tongue suppresses the singular requirement and substitutes the war roll.
- **Asking** records one exact first question. It does not judge whether the wording is fair; the dead may later refuse or correct it.
- **Keeping** records keeper, destination, and authority. False declarations can pass formally but leak silence when later action contradicts them.
- Ash bypasses Name; the roof shaft bypasses Name and may bypass Asking; the counterweight gallery can alter all three at the cost of machinery and noise.
- Breaking a landing opens movement but converts a later procedural advantage into an extraction complication.

### Borrowed command network

- Five map objectives: vessel, anchor, war roll, intruder, witnesses.
- Six living posts: White Hart Gate, East Passage, Rook Gallery, Chapel Watch, Drowned Court, Reed Weir.
- Four shutters: white verification, red seizure, blue machinery, black waiting ambush.
- One voice prompts verification; two agreeing voices prompt action; disagreement makes a post hold for Vane; Mael's override treats one voice as two.
- Command collars and shutters are physically swappable.
- A whole name, credible recovered reason, and inward return cock allow one answer to qualify or retract its command and cease active service.

## Target facts currently fixed

- Black-bronze cauldron, broad enough to receive a person's reflected presence.
- Nine ritual bands preserve Face, Road, Measure, Hearth, Hand, Season, Bar, Reply, and Rest.
- Four separate custody dogs secure the oak cradle and can be released without opening a ritual band.
- A band raised to its arrest is not yet spent; commitment occurs when its matching comb enters water under spoken names.
- The full rite requires anchor, whole name, honest first question, willing witnesses, dead consent, and named custody.
- The proper Full Asking returns the silences when the dead voluntarily departs.
- Branoc has never been summoned, and his oath-ring is the first viable personal anchor.
- Vessel and cradle must leave together if the Full Asking is to remain whole.
- Movement wakes the twelve borrowed voices through copper channels.
- A living custodian must name themselves, a destination, and authority at consecrated thresholds.
- Exact testimony appears in three black-water copies marked by the bands present.
- Mael's Great Muster preserves expertise while severing the dead from the reasons behind it.
- Committed silence requires communal restoration over years or generations rather than metal repair.

## Failure-forward anchors

- Vessel recaptured, testimony escapes: one leaf and two witnesses stay the oath until noon.
- Vessel escapes, ring seized: Muster stopped; Branoc unavailable until anchor recovery.
- Ring escapes, vessel recaptured: the first opportunity survives as leverage.
- Keeper captured: movement continues, but future Asking remains closed until custody is corrected.
- Punt grounds: tow, hide, reverse, bargain, or call on Lark labor.
- Surrender can establish Night of Hearing, Vane custody, Mere House transfer, or a noon hearing.
