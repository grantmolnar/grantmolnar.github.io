# Vault Operations: The Cauldron of Nine Silences

This sheet is the compact table reference for the target chamber. The Cauldron Vault encounter in `adventure.json` remains authoritative.

## Chamber zones

| Zone | Function | Immediate leverage |
|---|---|---|
| Witness steps | Three opposed copies of exact testimony | Credible audience, public record, objections without abridgment |
| War table | War roll, nine combs, question board, keys | Delay, substitution, evidence of coercive design |
| Keeper's tree | Four custody levers, route wheels, arrest bells, brake | Movement, joint custody, control of commitment interval |
| Central turntable | Cauldron, cradle, spring, four custody dogs | Target handling and route choice |
| North channel | Rookwalk lifting shaft | Vertical extraction; requires windlass and counterweight |
| East channel | Smoke Kitchen service rise | Covered extraction; requires capstan, team, or leverage |
| Southwest channel | Reed Weir descent | Fast tidal extraction; requires braking and correct tide |

## The nine bands

| Band | Faculty preserved | If committed before Branoc's Asking | Muster product |
|---|---|---|---|
| Face | Recognition and personal identity | Branoc may not recognize ring, Edric, kin, or himself beyond office | Anonymous facts grouped by craft or rank |
| Road | Memory in sequence | Events and clauses arrive out of order | Isolated routes, incidents, and remembered actions |
| Measure | Judgment and priority | Branoc recites words but cannot govern their interpretation | Ratios and choices without reasons |
| Hearth | Affection and obligation to persons | Tactical answer loses the people it was meant to protect | Efficient counsel detached from human cost |
| Hand | Self-command and authorship | Prompting can shape Branoc's wording | Answers easier to direct and summarize |
| Season | Understanding past and present | Branoc speaks as though the old battle is occurring now | Historical comparisons treated as current instruction |
| Bar | Refusal and withdrawal of consent | Branoc cannot decline or stop questioning | Unresisting access to later answers |
| Reply | Reciprocal speech | Branoc cannot question witnesses or correct framing | Literal answers to prepared questions |
| Rest | Voluntary departure and return of silences | Branoc cannot leave cleanly; presence becomes another custody burden | Persistent echoes that can be harvested again |

## Band states

| State | What happened | Sacred cost | Available response |
|---|---|---|---|
| Closed | Clasp seated | None | Full Asking or intact movement |
| Raised to arrest | Key turned; bell sounded; band a finger-width open | None yet | Reseat, jam, bargain, extract, invoke respite, begin Asking |
| Committed | Matching comb crossed water under spoken names | Faculty spent | Record loss, stop later bands, attempt diminished Asking |

Do not treat the arrest bell as automatic loss. The fifth-bell crisis culminates when the first comb touches water, not when Mael turns the key.

## Trial needles versus committed bands

The twelve borrowed answers were produced through hair-fine name needles inserted into inspection holes while Hearth, Hand, and Reply remained closed. Each trial prevented the nine faculties from joining and stole one thread of answer. The three bands are strained but still present.

A committed band is different in kind, not merely degree: the clasp rises, a full comb crosses water under many spoken names, and one entire faculty leaves the cauldron. Releasing a mask may ease the trial strain; it cannot restore a committed silence.

## Four custody dogs

The dogs are separate from the nine bands.

| Release method | Speed and noise | Later consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Keeper's levers | Quiet; requires recorded custody declaration | Cleanest threshold record |
| Square service keys | Quiet; keys held by Caddoc, Vane, steward, or founder authorization | Creates an accountable key trail |
| Remove oak shear pins | Slow and wet beneath cradle | Preserves machinery and ritual integrity |
| Tear from floor | Immediate and loud | Clear movement alarm; damaged braking on associated route |

## Full Asking checklist

1. Close route wheels and twelve speaking cocks.
2. Seat all available bands and name any committed faculty.
3. State keeper, destination, and future authority.
4. Place the true anchor in the spring cup, not in the water.
5. Speak private oath-name and public name together.
6. State the exact first question before invitation.
7. Ask whether the dead consents to attend for that question.
8. Receive the presence without assuming postmortem omniscience.
9. Preserve the complete exchange on three witness leaves.
10. Permit voluntary departure and confirm that the silences settle.

## Branoc's exact command

> Bar the great leaves against the riders. Keep the wicket and the river stair until the outer ward is within. If the wall cannot hold, leave Dunwarren and save the people. A fort is a vessel; the realm is those it shelters.

Branoc knows what he commanded. He died before learning exactly what Edric did afterward. His testimony therefore establishes command and governing priority, not every later motive or legal consequence.

## Diminished Asking guide

| Loss | Result |
|---|---|
| One band | Recognizable Branoc missing the named faculty |
| Two bands | Intermittent presence; accurate fragments with serious interpretive dispute |
| Three to five | Severed inquiry rather than the promised Full Asking |
| Six or more | Royal and military residue cannot be distinguished from Branoc as a moral agent |

The identity of the missing band matters more than the count. Loss of Bar or Hand directly compromises consent and authorship.

## Muster sabotage and substitution

| Intervention | When discovered | What it buys | What it does not solve |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shallow-cut replacement roll | First combing | Diagnostic delay at arrest | Permanent custody |
| Resin-filled or maintenance comb | First contact with water | Visible apparatus failure | A band left arrested indefinitely |
| Wrong-band comb | Checklist or water rejection | Conflict among Caddoc, Hew, and Mael | The underlying intent to continue |
| Key filed one tooth short | Key reaches arrest | Concrete reversible interval | Later replacement key |
| Question-board substitution | Public reading or officiant refusal | Political contradiction and narrower debate | Mael's ability to speak another question |
| Close or cross speaking cocks | Movement or alarm | Divided guard response | All detection of the moving target |

## Night of Hearing

Requirements:

- chapel witness: ordinarily Brother Caddoc;
- founder authority: Lady Aderyn with the founder's seal;
- military surety: Captain Vane or Marshal Hew accepting living-defense responsibility.

Effect:

- all bands seat;
- the keys enter joint custody;
- no use or removal occurs before dawn without all three authorities;
- Branoc's Asking may proceed before the renewal oath;
- permanent ownership remains unresolved.

## Mael's bargaining threshold

Mael can accept delay and joint custody if four living functions are preserved:

1. temporary levy coordination under an accepted noble;
2. immediate living intelligence and engineering council for Hew;
3. civil and household protection through dawn under Vane;
4. cauldron custody shared by Caddoc and at least one non-Taran keeper.

He may demand a contrary-bench seat, one non-leading military follow-up to Branoc, and a prohibition on foreign transfer. He will not accept an undefended morning merely to cleanse the old law.

## Restoration after commitment

| Faculty | Long restoration practice |
|---|---|
| Face | Restore erased names to graves and records |
| Road | Preserve whole lives rather than useful excerpts |
| Measure | Record reasons and priorities alongside verdicts |
| Hearth | Fulfill obligations to people the dead loved |
| Hand | Repudiate coercion and preserve unabridged speech |
| Season | Keep anniversaries without making the past govern unchanged |
| Bar | Honor refusals by those with less power |
| Reply | Answer the dead's unresolved questions through living action |
| Rest | Let remains, memories, and offices cease being instruments |
