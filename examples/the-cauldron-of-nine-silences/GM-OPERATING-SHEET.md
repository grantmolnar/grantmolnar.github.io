# GM Operating Sheet: The Cauldron of Nine Silences

This sheet condenses the adventure's moving parts for table use. The encounter prose in `adventure.json` remains authoritative. Track time, security posture, custody, ritual state, and the outcomes that can survive a failed extraction here.

## The causal spine

1. Red Fen forced the gate works that exposed Branoc's sealed chamber three weeks ago.
2. Branoc has never been summoned; his recovered oath-ring is the first viable personal anchor.
3. Gwyne offered a neutral three-witness authentication. Mael demanded sole custody and searched the excavation workers before any hearing.
4. The household memorial rolls brought for renewal now form the Great Muster's war register.
5. At the fifth bell Mael spends the nine accumulated silences; at dawn he presents the answer before the nobles renew the Compact under an incomplete recital of Branoc's command.
6. Gwyne's notice to Mere House produced a rival Trust claim and the Green Larks' third-bell intervention.
7. Sabotage may delay the Muster; intact theft, witnessed seizure, or an immediate Full Asking preserves Branoc's opportunity.

The agreed first question is:

> What command did you give Edric Taran at the western gate?

## Bell clock

| Bell | Public event | Security change | Heist consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Before first | Guests, supplies, and performers assemble | Checks are local and inconsistent | Crew selects assets and tests one route |
| First | White Hart gates open; kitchen count begins | Witness verification and worker tallies activate | Social and service covers become committed |
| Second | Mael joins the households' memorial rolls into the Great Muster register and announces dawn renewal | War-roll staff move apparatus; Vane expects disorder | Ritual deadline and political deadline become visibly one operation |
| Third | Green Larks enter under a court distraction | Rival crew takes one neglected asset or route | Tomas begins moving toward the receiving end |
| Fourth | Mael descends with war roll and keys | Band locks are prepared; command network fully active | Remaining preparation directly narrows extraction tide |
| Low tide | Reed Weir black lane opens | Weir post receives borrowed-voice orders | Forty-minute extraction window, best in middle twenty |
| Fifth | Great Muster begins unless prevented | Bands are raised and committed in sequence | Every committed silence diminishes future Askings |
| Dawn | Gate Compact renewal | Nobles swear, refuse, delay, or divide | One authenticated leaf and two witnesses stay oath until noon |

Advance pressure for meaningful delay, public exposure, major noise, failed cover, or an additional planning asset after the first bell. Do not advance it for ordinary conversation or every cautious action.

## Security posture

Track the fort's posture rather than a universal alert score.

| Posture | What the fort believes | Typical response |
|---|---|---|
| Routine | Feast disorder, local staffing errors | Verify seals, recount workers, question messengers |
| Suspicious | One unauthorized route or missing asset | Close one passage, send one patrol, check named witnesses |
| Divided | Several objectives or routes appear active | Posts hold and seek Vane; borrowed voices contradict one another |
| Royal fault | Branoc's ring or private oath-name is recognized | Protection, seizure, and witnessed-inquiry orders collide |
| Recovery | Cauldron, anchor, testimony, or keeper is moving | Barriers, chocks, rope seizure, living arrests, objective-specific pursuit |
| Public contest | Founder, chapel, Vane, Hew, or opposed witnesses are present | Force becomes politically expensive; bargains and bounded custody become possible |

Vane's standing order is always relevant:

> Hold the route, take the bearers living, preserve the water, and do not strike the cradle.

Mael can issue a black override, but guards must choose whether to obey it before witnesses.

Choose three without added pressure:

- genuine invitation for a dead knight;

A fourth asset costs time, creates a witness, or lets the Green Larks act first.

## Underkeep quick procedure

### Barrow Stair

Record three declarations:

1. **Name:** one dead person, anchor, and memorial authority.
2. **Asking:** one exact first question.
3. **Keeping:** living keeper, destination, and present authority.

The stair records declarations; it does not read hearts. A false or incomplete statement may pass formally and produce later silence leakage, contested testimony, or blocked future use.

Bypasses trade present access for later consequence:

- ash bypasses Name;
- roof shaft may bypass Name and Asking;
- counterweight gallery alters all three at the cost of machinery and noise;
- breaking a landing opens movement but removes later procedural leverage.

### Borrowed command network

Five objectives: vessel, anchor, war roll, intruder, witnesses.

Six posts: White Hart Gate, East Passage, Rook Gallery, Chapel Watch, Drowned Court, Reed Weir.

Four shutters: white verification, red seizure, blue machinery, black ambush.

- One voice prompts verification.
- Two agreeing voices prompt action.
- Disagreement makes a post hold for Vane.
- Mael's override counts one voice as two.

Ways to intervene:

- create multiple apparent objectives;
- cross command collars or shutters;
- present Branoc's ring to create royal fault;
- restore a whole name and missing reason;
- cool or block copper lines;
- turn the room into evidence before living authorities.

Restoring one answer changes its order; it does not summon the dead person or replenish a spent silence.

## Vault state

### Nine bands

| Band | Faculty preserved | Loss if committed |
|---|---|---|
| Face | Recognition and identity | Branoc cannot reliably recognize names, ring, or self |
| Road | Ordered memory | Facts arrive without sequence |
| Measure | Judgment | Clauses survive without governing priority |
| Hearth | Affection and human meaning | Tactical truth loses whom it was meant to protect |
| Hand | Self-command | Leading questions shape grammar and answer |
| Season | Temporal understanding | Past emergency is mistaken for present conditions |
| Bar | Refusal | Consent becomes empty |
| Reply | Reciprocal speech | Dead can answer but not question or correct |
| Rest | Voluntary departure | Presence cannot end cleanly |

A band raised to its arrest is still reversible. Commitment occurs when the matching name comb enters the water under spoken names.

### Four custody dogs

The custody dogs release the cradle without opening a ritual band. Keys are held by Caddoc, Vane, vault steward, and a founder-authorized fourth key. Force or substitution may release a dog but should name damage, noise, or later custody dispute.

### Full Asking checklist

- true anchor;
- whole intimate and public name;
- exact honest first question;
- willing and opposed witnesses;
- dead person's informed consent;
- nine intact silences;
- named custody after departure.

A proper departure returns all nine silences to equilibrium. Three black-water witness leaves preserve the whole exchange.

### Branoc's answer

> Bar the great leaves against the riders. Keep the wicket and the river stair until the outer ward is within. If the wall cannot hold, leave Dunwarren and save the people. A fort is a vessel; the realm is those it shelters.

Branoc knows Edric repeated the first clause. He died before learning which later clauses Edric obeyed, concealed, or could no longer execute. His testimony does not itself decide present succession, land restoration, or military command.

## Mael's annexation test

When Mael faces opposition, ask four questions in his voice:

1. Who commands the living defense before dawn?
2. Who holds each key, witness leaf, wagon, and keeper's authority?
3. When does that authority expire?
4. What exact override exists if the western gate fails?

He does not repent when the answers are credible. He tries to write himself into the contrary bench, countersignature, override, or custody share. Read every final office and limit aloud; he remains only where the instrument names him.

## Principal resolutions

| Resolution | What it achieves | What remains unresolved |
|---|---|---|
| Intact extraction | Stops Muster, preserves future Askings, transfers physical control | Permanent custody, defense, and publication |
| Full Asking in vault | Preserves Branoc's first testimony if bands remain whole | Mael may still retain vessel and attempt later use |
| Night of Hearing | Lawful delay until dawn under three authorities | Custody after dawn and Mael's longer-term power |
| Temporary sabotage | Delays apparatus without necessarily harming bands | Mael can repair while retaining target |
| Component substitution | Buys minutes and misdirects blame | Temporary, possibly dangerous if misunderstood |
| Negotiated custody | Can preserve vessel and living defense | Depends on enforceable limits and witnesses |
| Partial Muster | Produces military counsel | Named faculties lost for generations |
| Destruction | Prevents future coercion | Ends Branoc's opportunity and sacred institution |

## Extraction ledger

Track four objectives separately.

| Objective | Preserves | Likely pursuers |
|---|---|---|
| Vessel | Future Full Askings and nine silences | Vane, Mael, Hew, Green Larks |
| Anchor | Branoc's future invitation and authentication | Mael; coalition if vessel lost |
| Testimony | Exact answer and ability to stay dawn oath | Mael, heralds, rival political actors |
| Keeper | Authority to move, correct, or permit future use | Vane, Trust, permanent claimants |

Do not let loss of one objective silently erase the others.

## Reed Weir

### Tide states

- **Pinned water:** inner gate closed; prepare load, bargain, or alter post.
- **Black lane, first ten minutes:** water moving too fast for safe loading.
- **Black lane, middle twenty:** nine burden marks safely.
- **Sucking water, last ten:** eight marks safely; grounding likely.
- **Bare road:** drag, hide, reverse, hold, or seek another exit.

Cauldron and cradle occupy six marks. Gwyne's punt has nine. Tomas's skiff carries people and stabilizes the punt but cannot bear the cradle alone.

### Tomas's default offer

- second skiff and four trained hands;
- clean reed route and false north-road signs;
- Mere House destination;
- no royal Asking before three hearths convene;
- all testimony to the Trust.

Move him with the exact non-leading question, completed consent, public witness obligations, shared custody, or a limit on future questions. He prefers a live dispute over damaged sacred power.

## Failure-forward sequence

When an extraction beat fails:

1. Name which objective is endangered.
2. Change position, time, witness, or custody; do not erase earlier success.
3. Offer two or three grounded responses from existing routes, factions, or machinery.
4. Let the chosen response create a new claimant or consequence.

Examples:

- grounded punt: tow, lighten people, expose runner road, reverse, or bargain for Lark labor;
- vessel recaptured: testimony and ring may still escape;
- ring seized: Muster may still be stopped and testimony preserved;
- keeper captured: movement continues but future Asking remains closed;
- crew surrenders: establish Night of Hearing, Vane security, Mere House custody, or noon hearing under terms.

## Morning-after questions

Before closing the adventure, answer:

1. Did Branoc speak, refuse, or remain unavailable?
2. Where are the vessel, ring, three witness leaves, and recorded keeper?
3. How many bands remain whole, arrested, strained, or spent?
4. Who commands the living defense before noon?
5. What can Mael still lawfully or practically do?
6. What claim has the Nine Hearth Trust gained or lost?
7. Which workers, witnesses, and borrowed voices remain exposed?
8. What exact hearing, restoration, recovery, or pursuit follows?
