# Extraction and Aftermath: The Cauldron of Nine Silences

This sheet is the compact operating reference for extraction and aftermath. The Reed Weir encounter in `adventure.json` remains authoritative.

## What can be won or lost separately

| Objective | What it preserves | If lost |
|---|---|---|
| Vessel | Cauldron, cradle, intact silences, and future Full Askings | The captor controls the physical institution but not necessarily its custody or existing testimony |
| Anchor | Branoc's oath-ring and the present opportunity to call him again | Branoc cannot be invited again until the ring is recovered, though existing witness leaves remain valid |
| Testimony | Three exact black-water witness leaves | Political claims depend on living recollection, ordinary copies, and contested witnesses |
| Keeper | The recorded authority for destination and next use | The cauldron can move, but future Asking remains closed until custody is corrected or transferred |

Record where each objective travels. Splitting them protects partial success but creates several vulnerable routes.

## Tide states

| State | Water | Extraction consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Pinned water | Before the upper notch | Empty boats can approach; loaded departure needs shore lines and skilled handling |
| Black lane | Middle twenty minutes | Cleanest departure; nine safe burden marks on the black punt |
| Sucking water | Last ten minutes | Safe load falls to eight; poles replace oars; halts risk grounding |
| Bare road | After the lower notch | Punt cannot float the target; drag, hide, reverse route, bargain, or wait for flood |

Advance a tide state for substantial delay, a lost load-control operation, a Full Asking begun after low water opens, or a consequence explicitly paid in tide. Missing the window ends the prepared boat escape, not the adventure.

## Burden marks

| Load | Marks |
|---|---:|
| Cauldron and complete cradle | 6 |
| Each required boat hand | 1 |
| Named keeper | 1 |
| Incapacitated person | 1 |
| Bulky tool chest, handcart, or large evidence coffer | 1 |
| Ring, three rolled leaves, worn gear, compact rite kit | 0 additional |

The black punt carries nine marks during the black lane and eight during sucking water. It therefore carries the target, two boat hands, and one keeper under ideal conditions. The Lark skiff carries four people but cannot bear the cradle alone.

## The Nine Hearth Trust

Before continuous Taran custody, nine communities kept the cauldron in rotation: Mere, Ash, Rowan, Gorse, Reed, Stone, Bell, Road, and Lamp. War, inheritance, and questions concerning a keeper's own house required three disinterested hearths. House Taran's emergency writ interrupted the circuit fifty-three years ago. The surviving calendar contains repeated renewals but no final release.

Mother Eluned of the Mere House hired the Green Larks. Her claim is serious but incomplete: only Mere, Rowan, and Bell retain complete keeper lines, while two further claims have broken seals. She wants the cauldron carried to Mere House and no royal Asking conducted before three hearths convene.

## Tomas Wren's bargain

Tomas supplies a second skiff, four skilled hands, tow and false-trail rigging, a route to Mere House, and silence concerning the crew. His opening price is Mere House destination, Trust authority, no Full Asking before three hearths convene, and unabridged delivery of any witness leaves.

Useful leverage:

| Leverage | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Exact non-leading question | Shows the crew seeks testimony rather than endorsement |
| Dawn contradiction clause | Proves delay may make the answer legally useless |
| Joint current and receiving keepers | Gives the Trust a real share without suppressing the urgent Asking |
| Branoc's consent to publication | Prevents Tomas from claiming protection requires silencing a willing witness |
| Prohibition on later royal questions | Limits precedent after the first inquiry |

Tomas attacks logistics before people. If opposed, he takes boats, keys, capstans, tow lines, or an unattended anchor.

## Extraction beats

1. State which bands remain and whether Branoc spoke.
2. Place vessel, anchor, testimony, and keeper.
3. Choose route and transport.
4. Select the first pursuer from Heat and command-network state.
5. Name the objective that pursuer can reach.
6. Present one concrete obstacle: brake, grate, load, rope, road, witness, authority, or bargain.
7. On success, move to the next stage. On failure, concede time, position, exposure, an objective, injury, or a custody term.
8. Settle destination and authority at the final threshold.

A normal extraction needs two or three beats after the vault.

## Failure-forward outcomes

| Loss | What remains possible |
|---|---|
| Vessel recaptured, testimony escapes | One leaf and two witnesses stay renewal until noon |
| Vessel escapes, ring seized | Great Muster stopped; future Askings preserved; Branoc unavailable until anchor recovery |
| Ring escapes, vessel recaptured | Branoc opportunity survives as leverage |
| Keeper captured | Target can move but next Asking remains closed until custody is corrected |
| Punt grounds | Drag, hide, reverse route, bargain, or call on Lark labor |
| One band committed | Preserve eight, record the faculty lost, conduct diminished Asking, begin restoration |
| Crew surrenders under terms | Night of Hearing, Vane custody, noon hearing, or Mere House transfer remain possible |

Do not make one failed action erase all four objectives.

## Testimony thresholds

| Evidence | Immediate political effect |
|---|---|
| Living recollection only | Contestable report; useful but insufficient to stay the oath alone |
| One authenticated leaf plus two witnesses | Renewal stayed until noon |
| Three leaves | Durable exact record in separate custody |
| Three leaves plus founder's seal | Public hearing required before replacement Compact certification |
| Public Full Asking | Maximum credibility and immediate confrontation over interpretation and custody |

The leaves preserve Branoc's exact words, questions, refusals, and uncertainty. They do not depose Mael, restore lands, or decide criminal guilt automatically.

## Branoc's answer and the morning after

Branoc ordered Edric to bar the great leaves against the riders, keep the wicket and river stair open until the outer ward entered, and abandon Dunwarren if necessary to save the people. Edric preserved the first clause and violated or buried the protections and fallback.

Aderyn's one-season replacement covenant can place Vane over the household guard, Hew over a living intelligence and engineering council, levies under named valley consent, wards with their households, and cauldron custody before a separate noon hearing.

## Immediate end states

| State | Dawn consequence |
|---|---|

| Testimony leaves, vessel stays | Oath stayed; Mael's custody becomes publicly constrained |

## Final record

Before closing play, write down the state of all nine bands; whether Branoc spoke; the location of each witness leaf; vessel, ring, keeper, and destination at dawn; authority for the next Asking; and the living defense arrangement replacing, limiting, or failing to replace the Great Muster.
