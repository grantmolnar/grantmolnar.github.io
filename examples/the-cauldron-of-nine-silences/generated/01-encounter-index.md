# Encounter Index: The Cauldron of Nine Silences

## The Crooked Magpie (`crooked-magpie`)

**Role:** necessary, start

A ruined tide-road tavern offers Branoc's ring, one exact question, three approaches, and a custody dispute already waiting at the exit.

## The White Hart Court (`white-hart-court`)

**Role:** necessary

Mael turns hospitality, memorial rolls, and Red Fen's dead into a public argument for inherited emergency power.

## The Smoke Kitchens (`smoke-kitchens`)

**Role:** necessary

Soot, tallies, serving bells, and overworked staff govern the keep more precisely than its nobles suspect.

## The Rookwalk (`rookwalk`)

**Role:** necessary

Broken chains and message rooks offer speed, surveillance, and a spectacular extraction route that cannot remain secret.

## The Chapel of the Last Word (`chapel-last-word`)

**Role:** necessary

Caddoc's chapel can authenticate Branoc, expose the wounded rite, and open the underkeep—if the crew can answer for custody.

## The Widow's Solar (`widows-solar`)

**Role:** optional

Aderyn holds founder authority and a replacement defense, but will not trade one ungoverned morning for another.

## The Barrow Stair (`barrow-stair`)

**Role:** necessary

Three stone mouths record whom the crew calls, what it will ask, and who answers for the cauldron afterward.

## The House of Borrowed Voices (`house-borrowed-voices`)

**Role:** necessary

Twelve accurate answers without remembered purposes predict the heist and command living guards through visible machinery.

## The Cauldron Vault (`cauldron-vault`)

**Role:** necessary

The target is a rite, a six-mark load, and a constitutional crisis whose nine visible faculties can still be saved or spent.

## The Reed Weir (`reed-weir`)

**Role:** necessary, end

The tide road forces vessel, anchor, testimony, people, and custody into boats too small to carry every claim together.
