## Summary

Marshal of the damaged field command and keeper of the war roll, seeking actionable counsel for a real northern threat while remaining capable of replacing extracted answers with a living intelligence and engineering council.

## Reference Material

## The war roll at his wrist

Marshal Hew Pale keeps the war roll and measures danger in seasons, road days, grain figures, uncertain bridges, and scouts returned. Red Fen killed or scattered a third of his field command. He needs a defensible morning and will not accept ritual purity as a substitute for current maps, engineers, stores, ferrymen, and officers able to issue orders.

## A living council by morning

Hew knows that a fact is not a plan. He can verify the military names selected for the Great Muster, insist on narrow actionable questions, serve as military surety for a Night of Hearing, and chair a living council of scouts, engineers, granary keepers, and valley captains. Evidence that stripped answers cannot rank competing goods can move him from the Muster toward that council.

## Military surety, not title

Hew does not authenticate Branoc, keep the cauldron, grant founder authority, or decide whether Edric's recital created lawful present title. His strategic need cannot make extracted expertise into consent or living command. His current support for Mael, access to the war roll, military deployments, and willingness to accept a replacement covenant remain matters for play.
