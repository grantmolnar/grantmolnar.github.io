# Lord Mael Taran

## Summary

Lord of Dunwarren and bearer of House Taran's inherited emergency regime, using Red Fen's real military crisis to renew the Gate Compact, centralize custody, and conduct the Great Muster.

## Reference Material

## The warrant he inherits

Lord Mael Taran exercises House Taran's inherited emergency powers from Dunwarren. The doctrine descends from Edric Taran's recital of Branoc's western-gate command and has long justified control of roads, grain, levies, noble wards, and the cauldron whenever danger is declared. Red Fen makes the present danger real: the vale has lost a third of its field command and the northern road remains exposed.

## Every key beneath one command

Mael wants a defended vale, renewal of the Gate Compact, completion of the Great Muster, and continued Taran custody of the cauldron. He describes coerced counsel as inherited service and treats every new danger as reason to place another key, witness, keeper, wagon, or dead voice beneath one command. He nevertheless listens for an executable replacement: named offices, resources, keys, countersignatures, expiry, and a defense order that survives until morning.

## What survives until morning

Mael can direct Dunwarren's household resources and invoke the existing emergency regime, but neither Edric's recital, Branoc's oath-ring, the memorial rolls, borrowed answers, nor physical possession proves present royal title or unlimited ritual authority. Brother Caddoc keeps the rite, Aderyn holds founder authority, Vane's military orders remain bounded, and the dead retain refusal and reply. Mael may accept a constrained role, but only the office, override, custody share, and duration actually granted by a living covenant belong to him. Current custody, guard loyalty, bell state, and bargain terms remain matters for play.
