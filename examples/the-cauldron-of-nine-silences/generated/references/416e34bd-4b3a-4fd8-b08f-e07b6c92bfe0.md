## Summary

## Reference Material

## Near the throne, not seated

Lady Aderyn Taran is the widow of Mael's elder brother. She holds household keys, old servants, the founder's seal, and the right to speak before her son's inheritance is sworn around him, but she is near the throne rather than seated on it. She repeats no sentence she has not chosen to own and measures every proposal against the morning after.

## The morning after

Aderyn wants Branoc's testimony heard without leaving the vale leaderless. She supplied Gwyne with information, withheld the founder's seal until the ring could be tested, and prepared a defense covenant joining roads, grain, scouts, watch duty, and expiring authority. She asks who will witness Branoc, who chooses the first question, where the cauldron will rest afterward, and what living command remains if the old Compact fails.

## What the founder's seal can open

Aderyn's seal can authenticate founder provisions, open old mechanisms, support the Night of Hearing, require delay over authenticated testimony, and lend public standing to a witnessed seizure or replacement covenant. It does not reveal Branoc's command, make Gwyne's accusation true, bind Vane or Hew without their assent, award the cauldron, or create unilateral royal title. Her current allegiance, willingness to release the seal, and final custody position remain matters for play.
