# Reference Index: The Cauldron of Nine Silences

## People

- [**Gwyne Marr**](08a5bb82-4600-4c11-832f-6b67ae17ca04.md) — Outer-ward advocate and coalition broker who frames the heist, protects the exact question to Branoc, and refuses to let ancestry, possession, or desired outcomes outrun the surviving evidence. Aliases: Gwyne.
- [**Lord Mael Taran**](c9a4c98c-ba0b-43e5-8c0d-9f111b757c91.md) — Lord of Dunwarren and bearer of House Taran's inherited emergency regime, using Red Fen's real military crisis to renew the Gate Compact, centralize custody, and conduct the Great Muster. Aliases: Lord Mael, Mael Taran, Mael.
- [**King Branoc**](02aca2e8-7cb6-42f7-8365-1e06e245d790.md) — The dead king of the western-gate crisis, newly reachable through his oath-ring for a first Full Asking whose testimony can correct the inherited recital but cannot govern the changed present. Aliases: Branoc.
- [**Lady Aderyn Taran**](416e34bd-4b3a-4fd8-b08f-e07b6c92bfe0.md) — Widow of Mael's elder brother and holder of founder authority, able to support delay, witnessed seizure, and a replacement defense without claiming the throne or settling cauldron ownership. Aliases: Lady Aderyn, Aderyn.
- [**Brother Caddoc**](1be57339-886c-4e53-83cc-bde88fac7421.md) — Exacting hereditary keeper of the Full Asking who can authenticate Branoc's ring, conduct the rite, and serve as chapel witness while bearing responsibility for Mael's damaging trials. Aliases: Caddoc.
- [**Edric Taran**](63306f5c-35d7-41d0-a904-86838922f507.md) — House Taran's western-gate commander, whose later recital became the Gate Compact's hereditary emergency doctrine even though no surviving record carries Branoc's own hand or seal. Aliases: Edric.
- [**Captain Rhod Vane**](8a6dd529-71e1-49b7-a962-7c5bfc0ee131.md) — Captain of Dunwarren's living guard, whose bounded orders, sacred-object recovery articles, and concern for a defensible morning can separate civil protection from Mael's disputed ritual command. Aliases: Captain Vane, Rhod Vane, Vane.
- [**Mother Eluned**](49140281-8273-4d4d-8272-b1f0a769aec3.md) — Senior surviving Nine Hearth keeper and patron of the Green Larks, pressing a serious but incomplete communal-custody claim against both Mael and any coalition that would make another royal use precede three-hearth deliberation. Aliases: Mother Eluned of the Mere House, Eluned.
- [**Tomas Wren**](722a7e08-d425-43b3-acbc-a9d002d90929.md) — Courteous Green Lark leader and transport specialist who turns threats into shared carrying problems, brings Mother Eluned’s rival commission into the heist, and demands a receiving keeper before movement becomes custody. Aliases: Tomas.
- [**Marshal Hew Pale**](5721becd-9641-4b74-a3f8-dd9ecbb0c9ac.md) — Marshal of the damaged field command and keeper of the war roll, seeking actionable counsel for a real northern threat while remaining capable of replacing extracted answers with a living intelligence and engineering council. Aliases: Marshal Hew, Hew Pale, Hew.
- [**Nessa Pike**](7752c66e-eaec-4ef4-93e9-517c428beca2.md) — Mistress of Dunwarren's kitchens, whose tally authority, worker protection, household memory, and control of bells, carts, and service routes give ordinary people leverage over the heist's noble and ritual claims. Aliases: Nessa.
- [**Efa Rill**](06e96b1a-a1e4-4f3a-9432-7c6eb1412e95.md) — Mason and named excavation witness who removed Branoc's oath-ring before Mael closed the chamber, preserving the first viable chain from the western-gate remains to the anchor without claiming more than she observed. Aliases: Efa.

## Organizations

- [**The Green Larks**](b002fc7a-db5f-4ac1-9f6d-343357c2d956.md) — A mobile recovery crew hired by Mother Eluned to take the cauldron intact to Mere House, using distractions, transport craft, route opportunism, and custody bargaining rather than treating the vessel as ordinary loot. Aliases: Green Larks, the Larks.
- [**The Nine Hearth Trust**](0d9aca64-f4d8-4b8d-a3c1-959307d7159e.md) — The surviving remnant of a nine-community funerary circuit that kept the cauldron in rotation before House Taran's continuous emergency custody, preserving a serious communal claim without supplying one uncontested owner. Aliases: Nine Hearth Trust, the Trust, the Nine Hearths.

## Objects

- [**The Cauldron of Nine Silences**](2c44f943-72c8-464a-bac2-7cb5f0c60c27.md) — A communal funerary vessel and nine-banded oak cradle whose accumulated silences can invite one honored dead person as a person, while movement, custody, and any spent faculty remain distinct operational problems. Aliases: the Cauldron, Cauldron of Nine Silences, the vessel.
- [**Branoc's Oath-Ring**](5853b0fa-8880-4f6c-b73f-690ae6929cfa.md) — The newly recovered intimate anchor that can identify and invite Branoc through private name, lifetime wear, royal flaw, and chamber provenance, while proving neither his command nor its holder's authority. Aliases: the oath-ring, Branoc's ring, the ring.

## Other

- [**The Gate Compact**](8cf58ff7-1439-4292-adfd-44715acb99f9.md) — The repeatedly renewed emergency covenant built on Edric Taran's recital of Branoc's western-gate command, joining House Taran's control of defense and custody to a historical claim now subject to exact testimony. Aliases: Gate Compact, the Compact.
- [**The Full Asking**](ea9eab0a-65ee-461c-8f64-87ced3c2ee47.md) — The complete witnessed invitation by which one honored dead person may return in voice, memory, judgment, and will, answer or refuse freely, and leave an exact record without acquiring rule over the living present. Aliases: Full Asking, the Asking.
- [**The Great Muster**](dd8d50e6-df51-48db-b98c-cc2a6eb27731.md) — Mael's fifth-bell mass extraction, using household memorial rolls, copper combs, and the cauldron's accumulated silences to obtain accurate military answers after severing the reasons and attachments that once governed them. Aliases: Great Muster, the Muster.
