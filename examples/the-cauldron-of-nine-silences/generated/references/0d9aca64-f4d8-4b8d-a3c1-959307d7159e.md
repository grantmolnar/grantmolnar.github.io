# The Nine Hearth Trust

**Reference ID:** `0d9aca64-f4d8-4b8d-a3c1-959307d7159e`

**Tags:** communal custody, nine hearths, keeper circuit, Mere House

## Summary

The surviving remnant of a nine-community funerary circuit that kept the cauldron in rotation before House Taran's continuous emergency custody, preserving a serious communal claim without supplying one uncontested owner.

## Reference Material

## Nine hearths on one road

Nine communities once kept the cauldron in rotation along a carrying road. Their trust treated custody as a circuit of named keepers and destinations rather than a throne or permanent household possession. House Taran's fifty-three years of renewed emergency custody interrupted that practice without erasing every line, seal, or obligation.

## Three surviving keeper lines

Mere, Rowan, and Bell retain complete keeper lines. Two further claims survive through broken seals and descendant households. Mother Eluned, senior keeper at Mere House, presses the Trust's claim and commissions the Green Larks to recover the vessel before another royal use. A three-hearth council can recognize receiving custody and deliberate over future use.

## What three hearths cannot decide

Three hearths are not nine, disputed seals challenge Eluned's seniority, and the old circuit did not foresee a stolen personal anchor, a coerced Great Muster, and a dawn renewal oath together. The Trust can supply history, keepers, destination, and a rival lawful framework. It cannot alone settle permanent ownership, Branoc's first question, the current defense, or every community's consent. Which seals, keepers, and temporary terms are accepted remains a matter for play.
