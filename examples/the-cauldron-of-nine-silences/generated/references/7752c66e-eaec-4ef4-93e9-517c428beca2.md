# Nessa Pike

**Tags:** kitchens, workers, household legitimacy, service route

## Summary

Mistress of Dunwarren's kitchens, whose tally authority, worker protection, household memory, and control of bells, carts, and service routes give ordinary people leverage over the heist's noble and ritual claims.

## Reference Material

## Every body, bowl, key, and ladle

Nessa Pike runs Dunwarren's kitchens through counts, cords, tasks, bells, stores, and the knowledge that every body, bowl, key, and ladle belongs somewhere. She will conceal competent help, expose anyone who endangers her staff, and oppose Mael's steward when workers are counted as equipment. Her authority is practical, immediate, and older than the court the kitchens feed.

## Bells, carts, and kitchen witnesses

Nessa controls the first two serving bells, can delay a count, hide one person, clear the staff yard, place the reinforced cart, and turn kitchen workers into witnesses around a moving load. Her household rules preserve reasons omitted from several borrowed answers: stores exist for people, triage protects the lives that sustain others, and no key should close every route.

## What she will not carry

Nessa can lend ordinary-household legitimacy and material support, but she will not burn, poison, abandon an injured worker, or carry a sacred burden merely to settle a noble quarrel. She has no ritual keeping, founder authority, military command, or permanent custody claim. Her present protection, bell timing, available labor, and willingness to open a route remain matters for play.
