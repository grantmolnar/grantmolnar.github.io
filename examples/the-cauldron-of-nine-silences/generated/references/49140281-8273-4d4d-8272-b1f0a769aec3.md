# Mother Eluned

**Reference ID:** `49140281-8273-4d4d-8272-b1f0a769aec3`  
**Kind:** Person  
**Aliases:** Mother Eluned of the Mere House, Eluned  
**Tags:** Nine Hearth Trust, Mere House, communal custody, keeper

## Summary

Senior surviving Nine Hearth keeper and patron of the Green Larks, pressing a serious but incomplete communal-custody claim against both Mael and any coalition that would make another royal use precede three-hearth deliberation.

## Reference Material

## The Mere House keeper

Mother Eluned of the Mere House is senior among the surviving Nine Hearth keepers and acts for the remaining Trust. She hired the Green Larks to recover the cauldron intact, declare temporary Trust custody, and carry the vessel to Mere House before another ruler turns it into an instrument of immediate need.

## Three hearths before another question

Eluned insists that future use return to communal keeping. She rejects a royal Asking before three hearths can convene because the cauldron was built as a circuit among communities, not as the possession of whichever court can reach it. The exact first question, Branoc's consent, the dawn deadline, unabridged witness leaves, and genuinely shared custody can force her agents to distinguish necessary testimony from permanent royal precedent.

## What seniority cannot settle

Eluned may convene surviving hearths, recognize a receiving keeper, seat the bands at Mere House, and press the Trust's older custody claim. Only three complete keeper lines survive, two broken seals contest seniority, and the old law never anticipated a stolen anchor, coerced Muster, and renewal oath on the same night. She offers a legitimate claimant and destination, not a final judgment or uncontested ownership. Her present instructions, willingness to compromise, and recognition of any temporary keeper remain matters for play.
