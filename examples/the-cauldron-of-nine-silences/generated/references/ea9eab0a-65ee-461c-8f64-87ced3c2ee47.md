# The Full Asking

**Reference ID:** `ea9eab0a-65ee-461c-8f64-87ced3c2ee47`  
**Kind:** Other  
**Aliases:** Full Asking, the Asking  
**Tags:** ritual, testimony, dead consent, exact question

## Summary

The complete witnessed invitation by which one honored dead person may return in voice, memory, judgment, and will, answer or refuse freely, and leave an exact record without acquiring rule over the living present.

## Reference Material

## Name, question, witness, keeper

A Full Asking invites one honored dead person through a true personal anchor. It requires the whole name, an honest first question, willing witnesses, the dead person's consent, nine intact silences, and named living custody afterward. The dead returns in voice, memory, judgment, and will rather than as a reconstructed body or a store of literal facts.

## Freedom in the water

The invited person may answer, refuse, challenge the question, accuse, forgive, qualify, admit uncertainty, ask questions in return, impose conditions, or depart. The promised first question for Branoc remains: *What command did you give Edric Taran at the western gate?* Three black-water leaves can preserve the whole exchange, including objections, uncertainty, and replies from the living.

## Testimony, not command

A complete answer supplies testimony and governing priorities, not self-executing law. Branoc can establish his own words and ask what followed; he cannot inspect the present road from memory or decide every later motive, succession, restoration, defense, or custody question. The Night of Hearing may secure one night for an Asking through chapel witness, founder authority, and military surety, but permanent ownership and present command remain unresolved.
