## Summary

Exacting hereditary keeper of the Full Asking who can authenticate Branoc's ring, conduct the rite, and serve as chapel witness while bearing responsibility for Mael's damaging trials.

## Reference Material

## Keeper without the title

Brother Caddoc is the cauldron's hereditary keeper without the title Mael recognizes. He is exact about ritual verbs: the dead are *invited*, never raised, and *depart*, never dismissed. He joined the first borrowed-counsel trials to save the northern bridges and continued after seeing the cost because he feared an untrained operator would destroy the cauldron. That defense explains his complicity and does not absolve it.

## Nine silences and one witness

Caddoc keeps the complete Full Asking, the nine faculties, the custody clause, the Night of Hearing, the carrying calendar, and the chapel's royal wax casts. He can provisionally authenticate Branoc's ring, conduct the rite, serve as chapel witness, open the Barrow Stair through his keeper phrase, and explain the last reversible interval before a silence is committed. His authority concerns ritual integrity, testimony, and keeping; it is not founder authority, military surety, ownership, or a power to compel the dead.

## The question before the key

Caddoc asks where an anchor came from, what exact question will be asked, who will witness, and who will keep the cauldron afterward. Threats may force access but cannot produce competent help. A recovered reason may let one borrowed answer qualify or retract its command, but it does not restore a complete person or replenish a spent silence. Caddoc's present obedience, location, keys, and willingness to accompany the vessel remain matters for play.
