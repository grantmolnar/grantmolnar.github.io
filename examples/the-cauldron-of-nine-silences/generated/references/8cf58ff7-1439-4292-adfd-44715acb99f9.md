# The Gate Compact

## Summary

The repeatedly renewed emergency covenant built on Edric Taran's recital of Branoc's western-gate command, joining House Taran's control of defense and custody to a historical claim now subject to exact testimony.

## Reference Material

## Edric's recital beneath every renewal

The Gate Compact rests on Edric Taran's sworn recital that Branoc ordered the western gate closed and Dunwarren preserved. No surviving copy carries Branoc's own hand or seal. Later renewals treated Edric's report as direct royal command and let House Taran claim roads, grain, levies, noble wards, and cauldron custody whenever danger was declared.

## Dawn oath and noon hearing

The seven valleys renew the Compact at dawn. Each household brings a memorial roll for the customary recitation of service; Mael has joined those rolls into the Great Muster's war register and means to present its answer before the oath. One authenticated witness leaf and two living witnesses can invoke the Compact's contradiction procedure and stay renewal until noon, creating a hearing rather than an automatic verdict.

## What the Compact cannot inherit

The Compact can organize real defense and preserve existing offices while its terms remain accepted. It cannot prove the recital on which it rests, make the Great Muster consensual counsel, settle permanent cauldron ownership, or turn Branoc's eventual testimony into present-day command. Which households swear, delay, refuse, or accept a replacement covenant remains a matter for play.
