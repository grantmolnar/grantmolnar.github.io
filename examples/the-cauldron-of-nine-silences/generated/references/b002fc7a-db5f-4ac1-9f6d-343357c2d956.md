# The Green Larks

**Reference ID:** `b002fc7a-db5f-4ac1-9f6d-343357c2d956`  
**Kind:** Organization  
**Aliases:** Green Larks, the Larks  
**Tags:** rival crew, Nine Hearth Trust, extraction, Mere House

## Summary

A mobile recovery crew hired by Mother Eluned to take the cauldron intact to Mere House, using distractions, transport craft, route opportunism, and custody bargaining rather than treating the vessel as ordinary loot.

## Reference Material

## A keeper's knot, not a buyer's purse

The Green Larks are a professional recovery crew acting for Mother Eluned and the surviving Nine Hearth Trust. They enter Dunwarren at the third bell under a court distraction and seek the cauldron intact before Mael or Gwyne's coalition turns emergency possession into precedent. Their keeper's knot and carrying token mark a custodial commission, not a buyer's purse.

## Take the road another crew neglects

The Larks exploit neglected routes, compromised workers, false objectives, tow lines, skiffs, capstans, and other crews' abandoned equipment. They lack a dedicated roof specialist but are strong at movement, disguise, and bargaining around a load. When opposed, they prefer seizing logistics or forcing a public custody declaration to breaking the vessel or beginning an indiscriminate fight.

## Movement is not keeping

The Larks can move and preserve the target, produce witnesses, and make Mere House a concrete destination. They do not embody the whole Trust, decide permanent ownership, authenticate Branoc, or become keepers by physical possession. Their members present, route, equipment, objectives controlled, and current compact with another crew remain in encounters, ledgers, and play state.
