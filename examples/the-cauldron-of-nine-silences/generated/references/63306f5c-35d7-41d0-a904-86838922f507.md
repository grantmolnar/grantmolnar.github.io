# Edric Taran

**Reference ID:** `63306f5c-35d7-41d0-a904-86838922f507`  
**Kind:** Person  
**Aliases:** Edric  
**Tags:** House Taran, Gate Compact, recital, western gate

## Summary

House Taran's western-gate commander, whose later recital became the Gate Compact's hereditary emergency doctrine even though no surviving record carries Branoc's own hand or seal.

## Reference Material

## The recital that became law

After the western-gate crisis, Edric Taran testified that Branoc ordered him to close the gate, abandon the outer ward, and preserve Dunwarren at any cost. The earliest surviving Compact identifies those words as Edric's sworn account after Branoc and the outer-ward witnesses were dead, displaced, or voiceless. Later renewals silently recast the recital as direct royal command and built House Taran's emergency privileges upon it.

## Wicket, stair, and outer ward

Branoc actually ordered Edric to bar the great leaves against the riders, keep the wicket and river stair open until the outer ward was within, and abandon the fort if necessary to save the people. Edric closed the wicket and river stair before the outer ward was inside, held the fortress, and later preserved only the first command in his recital. Branoc died before learning which portions Edric obeyed.

## What his oath cannot settle

Edric's recital is a consequential witness statement, not Branoc's own recorded answer and not proof of Mael's present authority. Branoc can establish his command and priorities but cannot establish Edric's later motives. Judgment of Edric's conduct, land restoration, memorial correction, compensation, succession, and blame require living evidence and political action beyond the Full Asking.
