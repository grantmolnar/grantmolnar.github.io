## Summary

A communal funerary vessel and nine-banded oak cradle whose accumulated silences can invite one honored dead person as a person, while movement, custody, and any spent faculty remain distinct operational problems.

## Reference Material

## One person through nine silences

The Cauldron of Nine Silences is a funerary vessel, not a resurrection engine or an ordinary instrument for questioning corpses. Through a true personal anchor and witnessed invitation, it can receive one honored dead person in voice, memory, judgment, and will. Its power accumulated through nine communal silences kept across generations.

## Face, Road, Measure, Hearth

Nine iron bands hold those silences in relation. Face preserves recognition; Road, ordered memory; Measure, judgment; Hearth, affection; Hand, self-command; Season, temporal understanding; Bar, refusal; Reply, reciprocal speech; and Rest, voluntary departure. They are not interchangeable charges. A committed or damaged band removes a named faculty and may require communal restoration over years or generations.

## The keeper at the threshold

The bronze vessel and living-oak cradle form one operating object. Four custody dogs govern movement without opening a ritual band. Consecrated thresholds require a living keeper, destination, and authority for future use; possession of vessel, keys, wheels, or ropes does not replace that declaration. Current band condition, dog state, load, route, testimony, custody, and restoration progress belong to `VAULT-OPERATIONS.md`, `EXTRACTION-AND-AFTERMATH.md`, encounters, and play state.
