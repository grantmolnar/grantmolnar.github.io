# Tomas Wren

**Reference ID:** `722a7e08-d425-43b3-acbc-a9d002d90929`  
**Kind:** Person  
**Aliases:** Tomas  
**Tags:** Green Larks, transport, rival commission, custody

## Summary

Courteous Green Lark leader and transport specialist who turns threats into shared carrying problems, brings Mother Eluned’s rival commission into the heist, and demands a receiving keeper before movement becomes custody.

## Reference Material

## A road to Mere House

Tomas Wren leads the Green Lark recovery party sent by Mother Eluned. He enters Dunwarren at the third bell and treats every claim as a road, load, hand, boat, and destination someone must actually provide. He wants the cauldron intact at Mere House and regards Branoc's urgent first question as important evidence obtained through a dangerous royal precedent.

## Skiff, crossbeam, and bargain

Tomas can provide a second skiff, four skilled extraction hands, tow lines, a folding crossbeam, false pursuit signs, and quiet movement through the reeds. He bargains when another crew controls the route and seizes logistics when refused: capstan, winding bar, tow line, unattended anchor, or the public custody claim itself. At the final threshold he must name Eluned and the Trust or relinquish the pretense that he is moving the vessel for no patron.

## Receiver, not owner

Tomas is a receiving agent and transport leader, not owner, ritual keeper, founder authority, or the whole Trust. Possession of a boat, rope, ring, or cradle does not grant authority for a Full Asking. The exact question, dawn clause, Branoc's conditions, and divided temporary keeping may move him; his route, crew strength, equipment, current bargains, and control of any objective remain matters for play.
