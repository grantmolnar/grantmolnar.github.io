# Branoc's Oath-Ring

**Reference ID:** `5853b0fa-8880-4f6c-b73f-690ae6929cfa`  
**Kind:** Object  
**Aliases:** the oath-ring, Branoc's ring, the ring  
**Tags:** personal anchor, Branoc, authentication, provenance

## Summary

The newly recovered intimate anchor that can identify and invite Branoc through private name, lifetime wear, royal flaw, and chamber provenance, while proving neither his command nor its holder's authority.

## Reference Material

## The hand beneath the gate

Efa Rill removed the gold oath-ring from the hand of the crowned skeleton exposed beneath Dunwarren's western gate. The chamber mortar, the missing impression in the hand's dust, the ring's private oath-name, its royal wax flaw, and wear from long bodily use connect the object to Branoc more closely than public regalia or inherited legend could.

## An invitation, not an answer

The ring is the first viable personal anchor found since Branoc's death. Chapel records and royal wax casts can authenticate its marks, allowing the Full Asking to invite Branoc as a person rather than a title assembled from public memory. The anchor permits an invitation; it does not compel acceptance or predetermine an answer.

## What old gold cannot prove

The ring establishes identity and provenance. It does not prove what Branoc commanded, validate Edric's recital, transfer cauldron custody, grant royal title, or make its possessor Branoc's agent. Its current bearer, concealment, damage, seizure, and route remain matters for encounters, the heist ledger, and play state.
