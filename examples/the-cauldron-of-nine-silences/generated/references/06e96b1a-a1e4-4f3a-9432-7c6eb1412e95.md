# Efa Rill

**Tags:** mason, excavation, ring provenance, witness

## Summary

Mason and named excavation witness who removed Branoc's oath-ring before Mael closed the chamber, preserving the first viable chain from the western-gate remains to the anchor without claiming more than she observed.

## Reference Material

## What the fallen stone exposed

Efa Rill worked the emergency underpinning beneath Dunwarren's western gate. When the roof settled into a chamber absent from every surviving plan, she saw the crowned skeleton, the inward fall, the blade wound, and the order in which officers and workers arrived. Before Mael's men declared the remains plague dead and closed the work, she removed the worn gold oath-ring from the skeleton's hand.

## Sequence before inference

Efa speaks in sequence and stone: which course moved, what fell, where the body lay, what dust held the missing object's impression, and who reached the chamber before it was sealed. She will testify only if her name and act are preserved rather than used as an invisible source. Her account joins the ring to Branoc's chamber and exposes the searches and arrests that followed the coalition's failed neutral-hearing offer.

## What her witness can establish

Efa can establish provenance and the conduct of the excavation. She cannot authenticate the ring's private oath-name, prove Branoc's command, know Edric's motives, or decide Mael's present authority. Her injury, location, protection, willingness to enter Dunwarren, and availability as a live witness remain matters for play.
