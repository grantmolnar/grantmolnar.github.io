# The Great Muster

**Reference ID:** `dd8d50e6-df51-48db-b98c-cc2a6eb27731`  
**Kind:** Other  
**Aliases:** Great Muster, the Muster  
**Tags:** war register, extracted counsel, fifth bell, nine silences

## Summary

Mael's fifth-bell mass extraction, using household memorial rolls, copper combs, and the cauldron's accumulated silences to obtain accurate military answers after severing the reasons and attachments that once governed them.

## Reference Material

## Hundreds of names beneath one question

The Great Muster converts the households' memorial rolls into a chained war register and addresses narrow military questions to many honored dead. Copper name combs, band keys, question boards, and the underkeep command network extract useful answers without inviting those dead as whole persons. Mael intends to present the result as inherited service before the dawn renewal.

## Answers without reasons

The answers remain factually competent within their domains, but the rite strips away love, fear, loyalty, promise, and purpose. The result cannot rank grain against people, walls against households, speed against the wounded, or victory against those made to bear it. Each committed band spends a named communal silence and diminishes future Full Askings for generations. Earlier trial needles created the twelve borrowed voices without committing a whole band.

## What the Muster cannot make lawful

The Muster may produce actionable military information. It does not create consent, restore context, authenticate Branoc, prove Edric's recital, renew the Gate Compact by itself, or grant Mael permanent custody. Which names, questions, combs, bands, answers, and living posts are active remains in the heist, underkeep, and vault operating sheets and in play state.
