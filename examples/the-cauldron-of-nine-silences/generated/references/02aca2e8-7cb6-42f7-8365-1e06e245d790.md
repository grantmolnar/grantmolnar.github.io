# King Branoc

**Reference ID:** `02aca2e8-7cb6-42f7-8365-1e06e245d790`  
**Kind:** Person  
**Aliases:** Branoc  
**Tags:** king, Full Asking, western gate, testimony

## Summary

The dead king of the western-gate crisis, newly reachable through his oath-ring for a first Full Asking whose testimony can correct the inherited recital but cannot govern the changed present.

## Reference Material

## The man beneath the gate

King Branoc died during the western-gate crisis in the command chamber later sealed beneath Dunwarren. His body and intimate regalia were believed lost, and he has never been summoned. The recovered oath-ring bears his private oath-name, royal wax flaw, bodily wear, and chamber mortar. Those features can authenticate Branoc as the person invited; they do not establish what he commanded or grant authority to the ring's holder.

## The king may answer or refuse

The agreed first question is: *What command did you give Edric Taran at the western gate?* A proper Full Asking invites rather than compels. Branoc may answer, refuse, accuse, qualify, admit uncertainty, or ask what became of the people his answer concerned. His questions, conditions, uncertainty, and voluntary departure are part of the testimony and must not be abridged into a convenient royal ruling.

## What the dead king cannot command

Branoc's command was: “Bar the great leaves against the riders. Keep the wicket and the river stair until the outer ward is within. If the wall cannot hold, leave Dunwarren and save the people. A fort is a vessel; the realm is those it shelters.” He died before knowing which parts Edric obeyed and can testify to his own words and priorities, not Edric's later motives. Branoc favors evacuation, distributed stores, living scouts, and authority limited to named communities, but he is not automatically competent to direct a changed war. His answer is historical and constitutional evidence, not present-day command.
