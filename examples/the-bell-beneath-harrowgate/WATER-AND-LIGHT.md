# Water and Ward-Light Operating Aid

Use this sheet when the party begins operating the Lantern Cistern or Reliquary Under Water. Track broad, consequential states. Do not count gallons, individual lamps, or rounds of air unless the table wants that level of detail.

## Starting state

- **Global water:** Rising above.
- **Lantern Cistern:** Black-water pulses are lifting the oil skin; the local separator is unsynchronized.
- **Reliquary Under Water:** Alternating between high and middle water.
- **Processional Bough:** Lit.
- **Ossuary Bough:** Lit.
- **Mourning Bough:** Dark and drained.
- **Deep Bough:** Lit at half strength.
- **Portable ward-oil:** Four measures can be recovered without darkening another bough.
- **Tremor clock:** Use the current campaign step. Rhea Colm has approximately one step of reliable air unless the Mourning bell line is primed or pumped.

## Adjudication principle

Every major operation should answer three questions:

1. What becomes safer, easier, or available?
2. Where does the displaced water, light, memory, or danger go?
3. What person, resource, or route becomes newly exposed?

A successful operation may still impose its declared cost. A failed operation should usually create an uncontrolled version of the intended transfer rather than simply doing nothing.

## Portable ward-oil

One measure can do one of the following:

- sustain one lamp bough through the next tremor-clock advance;
- operate one portable ward lamp for an expedition interval;
- ward two carried objects against pale scavengers for one encounter;
- establish one defensible safe camp if smoke can escape;
- prime one Reliquary saint, Chapel station, or Iron Tongue mechanism; or
- create one short black-water fire barrier.

### Carrying oil

- A silvered flask carries one measure safely through black water.
- A sealed metal or ceramic vessel carries one measure through ordinary travel but may leak or flare under a major impact.
- An improvised open vessel requires two hands and turns climbing or swimming into a consequential risk.
- Mixing ward-oil with black water creates a brief hot flame followed by memory-bearing silver vapor.

## Ward-light state effects

| State | Route effect | Creature effect | Memory effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Full | Most marked routes are easy to read. | Wick-eaters and lesser load ghosts are suppressed. | New toll-memory continues flowing to the Iron Choir from all branches. |
| Selective | Lit routes are safer; dark-route boundaries become encounter sites. | Wick-eaters migrate toward the edges of lit territory. | Darkened branches release stored memory and stop new transmission. |
| Failing | Light gutters during pressure changes. | Suppressed creatures act when flames dip. | Memory arrives in broken, intrusive fragments. |
| Extinguished | Route marks vanish unless otherwise known. | Wick-eaters move freely and follow warm memories or reflected light. | Stored memory can be recovered locally; no new transmission occurs. |
| Burning out of control | One route becomes a fire-and-smoke hazard. | Most pale scavengers flee or burn. | Silver vapor spreads involuntary memories into adjacent regions. |

## Common operations

### Establish selective light

1. Choose the boughs to preserve.
2. Recover or spend oil measures.
3. Mark newly dark routes.
4. Move wick-eaters toward the nearest lit-dark boundary.
5. Release one useful memory fragment from each deliberately darkened branch.

### Release stored memory safely

1. Pinch the selected bough's feed.
2. Cool its lamp cups.
3. Draw silver wick through Senn's ceramic comb.
4. Name who receives or carries the released memories.
5. Darken the branch and introduce nearby scavenger pressure.

### Stabilize the Reliquary at middle water

1. Balance the Open Palm cache or replace its weight.
2. Hold the Kneeling Saint halfway.
3. Pulse the Returning Breath bell.
4. Assign a second operator, pallbearer, ghost crew, or temporary counterweight.
5. Record where the excluded pressure goes.

### Perform a controlled drain

1. Isolate the Lantern separator if possible.
2. Secure Rhea's air and any loose salvage.
3. Raise the Kneeling Saint gradually.
4. Decide what additional pressure enters Black Rain.
5. Change route access and load-ghost strength immediately.

### Flood deliberately

1. Close the lower culvert.
2. Accept water from the Ropeworks or upper line.
3. Secure oil and floating evidence.
4. Open the royal niche or use expanded air pockets.
5. Record any contamination sent toward Lantern.

## Wick-eater pressure

When a wick-eater encounter is called for, choose or roll one motive:

| d6 | Motive |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Follow a portable light through a dark route. |
| 2 | Feed on a recently released memory. |
| 3 | Steal the reason one character entered the dungeon. |
| 4 | Gather around a lit-dark boundary and make passage costly. |
| 5 | Carry a recognizable Low Choir memory toward its owner. |
| 6 | Flee an approaching black-water pulse and reveal a hidden conduit. |

Wick-eaters prefer mnemonic harm before bodily harm. Their movement should make the light map matter.

## Memory fragments

A released fragment should be useful and personal. Examples:

- a Low Choir child naming a dry Garden route;
- Corven Braid hiding the second contract schedule;
- a lamplighter recording the deliberate weakening of the Deep Bough;
- a Reliquary worker demonstrating the half-raised Kneeling Saint;
- a civic delegate consenting to one Common Measure handweight;
- a Warden ordering the public design rejected despite a successful balance trial.

Record the fragment's owner. Returning it later should matter socially even if it has already served as information.

## Safe camps

A camp in the Lantern Cistern or sealed Reliquary may delay one tremor advance only when all of the following are true:

- the local water state is stable;
- one defensible route is lit or physically sealed;
- smoke and memory vapor have an exit;
- a watch is assigned to a machine control rather than only a doorway; and
- the party spends one oil measure, has Senn's cooperation, or has made a durable Reliquary seal.

A careless full rest still advances the tremor clock.
