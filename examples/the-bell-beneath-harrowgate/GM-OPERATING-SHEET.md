# GM Operating Sheet: The Bell Beneath Harrowgate

This sheet is the shortest table-facing summary. Use the regional aids for detailed operations and the adventure source for full scenes.

## Premise

The Deep Bell is ringing itself apart beneath Harrowgate. Lady Vey funded a conditional Crown survey; Chief Factor Halren Coss and Councilor Rhun added a concealed race-for-possession rider, and Orren cut a loaded royal chain from an abridged sequence when Maelin Rook refused. Every uncontrolled toll moves pressure through water, ward-light, memory, and counterweights; the Low Choir pays an involuntary mnemonic cost. The party must descend, rescue people, operate the buried house, and decide what future burden Harrowgate will accept.

## Starting truths

- Two entrances are open: processional stair through the Chapterhouse and quarry cleft.
- The Bell is a pressure-release machine, not a summons.
- The damaged cycles are shortening; Tarrow expects the fourth toll before midnight unless operations below delay it.
- No single faction, claimant, artifact, or room provides a complete solution.
- Water, light, memory, physical load, legal authority, and consent move separately even when mechanisms try to combine them.

## Ask after every major operation

1. What becomes safer or available?
2. Where does the displaced water, light, memory, load, or authority go?
3. Which person, route, or resource becomes exposed?
4. Who must maintain the new state?

## Likely combat-capable scenes

- Quarry lamp hunter: cross, lure, rescue, or kill.
- Hall burden-bearers and bronze examiner: pay, contest, or survive the toll.
- Ropeworks load ghosts: correct the work sequence while machinery moves.
- Reliquary pallbearer and drowned load ghosts: rescue and control water.
- Salt Wardens: fight over command, shell, tablet, or gate without requiring extermination.
- Chapel appeal wings: operate brakes while roles and positions change.
- Grave defenses and Orren loyalists: divide claims and extract assets.
- Iron Cantor and Deep Rank: bound or break commands.
- Black Rain manifestations: stage pressure while routes attack operators.
- Deep Bell chain saints, burden echoes, and claimants: complete phase objectives.
- Mouth manifestations: enact or reject a settlement under nonhuman pressure.

## Core operating maxims

- Ward-light is both fence and memory channel.
- Reliquary saints can hold breath and move water.
- A Warden survives in shell, tablet, and witness.
- Warden command requires claim, muster, and accepted duty.
- The Garden makes food, water, and memory from the dead.
- Root shutters save the surface by spending Garden capacity.
- Memory cups require a willing carrier.
- Inherited names carry chosen duties, not ownership.
- Low Choir compact: witness, passage, provision, maintenance, limit, remedy.
- Chapel acceptance: authority, consent, duration, remedy.
- Quiet beast handling: appetite, boundary, return.
- Genealogies divide custody, maintenance, and release.
- Wells separate mass, load path, and timing.
- Iron commands require source, address, and limit.
- Avarra survives as Measure, Refusal, and Witness.
- Black Rain can be routed, delayed, or divided, not erased.
- Bell phases: Taking Breath, Drawing Load, Crown Descent, Sounding.
- Crown release requires transferring or spending its load.
- Mouth settlement: offering, boundary, return.

## Finale readiness checklist

The party need not complete every item. Missing items define costs.

### People and authority

- Surface evacuation or accepted exposure
- Low Choir position and witnesses
- Warden crews or substitutes
- Named maintainers
- Custody plan for Crown and evidence
- Authority, consent, duration, remedy for any replacement burden

### Physical systems

- Crown chain repaired, braced, or deliberately bypassed
- Wells mass, path, and timing
- Black Rain route
- Quarry or other pressure capacity
- Garden shutters prepared or consciously unavailable
- Ward-light and memory state
- Extraction or retreat route

### Knowledge

- Four Bell phases
- Avarra's three faculties
- Crown release locks or bypass
- Command grammar
- Consequence of any Well drop
- Population exposed by each final route

## Finale architectures

| Architecture | Preserves | Spends or risks | Required question |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Controlled continuance | Central Bell and immediate stability | Ongoing burden and reform campaign | Who bears the cost until the term ends? |
| Replacement burden | Central pressure mechanism without Crown | New frame, consent, maintainers | Can bearers withdraw and transfer duty? |
| Distributed stabilization | Crown release and visible divided systems | Permanent civic expense and complexity | Who funds and reviews every route? |
| Controlled collapse | End of Bell and old machinery | Districts, Garden, routes, deep history | Where will fracture go, and who is evacuated? |
| Crown release / claim | Crown custody or liberation | Nearest available load path | Who takes the load and who holds the Crown? |
| Mouth settlement | New relationship to fault phenomenon | Recurring offering and uncertainty | What boundary and return can be enforced? |

Hybrids are expected when preparation supports them.

## End-state questions

State four things plainly:

1. What happened to Harrowgate's people, districts, water, and future safety?
2. What happened to Low Choir memory, homes, and political standing?
3. Who controls, carries, destroys, divides, or loses the Crown and Avarra's faculties?
4. What duty remains, who accepted it, how long it lasts, and what remedy exists?

Then resolve Orren, Maelin, Warden command, household compacts, treasure custody, rescued specialists, skipped optional regions, and the next review date.
