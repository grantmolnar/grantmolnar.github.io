# Low Choir and Chapel Operating Aid

Use this sheet when the party reaches the Feast of Empty Chairs, negotiates a replacement compact, crosses the Inverted Chapel, rescues Merrit or Elra, or attempts to make a new burden legible to the lower machine.

## Starting state

- The Feast is **sheltered but divided**. The third toll took seventeen names; three have returned through the Garden.
- The Low Choir has twenty-three sound empty cups, eleven cracked listening vessels, and seven protected personal cups.
- The Warm Table has roughly three household-days of toothcap and salt greens before feeding guests or preparing evacuation.
- The Chapel is in **Inverted Rest**.
- Mourning Brake: intact. Breath Brake: wet but operable. Judgment Brake: controlled and trapped by Elra Mott. Service Brake: fouled and partly damaged.
- Merrit Dole is bound beneath the altar. Three appeal wings remain, one damaged.
- The Wells transfer door is sealed until Upright Hearing.

## Memory-cup operations

| Operation | Requirements | Result | Cost / risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Witness | Two independent witnesses, or one plus material corroboration | Stable testimony usable by Scriptorium, Chapel, Wardens, or Iron Choir | Repaired testimony remains marked as incomplete |
| Carry | Prepared cup and willing carrier | Borrow route, recognition, testimony, or response memory | One vivid personal memory becomes inaccessible until return |
| Return | Cup, compatible Iron Tongue, Garden root, or witnessed transfer | Borrowed memory leaves carrier; displaced personal memory returns | Broken vessel may scatter memory into light or water |
| Shield toll | Four prepared cups and four willing donors | Protect one household cluster, camp, or operating crew from one mnemonic discharge | Donated memories remain unavailable if cups are lost |

Coercive drinking does not create a stable carrier. It produces confusion, false familiarity, and unreliable proof.

## Inherited-name rule

An inherited name can preserve:

- a selected duty or debt;
- standing to testify;
- access to a keyed route, cup, or office;
- kinship continuity; or
- a claim to be heard where the erased person would otherwise vanish from the record.

It does not automatically transfer body, marriage, property, guilt, punishment, rank, or complete identity. Record what the bearer accepts, what witnesses recognize, and what the bearer explicitly refuses.

## Safe rotation

Control two adjacent brakes, or one brake plus the Wells counterchain, external rigging, water clutch, Ropeworks line, or sufficient distributed mass.

1. Clear stopping dogs.
2. Establish communication.
3. Provide counterweight.
4. Release adjacent brakes together.
5. Arrest at the next state.
6. Set both dogs before moving loads.

On failure, change position or state: early catch, hot brake, partial turn, guardian intervention, Merrit taking load, or unexpected movement in the Wells. Do not reduce every failure to lost time.

## Merrit Dole

Merrit's declaration was broad, emotional, and unauthorized. Treating it as permanent consent repeats the Chapel's original abuse.

Release methods:

- witnessed revocation at Mourning Turn;
- Nara's testimony about authority;
- temporary transfer followed by immediate formal release;
- counterweight and manual extraction;
- cut the bearer station; or
- catch and extract him during a controlled drop.

He offers mourning responses, Feast guidance, and two Iron Tongue names. He will not serve as permanent bearer.

## Elra Mott

Elra coordinates the Judgment Brake for any two useful assurances: protected route, written testimony protection, medical care, credible survivor news, ordinary salvage share, or recovery of her companions.

She carries Orren's order memory and can operate pivots or repair the Wells signal clutch. Threatening her can work immediately, but she will run the brake rather than be abandoned again.

## Appeal wings

Signal them through two tubes, present a valid appeal or command, assign them a bounded message, lure them with moving mass, darken their perception, repair the damaged wing, or destroy them.

In conflict they exchange positions, hook equipment, pin declared roles, and arrest motion. A repaired wing can carry one declaration or light load to the Wells or Iron Choir.

## Four acceptance declarations

| Station | Must name | Common defect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Authority | Who can establish the burden and the limits of that power | Grand title with no jurisdiction over actual bearers |
| Consent | Each contributing community or person and how they assented | Representative claims consent they do not possess |
| Duration | Term, inspection interval, transfer, and reconsideration | “Until safe” or “forever” without testable boundary |
| Remedy | Action after breach, withdrawal, overload, or abuse | Promise to negotiate later; no operator empowered to act |

A declaration cannot independently witness itself. The same speaker may fill multiple stations only with separate witnesses.

## Acceptance outcomes

- **Full recognition:** opens the Wells transfer door and makes the frame legible to lower machinery.
- **Partial recognition:** supports one toll, one operator, one route, or identifies the defective component.
- **Revised attempt:** remove weights, add remedy, change term, or replace withdrawn contribution during the test.
- **Forced acceptance:** works through Execution Turn but adds an execution clause seeking a living substitute under excess strain.
- **Abandoned attempt:** preserves the frame and leaves other finale architectures open.

The Chapel does not balance the Deep Bell. It records a burden that the Wells can receive.
