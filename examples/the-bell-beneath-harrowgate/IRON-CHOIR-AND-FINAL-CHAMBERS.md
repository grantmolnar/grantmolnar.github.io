# Iron Choir and Final Chambers

This aid runs the Choir of Iron Tongues, Black Rain Cistern, Deep Bell, and Mouth Below without reducing the finale to a hidden sequence. Track destinations, operators, and limits. Every successful operation moves a real burden somewhere.

## Choir command grammar

A reliable remote command needs all three terms.

| Term | Question | Valid examples | Failure when omitted |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Source | Who or what takes responsibility? | recognized Warden muster, Chapel acceptance, royal faculty, present operator | command repeats as a question or the Cantor supplies old authority |
| Address | What exact mechanism, person, or register is acted upon? | Mourning Bough, Returning Breath saint, Civic Well catch | command spreads to every similar target |
| Limit | When or under what condition does it stop? | until Crown Descent, one surge, until relieved by named operator | command continues until damage or stronger interruption |

The Choir can prepare, isolate, open, close, call, delay, or hold. It cannot create local capacity, consent, evacuation, or physical mass.

## Memory restoration

Restoration requires:

1. an identified memory fragment;
2. a willing recipient or suitable vessel; and
3. recognition by a living witness or durable record.

Choose how a returned memory arrives: vivid and disruptive, partial but integrated, or stored until voluntarily received. Unattached fragments drift, combine, or attract white name moths.

## Avarra faculties

| Faculty | Can do | Cannot decide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Measure | compare loads, test proportionality, fit transfers | who ought to bear the load |
| Refusal | deny coerced use, break unauthorized royal commands | how Harrowgate survives afterward |
| Witness | testify to surrender and later alterations | present political legitimacy by itself |

Two independent anchors can reconstruct a usable retained will. Suppressing one faculty creates a narrower and more dangerous adviser.

## Deep Bell phase procedure

Advance after a major failed operation, extended conflict, or unopposed command cascade. Prepared systems can delay or repeat one phase.

| Phase | What moves | Favored operations |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Taking Breath | light, air, heat, memory | issue commands, establish witnesses, set water routes, move replacements |
| Drawing Load | Ropeworks, Wells, bridges | splice chains, fit mass, distribute paths, rescue from tilting approaches |
| Crown Descent | Crown, royal locks, Black Rain | transfer load, fit replacement, free Crown, invoke Avarra faculties |
| Sounding | all open routes receive force | test settlement, complete collapse, survive impact, account for costs |

## Holding one phase

One of the following can delay a phase once before a local cost appears:

- bounded Iron Choir command;
- correctly timed Wells catch;
- Reliquary breath reserve;
- velvet hound consuming patterned vibration;
- operators physically holding a brake;
- permanent Well or Garden sacrifice.

State the cost before repeating a hold.

## Finale architecture matrix

| Architecture | Minimum physical preparation | Authority and consent | Primary unresolved cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Controlled continuance | royal chain, balanced Wells, lawful water route | bounded continuity term and operators | old system survives and requires reform |
| Replacement burden | sufficient frame, mass, fittings, transfer path | Chapel acceptance and continuing bearer consent | replacement can become a new sentence |

Hybrid endings are expected. Name every load path and every future maintainer.

## Crown release checklist

- Which path receives the Crown's physical mass?
- Which authority opens or forces the six locks?
- Are Measure, Refusal, and Witness together or divided?
- Who carries the Crown after release?
- What old commands remain available to the carrier?
- What remedy exists if custody is abused?

## Cascading survival

When an uncontrolled Sounding begins, each active team may save one category:

- people on an approach;
- Harrowgate above;
- Low Choir memory;
- Crown and retained faculties;
- a future operating system;
- access to the Mouth Below.

Saving a category assigns force elsewhere unless someone accepts a named extraordinary sacrifice.

## Mouth negotiation

Run each exchange as action and response.

1. Enact a small offering.
2. Show the Mouth's interpretation.
3. Correct or repeat the pattern.
4. Demonstrate the boundary.
5. Demonstrate the return and failure condition.

Three clear exchanges establish a provisional settlement. Additional exchanges increase precision and deepen the Mouth's expectation of the participants.

## Mouth settlement tests

Ask:

- Can Harrowgate actually repeat the offering?
- Is the boundary geographic, material, temporal, and specific?
- Is the return observable rather than promised?
- Who witnesses compliance?
- What happens when either side fails?
- Does any person, people, or inherited office become the new involuntary interpreter?

## Partial outcome prompts

A partial success should preserve some goals and assign remaining work.

- Bell stable for one season: who uses the time?
- Crown freed, load unresolved: what emergency begins above?
- Memories released, identities mixed: who performs the long restoration?
- Harrowgate saved, Low Choir displaced: what passage and provision were promised?
- Bell destroyed, Mouth awakened: what new warning pattern appears?
- Replacement works, consent disputed: who can halt or appeal it?
- Crown lost below: who follows, and what can now speak royal cadence?

## Final four answers

Before ending the adventure, state:

1. Harrowgate's physical and civic condition;
2. the Low Choir's memory, homes, and standing;
3. Crown and Avarra-faculty custody; and
4. the remaining duty, its term, and its remedy.
