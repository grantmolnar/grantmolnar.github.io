# Dungeon State Ledger

Keep this ledger beside the encounter map. It records the dungeon as a changing machine rather than a fixed sequence of cleared rooms.

## Tremor clock

| Step | State | Immediate effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Unsettled | Dust, minor slips, and creature migration. |
| 2 | Shifting | One easy vertical route becomes difficult until braced or rebalanced. |
| 3 | Black rain | Water reverses through one open conduit; change a connected room's water state. |
| 4 | Fourth toll | Harrowgate suffers a major collapse, memory bleed affects the dungeon, and the bell loses stability. |

Advance for an unsafe full rest, a major failed machine operation, abandoned prolonged combat, or hours spent on excavation or heavy salvage. A successful major brace, Warden crew, controlled release, or well-prepared safe camp may delay one advance.

## Water state

Choose one global state and note local exceptions.

- **Balanced:** intended levels.
- **Rising above:** Lantern Cistern and surface wells worsen; Black Rain pressure falls.
- **Rising below:** upper flooded regions drain; Black Rain and Mouth approaches worsen.
- **Venting:** pressure moves through Garden roots and quarry vents.
- **Ruptured:** water follows a new permanent route.

## Finale prerequisites

### Controlled continuance

- Crown chain repaired or securely spliced.
- Wells and Ropeworks sufficiently balanced.
- Water has a lawful release route.
- Operator or Warden crew available.
- Low Choir cost acknowledged or mitigated.

### Replacement burden

- Replacement design or sufficient material.
- Chapel acceptance procedure available.
- Wells fittings and skilled operator.
- Authority and consent recognized by control chains.
- Crown extraction route.

### Distributed stabilization

- Crown freed.
- Water and counterweights redistributed.
- Harrowgate commits ongoing labor and resources.
- Low Choir and Warden roles settled.
- Failure and maintenance plan established.

### Controlled collapse

- Harrowgate evacuation underway.
- Quarry vents opened.
- Garden root sluices prepared or replaced.
- Black Rain level and sequence known.
- Ropeworks or Wells demolition prepared.

### Mouth Below settlement

- Safe descent established.
- A negotiable burden, memory, or offering identified.
- Exit or rescue plan retained.
- Consequences for the bell and Harrowgate understood well enough to accept.
