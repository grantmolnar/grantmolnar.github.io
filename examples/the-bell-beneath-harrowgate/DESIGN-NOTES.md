# Design Notes: The Bell Beneath Harrowgate

## Design objective

This adventure is a dungeon crawl first. Its principal questions are spatial, operational, tactical, and moral:

- Which entrance does the party establish?
- Which routes remain usable after water, light, or counterweights change?
- Who is rescued, bypassed, bargained with, or left behind?
- Which optional depths are worth their time and danger?
- Which tools, allies, and obligations does the party carry into the finale?
- What kind of settlement replaces the burden the old dungeon imposed?

The dungeon should not behave like a sequence of rooms unlocked by correct deductions. Information matters because it reveals routes, operating procedures, faction positions, and consequences. The party should frequently know what a lever does and still have to decide whether to pull it.

Every major region should eventually provide five things:

1. a reason to enter beyond collecting clues;
2. at least two credible methods of traversal, negotiation, operation, or violence;
3. a resource, faction relationship, or system state that carries forward;
4. a cost the party can knowingly accept; and
5. at least one route that changes the shape of later exploration.

## Premise

For three nights, an ancient bell has rung beneath Harrowgate without rope or ringer. The first toll cracked chimneys and was mistaken for quarry settling. The second reversed the north well; the council closed streets and signaled below, but the Ember Company did not answer. The third collapsed a tenement and erased family names from living memory. At dawn, a dying porter returned with the first report that the Company itself had cut a royal chain. The shortening cycle is already rebuilding toward a fourth toll.

Five days ago, Lady Merrow Vey's recovery trust hired the Ember Company to verify the Crown of Measure and recover it only after the buried house was safe. Chief Factor Halren Coss secretly accepted Councilor Samet Rhun's accelerated-possession rider and issued a second payment schedule. Rhun supplied an abridged royal extraction sequence that omitted the unload and synchronization operations around the Crown Drum. Factor Orren Saye treated the outer chain as a release restraint and ordered it cut while the drums were live. Captain Maelin Rook resisted. The company split, the Water Drum reversed, the Memory Drum half-braked, and the first uncontrolled toll followed within the hour.

The adventurers enter now because the third toll and the porter's testimony have converted a hidden salvage failure into a public rescue and stabilization crisis. Lady Vey's blood claim is genuine, as is her responsibility for authorizing the secret expedition; she did not authorize Rhun's rider or the chain cut. The Salt Wardens claim custodial authority. The Low Choir bears the human cost of each toll. The town above wants to survive. None of these claims resolves the others.

## What is actually happening

The Deep Bell is an engineered pressure-release system. It distributes mountain strain through three linked classes of burden:

- **Water:** fault pressure becomes heat, black water, and controlled movement through cisterns and sluices.
- **Light and memory:** ward-light marks safe routes and stores the mnemonic discharge released when the bell sounds.
- **Counterweight and authority:** immense physical loads move only when the machine recognizes a declared operator and an accepted burden.

Queen Avarra once tried to command the underlying fault or intelligence now called the Mouth Below. After her defeat, the victors coerced her surrender, placed the Crown of Measure in the clapper cage, and used her retained authority to make the new system legible. Later custodians routed the mnemonic damage away from surface witnesses and into the labor community sealed below. Their descendants are the Low Choir.

The present failure is equally material and institutional. Lady Vey authorized a quiet, conditional recovery. Her factor and Councilor Rhun converted it into a race for possession; Orren accepted an incomplete operating sequence because possession promised leverage before public authorities could intervene. Rook's refusal was not abstract scruple: the three drums visibly carried live load. This division lets the evidence assign responsibility without reducing Lady Vey to a secret villain or Orren to inexplicable sabotage.

The machine is therefore neither purely magical nor purely mechanical. Engineering, law, oath, and memory are materially connected. This is not an excuse for arbitrary puzzle logic. Each operation should have observable inputs, outputs, and displaced burdens.

## The three operating systems

### Water

Track the broad state of the water system rather than exact cubic volume:

- **Balanced:** local water levels match their intended rooms.
- **Rising above:** lower pressure is being pushed toward the Lantern Cistern and surface wells.
- **Rising below:** upper regions drain while Black Rain and the Mouth approaches worsen.
- **Venting:** pressure is moving toward the quarry and Garden roots.
- **Ruptured:** water follows a new permanent route created during play.

Local sluices can make one region differ from the global state. Changes should open one opportunity while creating one risk.

### Ward-light and memory

Track where the lamp-tree sends light:

- **Full:** upper routes are safer; stored Low Choir memory continues moving toward the Iron Tongues.
- **Selective:** the party chooses protected routes and leaves others active.
- **Failing:** lights gutter according to pressure and creature activity.
- **Extinguished:** many creatures move, but memory can be released or redirected.
- **Burning out of control:** light becomes a spreading hazard and consumes a limited resource.

Portable ward oil is valuable because it allows local exceptions. The correct use of light should depend on where the party intends to go and what it has already released.

### Counterweight and burden

Track the Crown, Water, and Memory drums plus the lower wells:

- **Balanced:** intended travel and pressure transfer are possible.
- **Braced:** safe in the short term, but one or more lifts or mechanisms cannot move.
- **Redistributed:** a named region or population bears more of the load.
- **Severed:** one subsystem is isolated at permanent cost.
- **Cascading:** each major delay or violent operation advances failure.

An accepted burden is both material and juridical. A replacement must have enough physical mass or magical force, but it must also be recognized through the Chapel and control chains. This gives social and archival work physical relevance without allowing rhetoric to substitute for engineering.

## The fourth-toll clock

The clock is a pressure mechanism, not a demand for speed at all costs. Tarrow's prediction of a fourth toll before midnight is the damaged cycle's present trajectory, not a fixed stopwatch; operations below can hasten or delay it.

1. **Unsettled:** minor slips and migrations.
2. **Shifting:** a route or mechanism becomes harder until addressed.
3. **Black rain:** water reverses through one open connection.
4. **The fourth toll:** surface collapse, memory bleed, and loss of bell stability.

Advance it for major time expenditures, unsafe full rests, abandoned loud conflicts, or failed operations against the bell systems. Do not advance it for every careful search or conversation. Players should be able to buy time through bracing, local repairs, negotiated labor, shortcuts, and controlled releases.

The fourth toll is not automatic failure. It worsens the dungeon and surface cost, changes faction positions, and removes some clean outcomes.

## Factions

### The Ember Company

A professional expedition broken by the chain-cutting order.

- **Captain Maelin Rook:** rescue her people, contain the damage, and expose the hidden rider, abridged sequence, and people who converted a conditional survey into a race for possession.
- **Factor Orren Saye:** recover the crown before civic or Warden authorities can prevent it; he knew the possession rider, accepted the incomplete sequence, and still believes physical custody is the only leverage records cannot be made to forget.
- **Engineer Pell Varo:** repair enough machinery to extract survivors and prevent a catastrophic toll; he can perform only a limited number of major jobs.
- **Scattered survivors:** some remained loyal, some followed Orren, and some deserted both.

The company should not become a single enemy encounter. Its fragments create rescues, bargains, ambushes, technical help, and testimony.

### The Salt Wardens

Preserved custodians whose memories reside in salt tablets.

- **Continuance:** stabilize the bell and keep it operating until a lawful replacement exists.
- **Release:** end Queen Avarra's sentence and the Low Choir's burden even if Harrowgate must accept evacuation or loss.
- **Literal obedience:** execute old orders because no accepted authority has revoked them.

The Wardens can provide guides, soldiers, recognition marks, and operators. Their internal command problem should be solvable through proof, succession, persuasion, force, or transfer of duty.

### The Low Choir

Descendants of laborers whose memories are consumed by the toll.

They are not an undifferentiated oppressed faction. Some want immediate silence. Some fear losing the systems that provide light, food, and water. Some want recognition and a new compact with Harrowgate. Some want the crown because their bloodline is part of the chained genealogy.

Their principal leverage is not secret knowledge alone. They occupy routes, maintain the Garden, understand memory vessels, and can make or break a humane replacement settlement.

### Harrowgate and House Vey

The town is physically absent from most dungeon rooms but present through the clock, rescue capacity, surface preparations, and promises made in its name.

Lady Vey's claim is genuine enough to matter and incomplete enough to contest. The adventure should not make her secretly false or secretly rightful. Returning the crown may be coherent if paired with a replacement plan. Denying her may be coherent if the party can justify who bears authority instead.

## Topology

The dungeon is vertical but not linear. Two starts lead into an upper ring. Four middle regions create lateral loops. Three deep control regions converge on the finale. Optional regions provide tools, treasure, and alternate claims.

## Region rhythm

The intended rhythm alternates kinds of pressure:

- commission and route choice;
- vertical hazard and rescue;
- social-puzzle gate;
- major machine operation;
- archive and claims;
- water and resource management;
- faction command;
- ecology and lateral movement;
- refuge and moral negotiation;
- vertical set piece;
- optional creature toolbox;
- optional treasure and claimant conflict;
- engineering repair;
- memory control;
- fault pressure;
- final operating choice;
- optional super-depth.

A table should not need to visit every encounter. The mandatory structure gives resilient access to the deep machine; optional regions should improve or complicate outcomes rather than conceal the only workable answer.

## Encounters

Combat remains important but should use each region's material identity.

- Burden-bearer statues grapple and redistribute equipment.
- Load ghosts repeat fatal labor and can be answered by accepting responsibility.
- Water threats exploit depth, air, current, and contamination.
- Wardens fight as disciplined crews who close routes and operate defenses.
- Quiet beasts consume specific forms of excess and can be redirected.
- Deep conflicts change machinery state while they occur.

Avoid filling every corridor with unrelated monsters. Wandering pressure should arise from faction movement, released maintenance creatures, toll migration, water reversal, and consequences of the party's own operations.

## Treasure

Treasure should be extractable, disputable, and useful before sale.

- ward oil, silver memory wire, salt tablets, maintenance plates, and compact winches are dungeon tools;
- royal metal, reliquary work, old weapons, and the Narrow Grave treasury have clear value but competing claims;
- quiet-beast organs and black-water minerals are dangerous salvage;
- memory cups and Iron Tongues may contain irreplaceable personal or civic records;
- the Crown of Measure is both treasure and a load-bearing component.

Extraction matters. A functioning Ropeworks, braced quarry route, allied labor force, or surface stretcher team changes what the party can actually keep.

## Endings

The completed finale architectures grew from the distinctions protected in the foundation:

1. **Controlled continuance:** repair the chain and operate the bell while negotiating reforms and limits.
2. **Replacement burden:** fit a new accepted clapper burden and free the crown.
3. **Distributed stabilization:** free the crown and replace the bell's role through water, counterweight, and ongoing civic work.
4. **Controlled collapse:** evacuate, open vents, and destroy the old system without destroying most of Harrowgate.
5. **Royal claim:** give or lose the crown to a claimant who uses Avarra's retained authority.
6. **Mouth settlement:** descend below the old machine and bargain with or redirect the underlying phenomenon.
7. **Cascading failure:** survive an uncontrolled toll and choose what can still be rescued.

A good ending should answer four questions: what happened to Harrowgate, what happened to the Low Choir, who controls or carries the crown, and what duty remains after the party leaves.

## Session 2 operating decisions

### Light is allocated safety

The Lantern Cistern now divides the ward network into four boughs rather than treating illumination as a global switch. Each bough protects a recognizable district, consumes or yields ward-oil, and carries memory toward the Iron Tongues. This creates several legitimate policies:

- preserve broad safety and continue the mnemonic extraction;
- darken selected districts and accept creature migration;
- release stored memories deliberately;
- carry oil away for camps, tools, and deep mechanisms; or
- weaponize contaminated oil at the risk of fire and memory vapor.

Wick-eaters make the boundary between light and darkness more important than either state alone. They should migrate in response to the party's map rather than appear as unrelated wandering monsters.

### Water is displaced danger

The Reliquary uses four broad levels: high, middle, low, and ruptured. Each favors different work. High water weakens ghosts and opens the royal niche. Middle water supports rescue and operation. Low water exposes salvage and the Chapel route. Rupture creates speed and irreversible lower pressure.

The six copper saints permit local control. This prevents the global Water Drum from becoming the only meaningful lever and allows a prepared party to rescue Rhea, preserve air, and stabilize the room without treating Black Rain as an invisible sink for every problem.

### Replacement is now material

The Common Measure gives the replacement-burden ending concrete components and dependencies:

- voluntary civic weights;
- a copper acceptance collar;
- a transfer proof in the Pilgrim's Yoke;
- recognition at the Inverted Chapel;
- physical balance at the Counterweight Wells; and
- living maintainers after the crisis.

The design was rejected politically rather than mechanically. Recovering it therefore creates leverage without revealing a secret perfect answer. The party must still secure consent, authority, material, and ongoing labor.

### Named survivors carry distinct capabilities

- **Tavia Hest** is a blinded scout who can guide dark routes, identify Company marks, and expose the overlapping contract.
- **Rhea Colm** is a surgeon and cautious witness who can stabilize later rescues and distinguish mnemonic harm from ordinary confusion.
- **Lamplighter Senn** is an exact custodian of an unjust account, useful precisely because his duties conflict.

None is required for progress. Each makes a different kind of later play easier and gives rescue a concrete operational consequence.

## Session 3 operating decisions

### Warden personhood is distributed continuity

The Salt Wardens now have a concrete ontology that can support play without pretending the adventure has solved personhood abstractly. A continuing Warden consists of three related forms:

- the preserved shell, which retains bodily habit and affective residue;
- the salt tablet, which preserves selected memory, name, duty, and machine recognition; and
- witnesses who recognize that shell and tablet as one continuing person.

This makes damage and transfer operationally useful while preserving moral friction. A shell without a tablet is not a disposable automaton merely because it lacks judgment. A tablet speaking from brine is not automatically entitled to command a body it no longer inhabits. Witnesses can restore continuity, but they can also lie, suppress testimony, or impose an identity convenient to a faction.

Repair deliberately preserves uncertainty. Missing memory may be marked as unknown, supplemented by named testimony, or recovered from an external mnemonic source. It cannot be silently invented. Transfer into a relief shell remains possible but may produce bodily dissonance. Temporary transfer into a living guest can create emergency command at a personal cost. Release is a voluntary end to duty, not destruction by euphemism.

Ora Pell and Maston Grey distinguish two superficially similar blank sentinels. Ora removed her tablet to prevent her body being used for an abusive order and does not want automatic rejoining. Maston's tablet was taken to erase testimony and demands restoration. The party therefore cannot adopt one universal repair rule without choosing whose consent matters.

### Command is coalition plus bounded obligation

The Barracks command chair recognizes three independent forms:

1. a claim embodied in a command or temporary tablet;
2. witnessed assent from the ranks; and
3. a specific duty the machinery can test.

No single crown seal, recovered tablet, vote, or combat victory is therefore the only key. Prefect Quoin can be restored, one faction can gain command, two leaders can divide authority, a living guest can accept a narrow emergency duty, or a muster challenge can compel local obedience. The party may also force or bypass the inner gate and accept continuing Warden opposition.

Every command route should sharpen a later issue:

- Vesra supplies the strongest repair and Wells support but resists premature Crown removal.
- Dalca supplies release witnesses and Garden cooperation but rejects restoration of the old sentence.
- Cleth supplies pursuit and Deep Rank control but privileges royal authority and may militarize Low Choir routes.
- Quoin confirms a release clause and joint emergency authority but cannot recover a missing deciding vote.
- Joint command gains the broadest workforce while carrying explicit vetoes into the finale.
- A guest marshal turns command into a burden a player character must later relinquish or redefine.

Forward movement never depends on winning the Barracks' political dispute. Force and bypass remain valid, with alarms, pursuit, and loss of skilled crews as visible costs.

### The Garden is infrastructure already serving people

The Garden of Teeth is not treasure growing unattended in a tomb. Its three beds perform linked work:

- the Jaw Catch removes physical contamination and grows toothcap food;
- the Silver Root catches names, relationships, and mnemonic heat; and
- the Quiet Loam cools water, grows salt greens, and preserves command-responsive routes.

This lets exploration, harvesting, repair, and destruction share one accounting system. Toothcaps are rations, but also household food. Silver root repairs tablets and memory vessels, but also maintains filtration. Bone loam can replace counterweight mass, but is currently stabilizing water and feeding crops. The GM records household-days, water batches, route loss, or memory release rather than treating these resources as free loot.

The name hazard attacks recognition and relationship before factual memory. It escalates visibly and has several preventive and restorative methods. Offering a living name can produce immediate leverage, but it is never the mandatory toll for passage. Work names, mouth covers, cooled water, route rhythms, ward-light, salt, upper roots, and keeper guidance all support ordinary dungeon problem solving.

### Controlled collapse now has graduated costs

The Garden contains three pressure shutters rather than one sacrificial switch.

- Upper Jaw buys modest relief through abandoned quarry space.
- Widow's Run spends stored memory and risks a surface mnemonic event.
- Last Teeth makes the safest full collapse sequence possible by sacrificing most Garden food, ancestral remains, filtration, and dry routes.

The party can prepare shutters without opening them. Evacuating food, moving tablets, bracing routes, repairing remote control lines, and coordinating surface vents all reduce the human cost. This makes collapse a campaign-scale engineering choice assembled across the dungeon rather than an emergency lever introduced in the finale.

## Session 4 operating decisions

### The Low Choir is a set of households, not one negotiating voice

The Feast of Empty Chairs now turns the Low Choir's broad political range into four overlapping household positions. The Warm Table protects food, water, and a survivable transition. The Open Cup demands an end to involuntary tolls and prepares evacuation. The Kept Chain seeks a bounded civic compact. The Narrow Line insists that buried genealogy and crown disposition be heard together.

These are not four mutually exclusive parties. A person can eat with one household, entrust records to another, and support a third outcome. This prevents the GM from reducing the region to a single approval check. The party negotiates for specific labor, witnesses, routes, weights, food, and consent. Unanimity is neither expected nor required.

Nara-of-the-Seventh-Name is the principal compact advocate, but not the Low Choir's queen. Bera Loam, Tern-of-One-Name, and Illa Crownless each have resources and red lines that remain coherent even when they disagree with Nara. Their arguments are about material transition costs and enforceable authority, not abstract alignment labels.

### Memory vessels preserve testimony by moving cost, not abolishing it

Memory cups now support five operations: listening, witnessed stabilization, voluntary carrying, return or redirection, and toll shielding. Carrying a memory temporarily displaces one of the carrier's own vivid recollections. Coercive transfer produces an unstable record rather than a convenient domination tool.

This keeps memory useful in ordinary dungeon play. A cup may supply a route rhythm, recognition, testimony, or acceptance response. It may also protect a camp from one toll. The party can choose to carry a memory, ask an NPC, preserve the cup, transfer it to an Iron Tongue, root it in the Garden, or risk releasing it into water or light. No one solution is assumed.

Inherited names follow the same anti-possession principle. A bearer can accept standing, kinship continuity, testimony, or a named duty without inheriting the original person's body, guilt, property, punishment, or entire identity. This distinction will matter in the King's Narrow Grave and any later royal settlement.

### A humane compact is a bundle of concrete obligations

The Low Choir's six compact weights are witness, passage, provision, maintenance, limit, and remedy. They give the Common Measure social and operating content without making Nara's exact proposal the only valid replacement design. Another coalition can create equivalent obligations if actual bearers consent and consequences are explicit.

No single surface authority can currently promise everything. Marr can bind emergency civic acts. Tarrow can commit technical labor. Lady Vey can bind House Vey's resources and claim. A recognized Warden commander can bind buried operators. Low Choir households can bind only their own labor, memory, and weights. The likely solution is therefore a coalition of bounded promises rather than a miraculous signature.

### The Chapel is both route machine and procedural machine

The Inverted Chapel now has five states: Inverted Rest, Mourning Turn, Upright Hearing, Execution Turn, and Dropped Nave. Four pivot brakes and the Wells counterchain control movement. Different states open different routes, reduce or intensify execution compulsions, and shift physical load into the lower machine.

The party can also bypass rotation through underside traverses, a tensioned judgment chain, a Low Choir mourning net, a Reliquary breath lift, the condemned chute, the Wells counterchain, or deliberate structural damage. Rotation is valuable because it preserves routes and acceptance capacity, not because the adventure withholds progress until the correct orientation is found.

Merrit Dole and Elra Mott create two human operating problems. Merrit demonstrates that a dramatic statement is not durable consent and that revocation must be possible. Elra controls one brake because Orren abandoned a compromised subordinate, not because every rival delver needs to become a combat encounter. Both can be rescued, negotiated with, bypassed, or sacrificed with visible consequences.

### Acceptance tests legibility, not moral purity

At Upright Hearing, a candidate burden needs four witnessed declarations: bounded authority, specific bearer consent, duration and transfer terms, and an enforceable remedy. Material fittings and living maintainers remain necessary. The Chapel recognizes a burden so the Wells and control chains can receive it; it does not physically solve the finale.

Forced acceptance remains possible in Execution Turn through royal authority, Warden command, or brute force. The result carries an execution clause that seeks a living substitute when strain exceeds the missing consent or remedy. This is a dark but functional option, not a disguised automatic failure. The party should understand the clause before choosing it.

## Session 5 operating decisions

### Living maintenance tools remain creatures, not consumable powers

The quiet beasts are defined by appetite, boundary, and return. This gives each creature a legible operating procedure without turning it into a system-specific ability. Cinderback consumes heat, the velvet hounds consume patterned vibration, the white moths consume unattached memory, and Sable consumes contagious alarm. Each solves a class of dungeon problem and becomes dangerous in a related, predictable way after the named excess is gone.

A release therefore creates responsibility rather than a free advantage. The party must decide who handles the beast, which route it crosses, where the work ends, and what becomes its new target if return fails. Killing a beast yields limited salvage but removes its judgment and future utility. Leaving the Menagerie alone remains a fully valid choice.

### Royal law is intentionally divided among three real claims

The Narrow Grave does not reveal that Lady Vey is either secretly illegitimate or secretly absolute. The Golden Branch gives her a genuine custody and representational claim. The Burden Chain gives operating standing to those who actually maintained and paid for the bell. The Ash Witness preserves Avarra's surrender limits and the standing of people entrusted with those terms.

These jurisdictions overlap without collapsing. A coalition can divide Crown custody, machine authority, funds, and release testimony. A claimant can also reject that arrangement and use force, but the adventure will not reward the GM for announcing one hidden true heir.

Aldren Harrow's retained voice embodies the old regime's desire to keep victory indivisible. It is useful evidence and dangerous command capacity, not a complete person whose judgment settles the present.

### Treasure carries both mass and future use

The Grave's six crown-bars can serve as compatible counterweight material, but the same wealth could finance evacuation, repair, provision, passage, remedy, or dynastic restoration. The maintenance trust and debt records make treasure extraction politically and operationally consequential.

The Wells and the treasury defenses measure different things. The Wells care whether material supplies mass through a viable path. The Grave cares whether equivalent value or obligation remains in the trust. A party may satisfy one system and violate the other. This distinction keeps salvage from becoming a single optimization problem.

### Counterweight repair has three independent dimensions

Every major Wells operation must account for mass, load path, and timing. Enough weight in the wrong chain harms a different population or structure. A lawful accepted burden without enough mass is legible but physically insufficient. A correct design moved outside a safe brake window shocks the bell.

The Royal, Civic, and Funerary Wells make those differences visible. They also connect prior work: Chapel acceptance supplies recognized paths, the Reliquary and Black Rain supply water mass, the Ropeworks supplies timing and upper transfer, the Garden supplies pressure and material, the Choir supplies memory and remote commands, and optional regions supply tools or compatible metal.

### Dropping a well is a controlled sacrifice, not a failure state

Each final drop buys immediate time by making one category of loss permanent. A Royal drop spends crown routes and clean restoration. A Civic drop spends distributed-settlement machinery and buried infrastructure. A Funerary drop spends stored memory and worsens lower pressure.

The operation should always remain available under sufficient access, even after partial failures. It narrows future endings rather than ending play. The party should know which loss it is choosing before the final release whenever it has secured ordinary operating information.



## Session 6 operating decisions

### Command is responsibility made explicit

The Iron Choir does not grant arbitrary remote power. A reliable command needs a recognized source, a specific address, and a limit. This grammar connects Warden muster, Chapel acceptance, and operating practice while exposing the old custodians' central abuse: involuntary commands were often designed without a stopping condition.

Breaking the Choir protects the dungeon from remote coercion but also destroys coordination and may erase unique memories. Controlling it creates leverage that can itself become sovereign power. Restoring memory requires identification, a willing recipient or vessel, and living or durable recognition; the machinery cannot decide belonging by itself.

### Avarra is divided capacity, not a final-answer ghost

Queen Avarra survives as Measure, Refusal, and Witness. Measure compares loads and consequences. Refusal denies coerced use. Witness preserves surrender terms and historical testimony. The three can be reconstructed, separated, suppressed, carried, or destroyed.

This makes her morally and operationally important without allowing one spectral monarch to settle every dispute. Avarra wants her sentence ended and opposes replacing it with another involuntary burden. She can accept bounded emergency continuance to prevent immediate mass death, but she will not certify dynastic absolutism or promise mastery over the Mouth.

### Pressure always has a recipient

The Black Rain Cistern translates fault strain into visible routes. Quarry venting, Reliquary loading, Mouth feeding, and foundation transfer all move pressure rather than deleting it. Water level and total pressure are tracked separately so drainage cannot masquerade as resolution.

Safe quarry venting is assembled across the adventure: surface mouths, evacuation, Garden shutters, and staged release. Rushed venting remains possible, but its local disaster is named in advance whenever the party has gathered ordinary operating information.

### The finale has timed windows but no prescribed script

The Deep Bell moves through Taking Breath, Drawing Load, Crown Descent, and Sounding. Each phase favors different operations. Preparation creates delays, alternate routes, and parallel capacity rather than a checklist of mandatory keys.

Six major finale architectures and cascading survival are fully specified. They can be hybridized. Every architecture answers where physical load goes, what authority is recognized, what happens to Low Choir memory, and what future maintenance or remedy remains.

### The Crown is authority and mass

The Crown cannot leave its load behind. Chapel recognition without physical mass is insufficient; mass without a viable path is dangerous; timing without consent can complete a coercive transfer. The cleanest endings align all three, but desperate extraction remains possible through permanent sacrifice, collapse, or personal burden.

Crown custody is deliberately unresolved by engineering. Lady Vey, Harrowgate, Wardens, Low Choir households, Avarra's retained will, or another trust may receive it. Each choice creates a different political continuation.

### The Mouth learns recurrence rather than intention

A Mouth settlement requires an offering, a boundary, and a return. Negotiation occurs through repeated demonstrations, not ordinary debate. The Mouth remains more responsive than a natural hazard and less human than a person.

The old Bell made a repeated exchange look like machinery. A new settlement can be more explicit and less coercive, but it cannot remove ongoing duty. Unbounded offerings, walking cadence, false returns, and royal recurrence provide campaign-scale failure modes rather than arbitrary annihilation.

## Session 7 operating decisions

### The demonstration must exercise disagreement, not certify an answer

The Open Chain was designed so that each member is correct about one category of hidden cost and tempted to make that category supreme. Dema locates physical consequences but may accept emergency coercion. Calve protects consent and release but may confuse liberation with abandonment. Ilyra protects custody and evidence but may turn defined custody into private title. Torren keeps named people visible but may postpone structural decisions indefinitely. Miren creates limits and remedy but may mistake a complete clause for a living coalition.

The demonstrated settlement is therefore a negotiated hybrid none proposed at the entrance. It is intentionally noncanonical. The party uses the Common Measure as a temporary governor rather than a permanent replacement, frees the Crown, closes involuntary memory intake, preserves the Bell without allowing it to sound, and distributes its work into visible civic systems.

### Revisits are proofs that the dungeon is reactive

The canonical route revisits the Ropeworks, Barracks, Chapel, Feast, Wells, Black Rain, Quarry, Garden, and Chapterhouse. Each return follows a state change generated elsewhere:

- Reliquary water changes the Water Drum.
- Garden tablets change Warden command.
- Grave evidence changes Chapel recognition.
- Feast commitments change Wells operation.
- Wells preparation changes the compact's material credibility.
- Choir knowledge changes Black Rain commands.
- Black Rain staging changes Quarry and Garden work.
- Surface evacuation changes the final vent plan.

A revisit is not repetition for its own sake. It lets players see earlier decisions become infrastructure.

### Optionality should remain visible when skipped

The demonstration enters the King's Narrow Grave because its custody evidence, compatible mass, and Orren confrontation suit this party's priorities. It skips the Menagerie and Mouth.

The skipped regions still affect play. Menagerie principles are discovered through other evidence, and distant beast activity remains possible. The Mouth route and settlement grammar are established, then deliberately rejected because the party lacks a bounded offering and return. Optional regions are leverage and campaign continuations, not concealed keys or content that vanishes when unvisited.

### Wandering pressure should create choices

The pressure procedures advance systems, routes, people, and factions rather than subtracting abstract resources. Every event answers which system moved, where its consequence went, who noticed, and what new choice appeared.

Encounter tables are grouped by operating region and define objectives such as crossing, rescue, holding a phase, preserving evidence, redirecting a creature, or bounding a command. Combat remains available without making extermination the only complete result.

### Extraction is a state change

Substantial salvage is adjudicated through object, carrier, route, prior function, custody, and delay. Crown-bars are wealth and mass. Memory is evidence and personal recollection. Warden tablets are records and persons. Quiet beasts are living tools with appetites and return requirements.

The treasure model does not forbid portable reward. It prevents valuable structural, political, or personal functions from disappearing when an object enters an inventory.

### Safe camp requires operating safety

A camp must secure the local mechanism as well as the entrances. The Lantern requires stable water, light or seals, smoke egress, and a control watch. The Barracks requires truce and tablet custody. The Feast requires household invitation and resource accounting. The Wells require active operators.

Rest may soften one pressure consequence when the party has built a genuine camp. It never freezes the whole dungeon.

### The journal demonstrates correction without erasure

The canonical journal initially records a permanent Funerary Well drop, then appends a correction after the table verifies that only automatic mnemonic intake was severed and the physical Well remained braced. The mistaken event remains raw history; the active projection excludes it.

The correction is not decorative. It preserves a finale path and demonstrates that records can be authoritative while remaining corrigible.

### Completion means operational breadth, not exhaustive discovery

The demonstrated party spots 175 of 222 clues, misses eleven opportunities during specific visits, leaves forty-seven clues unseen, establishes all fifty revelations, and never visits two available optional encounters. The eighteen second-look additions are alternate operating evidence rather than retroactive journal discoveries. The route shows dense clue support without implying that a real group must discover every authored clue or enter every region.

## Session 8 voice and coherence decisions

### The dungeon speaks through work

The final prose treats machinery as labor made durable. Chains, cups, tablets, roots, brakes, gutters, and weights are never decorative symbols alone; each performs a task, records a claimant, or reveals who receives a consequence. Mythic images remain concrete enough for a GM to operate at the table.

### Every institution has a sentence habit

- Harrowgate officials speak through warrants, districts, timetables, and omissions.
- Salt Wardens speak through conditions, ending clauses, imperatives, and muster forms.
- Low Choir speakers attach every principle to food, passage, names, witnesses, and remedy.
- Ember Company survivors speak through ropes, loads, exits, contracts, and the names of people left below.
- Royal claimants divide custody, ownership, use, command, and evidence—except when desire tempts them to merge those powers.
- Stored voices repeat exact phrases while losing the person who once supplied their limit.

### Revelation titles are table maxims

The fifty titles now function as compact remembered conclusions: *Pressure moves; it does not vanish*; *The Crown cannot leave its load behind*; *Every appetite outlives its task*. Descriptions retain the complete operating content, and identifiers preserve all journal and graph relationships.

### Clues remain evidence, not labels

The fifty-nine early route clues no longer repeat the same generic operating-record sentence. Each now identifies a physical carrier—stake line, rung mark, petition plate, muster chain, silver wick, drowned register, fault vane, or iron address—and tells the GM how the evidence is found. The clue topology is unchanged.

### The victory remains deliberately unfinished

The voice pass does not beautify distributed maintenance into a frictionless solution. Harrowgate survives through crews, money, inspections, corrigible records, and authority that must be renewed or dismantled. The final image is civic work continuing after the grand machinery falls silent.
