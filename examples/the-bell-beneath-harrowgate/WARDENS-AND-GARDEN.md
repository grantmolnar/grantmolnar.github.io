# Salt Wardens and Garden of Teeth Operating Aid

Use this sheet when the party enters the Salt Barracks, handles a salt tablet, crosses the Garden, or prepares the root pressure shutters. The two regions are linked: the Barracks depends on Garden brine and memory fiber, while the Garden contains missing Warden tablets and can bypass the inner gate.

## Starting state

### Salt Barracks

- Twenty-three Wardens remain capable of judgment in the Barracks: twenty factional Wardens, Heth Brine, and two unaligned salt-room attendants.
- Sister Vesra has nine Continuance supporters, Prior Dalca has six Release supporters, and Sergeant Cleth Varn has five literal-order supporters.
- Quartermaster Heth Brine maintains the salt room and relief shells without accepting any faction's unrestricted command.
- Ora Pell and Maston Grey patrol as blank sentinels. Their tablets are in the Garden.
- Prefect Ardel Quoin's shell is sealed beneath the empty command chair. His cracked command tablet is in the Garden's Bitter Bed.
- Three additional Deep Rank Wardens have gone below under Orren Saye's sealed order.
- The inner gate recognizes Orren's inspection sequence and warns the deep works about unrecognized command claims.

### Garden of Teeth

- The Garden is tended but the Jaw Catch is overloaded by Reliquary pulses.
- The Silver Root remains functional and holds a dangerous accumulation in the Widow Root.
- The Quiet Loam produces gray water and food, but Orren's command phrase has left a brittle route through it.
- Edda-of-the-Warm-Stone and Kell Nine-Spoons are present with three other keepers.
- Upper Jaw, Widow's Run, and Last Teeth are closed but physically operable.
- The Ossuary lamp bough begins lit unless changed in the Lantern Cistern.

## Warden continuity

A functioning Warden has three related forms.

| Form | What it carries | Failure when isolated |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Preserved shell | Bodily habit, sensation, reflex, emotional residue | Can repeat orders and tactics but cannot judge exceptions or identify its own present consent. |
| Salt tablet | Name, selected memory, sworn duty, machine-recognition language | Can testify in brine but has no embodied judgment and may be mistaken for the whole person. |
| Witnessed muster | Social and legal recognition that shell and tablet continue one person | Can restore standing or continuity, but may suppress uncertainty or impose a convenient identity. |

Do not resolve a tablet scene by asking only whether the magic works. Ask who consents, what evidence survives, what remains uncertain, and who will recognize the result.

## Tablet operations

| Operation | Requirements | Result | Risk or cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Read | Cool clean brine and a listening witness | Tablet recites surviving record. | Sequence and feeling may be absent; Garden-grown material may imitate certainty. |
| Rejoin | Tablet, recognized shell, and witnesses | Restores judgment and machine standing. | Hurried work may confuse recent sequence; involuntary rejoining can repeat an abuse. |
| Repair | Filtered Garden brine, one recovered memory, witness testimony | Grows stable salt across cracks. | Missing facts must remain unknown or explicitly attributed; false testimony becomes structural. |
| Transfer | Relief shell or living temporary bearer, tablet, consent, witnesses | Preserves duty and record in a new body. | Bodily dissonance, disputed continuity, or command pressure on a living bearer. |
| Release | Clean water, stated name, relinquished duty, witnesses | Ends preservation and dissolves the tablet into recoverable memory water. | Irreversible death of the shell; dispute over custody of released memories. |
| Counterfeit / forced fit | Tablet and an unrecognized shell | May operate simple mechanisms briefly. | Dissonance, fracture, hostile witnesses, and false command recognition. |

### Repair principle

Repair may restore material and connect evidence. It cannot create a missing vote, feeling, or event. Mark repaired intervals as:

- recovered from the tablet;
- supplied by a named witness;
- supplied by an external memory vessel; or
- still unknown.

## Command recognition

The command chair evaluates three forms independently.

1. **Claim:** a command tablet, temporary tablet, or recognized lawful office.
2. **Muster:** witnessed assent from at least one functioning Warden in two ranks.
3. **Duty:** a bounded obligation the buried house can test.

| Recognitions present | Authority |
| --- | --- |
| One | The chair carries speech but issues no obedience. |
| Two | Local emergency command until the next toll or until the stated duty ends. |
| Three | Durable Barracks command and lower-system recognition. |

A vague duty such as “obey us” or “save everyone” fails the plumb. A bounded duty names the people, system, limit, or ending condition involved.

## Warden conflict behavior

Wardens fight as a room-scale unit.

- Gate Rank closes routes and divides groups with petition shields.
- Balance Rank changes floor tension and redistributes carried weight.
- Mourning Rank exposes active memories with dry salt.
- Deep Rank sounds commands into distant mechanisms.
- Blank sentinels repeat the last successful tactic until their order, route, or tablet state changes.

Violence should affect command, personnel, and later operations. A defeated faction does not vanish from the story if its sealed orders or deep crews remain active.

## Garden safe crossing

The Garden never requires a true name for ordinary passage.

- Use copper or waxed-cloth mouth covers.
- Communicate by sign or temporary work names.
- Keep water cool and moving.
- Follow keeper route rhythms.
- Use the lit Ossuary bough, accepting continued memory transmission.
- Salt a narrow lane, spending part of a harvest.
- Traverse the upper roots if they remain stable.
- Flood a channel and cross by pallet, accepting displaced contamination.

### Name-hazard escalation

1. Teeth whisper work names and titles.
2. A trusted voice speaks a true name.
3. The Garden names a relationship or duty.
4. Someone briefly forgets why the exposed person matters.
5. A cap, bridge, or pale likeness grows from the offered identity.

Stop escalation with cooling, silence, salt, ward-light, physical removal, or deliberate restatement of the endangered relationship. On a full loss, record one relationship, legal recognition, or obligation. Recover it from the grown cap, a memory vessel, salt veil, or reenactment outside the Garden.

## Garden operations

### Repair the Jaw Catch

1. Isolate or reduce the incoming pulse.
2. Remove hot sediment by labor, pallbearer, beast, or water redirection.
3. Replace lost loam or sacrifice one root section.
4. Decide where contaminated sediment is stored or moved.
5. Restore flow and record food saved.

### Recover a rooted tablet

1. Identify the tablet and hear its present wish if possible.
2. Cool the surrounding root.
3. Choose careful cutting, hurried tearing, in-place reading, or in-place repair.
4. Provide a brine vessel and witness.
5. Record lost root, memory, tablet material, and faction claim.

### Recover a caught memory

1. Cool and isolate the selected silver root.
2. Name the intended recipient or vessel.
3. Draw the memory into cup, veil, tablet, or witness.
4. State whether the relationship is accepted, disputed, or merely carried.
5. Stabilize the cut root or accept filtration loss.

### Prepare a pressure shutter

1. Clear both separated handles or establish substitute operators.
2. Inspect the destination vent.
3. Evacuate food, tablets, people, or memories from the affected beds.
4. Brace routes and repair any remote linkage.
5. Mark the shutter prepared without opening it.

## Pressure shutters

| Shutter | Relief | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Upper Jaw | Modest local relief; may delay one pressure escalation when the surface vent is open. | Damages an abandoned quarry gallery and one upper root route. |
| Widow's Run | Large relief through Silver Root and lower streets. | Releases stored memory into Harrowgate and damages mnemonic filtration. |
| Last Teeth | Accepts the first wave of the safest controlled-collapse sequence. | Destroys most Garden food, ancestral remains, dry routes, and filtration. |

Preparing Last Teeth does not open it. Evacuation, salvage, surface coordination, and alternate food or water plans should reduce the cost before the finale.

| State | Benefits | Costs and pressures |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tended | Food, gray water, guides, stable routes, controlled names. | Requires keeper cooperation and restraint. |

## Carry-forward checklist

Before leaving either region, record:

- recognized commander, command duration, duty, assent, and objections;
- Warden crews, casualties, releases, transfers, and sealed orders;
- Quoin, Ora, Maston, Vesra, and any guest-marshal tablet state;
- inner-gate and deep-alarm state;
- Garden broad state and each filter bed;
- Edda, Kell, keeper disposition, food, and gray water;
- names, relationships, and memories taken or returned;
- extracted root, brine, loam, or rations and their existing claims;
- each pressure shutter and remote linkage;
- changed routes; and
- where water, contamination, spores, wick-eaters, blank sentinels, and released memories moved.
