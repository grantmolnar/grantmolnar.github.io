# Full Playthrough: The Open Chain Descends

This is one descent through *The Bell Beneath Harrowgate*. It is not a canonical solution. The Open Chain uses both entrances, returns to changed rooms, enters one optional depth, leaves two others unopened, and builds a hybrid ending from imperfect machinery and obligations people can still contest.

The corresponding `play-state.example.json` is the authoritative append-only journal. It fixes the factual order; this account gives the decisions breath and consequence.

## Session One: Two Ways Down

The bell had rung three times without a rope moving. The first was dismissed as quarry settling. The second reversed a well and drew unanswered signals from both old entrances. The third broke a tenement; at dawn, a dying Ember porter named Orren's chain-cut order and Maelin Rook's refusal. Each cycle had closed sooner than the one before. Harrowgate finally knew both that someone was alive below and why the Bell had begun to fail.

Torren Pike had carried two wounded porters out before the first toll and kept Orren's preparatory order inside Company channels. He had not seen the cut and could not prove the order had been carried out. When the dawn survivor confirmed it, Torren gave Warden Marr his copy and volunteered to descend again. He entered the chapterhouse as both rescue runner and witness who had waited too long.

The five members of the descent commission met beneath a chapterhouse roof split clean enough to show morning sky. The Council had left them a seal, a warrant, and no agreed answer to what the seal was meant to preserve. Dema Harl spread the foundation maps across a fallen pew. Miren Glass read the warrant aloud and then read the sentence she had added in her own hand: the commission ended with the present emergency and could not renew itself by declaring the emergency unfinished.

Ilyra Vey objected that a municipal notary could not amend a Council instrument after sealing. Miren agreed. She placed the town seal beside the addition and asked whether Ilyra preferred to descend without any lawful witness to the limit. Ilyra stamped the margin with her personal factor's mark, not the Vey signet. Dema drew three circles on the map above old quarry vents. Torren Pike put one finger on each circle and named the street, the nearest stair, and the people most likely to refuse evacuation. Sister Calve Norn tied burial thread around the map rather than around any person.

The Chapterhouse survey established the first practical truths. The Bell was not calling anything. It was relieving pressure, badly. The old house had two usable entrances and several interior loops. The processional stair led toward the Hall of Bent Knees. The quarry cleft offered a dangerous descent into the upper service works. The Ropeworks and Chain Scriptorium could be reached from more than one direction. The survey also showed that any controlled collapse required open quarry mouths and an evacuation that had not yet begun.

The party divided. Dema and Torren took the cleft because they could judge stone and rope. Calve, Ilyra, and Miren took the processional stair because anything called a petition hall was likely to care what authority entered it.

### The quarry descent

The Quarry Cleft narrowed around a broken cage lift and opened over a shaft where reflected ward-light moved like a second moon. A six-limbed lamp hunter clung upside down to the far wall. Its head turned toward the brightest object, not the nearest body. Below it, stranded porter Gannet Sol had wrapped himself into the cage cable and was trying not to breathe loudly enough to shake the links.

Torren wanted to cross in darkness. Dema pointed out that the old vent marks were written in reflective mineral and would vanish with the light. They hooded a cold quarry lamp, lowered it on an empty haul line, and let it drift down the opposite face. The hunter followed. Torren crossed to Gannet, cut the twisted harness free, and sent the porter upward before retrieving the lamp.

The service marks confirmed routes toward the Lantern Cistern, Garden of Teeth, and Menagerie of Quiet Beasts. They also confirmed that the Ember Company had not simply vanished. Someone had cut the Queen's chain, and another Company hand had countermanded the operation. Torren recognized both knot signatures. Orren Saye had ordered the cut. Captain Maelin Rook had tried to stop the lower crews from completing it.

Dema found the buried vent mouths. Two remained physically clear below. The third ran beneath the infirmary district and had been built over in the same project whose map she had signed. She said nothing until Torren asked why she kept measuring the same wall. Then she told him. He did not absolve her. He marked the two usable mouths and added the infirmary to the evacuation list.

### The Hall of Bent Knees

On the processional stair, the burden-bearer statues moved only when the three petitioners attempted to pass without offering weight. Ilyra presented the Vey seal. A bronze examiner descended from the lintel, touched the signet, and placed it on the confiscation scale. The hall did not deny that House Vey possessed a claim. It denied that a claim paid the petitioner's toll.

Miren laid down the emergency seal and the written limit on the commission. Calve placed her clapperless handbell beside it. Ilyra, after a long pause, unbuckled the jeweled clasp from her cloak and added it to the scale. The Hall accepted the combined burden: public authority, renounced comfort, and a witness that could not compel an answer by force.

One statue seized Calve by the shoulder when an inherited funerary formula in the wall recognized her order. Calve answered that the oath belonged to the dead sister who made it, not to every later bearer of the same cord. Miren repeated the distinction for the record. The statue released her. The hall's petition plates revealed routes to the Ropeworks, Scriptorium, Lantern, Barracks, and Garden.

When Dema and Torren rejoined them, Ilyra recovered her seal from the examiner but left the jeweled clasp on the scale.

### The Ropeworks and the Scriptorium

The Ropeworks of Three Burdens was already in motion. The Crown Drum jerked against a half-cut royal chain. The Water Drum alternated between surge and slack. The Memory Drum turned smoothly, which Dema first called good news until Calve asked whose memory it was moving.

Load ghosts repeated the work of dead crews. They were not immediately hostile. They became violent when Torren crossed an old haul rhythm and when Ilyra tried to remove a brass tally before replacing its weight. They fought for stations, not a cleared room. Dema braced the Crown Drum. Torren and the ghosts worked a counter-rhythm into the Water Drum. Miren copied the load tallies. Calve refused to stop the Memory Drum until they knew whether stopping it would release or destroy what it carried.

Torren found Maelin Rook's counter-order tied into an emergency knot line. It instructed Company crews to cease cutting, rescue the lower teams, and secure evidence of the second contract. Orren's order lay over it in a newer cord. Torren added Maelin's name to the living list rather than the dead one.

The Chain Scriptorium hung beneath the Ropeworks on plates that served as both archive and structure. Removing the most valuable records would have changed the load path. Ilyra proposed copying first and arguing over custody later. The others agreed, partly because the alternative was to choose between scholarship and collapse while standing under both.

The Scriptorium disclosed the buried-house map and the division of royal jurisdiction. The Golden Branch governed custody. The Burden Chain governed maintenance and operating authority. The Ash Witness preserved the limits under which Queen Avarra had surrendered. Later kings had layered their own orders over the three records but had never successfully made them one thing.

Miren saw the political shape before Dema finished tracing the physical one. The Bell had been made difficult to own on purpose. The current crisis had been caused by people treating divided powers as missing permissions.

They left the plates in place, copied the map, sealed the genealogy chains as evidence, and returned to the upper camp with the Crown Drum braced, the Water Drum slowed, and the Memory Drum still turning under protest.

## Session Two: Water That Remembers

The fourth toll had not yet begun, but the black water in the upper pipes had started moving against gravity. The party returned with a clearer rescue order: Lamplighter Senn and Tavia Hest in the Cistern, Rhea Colm in the Reliquary, then back to the Ropeworks before the altered water state made the upper drums unpredictable.

### Selective light

The Lantern Cistern was not one lamp but four branches fed from a basin where oil floated over black water. Processional and Ossuary boughs still burned. Mourning was dark. Deep guttered at half strength. Wick-eaters moved along the boundary between lit and unlit stone, feeding first on purposes and only later on flesh.

Lamplighter Senn had maintained the old account so precisely that he could say which neighborhood's memories had entered which branch. Tavia Hest, blinded during the Company retreat, could identify routes by heat and echo but could no longer read the reflective marks. They had survived because Senn refused to spend Mourning's last oil on the upper works, while Tavia had kept the wick-eaters occupied with fragments of her own reasons for descending.

The party established selective light. Processional remained lit to hold the upper ghosts slow. Mourning was relit so the Reliquary's breath lines would become visible. Ossuary was dimmed rather than extinguished, releasing useful memory without driving every scavenger into the Garden. Deep remained at half strength so the lower mechanisms could still be read without feeding the Iron Choir at full flow.

Calve listened to a released memory of a civic delegate placing one voluntary handweight on the Common Measure. Miren listened to another delegate refusing to bind future households. The two memories contradicted the later official summary, which claimed the design had failed mechanically. Senn said the balance trial had succeeded. The design had been rejected because it required public consent, transferable duty, and funded maintenance.

Tavia identified Corven Braid's route into the Reliquary and confirmed that Orren carried Halren Coss's second payment schedule. The party took Senn, Tavia, two oil measures, and a controlled Mourning line downward.

### The Reliquary Under Water

The Reliquary alternated between high and middle water. Six copper saints controlled breath, seals, weight, burden, royal pressure, and drainage. Rhea Colm's air pocket had begun to collapse because Returning Breath was not receiving a clean pulse. The copper pallbearer paced the central aisle, attempting to complete a funeral whose body had been removed years ago.

Dema wanted to lower the water immediately. Torren pointed out that rapid drainage would kill Rhea's air pocket. Calve noticed that the pallbearer responded to the position of the empty bier rather than to intruders. Ilyra and Torren moved the bier into the unfinished route while Dema, Senn, and Miren primed Returning Breath with ward-oil. Calve walked beside the pallbearer and named the absence it was carrying.

At middle water, all six saint stations became operable. Torren reached Rhea. She had spent her last reliable breaths keeping an injured Company porter alive long enough to die by his own name rather than as an unknown body in the current. She emerged angry, hypothermic, and able to explain exactly which memories had been contaminated by black water.

The party stabilized the Kneeling Saint halfway, balanced the Open Palm cache, and recovered the emergency seals. From Many Hands they took the Common Measure components and voluntary civic weights. From Beneath the Yoke they released the Pilgrim's Yoke, a transfer frame that could temporarily carry a missing burden but marked its bearer with physical and mnemonic strain. From Corven Braid's drowned satchel they recovered the lawful Vey survey contract, Halren Coss's concealed bonus schedule, and Councilor Rhun's rider paying for possession before public notice. The base contract required safe detachment and witnessed transfer. Orren's later papers paid him to ignore both.

Ilyra sealed the contract evidence and refused to classify the Common Measure as royal salvage. Miren wrote “candidate public instrument” on its rack. Dema wrote “unproven under current load.” Calve wrote nothing until asked whether she would ever consent to its use. She answered: “As a bridge, perhaps. Not as a new person we can forget is bearing us.”

### Return to the Ropeworks

The party did not proceed downward from the Reliquary. It returned to the Ropeworks because the controlled middle-water state had changed what the Water Drum was receiving. The first revisit proved that operating one region changed another.

Dema and Torren used the Reliquary return pulse to rebalance the Water Drum. Rhea identified the black-water beat that would have been mistaken for a normal surge. The upper line settled into a predictable rhythm. Time spent on rescue had become a durable operating change.

The Memory Drum still turned. Calve placed one hand against its housing and said they would return for it with witnesses.

## Session Three: The Salt Muster

The Salt Barracks controlled an intact inner gate and enough skilled crews to make later operations possible. It also contained three incompatible claims about what duty required.

Sister Vesra's Continuance faction wanted the Wardens repaired and the Bell stabilized before any release. Prior Dalca's Release faction argued that the old sentence had expired and that continued service without renewed consent was another death. Sergeant Cleth Varn's literalists held the last royal orders as binding until a recognized authority replaced them. The empty command chair accepted none of the three positions by itself.

### Shell, tablet, and witness

The party began with the blank sentinels. Ora Pell's tablet had been removed by her own act to prevent her body being used for an abusive order. Maston Grey's tablet had been taken by others to erase testimony. The physical state looked similar. The moral state was not.

Calve refused Vesra's proposal to rejoin both as a precaution. Miren took testimony from shells, tablets, and witnesses separately. Dema asked what functions would fail if the sentinels remained blank. Heth Brine answered that the inner gate could be staffed by living crews. Convenience was not necessity.

The Garden held missing tablets and repair fiber, so the party postponed the command vote and crossed by the Bitter Bed route.

### The Garden of Teeth

The Garden grew food, clean water, memory fiber, and routes from the dead. Its Jaw Catch removed physical contamination. Silver Root held names and relationships. Quiet Loam cooled water and fed the Low Choir. The temptation to treat it as a storeroom vanished once Bera Loam's household tally was found hanging beside the toothcap beds.

Edda-of-the-Warm-Stone guided the party through the name hazard. It did not first erase facts. It loosened relationships. Torren could remember that Gannet Sol had been rescued but briefly could not remember why that mattered to him. Calve anchored him by asking him to repeat the missing-person list in the order he had written it.

They found Maston Grey's tablet embedded in Silver Root, carrying a clear memory of Cleth's predecessor ordering evidence suppressed. They also found Ora Pell's tablet in a sealed niche with her own refusal cut into the salt. The party took Maston's tablet and left Ora's where she had placed it.

Dema inspected the root pressure shutters. Upper Jaw could divert a modest pulse into the evacuated quarry if its mouths were open. Widow's Run would spend stored memory. Last Teeth would preserve the surface most completely by destroying much of the Garden and its food system. She cleared and rigged Upper Jaw but did not open it. Torren added every keeper and food stair to the evacuation ledger. Miren recorded that Cleth's faction would receive a veto over Last Teeth only if the veto could not block smaller prepared routes.

They harvested limited silver-root fiber, toothcap rations, and clean water. They did not take bone loam for the Wells because doing so would have displaced a hidden load into the Garden's filtration beds.

### The Feast of Empty Chairs

The Low Choir was not one oppressed constituency waiting to agree with itself. Four household positions met the party.

Bera Loam's Warm Table wanted food, safety, and no further promises that endangered children for distant justice. Tern-of-One-Name's Open Cup wanted recovered memories released and voluntarily carried. Nara-of-the-Seventh-Name's Kept Chain wanted institutions preserved if the people inside them could govern and leave. Illa Crownless's Narrow Line wanted the old royal and civic claims severed as completely as possible.

The party returned Soli's household memory to willing witnesses rather than placing it in Miren's public packet. This cost them a powerful general proof and earned them the first measure of trust.

The households would not accept a single vow. They drafted six compact weights instead: witness, passage, provision, maintenance, limit, and remedy. Miren insisted that every weight name a bearer and a failure response. Ilyra offered House Vey money for provision and remedy. The households refused until she separated funding from title. Calve proposed a right to withdraw from mnemonic work. Dema required enough notice to reroute physical load. Torren required named evacuation routes and replacement crews.

No final compact was completed. The tables agreed to send witnesses back to the Barracks.

### Return to the Barracks

Maston Grey's shell, tablet, and witnesses agreed to rejoining. Ora Pell's did not. Maston was restored. Ora remained blank by choice and accepted a role as an uncommanded route sentinel whose body could not receive deep orders.

The command chair then heard the three claims again. Vesra offered repair crews and Wells expertise. Dalca offered release witnesses and Garden cooperation. Cleth offered disciplined pursuit and control over the Deep Rank. None could command alone without converting its strongest concern into the whole duty.

The settlement created joint emergency command between Vesra and Dalca. Their accepted duty was to supply crews for rescue, stabilization, and a final operation that preserved a real release path. Cleth retained a narrow veto against opening Last Teeth without evacuation proof. Prefect Quoin's recovered testimony confirmed that joint emergency authority and voluntary release were both legal under the old sentence.

The inner gate opened. The command did not become friendship. It became enough coordination to continue.

## Session Four: The Hanging Judgment

The Inverted Chapel hung beneath its old nave, turning routes, roles, and bodies when its pivot brakes moved. Merrit Dole remained bound into an appeal station. Elra Mott, an Ember Company rigger, held another brake and believed every approaching party served Orren.

The party entered with mixed Warden crews rather than a royal command. They moved the Chapel through Mourning toward upright. Chain-wing guardians exchanged positions between operators, forcing people into the roles they had attempted to delegate. Ilyra found herself at the execution brake after proposing that Orren's warrant could bypass the appeal sequence. She released the brake rather than pull it. Miren found herself beneath the authority station and had to state exactly what allowed the party to turn the Chapel.

Merrit was released. Elra surrendered after Torren recited Maelin Rook's counter-order and Rhea confirmed the contract rider. The Chapel reached upright without entering execution turn.

### Provisional acceptance

The Common Measure could not simply be installed because it had balanced once. The Chapel required authority, consent, duration, and remedy.

Miren supplied temporary civic authority bounded to the Crown release operation. The proposed bearers consented only to carrying a transfer, not to becoming permanent weights. The duration was one toll cycle. The remedy required immediate unloading, medical and mnemonic care, compensation, and the right to halt the operation if Refusal rejected the transfer.

The Chapel granted provisional recognition. The frame remained incomplete because Avarra's surrender limits had not yet been incorporated. The King's Narrow Grave lay behind the chained genealogy and held the best evidence.

### The optional Grave

The party chose the Grave and skipped the Menagerie. Quiet beasts might have consumed heat, vibration, panic, or loose memory during the finale. Royal custody, maintenance mass, and the Ash Witness mattered more than another living tool they did not yet know how to handle safely.

Orren Saye had reached the Grave first. His surviving loyalists held the succession vestibule while the narrowing walls forced every claimant toward a single answer. Orren offered Ilyra a place beside him. Her Vey blood and factor's warrant could satisfy the Golden Branch. Rhun's rider and possession of the cut chain could manufacture operating necessity. Together, he said, they could recover the Crown before committees above spent another district debating consent. Ilyra answered that her house had funded the descent, not the omission of three load-bearing instructions. Orren said omitted instructions were how old institutions kept possession while calling delay prudence.

The offer was not absurd. It was why Ilyra listened.

Torren wanted to attack before the Grave recognized them. Calve asked whether he wanted Orren dead or answerable. Torren said he wanted the missing Company members alive. Dema pointed out that Orren knew which lower route Maelin had taken. They entered under divided claims.

The Golden Branch recognized Ilyra's custody standing but not exclusive use. The Burden Chain recognized Dema's maintained obligation once she admitted her role in the vent closures. The Ash Witness recognized Calve and Miren as witnesses to surrender limits because neither claimed the Crown. Orren attempted to combine the first two and suppress the third. The coffin knight activated when he described Avarra's surrender as unconditional.

The fight was a contest over positions and claims. Torren and Elra held Orren's loyalists away from the treasury. Dema jammed the narrowing walls with a crown-bar cradle. Calve spoke the surrender limits while the knight attempted to force her to kneel. Miren read each operative claim into the record. Ilyra used the Golden Branch voice to order the gilt mourners to preserve the Crown trust rather than defend Orren's private contract.

Orren lost when the Grave recognized that his authority depended on the maintenance trust he intended to strip. Torren had the opportunity to leave him between the narrowing walls. He pulled him free instead and told him he would answer under his own name.

The party removed four of six crown-bars, three royal splice links, the seven-step Royal Brake sequence, the debt records, and the funds earmarked for evacuation and Low Choir remedy. They left ceremonial goods and two crown-bars in the Grave under seal. The Golden Branch copy went into Ilyra's custody. Dema carried the Burden Chain. Calve and Miren jointly carried the Ash Witness. No one held all three.

### Return to the Chapel

The Ash Witness changed the Common Measure's recognition. Avarra had not surrendered an unlimited right to transfer her burden. The frame could govern one Crown release operation, then had to be dismantled or renewed by a fresh compact. Refusal could stop the transfer. Witness had to preserve its terms.

The Chapel accepted the amended instrument. It did not promise the ending would work. It made the attempted burden legible.

## Session Five: Gold Is Weight

The Counterweight Wells separated three things that every hurried plan tried to combine: mass, load path, and timing.

Four crown-bars provided mass. They did not determine where force would travel. The Pilgrim's Yoke could bridge a missing path. It did not make the bearer a lawful permanent support. The Common Measure could govern timing and recognized transfer. It could not replace maintenance crews.

Captain Maelin Rook was alive at the Wells with a remnant of the Ember Company. She had held the Royal Well after refusing Orren's order. Torren's reunion with her was not warm. Maelin asked why he had returned only after bringing officials. Torren answered that the officials had brought seals, money, and a reason the next porter might not be abandoned. Maelin accepted the answer provisionally.

The first Wells operation placed four crown-bars and the Pilgrim's Yoke into the Royal deficit. The Civic Well received a path through the temporary Common Measure governor. The Funerary Well was severed from automatic Low Choir memory intake but remained physically braced. Dema warned that cutting the mnemonic line did not remove the load. It merely prevented people from being used as the nearest open path.

The party returned to the Feast because the Wells could not invent maintainers.

### The funded compact

The six weights became an operating compact.

- **Witness:** Low Choir and civic observers held access to the Choir register, Wells accounts, and Chapel review.
- **Passage:** surface and deep routes remained open to households, inspectors, and replacement crews.
- **Provision:** House Vey funded food, pumps, oil, and wages without receiving title in exchange.
- **Maintenance:** named mixed crews trained successors and published the work schedule.
- **Limit:** the emergency arrangement lasted one year and could not renew itself through a declaration by its own operators.
- **Remedy:** injured bearers, displaced households, and lost memories had funded claims, replacement housing, and a forum able to halt dangerous work.

Calve secured a right to withdraw from mnemonic work. Dema secured notice sufficient to reroute physical load. Torren secured named rescue and evacuation responsibilities. Ilyra converted part of the royal trust into immediate maintenance funding under divided custody. Miren required quarterly accounts and automatic Chapel review.

They returned to the Wells with actual people attached to the plan.

### The corrected Well record

During the second operation, a shouted report reached Miren that the Funerary Well had dropped. She entered the consequence immediately because a permanent drop would have sacrificed lower memory access and changed every final route.

Dema stopped her before the ink dried fully. The crew had severed automatic intake and set a brace. The Well remained physically active. Miren did not scrape out the error. The journal appended a correction voiding the mistaken operation, then recorded the true state.

The correction mattered beyond bookkeeping. It demonstrated that the emergency record could admit error without pretending it had never governed action. Cleth's Wardens had already begun adjusting to a permanent loss. The correction recalled them and preserved the Funerary path as a controlled, non-mnemonic support.

At the end of the session, no Well had been dropped. Royal and Civic paths were prepared. Funerary remained braced and severed from involuntary memory intake.

## Session Six: The Routes of Strain

The final preparation required commands, pressure routing, surface evacuation, and a public decision made before the party entered the Bell.

### The Choir of Iron Tongues

The Iron Choir stored voices and commands without preserving them as whole persons. The danger was not only that the Iron Cantor might attack. The danger was that any faction might use a true voice without its limits.

The party reconstructed Queen Avarra's retained will as three faculties. Measure remained bound to the Crown and could test equivalence. Refusal was copied into a portable silent tongue held by Calve. Witness was routed into an open register held jointly by Miren and Low Choir custodians. No one person or object controlled all three.

The command gallery accepted a strict grammar: source, address, and limit. Vesra could command a named brake for one phase. Dalca could release a specified duty after transfer. Dema could stage a manifold but not open every route. Miren could record and halt a command that exceeded its stated limit. The Iron Cantor remained intact but lost the ability to treat all lower mechanisms as one undifferentiated instrument.

The Choir revealed the route to the Mouth Below and the grammar of a possible settlement. The party established that the option existed and chose not to descend. They had neither a prepared offering nor a return structure they trusted. The Mouth remained a valid alternative, not an omitted final room.

### Black Rain

The Black Rain Cistern held accumulated strain in water that fell upward. Four routes could receive it: quarry vents, Reliquary return, Mouth sluice, and Foundation Weir. None erased pressure.

The party prepared three bounded paths: quarry, Garden roots, and Royal Well. The Foundation Weir was capped because it would spend Harrowgate's foundations. The Mouth sluice remained closed because opening an unbounded relationship was not the same as negotiating one. Rain Wardens accepted the staging only after Dema showed the surface evacuation map and Miren entered the route limits into the command record.

The first visit prepared the manifold. It did not yet lower Black Rain to the final Draw level.

### Return to the quarry

Dema, Torren, and surface crews returned to the Quarry Cleft. The northern and western mouths were cleared above and below. The infirmary mouth remained closed. Its share would be reassigned to Upper Jaw and the Royal Well.

The lamp hunter had to be moved before the vent pulse. Torren remembered the cold-light lure from the first descent. They guided it into a marked refuge instead of killing it or allowing the blast to trap it. Gannet Sol, now on a surface crew, tied the final warning line across the cleft.

### Return to the Garden

Calve, Miren, and the keepers isolated Upper Jaw from the food beds. Widow's Run and Last Teeth remained closed. The operation would spend a route and some filtration capacity, not the Garden's memory store or whole community.

Calve asked Edda whether preserving the Garden's households meant preserving the institution that had grown from coerced burial labor. Edda answered that a root did not consent to the grave but could still choose where to grow after it reached water. Calve entered the sentence in Witness and did not attempt to turn it into doctrine.

### Return to the Chapterhouse

Ilyra and Miren returned to the surface because the final operation could not be legitimized afterward.

The emergency council evacuated two districts. It refused to evacuate the infirmary district, which confirmed the decision to keep that vent closed. House Vey released six months of maintenance and remedy funds immediately rather than promising them after Crown recovery. The Crown trust received four divided keys: civic, Low Choir, House Vey, and funerary witness. Any custody transfer required three keys, including either civic or Low Choir assent. Use of the Crown required a separate public decision.

Miren issued the operation notice in ordinary language. It said that the party intended to free the Crown, prevent a full Sounding, divide pressure among prepared systems, and establish a one-year maintenance arrangement. It also said where failure would go.

### Final Black Rain stage

The reunited party returned to the Cistern. Black Rain was lowered to Draw level and divided among quarry, Upper Jaw, and Royal paths. The Foundation Weir remained capped. The Mouth remained closed. The Rain Wardens took their stations.

All that remained was to enter the Bell before the staged system drifted out of phase.

## Session Seven: A Bell That Does Not Sound

The Deep Bell had no safe floor. Its four approaches met on moving frames around a bell large enough to contain weather. The broken Queen's chain ran through the Crown cage. Burden echoes climbed every approach wearing the forms of people and institutions the party had used.

The party did not enter alone. Maelin Rook brought Company riggers. Vesra and Dalca brought mixed Warden crews. Low Choir maintainers held the Civic line. Rain Wardens and Garden keepers remained at remote stations. The final operation was distributed before it began.

### Taking Breath

The Bell inhaled through the upper water lines. The processional and Mourning boughs flared. Burden echoes attempted to assign every open station to the person most likely to accept it permanently.

Dema held the phase at the Crown Drum while Torren and Maelin secured the four approaches. Ilyra presented the Golden Branch at the custody locks. Dema presented the Burden Chain at the operating locks. Calve and Miren presented the Ash Witness at the surrender locks. All six opened because the jurisdictions were acknowledged without being merged.

Orren, held above under guard, had claimed that only a sovereign voice could do this cleanly. The Bell proved otherwise. It required complete jurisdiction, not one owner.

### Drawing Load

The Bell drew strain from water, Wells, roots, foundations, and memory. The Common Measure entered the Wells yoke as a temporary governor. Four crown-bars and the Royal Well took the first parallel load. The Civic Well timed the transfer. The Funerary Well remained physically engaged but accepted no new Low Choir memory.

The Iron Cantor attempted a command cascade, using Avarra's Measure as source and addressing every lower mechanism at once. Calve raised the silent tongue. Refusal rejected the command because its limit was absent. Miren entered the rejection in Witness. Vesra reissued bounded commands to named brakes. Dalca issued a release command that would take effect after Crown Descent.

They broke the command cascade by making command smaller.

### Crown Descent

The Crown came down through the cage carrying both physical mass and recognized burden. The Common Measure brought Crown and prepared paths into parallel measure. For a moment, the frame attempted to treat its bearers as permanent weights. Miren read the one-operation duration. Calve invoked Refusal. Dema opened the Royal path. Torren and Maelin transferred the Pilgrim's Yoke before any one bearer could become the nearest unbounded support.

Ilyra took the Crown when it came free. The Golden Branch recognized her custody. She did not invoke its exclusive command. She passed one trust key to Nara's representative before leaving the cage.

The clapper was locked away from the bell skin. The Crown was free. The load was not yet settled.

### Sounding

The Bell attempted to complete its tone.

Dema gave the staged command. The northern quarry mouth opened first. The western followed after the pressure gauge crossed its second mark. Upper Jaw opened last, taking the share that would otherwise have gone beneath the infirmary. The Royal Well dropped through its prepared travel without being sacrificed. The Civic governor held long enough to keep the pulses separate. The Funerary line carried structure but no involuntary memory.

The sound that reached Harrowgate was not a bell tone. It was three low impacts separated by enough silence for windows to survive between them.

The Bell did not shatter. It did not remain the sovereign center of the system. Its clapper hung locked away from the skin, and its pressure work had been divided among pumps, vents, roots, brakes, and crews whose names existed in public records.

### What Harrowgate kept

Harrowgate survived. Two evacuated districts suffered cracked streets, collapsed cellars, and black-water seepage, but no mass casualty. The infirmary district remained standing. The Garden lost Upper Jaw capacity and would require years of regrowth, but its food and memory beds survived. The Low Choir's automatic memory draw ended. Stored memories remained to be identified and returned through a long communal process.

The Crown entered divided public custody. House Vey retained a real custody role but not ownership or exclusive use. Avarra's Measure remained with the Crown. Refusal remained portable and independent. Witness remained open to the Low Choir and public record.

The Common Measure was dismantled after the operation, as promised. Its weights became the first accounting tokens of the new maintenance compact rather than the body of a new sovereign burden.

The Bell remained accessible as a dangerous historical mechanism. The Mouth Below remained unopened. The Menagerie remained unvisited. Orren remained alive to testify. Maelin Rook and the surviving Ember Company accepted responsibility for repair and rescue work under public contract rather than private salvage claim.

The new system was expensive, inelegant, and impossible to mistake for nature. It needed crews at dawn, money before promises, inspection routes, open records, and the authority to stop what yesterday’s authority had begun. Above the silent Bell, Harrowgate’s first maintenance shift reported for work. That was the victory’s final sound.

## Demonstrated end state

### Harrowgate

- The town survives without a full fourth toll.
- Two quarry districts are evacuated during the operation and require reconstruction.
- The infirmary district is preserved by leaving its vent closed.
- Distributed pumps, vents, roots, and Wells replace the Bell's centralized pressure function.
- Maintenance is funded for six months immediately and governed by a one-year compact with automatic review.

### The Low Choir

- Automatic Funerary memory extraction ends.
- The Feast and Garden remain inhabited and productive.
- Households retain the right to withdraw from mnemonic work.
- Witness, passage, provision, maintenance, limit, and remedy are active obligations rather than aspirational clauses.
- Memory restoration remains a long campaign task.

### The Crown and Avarra

- The Crown is physically free.
- Custody is divided among civic, Low Choir, House Vey, and funerary witnesses.
- Measure, Refusal, and Witness remain separable.
- No claimant possesses all royal faculties or every custody key.
- Future use of the Crown requires a new public decision.

### Remaining duties

- Rebuild Upper Jaw and damaged quarry routes.
- Maintain Royal, Civic, and Funerary Wells without restoring involuntary mnemonic intake.
- Identify and return stored memories.
- Try Orren and examine the Councilor Rhun rider.
- Account for unvisited Menagerie creatures and the unopened Mouth Below.
- Review the compact after one year and dismantle emergency authority unless it is affirmatively renewed under new terms.
