# The Ember Company

**Reference ID:** `0ee5aa87-9379-4170-afd6-97de3c5badd6`  
**Kind:** Organization  
**Aliases:** Ember Company, the Company  
**Tags:** expedition, contract, salvage, divided command

## Summary

The contracted expeditionary company divided between Captain Rook's rescue-first counter-order and Factor Orren's concealed race for Crown possession.

## Reference Material

## Contracted expedition

The Ember Company is a survey, salvage, engineering, and recovery outfit hired through Lady Merrow Vey's recovery trust. Its main crew entered under a conditional contract requiring stabilization and witnessed transfer before Crown recovery. Chief Factor Halren Coss's concealed payment schedule and Councilor Rhun's rider created a second incentive that not every Company member saw.

## The split below Harrowgate

At the Ropeworks, Captain Maelin Rook refused to cut a loaded royal chain. Factor Orren Saye proceeded under the abridged sequence, and the expedition divided. Company chalk, route ledgers, rigging, tools, testimony, and surviving crews preserve both the possession party and Rook's rescue-first counter-account. Membership does not make every survivor equally informed or culpable.

## Officers, contracts, and liability

Recognized officers may direct Company personnel who still accept their command, authenticate field practice, supply labor and tools, and negotiate withdrawal, repair, or liability. The Company cannot bind Lady Vey, Harrowgate, the Salt Wardens, the Low Choir, or Queen Avarra, and its contract does not create title by possession. Current survivors, injuries, loyalties, custody, equipment, and reconciliation remain play state.
