# Queen Avarra

**Reference ID:** `7450c8fa-6633-4b79-a24d-1f4b639ae2dc`  
**Kind:** Person  
**Aliases:** Avarra, Avarra of the Crown of Measure  
**Tags:** queen, Crown of Measure, retained will, surrender

## Summary

The conquered sovereign whose coerced Crown became the Deep Bell's command and load junction; her divided faculties can measure, refuse, and witness but cannot rule the present or choose justice for others.

## Reference Material

## Sovereign and sentence

Queen Avarra ruled before Aldren Harrow's conquest. During the siege she mistook the fault's response to royal cadence for obedience. Her later surrender was limited, witnessed, and coerced; the Crown of Measure was then made clapper, counterweight, and command authority within the Deep Bell. The indefinite hereditary sentence and the transfer of mnemonic cost to the Low Choir were later additions, not terms she granted.

## Retained faculties

Avarra does not survive as one complete person waiting inside the Crown. Measure, Refusal, and Witness persist across the Crown, Iron Choir, surrender records, and inherited testimony. Reconstructed together, they can compare burdens, reject commands made through her Crown, and testify to the original limits. They know later history only through memories fed into the machine.

## Terms she will not leave implicit

Avarra wants her sentence ended without coercing another person into her place. She may aid a replacement, distributed settlement, controlled collapse, royal unbinding, or a bounded emergency continuance with a real end and remedy. Her retained will can influence old mechanisms and the Mouth's learned cadence, but it cannot rule present Harrowgate, erase other claims, or certify that efficiency is justice. Whether her faculties are reconstructed, separated, carried, freed, suppressed, or lost remains a matter for play.
