# Reference Index: The Bell Beneath Harrowgate

## People

- [**Lady Merrow Vey**](70f54526-9ee3-4fbd-97e4-ba7b1393fdd6.md) — The last acknowledged royal descendant: lawful sponsor of the secret recovery expedition, holder of a genuine but bounded Crown claim, and principal able to bind House Vey but not Harrowgate or the buried communities. Aliases: Lady Vey, Merrow Vey.
- [**Captain Maelin Rook**](70dbadad-c4c0-459a-8df6-1bc5417bbb0d.md) — The Ember Company captain who refused Orren's loaded chain cut, keeps rescue and stabilization ahead of possession, and can command surviving Company hands without deciding their public judgment. Aliases: Maelin Rook, Captain Rook, Rook.
- [**Factor Orren Saye**](ddb3c840-1e11-4f47-b790-8dc67ec28dc7.md) — The Ember factor who knowingly used a concealed possession rider and abridged sequence to order the loaded royal chain cut, yet remains a negotiable source of evidence, routes, labor, and dangerous leverage. Aliases: Orren Saye, Orren, Factor Saye.
- [**Engineer Pell Varo**](0e3023d6-2580-41fb-bd67-6bfe72480d77.md) — The Ember field engineer who reads the buried house as linked load systems and can coordinate major repairs without pretending that engineering removes their human or political cost. Aliases: Pell Varo, Engineer Varo.
- [**Queen Avarra**](7450c8fa-6633-4b79-a24d-1f4b639ae2dc.md) — The conquered sovereign whose coerced Crown became the Deep Bell's command and load junction; her divided faculties can measure, refuse, and witness but cannot rule the present or choose justice for others. Aliases: Avarra, Avarra of the Crown of Measure.
- [**Chief Factor Halren Coss**](c776856e-eea8-4a35-b64d-789cf16a8086.md) — The Ember Company chief factor who converted Lady Vey's conditional recovery into a concealed race for possession and issued the second payment schedule used below Harrowgate. Aliases: Halren Coss, Chief Factor Coss, Coss.
- [**Councilor Samet Rhun**](9c2e68e3-bf5b-4d1a-8388-2527775e95fa.md) — A Harrowgate councilor and creditor who supplied the debt rider and abridged royal sequence meant to turn rapid Crown possession into security for town obligations. Aliases: Samet Rhun, Councilor Rhun, Rhun.
- [**Prefect Ardel Quoin**](6bb941f4-f47d-44f5-9427-a2d03b8cb931.md) — The last undisputed Salt Warden commander, whose voluntary tablet removal preserved evidence of a lawful release clause while leaving command and personal continuity incomplete. Aliases: Ardel Quoin, Prefect Quoin, Quoin.
- [**Keeper Odran Greve**](eba87b8e-8c93-4624-bdde-f0fd3ae282bc.md) — The last living-born keeper of the Chain Scriptorium, carrying more than a century of returned working memory and guarding the context that makes buried records intelligible. Aliases: Odran Greve, Keeper Greve, Odran.
- [**Tavia Hest**](b91c3329-612c-4320-98aa-37f7a40d81cc.md) — A blinded Ember Company scout whose touch-reading of route chalk, dark-route discipline, and testimony about the overlapping contract remain useful beyond her rescue. Aliases: Tavia, Scout Hest.
- [**Rhea Colm**](ca9adfa1-d0d7-465b-91c7-dcf0cdd67742.md) — An Ember Company surgeon and trained witness whose triage, mnemonic diagnosis, and contract testimony connect the Reliquary rescue to later survivor care and public evidence. Aliases: Rhea, Surgeon Colm.
- [**Nara-of-the-Seventh-Name**](b365284a-6604-45d3-9305-997ad660f7ad.md) — A Low Choir compact advocate who carries seven names without treating inherited duty as ownership of the bearer. Aliases: Nara Vale, Nara.

## Places

- [**The Chain Scriptorium**](8e4c88cb-067e-4701-9364-7c61e104a710.md) — A burden-indexed brass archive whose moving chains preserve maintenance law, dynastic claims, condemned promises, and the context needed to distinguish evidence from extraction. Aliases: Chain Scriptorium, the Scriptorium.
- [**The Feast of Empty Chairs**](eb5bc5a6-28dd-43db-a2f0-a4b5e13e6206.md) — The Low Choir's inhabited refuge and negotiating hall, where empty places, memory cups, inherited names, and six compact weights make obligation visible. Aliases: Feast of Empty Chairs, the Feast Hall, the Feast.
- [**The King's Narrow Grave**](2f1ca17b-5166-4598-be11-617cd4cdcaff.md) — A narrowing royal claim chamber and treasury where three incompatible genealogies, the retained king's voice, and material Crown assets divide custody from command. Aliases: King's Narrow Grave, the Narrow Grave, the royal grave.
- [**The Counterweight Wells**](adcf2968-b7f4-45fa-85e1-7da7118b404e.md) — Three linked shaft-and-brake systems that force every proposed Bell solution to separate mass, load path, and timing before anyone calls it balanced. Aliases: Counterweight Wells, the Wells.
- [**The Deep Bell**](4b3bdda6-6e52-4409-bfe7-fb9c7d53e0db.md) — The buried sovereign machine that converts mountain strain into water, counterweight, command, and mnemonic burdens through a four-phase toll. Aliases: Deep Bell, the Bell.
- [**The Mouth Below**](05e3da93-ea8c-4cb4-a611-19a8c9612a4c.md) — A fault, resonant cavity, and learning phenomenon beneath the Bell that answers repeated exchanges more readily than spoken intention. Aliases: Mouth Below, the Mouth, the Long Listener.

## Organizations

- [**The Salt Wardens**](7d10d123-1d6b-4b7f-b09b-ac6d976a98f4.md) — Salt-preserved officers divided over whether to continue, replace, or end the Deep Bell's inherited burden. Aliases: Harrowgate Wardens, the Wardens.
- [**The Ember Company**](0ee5aa87-9379-4170-afd6-97de3c5badd6.md) — The contracted expeditionary company divided between Captain Rook's rescue-first counter-order and Factor Orren's concealed race for Crown possession. Aliases: Ember Company, the Company.
- [**The Low Choir**](05588cfb-6f87-4072-a6e0-44523968d7c4.md) — The living descendant community beneath Harrowgate whose names, memories, labor, and households have borne the Deep Bell's hidden mnemonic cost for generations. Aliases: Low Choir households, the Choir below.

## Objects

- [**The Crown of Measure**](eeae1101-1cb5-471f-92d8-eb27757ca095.md) — A gold-black command and burden instrument whose custody, load, and retained royal faculties remain disputed. Aliases: Avarra's Crown, the royal Crown.
- [**The Common Measure**](ca76d25a-8e6f-40e0-9cc5-96ab287f0bc1.md) — A rejected but workable distributed-burden design that replaces singular royal authority with voluntary weights, witnessed acceptance, bounded duration, and continuing maintenance. Aliases: Common Measure, the Measure frame.
