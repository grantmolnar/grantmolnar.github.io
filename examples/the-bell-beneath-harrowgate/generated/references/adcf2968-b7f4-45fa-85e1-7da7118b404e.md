# The Counterweight Wells

**Reference ID:** `adcf2968-b7f4-45fa-85e1-7da7118b404e`  
**Kind:** Place  
**Aliases:** Counterweight Wells, the Wells  
**Tags:** counterweights, load path, timing, finale system

## Summary

Three linked shaft-and-brake systems that force every proposed Bell solution to separate mass, load path, and timing before anyone calls it balanced.

## Reference Material

## Three different requirements

The Counterweight Wells are three vertical load systems joined to the buried house. The Royal Well governs Crown mass and dynastic mechanisms. The Civic Well governs streets, foundations, and public works. The Funerary Well governs mnemonic and mortuary paths. A correct mass in the wrong Well or at the wrong time remains a failure.

## Operations and irreversible choices

Crews can brace a Well, exchange load, install an accepted burden, remove Crown load, conduct a temporary human hold, or drop one Well permanently. Royal, Civic, and Funerary drops send force into different routes and create different physical, political, and mnemonic losses. The Wells reveal rather than erase where a proposed solution sends its cost.

## What every transfer spends

The Wells can provide mass, path, timing, brake access, and a test of whether an operation closes its physical account. They cannot create consent, maintenance, memory justice, or replacement material. Their current strain, crews, plates, brake houses, suspended loads, dropped shafts, and available routes remain authoritative in the operating ledger and play state.
