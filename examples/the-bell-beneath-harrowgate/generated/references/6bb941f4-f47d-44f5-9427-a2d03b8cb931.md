# Prefect Ardel Quoin

## Summary

The last undisputed Salt Warden commander, whose voluntary tablet removal preserved evidence of a lawful release clause while leaving command and personal continuity incomplete.

## Reference Material

## Inquiry and removal

Prefect Ardel Quoin was the last undisputed commander of the Salt Wardens. He opened an inquiry into whether their inherited sentence had reached its end condition. After learning that the original compact permitted release when an accepted replacement burden existed, Quoin asked a Garden keeper to remove his command tablet before literal-order officers could suppress the finding.

## Incomplete continuity

Quoin's shell remains sealed beneath the Barracks command chair while his cracked tablet is rooted in the Garden's Bitter Bed. The tablet remembers the inquiry, the release clause, and consent to removal, but not the keeper's face, the final vote, or several surrounding years. It may be read, repaired, restored, or kept as an independent witness without making any option a perfect recovery.

## Command after interruption

A sufficiently witnessed restoration can support temporary joint command, a final muster, and bounded stabilization or replacement orders. Quoin will not compel Wardens to dissolve or surrender the Crown to a private claimant. His damaged testimony cannot prove which present faction deserves command, and his current shell, tablet, witnesses, repair, and chosen form of continuity remain matters for play.
