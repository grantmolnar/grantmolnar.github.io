# The King's Narrow Grave

**Reference ID:** `2f1ca17b-5166-4598-be11-617cd4cdcaff`  
**Kind:** Place  
**Aliases:** King's Narrow Grave, the Narrow Grave, the royal grave  
**Tags:** royal succession, treasury, genealogy, Crown custody

## Summary

A narrowing royal claim chamber and treasury where three incompatible genealogies, the retained king's voice, and material Crown assets divide custody from command.

## Reference Material

## Three successions

The King's Narrow Grave protects three valid genealogies with different jurisdictions. The Golden Branch supports blood custody through House Vey. The Burden Chain tracks succession through maintained obligation. The Ash Witness preserves limits on Queen Avarra's surrender and later royal alterations. None grants complete ownership of the Crown, the Bell, or the people below.

## Voice, defenses, and treasury

Aldren Harrow's retained command voice tests claimants through narrowing walls, gilt mourners, indivisible commands, and a coffin knight. The treasury contains crown-bars, a royal brake sequence, chain-splice material, maintenance and resettlement funds, ceremonial goods, and records of debt. These assets can become proof, repair material, counterweight mass, public remedy, or private treasure.

## What each claim can open

The Grave can recognize limited standing, release specific resources, and expose the difference among custody, maintenance, surrender, and creditor claims. It grants no throne and does not settle Harrowgate's future by opening. Current defenders, Orren's position, damaged genealogies, bars removed, treasury custody, and routes remain changing state.
