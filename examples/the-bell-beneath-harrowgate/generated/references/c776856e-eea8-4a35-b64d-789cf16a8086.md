# Chief Factor Halren Coss

**Reference ID:** `c776856e-eea8-4a35-b64d-789cf16a8086`  
**Kind:** Person  
**Aliases:** Halren Coss, Chief Factor Coss, Coss  
**Tags:** Ember Company, chief factor, concealed schedule, institutional liability

## Summary

The Ember Company chief factor who converted Lady Vey's conditional recovery into a concealed race for possession and issued the second payment schedule used below Harrowgate.

## Reference Material

## Office and concealed escalation

Chief Factor Halren Coss is the senior Ember Company officer behind the Harrowgate expedition. Lady Merrow Vey's written contract authorized survey and recovery only after the buried house was stabilized. Coss privately accepted Councilor Samet Rhun's accelerated-possession rider, used Vey's seal to issue a second payment schedule, and rewarded physical custody before public notice.

## Evidence and responsibility

The waxed contract case, Company testimony, and complete extraction plates can establish that Coss exceeded the authority in Vey's copy. They do not by themselves establish what Vey knew before the disaster. Coss's concealed schedule explains Orren Saye's incentives without excusing Orren's loaded chain cut or making the wider Company equally culpable.

## Accounts that bind him

Coss can answer for Company accounts, deputies, schedules, and institutional liability. His office may offer records, money, personnel, or a settlement if he later enters play. It cannot turn possession into title, bind House Vey or Harrowgate, validate Rhun's debt claim, or erase Captain Rook's counter-order. His knowledge, later response, and willingness to repair the harm remain open.
