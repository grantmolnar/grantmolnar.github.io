# Factor Orren Saye

**Reference ID:** `ddb3c840-1e11-4f47-b790-8dc67ec28dc7`  
**Kind:** Person  
**Aliases:** Orren Saye, Orren, Factor Saye  
**Tags:** Ember Company, factor, Crown pursuit, concealed rider

## Summary

The Ember factor who knowingly used a concealed possession rider and abridged sequence to order the loaded royal chain cut, yet remains a negotiable source of evidence, routes, labor, and dangerous leverage.

## Reference Material

## Office and decision

Factor Orren Saye led the Ember Company's Crown party under Lady Vey's base contract and Chief Factor Halren Coss's concealed payment schedule. He knew Councilor Rhun's possession rider required physical custody before public notice and accepted an extraction sequence missing the Crown Drum's unload and synchronization operations. When Captain Maelin Rook refused the cut, Orren ordered the loaded outer chain severed anyway.

## Position

Orren believes Harrowgate's authorities, the buried custodians, and the royal house all use records selectively, and that possession of the Crown is the only leverage none can quietly erase. He is controlled, exact, and willing to bargain. He points correctly to concealed history and divided responsibility while refusing to let those truths excuse his own decision.

## Leverage without title

Orren may offer the rider, payment schedule, royal sequence plate, Company testimony, route knowledge, loyalist labor, and ordinary salvage. He may seek temporary claimant recognition or negotiate Crown custody. He cannot bind Lady Vey, Chief Factor Coss, Harrowgate, the Salt Wardens, or Queen Avarra, and possession alone does not settle title or repair the Bell. His location, allies, custody, survival, and later bargains remain matters for play.
