# Engineer Pell Varo

**Aliases:** Pell Varo, Engineer Varo  
**Tags:** Ember Company, engineer, repair, burden systems

## Summary

The Ember field engineer who reads the buried house as linked load systems and can coordinate major repairs without pretending that engineering removes their human or political cost.

## Reference Material

## Trade and knowledge

Engineer Pell Varo is the Ember Company's principal field engineer below Harrowgate. He reads the three Ropeworks drums as one burden-transfer system, understands the Counterweight Wells' separate questions of mass, path, and timing, and marked the brake windows that govern a toll. His surviving notes connect routes, load lines, and repair operations across the buried house.

## Priorities

Pell wants survivors extracted and enough of the machinery stabilized to prevent a catastrophic toll. He is furious that Orren treated an incomplete sequence as permission to move a live load. He has no independent claim to the Crown and does not pretend that an engineering operation can decide who ought to bear its political or mnemonic cost.

## Repairs he can answer for

Pell may diagnose machinery, explain displaced burdens, coordinate a complex movement, and perform major field work when supplied with tools, access, protection, and supporting crews. He cannot make a burden disappear or promise that one repair leaves every other system safe. His injuries, availability, completed jobs, remaining capacity, and chosen route remain encounter and play-state questions.
