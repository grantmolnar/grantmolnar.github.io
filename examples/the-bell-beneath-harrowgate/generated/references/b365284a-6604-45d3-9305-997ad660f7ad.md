## Summary

A Low Choir compact advocate who carries seven names without treating inherited duty as ownership of the bearer.

## Reference Material

## Seven names, one bearer

Nara Vale was born with one name and now carries six names lost during earlier tolls: Mere, Hama, Voss, Rill, Tett, and Orra. She does not claim to be those people. She keeps their names alive so unfinished kinship, contracts, debts, and testimony still have a living address. Her title places her own name seventh and warns against mistaking inherited duty for ownership of the bearer.

## The Kept Chain

Nara speaks for the Kept Chain households within the Low Choir. They preserve cups, duty weights, surface correspondence, witnesses, and the practical labor needed to fit a replacement burden. She tests every proposal by operators, gate hours, food, inspection, signatures, stop conditions, and remedies. She accepts bounded emergency continuance when it buys time for rescue and a real transition; she rejects an indefinite promise to reform after Harrowgate is safe.

## What one advocate can bind

Nara is a principal compact advocate, not the Low Choir's queen. The Warm Table, Open Cup, Kept Chain, and Narrow Line overlap without surrendering their separate consent. She can offer her households' witnesses, three voluntary weights, liturgy, records, and labor. She cannot bind every buried household, certify a coerced Common Measure, or waive another claimant's route, memory, Crown, or maintenance interest. Her location, current support, delegated representative, and final settlement remain live play state.
