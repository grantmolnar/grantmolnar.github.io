## Summary

An Ember Company surgeon and trained witness whose triage, mnemonic diagnosis, and contract testimony connect the Reliquary rescue to later survivor care and public evidence.

## Reference Material

## Surgeon in Returning Breath

Rhea Colm is an Ember Company surgeon trapped with a crushed leg inside the Saint of Returning Breath. She used the shared air manifold to preserve another wounded person's name and can distinguish ordinary shock from imposed or contaminated memory. She speaks in triage order: who is breathing, who can be moved, and which testimony will worsen if delayed.

## Knowledge and aims

Rhea knows that Orren's loyalists took the royal niche route, Captain Rook followed rather than abandoning the wounded, and a dead clerk carried papers under a second Vey seal that did not match the main contract. She wants the Common Measure preserved and Orren returned alive if possible. Her recurring question is practical: what takes the weight when the gold is removed?

## Breath, triage, and proof

If rescued, Rhea can stabilize survivors, identify drowned bodies, diagnose mnemonic contamination, read medical and recognition protocols, and provide a careful account of the Company split. She does not authenticate every contract term or settle responsibility by herself. Her remaining air, injury, rescue, later location, patients, and willingness to aid the expedition remain changing state.
