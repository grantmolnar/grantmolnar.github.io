# The Crown of Measure

**Reference ID:** `eeae1101-1cb5-471f-92d8-eb27757ca095`  
**Kind:** Object  
**Aliases:** Avarra's Crown, the royal Crown  
**Tags:** royal instrument, burden, custody, Avarra

## Summary

A gold-black command and burden instrument whose custody, load, and retained royal faculties remain disputed.

## Reference Material

## A burden instrument

The Crown of Measure is a gold-black circlet built on the scale of a city wall: six arches close around a central measuring tongue inside the Deep Bell's clapper cage. It is not merely regalia. The Crown couples royal command, legal recognition, memory cadence, and a substantial share of the Bell's physical load.

## What the Crown carries

Queen Avarra's retained faculties pass between Crown and Iron Choir as Measure, Refusal, and Witness. The Crown can help compare burdens, reject a coerced acceptance, preserve surrender terms, open royal mechanisms, and complete certain command cadences. It cannot choose a just bearer, create consent, erase displaced strain, or make one genealogy exclusive.

## Custody is not settlement

The Golden Branch can recognize descent and open custody mechanisms. The Burden Chain recognizes communities and offices that maintained the machine. The Ash Witness preserves Avarra's conditions and release testimony. Possession may confer leverage and physical access without conferring lawful command. The Crown's condition, current custodian, attached load, available faculties, and final disposition remain live state; freeing it requires transferring or spending what it carries.
