## Summary

Salt-preserved officers divided over whether to continue, replace, or end the Deep Bell's inherited burden.

## Reference Material

## Personhood and maintenance

A Salt Warden is a maintained person whose continuity depends on a preserved shell, a salt memory tablet, and witnesses who recognize the relation between them. A shell can obey without weighing exceptions; a tablet can recite records without deciding what its remembered feelings mean. Repair may preserve uncertainty, but theft, forced transfer, or missing witnesses makes identity and authority disputed.

## Present division

The Continuance faction, represented by Sister Vesra Quill, would repair people and machine before any release. Prior Dalca's Release faction argues that the old sentence has expired and that repair must not renew captivity. Literal-order Wardens obey the last command they recognize. Prefect Ardel Quoin's missing command tablet and sealed shell leave all three positions without an undisputed commander.

## Command, bargains, and refusal

Wardens recognize declared burden, witnessed duty, valid command tablets, and bounded orders. They can supply disciplined labor, Wells crews, route seals, command keys, tablet repair, and testimony. They require every new duty to identify its scope and remedy; an alliance that merely restores obedience without resolving consent repeats the buried house's original failure.
