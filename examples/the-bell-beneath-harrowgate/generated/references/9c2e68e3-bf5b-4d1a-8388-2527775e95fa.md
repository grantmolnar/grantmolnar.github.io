## Summary

A Harrowgate councilor and creditor who supplied the debt rider and abridged royal sequence meant to turn rapid Crown possession into security for town obligations.

## Reference Material

## Creditor and councilor

Councilor Samet Rhun is a Harrowgate creditor with civic standing but no independent royal or custodial title. He proposed treating physical possession of the Crown of Measure as security for town debt, supplied an accelerated-possession rider, and required custody before public notice.

## The abridged sequence

Rhun also supplied the royal extraction copy used by Orren Saye. It labels the outer Crown chain as a release restraint while omitting the unload and synchronization operations that reveal live burden on the Crown Drum. The complete Ropeworks and Scriptorium records expose the omissions; they do not prove that every person who handled the copy understood them.

## A creditor claim without custody

Rhun may advance a creditor claim, answer for his rider, and influence Harrowgate politics. He cannot pledge the whole town without lawful public authority, bind Lady Vey or the Ember Company beyond accepted agreements, convert the Crown into ordinary collateral, or decide which buried community bears the debt. His later political position and accountability remain open.
