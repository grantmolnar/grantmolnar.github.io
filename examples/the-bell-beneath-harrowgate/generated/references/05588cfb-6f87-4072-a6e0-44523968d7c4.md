# The Low Choir

**Reference ID:** `05588cfb-6f87-4072-a6e0-44523968d7c4`  
**Kind:** Organization  
**Aliases:** Low Choir households, the Choir below  
**Tags:** community, memory, households, inherited burden

## Summary

The living descendant community beneath Harrowgate whose names, memories, labor, and households have borne the Deep Bell's hidden mnemonic cost for generations.

## Reference Material

## A living community, not a mechanism

The Low Choir descends from workers, prisoners, petitioners, and households kept below the old royal machine. Later custodians redirected the Deep Bell's mnemonic cost into their names and memories. The community preserves people through households, memory cups, inherited names, work, witness, and chosen obligation; it is not identical with the Iron Choir apparatus that stores and speaks extracted voices.

## Households and positions

Low Choir households disagree about refuge, passage, continued maintenance, Crown custody, public return, and the meaning of inherited names. The Feast of Empty Chairs makes four broad positions visible, but no caucus or speaker owns every household. Their compact practice asks who witnesses, who may pass, what is provided, who maintains it, where the limit lies, and what remedy follows failure.

## Households bind themselves

Households can bind their own stores, routes, witnesses, memories, labor, and continuing duties. Named representatives can negotiate only within the standing they actually hold. No single speaker can surrender every memory, accept a new hereditary burden for all, or make Harrowgate's promises true by reciting them. Current household positions, rescued names, cups, casualties, routes, and final compact remain matters for play.
