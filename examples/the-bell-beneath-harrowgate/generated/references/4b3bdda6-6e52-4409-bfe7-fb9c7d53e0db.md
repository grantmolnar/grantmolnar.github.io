# The Deep Bell

**Reference ID:** `4b3bdda6-6e52-4409-bfe7-fb9c7d53e0db`  
**Kind:** Place  
**Aliases:** Deep Bell, the Bell  
**Tags:** bell, pressure system, Crown of Measure, finale

## Summary

The buried sovereign machine that converts mountain strain into water, counterweight, command, and mnemonic burdens through a four-phase toll.

## Reference Material

## What the Bell does

The Deep Bell is a vertical pressure machine built around the Crown of Measure. Its cycle takes breath, draws load from connected systems, lowers the Crown as clapper and command junction, and sounds into Harrowgate. Later alterations made the Low Choir the nearest open mnemonic path, turning repeated stabilization into inherited human cost.

## Four phases and connected systems

Taking Breath gathers pressure. Drawing Load pulls through water, roots, foundations, Wells, and memory. Crown Descent brings physical mass and recognized authority into the bell cage. Sounding releases what the previous phases assigned. Ropeworks drums, ward-light, the Reliquary, Garden, Chapel, Wells, Iron Choir, Black Rain, and the Mouth all change what the cycle receives and where it returns force.

## Repairs, endings, and named costs

The Bell can be held, repaired, supplied with a replacement burden, distributed among visible systems, broken through prepared collapse, stripped of the Crown, or subordinated to a Mouth settlement. No architecture removes cost; each requires named bearers, casualties, authority, maintenance, and remedy. Current phase, pressure, damage, crews, Crown condition, active paths, and final settlement remain changing state.
