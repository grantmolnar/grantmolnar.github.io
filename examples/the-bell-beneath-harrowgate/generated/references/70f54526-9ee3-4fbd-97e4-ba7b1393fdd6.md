# Lady Merrow Vey

**Reference ID:** `70f54526-9ee3-4fbd-97e4-ba7b1393fdd6`  
**Kind:** Person  
**Aliases:** Lady Vey, Merrow Vey  
**Tags:** House Vey, Crown claim, recovery trust, surface authority

## Summary

The last acknowledged royal descendant: lawful sponsor of the secret recovery expedition, holder of a genuine but bounded Crown claim, and principal able to bind House Vey but not Harrowgate or the buried communities.

## Reference Material

## Claim and complicity

Lady Merrow Vey is the last acknowledged descendant of Harrowgate's buried kings and the principal of the recovery trust that hired the Ember Company. Her written contract authorized a quiet survey and Crown recovery only after the buried house was made safe, with witnessed transfer and no damage to the Bell. She did not authorize Councilor Rhun's possession rider, Halren Coss's second payment schedule, or Orren Saye's chain cut. Her secrecy, money, seals, and chosen agents nevertheless created the opportunity for all three.

## What she wants

Vey wants Harrowgate stabilized and the Crown of Measure returned to House Vey afterward. She treats the Crown as stolen dynastic property, but the surviving genealogies give her a genuine custody claim rather than exclusive authority over Avarra, the Bell, or every burden attached to them. She may repudiate the concealed rider and Orren's method without becoming innocent of the expedition.

## What her seal can bind

Vey may bind House Vey's grain stores, retainers, genealogy archive, money, warrants, and claim. She may lend genealogy seals, delegate an agent, support evacuation or maintenance, and accept divided or supervised custody. She cannot bind Harrowgate's elected officers, the Salt Wardens, Low Choir households, Queen Avarra's retained will, or another blood line. Her present concessions, resources committed, and final Crown position remain matters for play.
