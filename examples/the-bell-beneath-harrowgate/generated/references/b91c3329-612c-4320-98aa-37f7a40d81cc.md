## Summary

A blinded Ember Company scout whose touch-reading of route chalk, dark-route discipline, and testimony about the overlapping contract remain useful beyond her rescue.

## Reference Material

## Scout and witness

Tavia Hest is an Ember Company scout who survived the Lantern Cistern by tying herself to a valve standard after a flare burned her eyes. She can identify Company route chalk by touch and testify that Orren ordered Corven Braid to carry a second contract sealed by Chief Factor Halren Coss.

## Priorities and capabilities

Tavia wants Corven recovered, the lower ward marks denied to Orren, and wounded Company members treated as people rather than discarded accomplices. Once her face is washed and the burned cloth is changed, her sight returns gradually. Even before then she can guide dark routes, find the Reliquary water lift, range ahead without losing direction, and assist survivor extraction.

## Sight, testimony, and dependence

Tavia's testimony establishes a second contract and Company route practice; it does not prove every term, Vey's prior knowledge, or Orren's final location. Her injuries, treatment, mobility, confidence, current route, and willingness to continue remain changing state.
