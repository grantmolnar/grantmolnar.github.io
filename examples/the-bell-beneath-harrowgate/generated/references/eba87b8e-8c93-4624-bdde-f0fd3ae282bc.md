## Reference Material

## Keeper of burden records

Odran Greve is the last living-born Keeper of the Chain Scriptorium. He survives inside its rotating index cage after the archive returned more than a century of working memories to him. He speaks as if each sentence needs a hook and shelf because several offices still press their unfinished duties through his memory.

## Knowledge and custody

Odran can trace water, royal, memory, execution, maintenance, personhood, Menagerie, and emergency-descent records by the burdens they governed. He knows the Crown's coerced repurposing, the later transfer of mnemonic cost to the Low Choir, the accepted-burden precedent, and the relationship among the three genealogies. He can expose omissions in Orren's sequence and open several record-defined routes.

## What stays on the chains

Odran may interpret plates, authenticate their physical order, lend a bounded record set, and provide maintenance testimony. He will not surrender the whole archive at once: removing records without their hanging context can make theft look like proof and can physically destroy the index. His survival, evacuation, plates removed, routes opened, and memories retained remain encounter and play-state questions.
