## Summary

The Ember Company captain who refused Orren's loaded chain cut, keeps rescue and stabilization ahead of possession, and can command surviving Company hands without deciding their public judgment.

## Reference Material

## Command and the Company split

Captain Maelin Rook commanded the Ember Company's descent. At the Ropeworks she saw live strain on the Crown, Water, and Memory drums and refused Orren Saye's order to cut the outer royal chain. Orren proceeded under the abridged sequence, and the expedition divided around the refusal. Rook's counter-orders, field signs, restrained demolition work, and care for the wounded preserve a second Company account of the failure.

## Priorities and manner

Rook puts rescue and stabilization before Crown possession. She wants the concealed rider, incomplete sequence, and divided responsibility entered into the public record. She is practical about dangerous work and unsentimental about command, but she will not let surface patrons or buried authorities use the emergency to discard her people before their conduct is heard.

## Orders she will give

Rook may command Ember personnel who still recognize her, coordinate rigging and rescue labor, authenticate Company field practice, and negotiate a disciplined withdrawal or renewed operation. She cannot bind Lady Vey, Harrowgate, the Salt Wardens, or Company members who have deserted or chosen Orren. Her survival, location, available crew, injuries, and disposition remain encounter and play-state questions.
