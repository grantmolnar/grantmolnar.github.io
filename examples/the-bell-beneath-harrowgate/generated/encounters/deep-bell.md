# The Deep Bell

**Role:** necessary, end  
**Tags:** finale, bell, choice, deep

Four moving approaches converge on the Bell, where the Crown cannot leave its load behind.

## Opening View

The bridge moves sideways beneath your first step. Across the void, three other approaches answer in different directions. Only then does the Deep Bell emerge from the dark: a bronze horizon patched with royal plate, Warden bronze, quarry clamps, and mourning wire. The Crown burns in the clapper cage. The speaking rail clicks once. There is no floor to receive what comes next.

## Encounter Material

## Situation

The Deep Bell hangs over the Mouth Below between four load systems. Its skin is patched with royal metal, Warden bronze, quarry clamps, and Low Choir memorial wire; every patch records who was available to spend when an earlier repair failed. This operation will add another record.

There is no safe floor. Four approaches reach different parts of the mechanism:

- the **upper control walk** from the Choir reaches the preparatory catches and speaking rail;
- the **maintenance bridge** from the Counterweight Wells reaches the load yokes and chain splices;
- the **lower bridge** from Black Rain reaches the clapper cage, water brakes, and collapse charges;
- the **mortuary walk** from the King's Narrow Grave reaches the Crown cradle and royal release locks.

No approach suffices alone. Cross by climbing the bell skin, traversing moving chains, using appeal wings or Quiet Beasts, throwing lines through the cage, or rotating the Bell. Every crossing is exposed when the cycle advances.

Inside the clapper cage hangs the Crown of Measure: six gold-black arches around a measuring tongue, with the cut royal chain trailing like a severed tendon. Measure, Refusal, and Witness move between Crown and Choir according to the current system state.

## The four phases of a toll

Track the toll in four phases. Advance after a major failed operation, prolonged conflict, or unopposed command cascade. Prepared systems can delay, repeat, or skip a phase.

### 1. Taking Breath

The Lantern and Reliquary networks draw air, heat, and memory toward the deep works. Lamps brighten or fail. People carrying loose memories hear them speak. The upper control walk is stable.

Actions suited to this phase include issuing Choir commands, establishing witnesses, setting water routes, evacuating exposed people, and moving a replacement burden into position.

### 2. Drawing Load

The Ropeworks and three Wells take weight. Bridges tilt. The maintenance yokes become accessible. Anyone holding a voluntary or coerced burden feels the machine trying to classify it.

This is the safest phase for splicing chains, fitting mass, shifting Wells paths, or arranging a distributed stabilization. It is the most dangerous phase for people trapped on unbalanced bridges.

### 3. Crown Descent

The Crown drops toward the bell skin. Black Rain rises. The royal and mortuary locks align. Avarra's faculties become loud enough to speak through metal carried by the party.

This is the only clean phase for removing the Crown, transferring its load, or placing a recognized replacement. A forced extraction is possible at other times but tears mass through an uncontrolled path.

### 4. Sounding

The measuring tongue strikes. Force passes into whichever water, memory, and counterweight routes remain open. Everyone in the shaft must anchor, yield, or ride the motion. The town above receives whatever the party did not route elsewhere.

A controlled sounding can complete a repair or test a replacement. An uncontrolled sounding advances the catastrophe, destroys one vulnerable approach, and creates memory bleed throughout the dungeon.

After Sounding, the cycle returns to Taking Breath if the Bell survives.

## Immediate pressures

Begin in Taking Breath unless prior failures accelerated the sequence. Select or infer three active pressures:

- a Wells load is slipping;
- Black Rain is at High or Crown level;
- the Iron Choir is issuing an automatic continuity command;
- the royal gutter or lower bridge is failing;
- Deep Rank Wardens are securing the Crown for continuance;
- Orren, Lady Vey's agents, or another claimant is attempting the mortuary locks;
- Low Choir memory is being drawn into the Funerary path;
- the fourth toll is already damaging Harrowgate; or
- the Mouth Below is answering a prior uncontrolled offering.

Begin with enough information for a plan. The difficulty lies in executing linked operations under pressure, never in hiding the relevant lever.

## Queen Avarra's retained will

Avarra may arrive divided among Crown, Choir, records, or portable tongues. Bring Measure, Refusal, and Witness into cadence and she can speak in short intervals.

She remembers the siege, constrained surrender, and first Bell design; later history reaches her only through memories fed to the machine. She compares burdens, refuses royal commands through her Crown, and identifies the Low Choir transfer and hereditary sentence as later additions.

Her wishes are clear but bounded:

- end the use of her Crown as an involuntary clapper;
- do not place another unwilling person or people into the same sentence;
- preserve Harrowgate if doing so does not require pretending the old arrangement is just; and
- leave future duty visible, transferable, and answerable to those who bear it.

She is capable of compromise. If immediate release would kill thousands and no prepared alternative exists, she will accept a bounded emergency continuance with a stated end, living witnesses, and enforceable remedy. She will not declare a descendant absolute, certify a coerced Common Measure, or promise that the Mouth Below can be mastered.

A claimant may operate the Crown through isolated Measure while suppressing Refusal. A destroyer may break the Bell through Refusal without hearing Witness. Make the mutilation and its advantage visible.

## The Bell's defenses

Build opposition from unresolved earlier states, not one final monster.

### Burden echoes

As Drawing Load begins, the Bell extrudes figures from soot, mineral water, and memory wire. Each resembles someone currently carrying an obligation: a Warden commander, a Low Choir witness, a claimant, a rescued delver, or a party member. An echo repeats the obligation without its limit. A promise to protect becomes refusal to move. A claim to the Crown becomes possession at any cost. A rescue duty becomes dragging everyone toward one route.

An echo can be fought, named, given a limit, discharged through a fulfilled act, or redirected into a prepared burden. Destroying it releases its load into the nearest chain.

### Chain saints

Four enormous jointed forms climb the suspension chains. They were built from decommissioned Reliquary saints and royal armor. Each protects one approach and can transfer itself between chains during Sounding. They obey valid Choir command, recognized Warden muster, or the old continuity recital.

Their combat purpose is to separate teams, close access to timing windows, and carry materials to the wrong load path. They can be disabled locally, reassigned, deceived with a legitimate narrow command, or given a safer maintenance duty.

### Claimant conflict

Any faction still pursuing exclusive control arrives through its available route. This may be Orren with contract survivors, Lady Vey's retainers, Deep Rank Wardens, a Low Choir release party, or surface authorities attempting to prevent collapse. Keep their goals intelligible. They may bargain during the operation, defect when costs become visible, or fight over the Crown cage while the Bell continues moving.

## Core operations

### Hold the cycle

The party can delay one phase through a prepared Choir command, a correctly timed Wells catch, a Reliquary breath reserve, a velvet hound consuming the phase's vibration, or people physically holding a brake. Every method has a local limit. Repeating it without relief causes injury, beast migration, mechanism damage, or lost memory.

### Repair the royal chain

A durable repair requires compatible metal, a splice path, and a safe Drawing Load window. Royal splice links and crown-bars are ideal. Salt bronze, silver root, or beast tissue can make bounded repairs. The repaired chain restores controlled continuance and clean future Crown transfer. It also restores the old machine's capacity to keep operating.

### Transfer the Crown's load

The Crown cannot be removed cleanly while it remains the recognized and physical burden. The party must either:

- fit an accepted replacement with sufficient mass and a viable Wells path;
- distribute the load among water, counterweights, roots, and ongoing civic operations;
- drop or spend a Well to take the shock;
- route the load into a controlled collapse; or
- negotiate a new destination through the Mouth Below.

Cutting the Crown free without a destination is still a choice. The load goes to the nearest open path, usually Harrowgate's foundations, the Funerary memory route, or the person holding the Crown.

### Fit the Common Measure or another replacement

A replacement needs Chapel recognition, actual material, named maintainers, and consent that remains valid at Crown Descent. At Drawing Load, move the frame through the Wells yoke. At Crown Descent, bring Crown and replacement into parallel measure. Measure can test the transfer. Refusal can reject coercion. Witness can record the terms.

A partial replacement may carry some functions while water and civic labor carry the rest. A frame accepted under coercion works briefly but produces burden echoes, Chapel execution claims, and future political crisis.

### Free the Crown

The six release locks on the mortuary walk answer the Golden Branch, Burden Chain, and Ash Witness in pairs. Any one genealogy can open two. Two compatible jurisdictions can open four. All six open cleanly when custody, operating authority, and surrender limits are acknowledged.

Force, royal command, or Crown Descent alignment can bypass missing locks. The Crown then comes free with damage or an attached claim. Whoever carries it can invoke Avarra's retained authority over old mechanisms and the Mouth's learned royal cadence. That power is real, limited, and politically explosive.

### Break the Bell

The Bell can be destroyed by severing suspension chains, detonating quarry charges on the lower bridge, dropping a Well, inducing a Black Rain surge, or turning its own Sounding against a prepared fracture line. Destruction without venting and evacuation sends the shaft collapse into Harrowgate. Controlled collapse requires choosing a route for water, stone, and people.

## Finale architectures

Use these as operating architectures, not scripts. Combine them when preparation supports a hybrid.

### Controlled continuance

**Purpose:** keep the Bell operating while ending the immediate uncontrolled tolls.

**Requirements:** a repaired or securely braced royal chain; balanced Wells and Ropeworks; one lawful water route; operators; and a decision about ongoing memory cost.

**Procedure:** stabilize during Drawing Load, restore the Crown path at Crown Descent, issue a bounded continuity command, and survive one controlled Sounding as a test.

**Costs and variants:** the old system survives. The Low Choir burden may be reduced, voluntarily compensated, transferred, or shamefully continued. Avarra accepts only an emergency term with a real end and remedy. This ending buys time and creates a campaign about reform, maintenance, and whether promises made under crisis are kept.

### Replacement burden

**Purpose:** free the Crown while preserving a centralized pressure-release mechanism.

**Requirements:** an accepted frame, sufficient mass, compatible fittings, Wells path, maintainers, and continuing consent.

**Procedure:** bring replacement and Crown into parallel measure during Crown Descent, transfer physical and recognized load, open the release locks, then complete a test Sounding.

**Costs and variants:** a well-designed Common Measure distributes obligation and makes future transfer possible. A dynastic, Warden, or coerced replacement may work but recreate the injustice in another form. Partial acceptance produces a hybrid with water and civic labor.

### Distributed stabilization

**Purpose:** silence the Bell as a central sovereign machine and divide its work among visible systems.

**Requirements:** Crown release; controlled Black Rain routes; functioning Wells or substitutes; Garden and quarry pressure capacity; ongoing Harrowgate labor and funding; settled Low Choir and Warden roles.

**Procedure:** during Drawing Load, unload the Crown into several prepared paths. At Crown Descent, lock the clapper away from the bell skin. At Sounding, let each system take a bounded share and verify that no hidden Funerary draw occurs.

**Costs and variants:** Harrowgate must maintain pumps, vents, roots, brakes, and evacuation plans permanently. The solution is less elegant, more expensive, and harder to hide. It is also harder for one claimant to own. Failure in later years becomes political and administrative rather than mythical.

### Controlled collapse

**Purpose:** destroy the Bell and allow the mountain to settle through prepared sacrificial routes.

**Requirements:** surface evacuation; quarry vents or another collapse zone; Garden shutters prepared or replaced; Black Rain sequence known; severance or demolition access.

**Procedure:** lower Black Rain into the chosen route, open vents in staged order, isolate memory and water systems where possible, then break the Bell during Sounding so its force follows the prepared fracture.

**Costs and variants:** the deep complex is partly or wholly lost. Harrowgate loses districts, wealth, and perhaps access to rescued history. A well-prepared collapse preserves most lives. A rushed one still may be preferable to cascading failure. The Crown can be removed first, buried, destroyed, or lost below.

### Crown release and royal claim

**Purpose:** prioritize possession or liberation of the Crown even without a complete replacement.

**Requirements:** release-lock access or force; a way to take the load, however costly; an extraction route.

**Procedure:** unload through a Well drop, Foundation Weir, temporary human hold, or Mouth path; free the Crown at Crown Descent; then stabilize or flee before Sounding.

**Costs and variants:** giving the Crown to Lady Vey, another claimant, a Warden trust, the Low Choir, Harrowgate, Avarra's retained will, or no one creates different campaigns. Exclusive royal use can command old mechanisms and influence the Mouth, but Refusal may oppose it. Pure liberation without a custody plan invites theft and renewed conflict.

### Mouth Below settlement

**Purpose:** replace the Bell's repeated mechanical bargain with an explicit relationship to the underlying phenomenon.

**Requirements:** safe descent, an offering, stated boundary and return, an exit plan, and acceptance of uncertainty.

**Procedure:** hold or redirect the cycle while representatives descend; negotiate below; return with a pattern the Bell, Choir, or Black Rain gates can enact; then test it at Sounding or silence the Bell under the new arrangement.

**Costs and variants:** the Mouth may accept periodic vibration, opened landscape, named memory, royal cadence, or another recurring exchange. No settlement makes it human. The result can restore memories, reduce pressure, or end royal control, but it creates a new duty and a new kind of risk.

### Cascading survival

**Purpose:** determine what can still be saved after the prepared architectures fail.

When Sounding begins uncontrolled, ask each active team to save one category:

- people on an approach;
- Harrowgate above;
- Low Choir memory;
- the Crown and Avarra's faculties;
- a future operating system; or
- access to the Mouth Below.

Every saved category assigns force to another unless someone accepts an extraordinary sacrifice. A cascading ending should still answer who survived, where the pressure went, who carries authority, and what remains possible next.

## Partial successes

Use partial outcomes instead of treating the finale as binary.

- **Bell stabilized for one season:** enough time for evacuation or political settlement, but another descent will be required.
- **Crown freed, load unresolved:** the party escapes with the Crown while Harrowgate enters an emergency campaign.
- **Low Choir memory released, identities mixed:** involuntary extraction ends, but restoration becomes long communal work.
- **Town saved, deep community displaced:** the machine or collapse preserves Harrowgate while the Low Choir must relocate.
- **Machine destroyed, Mouth awakened:** the old injustice ends and a new phenomenon begins shaping the region.
- **Replacement works, consent disputed:** physical victory produces legal and moral instability.
- **Avarra witnessed but not freed:** her testimony changes every claim even though the Crown remains.
- **Crown lost below:** no faction controls it, but the Mouth now has royal cadence.

## After the operation

State four things plainly after the operation:

1. what happened to Harrowgate's people, districts, water, and future safety;
2. what happened to the Low Choir's memory, homes, and political standing;
3. who controls, carries, destroys, or loses the Crown and Avarra's retained faculties; and
4. what duty remains, who accepted it, and what remedy exists when they fail.

Then resolve surviving factions and rescues. Orren's contract, Lady Vey's claim, Warden command, Low Choir compacts, treasure custody, and Ember Company responsibility do not disappear because the engineering succeeded.

## Carry-forward states

Record:

- final phase reached and whether a controlled Sounding occurred;
- surviving approaches and chain saints;
- Bell frame, clapper, and royal chain condition;
- Crown location, carrier, and recognized custody;
- Measure, Refusal, and Witness condition;
- chosen architecture and any hybrid elements;
- load destinations and permanent sacrifices;
- Harrowgate and Low Choir casualties, evacuations, and resources;
- factions present, agreements made, and authority recognized;
- recovered treasure and extraction route; and
- explicit future duties, limits, and remedies.

At the Bell, every earlier choice becomes physical. Reward preparation, permit costly improvisation, and leave a result whose bearers and casualties can be named.
