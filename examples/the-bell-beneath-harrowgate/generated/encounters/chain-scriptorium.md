# The Chain Scriptorium

**Role:** necessary  
**Tags:** archive, routes, history, machinery

A hanging archive files truth by duty and exposes the claimant behind every question.

## Opening View

Keeper Odran Greve bars the threshold with one open hand. “Not what do you want,” he says. “Under what duty do you ask?” When someone answers, brass records begin travelling through the dark: water low, royal claims high, memory plates warm between them. A genealogy descends one link, stops, and sways. Odran watches the stopped link rather than the speaker.

## Encounter Material

## Situation

The buried house hangs plans, judgments, repair orders, and dynastic claims by the burden they governed. Water records hang low and green, royal plates high, and silvered memory plates warm between them.

At the central post, state a purpose and accept the records it implies. The chains answer with prior uses of the same duty: evacuation routes and casualty rolls for rescue, confiscation inventories and hidden returns for theft, succession law and execution orders for sovereignty.

## Navigating the archive

Search by:

- declaring a purpose and accepting the records it implies;
- physically following chain families from one mechanism to another;
- using genealogy seals to access royal plates;
- releasing the brake that keeps condemned records above the floor; or
- asking a Salt Warden or Low Choir guide to read old burden notation.

On a failed search, bring forward a dangerous or inconvenient truth. Never return nothing.

## Keeper Odran Greve

**Odran Greve** turns within the rotating index cage, frail beneath a century of returned working memory. Each sentence arrives as if it needs a hook and shelf. “A room that remembers what an office forgot,” he calls the Scriptorium; several such offices still speak through him.

Odran knows:

- Queen Avarra's limited surrender permitted her Crown to stabilize the fault, though the surrender itself was coerced;
- the bell's regular toll transferred mountain strain into the Black Rain Cistern;
- later wardens redirected the toll's mnemonic cost onto the labor community now called the Low Choir;
- the crown can be replaced by another accepted burden of equal declared authority; and
- a controlled collapse can save most of Harrowgate if the quarry vents and black-water channels are opened first.

Odran will not hand over every plate at once. “A record can be stolen by removing it,” he says, “or by removing everything that tells you where it hung.” Too much extraction destroys the physical index and sends whole chain families sliding into lower shafts.

## The chained genealogy

Lady Vey's descent is genuine but incomplete. The high royal plates divide three jurisdictions:

- the Golden Branch traces blood custody through House Vey and collateral lines;
- the Burden Chain traces operating standing through offices, households, guilds, Wardens, and crews that maintained the machine; and
- the Ash Witness preserves Avarra's surrender limits through living and inherited testimony.

None grants simple ownership. Together they separate custody, maintained obligation, and release testimony. The King's Narrow Grave can be found by aligning all three instead of privileging one.

## Dangers

**Index shear.** Sudden movement turns the hanging rows into cutting pendulums. The party can drop, brace, ride, or deliberately redirect the chain wave.

**Condemned records.** Plates lifted out of the lower dark carry executed promises. Reading one may impose a temporary compulsion, reveal a hidden route, or summon a load ghost that insists the old sentence be completed.

**Orren's search party.** Factor Orren Saye or his agents may arrive seeking the royal chain sequence. They prefer bargaining, theft, and hostage leverage to a fair fight. Orren believes the town will confiscate the crown unless he reaches it first. Odran can compare Orren's copied schedule with the complete hanging plate: the copy preserves the release command and removes the three prerequisite operations that would have shown the Crown Drum was still carrying live load.

## Routes and useful plates

The archive can identify or directly open routes to:

- the Ropeworks inspection gallery;
- the Salt Barracks through a muster-record door;
- the Reliquary Under Water by its salvage register;
- the Feast of Empty Chairs through ration chains;
- the Inverted Chapel through execution records;
- the King's Narrow Grave through the three genealogies; and
- the Choir of Iron Tongues through maintenance concordances.

Recovering a maintenance plate lets Pell Varo or another skilled operator perform an additional major repair.

## Hard choices

- Taking the royal genealogy strengthens Lady Vey's claim but removes evidence of the other two successions unless copied.
- Releasing Low Choir memory plates restores names at the cost of extinguishing one family of ward lamps.
- Giving Odran safe passage deprives the archive of its keeper; leaving him preserves navigation but risks his death in the fourth toll.
- Destroying condemned plates prevents old compulsions from returning and also erases proof of crimes committed by the buried kings.

## Outcomes

Before the party leaves, give them one route, one operating truth, and one disputed claim. They need not exhaust the archive; every plate removed changes what can later be proved.
