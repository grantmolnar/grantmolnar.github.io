# The Black Rain Cistern

**Role:** necessary  
**Tags:** water, pressure, engineering, deep

Upward rain gives every spared strain an address in body, street, root, or machine.

## Opening View

The first drop strikes your chin from below. Black rain rises from a lake with no visible surface, clears against the ribbed ceiling, and runs into four great gutters. Surveyor Kesh Arden has written a Harrowgate street beside every gauge mark. Sister Pell tastes the water, closes her eyes, and refuses to name its owner. Every gutter is already somebody’s address.

## Encounter Material

## Situation

The Black Rain Cistern makes mountain strain visible. A bottomless lake hangs below; black drops rise, strike the ribbed ceiling, turn clear, and enter four great gutters. The water changes color. The force passes into stone, roots, counterweights, foundations, and the Deep Bell.

The builders could route pressure, never erase it. Every clean-looking solution sends force toward quarry vents, the Reliquary, the Mouth Below, or Harrowgate's foundations. Later custodians hid the recipient by calling the cistern a drain.

The Ember Company opened the royal spillway while the quarry mouths were still sealed. The next surge tore one gutter from its frame and filled the chamber with upward rain. The Iron Choir is now calling the gates toward their old automatic sequence. That sequence assumes intact roots, staffed pumps, and an evacuated lower cloister. None of those assumptions is presently true.

## Cast

- **Surveyor Kesh Arden**, a Harrowgate quarry apprentice, was pulled through a cracked vent while measuring the third toll. He has survived on a shelf above the Foundation Weir, scratching street names beside each pressure mark. He speaks quickly until asked who lives above a vent; then he gives every household he knows.
- **Sister Pell-of-the-Last-Dry-Step** is a Low Choir pressure reader. Black rain has replaced pieces of her recent memory with sensations from the fault. She predicts surges in images—iron under the tongue, a stair bending west—and always adds, “Mine or the mountain’s; use it, but do not call it mine yet.”
- **The Rain Wardens** are four ceramic diving forms stored in wall alcoves. They enter black water, carry gate chains, and return coated in mineral glass. Two answer old Warden command. One answers the Iron Choir. One answers any person who has been struck by black rain and survived.
- **The Upward Drowned** are pressure bodies shaped from water, grit, and borrowed posture. They resemble people climbing through deep water in reverse. Breaking one releases force into the nearest open path.

## Immediate pressures

The cistern begins at **High Working Level** unless prior water operations changed it. The torn royal gutter is shedding clear water toward the foundations. The quarry gutter is sealed by surface rubble. The Reliquary return is partly open. The Mouth sluice is breathing in pulses.

At the next pressure rise, one of four things will happen unless the party intervenes:

- the Foundation Weir lifts and cracks Harrowgate's inhabited ridge;
- the Reliquary return floods the copper saints and any lower rescue route;
- the Mouth sluice opens without terms and teaches the underlying phenomenon a new path upward; or
- the torn gutter fails, dropping a section of the Bell's lower bridge.

Kesh can identify the surface consequences. Pell can identify the sequence. Neither can operate all stations alone.

## The four routes of strain

### Quarry vent manifold

Six bronze throats lead toward the quarry and abandoned lower cloister. Three surface mouths are known. Two have been built over. One opens into a forgotten lime pit. Venting here converts water pressure into rockfall, steam, and violent air movement.

A safe staged vent requires:

- at least two surface mouths opened or one mouth and a sacrificial collapse zone;
- Garden root shutters prepared so the pressure does not tear through food beds and inhabited ways;
- people cleared from the quarry and affected streets; and
- the manifold opened in rising order rather than all at once.

Opening the manifold without those preparations still relieves the Bell. It spends buildings, routes, and lives instead of preparation.

### Reliquary return

This broad copper channel sends water through the six saints, where breath, heat, and suspended grit can be separated. It is the most controllable route while the Reliquary remains intact. It also carries black-water memory into a chamber already holding the dead and the injured.

At low and middle Reliquary levels, the return can absorb one major surge. At high level, it threatens Rhea Colm's former station, the royal niche, and the lower culvert. A cooperative pallbearer or prepared saints can distribute the surge. An uncontrolled return animates drowned load ghosts and may overwrite restored memory.

### Mouth sluice

A vertical throat descends beneath the lake. When opened, it sends vibration and black water directly into the Mouth Below. This buys immediate pressure relief and can create a descent route. It also constitutes an offering before the party has stated any boundary. Repeated use teaches the Mouth that Harrowgate answers strain by feeding it memory-laden water.

The sluice can be opened narrowly for sound, halfway for a negotiable flow, or fully as an emergency sacrifice. Closing it after a full opening may require the Deep Bell's Refusal faculty, a Funerary Well release, or a bargain made below.

### Foundation Weir

The final gutter leads upward through Harrowgate's ridge. It was designed as a last resort: distribute strain across empty terraces, let walls crack, and preserve the deep system. Those terraces are no longer empty. The chapterhouse, poor streets, old quarry housing, and part of Lady Vey's estate now stand above them.

The weir can spread one surge broadly, focus it into a chosen collapse zone, or be blocked. Blocking it does not remove the load. It forces the next surge toward another route. Using it without evacuation protects the machine by spending the town.

## Cistern levels

Track five broad states.

1. **Low:** the lake hangs far below; the lower bridge and Mouth throat are exposed. Pressure is accumulating elsewhere.
2. **Working:** upward rain is steady; any one prepared route can carry the next surge.
3. **High:** rain fills the chamber; travel requires anchored lines, Rain Warden assistance, or movement through dry gutter ribs.
4. **Crown:** black water reaches the royal gauge. The next major change begins the Deep Bell's Crown Descent phase immediately.
5. **Ruptured:** the chamber has chosen a new permanent path through broken stone. Mark which population, region, or landscape receives it.

A level change means nothing without a destination; low water may still conceal high total strain.

## Moving through upward rain

Ordinary falling becomes unreliable. Loose objects rise until they strike the ceiling gutters. A person who loses contact with a line may be carried upward, pinned beneath a channel, and swept laterally into another route. Clear water falls normally once separated, producing intersecting curtains.

Useful methods include:

- Rain Warden carries;
- Counterweight Wells tension lines;
- a Menagerie velvet hound consuming patterned vibration along one crossing;
- Cinderback drawing heat from a gate housing;
- Reliquary saint breath sent through the return;
- Garden roots grown or rigged across the ribs; and
- deliberate release of a small surge to create a brief dry interval.

Build combat around anchors, gates, rescue, and route choice. Killing an Upward Drowned without controlling where its pressure goes can be worse than letting it pass.

## Pressure readings

Three instruments make the chamber legible.

- The **water crown** shows which route will receive the next uncontrolled surge.
- The **fault harp** sounds the total pressure irrespective of visible level.
- The **foundation pins** correspond to districts above and show where accumulated strain has already entered Harrowgate.

Together they show pressure routed, delayed, divided, or converted—never erased. Anyone claiming disposal has failed to name the sacrifice.

## Common operations

### Stage a quarry vent

Open surface mouths, prepare Garden shutters, clear exposed areas, and sequence the six manifold throats from smallest to largest. A fully prepared vent lowers Black Rain by two states and creates a safe controlled-collapse path. A partial preparation lowers it by one state and assigns a specific local disaster. An unprepared full release lowers it immediately and devastates the quarry district.

### Load the Reliquary return

Set the six saints, establish an air and memory path, and open the copper channel during a Wells brake window. Success moves one surge into manageable water and heat. Failure raises the Reliquary level, activates drowned load ghosts, or washes memory into the wrong recipients.

### Feed the Mouth sluice

Choose the width and duration of the opening and identify what accompanies the water: anonymous vibration, named memory, royal cadence, black-water minerals, or a deliberate message from the Iron Choir. This operation can begin a settlement or create a predatory expectation.

### Focus the Foundation Weir

Use the foundation pins and Harrowgate surveys to choose a collapse line. Surface evacuation and shoring determine casualties. This can save the Deep Bell or create time for another operation, but it makes the party responsible for where the town breaks.

### Repair the torn royal gutter

Compatible royal metal, three splice links, silver-root fiber, or a living Quiet Beast can hold the gutter. Repair preserves the lower bridge and clean Crown extraction. A brace may last one toll. A full repair restores the route but increases the Bell's ability to continue the old arrangement.

## The Rain Wardens

The four ceramic forms can dive into black water without losing their names because each carries a blank tablet that records only present duty. They are strong gate operators and dangerous literalists.

A Rain Warden accepts a command identifying route, limit, and return. Without a return condition it remains in the lake. Without a limit it keeps opening a gate. Without a route it follows the loudest pressure. A recognized Barracks commander, Iron Choir source, or person marked by black rain can direct one.

If the party destroys a Rain Warden, its blank tablet fills with the nearest person's last fear. That fear may later speak through the Iron Choir or Mouth.

## Queen Avarra and Black Rain

Witness fragments reveal that Avarra first encountered upward rain during the siege that ended her reign. She believed the fault could be commanded because it answered cadence. In truth, it learned recurrence. The more consistently royal orders fed it strain, the more it shaped itself around royal sound.

The Black Rain Cistern was built after her surrender to make that dangerous relationship measurable. The Crown did not create the fault. It made a repeated bargain look like machinery.

## Failure states

- **Foundation lift:** Harrowgate's ridge rises and drops unevenly, destroying buildings and surface routes.
- **Reliquary wash:** black-water memory floods the saints and anyone linked to them.
- **Mouth learning:** the lower sluice opens without terms and the phenomenon begins answering Harrowgate's uncontrolled distress.
- **Bridge shear:** the torn gutter takes part of the lower approach to the Deep Bell.
- **Mineral birth:** an Upward Drowned reaches clear water and hardens into a glass pressure figure that continues toward the surface.

## Treasure and salvage

Black-water pearls form in dead eddies. Each holds compressed vibration and can power a single command, shatter stone, or serve as an offering below. Mineral glass from the Rain Wardens is valuable and useful for pressure gauges. The fault harp can be dismantled, but doing so blinds every later attempt to distinguish low water from low total strain.

## Carry-forward states

Record:

- cistern level and total pressure reading;
- which of the four routes is open, prepared, damaged, or permanently chosen;
- surface vent mouths and evacuation zones;
- Garden shutter preparation;
- Reliquary capacity and contamination;
- Mouth sluice width, duration, and what was sent through it;
- Foundation Weir districts exposed;
- Kesh Arden and Pell-of-the-Last-Dry-Step's condition;
- Rain Warden survival and command; and
- whether the royal gutter and lower bridge are intact.

The cistern makes the ending honest: choose where force goes, name who prepares, and never call it gone.
