# The Quarry Cleft

**Role:** necessary, start  
**Tags:** entrance, vertical, hazard, rescue

A fresh quarry wound offers speed, a stranded porter, and a hunter that follows open light.

## Opening View

The rope has been cut carefully. Its lower knots are gone, but enough remains for a rescuer and not enough for a hurried pursuit. Below, a quarry cage hangs crooked on one rail. Someone coughs in the dark beneath it. The moment a lamp shutter opens, something pale turns its smooth head toward the light.

## Encounter Material

## Situation

The eastern quarry has bitten through the buried house. A night collapse exposed bronze rungs, broken ceramic lines, and shelves narrow enough for one careful worker. The Ember Company used the cleft after retreating from the processional stair. Its rope still hangs, but someone cut the lower knots from beneath: pursuit delayed, rescue still possible.

Three hazards stand between the lip and the Lantern Cistern or Garden of Teeth.

## The descent

**The broken hoist.** A quarry cage hangs twenty feet below the lip, caught on one bent rail. Its brake can be restored, sacrificed as a falling platform, or ignored. Ember Company packs inside contain food, lamp oil, a field surgeon's roll, and a ledger identifying six members who entered by this route.

**The breathing shelf.** Warm air pushes through a horizontal crack in a slow rhythm. Crossing during the exhalation is easy. Crossing against it risks a fall or losing loose equipment into the dark. The rhythm matches, but does not perfectly predict, the Deep Bell's strain cycle.

**The lamp hunter.** A pale, six-limbed quarry predator clings to the shaft below. It has no eyes. It follows heat and lamp smoke, attacking the brightest descending target before withdrawing through maintenance slots. Darkness, cold light, baited oil, noise from another shelf, or direct violence can all answer it. Killing it is useful but not required; driving it toward the Menagerie changes that optional region later.

## The stranded porter

**Jessa Noll**, an Ember Company porter, is wedged in a service niche behind the breathing shelf. Her ankle is broken and her memories arrive out of order. She can still report three reliable facts:

- Captain Maelin Rook opposed cutting the chain;
- Factor Orren Saye took four delvers toward the royal grave after the first uncontrolled movement; and
- engineer Pell Varo descended toward the Ropeworks with tools and two wounded companions.

Jessa has a brass tally stamped with the Ropeworks load sequence. She will surrender it freely if promised extraction. Carrying her up costs time and hands; stabilizing the cleft lets the town volunteers retrieve her later.

## Routes

At the bottom, three ways open:

- an oil-slick service walk enters the Lantern Cistern;
- a root-choked crack descends into the Garden of Teeth; and
- a cage-transfer tunnel leads toward the Menagerie of Quiet Beasts.

The processional side can also be reached by following a narrow inspection ledge toward the Hall of Bent Knees, creating the first upper-dungeon loop.

## Treasure and salvage

The broken cage holds climbing gear, compact rations, three flasks of high-grade ward oil, silver wire, a surgeon's roll, and the Ember Company's sealed base contract with Lady Vey's recovery trust. It authorizes survey and Crown recovery only after the buried house is made safe, requires witnessed transfer, and does not authorize damage to the Bell. It contains no accelerated-possession rider or second payment schedule.

## Consequences

- **Cleft braced:** volunteers can evacuate wounded and carry modest salvage without advancing the tremor clock.
- **Hoist restored:** later travel between surface and cistern is fast, but the mechanism's noise draws attention.
- **Lamp hunter loose:** it may appear in any unlit upper encounter or return to the Menagerie.
- **Jessa abandoned:** the next tremor worsens her condition and damages trust with surviving delvers.
- **Base contract exposed:** Lady Vey must answer for the secret expedition, while the missing rider and second schedule narrow the later escalation to Coss, Rhun, Orren, or evidence still below.
