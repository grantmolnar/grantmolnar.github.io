# The Lantern Cistern

**Role:** necessary  
**Tags:** water, light, hazard, resource

Four lamp boughs divide safety, memory, and scarce oil above black water full of pale hands.

## Opening View

The fourth lamp bough is dark. Beneath it, the dead scout under the oil-black water walks the same three steps, turns, sinks, and rises to begin again. Pale hands keep pace below him. Tavia Hest, bound to a valve standard with cloth over her burned eyes, hears your gear and says, “Tell me you brought rope before you tell me you brought fire.”

## Encounter Material

## Situation

The Lantern Cistern is a round chamber built around a basin of warm black water. A copper mast divides above it into four lamp boughs, each carrying hundreds of shallow cups on silver wire. Ward-oil lies across the basin in a skin thin enough to mirror flame and thick enough to conceal the pale things feeding beneath it. Every branch lights a route; every silver wick carries something else away.

The basin was designed to separate oil from the warm water pumped up from the Black Rain Cistern. The lamp-tree then sends both light and mnemonic discharge through silver-wick lines into the rest of the buried house. Since the Water Drum reversed, black water enters in pulses. Each pulse lifts the oil skin, rocks the hanging cups, and threatens to carry fire into the service galleries.

Treat the cistern as a distribution station. A lit bough quiets its routes and carries future toll-memory toward the Choir. A dark bough releases what its wicks hold, frees scavengers along that route, and yields oil for elsewhere.

## First view and immediate pressure

The party arrives while three lamp boughs burn and the fourth gutters. On the outer service ring, **Tavia Hest**, an Ember Company scout, has tied herself to a valve standard. Her eyes are wrapped in oil-soaked cloth after a flare burned them. She extinguished the Mourning Bough to escape a creature that followed the light reflected in her polished buckles. She now believes, incorrectly, that every lamp attracts the things below.

At the center of the basin, a dead scout named **Corven Braid** drifts face-down beneath the oil skin. The lamplighter key is clipped to his belt. Each black-water pulse makes his body stand, walk several steps along the submerged grate, and sink again. A pale shape follows beneath him.

Put five decisions in view:

- recover the lamplighter key or bypass its keyed valves;
- keep Tavia alive through the next water pulse;
- decide whether to relight the Mourning Bough;
- prevent a lamp cascade if the oil skin reaches the lowest cups; and
- choose how much light and oil to preserve for later regions.

## Lamplighter Senn

**Lamplighter Senn** is a Salt Warden dried translucent around a lattice of silver wick. Turn the key—or speak three lamp names in order—and he steps from the mast. He names each branch for the workers, prisoners, petitioners, and Low Choir children whose memories passed through it. His speech is arithmetic: “Processional keeps twelve routes. Mourning keeps Rhea breathing. Deep keeps the gauges honest.” He has kept the account accurately enough to make the word unbearable.

Senn's first obligation is to prevent a basin fire. His second is to keep at least one safe route to every occupied district. His third is to preserve the memory carried by the wicks. Those duties now conflict. He will cooperate with anyone who names what they are willing to leave dark and who will bear responsibility for the people on that route.

Senn knows:

- the four lamp boughs and every region each protects;
- how to release stored memory without setting the oil alight;
- that the silver wick is carrying Low Choir memory toward the Iron Tongues;
- that the Reliquary's copper saints share an air manifold with the cistern bells;
- that the Deep Bough was dimmed before the Ember Company arrived; and
- that one branch can be kept lit for a full tremor interval with one portable measure of ward-oil.

He does not know why the Deep Bough was dimmed. That order came from the Salt Barracks, not the lamplighters.

## The four lamp boughs

Track each bough as one regional state; ignore individual cups unless a scene makes one matter.

### The Processional Bough

Feeds the Hall of Bent Knees, Ropeworks, and Chain Scriptorium. While lit, load ghosts in those regions remain slow and ceremonial, and the main upper routes are easy to read. Darkening it yields one portable oil measure and releases memories of petitioners, condemned workers, and the Ember Company's first passage.

### The Ossuary Bough

Feeds the Salt Barracks, Garden of Teeth, and Menagerie handling ways. While lit, the Garden's bone moths remain in their beds and Warden recognition marks remain visible. In darkness, root routes become easier to follow by sound, but purpose-bred scavengers leave their stalls. Darkening it yields one portable oil measure.

### The Mourning Bough

Feeds the Reliquary Under Water, Feast of Empty Chairs, and Inverted Chapel. It begins extinguished. While lit, copper saint mouths glow beneath the water and Low Choir mourning stairs remain protected. Relighting it suppresses drowned load ghosts but resumes carrying memories from the inhabited Feast Hall toward the Iron Tongues.

### The Deep Bough

Feeds the Counterweight Wells, Choir of Iron Tongues, Black Rain Cistern, and the upper control walks of the Deep Bell. It begins lit only at half strength. Full light makes deep mechanisms legible and suppresses fault-born scavengers. Darkness conceals movement from the Iron Choir and prevents new memory from reaching it, but the lower routes become substantially more dangerous. Bringing it to full strength costs one portable oil measure; darkening it yields one.

## Ward-oil economy

There are four portable oil measures available when the room is first secured:

- one in Senn's emergency cabinet;
- one in Corven Braid's sealed survey can;
- one trapped in the gutter beneath the Processional Bough; and
- one recoverable by skimming the basin without contaminating it with black water.

In addition, darkening the Processional, Ossuary, or Deep Bough yields one measure. The extinguished Mourning Bough has already been drained by Tavia.

One measure can:

- keep one bough operating through the next tremor-clock advance;
- light a portable ward lamp for one expedition interval;
- make two weapons or shields repellent to pale scavengers for one encounter;
- establish a safe camp in a defensible room, provided smoke can escape; or
- prime one Reliquary saint, Chapel station, or Iron Tongue mechanism that has lost its local supply.

Oil mixed with black water burns hot and almost smokeless for a few breaths, then produces silver vapor carrying involuntary memories. It is an effective barrier or emergency purge, but anyone breathing the vapor experiences a memory that belongs to someone below. Record whose memory was taken and whether it is later returned.

## The pale scavengers

The shapes beneath the oil are **wick-eaters**, soft white creatures with lampblack mouths and hands like drowned paper. They feed on the memory residue that condenses around active silver wick. Direct ward-light pins them below the water, but reflected or portable light draws their attention through dark spaces.

Move them toward whatever the party's light plan makes newly edible.

- In full light, they remain beneath the grates and can be observed or trapped.
- Under selective light, they migrate toward the boundary between lit and dark routes.
- In complete darkness, they climb out and seek warm memories: names spoken repeatedly, written journals, ancestral relics, and people recently affected by a toll.
- Released memory can distract, feed, or even briefly communicate through them.
- Black-water fire destroys them locally but risks a lamp cascade.

A wick-eater's first wound is mnemonic: it takes why the victim came. Continued contact strips names, loyalties, and practiced routes.

## Operating the cistern

### Recover the key

Corven's body can be reached by swimming under the oil skin, hooking him from the service ring, lowering a Ropeworks line, walking the submerged grate between pulses, or luring the following wick-eater away with released memory. Cutting the key free without recovering the body is faster. Bringing Corven out gives Tavia a reason to cooperate and provides an intact route ledger in his boot.

### Bypass the key

The valves can be forced, picked through the mast, commanded by a recognized Warden, or operated from the Ropeworks Water Drum. A bypass risks opening two bough feeds at once, causing a flare cascade unless someone controls the cup chains.

### Restore full light

Relight the Mourning Bough and strengthen the Deep Bough. This costs two portable oil measures. Upper and middle routes become safer, drowned load ghosts recede, and the Iron Tongues receive every new mnemonic discharge. Senn prefers this until confronted with what the Low Choir pays.

### Establish selective light

Choose which boughs remain lit. Selective light gains oil and route control while concentrating wick-eaters at lit-dark boundaries. Mark both lit routes and newly dark routes.

### Release stored memory

Senn can pinch a bough's feed, cool its cups, and draw the silver wicks through a ceramic comb. The bough goes dark, and everyone nearby hears names, work songs, arguments, and final thoughts. People whose memories are present recover them if they are close enough or later receive the combed wick. The released memories can expose routes, Ember Company actions, or Low Choir identities. The operation takes time and attracts wick-eaters without causing a fire.

### Burn the basin clean

Opening the black-water pulse while dropping selected cups creates a brief sheet of silver flame. It destroys wick-eaters, sterilizes contaminated oil, and can deny pursuit through one gallery. It also consumes two oil measures, fills adjacent rooms with memory vapor, and risks setting an entire bough beyond control.

### Reverse or isolate the separator

The local separator can be locked against the global water state, allowing the Reliquary to change level without immediately flooding the cistern. This requires the key, Senn, or coordinated work from the Ropeworks. A rough repair holds until the next tremor advance; a proper repair by Pell or a Warden crew is durable.

## Tavia Hest

Tavia is observant, ashamed, and still useful. Her sight returns gradually if the burned cloth is changed and her face washed with clean water. She can identify Ember route chalk by touch, guide the party to the Reliquary water lift, and testify that Orren ordered Corven to carry a second contract sealed by Chief Factor Halren Coss.

She wants Corven recovered and the Deep Bough darkened so Orren cannot use the lower ward marks. She will not knowingly condemn the wounded company members in the Ropeworks. If rescued and treated honestly, she can range ahead through dark routes without losing direction and later help extract survivors.

## Routes

- a rung ladder behind the Processional feed reaches the Ropeworks;
- the quarry service tunnel enters above the emergency cabinet;
- a petitioner's oil pipe reaches the Hall of Bent Knees;
- the Ossuary drain passes into the Garden of Teeth;
- the Mourning conduit and bell line descend to the Reliquary Under Water; and
- a narrow maintenance throat follows the Deep Bough toward the Black Rain Cistern.

The Black Rain throat is passable only when the separator is isolated or the party accepts a hot-water surge. It is an early deep route, not a safe shortcut.

## Clues and useful discoveries

Searches, operations, and conversations here can reveal:

- the bell's systems exchange water, light, memory, and burden rather than operating independently;
- ward-light suppresses scavengers while carrying stolen memory into the Iron Choir;
- the Reliquary can be entered and ventilated through its saint manifold;
- the Garden drain remains usable even when its upper door is barred;
- the Deep Bough was deliberately weakened on Warden orders; and
- Corven's ledger marks a fault-line maintenance route into the Black Rain Cistern.

## Outcomes

Record:

- which boughs are lit, dark, failing, or burning;
- how many portable oil measures remain;
- whether stored memories were released and to whom;
- whether Senn remains at his post, joins the party, or is destroyed;
- the fate and disposition of Tavia Hest;
- whether Corven's body, key, ledger, and second contract were recovered;
- whether the local separator is synchronized, isolated, or ruptured; and
- where the wick-eaters migrate.

Light here always has a recipient and a payer. Make both visible before the party carries its oil deeper.
