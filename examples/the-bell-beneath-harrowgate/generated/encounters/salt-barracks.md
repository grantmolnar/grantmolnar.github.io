# The Salt Barracks

**Role:** necessary  
**Tags:** faction, wardens, barracks, social

Twenty-three Wardens dispute command while two empty shells patrol and their buried prefect taps below.

## Opening View

One tap comes from beneath the empty command chair. A pause. Two taps. The white-armored sentinels do not turn toward it. “Name. Burden. Authority,” they say to you instead. On the stone bunks behind them, twenty-three preserved Wardens sit in three ranks, each rank watching the others for the first sign of obedience.

## Encounter Material

## Situation

The Salt Barracks is a white vault arranged around an empty command chair. Twenty-three Wardens retain judgment here; twenty follow one of three command positions, while Heth Brine and two attendants keep the salt room. Two more shells patrol without judgment, repeating the last route written before their tablets vanished. Most of the others sit still until duty, alarm, or contradiction gives them reason to wake.

The Wardens are not ghosts wearing armor. Each is a maintained person assembled from three continuities:

- a **salt-cured shell**, which preserves bodily habit, sensation, and emotional reflex;
- a **memory tablet** fitted behind the breastbone, which carries a name, sworn duties, selected memories, and the language by which the buried house recognizes an operator; and
- a **muster of witnesses**, living or preserved people who can say that the shell and tablet still belong to the same continuing person.

A Warden remains whole only while shell, tablet, and witnesses still agree. A shell can march and fight but cannot weigh exceptions. A tablet in brine recites records but cannot decide what its remembered feelings mean. A transferred Warden may function without witnesses, yet the command chains mark that person disputed. The safeguard against theft also gives every faction a different way to deny personhood.

The current crisis began before the Ember Company arrived. **Prefect Ardel Quoin**, last undisputed commander of the garrison, ordered an inquiry into whether the Wardens’ sentence had ended. During that inquiry his command tablet disappeared, his shell was sealed beneath the empty chair, and three incompatible duty boards appeared. Each faction accuses another of removing him. In truth, Garden keepers hid the tablet at Quoin’s own request after he learned that the Deep Bell’s continuance order included a lawful release clause. The Garden has since grown through the cracked tablet, obscuring several years of memory and making its testimony incomplete.

Orren Saye exploited the dispute. He passed the Barracks under a royal claimant’s inspection mark, promised the literal-order Wardens that the Crown of Measure would be restored to a lawful line, and sent one sealed order deeper. The inner gate is therefore open to his party, closed against most others, and prepared to report any new command claim to the Wells and Iron Choir.

## First contact

Entrances from the Hall, Ropeworks, and Garden display different credentials. Reception depends on what the party carries.

- A recognized petition mark from the Hall earns formal speech and one hearing before weapons are drawn.
- A Warden law mark, a recovered salt tablet, or Odran Greve’s maintenance testimony earns technical standing.
- Low Choir guides are treated as protected workers by Continuance, emancipated citizens by Release, and assigned dependents by the literal-order faction.
- Lady Vey’s seal, Orren’s contract, or a visible crown component causes the literalists to form ranks around the command chair.
- An openly damaged Warden tablet causes every faction to stop the scene and demand custody.

Without recognized standing, two blank sentinels approach. They ask three questions in the old order: **Name. Burden. Authority.** They accept literal answers, but they cannot interpret jokes, aliases, changed obligations, or divided authority. A party can answer, retreat, bypass them through the bunks, or let another Warden interrupt. Violence awakens the room one rank at a time rather than producing an immediate mass battle.

## The chamber

### The four ranks

Each rank once served a different part of the machine.

- **Gate Rank** holds petition shields, hooked spears, and route seals for the Hall and Feast service hall.
- **Balance Rank** carries short winches, plumb weights, and muster harnesses used in the Counterweight Wells.
- **Mourning Rank** keeps salt veils and memory bowls for toll casualties. Several of its bunks are empty because those Wardens accepted dissolution during earlier crises.
- **Deep Rank** bears blackened armor and tongue-shaped command keys for the Iron Choir. Three have obeyed Orren’s sealed order and already gone below.

Searching a bunk is not ordinary looting. The equipment is inventoried to the person sleeping beside it. A Warden ally can loan tools, grant salvage from an empty rank, or witness lawful requisition. Taking gear secretly marks the bearer as a grave robber to Barracks mechanisms and to any Warden who later recognizes the item.

### The empty command chair

The chair is carved from one block of salt-veined granite. A narrow cavity beneath it contains Quoin’s shell, still capable of reflex and feeling but unable to identify itself. The shell taps three rhythms against the stone: muster, dissent, and Garden water. Anyone who listens without treating the tapping as an attack can learn that Quoin consented to the tablet’s removal.

The chair recognizes command through three independent forms rather than one magic token:

1. a command tablet or lawful temporary tablet;
2. the witnessed assent of at least one functioning Warden from two ranks; and
3. acceptance of a defined duty that the buried house can test.

A claimant with all three can issue durable orders. Two are enough for a local emergency command lasting until the next toll. One permits speech through the chair but no obedience. This allows proof, coalition, personal sacrifice, or force to matter without making Quoin’s damaged tablet the only path forward.

### The salt room

Behind the Mourning Rank lies a workshop of brine troughs, blank tablets, spare salt ribs, and three unfinished relief shells. **Quartermaster Heth Brine**, whose left arm belongs to a former Gate Warden, tends the room. Heth is not neutral, but refuses to let factional advantage determine whether a damaged person receives care.

Heth can teach or perform the principal tablet operations:

- **Read:** place a tablet in cool brine and let it speak surviving inscriptions. This reveals records but leaves the shell insensible.
- **Rejoin:** return an intact tablet to its recognized shell before witnesses. A hurried rejoining may scramble recent sequence but normally restores judgment.
- **Repair:** grow salt across a crack using filtered Garden brine, one recovered memory, and witness testimony. Repair cannot create certainty where no evidence survives. The repaired Warden chooses whether an unknown interval remains blank or is marked with other people’s accounts.
- **Transfer:** fit a tablet into a relief shell. The Warden retains duty and selected memory but gains unfamiliar reflexes and may reject the new body. A living bearer can accept a tablet temporarily, but the command pressure intrudes on sleep, speech, and personal priorities until removed.
- **Release:** dissolve a tablet in clean water after the Warden states a name and relinquishes duty before witnesses. The shell dies. Its memories enter the water and may be returned to families, the Garden, or the Iron Choir.

Heth will not perform transfer or release without the Warden’s consent unless the tablet cannot answer and recognized witnesses agree that an emergency duty was already chosen. That standard is inconvenient in a crisis and intentionally so.

## The three positions

### Sister Vesra and Continuance

**Sister Vesra of the Balance Rank** keeps one hand on a hanging plumb weight and speaks in conditions: if the Crown moves, then this chain takes it; if that chain fails, then these streets fall. Nine Wardens support her, though two are wavering. She believes the first duty is to prevent Harrowgate’s destruction. She will repair, replace, or redistribute the burden, but will not consent to Crown removal while the nearest consequence remains unnamed.

Vesra’s tablet lacks six years. During that interval the first Low Choir petitions were suppressed, and she cannot remember whether she participated. She does not deny the possibility. She insists that guilt she cannot prove should not be used as a convenient reason to abandon people now alive. Returning one of her missing memories may harden her, break her position, or let her offer a more honest compact.

Vesra offers disciplined guides, a Wells crew, law marks, and temporary control of the inner gate. Her price is a declared stabilization plan and a promise not to remove the Crown while the bell remains physically dependent on it.

### Prior Dalca and Release

**Prior Dalca of the Mourning Rank** loses a little salt whenever he closes his hands. Six Wardens support him. He quotes the ending clauses of old orders and leaves the beginnings unstated. The Wardens’ duty, he argues, was a sentence with an end condition, not an eternal identity. Avarra’s burden, the Low Choir’s inherited harm, and the garrison’s preservation have all outlived the authority that imposed them.

Dalca favors the Common Measure, distributed stabilization, or controlled collapse. He will also accept evacuation followed by the machine’s destruction. He refuses any repair that simply restores the old sentence. His personal urgency is real: his tablet is sound, but his shell cannot survive many more tolls, and he wants the right to choose release before duty makes the decision for him.

Dalca offers Garden contacts, the Mourning routes, witnesses for voluntary burden, and Wardens willing to dissolve their tablets into a replacement memory medium. His price is a credible end to inherited service and recognition that release is not desertion.

### Sergeant Cleth Varn and literal obedience

**Sergeant Cleth Varn of the Gate Rank** commands five Wardens in the Barracks and claims the two blank sentinels whenever no higher order is recognized. Three Deep Rank supporters have already gone below under Orren’s seal. Cleth speaks in imperative verbs and refuses consolation. He does not call the old orders just. He asks what preserved soldiers become if they revoke their own authority while keeping their weapons. His board says: hold the gate, protect the chain, surrender command to a lawful royal line.

Orren showed Cleth Lady Vey’s sealed genealogy and base recovery contract without revealing the accelerated-possession rider or the missing safety lines in his extraction copy. Cleth therefore believes Orren’s party is conducting an ugly but lawful restoration. Proof of the rider and abridged sequence weakens this position but does not answer who now possesses authority.

Cleth offers immediate gate discipline, Deep Rank command keys, and a direct pursuit route after Orren. His price is recognition of a lawful commander—Lady Vey, Quoin restored, a valid emergency marshal, or another claimant the chair accepts. If defeated without such a replacement, Cleth’s sealed orders continue traveling below.

## Resolving the command crisis

Secure passage, manageable opposition, and a command relationship the party understands; unanimity is unnecessary. Several settlements work.

### Restore Prefect Quoin

Recover the tablet from the Garden, repair or acknowledge its missing intervals, rejoin it to the shell, and provide two witnesses. Quoin remembers the lawful release clause but not who removed the tablet. Quoin’s first order is a temporary joint command: stabilize immediate danger, permit Crown replacement if accepted, and convene a final muster after the crisis. This is broad but not morally clean. Quoin will not command Wardens to dissolve or surrender the Crown to a private claimant.

### Recognize a faction commander

Vesra or Dalca can take the chair if the party secures assent from another rank and helps define a testable duty. Vesra’s command is strongest for controlled repair and Wells operations. Dalca’s is strongest for replacement, evacuation, and release. Cleth can command if shown a valid royal authority, but his orders privilege Crown restoration and may place Low Choir routes under military control.

### Establish joint command

Two faction leaders can share emergency authority by dividing duties: one responsible for Harrowgate’s physical survival, one for release and affected people. The chair accepts this only if each leader states what the other may forbid. Joint command gains more personnel but requires the party to carry disagreements into the finale rather than settling them here.

### Appoint a guest marshal

A living character may accept a blank temporary tablet and a defined emergency duty before two ranks. This grants local command and access to the lower muster stair. Until released, the character hears every unresolved Barracks order as a physical tug and cannot honestly claim ignorance of Warden casualties. A self-serving or vague duty causes the tablet to crack.

### Conduct a barracks coup

The old muster law permits a nonlethal command challenge in the rectangle between bunks. The challenger must hold the command plumb while opponents try to take it, force a knee, or prove the declared duty contradictory. The room’s machinery shifts shields, opens brine channels, and changes footing according to which rank supports whom. Victory compels local obedience but does not erase dissent. Killing a Warden during the challenge breaks the muster and turns the remaining factions into independent armed groups.

### Break or bypass the gate

The inner gate can be forced with Ropeworks power, cut through after local water isolation, or bypassed through the Garden root door. Progress remains possible. The costs are explicit: warning tongues sound below, hostile Wardens follow on disciplined intervals, and no Barracks crew operates the Wells unless later reconciled. Rejecting the faction scene never blocks progress.

## Missing tablets and blank sentinels

The two blank sentinels are **Ora Pell** and **Maston Grey**. Their tablets lie in the Garden beside Quoin’s. Ora removed hers voluntarily after refusing an order to seize Low Choir children; Maston’s was taken by Cleth’s predecessor to erase testimony. Their shells now execute the same patrol, one by choice and one by theft, and the difference cannot be learned from the shells alone.

Recovering the tablets creates distinct decisions:

- Ora’s tablet asks not to be rejoined. She will testify in brine and then choose release or archival transfer.
- Maston’s tablet demands rejoining and a formal accusation against the old command chain.
- Rejoining either without hearing the tablet gives the party a useful soldier while repeating the Barracks’ central abuse.
- Leaving the shells blank preserves an immediate hazard and an unresolved person.

## Conflict and pressure

If fighting begins, Wardens operate the room rather than merely exchanging attacks.

- Gate Rank closes exits and separates intruders with petition shields.
- Balance Rank changes floor tension and uses harnesses to redistribute carried weight.
- Mourning Rank throws dry salt that draws active memories into visible shapes.
- Deep Rank sounds command tongues that awaken or redirect distant mechanisms.
- Blank sentinels repeat the last successful tactic until someone changes their order, route, or tablet state.

At Shifting or worse, a cracked brine pipe begins dissolving tablets stored in the salt room. At Black Rain, Garden water enters warm and starts unwanted repair growth. At the fourth toll, every Warden loses one recent memory unless protected by ward-light, clean brine, or living witnesses who immediately restate it.

## Resources and routes

A cooperative Barracks can provide:

- up to two disciplined Warden crews for specific operations;
- law marks that open service doors and identify carried equipment as requisitioned;
- three compact muster winches and a counterweight harness;
- Deep Rank command tongues for the Iron Choir;
- salt veils that protect a named memory through one toll;
- blank tablets for emergency command or voluntary transfer; and
- reliable routes to the Feast hall, Garden, Counterweight Wells, and Iron Choir approach.

The lower muster stair descends directly to the Counterweight Wells. The Feast service hall runs behind the Gate Rank. The Garden door opens into the Bitter Bed, where the missing tablets are rooted. A narrow command chase follows speaking tubes toward the Iron Choir; it is fast, exposed, and already marked by Orren’s people.

## Carry-forward states

Record more than “allied” or “hostile.”

- **Command:** Quoin restored, Vesra, Dalca, Cleth, joint command, guest marshal, disputed, or broken.
- **Duty:** continuance, release, royal restoration, divided, or a newly defined emergency duty.
- **Personnel:** crews assigned, dissolved, transferred, damaged, or pursuing the party.
- **Tablets:** Quoin, Ora, Maston, Vesra’s missing years, and any blank temporary tablets.
- **Gate:** cooperative, controlled, forced, bypassed, or alarming the deep works.
- **Promises:** stabilization limits, release rights, recognition of Low Choir standing, Crown custody, and post-crisis muster.

Every command settlement eases one later operation and sharpens one later dispute. Record who reaches the finale able to command, refuse, and remember why.
