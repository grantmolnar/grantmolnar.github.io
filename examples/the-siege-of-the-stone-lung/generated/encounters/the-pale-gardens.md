# The Pale Gardens

**Role:** necessary  
**Tags:** ecology, food, investigation

Poisoned tools and starving rootstock write two different disasters across the city's living filters.

## Opening View

Officials with torches enter the Pale Gardens to burn every bed marked by bitter orange mold. Growers drag translucent filter trays clear while cave moths batter vents the public map calls sealed. Beneath an overturned cart, a Refuge porter clutches a message for Deren Vask; children below faint whenever the Lung draws. Sen Tahl lays two cuttings before the fire: orange growth from a sabotaged tool, colorless root from an old southern bed. One is poisoned. The other is starving. The torch descends toward both.

## Encounter Material

## Two failures in one bed

Several beds have gone translucent. Black cave moths crowd vents the plans call dead. Bitter orange mold appears wherever Host infiltrators handled tools. Two frightened officials bring Sen Tahl opposite orders: burn every marked bed before the mold spreads, or keep harvesting and risk poisoning the city.

The orange mold is recent sabotage and does not match the preserved strain from the old blight. The translucent beds are starving for fresh air. One fire would consume the poison, the harvest, the living filters, and the clearest record of the Lung's failing draw.

## Separating the failures

Compare spore color, root moisture, insect behavior, and timing against the Lung's stroke. Sabotage follows hands, carts, and tool sheds. Airflow damage forms a gradient toward a sealed southern vent absent from the current plans.

Saving the Gardens may require:

- isolating and burning the poisoned beds;
- opening narrow service vents without drawing black breath into the food caverns;
- protecting growers from infiltrators and frightened soldiers; and
- keeping command from feeding filter beds to emergency furnaces.

## Sen Tahl

Sen is patient with ignorance and merciless with waste. She cares less about naming the liar of eighteen years ago than keeping enough living material to read the air tonight. Give her workers and room to act, and the Gardens become a ward-by-ward tracer. Burn the wrong beds, and she still defends the city, but brings neither filters nor reliable readings to the Lung.

## The old rootstock

Several beds descend from stock labeled for a southern exchange gallery. Their rings show that fresh flow stopped abruptly in the mold-blight year. Purge-bed labels and unfinished filter frames show that quarantine was meant to end after the contaminated flow cleared. Someone later scraped the labels and reassigned the beds. Rootstock cannot name the magistrate who made the closure permanent. It can refute the public story that Lorn's caverns failed by slow exhaustion.

## Refuge ties

Ration porters from the Refuge Galleries are trapped when quarantine closes the terraces. One carries a plea from Deren Vask, an old vent mason who recognizes the southern marks. Another reports children fainting below during the same stroke that drives moths against the sealed vents. Gardens and Refuge are breathing one failure at different depths.

## If neglected

Officials burn the suspect terraces. The sabotage dies with them. Kest Mourne loses weeks of food, its best filters, and the spores needed to judge a controlled reopening. Ration cuts begin in the Refuge before the smoke clears.
