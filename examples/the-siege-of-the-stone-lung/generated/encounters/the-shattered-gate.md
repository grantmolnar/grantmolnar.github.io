# The Shattered Gate

**Role:** necessary  
**Tags:** battle, fortification, siege

A tower splits, six defenders signal through dust, and the Host refuses a breach it could take.

## Opening View

The city's last straight beam hangs halfway into the right tower when the crack widens and traps the brace crew above it. Six defenders flash a mirror through the dust. Beneath the stair, an unexploded resonator rises in pitch with the Stone Lung. Outside, Host soldiers plant two wedges, then withdraw from a breach wide enough to enter. Brann's reserve starts toward the trapped crew; his runner points instead toward the lost gallery. The tower answers both orders by dropping its first block.

## Encounter Material

## The breach

The outer doors still stand. The right tower does not. A crack runs from parapet to foundation; the upper stair is blocked, and each stroke of the Stone Lung sifts dust from the widening seam. Captain Brann Sile has shortened the line twice. One more major assault breaks it unless the tower is stabilized, the northern gallery recovered, or the defenders given room to withdraw without rout.

The Host attacks with unnerving discipline. Shield companies advance during the Lung's exhalation, plant resonant wedges, and fall back before the defenders can trap them. Twice they have refused an open way into the city. The wall is holding an army's attention while work below proceeds on schedule.

## Immediate problems

Choose the pressure that best matches current play.

- **The split tower:** A brace crew is trapped above the crack with an unexploded resonator lodged beneath it. Stabilization preserves the outer line. A fast rescue saves the crew and may cost Brann the tower.
- **The lost gallery:** Six defenders and a signal mirror are stranded behind a collapsed stair. Reaching them reveals what the Host watches between assaults.
- **The false withdrawal:** The Host leaves wounded soldiers and a broken signal slate within bowshot. The bait covers sapper work beneath the approach. Careful recovery gains prisoners and orders; pursuit spends the Gate's reserve.

No victory over the Host is required here. Save soldiers, steal minutes, and learn why the enemy will not take what the wall appears to offer.

## Brann's test

Every proposal must name what leaves Brann's wall, what that absence buys, and whether the line survives until the next assault. He accepts rescue, prediction, and controlled withdrawal as military work. Bring the assault schedule and he will abandon stone before people.

## Prisoners

A captured lower-road scout, Nara Ves, carries Venn's standing order to spare public air shafts. Her sapper escort carries Bas's later instruction to ignore that limit after the third hammer signal. Both are tired, frightened, and incomplete witnesses. Keep them alive and compare their orders. Neither knows the whole operation; together they reveal a command beginning to split.

## Defensive outcomes

A clean success delays the final assault and frees a small reserve. A costly success keeps the Gate but fixes every survivor in place. On failure, the defense falls back through two inner turns, enemy skirmishers enter the sector ring, and the Stone Lung begins with less time.
