# The Lantern Court

**Role:** necessary, start  
**Tags:** command, social, siege

Six civic lanterns breathe above a command floor where every allocation saves one sector by exposing another.

## Opening View

Before Ilyra Dain finishes reading the six-lantern commission, a clerk scrapes three names from the Shattered Gate board and writes nine beneath them. The Gardens lantern gutters blue. A cistern runner sets an oily flask beside a ringing shell fragment and the city's last unassigned iron. Six officers reach for the same spears, beams, hoods, and demolition cart. Ilyra hands over the copied writ and turns every sector token to contested. The Stone Lung exhales; one token must turn toward help before the flames steady.

## Encounter Material

## Arrival

On the nineteenth morning, the Pale Gardens lantern gutters blue just as a runner from the Shattered Gate asks for every uncommitted spear. Casualty clerks are still scraping yesterday's names from the boards. First Warden Ilyra Dain lays six copies of an emergency writ across the sector map and asks the company to carry decisions where ordinary command cannot.

## The six-lantern commission

Choose why this local, foreign, or mixed company can cross lines that stop ordinary officers: local defenders trusted in several wards, specialists trapped inside the encirclement, envoys whose road home has closed, mercenaries whose contract has become a civic obligation, or a mixed company already proven under pressure. The writ names no profession, company title, or prescribed route.

Its copies authorize cross-sector movement, bounded requisitions, emergency reallocations, prisoner exchanges, evacuations, and temporary parleys. They do not authorize surrender, punishment of civilians, permanent transfer of the Lung, or technical work a competent crew judges impossible. A sector leader may demand reasons and remains answerable for carrying the order out. The commission lets a verified fact cross the city, but it works only when the company brings a workable allocation with it; parchment cannot supply missing crews, roads, or hours.

## Command under pressure

Nothing here happens in private. Messengers shoulder past one another. Casualty boards are scraped and rewritten before the families of the dead. Every allocation has witnesses and losers. Speaker Edda Marr urges silence around the old wards. Gate officers demand reserves. Ventwright Tamar Ohn asks for machinery already promised to military repair. Refuge stewards ask how much air command can guarantee; no officer gives them a number.

On each return, bring two or three demands to the same table. A useful first collision is:

- Captain Brann Sile asks for the iron reserve before the Lung's next exhalation.
- The Cinder Foundry asks whether its last straight beams belong at the Gate or beneath a damaged ventilation frame.
- The Refuge Galleries report that a former vent mason is telling the crowd Kest Mourne stole another settlement's air.

An allocation changes conditions; it does not close a location. Soldiers sent to the Gate reduce casualties there and leave the cistern crews exposed. The demolition cart can open the Countermine or remove a live shell from the Foundry, not both. Name the people who receive the order and the people who lose it.

## The siege ledger

Mark each sector **contested**, **held**, or **breached**. A clean resolution holds the sector and preserves its full finale asset. A costly resolution holds it with a diminished asset. Abandonment or decisive failure breaches it and applies the encounter's **If neglected** result.

Whenever the commission returns to the Court—or after two field sectors without a return—report one benefit from a held sector and one worsening elsewhere. The Gate releases a reserve. The Foundry sends braces. Refuge stewards form an evacuation crew. Elsewhere, a shell buries the tuning benches, a ration cut starts a crush, or the lower sluice gives way. While the commission works one front, the others keep moving.

Early reconnaissance at the Stone Lung does not by itself begin Heartstrike. Start the three-stroke finale when Bas detects an attempt to seize the charge gallery, sever the main firing cable, or move the southern valves; when his plan has been exposed and another major opportunity lets him act; or when three breached sectors complete his assault route. A peripheral relay cut, hidden watch, or study of the crown may delay or mislead him instead. Give a full-cycle warning. Do not begin before an approach to the Lung has been established, and never resolve Heartstrike offscreen. Table time spent on careful work is not siege time unless the fiction changes.

## Ilyra at the table

Ilyra will reverse a field order when the company brings both evidence and a sequence people can execute. She will not trade Kest Mourne for absolution, and she will not let the siege excuse a lie that prevents a viable remedy. She can bind a temporary operational compact with Venn for this battle; permanent joint control still requires public records, civil process, and recognized Lorn witnesses. Name the reserve, crew, road, and hour a proposal requires; accusation by itself moves nothing.

## Edda's sealed answer

Six weeks ago Venn sent an original Lorn conduit plate and demanded joint inspection after Lorn's last reliable chimney failed. Edda called it enemy fabrication, sealed it beside the old minutes, and urged refusal. She does not know what has accumulated behind the southern wall. A private accusation makes her close ranks because it exposes an officer without changing any standing order.

## How Kest Mourne admits a fact

Do not resolve the city's culpability through remorse. Choose the witnesses. A finding becomes command when it is copied into all six sector ledgers, read down the command tubes, used to correct the wall map, and attached to an order that changes work already costing lives. A verified plate joined to a survivable command can force Edda to release the minutes and let six sectors act. Anything less can later be dismissed as rumor.

## Using the Court

Bring evidence here as command work: a damaged object that needs judgment, a field report that contradicts the map, or a messenger spending a final sentence on one usable fact. Each verified fact should release troops, open records, order an evacuation, or make a temporary parley executable. The Court changes the city's direction; it is not a recurring briefing room.

## If neglected

The Court remains standing and ceases to command. Rumor outruns the runners. Sector officers hoard crews and material. Edda moves the sealed minutes. A later return must restore trust or bring enough visible authority to make six defenses act as one city again.
