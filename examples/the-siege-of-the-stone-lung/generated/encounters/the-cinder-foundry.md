# The Cinder Foundry

**Role:** necessary  
**Tags:** engineering, hazard, labor

A resonant shell waits in a cold furnace while the city's last skilled shift demands one named purpose.

## Opening View

Mera Coil stands between a gate officer and the east furnace's ignition lever. Inside the cold furnace, a buried shell sings deeper with every stroke of the Stone Lung; nine chalk circles mark the workers already killed obeying orders around it. The last shift has divided one stock of iron into straight beams, filter frames, counterweight blanks, and an unfinished cutting rig. Tamar Ohn lowers a tuning fork. It trembles before touching stone. ‘Name the work,’ Mera says, while the officer reaches again for the lever.

## Encounter Material

## Heat and cadence

Every few minutes, the unexploded shell buried in the east furnace answers the Stone Lung with a low note. Foundry Master Mera Coil has killed the furnace and cleared half the hall. Gate officers arrive with an order to relight it.

Ventwright Tamar Ohn warns that heat or one careless hammer blow may tune the red chimney to the shell. Nine workers have already died obeying siege orders. Mera will not spend a tenth crew to save an officer from waiting.

## The live shell

The shell remembers vibration. Ceramic ribs collect the Lung's cycle and return it through the surrounding stone. It can be cooled and extracted, detuned where it lies, or buried with the east furnace. Intact, it gives the cleanest frequency model. Broken, it still yields maker's marks, tolerances, and the higher setting Bas cut beyond Venn's limit.

## Competing production

After the shell is safe, one full shift of skilled labor remains before the next assault. The shift can complete one program or divide itself among reduced packages already begun:

- radial braces for the Shattered Gate;
- a cutting frame and tuned wedges for Heartstrike;
- counterweights for retuning the Stone Lung; or
- filter housings for the Pale Gardens and Refuge Galleries.

Record what is full, what is diminished, and what never leaves the floor. Imported labor, a preserved east furnace, or new iron can enlarge the shift. Without one of those, each additional order consumes another asset; it does not create more work from the same hands.

## Tamar Ohn

Tamar knows the Lung as a machine sustained by repairs, substitutions, and habits absent from every diagram. It is drawing too hard through the northern branches, yet the public plans show no southern branch to reopen. Her first answer is defensive: brace the crown, choke the unknown leak, save Kest Mourne. Old diagrams joined to measured pressure can show her a wider balance still exists.

## Mera Coil

Mera's loyalty belongs to the people who put their hands near the metal. Respect it, and she becomes the forewoman who can hold several lethal stations to one cadence. Override her, and equipment may arrive sooner, but no disciplined crew follows it below.

## If neglected

The shell splits the east furnace. The Foundry survives, but the tuning benches disappear under stone and the workers scatter. Crude repair remains possible. Every precise task at the Stone Lung becomes slower, louder, and more dangerous.
