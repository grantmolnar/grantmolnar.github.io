# The Cisterns of Black Breath

**Role:** necessary  
**Tags:** exploration, hazard, utilities

Three workers hammer beneath an oily waterline where old valves expose the lie in the city's maps.

## Opening View

Three breathing hoods lie beside the Black Cisterns' inspection arch. Four people are needed below. The rescue rope has gone slack, but a trapped worker still hammers from behind the barred sluice: pressure, pause, pressure, silence. Oily water washes over valve numbers hidden beneath fresh lime. Cala Orm points from the nearest bypass to the channel feeding the Refuge Galleries. Opening it may save the crew. The Stone Lung draws, a sweet mineral breath crosses the pool, and the worker misses the next beat.

## Encounter Material

## Below the waterline

Three workers are missing beyond a barred inspection arch, and an oily skin has appeared in water still feeding Kest Mourne's public pumps. Officials call it enemy poison. The smell says otherwise: mineral water, wet stone, and the faint sweetness of trapped gas.

Methane-rich black breath seeps through fractures around the southern seal. Flame can ignite it. Heavy armor and haste can carry a rescuer into air too thin for consciousness. Warn before killing: lamps extinguish in sequence, insects lie dead along a sharp boundary, and a breathing hood rests where someone understood the danger too late.

## The trapped crew

The workers closed a sluice to keep the upper supply clean. One remains conscious behind the gate and reports pressure through measured hammer taps. Reaching them preserves the city's water and a full cycle of readings. Opening the wrong bypass saves them quickly and sends black water toward the Refuge.

## The false map

Current plans show natural stone south of the fourth pool. Beneath the limewash lie old valve numbers and the chisel lines of deliberate masonry. A brass inspection plate survives in the drain. Its reverse shows one exchange system joining the Cisterns, Stone Lung, and Lorn.

The wall also explains why demolition means fire. Pressure has packed black breath behind the seal. Heartstrike will mix it with the Lung's hot release and carry ignition through both branches.

## Water Reeve Cala Orm

Cala has been accused of poisoning the city through neglect. Restoring the supply matters more to her than clearing her name. She knows the lower valves, owns several working breathing hoods, and can turn a disused overflow into a gas vent with labor and an empty gallery above. Save her crew, and she reaches the Lung as an operator. Take the map and leave them, and no one who trusts the readings remains at the controls.

## The lower-road grate

A maintenance channel behind the fourth pool opens onto an old road occupied by Host sappers. Air and water move through it in opposite phases. Follow the draft to enter the Countermine from below and hear enemy digging aimed at the Stone Lung rather than the wall.

## If neglected

The lower sluice fails. Kest Mourne keeps part of its water and loses the breathing hoods, the pressure record, and the safest vent for black breath. Refuge illness worsens. Heartstrike arms against a machine no one has measured.
