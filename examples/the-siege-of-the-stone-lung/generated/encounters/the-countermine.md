# The Countermine

**Role:** necessary  
**Tags:** infiltration, military, negotiation

A collapsed lower road traps enemies together around the live cable and divided orders behind Heartstrike.

## Opening View

The breakthrough drops defenders, Host sappers, and half the Countermine roof into one dust cloud. Badges vanish. Kest Mourne's miners hold a trapped sapper at blade point while enemy hands lift stone from a buried defender. Between them, a braided cable pulses toward the Stone Lung; a field tablet flashes the next hammer signal. Mael Rusk calls for a roof truce. Farther down the Host line, someone orders the cable kept live. The next pulse travels under every raised weapon.

## Encounter Material

## Breakthrough

Kest Mourne's miners dig toward enemy hammers and break into an older lower road omitted from every current map. The first collapse traps defenders and Host sappers on opposite sides of a resonance cable running toward the Stone Lung. Dust erases uniforms. The next hammer signal will bring the ceiling down on everyone left in the chamber.

Begin with rescue, violence, or a shouted roof truce. Enemies who have spent nineteen days killing one another must clear the same fall, cut the same cable, exchange prisoners, or obey orders neither side fully trusts.

## The Heartstrike line

Ceramic insulators carry a braided cable deeper into the city. Its pulse matches the shells at the wall. A field tablet reduces the operation to three movements:

1. surface assaults fix Kest Mourne's reserves at the Gate and inner turns;
2. sappers expose the southern seal and place the charge; and
3. Heartstrike fractures the seal during the Lung's release.

The yield table predicts an ignition large enough to burn Kest Mourne and Lorn. Bas has marked it acceptable.

## Venn's sappers

Sap Captain Mael Rusk carries Venn's sealed order: seize the Lung and open the southern branch under control, but do not fracture it while black breath remains unvented. Bas's later valid-looking countermand cites the Host's technical articles, conceals the true yield, and tells the charge crews that delay condemns the families waiting in Lorn. Rusk holds two orders from different chains, both valid-looking, not a simple invitation to betray his marshal.

Kind words do not move him. He needs proof that Venn's controlled objective remains possible, a protected way for his crews to obey her over Bas, and assurance that Kest Mourne will not use the pause to reseal the branch. Rescue beneath the collapse, a prisoner exchange, an authenticated revocation, or a measured retuning sequence can become duty he is able to defend.

## Ways through

The commission may:

- cut the exposed cable, delaying Heartstrike and warning Bas;
- follow it covertly to the charge gallery;
- use Rusk's slate to reach Venn;
- collapse the surface branch and force a direct battle; or
- send a false completion signal while defenders reposition.

Each road gains access by spending something. Here the siege's military, technical, and political lines occupy the same narrow tunnel.

## Gate intelligence

A second tablet gives the Host's complete assault schedule, including feints and reserve timings at the Shattered Gate. Brann can save the outer line with it even if no truce survives below.

## If neglected

Bas's crews complete the charge uncontested. Venn learns its true yield only after Heartstrike begins and must oppose her own engineer under fire. The Foundry and memorial routes remain open, but prepared sappers hold the charge gallery.
