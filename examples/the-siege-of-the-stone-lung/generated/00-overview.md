## Synopsis

On the nineteenth day of the siege, Kest Mourne's right gate tower splits from parapet to foundation and the civic lantern above the Pale Gardens burns blue. The Host of the Lower Road has closed every caravan route and driven sappers beneath the walls. First Warden Ilyra Dain answers with a temporary six-lantern commission: cross the city's defensive sectors, spend its last reserves where they can still matter, and carry field truth back into command before the Stone Lung reaches its next crisis.

## Premise

An Underdark city is failing by sectors while an enemy host times its assaults to the strokes of the ancient engine that gives the city air. A local, foreign, or mixed company receives bounded authority across six fronts. It must buy military time, distinguish sabotage from structural failure, uncover the old theft beneath the siege, and decide what survival obliges Kest Mourne to restore.

## Explanation

The Host came for the Stone Lung, not Kest Mourne's streets. Eighteen years ago, a lethal mold blight forced Kest Mourne to seal the engine's southern branch, cutting the settlement of Lorn away from most of its fresh-air exchange. The quarantine was justified; what followed was not. The blight died, the promised purge and filter works were abandoned, and the council made the closure permanent while falsifying the conduit maps. Lorn survived on auxiliary chimneys until its last reliable shaft collapsed six weeks ago. Marshal Sera Venn sent the original Lorn conduit plate and demanded a joint inspection. Kest Mourne called it a forgery and sealed it beside the old minutes. Venn now intends to seize the Lung and compel a controlled reopening. Her chief engineer, Korr Bas, holds wartime technical command over the Host's resonance corps once its crews reach their work. Certain that delay is another death sentence for Lorn, he has hidden a larger yield and issued Heartstrike orders beyond Venn's limits. Methane-rich black breath has gathered behind the southern masonry; the charge will ignite it and drive flame through the deep galleries of both cities. The commission must keep Kest Mourne alive long enough to expose the old decision, separate Venn's enforceable claim from Bas's catastrophe, and settle whether the Lung will be closed, sacrificed, or made to breathe for both settlements again.
