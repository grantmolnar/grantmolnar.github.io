## Reference Material

## The city measured in minutes

The Shattered Gate is Kest Mourne's principal outer approach. Its doors, paired towers, northern gallery, inner turns, stairs, signal positions, and upper-city road make one defensive system. Holding or yielding any part changes how long soldiers, engineers, wounded, and orders can still move through the city.

## A wall that protects other work

The Gate matters beyond the stone it occupies. Braces and iron come from the Foundry. Assault timings can arrive from captured lower-road records. A reserve held here may instead escort a technical crew or contest the Stone Lung. The Gate's military value is therefore time, controlled withdrawal, and a road kept usable for another obligation.

## The map cannot hold the breach

The record fixes doors, towers, galleries, turns, stairs, signals, and the upper road. It does not say which tower stands, where defenders have withdrawn, whether prisoners were taken, or how much reserve remains. Damage, crews, casualties, enemy access, signals, and the defensive outcome belong to the current siege.
