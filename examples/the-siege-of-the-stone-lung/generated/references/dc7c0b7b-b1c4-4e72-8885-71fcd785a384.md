# The Six-Lantern Commission

**Reference ID:** `dc7c0b7b-b1c4-4e72-8885-71fcd785a384`

## Summary

Kest Mourne's bounded emergency writ, allowing one company to carry verified facts and finite resources across sector boundaries without inheriting surrender, punishment, permanent-control, or impossible technical powers.

## Reference Material

## Six flames on one copied writ

Ordinary authority in Kest Mourne stops at sector boundaries. The six-lantern commission lets one proven company cross those boundaries while the city is under siege. Copies go to all six principal sectors so a field decision can be recognized without pretending the company has replaced the officers, crews, and civil bodies already there.

## What crosses a sector line

The writ permits cross-sector movement, bounded requisitions, emergency reallocations, prisoner exchanges, evacuations, and temporary parleys. Sector leaders may demand reasons and remain answerable for execution. A verified fact can therefore release a reserve, redirect material, protect a witness, or make a local truce executable before ordinary council procedure catches up.

## What ink cannot create

The commission does not permit surrender, punishment of civilians, permanent transfer of the Stone Lung, or technical work that competent crews judge impossible. Ink cannot summon soldiers, tools, breathing hoods, witnesses, roads, or cooperation. Who carries a copy, what it has already moved, which officer accepts it, and whether a compact survives are decided in play.
