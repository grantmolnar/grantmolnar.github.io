# The Cinder Foundry

## Summary

Kest Mourne's three-hall forge and repair works, coupling gate braces, pumps, filters, counterweights, tuned tools, skilled labor, and the Foundry gantry into one finite production system.

## Reference Material

## Three halls around a red chimney

The Cinder Foundry descends through three working halls around a chimney of red stone. It casts gate braces, repairs pumps, shapes filter housings, maintains the Stone Lung's counterweights, and keeps the tuning benches used for precise resonance work. The east furnace and upper gantry connect production directly to the city's defense and breathing engine.

## One shift cannot become four

Foundry work is an allocation of skilled hands, heat, iron, benches, and safe cadence. Straight beams can reinforce the Gate, tuned wedges can reach Heartstrike, counterweights can support retuning, and frames can carry living filters. Each program has different tools and tolerances; dividing one shift produces diminished packages rather than additional labor.

## Nothing leaves the floor by description

The Foundry's durable capacity is heat, iron, benches, skilled cadence, and its roads to other sectors. A sheet cannot say whether the furnace is safe, a shell remains buried, benches survived, iron is available, or a crew accepted an order. What is completed, diminished, abandoned, or actually delivered to the Stone Lung is settled in play.
