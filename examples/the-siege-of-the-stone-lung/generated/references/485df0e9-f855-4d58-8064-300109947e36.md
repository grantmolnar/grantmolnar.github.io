## Summary

Commander of the Host of the Lower Road, pursuing enforceable access to the Stone Lung while retaining strategic authority distinct from Korr Bas's narrower technical command.

## Reference Material

## The claim behind the siege

Marshal Sera Venn commands the Host of the Lower Road. Lorn's last reliable auxiliary chimney failed after years of decline under a supposedly temporary southern seal. Venn sent Kest Mourne an original conduit plate and demanded joint inspection; the city rejected it under the authority of its own false map. She chose siege and controlled seizure rather than accept another refusal.

## Access that cannot be withdrawn

Venn's objective is enforceable access to the Stone Lung, not friendship with Kest Mourne and not indiscriminate extermination. She recognizes prisoner exchange and bounded local truces, but a promise made while one city retains every control does not answer Lorn's claim. Public records, divided custody, mixed crews, and physical access can create leverage that survives the emergency.

## Strategic command and its limit

Venn may direct Host units, negotiate ceasefire corridors, exchange prisoners, and authenticate strategic orders or revocations. The Host's wartime articles nevertheless give qualified engineers technical authority once their crews reach assigned work, creating a boundary Korr Bas can exploit. Her current assault, truce, revocation, negotiation, access demand, unit position, and operational cooperation remain live adventure state.
