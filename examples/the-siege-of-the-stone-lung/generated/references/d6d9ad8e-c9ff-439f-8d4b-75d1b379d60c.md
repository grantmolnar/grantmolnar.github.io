# The Original Lorn Conduit Plate

## Summary

An original technical plate from Lorn showing the shared southern exchange system and the old relationship among Lorn, Kest Mourne's lower works, and the Stone Lung.

## Reference Material

## Lines cut before the map was changed

The original Lorn conduit plate depicts a southern exchange joining Lorn, Kest Mourne's lower water and vent works, and the Stone Lung. Its measurements, maker's conventions, valve marks, and branch geometry predate the later civic map that shows natural stone where the southern works should be.

## A demand for inspection, not surrender

Lorn preserved the plate after its auxiliary chimneys began to fail. It supports a demand that both settlements inspect the old controls together and explains why a promise of future air is weaker than physical access, divided custody, and records neither city can quietly erase. The plate does not itself settle permanent control.

## Engraving must meet the stone

The plate can be tested against masonry, valve numbers, rootstock, pressure readings, and surviving score marks. It does not determine who possesses it, whether a copy is authentic, which audience accepts it, what inspection or publication follows, or what order and legal effect result. Its force comes from comparison and use in the current crisis.
