## Summary

## Reference Material

## Six flames, one bounded writ

First Warden Ilyra Dain commands Kest Mourne's defense from the Lantern Court. Her six-lantern commission exists because ordinary command stops at sector boundaries while bombardment, air, food, water, and evacuation do not. The writ permits cross-sector movement, bounded requisitions, emergency reallocations, prisoner exchanges, evacuations, and temporary parleys. It does not permit surrender, punishment of civilians, permanent transfer of the Stone Lung, or a technical order that competent crews judge impossible.

## A fact must become an order

Ilyra inherited false infrastructure records rather than the decision that made them. She will spend lives to hold defensible ground, but she distrusts gestures that only look resolute. A report matters to her when its source can be tested, its cost can be named, and its conclusion can alter work already under way. She can reverse an order without pretending the first order was wise.

## Emergency command is not ownership

Siege law lets Ilyra direct available reserves, order evacuation and exchange, and bind a temporary operational compact. Permanent joint control of the Lung requires civil process, public records, and recognized Lorn witnesses. Her current orders, allocations, disclosure decision, reserve movement, compact, and willingness to parley remain live adventure state.
