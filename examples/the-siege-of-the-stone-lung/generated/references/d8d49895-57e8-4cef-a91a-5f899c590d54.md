## Summary

Kest Mourne's public command floor, where six sector lanterns, casualty boards, wall maps, runners, ledgers, and command tubes turn verified field facts into witnessed citywide orders.

## Reference Material

## A command floor that breathes

The Lantern Court stands beneath six iron lanterns, one for each principal defensive sector. Their flames narrow and brighten with the Stone Lung's cycle. Casualty boards, sector tokens, the wall map, material tallies, and arriving runners make the city's competing needs visible in one public room.

## A fact becomes work

The Court is where evidence enters command. A damaged object needs judgment; a report contradicts the map under the officers' feet; a messenger spends a final sentence on one usable fact. Kest Mourne recognizes a civic correction when it reaches the sector ledgers, travels down the command tubes, alters the wall map, and changes an order already costing lives.

## Six lanterns cannot summon hands

The Court can compare claims and send recognized orders through the city. It cannot make one crew serve two sectors, turn rumor into proof, or restore authority by holding another briefing. The boards, tokens, runners, ledgers, orders, and even the Court's remaining coherence change with the siege.
