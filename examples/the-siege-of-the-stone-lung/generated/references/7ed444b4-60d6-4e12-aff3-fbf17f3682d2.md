# The Host of the Lower Road

## Summary

Lorn's siege army, combining shield companies, sappers, scouts, signal crews, and resonance engineers under strategic command whose wartime articles preserve a narrower technical chain.

## Reference Material

## An army raised for access

The Host of the Lower Road is Lorn's siege force. Shield companies fix defenders at the wall; sappers and scouts work the buried approaches; signal crews connect dispersed commands; resonance engineers make stone itself part of the assault. Its strategic objective is enforceable access to the Stone Lung rather than possession of every street in Kest Mourne.

## Two chains inside one command

Marshal authority sets the war aim, unit movement, exchanges, and strategic limits. The Host's wartime articles give qualified engineers bounded control over calibration and firing procedure after their crews reach assigned work. That separation protects technical competence and also creates room for a valid-looking overreach when communications fail or necessity is falsely claimed.

## Doctrine does not place a soldier

The record fixes the Host's formations and divided command, not the position of any company or the authority of any current message. Assaults, signals, prisoners, truces, revocations, crew loyalty, access, casualties, and Lorn's settlement posture change with play.
