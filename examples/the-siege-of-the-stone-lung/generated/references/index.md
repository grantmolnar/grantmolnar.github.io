# Reference Index: The Siege of the Stone Lung

## People

- [**First Warden Ilyra Dain**](f4f7d2d8-d36a-4156-a898-981753326347.md) — Kest Mourne's First Warden, a siege commander who lets verified facts reverse orders and whose six-lantern commission crosses sectors without replacing civil or technical authority. Aliases: Ilyra Dain, Ilyra, First Warden Dain.
- [**Speaker Edda Marr**](a3b7aaad-961e-49e0-b33e-a37815d2cffc.md) — The civil-council speaker who guards Kest Mourne's sealed blight minutes and embodies the difference between a private admission and a correction the whole city must obey. Aliases: Edda Marr, Edda, Speaker Marr.
- [**Ventwright Tamar Ohn**](b79f9499-c3d6-40c5-afe0-ec75c1982ec5.md) — Kest Mourne's senior ventwright, responsible for technically executable work on the Stone Lung and entitled to refuse commands that surviving crews and machinery cannot safely perform. Aliases: Tamar Ohn, Tamar, Ventwright Ohn.
- [**Gate Captain Brann Sile**](4727a70d-0368-4a88-b86a-b250cc04e3eb.md) — The captain responsible for the Shattered Gate, whose practical military authority is measured in defensible ground, named reserves, and minutes bought for the rest of Kest Mourne. Aliases: Brann Sile, Brann, Captain Brann.
- [**Marshal Sera Venn**](485df0e9-f855-4d58-8064-300109947e36.md) — Commander of the Host of the Lower Road, pursuing enforceable access to the Stone Lung while retaining strategic authority distinct from Korr Bas's narrower technical command. Aliases: Sera Venn, Venn, Marshal Venn.
- [**Engineer Korr Bas**](02ddfa9a-79a7-42d8-b614-243664f38efd.md) — The Host's chief resonance engineer, who converted bounded technical command into the valid-looking Heartstrike overreach he considers preferable to Lorn's continued suffocation. Aliases: Korr Bas, Bas, Chief Resonance Engineer Bas.

## Places

- [**The Lantern Court**](d8d49895-57e8-4cef-a91a-5f899c590d54.md) — Kest Mourne's public command floor, where six sector lanterns, casualty boards, wall maps, runners, ledgers, and command tubes turn verified field facts into witnessed citywide orders. Aliases: Lantern Court, the Court, six-lantern command floor.
- [**The Shattered Gate**](d7db595d-38cd-4c33-b557-13016ba8e619.md) — Kest Mourne's principal outer defense, joining towers, galleries, inner turns, signal positions, and the upper-city road through which military time and a movable reserve reach the rest of the siege. Aliases: Shattered Gate, outer gate, Gate sector.
- [**The Cinder Foundry**](81df1171-965f-48c4-9f43-da801acc0f02.md) — Kest Mourne's three-hall forge and repair works, coupling gate braces, pumps, filters, counterweights, tuned tools, skilled labor, and the Foundry gantry into one finite production system. Aliases: Cinder Foundry, the Foundry, red-stone foundry.
- [**The Pale Gardens**](4c80a05c-1d71-41be-ab35-c931f7dee682.md) — Kest Mourne's food and living-filter terraces, where rootstock, spores, insects, service vents, and ward connections preserve a biological record of airflow that mechanical plans can miss. Aliases: Pale Gardens, the Gardens, fungus terraces.
- [**The Refuge Galleries**](61728d3c-7a1b-4838-9f0e-6076d8ba0cbf.md) — Processional galleries converted to civic refuge, joining relief records, the memorial wall, family tablets, an old service door, and the memorial stair to the Stone Lung's southern controls. Aliases: Refuge Galleries, the Refuge, lower galleries.
- [**The Cisterns of Black Breath**](308b133e-a63a-4417-b61b-9d743637f728.md) — Kest Mourne's oldest water works, combining public pools, lower valves, an inspection arch, overflow routes, and a maintenance channel whose stonework reaches the hidden southern exchange system. Aliases: Black Cisterns, the Cisterns, lower cistern line.
- [**The Countermine**](52fb318a-cbfe-4e77-a507-4c9a487073d0.md) — The defensive excavation where Kest Mourne's miners meet an older lower road, Host sapper works, signal routes, and the most direct military approach to Heartstrike and the Stone Lung. Aliases: Countermine, the lower road, sapper gallery.
- [**The Stone Lung**](c4c8f3a7-9a58-4daa-ab12-7df774a22794.md) — The pre-imperial pressure engine beneath Kest Mourne, whose vanes, geothermal throat, counterweights, branches, southern controls, and three military approaches exchange air between Kest Mourne and Lorn. Aliases: Stone Lung, the Lung, breathing engine.

## Organizations

Aliases: the Host, Lower Road Host, Lorn host.

Aliases: sealed minutes, blight minutes, council minutes.

Aliases: Lorn conduit plate, original conduit plate, southern conduit plate.
- [**Heartstrike**](c66d1a1b-0e9e-4868-bdc3-d19740c8dd8a.md) — The Host's resonance operation for fracturing the Stone Lung's southern seal during release, built from tuned shells, ceramic ribs, cables, relays, governors, and a manual firing path. Aliases: Heartstrike charge, resonance charge, Heartstrike line.
