## Summary

Kest Mourne's senior ventwright, responsible for technically executable work on the Stone Lung and entitled to refuse commands that surviving crews and machinery cannot safely perform.

## Reference Material

## An engine known by repairs

Ventwright Tamar Ohn maintains the accessible parts of the Stone Lung and the civic systems coupled to its strokes. She knows the machine through replaced bearings, improvised braces, seasonal draft, crew habits, and failures absent from formal diagrams. Her authority comes from reproducible work rather than reverence for inherited plans.

## Name the sequence and the crew

Tamar treats every remedy as an ordered operation: measure, isolate, brace, vent, move people, turn a control, and verify the next stroke. A military or civil command cannot make missing tools, labor, breathing protection, or pressure limits appear. The six-lantern writ expressly preserves a competent crew's right to reject a technically impossible order.

## Competence is not sovereignty

Tamar can judge whether machinery can survive an intervention, organize qualified operators, and state the material conditions a plan requires. She does not own the Lung, decide Lorn's political claim, or command soldiers merely because engineers need a road held. Her current diagnosis, recommendation, measurements, keys, available crew, and chosen remedy remain live adventure state.
