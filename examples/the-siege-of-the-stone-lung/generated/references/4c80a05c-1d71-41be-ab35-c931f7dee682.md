## Summary

Kest Mourne's food and living-filter terraces, where rootstock, spores, insects, service vents, and ward connections preserve a biological record of airflow that mechanical plans can miss.

## Reference Material

## Food that reads the air

The Pale Gardens descend beneath curtains of white fungus. Their terraces feed Kest Mourne and clean air returning from crowded wards. Rootstock, spore color, moisture, cave insects, and the timing of growth against the Stone Lung's stroke make the Gardens a living instrument as well as a food source.

## Two failures can share one bed

Sabotage follows hands, carts, tools, and sheds. Structural airflow failure forms gradients across beds and vents. The Gardens' stable value lies in preserving enough living material to separate those causes, map ward-by-ward flow, and produce filters for other sectors. Their old southern stock also preserves relationships erased from later civic plans.

## Biology does not choose the remedy

The Gardens can expose patterns and provide living material. They cannot name every saboteur, order a burn, allocate workers, authenticate a witness, or prove which vent is safe now. Contamination, harvest, surviving filters, readings, crews, quarantine, and loss must be discovered in the beds.
