## Summary

The Host's resonance operation for fracturing the Stone Lung's southern seal during release, built from tuned shells, ceramic ribs, cables, relays, governors, and a manual firing path.

## Reference Material

## A siege weapon tuned to a breath

Heartstrike is a resonance operation rather than one loose explosive. Tuned shells and wedges establish the frequency; ceramic ribs store and return vibration; a braided cable and relay chain carry the firing pulse toward the southern seal; governors and a manual path control when the accumulated force is released.

## Fracture is not controlled reopening

The operation is designed to break the southern masonry during the Stone Lung's release. A bounded version might expose the branch for seizure. A larger yield can mix pressure, heat, and methane-rich black breath into an ignition that travels through both settlements. Strategic authorization, technical calibration, and the physical ability to fire are distinct forms of control.

## The weapon exists before the countdown

The record fixes Heartstrike's architecture and danger, not its readiness. Placement, charge, cable continuity, governor state, firing signal, manual access, crew obedience, Bas's location and command reach, Venn's knowledge, and whether the weapon survives belong to the current siege.
