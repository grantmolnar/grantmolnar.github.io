# The Sealed Blight Minutes

**Reference ID:** `9fafc73c-5456-42fb-bad4-6b10eb9cf550`  
**Kind:** Object

**Tags:** Kest Mourne, civil council, mold blight, sealed record

## Summary

Kest Mourne's sealed council record of the mold emergency, temporary southern closure, expected harm to Lorn, planned purge and filter works, and later decision to preserve the closure after the blight ended.

## Reference Material

## A lawful emergency written down

The sealed minutes record a real lethal mold blight and the council's decision to close the Stone Lung's southern branch as quarantine. They also record that the closure would cut Lorn's air exchange and that purge beds, filters, and later reopening were part of the emergency plan.

## The later decision in the same hand

Subsequent entries preserve the choice that changed quarantine into policy: the contaminated strain died, promised works were abandoned, workers were reassigned, and the southern closure was kept while the public map and civic account ceased to show the branch. The minutes distinguish an initially justified act from the concealed permanence that followed.

## A record is not a public correction

The minutes preserve what a council wrote; they do not open their own seal. Who holds, reads, copies, publishes, believes, or acts on them is current state. Until witnesses and command procedure give the record effect, it remains evidence rather than correction.
