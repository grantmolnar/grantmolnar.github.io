# Gate Captain Brann Sile

**Reference ID:** `4727a70d-0368-4a88-b86a-b250cc04e3eb`  
**Kind:** Person  
**Aliases:** Brann Sile, Brann, Captain Brann  
**Tags:** Kest Mourne, Shattered Gate, gate command, reserves

## Summary

The captain responsible for the Shattered Gate, whose practical military authority is measured in defensible ground, named reserves, and minutes bought for the rest of Kest Mourne.

## Reference Material

## Minutes held in stone and bodies

Gate Captain Brann Sile commands the defenders assigned to Kest Mourne's outer gate. He reads the siege in frontage, trapped crews, usable stairs, reserve movement, and how long a shortened line can still protect the inner roads. He has little patience for a claim that removes people from the wall without naming what their absence buys.

## Proof that changes the line

Brann will cooperate with investigation when it solves an immediate military problem: predicts an assault, rescues a crew, identifies a false withdrawal, or gives him defensible room to abandon stone before people. Clear orders and verified battlefield intelligence matter more to him than rank performed at a distance.

## A gate command, not a city command

Brann can command his assigned soldiers, receive and guard battlefield prisoners, report the condition of the outer line, and execute lawful reserve or withdrawal orders. He cannot allocate every city resource, overrule competent technical refusal, or decide the Lung's permanent control. His current line, withdrawal, prisoners, reserve, report, casualties, and gate condition remain live adventure state.
