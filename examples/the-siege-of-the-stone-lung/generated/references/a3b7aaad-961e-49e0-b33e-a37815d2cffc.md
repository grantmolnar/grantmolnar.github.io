## Summary

The civil-council speaker who guards Kest Mourne's sealed blight minutes and embodies the difference between a private admission and a correction the whole city must obey.

## Reference Material

## Minutes beneath the council seal

Speaker Edda Marr leads Kest Mourne's civil council. She is the granddaughter of a magistrate who ordered the southern seal during the mold blight and knows that a secret public work was performed. She has protected the sealed minutes because she believes disclosure during a siege would fracture the city and vindicate the Host. Her fear about timing is serious; her defense of concealment is still wrong.

## Six copies or rumor

Kest Mourne does not admit a civic fact through one officer's remorse. A correction becomes operative when it enters all six sector ledgers, travels down the command tubes, changes the wall map, and is allowed to alter an order already killing people. Edda's office can release council records and make that civil acknowledgment possible. It cannot make an unexecuted confession binding by itself.

## Civil authority under bombardment

Edda can speak for council procedure, custody of its records, and the public acts by which the city corrects its own account. She does not command the Host, the Gate line, or the Stone Lung's technical crews, and she cannot settle permanent joint control alone. Her current custody, release, reading, copying, publication, and use of the sealed minutes remain live adventure state.
