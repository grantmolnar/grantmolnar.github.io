## Summary

The pre-imperial pressure engine beneath Kest Mourne, whose vanes, geothermal throat, counterweights, branches, southern controls, and three military approaches exchange air between Kest Mourne and Lorn.

## Reference Material

## Stone that takes a breath

The Stone Lung fills a pressure vault beneath Kest Mourne. Curved vanes of porous rock flex around a geothermal throat while counterweights climb and fall above them. Each stroke draws through one set of branches and releases through another, moving air, dust, flame, cloth, and pressure across both settlements.

## Three roads enter one machine

The Foundry gantry reaches the crown, counterweights, and charge harmonics. The memorial stair reaches the old civic platform and southern manual controls. The Countermine gallery reaches the lower-road assault and charge works. Each is a military road capable of carrying crews, witnesses, orders, tools, wounded, and armed bodies—not merely an entrance.

## Machinery is not sovereignty

The Lung was built to balance more than one branch, but its physical capacity does not determine who may command, inspect, repair, or control it. Current pressure, open branches, keys, approach control, crews, Heartstrike, damage, remedy, and the final transfer of the controls belong to the finale.
