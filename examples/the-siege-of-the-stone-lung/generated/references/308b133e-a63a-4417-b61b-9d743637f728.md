## Water above, old works below

The Cisterns of Black Breath lie beneath Kest Mourne's oldest wards. Upper pools feed public pumps; lower pools, sluices, numbered valves, inspection arches, drains, and overflow galleries control what reaches the city below. The fourth pool meets deliberate southern masonry where later maps claim natural stone.

## One buried system carries air and water

A maintenance channel behind the lower works opens toward the old lower road. Air and water move through it in opposite phases. The cistern geometry therefore joins public supply, the southern seal, the Stone Lung, and a route toward the Countermine. Breathing protection, measured hammer signals, and valve order matter because haste can move poison or dead air into occupied wards.
