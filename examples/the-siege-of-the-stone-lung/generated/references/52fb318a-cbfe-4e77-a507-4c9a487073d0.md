## Summary

The defensive excavation where Kest Mourne's miners meet an older lower road, Host sapper works, signal routes, and the most direct military approach to Heartstrike and the Stone Lung.

## Reference Material

## A road found by digging toward hammers

The Countermine begins as Kest Mourne's answer to enemy sappers and reaches an older lower road omitted from current maps. Its galleries connect mine faces, collapse chambers, signal routes, prisoner exchange points, and the direct approach to the Stone Lung's charge gallery.

## Every command must cross the tunnel

The lower road carries more than troops. Assault schedules, firing instructions, revocations, relay pulses, wounded, prisoners, and technical crews all depend on a passage that can be blocked, deceived, collapsed, or held. The Countermine is where military, technical, and political command occupy the same narrow geometry.

## Orders narrow inside the gallery

The record fixes the lower-road connections and the command routes that depend on them. It cannot settle the current collapse, cable, signals, prisoners, truce, sapper allegiance, access to Venn, charge readiness, or control of the approach. Every one of those questions is live in the tunnel.
