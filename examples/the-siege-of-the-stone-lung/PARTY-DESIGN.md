# Demonstration Company: The Basalt Hand

**Status:** Finalized and exercised by `play-state.example.json`.

## Purpose of the checked-in journal

The Basalt Hand is a subordinate demonstration company, not a required fresh-play company. It reaches the Stone Lung early, leaves a watch at the crown, and withdraws because access has outrun understanding. It returns with pressure readings, witnesses, evacuation crews, time bought at the wall, and a Host command divided over Heartstrike.

The journal exercises four runtime behaviors central to this adventure:

1. a terminal encounter can be visited, left, and revisited;
2. sector consequences can change after the first visit;
3. field evidence can make a later return to the command hub necessary; and
4. an overstated report can be corrected without erasing the original operation.

The Hand establishes every revelation and leaves fourteen authored alternatives unseen.

## The company

The **Basalt Hand** is a small Kest Mourne recovery company. Before the siege, it handled collapsed galleries, lost caravans, dangerous machinery, and disputes beyond the clean reach of any one civic office. Its members were inside the city when the Host closed the lower roads. Ilyra Dain commissions them because they can cross command boundaries without pretending to replace the people who know each sector.

They have no motto. Their contract seal is an open hand cut into dark stone.

### Maela Orin

Maela is a former gate lieutenant. She resigned after an inquiry blamed three deaths on a withdrawal she requested and her commander delayed. She now leads the Basalt Hand, negotiates its commissions, and turns broad authority into named duties and fallback positions.

**Useful habits**

- Reads a defensive line in time, reserves, and routes of retreat rather than territory alone.
- Makes commanders state what they intend to preserve and what they are willing to lose.
- Coordinates soldiers, labor crews, and civilians without folding them into one chain of command.

**Pressure point**

Maela trusts plans that can be bounded and rehearsed. She distrusts elegant machinery whose failure cannot be contained. At the Stone Lung, she favors defensive resealing until every stroke of shared retuning has a crew, a fallback, and a named consequence.

**Likely concern in this adventure**

She goes to the Shattered Gate first and refuses to strip it for an investigation. She lets the outer line fall only after it has bought a measured amount of time and Brann has an orderly withdrawal.

### Orren Caul

Orren is a pressure mason and independent machinery examiner. He trained in the ventwright guild and left after certifying that a public conduit map omitted load-bearing airways. The guild corrected the drawing in private and declined to renew his seal. Since then, he has inspected pumps, hoists, counterweights, and old civic works for people who cannot compel official attention.

**Useful habits**

- Distinguishes a machine's intended operation from the compensations keeping it alive now.
- Builds tests around cycles, tolerances, and failure sequences.
- Directs skilled crews without treating their knowledge as unskilled labor with better tools.

**Pressure point**

Orren wants the Stone Lung to remain intelligible. Once the vane model, tracer sequence, and pressure readings agree, he may forget how much political authority and enemy cooperation the operation still requires.

**Likely concern in this adventure**

He preserves the Cinder Foundry and reaches the Lung early through its service gantry. He is the first to call for withdrawal: the crown can be controlled, but the black breath has not been measured and the southern valves are still hidden.

### Siva Merrow

Siva is a spore physician and refuge organizer. She grew up in the lower wards, trained among the Pale Garden growers, and became known during a tunnel fever for refusing to separate medicine from water, air, crowding, and ration policy. She joined the Basalt Hand because emergencies kept proving her right.

**Useful habits**

- Reads fungal growth, lamp color, breathing patterns, and water sickness as one environmental record.
- Makes immediate conditions safer before asking frightened people for testimony.
- Organizes civilians as workers in evacuation, filtration, and evidence preservation.

**Pressure point**

Siva expects command secrecy to send danger downhill toward people with less authority. She may release evidence before Maela has prepared the city to survive it. In the Refuge Galleries, she must decide how long truth can wait without becoming another form of concealment.

**Likely concern in this adventure**

She stops the burning of the blue beds, treats the refuge air tally as evidence rather than rumor, and rejects any remedy that saves Kest Mourne by leaving Lorn to suffocate again.

### Hadrik Sol

Hadrik is an underroad guide and former caravan guard. He traded with Lorn before the southern roads closed and recognizes Host units by route marks, repairs, and marching habits. He has fought them for nineteen days without mistaking common equipment for common orders.

**Useful habits**

- Finds routes by draft, drainage, tool marks, and the habits of their users.
- Questions prisoners without expecting one frightened soldier to know an entire war.
- Recognizes when conflicting orders create room for a local bargain.

**Pressure point**

Hadrik sees a divided enemy as an opening and can overestimate how far frightened soldiers will defy command. Mael Rusk's truce survives only because the Hand gives him a way to protect his crews and Lorn while opposing Bas.

**Likely concern in this adventure**

He preserves Nara Ves and her escort at the Gate, cuts the visible relay during the first Lung visit, and calls the roof truce in the Countermine. He never mistakes Venn's opposition to Heartstrike for surrender of Lorn's claim.

## Working disagreements

The route grows from five disagreements.

- Maela takes the Gate's visible emergency seriously; Hadrik notices that the Host is declining openings a conventional besieger would take.
- Orren wants immediate access to the Lung once the Foundry gantry is known; Siva permits reconnaissance and forbids a valve operation without a gas path.
- Siva wants the sealed act disclosed in the Refuge; Maela insists on pairing disclosure with evacuation authority and an executable plan.
- Hadrik trusts the split between Venn and Bas; Maela requires Venn's authenticated revocation, a false completion signal, and Brann's repositioned reserve before counting the truce as a defensive asset.
- Orren accepts shared retuning when the measurements agree; Maela accepts it when every station can survive the next stroke.

## Fixed route

```text
Lantern Court
  -> Shattered Gate
  -> Cinder Foundry
  -> Stone Lung
  -> Pale Gardens
  -> Refuge Galleries
  -> Black Cisterns
  -> Countermine
  -> Lantern Court
  -> Stone Lung
```

In shorthand:

```text
Court -> Gate -> Foundry -> Lung -> Gardens -> Refuge -> Cisterns -> Countermine -> Court -> Lung
```

The first Lung visit secures the crown and proves the Hand is not ready. The second resolves the siege. The return to the Lantern Court turns field evidence into public disclosure, evacuation authority, reserve movement, and an offer of joint control.

## Resulting sector state

- **Shattered Gate:** held through the investigation; the outer line later falls in an ordered withdrawal after buying the finale's required time.
- **Cinder Foundry:** held at a cost; the east furnace is lost, but tuned tools, crews, braces, and one counterweight survive.
- **Pale Gardens:** held; food, filters, and tracer beds survive.
- **Refuge Galleries:** held; witnesses, records, evacuation labor, and the memorial stair remain available.
- **Black Cisterns:** held at a cost; pressure readings and one overflow survive, but the lower sluice is damaged.
- **Countermine:** held by local truce; Venn receives Bas's calculation, and Mael Rusk controls the charge-gallery approach.

The final remedy is shared retuning. It succeeds because the Hand returns with enough allied crews, measurements, authority, and military time to operate several stations on one cadence. The result gives both settlements air and gives neither absolution or permanent terms.
