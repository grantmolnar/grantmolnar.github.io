# Full Playthrough: The Basalt Hand

This playthrough follows the Basalt Hand through *The Siege of the Stone Lung*. It corresponds to `play-state.example.json`; `generated/05-play-summary.md` is the event-derived account.

The Hand goes first to the wall, reaches the Stone Lung before it is ready to solve it, and withdraws. The first Lung visit is reconnaissance. It returns only after the city has measurements, witnesses, evacuation crews, military time, and an enemy command divided against itself. Fourteen authored clues remain unseen.

```text
Court -> Gate -> Foundry -> Lung -> Gardens -> Refuge -> Cisterns -> Countermine -> Court -> Lung
```

## Session One: The wall is not the target

### The Lantern Court

On the nineteenth day of the siege, the six civic lanterns narrow with the Stone Lung's draw. The Shattered Gate sends a double red flare. The Pale Gardens lantern burns blue. A foundry runner sets a shell fragment on the command floor, and it continues to ring against the basalt. Refuge stewards submit an air tally that says the people below should be breathing easily.

They are not.

First Warden Ilyra Dain gives the Basalt Hand authority to cross sectors and no illusion that one order can save them all. The Gate wants the iron reserve. The Foundry wants straight beams and permission to keep a furnace cold. The Refuge wants air command cannot promise. The Gardens want time to study beds already marked for burning.

Maela makes every officer name the loss hidden inside the request. Sending the reserve to the Gate leaves the cistern crews unguarded. Relighting the furnace may restore brace production and wake the shell buried inside it. Reassuring the Refuge with false figures buys quiet and spends trust.

Hadrik compares the double flare with the Host's last withdrawal. The enemy left an opening near a public air shaft and did not take it. Orren keeps the ringing fragment. Siva takes the refuge tally and asks the stewards to keep the original before someone corrects it into obedience.

The Hand chooses the Gate. Its line is still organized, and an organized line can buy time. A broken one only consumes soldiers.

### The Shattered Gate

The right tower is opening from parapet to foundation. Its brace crew is trapped above the crack. The northern murder gallery is cut off. Beyond bowshot, Host soldiers leave wounded comrades and a broken signal slate where any angry defender can see them.

Captain Brann Sile asks for bodies. Maela gives him an order of work.

The wall holds while the Hand cuts into the lost gallery from inside. Hadrik watches the false withdrawal and finds fresh sapper marks beneath the approach. He calls back the soldiers preparing to pursue. Siva and Maela bring the isolated defenders through the opened passage. Orren reaches the resonator lodged beneath the brace crew and learns that removing it will take the tower with it.

He wedges its ceramic ribs instead. The altered support bleeds enough of the stored vibration into the crack to lower the crew. The tower remains standing. No one calls it repaired.

The Hand brings in two prisoners: Nara Ves, a lower-road scout, and a sapper escort carrying an order that contradicts hers. Venn's standing instruction forbids damage to public air shafts. Bas's countermand suspends that limit after the third hammer signal. Hadrik keeps them apart, promises ordinary treatment, and asks each only what they saw and what they were told.

Brann now has a recovered gallery, a tower that may survive another assault, and evidence that the Host keeps refusing the breaches it creates. Its companies are fixing defenders at the wall while the hammer signals continue beneath them. The prisoners prove that the enemy command is no longer giving one war.

The Gate holds. Brann can release a small reserve later, but the tower has a measured life. Maela writes the withdrawal route before leaving.

### The Cinder Foundry

The east furnace is cold. The shell inside it sings whenever the Stone Lung changes stroke. Gate officers are ordering Foundry Master Mera Coil to relight the hall and resume brace production.

Orren agrees with Tamar Ohn that heat will make the danger worse. He refuses Tamar's demand to abandon the hall. The shell stores vibration in ceramic ribs; the furnace is its sounding box. Maela clears the east works while Orren and Tamar break away the lining and change the shell's supports. Its stored pulse passes into a slag trench hard enough to craze the floor and kill no one.

The furnace is lost. The shell survives.

Its geometry is tuned to the Lung, not ordinary walls. Venn's maker seal remains beside a safety limit someone later cut away. Bas's larger setting is incised over it. The foundry's variable-vane drawings show a machine designed for a wider pressure balance than the public plans admit.

One shift of skilled labor remains. Maela orders two radial braces for Brann. Orren reserves one counterweight and a tuned cutting frame for the Lung. Siva claims filter housings for the Gardens and Refuge. The dead furnace means Mera can finish reduced sets, not miracles.

Behind the counterweight stores, Tamar opens a service gantry descending to the Lung's crown. Maela expects Orren to ask for more preparation. He asks to go below now. The Host is already calibrating its weapons against the machine; ignorance has become more dangerous than an early look.

### First visit to the Stone Lung

The Stone Lung moves before the Hand can take in its size. Porous vanes close around a geothermal throat. Counterweights climb into darkness. On the draw, dust, loose cloth, and the breath in their mouths all pull south.

Heartstrike is not yet complete. Bas's crews are still laying cable behind the false wall.

Orren maps the three-stroke cycle and marks the accessible counterweights. Maela leaves a foundry watch at the crown with a written withdrawal route. Hadrik follows a visible relay toward the southern wall and cuts it before the lower crews answer. Siva tests the cracks and stops everyone from touching the masonry.

There is gas behind it. They do not know how much. They have no safe overflow, no pressure sequence, and no authority to empty the wards above the likely vent.

The crown can be held. The branch cannot yet be opened. Orren calls the withdrawal.

A runner reaches the Court first and reports that Heartstrike has been disarmed. Hadrik sends the correction before the next allocation: the crown relay is cut; the lower charge and a manual trigger remain. The journal preserves both the overstated report and the correction. The truth is less comforting and more useful.

## Session Two: What the city sealed

### The Pale Gardens

Growers are carrying torches toward the blue beds when the Hand arrives. Sen Tahl has been told to burn every marked terrace before the color spreads.

Siva asks for one full stroke of the Lung.

The blue deepens only on the draw. Orange mold follows tool sheds and cart handles; it is enemy sabotage and burns cleanly when isolated. The translucent beds follow a different line. Orren traces soot pulled backward through a cracked foundry exhaust. Siva uses the blue spores as a living dye and watches them gather toward a sealed southern vent.

The harvest survives because the failures are separated before they are treated.

Old rootstock rings show that southern airflow stopped in the mold-blight year, abruptly and across every bed tied to that exchange. Someone scraped away the labels afterward. Deren Vask's message from the Refuge is no longer only an old man's accusation. The Gardens have kept the date in their roots.

Sen's crews prepare filter moss, tracer beds, and a ward-by-ward airflow map. The Hand leaves several ducts unsearched. It already knows where the reversal leads, and people are fainting below.

### The Refuge Galleries

The lower galleries breathe badly enough that families have begun moving uphill on their own. They block the passages used for medicine and water. Relief officers are preparing to arrest Deren Vask as a ration cart arrives short.

Siva refuses to begin with testimony. The Hand opens a cross-passage, moves lamps, and gives the lower bays a slower road upward. The missing rations are traced to a quartermaster feeding the Gate. The explanation does not excuse the secrecy, but it stops the crowd from turning one shortage into proof of every accusation at once.

Then Maela puts the records together.

The public memorial names the citizens killed in the mold blight. Family tablets name forty-three additional workers as transferred or lost. Their first signs form an old ventwright mark. Behind the memorial crypt, a stair descends toward the southern controls.

Deren says he helped close Lorn's branch. His crew was told the masonry would remain only until the blight passed. It remained. The maps changed. The Lorn workers vanished from Kest Mourne's rolls.

Edda Marr brings the sealed minutes because Ilyra has ordered her to produce them. They record a real emergency, a deliberate closure, the expected loss of Lorn's air, and the decision to preserve the seal after the mold was contained. Edda did not know about the black breath. She still argues that public confession during a siege may break the city before the enemy can.

Siva answers that silence has already moved the danger into the Refuge. Maela refuses to expose the record without an evacuation order and a plan the city can carry out. Ilyra promises, by runner, to read the minutes when the Hand returns with both.

The stewards copy the air tally into the evacuation order, post Deren’s testimony beside it, open the memorial stair, and organize civilians into crews.

### The Cisterns of Black Breath

Limewash covers old valve numbers around the fourth pool. A brass plate lies hidden in its drain. On the reverse, one diagram joins the Cisterns, the Stone Lung, and Lorn.

Beyond the barred arch, a trapped worker answers in hammer taps. The lower sluice is cracking. Cala Orm has kept contamination out of the upper supply, but the crew cannot hold much longer.

The Hand enters with breathing hoods and no flame. It reaches the workers, reproduces a full pressure cycle, and opens a disused overflow. The lower sluice breaks during the rescue. Black water is contained, but the safer of two gas routes is gone.

The readings complete Orren's operation. He writes one sequence across the brass plate: brace the crown, drive the black breath through the remaining overflow, follow Sen's spores, and open the southern valves by stages. Beside the broken sluice he marks the single vent path that can still fail.

Behind the fourth pool, a maintenance grate opens onto the old lower road beneath the enemy sapper line. The Hand has its road to the Countermine. It still lacks control of the charge gallery and authority to move both cities around the machine.

## Session Three: A truce under the roof

### The Countermine

Kest Mourne's miners break into the old road as the Hand approaches from the drainage channel. The first collapse traps defenders and Host sappers around the same resonance cable. Dust hides uniforms. Another hammer signal will finish the roof.

Hadrik calls a truce before either side can see well enough to shoot. Maela orders Kest Mourne's miners to put down tools, not weapons. Sap Captain Mael Rusk agrees because Bas has used his technical command to issue a valid-looking order at a concealed yield, because Nara Ves is alive, and because the Hand arrives wearing breathing hoods with a pressure sequence written on oilcloth. Rusk can now oppose Bas as obedience to Venn rather than surrender to Kest Mourne.

They clear the roof together.

The recovered tablets lay out Heartstrike without euphemism. Surface assaults fix Kest Mourne's reserves. Sappers expose the southern seal. The charge breaks it on the release. Bas's yield calculation accepts an ignition large enough to burn both settlements.

Venn's order permits seizure and controlled opening. It forbids fracture before the gas is vented. Bas is not escalating the same plan. He has abandoned it.

Rusk will not surrender his crews or Lorn's claim, and Hadrik does not ask him to. The bargain is narrower. Rusk sends a false completion pulse, holds the charge-gallery approach, and transmits Bas's yield table and the Hand's pressure sequence to Venn. Brann receives the assault tablet in time to move his reserve before the next feint becomes real.

The truce holds one road and no more. Rusk's sappers keep their weapons. Venn continues the siege until access to the Lung can be enforced. Bas loses the crews he expected to command at the decisive stroke.

### Return to the Lantern Court

The Hand lays four things on the command floor: the original conduit plate, Edda's minutes, Bas's yield table, and a staged retuning sequence.

Ilyra reads Kest Mourne's decision aloud before the sector officers. Edda says the admission strengthens Venn. Maela answers that Venn already has the physical claim and will soon have Bas's calculation. The secret now obstructs only Kest Mourne's own evacuation and bargaining.

Ilyra orders the lower wards emptied by bounds. Brann's movable reserve is released from the outer tower and sent to the Lung approaches. Refuge stewards receive authority over civilian routes and records. Foundry, garden, and cistern crews receive named roads, escorts, and commands instead of competing requisitions.

Kest Mourne offers Venn temporary joint control of the southern valves if she removes Bas and holds the assault during retuning. Venn accepts the mechanism, not peace. Lorn will receive air, witnesses at the controls, and an arrangement Kest Mourne cannot revoke in private after the army withdraws.

## Session Four: Seven lanterns

### The second visit to the Stone Lung

The Hand returns by three roads.

Orren and Tamar descend the Foundry gantry with Mera's surviving crew, two braces, one counterweight, and an escort pulled from Brann's reserve. Siva leads refuge workers and garden tracers down the memorial stair to clear the southern corridor. Hadrik and Rusk enter the charge gallery from the Countermine while Host troops move to reinforce it. Maela holds the speaking tubes, matching Brann's withdrawal, Venn's ceasefire corridor, and Ilyra's valve orders.

On compression, Bas sends troops through the Countermine and the Host strikes the Gate. Orren braces the crown while Brann commits his reserve to the Lung roads rather than the failing tower. Venn withdraws Bas's authority. His crews refuse him, but he still holds the manual trigger. Hadrik and Rusk isolate him instead of storming the gallery. The tuned cutting frame severs Heartstrike from the stone without releasing its stored resonance.

On the draw, Bas's remaining guards pin the southern valves while black breath enters the cracks around the wall. The broken sluice leaves one overflow. Siva follows the moving front with blue spores and sends refuge crews through passages they cleared earlier. Gas rises into abandoned high galleries. The single route makes the work slow enough that the upper ward evacuation becomes necessary.

At the Gate, the right tower fails. Brann uses the withdrawal written on the first day. The outer line falls; the reserve reaches the Lung approaches. Host skirmishers enter the sector ring and reach no occupied ward before Venn's truce signal.

On the release, Host skirmishers press the abandoned Gate approach while Orren opens the southern branch by stages. The first movement pulls too hard toward Lorn. Tamar changes the crown counterweight while Siva watches the tracers at the overflow. Ilyra and Venn give the second valve order together.

Air reaches both settlements.

Bas reaches once more for the manual strike and is taken alive by Rusk's crews. Venn orders the Host away from the inner approaches and places witnesses at the controls. She does not surrender. Maela does not ask her to accept a victory that would leave Kest Mourne with sole command of the Lung again.

### Resolution

Heartstrike is detuned. The black breath vents through the surviving overflow. The southern branch opens under a sequence witnessed from both sides.

The outer Gate is lost. The Foundry's east furnace is rubble. One lower sluice is broken. An upper ward remains empty until its galleries can be certified. Kest Mourne's public record now names what the city did eighteen years earlier.

Six civic lanterns burn evenly after the retuning. Ilyra sets a seventh unlit lantern beside them for Lorn. It remains dark until the settlements agree on permanent controls, reparations, and responsibility for the closure.

The seventh lantern solves nothing. That is why she leaves it where everyone can see it.
