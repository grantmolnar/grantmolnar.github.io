# Crown and Verge Operating Sheet

Use this sheet during Root Night and Glass Dawn. The authoritative scene prose remains in the Crown of Unfallen Rain and Glass Verge encounters in `adventure.json`.

## Crown diagnosis

Read each knot by its specific defect.

| Current | Working sign at the knot | Failed sign |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Living old soil | Dark motes root into the inner membrane | Clear empty center |
| Captured dawn | The cloud renews its own inner light | One bright release, then darkness |
| Circulating rain | Water crosses the knot and returns | Salt rind or one-way wetting |
| Remembered river course | Stable fall, storage, ground, and overflow behavior | Wandering condensation or wrong spill |
| Bounded heat | A narrow germination seam opens | Sealed waxy coat or burned empty shell |
| Bearer pulse | The knot beats between Bearer pulses | Filled knot with rigid load-bearing feeders |

Do not roll to conceal an observable defect. Roll, when the chosen system needs it, for timing, exposure, material loss, or precision of a difficult correction.

## Knot states

- **Empty shell:** at least one current absent.
- **Stormglass knot:** all six have touched it, but one remains weak, interrupted, or incorrectly related.
- **Viable knot:** all six work through one complete crown circuit.
- **Mature knot:** viable, heat-opened, internally pulsing, and able to slacken into a socket.
- **Surplus crown:** at least two mature knots remain visible while living-soil delivery continues.

A strong current can absorb one concrete strain in an adjacent weak relationship. It cannot replace an absent current.

## One complete crown circuit

Before the deep exhale, verify:

1. soil roots into the membrane;
2. stored dawn renews the cloud;
3. rain enters and returns;
4. condensation follows the chosen course;
5. bounded heat opens the coat; and
6. the knot maintains a small pulse between Bearer beats.

A failure identifies the relationship that did not close. Correct that relationship; do not restart six unrelated tests.

## Clean separation

All five conditions are required:

1. mature knot;
2. post-heat water return completed;
3. wet flexible cradle already bearing the weight;
4. three independent deep-exhale cues; and
5. slack feeder ring drawn free before the inward draw.

No blade is needed. The seed is a two-person burden or one balanced draft-animal load.

## Demonstrated surplus

Clean separation is ecologically defensible only when:

- living-soil delivery remains at least working;
- another mature knot stays attached and cycles; and
- no runaway fire or severed common return is consuming the remaining redundancy.

A sole mature seed can still be taken cleanly. The physical operation succeeds, but the party knowingly removes the only demonstrated continuation.

## Departure routes

| Route | Carries | Closes or narrows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Broad salt shelf | People, litters, teams, dismantled wagons, early light wagon | Loses wagon capacity at inward draw; all contact at direct sun |
| Wet middle ledge | People, assisted travelers, single-file animals, hand loads, one carried person | Requires working basin cooling; narrows at inward draw |
| Crown service root | Crown workers, climbers, one litter or seed cradle, compact tools | Opens on deep exhale; closes at inward draw |

Bearer redirection grants one specific extension to the favored band. It does not make every route broad or permanent.

## Dawn signals

1. **First reflection:** send heavy departures; crown diagnosis and final path work continue.
2. **Deep exhale:** clean separation and service-root crossings.
3. **Inward draw:** crown hardens; late routes lose capacity.
4. **Direct sun:** ordinary westward footing ends; remaining travelers continue east.

Show each signal before it closes an opportunity. A prepared operation already underway can finish.

## Departure record

For every group, record:

- names;
- route;
- movement capacities and carriers;
- animals and loads;
- plan actually heard;
- signal expected to close the route;
- seed position, if any; and
- westbound, rescue remainder, seed group, unresolved, or voluntary eastbound status.

Do not merge distinct choices into “the caravan decided.”

## Final state

Record every person, animal, current, fire front, mature or damaged knot, separation method, route, property claim, seed custodian, Merewash deliverable, and eastbound obligation.
