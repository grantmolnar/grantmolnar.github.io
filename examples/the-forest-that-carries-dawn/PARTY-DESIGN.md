# Party Design: The Saltward Four

The demonstrated party consists of four people already named on the Cairnroad caravan roster: Ansel Roe, Shai Moss, Kesh Rill, and Len Orf. They are not an established adventuring company. Hessa Clay begins calling them the **Saltward Four** because Orra repeatedly sends the same four names beyond the surviving camp and expects all four to return with an exact report.

This file is a validation artifact, not a casting requirement. A fresh group may adopt or replace any equal number of present-and-able roster entries under the authoritative fresh-party rule. The source does not assume the Saltward Four's specialties, route, discoveries, or ending.

This choice preserves the fixed forty-two-person opening count. The party is part of the emergency rather than a competent group standing outside it. Each member has a different reason to value rescue, departure, repair, and the Merewash rain-seed, and none has authority to settle the others' priorities.

## Ansel Roe

**Function:** Salt guide and acting road warden.

**What Ansel brings:** Ansel reads stable ground, reflected heat, wind over exposed salt, load limits, and the difference between a route that is visible and one that will still exist under the next traveler. He carries chalked pegs, a sighting cord, a folding shade, two signal mirrors, a broad knife, and the caravan's western route book.

**Why Orra sends him:** The caravan's ordinary road has vanished beneath moving roots. Orra needs someone who can describe a route by bodies and burdens rather than by a direction alone. Ansel is also one of the few people present who will tell her that a familiar road is gone before somebody dies proving it.

**Opening position:** Account for the missing people, establish a western departure, and leave the forest before its ecology becomes another excuse to delay. Merewash needs water, but forty-two living people are not acceptable stakes in an untested legend.

**Tie within the caravan:** Ela Fen contracted with Ansel for passage across the White Salt and intends to hold him personally responsible if she misses the first safe departure. He finds her accusatory certainty exhausting but accepts the obligation.

**Tie beyond the caravan:** Ansel has accepted a return contract from the eastern mines. Continuing into the Glass Waste with the forest would break it and leave his younger partners without their expected season's work.

**Private fear:** Years ago, Ansel preserved three loaded wagons by sending a poorer family down a narrow bypass first. Their cart overturned. Nobody died, but the family lost the tools by which they lived. He fears repeating the judgment under a more admirable name.

**Red line:** No route description is complete until it states who can use it, what burden it can carry, and which visible change closes it.

**Temptation:** Ansel can turn a defensible first departure into the only respectable choice. He may call later voluntary work irrational simply because he would not remain for it.

**Table voice:** Short, concrete, and skeptical of cardinal directions in a moving place. “West is not a route. The shelf takes walking people and one hand load. Say which of those you are.”

**Growth demanded:** Ansel must learn that plural departure does not mean failed command. His best ending establishes real western routes, carries the people who choose them, and records rather than erases those who knowingly continue east.

## Shai Moss

**Function:** Well-digger and field water assessor.

**What Shai brings:** Shai tests taste, mineral trace, temperature, seep rate, storage, soil acceptance, and whether water has a return path. He carries stoppered sample tubes, chalk salts, wax floats, a narrow auger, measuring cord, a copper cup, and a Merewash well log copied during the sixth failed rainy season.

**Why Orra sends him:** Nahl Reed can compare the forest with old watershed accounts, but Shai has spent his working life distinguishing a temporary pocket from a replenishing source. He can test whether warm rain, old soil, and a seed process are presently functioning without treating inherited description as proof.

**Opening position:** Rescue comes first, but Merewash's crisis makes investigation obligatory once a safe western route exists. Shai initially wants the rain-seed badly enough to mistake a promising current for available water.

**Tie within the caravan:** Corven Ash's seed grain and Neri Lute's reservoir tools are underwritten by the same Merewash council that hired Shai. Saving either cargo would improve the valley even if no seed can be taken.

**Tie beyond the caravan:** Six dry neighborhoods have promised labor for a reservoir Shai does not believe can fill without a new watershed. He is expected to return with an honest answer, not necessarily a solution.

**Private fear:** Shai once deepened a failing well after finding a cold seep. The seep was fossil water. The well served one season and then failed completely after the village had abandoned two shallower sources. He fears being seduced by proof that answers only the first question.

**Red line:** Drinkable, reachable, and renewable are separate claims. Desperation cannot merge them.

**Temptation:** Shai can let Merewash's need settle ecological uncertainty. He may call a cleanly detached seed a surplus before the continuing forest has been checked.

**Table voice:** Curious, patient, and exact about return. “Mark the cup. Let it travel. We do not know what the basin gives until we know what comes back.”

**Growth demanded:** Shai must accept departure without a seed as a real success and require stronger evidence for taking than for studying. His best ending carries one demonstrated surplus while preserving the measurements that would have made leaving it attached an honorable decision.

## Kesh Rill

**Function:** Senior teamster and beast keeper.

**What Kesh brings:** Kesh knows the caravan's animals as individuals, recognizes fear from directed avoidance, and can improvise hauling, litter, and counterweight work without treating a team as a machine. He carries spare halters, soft blinders, hoof wraps, bell mufflers, a light harness, a grooming hook, and feed portions scented for each missing pair.

**Why Orra sends him:** Six draft animals are missing with the wagon pockets. Their behavior can reveal heat, water, unstable roots, and the safest burden lines, but only if somebody understands what each animal ordinarily does. Kesh can also transfer handling responsibility when the party divides.

**Opening position:** Recover people and animals together wherever that is physically possible. Kesh distrusts any ecological plan whose first step is to drive a frightened animal through a hazard so the party can observe what happens.

**Tie within the caravan:** Kesh trained Tovan, Deme, Mara, Tobin, Olan, and Tess. He is responsible for the handling practices that put the missing teams where they were when the forest lifted the camp.

**Tie beyond the caravan:** Kesh intended to sell his share in the caravan at Merewash and stop traveling. He has no eastward contract and no home waiting beyond the Glass Waste.

**Private fear:** A panicked team once broke a bridge line after Kesh rang the wrong recall bell. The drivers survived; two animals did not. He fears using expertise as a reason nobody else may question his reading.

**Red line:** An animal's aversion may be evidence, but pain and panic are not tests to repeat.

**Temptation:** Kesh may preserve animal capacity at the expense of a human route or refuse necessary restraint because it resembles an earlier failure.

**Table voice:** Names first, species second. “Sedge freezes when the root moves. Clove pulls when the bell answers. Harnessing them together does not make either reaction disappear.”

**Growth demanded:** Kesh must distinguish humane use from refusal to use. His best ending recovers all six animals, assigns them work they can actually bear, and transfers their care before the last departure rather than making himself indispensable.

## Len Orf

**Function:** Caravan clerk, salvage factor, and recorder of necessity claims.

**What Len brings:** Len identifies ownership, debt, company stores, repair rights, and the difference between temporary use and consumption. He carries the caravan ledgers, seal tags, wax tablets, colored ownership cord, a small scale, blank necessity forms, and copies of the cargo mortgages most likely to survive their owners.

**Why Orra sends him:** The rescue will use rope, canvas, frames, copper, grain, oil catches, casks, and animals whose owners may be absent. Orra needs somebody beside the workers who can record why a sacrifice was made without delaying immediate life-saving action.

**Opening position:** Emergency authority should remain narrow. Len expects ecological repair to consume private property under the emotionally convenient claim that everyone benefits.

**Tie within the caravan:** Ira Senn has accused Len twice of undervaluing damaged copper. Pelen Marr trusts Len to distinguish Pelen's wagon frame from Ira's pipe stock even when both lie in the same wreck.

**Tie beyond the caravan:** Len's contract ends at Merewash. He has been offered a permanent account-keeper's place there if enough of the caravan's tools, grain, and claims arrive to make the settlement viable.

**Private fear:** Len once wrote “owner absent; presumed consent” on a storm salvage. The owner returned alive and discovered that the rescued cargo had been sold to pay other claims. Len has never again treated absence as a blank signature.

**Red line:** A justified sacrifice remains a debt, loss, gift, or dispute after the danger passes. It does not become ownerless because the result was good.

**Temptation:** Len can make urgent work wait for perfect process or treat a recorded obligation as more important than the body the material could save.

**Table voice:** Formal under pressure, but not bloodless. “Use the pipe. I have the owner, the length, the reason, and two witnesses. What I do not have is permission to call it free.”

**Growth demanded:** Len must use recordkeeping to enable accountable action rather than postpone it. His best ending leaves every major sacrifice attached to a name while recognizing that some owners will ratify, forgive, or transform the claim.

## Party tensions

### People first, but which people leave first

Ansel wants an early western departure as soon as one route is proven. Kesh resists sending handlers away while animals and low-hollow travelers remain unrecovered. Shai accepts staged departure but wants enough labor retained to test the rain process. Len insists that every group hear what it is leaving and what property remains committed behind it.

### A usable cup is not a surplus watershed

Shai is the party member most likely to press toward the seed. Ansel demands a departure route before committing labor. Len asks whose copper, canvas, casks, and seed grain the experiment spends. Kesh asks whether the proposal depends on forcing an animal through a current nobody understands.

### Repair and restitution

Pelen's frame, Ira's copper, Rusk's oil catches, Corven's grain, and company canvas can all solve different problems. Len distinguishes emergency rescue use from later ecological use. Ansel may accept the fastest workable sacrifice. Shai argues that repairing damage caused by the caravan is not mere speculation. Kesh separates property cost from harm imposed on living carriers.

### Knowledge does not require unanimity

The party can agree on what the forest is doing while choosing different departures. Ansel's successful route does not compel Jun or Sio to use it. Shai's surplus finding does not compel Ela to remain for the seed. Kesh's animal judgment does not authorize him to choose for human handlers. Len records these differences rather than resolving them into “the caravan decided.”

## Demonstration operating habits

The Saltward Four use four repeated questions when groups divide:

1. **Ansel:** What route is physically open, to whom, and until which sign?
2. **Shai:** What relationship is functioning, and what return proves it?
3. **Kesh:** Which living body bears the work, and what behavior shows strain?
4. **Len:** Whose material or authority is being used, and what obligation survives?

These questions are not mandatory player roles. They are a demonstration of how a system-neutral party can divide expertise without transferring final choice away from survivors, owners, or later departure groups.
