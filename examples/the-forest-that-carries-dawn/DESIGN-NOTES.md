# Design Notes: The Forest That Carries Dawn

## Governing premise

A caravan wakes inside a forest that crossed the White Salt during the night. The forest is moving east and will enter the Glass Waste at dawn. Some of the caravan can leave before then, but its people, animals, and wagons have been scattered among moving roots. Merewash, the caravan's destination, has endured six years of failed rain. During migration the forest can form a living rain-seed capable of beginning a watershed, but the seed is assembled by relationships among several organisms and can be separated safely only during the Bearer's breath.

The adventure is not about identifying a culprit. The forest did not choose the caravan, the caravan did not knowingly trespass, and no secret villain controls the migration. Play concerns rescue, observation, ecological intervention, route choice, divided human priorities, and whether the group can create a surplus without breaking the system that produces it.

## Why the adventurers are involved

The adventurers are members of the Cairnroad caravan roster. They may be guards, drovers, passengers, factors, healers, scouts, or hired specialists. They need not know one another well and need not care about the same people.

At the opening, Caravan Master Orra Venn has forty-two names to account for, three draft teams missing, and no map that remains correct for more than an hour. She gives the adventurers a narrow commission because they are conscious, mobile, and capable of returning with information. Their authority comes from immediate competence and the consent of people who travel with them, not from friendship, prophecy, noble command, or local office.

## Fresh-party roster rule

The forty-two-person opening count belongs to the authoritative adventure; the Saltward Four do not. For fresh play, use an equal number of present-and-able roster entries as player characters or replace that many entries one for one. Do not append a four-to-six-person party to the roster. A replacement may inherit only the location and functional capacity needed to preserve the opening count; the player supplies the new history, loyalties, and reasons to care.

Authoritative procedures name functions such as route recorder, property recorder, animal handler, and watershed observer. The players may fill them. Named roster NPCs step into unattended functions only when useful. The demonstrated party's route, discoveries, disagreements, and ending never determine what a fresh group must do.

## Why the old records are present

Sio's six-line route-song and Nahl's northern watershed report have independent provenance. Sio learned a family fragment from a witness to the previous crossing. Merewash hired Nahl after six failed rainy seasons to examine old watershed claims, and the report traveled with them because it records a seed carried from an earlier migration. Their partial agreement makes investigation reasonable without making either record an oracle.

The Cairnroad runs west and south from the White Salt toward Merewash. The forest carries the caravan east, away from that route and toward the Glass Waste. A westward departure therefore restores the intended journey; an eastward continuation is a new commitment rather than another version of reaching the destination.

## The commission can widen without becoming command

Orra's opening commission is limited to people, routes, and the dawn deadline. After one usable western route is known and every missing name is reached or under active rescue, she may hold a work muster. The muster records departure, rescue, repair, and investigation groups separately; it does not bind absent people or convert urgency into a caravan-wide vote.

Company labor can be assigned one operation at a time. Private labor and property enter seed work through named volunteers and specific consent. This gives later ecological action a real human mandate while preserving rescue-only and early-departure play as complete outcomes.

## Rescue does not become custody

The opening crisis authorizes rescuers to reach people, stabilize immediate danger, and carry route information. It does not authorize them to decide every later movement on behalf of the rescued.

A person pinned under a load cannot meaningfully choose to remain there. Free or stabilize the person first. After a workable alternative and current route information exist, remaining for repair, investigation, seed work, or continued migration can become a voluntary choice. Record who heard which plan. Rescue is complete when immediate danger and route knowledge have been addressed, not only when everyone has been returned to Orra's camp.

## Property remains an obligation

Emergency use of property is permitted when it is the least destructive workable means to save a named person from immediate danger. That rule does not automatically extend to ecological repair or a speculative rain-seed attempt. Company property, present-owner property, and absent-owner property therefore have different decision paths.

This distinction is not intended to prevent action. It makes sacrifice visible. A dismantled wagon, spent planter, lost grain load, or cut copper pipe remains a claim among survivors after dawn even when its use was justified.

## The forest is an ecology, not a person

The migrating forest is a temporary association of species carried by an immense root animal called the Bearer. Some organisms signal, remember, imitate voices, avoid injury, or respond to repeated patterns. None represents the whole. No successful scene should depend on asking the forest for permission and receiving a single answer.

The adventurers can infer tolerances, restore a damaged route, alter burdens, imitate signals, move organisms, and choose what cost they will impose. These are interactions with living systems, not diplomacy with a disguised monarch.

## The rain-seed is made, not found

A rain-seed is a living weather-knot assembled only during migration. It requires:

1. old soil carried by the soilbearers;
2. borrowed dawn released by lantern flowers;
3. circulating warm rain from the basins;
4. a remembered river course carried in fungal cords;
5. blackgrass heat to open the outer coat; and
6. the Bearer's root-breath to separate the mature knot without tearing the crown.

The group does not need to collect six arbitrary quest tokens. Each relationship changes a later region. Soil must arrive alive, light must be released at the correct stage, rain must continue circulating, a river memory must be selected rather than copied whole, fire must pass through a controlled corridor, and the mature seed must be separated during a bodily cycle.

## Currents are relationships, not inventory

Each ecological current is run through a source, a path, a receiver, a visible sign, and a tolerance. Workers may observe, redirect, take within tolerance, borrow from, spend, foul, or repair one of those relationships. The result should appear elsewhere in the ecology: wet stems, dark roots, waking cords, cooling margins, opening gates, or crown mist.

A clear experiment changes one condition and checks both the intended receiver and a neighboring dependency. When the setup can answer the question, the basic result should not be hidden behind a roll. System-specific uncertainty belongs in time, exposure, precision, material loss, and whether the work can finish before the region moves.

The organisms do not negotiate terms. They respond to shade, scent, moisture, heat, vibration, timing, and route geometry. Human negotiation concerns who contributes labor and property, whether the proposal borrows or consumes it, and what obligation survives dawn.

A strong current may provide a concrete redundancy or absorb one later strain in an adjacent weak current. It cannot replace an absent soil, light, water, heat, memory, or pulse relationship. This keeps the convergence ecological rather than turning it into a points budget.

## Preserved voice is evidence, not surviving authority

The Hollow of Kept Voices preserves repeated associations among speech, water, mineral trace, distance, and route. It can retain a flood height more faithfully than a name. A familiar cadence may be exact while present awareness, private memory, and personal identity are absent.

Give the group practical tests: a fresh voice cannot answer a new question, one measurement appears in several voices, personal details drift while slopes remain, and rerouted water changes the speech pattern. Characters may still grieve or speak to what they hear. Do not convert that grief into omniscient testimony, consent from the dead, or a final message tailored to solve the adventure.

## Necessary fire remains dangerous

Blackgrass fire belongs to the migration cycle because a narrow bounded front renews old root scars and opens prepared seed coats. That fact does not make spilled oil, dry canvas, pollen ignition, reflected heat, or a widened front ecologically useful.

Track intended heat and runaway fire separately. A strong heat current is low, continuous, bounded on both sides, directed toward a prepared receiver, and supported by a maintained escape route. More flame is not better evidence, and fully smothering the fire to save people is a legitimate choice that changes the final state rather than a hidden moral failure.

## Physiology does not confer permission

The Bearer responds to burden, cooling, vibration, obstruction, and injury. Those responses let workers diagnose pressure, correct damage, and time work. They do not reveal wishes or ratify seed-taking.

The deep exhale is a bodily interval announced by tension, vents, water, cords, and opening seams. It occurs once. A preliminary breath can test the system but cannot safely release a mature seed, and repeated forcing spends the final window. Limited redirection changes adjacency among routes while the Bearer continues east; it is not control over the whole organism.

## A sustainable seed requires repair

The caravan's iron-rimmed wagons crushed part of the soilbearer road when the forest lifted the camp. This was accidental, but it reduced the old soil reaching the crown. Under ordinary conditions the forest forms several mature knots and carries all of them east. With the route broken, only enough material for the forest's own continuation will arrive.

If the party restores the corridor, redirects surviving soilbearers, or supplies equivalent uncontaminated old ground, the crown can form one surplus seed. Taking that seed is materially different from stealing the forest's only future. The forest does not certify the act; the people taking the seed must judge the evidence and accept uncertainty.

## Uneven clues should express the world

The clue counts are intentionally unequal.

Widely observable truths have broad support. The approaching Glass Waste, the soilbearers' function, and the fact that the seed is assembled by convergence are visible in several regions and to several kinds of observer. Narrow timings have fewer sources. The warm basins' circulation and the blackgrass route can be learned reliably, but fewer places demonstrate them.

Do not add clues merely to regularize counts. Redundancy should follow how many independent processes genuinely reveal the conclusion. The source encounters are uneven as well: the camp, soil road, and rain basins are information-rich; the Glass Verge is late, dangerous, and comparatively sparse.

## Discovery persists; access does not

Once the group has observed a clue, later movement, burning, breakage, or closure does not erase what was learned. The physical route remains independent. A known canopy can become unreachable, and a closed wagon pocket can remain precisely located.

Evidence may move only through a carrier established in the fiction. Survivors, bells, animals, soilbearers, old-ground mist, glasswings, pollen, marked water, a living fungal braid, fire, embers, vents, feeder tension, and crown defects can carry evidence beyond the region where the process occurred. The clue retains that authored source. This prevents the moving forest from becoming a pretext for inventing unlimited new corroboration.

A later receiver can diagnose an absent or malformed relation. Diagnosis does not retroactively supply the missing current, reopen a closed route, or convert an unobserved source clue into knowledge. The group may understand exactly what it missed.

## Uneven support is allowed to produce uneven knowledge

A rescue-only group should not receive the full seed manual merely because the adventure contains it. The broad dawn and departure truths recur widely, while narrow basin, borrowed-light, and burn timings appear only where their processes can be observed. Avoiding all relevant sources may therefore leave a revelation unknown.

This is not a broken Three Clue Rule. Every revelation begins with at least three independent sources and survives one lost source with two remaining. The rule protects investigation from a single missed clue; it does not guarantee complete understanding to a party that declines the relevant ecology.

## Static clue graph, moving physical graph

Adventure Graph records a stable encounter-and-clue structure. At the table, physical adjacency changes over four migration stages:

- **Long Shade:** the forest has just lifted the caravan; broad root roads remain open.
- **Red Dusk:** outer roots draw inward, the blackgrass burn advances, and the warm basins climb.
- **Root Night:** the Bearer breathes, the crown descends, and several earlier paths separate.
- **Glass Dawn:** the eastern roots enter the Glass Waste; ordinary walking routes close behind them.

A clue that identifies an encounter reveals how to recognize or follow its region, not a permanent road. Distinguish knowing that a region exists from having a safe route to it at the current stage.

## Pressure is displacement, not a universal countdown

The dawn crossing is the largest deadline, but each opportunity closes differently.

- Missing wagons can be freed early or abandoned when their root pocket narrows.
- Soilbearers change course when the burn reaches the old scar.
- Lantern flowers release stored light before the Bearer's long central exhale.
- Warm basins climb beyond ordinary reach.
- Kept voices become louder but less distinguishable near dawn.
- The controlled burn becomes harder once glass reflection ignites the eastern grass.
- The crown becomes accessible only late.
- Departure paths close from the outermost roots inward.

This allows meaningful order without making every scene obey the same clock.

## Human disagreement is practical

Orra Venn wants every living person accounted for before resources are spent on Merewash. Nahl Reed believes a viable rain-seed could save thousands but knows that an unverified legend is not a warrant to spend forty-two lives. Pelen Marr is responsible for the wagons that damaged the soil route and wants to repair what he can. Hessa Clay keeps the roster and refuses to let missing people become abstract costs. Sio Tern knows fragments of an earlier migration account but distrusts inherited poetry when lives depend on measurements. Jun Alder, a young drover who has never had a stable home, may decide that leaving with the caravan is not the only form of rescue.

None of these people needs to be secretly corrupt. Their priorities can conflict because the available time and safe labor are limited.

## Failure changes the state

Failure should alter routes, injuries, burdens, clue availability, seed viability, caravan cohesion, or ecological damage before it ends the adventure.

Examples:

- A wagon is left behind but its people are freed.
- Draft animals escape into the forest and later draw soilbearers off course.
- A drained basin gives the caravan water but weakens the crown.
- A voice-course is copied inaccurately and produces an unstable watershed pattern.
- Fire reaches the crown too early and creates several dead seeds rather than one living knot.
- The group misses the root-breath and must choose between leaving the seed, tearing it free, or traveling farther with the forest.
- Some survivors leave at the Glass Verge while others remain aboard the moving roots.

## Clean separation and justified taking are different claims

The finale must distinguish technical success from ecological justification.

A mature seed can be separated cleanly only during the deep exhale, after its own circulation has closed and a wet support bears its weight. This is a physiological claim. A clean socket proves that the crown was not torn during removal.

Calling the seed a surplus requires additional evidence: living-soil delivery still functions, another mature knot remains attached, and no common return or runaway fire is consuming the remaining redundancy. This is an ecological inference. It remains an inference under uncertainty, not permission issued by the forest.

A sole mature seed may therefore be physically viable and cleanly taken while still imposing the largest demonstrated cost. Preserve that distinction in the encounter, final record, and aftermath.

## The seed begins another ecology

A rain-seed is not an instant weather device. Transport must preserve circulation, light, course, and pulse. Establishment at Merewash requires a compatible fall, storage, ground, overflow, and interval relation, followed by local soil, moderated dawn, returning water, a living course path, bounded germination heat, and room for the inherited pulse to enter the ground.

A successful first wet season should stabilize one watershed district, not end a six-year drought everywhere. Regional benefit requires continued care and later growth. A stormglass or torn seed can remain valuable without being mislabeled as self-sustaining.

## Departure remains plural

The caravan does not make one binding final choice. Different people may leave before crown work, remain for rescue, help complete a seed and then depart, or knowingly continue east. Route capacity, movement state, information actually heard, and named support determine whether each choice is real.

The finale should run crown work and departure in parallel. Silence, absence, injury, dependency, youth, and admiration never stand in for consent.

## No final menu should replace accumulated play

The finale should not ask the group to choose one of three prewritten solutions. The available outcomes emerge from what they repaired, preserved, damaged, learned, carried, and promised.

The Glass Verge displays the resulting state: who is present, which exits remain open, whether the soil route was restored, whether the six currents converge, whether the seed is viable, what the caravan can still move, and who chooses to cross or remain.

## Governing voice standard

The forest should be strange because each living thing does exact work, not because the prose assigns vague intention to the whole. Prefer material verbs over ecological abstraction.

- Soilbearers **carry** old ground and return empty.
- Lantern flowers **lend** dawn through living stems, moths, and wet pollen.
- Warm basins **send and receive** water; a path that never returns is a loss, not circulation.
- Fungal cords **keep measures** while names and biographies drift.
- Blackgrass **opens and clears** within wet, living bounds.
- The Bearer **bears, cools, strains, vents, and exhales**; it does not answer.
- The crown **assembles and displays** the paths that reach it.
- The Glass Waste **reflects, heats, and hardens**; it does not threaten.

Use “current,” “state,” “receiver,” and “capacity” where the GM needs an operating term. In openings, discoveries, and human speech, show the beetle, cup, drop, cord, flame, vent, knot, load, or narrowing salt that makes the term true.

Human voices should be equally material. Orra counts names and responsibilities. Hessa records what each person has actually heard. Nahl limits claims to observed watershed behavior. Pelen speaks in load, joinery, and repair without treating guilt as authority. Sio tests inherited lines against matter. Jun asks what a road permits and what it costs to continue. None speaks for the forest.

The adventure's moral pressure should arise from limited hands, moving routes, named property, incomplete evidence, and consequences that remain visible. Avoid a final oracle, a secret ecological sovereign, or language suggesting that beauty itself grants consent.
