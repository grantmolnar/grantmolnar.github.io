# Full Playthrough: The Saltward Four

This is one demonstrated night through *The Forest That Carries Dawn*. It touches all ten encounters to test the complete structure, not because fresh play demands this route. Another group may leave earlier, miss a living path, abandon the seed, or cross onto glass. Here, fifteen original clues remain unseen, the caravan begins departing before the crown is understood, and two rescued people ultimately choose the eastward road.

Ansel Roe, Shai Moss, Kesh Rill, and Len Orf begin as four names among forty-two, not as an established fellowship. Their identities and specialties belong to this demonstration only. Hessa Clay calls them the Saltward Four after their names keep returning on the same report line: route, water, animals, property.

The demonstrated route follows the four changes of the forest:

1. **Thirteen Names Beyond the Roots** — Long Shade: camp, wagon rescue, and soil-road repair.
2. **Water That Climbs** — Red Dusk: lanterns, basins, and remembered river course.
3. **One Long Breath** — Root Night: bounded fire, burden correction, and crown separation.
4. **The Last Salt** — Glass Dawn: parallel departure, final accounting, and divided travel.

No roll determines whether a clear experiment reveals its basic result. System-specific uncertainty would govern exposure, precision, injury, speed, and material loss. This demonstration records the resulting fictional judgments rather than prescribing mechanics.

## Session One: Thirteen Names Beyond the Roots

### Camp Under New Leaves

The caravan wakes under green light with its wagons no longer resting on White Salt. Roots rise and settle beneath the camp. Hessa Clay counts twenty-nine people present, including the four player characters. Thirteen names remain beyond the camp among five missing wagons.

Orra Venn gives the Saltward Four a narrow commission: find the missing people, report routes by actual burden, and return before the moving roots make their information false. She permits company stores to be used for rescue. She does not authorize private cargo to become general ecological material.

The camp is information-rich. The four hear three wagon bells from one northeast fork, sees loaded beetles moving crownward and empty beetles returning west, watches dew climb toward suspended roots, and sees green light sealed inside high flower cups. Smoke shifts toward the wagon scar. Behind the camp, ordinary salt contact narrows with every pulse.

Sio Tern recites the inherited six lines naming ground, dawn, rain, memory, fire, and loosening. Shai records them as a sequence of tests. The group does not stop to check Sio's final horizon line against the eastern reflection, so that clue is explicitly missed. The deadline is not yet established from Sio's authority.

The camp observations locate the forked wagons, Soilbearer Road, Lantern Canopy, Warm Rain Basins, Blackgrass Burn, and Glass Verge. They also establish that the beetles carry old ground between a western source and an eastern receiver. Orra divides work into rescue, route marking, and observation, but requires each group to carry reports back through Bera Holt or another named courier.

**State leaving the encounter:** forty-two names fixed; thirteen unresolved beyond the fork; several regions located; the western salt shelf provisionally visible but not yet certified for burdens; Sio's account treated as hypothesis rather than instruction.

### Wagons in the Forked Roots

The three rescue pockets are moving apart.

At the upper wheel, Tovan, Deme, Salen, and Miri hang above the blocked beetle road with Clove and Sedge still harnessed beneath them. Ansel insists that the group name the people before the cargo. Kesh muffles the wagon bells so Clove stops pulling while Sedge freezes through the next pulse. The four people are lowered first. The frame is then cut to free the team. Two spice chests reach the safe side; one remains aboard. A leaking oil jar is caught before its line reaches the polished soil track.

At the split freight bed, Pelen's leg is pinned between his frame and Ira's copper load. Len identifies Pelen's wagon frame, Ira's pipe, and Corda's climbing rope as three separate claims. A support is placed before the pin is cut. Pelen reaches a litter with a serious but stabilized leg injury. Bell crosses with most of the copper stock. Pelen offers his own frame for later repair; he does not offer Ira's pipe.

At the low hollow, Jun, Iri, Mael, and Daro stand above rising warm water. Crook remains with them; Moth and Reed have followed a higher water path. Thistle carries Daro on a litter while Jun and Iri guide the other three up the western climb. Mael hears a specific return plan before rescuers turn away. Crook follows Jun and reaches the western side. Moth and Reed remain missing animals, not unresolved people.

The rescue reveals more than location. Separate wagon pockets rise on one central warm pulse. Salt dust lies only on west-facing bark, making sustained eastward movement measurable. A repeated well depth fades along a pale fungal cord. The blocked delivery beneath the wreck shows how much old ground is no longer reaching the crown.

They do not test the fresh repetition of Tovan's roster call after reaching Tovan alive, and it does not pause long enough to observe rope slack during the later central exhale. Both clues are marked missed rather than quietly granted.

By the end of the rescue, all forty-two people are physically accounted for. Nobody dies. Pelen, Lio, Sen, and Daro require carried or assisted travel. The rescuers have located the Hollow of Kept Voices and Root-Breath Chamber and has materially confirmed that the forest will enter the Glass Waste at dawn.

**State leaving the encounter:** forty-two people accounted for; six draft animals reduced to two missing; spice wagon cut; copper wagon divided; low-hollow wagons unrecoverable intact; Hollow and Root-Breath Chamber located; dawn deadline established from present evidence.

### Soilbearer Road

Pelen directs repair from a litter. The Soilbearer Road begins weak: some loaded beetles bypass the wreck, but the oil line, crushed lip, packed gate hinges, and approaching heat prevent enough old ground from reaching the crown.

The repair crew separates four problems.

- Company canvas catches and covers the remaining oil.
- Pieces of Pelen's surrendered frame support two opposed beetle streams without crushing them.
- Ira lends one measured pipe length for a reversible scent-rill after Len records owner, use, witnesses, and the fact that the pipe may remain in the forest.
- A wet band of old ground is prepared where the blackgrass heat will later cross the repair.

Shai checks the crownward receiver and a neighboring rain channel after each test. The repaired route passes approach, burden, gate timing, and receiver tests. Additional old-ground mist rises after the first knot's demand is met. This is the first strong evidence that repairing the caravan's damage can create a true surplus rather than merely improve the rescuers' access.

The repair crew misses two clues. It does not warm the river-clay fungus, and it does not follow the empty beetles far enough toward reflected heat to gain another independent verge locator. The road remains information-rich without yielding every possible inference.

Living-soil delivery becomes strong. The repair also creates a protected path along which bounded heat can later pass after the soil arrives.

**State leaving the encounter:** soil current strong; surplus production materially plausible; one length of Ira's pipe installed and owed; Pelen's frame committed; oil contained; burn margin prepared; no private planter or seed grain spent.

### Session close

The Saltward Four return with four separate reports rather than one verdict.

- Ansel reports that a westward walking route exists but is already narrowing.
- Shai reports that the forest's water and seed claims remain unproven, while old-soil surplus is now demonstrable.
- Kesh reports all people and four of six missing animals recovered.
- Len reports which materials were company property, surrendered property, lent property, and abandoned property.

The caravan can leave. It cannot yet leave everyone, every burden, and every repair worker at once.

## Session Two: Water That Climbs

### Lantern Canopy

Red Dusk draws the outer roots inward. Shai and Len climb with Nahl, Della, and two riggers while Ansel and Kesh move toward the basins.

The canopy contains intact cups, dry branches, fallen living material, and flowers that still shine but no longer pass a current. The group refuses to sever a cup merely to prove that cut light is sterile, so that clue remains missed. It also misses the advancing heat line because its attention stays on water and pollen geometry.

Two intact cups are rehung. A wet pollen bridge bypasses one dry branch. The group follows bright pollen from repaired soil through lit water toward the crownward receiver and checks whether neighboring stems reopen.

The result is not general illumination. Captured dawn moves as a living pollen current from many flowers toward a separate lattice. The light current becomes working but not strong because one branch remains closed. Della's medicinal planters are not consumed.

**State leaving the encounter:** captured-dawn current working; one dry branch persists; two cups rehung; wet pollen bridge active; no severed flower; no planter spent.

### Warm Rain Basins

Kesh and Jun follow Moth and Reed through upward hoofprints. They do not drive another animal into the basins to see what happens. A cooled return path is opened, Jun gives the call the pair knows, and both animals return without injury.

Shai marks one measured cup. The marked water appears successively in cooling roots, fungal cords, lantern stems, and crown mist before returning to the basin chain. A pinched lower return is bypassed with a capillary loop. The basin crew fills two caravan casks only from the rate that replenishes after the mark completes its circuit.

The group misses the separate clue in the steam line at the bright edge because it does not approach the most reflective basin. It nevertheless establishes that the basins circulate rain and cannot be safely drained. Six mineral and pollen traces also converge in one mist, locating the descending crown.

The evidence now supports a broader conclusion. Soil, light, and water arrive from separate sources. Sio's six lines describe a convergence process rather than six objects to collect. The seed is made at a receiver, not picked from a special tree.

**State leaving the encounter:** all six missing draft animals recovered; rain current strong; two casks filled from sustainable yield; crown located; no basin drained; convergence model established.

### Hollow of Kept Voices

Shai, Len, Sio, Nahl, and Bera enter the hollow before Root Night sharpens and blurs its speech.

A fresh fragment repeats a measurement but cannot answer a new question. The same depth appears in three different voices. On repetition, slope, distance, and mineral color remain stable while names and relationships drift. This is enough to establish that the cords preserve river courses, not whole people.

The hollow team refuses to select a course because one voice sounds familiar. It compares five relations:

- moderate fall;
- deep seasonal storage;
- porous mineral ground;
- broad overflow margin; and
- long dry interval.

Those relations fit Merewash better than the steep, fast courses that would produce dramatic first rain and poor dry-season survival. One living braid carries the selected course toward the crown. A wet transcription travels west with Bera, but nobody mistakes the copy for the living current.

The group misses Tovan's repeated roster call and leaves before the cords slacken at Root Night. The remembered-course conclusion is therefore supported by fewer observed clues than the widely visible dawn and convergence conclusions. Its confidence comes from targeted testing, not equal clue count.

**State leaving the encounter:** Merewash-compatible living braid prepared; remembered-course current strong; wet transcription sent west; preserved voices explicitly denied present authority.

### Staged departure

Orra sends the first willing walking group to the western staging ground. Ela Fen leaves the inner camp after hearing the route and closing sign. Her departure does not endorse the seed attempt. Orra retains enough carriers, handlers, repair workers, and route keepers for Root Night.

**Session close:** all six draft animals recovered; soil and rain strong; light working; living course strong; western departure underway; no caravan-wide vote claimed.

## Session Three: One Long Breath

### Blackgrass Burn

Ansel and Len reach the burn at Root Night and work with Pelen, Rusk, Vara, and two margin crews. The objective is not “use fire.” It is to pass low bounded heat through three prepared segments while keeping oil, dry canvas, pollen, people, and the repair route outside the front.

The wagon-scar segment crosses between wet old-ground margins. The restored-corridor segment follows after living soil has passed and before the receiver dries. The crownward seam is held at its prepared edge until the Bearer's central exhale opens the gap.

The margin crews see shells open behind low heat while empty shells exposed too early fail. Earlier narrow scars retain living soil and flexible new roots. These observations establish fire as a necessary current without making uncontrolled flame desirable.

One dry canvas strip is spent as an outer warning margin. The remaining spice chest is recorded as abandoned property, not burned as convenient fuel. Rusk's captured oil is already west of the line.

The group misses the reflected second ignition because direct dawn has not yet reached the region. Bounded heat is working. Runaway fire remains absent.

**State leaving the encounter:** wagon scar and restored corridor safely burned; crownward seam prepared; bounded heat working; no runaway front; one company canvas strip spent.

### Root-Breath Chamber

Kesh and Shai enter with Nahl, Tobin, Corda, and a burden crew. They map three load bands rather than attempting to “control the Bearer.”

- The western carry band is overloaded by the abandoned axle and committed wagon pieces.
- The middle circulation band is warming where water return narrows.
- The crownward lift band is underfed before the restored soil stream arrives.

Kesh refuses to use Bell and Crook as a test weight. Dismantled wagon material provides a reversible load. The western burden is redistributed to the middle support band, the cooling route is reopened, and the restored soil stream enters under burden.

A preliminary breath confirms one service-root redirection and the wet middle ledge. It does not release a seed and does not consume the unique central exhale. Direct observation shows the crown stems slackening during one long interval and hardening immediately afterward. The prepared fire seam opens during the same phase.

The chamber crew misses the fungal voice-pattern clue because it does not reroute the water channels merely to hear the speech change. Root-breath timing is nevertheless established by two independent physical systems: stem tension and fire passage.

**State leaving the encounter:** burden map corrected; cooling active; service root and wet ledge prepared; root-pulse current working; deep exhale unused and approaching.

### Crown of Unfallen Rain

The crown descends while the verge crews begin mustering the first departures. The finale is already parallel.

The crown team diagnoses individual defects instead of making six random checks. Strong soil and rain arrive. The selected living braid enters one knot. The working light branch is redirected to the chosen receiver. Bounded heat waits at the seam. Balanced root pulse lifts the feeder ring.

Two knots mature. A third becomes viable but remains unopened. The crown team does not inspect the old torn knot closely enough to gain the historic cut comparison clue; it already has direct timing evidence from the chamber and burn.

Before separation, Len records five claims:

1. the selected knot is mature;
2. its own water return has closed;
3. a wet cradle bears its weight;
4. living-soil delivery continues;
5. another mature knot remains attached.

At the long central exhale, the crownward seam receives bounded heat. The selected knot completes its return. The feeder ring slackens. Len and the crown workers draw the ring free before the inward hardening.

One mature seed separates with a smooth living socket. One mature knot and one viable knot remain attached. The soil road continues to deliver. No common return is cut. The seed is therefore both cleanly separated and supported as a demonstrated surplus.

Shai and Nahl take joint custody of the closed wet cradle. Its living course is the Merewash-compatible braid selected in the hollow.

**State leaving the encounter:** one mature surplus seed carried; one mature and one viable knot attached; deep exhale spent once; no torn crown; forest continuation preserved under the available evidence.

### Session close

The inward draw begins. Nobody can repeat the separation. Crown workers and departure crews move toward the Glass Verge under the route capacities already prepared.

## Session Four: The Last Salt

### Glass Verge

The verge displays the accumulated state.

Ansel names three routes and their closing signs. Hessa reads every traveler. Orra asks each departure group to state what it heard and who bears each assisted person, animal team, hand load, wagon, or cradle.

The broad salt shelf carries the first walking group, dependents, most hand loads, Clove and Sedge, and one surviving camp wagon before the edge narrows.

The wet middle ledge carries the seed cradle under Shai and Nahl's custody. Bell and Crook bear the balanced support under Rua and Tess. Pelen, Lio, Sen, and Daro cross with carried or assisted support.

The crown service root carries Orra, Hessa, Ansel, Kesh, Len, the last route keepers, Moth and Reed, and the final records before the inward draw closes it.

Sio and Jun make separate eastbound choices. Each hears that a western route exists. Each hears the likely isolation, limited supplies, possible nonreturn, and absence of any promise that the forest is safe beyond the Glass Waste. Sio carries tested transcriptions and wants to observe whether the six-current account remains true on glass. Jun has been rescued, returned, informed, and given an actual western alternative before choosing continued migration. They take water, grain, blankets, tools, course records, and a named plan for shelter and reporting if the forest returns.

The verge team misses two late clues. It does not use the obsolete dead-guide route as evidence, and it does not rely on the distant wagon silhouette to rediscover people already accounted for. The misses matter to the journal without changing the final possibility space.

Direct sun closes the western footing.

## Demonstrated end state

### People

- Forty people reach the western White Salt alive.
- Sio Tern and Jun Alder continue east by separately recorded informed choice.
- No person is unresolved, dead, or silently absorbed into “the caravan.”
- Pelen, Lio, Sen, and Daro remain injured or mobility-limited but cross with named support.
- The Saltward Four all travel west.

### Animals

- Clove and Sedge cross on the broad shelf.
- Bell and Crook support the wet-ledged seed transport.
- Moth and Reed cross by the service root.
- Thistle crosses west without having been driven hard enough to leave her strained.
- All six missing draft animals and the camp mare survive.

### Property

The westbound caravan retains two spice chests, most copper pipe, Merewash seed grain, reservoir tools, medicinal planters, water casks, and dismantled useful loads.

The spice wagon, grain wagon, Pelen's frame, one measured pipe length, and several fittings remain with the forest. Ira ratifies the pipe's use but leaves compensation open. Pelen confirms his frame as a voluntary contribution. Abandoned spice and grain remain named claims; the successful seed does not erase them.

### Ecological currents

- **Living old soil:** strong.
- **Captured dawn:** working.
- **Circulating rain:** strong.
- **Remembered river course:** strong.
- **Bounded blackgrass heat:** working.
- **Bearer root pulse:** working.
- **Runaway fire:** absent.

The forest continues east with one mature knot and one viable knot attached. The repaired soil road remains active. The remaining light branch is imperfect but functioning. No common water return is severed.

### Carried seed

The carried knot is a mature surplus seed. It was separated cleanly during the only deep exhale after completing its water return into a prepared wet cradle. Its course is compatible with Merewash's observed fall, storage, ground, overflow, and interval needs.

It does not end the six-year drought at the verge. Shai and Nahl still must transport, establish, test, and care for another ecology.

## What this route demonstrates

### Uneven evidence survives contact with play

The journal finds seventy-four clues and names fifteen original misses. Five later second-look alternatives were not present when this route was recorded, so the current source contains twenty unspotted clues. The absences do not fall evenly. Dawn, motion, and convergence leave traces almost everywhere; the distinction between a remembered course and a surviving person rests on fewer, more deliberate tests. The adventure does not top up every conclusion to the same confidence after the fact.

### People can be saved while wagons are lost

All thirteen separated travelers survive. Three missing wagons do not. Frames become supports, rope becomes route, animals need handlers, and injured people need named carriers. Rescue is not measured by how much property remains intact.

### The strongest ending leaves work behind

The Saltward Four carry a mature surplus seed west and leaves another mature knot attached. It also leaves compensation claims, an injured wheelwright, abandoned goods, a difficult living transport, and two people beyond ordinary contact. At Merewash, the seed begins a watershed only if Shai, Nahl, and others continue the work. Success is a new burden borne willingly, not the erasure of burden.

### The forest never grants permission

No voice, pulse, flame, moth, or crown declares the taking right. The crown team sees continuing old-ground delivery, two mature knots, a closed water return, bounded heat, and a clean living socket. From those facts it judges that one seed is surplus. The judgment is accountable because the evidence is material and incomplete.

### Departure remains plural

Ela leaves before seed work is finished. Forty people eventually go west. Sio and Jun go east for different reasons after hearing the western route and the cost of refusing it. The Saltward Four remain together because each chooses west at the end. “The caravan decided” would be false in every useful sense.

### Glass Dawn reveals the shape already made

The final light offers no three doors. Earlier triage makes a complete human rescue possible. Earlier repair makes a surplus seed possible. Earlier route reports make divided departure possible. Earlier consent practice keeps Sio and Jun from becoming symbols claimed by other people. At the verge, the table sees those choices as bodies, loads, knots, animals, debts, and the last strip of salt going away beneath them.
