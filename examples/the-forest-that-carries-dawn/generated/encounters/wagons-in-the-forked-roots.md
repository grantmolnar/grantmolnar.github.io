# Wagons in the Forked Roots

**Role:** necessary  
**Tags:** caravan, rescue, salvage, moving-region

Five wagons divide into three moving rescue pockets where people, animals, intact cargo, and the broken soil road cannot all be saved by the same method.

## Opening View

“Miri?” “Here.” The spice wagon drops an inch, and every rope tied to it answers. Tovan lies beneath the raised wheel; copper knocks twice from the split freight bed; a mule bell rings in the low hollow. The three pockets are separating. Between pulses, a person can cross. Tovan looks up at the lines. “People first. Tell me which one.”

## Encounter Material

## Situation

The missing wagons did not travel together. When the root mat rose, five frames slid into three pockets along a divided root. Thirteen people and three teams remain among them. During Long Shade the pockets can still be crossed in sequence. At Red Dusk the fork begins to open, and every pulse puts more moving distance between one rescue and the next.

Wood complains before the roots move. Ropes go taut, axles lift, and a wagon that bore weight a breath ago becomes a hanging load. Watch the rhythm and movement can happen in the stillness; rush it and the method sets the cost—a cut frame, dropped chest, frightened animal, rescuer stranded across the next pulse, or route that no longer bears a litter.

Keep the pockets distinct. At each one, name the people, next pressure sign, present capacity, and what must be abandoned before the next pulse.

## The three pockets

### The upper wheel

A spice wagon hangs by its rear axle. **Hessa Clay's brother Tovan**, teamsters **Deme Orr** and **Salen Pru**, and nine-year-old **Miri Pell** are tied into the frame. They are not in immediate bodily danger while the axle holds. The wagon's iron rim, however, has crushed a stream of bronze-green beetles and blocked a dark-soil track beneath it.

Tovan wants the people lowered first. Deme is preserving the axle as a support, not defending the cargo. Salen asks to save one chest against old debts but yields it if the line must be cut. Miri has noticed that the surviving beetles push soil around the wreck rather than eat the spilled grain. **Clove** and **Sedge**, the harnessed spice team, can lower the frame or pull a bridge once freed, but doing both before Red Dusk requires relief and careful staging.

### The split freight bed

A copper-pipe wagon has broken across two roots. **Pelen Marr**, the caravan wheelwright, has a pinned leg but remains able to direct work. Reservoir laborers **Yaro Pell**, **Besh Renn**, **Corda Vale**, and **Fenn Sable** are safe on the western half. The mule **Bell** stands on the eastern half and may be carried away when the roots separate; its teammate **Crook** has already crossed toward the low hollow.

Pelen knows the wagon scar damaged something important because the beetles' route had been worn smooth before the crash. He does not know what the route serves. He offers his wagon frame, tools, and labor for a repair once the people are free. The copper belongs to Ira Senn, and Corda's climbing rope remains Corda's. Pelen names those limits without excuse. He distinguishes responsibility for the repair from guilt for a disaster nobody intended.

### The low hollow

The grain wagon and a passenger cart fell into a humid pocket below the fork. **Jun Alder**, a seventeen-year-old drover, shelters there with midwife **Iri Fen**, merchant apprentice **Mael Hove**, and elderly pilgrim **Daro Kes**. The pocket has warm rain, edible pale shoots, and a route deeper into the forest. It also has no clear road back large enough for the grain wagon.

Jun has calmed Crook by following a repeating leaf-click rather than calling over it. Iri remains with Daro until a carrier exists. Mael can walk but fears that any departing rescuer is abandoning the hollow. Jun is fascinated by the forest and frightened of returning to a life in which every place is temporary. This does not mean Jun has already chosen to remain. It means rescue should include asking what the person wants once immediate danger is removed.

## Pocket pressures

Each pocket changes in a different visible way. Announce the warning before applying the consequence.

Delay changes methods, burdens, and later capacity; it does not declare an automatic death.

## Running a rescue operation

For each operation, name the people moved, the load holding or threatening them, the destination, the means bearing the work, and what will be abandoned or delayed. Resolve rescue at that declared scale, never as one check for the whole region.

A weak result should impose a consequence that follows from the method: a rescuer ends across the next root pulse, an animal becomes loose or strained, a tool or cargo load is lost, an injury worsens, the soil track receives oil or iron, or the stage advances before intact salvage can be attempted. Do not replace a legible rescue problem with arbitrary fatality.

Use the functional movement states in `CARAVAN-AND-RESCUE-LEDGER.md`: walking, assisted, carried, stabilize first, and voluntary remainder. A trapped person must be freed or stabilized before remaining can count as a meaningful choice.

## Rescue order

Any order is possible. The moving fork makes three goals increasingly incompatible:

- free every person;
- recover every draft animal; and
- preserve intact wagons and cargo.

During Long Shade, excellent planning can achieve all three in one pocket and two in the others. By Red Dusk, intact heavy salvage from the upper wheel or split freight bed requires extraordinary means. By Root Night, people can still be extracted, but the wagons become part of the moving structure unless dismantled where they lie.

Ask what is being supported, cut, lowered, calmed, or abandoned. One rescue roll cannot settle all three pockets; tools and earlier choices set the available methods.

## The damaged soil route

The upper wheel and split freight bed lie across a preexisting track of compact dark soil. Hundreds of soilbearers approach, stop, and detour. Some attempt to climb the iron rims and fall. Others carry their pellets into side hollows where pale rootlets consume the soil without sending it onward.

Evidence here establishes several useful distinctions:

- the beetles carry soil gathered elsewhere;
- the track existed before the caravan arrived;
- the forest's roots open small gates when soilbearers pass in sufficient numbers; and
- the broken route can be repaired with non-toxic old soil, stable ramps, and a continuous scent trail.

The caravan has materials that can help: grain sacks, untreated boards, copper troughs, leather, rope, and soil from potted medicinal plants. Some are private property. Pelen can identify owners and alternatives.

At Red Dusk the advancing white front remains low on damp root until it touches the spice-oil stripe. There it climbs rope and canvas while the same flame dies against wet living soil. Show that change before asking how the party will clear, cool, or abandon the spill.

## Animal complications

The three missing teams respond differently.

- **Clove and Sedge**, the spice team, are still harnessed and can pull against the suspended wagon if panicked. Clove follows Tovan through bell noise; Sedge freezes during root pulses.
- **Bell and Crook**, the copper team, have split. Bell follows Pelen or Rua Marr; Crook follows Jun or Tess Coil.
- **Moth and Reed**, the grain team, escaped deeper along the warm-rain route and leave hoofprints toward the basins. Jun, Olan Peat, or Tess can recover them.

Animals can become later assets. A calm team can move repair material, draw a wagon bed into a new bridge, or carry injured travelers to the Glass Verge. A loose team can damage lantern roots, scatter soilbearers, or choose its own safe route out.

## Departure and consent

Once immediate danger is removed, ask each separated group what plan it has actually heard. Orra's original commission may no longer fit the reports brought back. The upper group may want the first exit. Pelen may volunteer for soil-route repair after treatment. Jun may ask to accompany anyone studying the forest rather than return directly to camp.

Rescuers may recommend and carry offers. Rescue does not create custody. A person who stays to help after being offered a workable exit is making a different choice from a person left because no one asked.

A complete report names who was seen, what route is open, what burdens it accepts, what visible change will close it, and what proposal each person actually heard. Tasa and Yaro Pell may receive news about Miri at different times; Hessa and Tovan may choose different work after reunion; Jun's later decision cannot be inferred from surviving the hollow.

## Late access and moving evidence

When the second gully closes, reduce the pocket by burden rather than declaring everyone lost. During Root Night a narrow extraction may still free people and hand loads, but intact wagons and most heavy cargo cannot cross. At Glass Dawn unresolved people and animals are carried east with the forest; they are exposed and cut off, not automatically dead.

Freed survivors, wagon bells, draft animals, rope marks, and wreck debris may carry evidence away from the pocket. State that origin when recording a wagon-sourced clue. A later bell or animal can preserve evidence of the fork, burden, or deep pulse; it cannot restore an intact wagon or make an absent rescue report reach the camp retroactively.
