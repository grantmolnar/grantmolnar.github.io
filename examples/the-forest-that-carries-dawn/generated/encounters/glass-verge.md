# Glass Verge

**Role:** necessary, end  
**Tags:** end, departure, glass-waste, moving-region

Reflected dawn reduces three departure routes while rescue, seed separation, and individual decisions proceed in parallel across the last White Salt.

## Opening View

The first wagon reaches the salt shelf as dawn kindles beneath the leaves. Its axle grinds; behind it, a litter team waits for room, and above them the crown service root hangs slack for one breath. Eastward, the forest is already taking back the ground. Hessa reads the roster over wheels, bells, and rising heat. Each name answers west, east, or not yet.

## Encounter Material

## Situation

Set the verge while the western roots still touch White Salt but every pulse sheds more of it. The crown follows from east to west; broad routes lose burden before they lose contact. Dawn is first a brightness under the leaves, then heat in the salt, then the end of ordinary westward footing.

By Root Night the caravan may be whole, divided, or still searching. The crown may hold no viable knot, one knot the forest needs, or a demonstrated surplus. Chamber work may have favored the western carry band, wet middle band, or crownward service root. Display accumulated state rather than presenting a menu; the finale adds no preset solutions.

At the verge every carried report becomes a body, load, animal, cradle, injury, or unresolved name. Keep distinct people from disappearing inside “the caravan.”

## Three roads before the salt closes

These are route families, not guaranteed corridors. Earlier rescue, burden correction, basin work, and Bearer redirection determine their exact starting points and how long they remain usable.

| Route | Capacity while open | Principal limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Broad salt shelf | Walkers, assisted people, litters, calm teams, dismantled wagons, and at first an intact light wagon | Farthest from the crown; it loses wagon capacity at the inward draw and all contact at direct sun |
| Wet middle ledge | Walkers, assisted people, single-file animals, hand loads, and one carried person at a time | Requires a working cooled basin edge; no intact wagon and no panicked team |
| Crown service root | Crown workers, climbers, one supported litter or rain-seed cradle, and compact tools | Opens fully only on the deep exhale and closes at the inward draw; it cannot carry wagons or loose animals |

A favored western carry band extends the broad shelf through one additional heavy movement. A favored middle circulation band keeps the wet ledge cool and broad enough for an additional carried person or animal passage. A favored crownward lift band brings the service root closer to the crown and lets a prepared seed team reach it without another transfer. Each effect changes a route’s capacity or duration.

If a route was damaged or never established, state the reduced capacity. Do not quietly restore it because the finale has begun.

## The dawn signals

Use four visible signals rather than a hidden round count.

### First reflection

The easternmost crown stems turn mirror-bright, blackgrass smolders without contact, and the broad shelf begins shedding salt. At this signal, intact wagons, most animals, and people not remaining for crown work should already be moving.

Show it before heavy departure closes. A prepared group already moving can finish its crossing. Beginning a new heavy salvage operation now means knowingly surrendering an earlier exit.

### Deep exhale

At least three cues coincide: root lines slacken, vent leaves turn outward, basin surfaces flatten, pale cords lose tension, the crown drops a handspan, or blackgrass flame leans inward. The crown service root opens, mature sockets soften, and clean seed separation becomes possible.

The exhale is also the last reliable interval for a supported litter from crown or chamber to reach the service root. It is not long enough to improvise every missing preparation from nothing.

### Inward draw

Water draws east, the vents close, crown stems harden, and the service root contracts. The broad shelf no longer accepts an intact wagon. The wet ledge remains only where circulation and cooling were preserved. Anyone still at the crown must leave by a prepared line, accept a damaging forced route, or continue east.

### Direct sun

The first direct sun touches the Glass Waste. Reflected heat produces a second fire threat, the last roots lift from salt, and ordinary westward footing ends. A crown knot that missed its living dawn filament remains dark in the glare; reflection does not backfill the broken path. People still aboard enter a hotter, faster migration with reduced supplies, no familiar exit, and no assurance of another clean separation interval.

## Departure muster

Run departure planning as an operational record, not a vote that binds everyone.

1. **Count:** Hessa or another recorder names everyone physically seen, everyone unresolved, and everyone whose location is known only by report.
2. **State capacities:** route leaders state which people, animals, litters, wagons, and loads each open route can actually carry.
3. **Give the current plan:** each departure group hears where it is going, what physical signal closes its route, and whether the seed attempt changes its timing.
4. **Record choice:** mark first-departure, rescue remainder, seed group, unresolved, physically unable, or voluntary eastbound status separately.
5. **Assign support:** name carriers, handlers, guides, route keepers, and who takes responsibility when a principal worker leaves.
6. **Send a record:** each westbound group carries the names and intentions known when it departed; later groups do not become unaccounted merely because they chose a different time.

A person may leave without approving seed-taking. A person may help complete the seed and still leave west. A person may continue east only after immediate rescue or stabilization has made that a real choice. Silence, absence, injury, youth, dependency, and admiration are not consent.

## Running the finale in parallel

The crown and verge should normally be active at the same time.

When a seed clears the service root, show it beside any attached mature knot and name whether soilbearers still enter the crown. Only that simultaneous view establishes whether one cloud leaves while another travels east.

- At first reflection, Orra or another route leader sends the heavy first departure while the crown team diagnoses and prepares a receiver.
- Before the deep exhale, crown workers complete any final living path, settle who bears the seed, and place people who need the service root.
- During the deep exhale, the seed team may perform one clean separation while prepared departure teams use the service root and other open routes.
- At the inward draw, remaining westbound people take the last routes their bodies and burdens can use. Workers who stay name what they are staying to do.
- At direct sun, record everyone still aboard, their supplies, the forest states they can observe, and whether their continuing search or repair remains possible.

Split parties are expected. One group does not lose its action because another acts elsewhere. Advance the signal when the underlying physical event occurs, after showing its approach and resolving work already underway.

## Principal positions at the verge

These are starting judgments, not commands.

- **Orra Venn** sends the company core west before the verge becomes heroic. If she stays, she names the last group in her charge and who accepts it.
- **Hessa Clay** reads unresolved and eastbound names over the route calls. Silence, absence, and unheard plans make no departure entry.
- **Nahl Reed** checks whether an attached mature knot still completes its circuit. Westward departure without a seed remains a real success.
- **Pelen Marr** puts his frame, fittings, labor, and judgment on the material list. Ira's pipe and Corven's grain keep their own names.
- **Sio Tern** taps the last inherited line against present glass, breath, and movement. If measure and world part, Sio keeps the observation.
- **Jun Alder** answers only after water, food, shelter, eastern hazards, possible nonreturn, and an open western route lie side by side. Wonder begins the question; it does not answer it.

## Failure-forward departures

A failed crossing changes who or what can use the route next.

- A litter line parts: the person reaches safety, but two carriers and the replacement line end on opposite sides.
- An animal balks: its load can be divided, abandoned, or carried while the handler chooses whether to remain.
- A wagon grounds: people and hand loads cross; intact property does not.
- The seed cradle shifts: the seed can be stabilized by spending a hand load, route keeper, or animal capacity.
- A route margin burns: the intended group crosses, but the route closes behind it.
- A late worker misses the westward line: continued migration, shelter in a cooled basin, or another eastbound route becomes the next problem instead of immediate offscreen death.

## Final record

End play by writing one exact sentence for each subject:

- every traveler: westbound, eastbound, unresolved, injured, or dead;
- each draft animal and the burden it carried;
- the condition of the three wagon pockets and any people still within them;
- each of the six ecological currents;
- bounded heat and any separate runaway fire;
- the Bearer's burden, favored band, and deep-exhale state;
- every mature, stormglass, torn, or attached crown knot;
- whether any separation was clean and whether it was a demonstrated surplus;
- the route and custodian of any carried seed;
- what Merewash actually receives besides or instead of a seed;
- the people who continue east and the supplies and obligations they carry; and
- property used, lost, disputed, ratified, or owed after dawn.

Several outcomes will be true together. Close when every name has a direction, every living relationship a state, and every surviving obligation a bearer.

## Late groups and closed westward access

The verge is a physical horizon and departure condition, not a clue-locked room. A group that reaches it late uses the capacity actually remaining on the broad shelf, wet ledge, or service root. Knowledge of an earlier route does not preserve its burden class, and a report not yet delivered cannot guide people who never heard it.

After direct sun, ordinary westward footing ends. Anyone still aboard continues east from the actual state of injuries, water, shelter, animals, fire, and current damage. Do not convert missed departure into automatic death or quietly reopen the salt behind the Bearer.
