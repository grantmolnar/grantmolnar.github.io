# Encounter Index: The Forest That Carries Dawn

## Camp Under New Leaves (`camp-under-new-leaves`)

**Role:** necessary, start

Hessa's roster turns a stranded camp into thirteen named rescues, two westward routes, and a commission that must be renewed before seed work begins.

## Wagons in the Forked Roots (`wagons-in-the-forked-roots`)

**Role:** necessary

Five wagons divide into three moving rescue pockets where people, animals, intact cargo, and the broken soil road cannot all be saved by the same method.

## Soilbearer Road (`soilbearer-road`)

**Role:** necessary

Beetles carry living ground across oil, iron, and closing gates; a repair must bear two-way traffic, preserve the substrate, and survive the coming burn.

## Lantern Canopy (`lantern-canopy`)

**Role:** necessary

Glasswings lend stored dawn along wet living stems; severed brilliance still shines, but no longer reaches a crownward receiver.

## Warm Rain Basins (`warm-rain-basins`)

**Role:** necessary

Warm rain climbs through a branching circuit that can sustain caravan drinking, rescue two mules, cool the Bearer, and still return—or be drained at a named cost.

## Hollow of Kept Voices (`hollow-of-kept-voices`)

**Role:** necessary

Fungal cords keep gradients, storage, ground, and overflow after names decay, allowing a defensible river course without mistaking memory for a surviving person.

## Blackgrass Burn (`blackgrass-burn`)

**Role:** necessary

A low renewal fire must cross the wagon scar, repaired soil corridor, and crownward seam without taking a second road through oil, canvas, pollen, or people.

## Root-Breath Chamber (`root-breath-chamber`)

**Role:** necessary

Inside the Bearer's load chamber, crews read burden, cooling, and rhythm to favor one route band and prepare for a single nonrepeatable deep exhale.

## Crown of Unfallen Rain (`crown-of-unfallen-rain`)

**Role:** necessary

The descending crown exposes every missing relationship and distinguishes empty shell, stormglass, viable seed, mature knot, and demonstrated surplus.

## Glass Verge (`glass-verge`)

**Role:** necessary, end

Reflected dawn reduces three departure routes while rescue, seed separation, and individual decisions proceed in parallel across the last White Salt.
