# The White Salt and the Glass Waste

**Reference ID:** `891a38f1-e18c-4cb3-b178-7a2b583b4491`  
**Kind:** Place  
**Aliases:** White Salt, Glass Waste  
**Tags:** White Salt, Glass Waste, dawn deadline, departure

## Summary

The caravan’s ordinary road lies west and south across exposed salt, while the reflective eastern waste hardens the migrating forest at direct dawn and ends ordinary westward footing.

## Reference Material

## One crossing, two countries

The White Salt is exposed western ground crossed by the Cairnroad on its way south and west toward Merewash. The migrating forest passed beneath the sleeping caravan there and continues east. The Glass Waste is the bright mineral country ahead, not distant water. Reaching it carries the forest away from the road and into a hotter passage beyond ordinary return.

## Dawn closes the salt

First reflection sheds salt from the broad western roots. The Bearer’s inward draw closes living paths and hardens crown tissue. At direct sun, reflected heat lifts the last roots from the salt and ordinary westward footing ends. Reflection can close a missed interval; it cannot replace captured living dawn, restore a spent route, or manufacture another clean exhale.

## Direction without destiny

A westward departure returns toward the caravan’s intended country. Continuing east accepts a different journey with the supplies, injuries, water, shelter, animals, and obligations actually aboard. Neither survival, wonder, youth, nor delay decides that choice for anyone. Exact route capacity and current departure groups remain in the migration, verge, and play records.
