# Reference Index: The Forest That Carries Dawn

## People

- [**Orra Venn**](66e19be9-14c6-41e3-833f-2c532fe37337.md) — The Cairnroad caravan master keeps forty-two names exact, assigns company labor, and preserves workable departures without claiming private property or another person’s choice. Aliases: Caravan Master Orra Venn, Orra.
- [**Hessa Clay**](4f85895f-92d6-4386-98ee-0228b34b72b9.md) — The caravan’s roster keeper separates sight, report, injury, movement, and informed departure so no person disappears inside “everyone” or “the caravan.” Aliases: Hessa, Roster Keeper Hessa Clay.
- [**Nahl Reed**](266c943b-9aa9-4cd1-aed2-12be6b9e2cb9.md) — The watershed assessor bound for Merewash tests a rain-seed report and living water paths against measurable evidence while refusing to turn ecological capacity into permission. Aliases: Nahl, Watershed Assessor Nahl Reed.
- [**Pelen Marr**](8b50ba07-c72a-4f4e-b256-262ddb032f89.md) — The wheelwright reads frames, iron, and load, accepts repair responsibility, and offers his own property and labor without treating guilt as title over anyone else’s goods. Aliases: Pelen, Wheelwright Pelen Marr.
- [**Sio Tern**](14d29a1c-1c91-4787-bfc0-f39c7dfcb204.md) — The elderly route-singer carries a six-line fragment from a previous crossing and tests inherited cadence against present matter without allowing intimacy to certify a condition. Aliases: Sio, Route-singer Sio Tern.
- [**Jun Alder**](05574ea0-6d5a-4187-ab70-91a2d2cb7836.md) — The young drover reads animals and leaf-clicks; the eastward road may become imaginable only after rescue and a specific informed alternative, never from wonder alone. Aliases: Jun, Drover Jun Alder.

## Places

- [**Merewash**](5a740217-a92f-4222-a49d-d8b2ce5251d3.md) — The drought-struck Cairnroad destination lies in clay and limestone above old salt lenses; any carried water course must provide durable storage and safe overflow rather than one destructive flood.
- [**The White Salt and the Glass Waste**](891a38f1-e18c-4cb3-b178-7a2b583b4491.md) — The caravan’s ordinary road lies west and south across exposed salt, while the reflective eastern waste hardens the migrating forest at direct dawn and ends ordinary westward footing. Aliases: White Salt, Glass Waste.

## Other

- [**The Bearer**](a5e45f21-b17d-4663-a069-0f340cc6851b.md) — The immense root animal carries the forest east through traction, venting, one deep exhale, and inward draw; its bodily responses can be accommodated but never treated as speech or consent. Aliases: the root animal, the immense root animal.
