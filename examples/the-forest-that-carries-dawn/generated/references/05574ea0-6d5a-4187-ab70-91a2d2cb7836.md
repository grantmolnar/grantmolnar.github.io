## Summary

## Reference Material

## A drover in the low hollow

Jun Alder is seventeen and already trusted with draft animals because Jun notices footing, breath, panic, load, and the small changes that precede a bolt or collapse. Jun also hears differences in leaf-clicks that older travelers dismiss as one sound. Useful observation does not become less useful because its maker is young.

## East becomes imaginable

The moving forest makes an eastward road imaginable to Jun before it makes that road safe or chosen. Wonder, aptitude, and the ability to read animals or living currents do not amount to destiny. Jun’s interest may matter, but it cannot settle Jun’s survival, obligations, supplies, or route.

## Before east becomes a road

An eastward choice requires completed rescue, food, water, shelter, the known isolation and possible nonreturn of the Glass Waste, and a real western alternative that Jun could take instead. Only then can curiosity become consent rather than pressure created by emergency, dependency, or lack of options.
