# Pelen Marr

**Reference ID:** `8b50ba07-c72a-4f4e-b256-262ddb032f89`  
**Kind:** Person  
**Aliases:** Pelen, Wheelwright Pelen Marr  
**Tags:** wheelwright, repair, property, responsibility

## Summary

The wheelwright reads frames, iron, and load, accepts repair responsibility, and offers his own property and labor without treating guilt as title over anyone else’s goods.

## Reference Material

## Frame, iron, and load

Pelen reads a wagon by frame, iron, grain, old repairs, and the load each part can safely carry. He can distinguish a support that will flex from one that will split, a fitting that may be recovered from one that will be consumed, and a repair that redistributes burden from one that merely hides it.

## Responsibility without title

Pelen accepts responsibility for repairs he made and harm his judgment helped cause. He may surrender his own frame, tools, fittings, and labor. Guilt does not give him title over Ira Senn’s copper, another merchant’s cargo, company stores, or a traveler’s body and route. A useful repair still requires the right owner or authority.

## Repairs he can defend

Pelen prefers repairs whose loads, losses, and owners can be named. He separates old fittings that have become dangerous from parts that have carried weight safely for decades, and he distinguishes borrowed, lent, surrendered, recoverable, consumed, and abandoned material rather than flattening them into salvage.
