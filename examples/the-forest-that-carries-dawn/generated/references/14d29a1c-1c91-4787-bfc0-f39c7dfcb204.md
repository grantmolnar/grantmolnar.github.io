## Summary

## Reference Material

## Six lines from the previous crossing

Sio carries six lines learned from a route-singer who survived an earlier crossing. The fragment preserves measured relations among movement, breath, rain, root, crown, and glass; it is not a complete map, prophecy, or command. Its independent transmission makes it useful evidence, but age and repetition do not make it sufficient evidence.

## Measure beneath memory

Sio tests cadence against present matter: intervals between calls, changes in leaf-clicks, returning phrases, moving burdens, and the timing of breath or glass. A remembered line becomes useful when its measure can be compared with what the region is doing now. The song can organize attention without replacing observation.

## What inheritance cannot certify

The Hollow may repeat a voice closely enough to expose a retained work-pattern, but familiarity does not prove personhood, consent, kinship, safety, or present intention. Sio can identify resemblance and drift. Sio cannot certify conditions that no living witness or measurable process has established.
