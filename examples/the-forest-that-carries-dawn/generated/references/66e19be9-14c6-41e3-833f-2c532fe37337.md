## Summary

The Cairnroad caravan master keeps forty-two names exact, assigns company labor, and preserves workable departures without claiming private property or another person’s choice.

## Reference Material

## Forty-two names before a plan

Orra begins with people, not cargo or routes. The Cairnroad company has forty-two named travelers in its care, and rescue remains the first obligation even when an elegant seed plan or a fast departure would be easier to count. She refuses *the caravan* as a substitute for exact names, missing groups, witnesses, injuries, and people who still need a real choice.

## Rope, canvas, and a bounded commission

Orra may assign company labor and release company rope, canvas, tools, and other company property. Her commission covers rescue, route assessment, and the Merewash deadline. It does not let her spend private cargo, conscript anyone into seed work, or declare that survival has settled another person’s route or consent.

## Who carries the last names

Orra will send a functioning company core west when delay would endanger everyone. She remains answerable for the last group under company responsibility unless that duty is transferred to a named person who knowingly accepts it. Current crews, injuries, routes, property states, and departure groups belong in the operating ledgers rather than this record.
