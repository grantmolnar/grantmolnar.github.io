# Encounter Index: The Last Bell of Bramblewick

## Hearth Hall and the Map Room (`hearth-hall-and-the-map-room`)

**Role:** necessary, start

Merrit lies beneath a clock stopped at nine while rain, broken glass, and six suspects' genuine traces fight to become the room's first settled story.

## Merrit Alder's Burrow (`merrit-alder-s-burrow`)

**Role:** necessary

Thirty-four years of village memory separate Merrit's useful records, private leverage, and the ghost-hearth audit that made him dangerous to Orlo.

## The Copper Kettle and Long Pantry (`the-copper-kettle-and-long-pantry`)

**Role:** necessary

A burst crock and supper work rebuild the critical half hour while Mara's passages and brandy payments offer a motive chronology will not carry.

## Moss Apothecary (`moss-apothecary`)

**Role:** optional

Perrin can place death before the bell and match the blow, but only by opening dosage records he altered for another wrong.

## Alder Orchard (`alder-orchard`)

**Role:** necessary

Storm work, dog tracks, and wet wood account for Bram at the murder hour while his cuff and pipe confess an earlier trespass.

## Bramble Mill (`bramble-mill`)

**Role:** optional

The hot brake, rising gauge, and ergot notice separate Cora's pre-supper struggle from the rain-bound work that clears the killing interval.

## The Common Chest (`the-common-chest`)

**Role:** necessary

Two keys open two abuses of common money: Hester's hidden bridge and Orlo's six-year school fund built on dead children's names.

## Chapel of the Open Door (`chapel-of-the-open-door`)

**Role:** necessary

Amity restores three ghost hearths to the children whose names were taken and preserves the loan that first put those names under Orlo's hand.

## Bramblewick School (`bramblewick-school`)

**Role:** necessary

Practice marks, keys, ash, warrants, and a washed cuff turn Orlo's careful grammar into a route from Merrit's room to the final stanza.

## The North Hedge (`the-north-hedge`)

**Role:** necessary

Rain has blurred the footprints but preserved their order: one dry crossing joins the unlatched garden door to the school wash yard.

## The First-Bell Moot (`the-first-bell-moot`)

**Role:** necessary, end

At first bell, Bramblewick must decide which joined sequence can bear detention, accusation, and the cost of separating murder from every other village wrong.
