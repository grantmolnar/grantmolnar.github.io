## A second key in another pocket

Nim Thatch is a retired mill clerk and Bramblewick's current autumn lot-holder. He keeps the Common Chest's second key independently from the reeve, so no single office can open the village's coin, warrant bundles, and sealed records by itself.

## Number the bundles

Once the purpose is stated and appropriate witnesses are present, Nim turns his key, checks strings and seals, numbers every removed bundle in public, and helps compare request hand, authorizing seal, receiving mark, and surrendered warrant. His strength is a complete chain rather than a room covered with paper.

## Two turns of brass prove no story

Nim's participation establishes orderly access and custody, not the truth of every entry or the proper public scope of every name. Current seal condition, removed bundles, exhibit custody, privacy choices, accusations, and collateral findings remain in encounter procedure and play state.
