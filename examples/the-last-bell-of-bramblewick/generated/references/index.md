# Reference Index: The Last Bell of Bramblewick

## People

- [**Merrit Alder**](ebd4c505-37a2-4c07-8358-c449ccd4561f.md) — Bramblewick's Hearth Book keeper preserved births, debts, burials, relief warrants, and household succession; his signed audit invitation gives outside witnesses standing without granting them judgment or private-search authority. Aliases: Keeper Merrit Alder, Merrit.
- [**Orlo Vane**](33339ed8-373a-456a-8899-fec4007260ea.md) — Bramblewick's schoolmaster teaches the memorial recitation, keeps school copywork and supplies, and holds authority over lessons—not over pupils' testimony, civic judgment, or the village's privacy rules. Aliases: Schoolmaster Orlo Vane, Orlo.
- [**Hester Rowan**](40ac8ad0-a605-46ce-8964-5dff2bc84c8b.md) — Bramblewick's reeve keeps order, arranges lawful access, and convenes public process, but must recuse from her own accounts and cannot replace custodians, constable, witnesses, or moot. Aliases: Reeve Hester Rowan, Hester.
- [**Wil Sloe**](459d69fc-b40f-403f-bd03-9d87be597512.md) — Bramblewick's constable holds scenes, persons, and physical exhibits, preserves movement and custody, and may detain for evidence-backed risk without deciding guilt or controlling civic records. Aliases: Constable Wil Sloe, Wil.
- [**Sister Amity Thorne**](fe655672-7df2-49e9-a4ed-0de4764069ca.md) — Bramblewick's memorial sister keeps burial names and lending history, certifies copies, and protects children and living beneficiaries without turning pastoral authority into a verdict. Aliases: Sister Amity, Amity.
- [**Nim Thatch**](59f78a00-e4c0-4b1c-9853-ae9409715e8a.md) — The retired mill clerk and autumn lot-holder carries the Common Chest's second key, authenticates orderly access, and can trace warrant chains without granting unilateral entry or public exposure of private recipients. Aliases: Nim, Autumn Lot-holder Nim Thatch.
- [**Bram Alder**](93ccf6ae-a597-41d7-bf78-623bc46d8ba7.md) — Merrit's nephew works Alder Orchard, carries household and estate responsibilities, and can open family spaces without deciding succession, evidence, or guilt. Aliases: Bram, Merrit's heir.
- [**Mara Kettle**](6ca613bc-901c-4903-b629-7616e1de7138.md) — The Copper Kettle's innkeeper directs supper, pantry, staff, school meals, and service records while keeping hospitality distinct from testimony and civic judgment. Aliases: Mara, Mistress Mara Kettle.
- [**Cora Bramble**](7b980ead-bd09-4034-a5a8-b18c1ceaaf45.md) — Bramblewick's miller controls grain safety, machinery, emergency stops, and mill records without making a disputed sample, clock, route, or account self-proving. Aliases: Cora, Miller Cora Bramble.
- [**Perrin Moss**](f306d63b-3fd3-4eaa-b581-035ed71172b0.md) — Bramblewick's apothecary compounds medicine, keeps treatment cards, and can conduct supervised examinations while separating observation, inference, and custody. Aliases: Perrin, Apothecary Perrin Moss.

## Places

- [**The North Hedge**](586737d7-f233-4db3-80b8-a3e064612a6d.md) — The working lane joins Hearth Hall's north garden, Alder Orchard's low gate, the mill sluice approach, and the school wash yard; shared use makes sequence and timing more reliable than identity from one mark. Aliases: North Hedge, north hedge lane.

## Organizations

- [**Bramblewick School**](83fb58dc-f264-4e88-aaa5-0981fd907b11.md) — Bramblewick's one-room school teaches letters and memorial recitation, administers ordinary supplies and meals, and protects pupils' independent voices from institutional ownership. Aliases: the school, Bramblewick schoolhouse.
- [**The Chapel of the Open Door**](b4d821cc-ccfa-42ac-80ed-fe842bd3d9ee.md) — Bramblewick's memorial chapel preserves burial names, annual notices, lending history, and certified copies while keeping living families and pastoral confidence outside needless public exposure. Aliases: Open Door Chapel, the chapel.

## Objects

- [**The Hearth Book**](5986fde0-8b35-423a-892c-b9168c87e3c5.md) — Bramblewick's civic register uses working folios and clean public copies to preserve household memory while leaving authorship, alteration, and legal effect open to proof. Aliases: Hearth Book, Bramblewick Hearth Book.
- [**The Common Chest**](b0fbb6aa-8f2c-49d4-86ee-22a96e08ef70.md) — Bramblewick's two-key chest holds common coin, warrants, and sealed records under divided access, witnessed opening, numbered removal, and bounded disclosure. Aliases: Common Chest, Bramblewick Common Chest.

## Other

- [**The First-Bell Moot**](6540034b-60ec-404a-9768-173890c55c68.md) — Bramblewick's storm-bound public hearing tests what the village knows well enough to do before ordinary magistrate access returns; it is a witnessed burden, not a trial by office or rumor. Aliases: First-Bell Moot, first-bell hearing.
