## Reference Material

## Water, grain, and the brake

Cora Bramble runs Bramble Mill. She governs grain intake, sack separation, wheel and sluice work, brake and capstan use, worker safety, and the decision to stop production before water or bad grain carries harm through the village. The mill's public importance gives her duties beyond her own accounts.

## Bell, gauge, brake, rope

Quarter chimes, gauge slates, stream height, heat in the brake, receiving marks, and the order of moving sacks can preserve independent observations. Cora may explain the machinery and ordinary record practice, but no single mark proves a disputed time, visitor, route, or purpose by itself.

## Stop the wheel before blame

Cora may halt machinery, quarantine a lot, preserve a comparison sample, assign workers, and bring mill records to public review. She cannot certify the cause of an injury, settle another custodian's account, or make a civic finding by technical competence. Current notices, samples, work sequences, disclosures, and accusations remain in encounters and play state.
