## Summary

## Reference Material

## Lessons beneath a low roof

Orlo Vane runs Bramblewick School, teaches letters through household records and memorial verses, and coordinates the Founders' recitation. He is exact with copywork, red-pencil corrections, school schedules, and the ordinary warrants that keep meals, slate, soap, lamp oil, and winter clothes moving through the school.

## The school bell stops at the door

Orlo may direct lessons, dismiss or gather pupils, keep school materials, and speak for the school's daily needs. He cannot compel children to answer an investigation, decide what their families disclose, convert protective concern into sole custody of civic records, or bind the chapel, chest, constable, reeve, or moot.
